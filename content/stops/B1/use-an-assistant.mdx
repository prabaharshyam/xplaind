Pick a real task, not a toy one: a summary of a document you know, an email you need to write, a small calculation you can check.

**Do this**

1. Ask the assistant to do the task. Read the answer slowly.
2. Pick one fact in it and look that fact up somewhere else.
3. Ask again with more detail about what you want. Compare the two answers.

Keep notes. The notes are your project for this stop.
