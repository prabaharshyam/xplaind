Do chapters 1 to 3 of the course. Then find your own company's or college's rules on AI tools, if they exist, and read them once.

**Keep these rules in your head**

- Do not paste anything confidential into a tool your organisation has not approved: client names, contracts, salaries, health data, passwords, source code that is not public.
- An AI answer is a draft. You stay responsible for what you send.
- If a tool is approved, check what it keeps: some keep your chats and some do not.

**You are done when you can** explain to a colleague, in a minute, what you would never paste into a public chat assistant and why.
