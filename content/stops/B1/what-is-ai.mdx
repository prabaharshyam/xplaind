Watch the talk once without notes. Then close the tab and say, out loud, what you remember in three sentences: what AI is, what machine learning adds, and what a language model does.

**What to look for**

- A language model is trained on a very large amount of text to predict what comes next.
- Machine learning means the computer finds patterns from examples, instead of following rules a person wrote for every case.
- AI is the wider name. Machine learning is one way to build it.

When you can say those three ideas without looking, take the check below.
