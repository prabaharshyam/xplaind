Reread the patterns article with a design in mind: which patterns would your product need?

**Design one product**

Pick something real, such as a support assistant for an Indian bank. Write:

1. what users need and what success looks like, with numbers
2. the parts: retrieval, models, tools, storage, monitoring
3. how a request flows through them
4. what can go wrong and the guard for each
5. how you would evaluate it before and after launch
6. rough cost per user per month
