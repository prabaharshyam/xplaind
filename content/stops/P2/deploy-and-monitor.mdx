Watch the LLMOps lecture, then the one-hour launch lecture.

**Steps**

1. Deploy one of your apps on a free tier of a hosting service.
2. Log every request: time, latency, tokens, errors, and a user thumbs up or down if you can.
3. Build one simple page or report that shows these numbers for the last week.
