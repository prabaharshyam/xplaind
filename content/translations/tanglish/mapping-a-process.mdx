## Idhu enna

Edhaiyum automate panradhukku munnaadi, AI vachaalum illaadhaalum, velai unmaiyaa eppadi nadakkudhu-nu theriyanum. **Process map**-na, oru starting event-la irundhu oru outcome varaikkum velai eppadi pogudhu-nu kaattura diagram. Ovvoru step-kum idhu kaattum:

- **enna** nadakkudhu;
- **yaar** pannuraanga;
- **endha system** illa tool: email, Tally, portal, WhatsApp, Excel;
- kaathirukkaradhu serthu **evvalavu time** aagudhu;
- **enga** errors, rework, delays nadakkudhu;
- "amount ₹50,000-ku mela irukka?" maadhiri **decision points**.

```flow
dir: LR
Invoice arrives | who: mail room, system: email -> Typed into Excel | who: accounts, 4 min
Typed into Excel -> Approved? | who: manager, waits 2 days: the bottleneck
Approved? -[yes]-> Paid | who: accounts, system: Tally, 3 min
```

Idha paper-la, whiteboard-la, illa free diagram tool-la varaiyalaam. Boxes-um arrows-um podhum. Varaiyuradhula value illa; adhukku nadakkura pechu-m, adhula pogura numbers-um thaan value.

Indha lesson Ludhiana-la oru textile exporter-oda accounts team supplier invoices-a eppadi pay panraanga-nu map panradha follow pannum. Mudiyum podhu, oru real process-a map panni, rough-aa alandhu, edhai first maathanum-nu decide panna mudiyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### 1. Clear edges irukkura oru process-a edunga

Clear start, end irukkura oru flow-a edunga: "accounts"-nu illa, "supplier invoice vandhadhula irundhu payment aagura varaikkum". Periya, theliva illaadha processes periya, theliva illaadha maps-a tharum, adha yaarum use pannaadhu.

### 2. Real process-a map pannunga, official-a illa

Manager solradhu perumbaalum official version. Velai paakuravangalukku workarounds-oda, kaathirukkaradhu-oda real version theriyum. Avangaloda ukkaarndhu, **kadaisiya real case**-a step by step solla sollunga: "Neththu vandha invoices-a nee enna pannina-nu kaattu." Avanga solra pothu ovvoru step-aiyum sticky note-la illa box-la ezhudhunga.

Useful kelvigal:

- "Adutha enna pannuva?"
- "Andha information enga irundhu kidaikkudhu?"
- "Adhukku evvalavu time aagudhu? Evvalavu neram kaathirukka?"
- "Thappaa ponaa enna aagum?"

### 3. Details serunga, mukkiyama exceptions

Ovvoru step-kum yaar, endha system, evvalavu time, evvalavu thadava thappaagudhu-nu note pannunga. Appuram exceptions pathi kelunga: "GSTIN illaadha pothu enna? Amount PO-oda match aagaadha pothu?" Exceptions rare, aana slow, pala neram mothha time-la perumbaalum adhula pogum.

### 4. Rough-aa alanga

Perfect data thevai illa. Priority podavum, apram gain-a prove panna-vum rough numbers podhum:

| Measure | Eppadi kidaikkum | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume | Kadantha maasa cases-a ennunga | Maasathukku 600 invoices |
| Oru item-ku hands-on time | Konja real cases-a time paarunga | Oru invoice type panna 4 nimisham |
| Waiting time | Emails illa system-la dates-a compare pannunga | Approval-ku 2 naal |
| Exception rate | Extra velai thevaipatta cases-a ennunga | 15% PO number illa |
| Error illa rework rate | Corrections-a ennunga | 3% thappaa type aachu |

Indha numbers thaan unga **baseline**.

### 5. Waste-a kandupidinga

Typical signs:

- ore data rendu, adhukku mela idathula type aagradhu;
- approvals illa replies-ku kaathirukkaradhu;
- emails, folders-la information thedradhu;
- ore vishayatha rendu thadava check panradhu;
- pesikka mudiyura systems-ukku naduvula kaiyaala copy panradhu.

### 6. Step by step decide pannunga

Waste-aana ovvoru step-kum, indha aindhu options-la onnai edunga:

```flow
Is the step needed at all? -[no]-> Remove it
Is the step needed at all? -[yes]-> Can it be simpler?
Can it be simpler? -[yes]-> Simplify it
Can it be simpler? -[no]-> Are the rules clear and fixed?
Are the rules clear and fixed? -[yes]-> Automate with rules
Are the rules clear and fixed? -[no]-> Is it reading or writing text?
Is it reading or writing text? -[yes]-> Automate with an AI step | plus a human check
Is it reading or writing text? -[no]-> Leave it, for now
```

Automate panradhukku munnaadi waste-a remove pannunga. Thevaiyillaadha step-a automate pannina waste vegamaa nadakkum, avvalavu thaan.

## Oru worked example

Ludhiana-la oru textile exporter-oda accounts team-la rendu assistants-um oru manager-um irukkaanga. Maasathukku sumaar 600 supplier invoices process panraanga. Manager "oru invoice-ku konja nimisham thaan"-nu nenaikkuraar.

Team kadantha Tuesday-oda invoices-a vachu, serndhu real process-a map panraanga:

| Step | Yaar | System | Time | Problems |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Invoice email, WhatsApp, illa courier-la varudhu; paper-a irundha scan panraanga | Assistant | Email, phone, scanner | Ovvoru paper invoice-ku 5 nimisham | Moonu channels check pannanum |
| 2. Details Excel-la type aagudhu | Assistant | Excel | 4 nimisham | Typing errors sumaar 3% |
| 3. Purchase order check panraanga | Assistant | Tally | 3 nimisham | 15%-ku PO number illa: purchase team-a phone panradhu, sumaar 20 nimisham |
| 4. Manager approve panraar | Manager | Email | Kaathirukkaradhu: sarasariyaa 2 naal | Periya delay |
| 5. Payment-ku details thirumba type aagudhu | Assistant | Tally | 3 nimisham | Double data entry |
| 6. Payment vaaram oru thadava schedule aagudhu | Assistant | Bank portal | 1 nimisham | Innum oru vaaram varaikkum kaathirukkalaam |

**Kandupidichadhu.** Exceptions-um paper invoices-um serthu, oru invoice-ku sarasariyaa sumaar 15 nimisham hands-on velai, adhu maasathukku sumaar 150 manineram. Data rendu thadava type aagudhu. Periya delay typing-la illa: approval-ku 2 naal kaathirukkaradhula.

**Decisions.**

| Waste | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Moonu arrival channels | Simplify: suppliers-a ore address-ku invoices-a email panna sollunga |
| Invoice details type panradhu | Automate: AI step fields-a padikkum, oru assistant check pannuvaanga |
| Tally-la thirumba type panradhu | Remove: check panna data-va oru thadava Tally-ku push pannunga |
| Approval-ku kaathirukkaradhu | Simplify: ₹1 lakh-ku keezha amounts-ku WhatsApp-la one-tap approval |
| PO numbers illaadhadhu | Exception-a remove pannunga: periya suppliers-kitta PO numbers kattaayam aakkunga |

Map owner-ku case-a solludhu, baseline numbers moonu maasathula gain-a prove pannum.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Manager-oda description-la irundhu map panradhu.** Velai paakuravanga kitta, oru real recent case-a vachu pesunga.
- **Exceptions-a ignore panradhu.** Time perumbaalum angae maraindhirukkum.
- **Numbers illaama irukkaradhu.** Volumes, times illaama priority podavum, improvement-a prove panna-vum mudiyaadhu.
- **Waste-a automate panradhu.** Automate panradhukku munnaadi remove, simplify pannunga.
- **Romba periya process-a map panradhu.** Clear start, end irukkura oru flow-a edunga.
- **Waiting time-a marakkiradhu.** Steps-ukku naduvula delays pala neram steps-a vida mukkiyam.

## Practice pannunga

Atlassian-oda process mapping guide-a padinga, keezha link irukku, apram GOV.UK-oda discovery phase guide-a padinga.

1. **Interview pannunga.** Neenga therinjukitta oru process-a edunga, coaching centre-la fee collection, leave approvals, stock reordering maadhiri. Adha pannura oru aal-a kadaisiya real case pathi interview pannunga.
2. **Map-a varaiyunga.** Ovvoru step-kum yaar, system, time, failure points-oda, volume, exceptions-ku rough numbers-oda varaiyunga.
3. **Decide pannunga.** Moonu periya waste sources-a mark panni, ovvonnukkum decide pannunga: remove, simplify, rules vachu automate, AI plus check vachu automate, illa vittudunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Numbers-oda unga map-a kaatti, edhai first maathuveenga, yen-nu explain panna mudiyum.
