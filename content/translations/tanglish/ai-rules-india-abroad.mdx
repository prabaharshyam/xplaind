## Idhu enna

AI-ku ethuvum rules illa-nu illa. Oru app AI-a vechu ungaloda loan-a decide pannum podhu, messages-a padikkum podhu, illa ungaloda mugatha generate pannum podhu, pala rules ippavae apply aagum. India-la irukkuravangalukku moonu romba mukkiyam:

| Rule | Adhu enna | Yaarai affect panradhu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** | Digital personal data pathi India-oda law, rules November 2025-la notify aagi, sumaar eighteen maadhathula padi padiyaa varum | India-la irukkuravangaloda digital personal data-va handle panra ethavadhu organisation, AI systems-layum |
| **India-oda AI Governance Guidelines** | Ministry of Electronics and IT-ya November 2025-la release panna safe, trusted AI-ku principles | Developers, deployers, regulators; perumbaalum voluntary, ippo irukkura laws-a nambi |
| **EU AI Act** | AI uses-a risk-padi pirikkura mudhal periya AI law | European Union-la AI use aagura companies, Indian firms-um serthu |

Matha laws-um AI-ku apply aagum: Information Technology Act, consumer protection, fraud, impersonation-ku criminal law, banking, insurance, health-ku sector rules. Oru bank-oda AI-a RBI, adhoda matha systems-a paakura maadhiriyae paakkum.

```flow
title: India-la oru AI app-a thodura rules, periyadhula irundhu sirusa
AI Governance Guidelines | mela principles -> Sector rules | banks-ku RBI, insurance-ku IRDAI
Sector rules -> General laws | IT Act, consumer, criminal
General laws -> DPDP Act | ungaloda personal data
EU AI Act | product Europe-la use aanaa
```

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### DPDP Act simple-aa

Ungaloda data eppadi use aagum-nu decide panra organisation-a DPDP Act **data fiduciary**-nu solludhu, neengala, andha aal-a, **data principal**-nu. Mukkiyamaana duties-um rights-um:

- **Notice, consent.** Enna personal data collect panraanga, yen-nu organisations theliva sollanum, perumbaalum ungaloda consent vaanganum.
- **Eppo venaalum withdraw.** Consent kuduthadhu maadhiriyae easy-aa withdraw pannalaam.
- **Paaka, correct, erase.** Ungaloda data summary-a kekkalaam, correct pannalaam, thevai illaama pona apram erase panna sollalaam.
- **Security, breaches.** Organisations ungaloda data-va protect pannanum, breach-a pathi ungalukkum Data Protection Board of India-vukkum solla vendum.
- **Children.** Kuzhandhaigalin data-ku parent-oda verifiable consent thevai, kuzhandhaigala track pannavo advertising-la target pannavo koodaadhu.
- **Penalties.** Periya penalties irukku, reasonable security safeguards edukkaadhadhukku ₹250 crore varai.
- **Complaints.** Mudhalla organisation-oda grievance officer-kitta, apram Data Protection Board-kitta complain pannunga.

Indha duties-um rights-um perumbaalum May 2027-la irundhu apply aagum, rules 13 November 2025-la notify aagi eighteen maadham kazhichu. Adhuvarai oru company-ku legally bind aagaama irukkalaam, aana kettaa perumbaalum work aagum, RBI maadhiri sector rules-padi regulated lenders-ku grievance officer irukkanum.

```flow
dir: LR
Neenga data tareenga -[consent]-> Organisation sonna padi mattum use pannum
Neenga consent-a withdraw panreenga -> Organisation niruthi erase pannum
Ungaloda data-va paakka kekkureenga -> Organisation summary kaattum
Neenga complain panreenga -> Grievance officer
Grievance officer -[theera illa-na?]-> Data Protection Board of India
```

### India-oda AI Governance Guidelines

Guidelines makkala mudhalla vachu, trust, fairness, accountability, transparency, safety-a promote pannudhu. AI policy-a coordinate panna pudhu institutions-um, transparency reports, makkal grievances solla vazhigal maadhiri industry-ku voluntary measures-um recommend pannudhu. Mukkiyamaa, idhu thani AI law-a **uruvaakkala**. Adhukku badhila, ippo irukkura laws-aiyum sector regulators-aiyum nambi, innovation-a ookkuvichu risk-a manage pannudhu.

### EU AI Act

EU AI Act AI uses-a risk levels-aa pirikkum:

| Risk level | Examples | Enna thevai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unacceptable | Unfair treatment-ku kondu pora social scoring, makkala harmful-aa manipulate panradhu | Banned |
| High | Hiring, credit scoring, education, essential services, sila medical uses-la AI | Risk management, nalla data, human oversight, records, transparency |
| Limited | Chatbots, AI generate panna content | AI kooda pesuraanga-nu makkalukku solradhu; sila generated content-a label panradhu |
| Minimal | Spam filters, games-la AI | Pudhu duties illa |

Europe-la use aagura AI products irukkura Indian companies idha follow pannanum, adhoda approach matha idangalla rules-aiyum customer expectations-aiyum shape pannudhu.

### Ungalukku idhu enna artham

- Oru app ungaloda data-va enna seiyum-nu sollradha, ottukkum munnaadi padinga.
- App-ku thevai illaadha permissions-a off pannunga.
- Ungaloda data-va paakka, correct panna, delete panna ungaloda rights-a use pannunga.
- Neraya situations-la AI kooda pesuringa-nu ungalukku solluvaanga-nu edhirpaarunga.
- Oru AI decision ungala paadhikkudhu-na, reject aana loan illa job application maadhiri, adhu eppadi edukkapattadhu-nu, appeal panradhu eppadi-nu kelunga.

## Oru worked example

Jaipur-la school teacher Deepa-ku, ammavoda surgery-kku ₹60,000 personal loan thevai. Oru lending app-a download panraanga.

**Permissions.** App avanga contacts, photos, SMS-ku access kekkudhu. Notice-a padikkuraanga: contacts "to improve services"-ku use aagum. Idhu vague, loan-ku thevai illa, adhanaala marukkuraanga. App appavum application-a process pannudhu; adhanaala andha permission thevaiyae illa-nu theriyudhu.

**Vegamaa reject.** Rendu nimishathula reason illaama reject aagiduraanga. Automated decision-nu sandhegam. Lender-oda grievance officer-ku, app-la irukkura mugavariyila ezhudhuraanga: "Please explain the main reasons for the decision on my application and arrange a review by a person." Oru vaarathula lender mukkiya reasons-oda badhil solludhu, pazhaya unpaid credit card dues entry, avanga adha paid-nu kaattina apram oru aal avanga file-a review pannuraanga. Loan approve aagudhu.

**Veliya varuradhu.** Oru maadham kazhichu thirumba kattitu app-a use panradha niruthuraanga. Consent-a withdraw panni, data-va erase panna kekkuraanga. Company email-la delete aachu-nu confirm panraanga, adha save pannikuraanga.

**Friend-ku help panradhu.** Avanga friend-oda data vera app-oda leak-la vandhappo, company enna disclose pannuchu-nu check panna, passwords maatha, company badhil solla-na Data Protection Board-ku eppadi complain pannalaam-nu note panna Deepa help panraanga.

| Situation | Deepa use panna right illa rule |
| --- | --- |
| Vague contacts permission | Notice, consent; thevai illaadhadha marukkradhu |
| Udane rejection | Grievance officer vazhiyaa reasons-um human review-um kekkuradhu |
| Use panradha niruthuradhu | Consent-a withdraw panni erasure kekkuradhu |
| Friend-oda leak | Breach notice duties; Data Protection Board-ku complaint |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Ella permissions-aiyum ottukkradhu.** Neraya apps thevaikku mela kekkum. Thevai illaadhadha marunga.
- **AI regulate aagala-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Data protection, consumer, criminal, sector laws ippavae apply aagudhu.
- **Rights-a use pannaama irukkradhu.** Ungaloda data-va paakka, correct panna, delete panna kekkalaam, complain pannalaam.
- **EU rules India-ku mukkiyam illa-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Europe-ku service panra Indian products idha follow pannanum, adhu ulaga practice-a shape pannudhu.
- **Automated rejections final-nu eduthukkradhu.** Reasons-um human review-um kelunga.
- **Guidelines-a law-nu nenaikkiradhu.** India-oda AI Governance Guidelines principles; kattaayamaana rules ippo irukkura laws-layum regulators-layum irundhu varudhu.

## Practice pannunga

Keezha link panni irukkura DPDP law pathi PRS Legislative Research summary-yum, EU AI Act-oda high-level summary-yum padinga.

1. **Rendu privacy notices padinga.** Neenga use panra rendu apps-a select pannunga. Ovvonnum enna data collect panradhu, yen, eppadi delete panradhu-nu ezhudhunga.
2. **Permissions-a sudham pannunga.** Phone-la oru app-oda permissions-a check panni, thevai illaadhadha off pannunga.
3. **Erasure request ezhudhunga.** Consent-a withdraw panni, ungaloda data-va erase panna sollura oru company-ku, neenga anuppura message-a moonu vaakkiyathula ezhudhunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Oru friend-ku avanga personal data mela irukkura moonu rights-a, enga complain pannalaam-nu, EU AI Act ban panra oru vishayatha explain panna mudiyum.
