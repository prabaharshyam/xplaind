## Idhu enna

Idhu Xplaind-kaaga ezhudhina oru worked engagement, indha path-la irukkura lessons-a use panradhu. Client oru fictional mid-sized non-banking lender, Chennai-la, Tamil Nadu-vum Karnataka-vum serthu 180 credit officers irukkaanga. Engagement sumaar aaru maadham nadakkudhu. Ungaloda capstone write-up-ku oru model-aa padinga.

| Vaarangal | Stage | Enna nadandhadhu |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Discovery, KPI | "Oru chatbot" credit-file assistant-aa maarichu; baseline 41 nimidam, 7% rework |
| 3-6 | Proof of concept | 300 files, oru golden set; image clean-up-ku apram 93% 96.4%-aa uyarndhadhu; gate pass |
| 6-8 | Engineering | Azure Central India, gateway, fallback, SSO, branch access, audit, CI-la evals |
| 7-16 | Pilot | 20 officers, weekly demos, risk log; 24 nimidam, 5.1% rework, 88% adoption |
| 17-26 | Production, handover | Memo approve aachu, 3 rollout waves, maasam ₹9 lakh, reverse shadowing |

Indha lesson, Chennai-la 180 credit officers irukkura oru fictional mid-sized lender-la muzhu engagement-a follow pannum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

Engagement indha path-oda stages-a follow pannudhu, ovvondrukkum adhoda evidence-oda.

**1. Mudhal call, discovery (vaaram 1-la irundhu 2).** Ketta vishayam "customers-ku oru chatbot". Interviews-um shadowing-um unmaiyaana valiya kaattinadhu: credit officers oru loan file-ku sumaar 41 nimidam bank statements, salary slips, bureau reports padichu, figures-a thirumba type panni, oru summary ezhudhinaanga, 7% files errors-kaaga credit committee-la irundhu thirumbi vandhadhu. Maasam 6,000 files. Play-back project-a reframe pannidhu, business owner-um, operations head-um, othukkittaanga.

**2. KPI, scope (vaaram 2).** Primary KPI: oru file-ku median officer time, baseline 41 nimidam, target 22. Guardrail: rework 7%-a thaandakoodaadhu. Adoption track pannom. Scope of work-la illaadhadhu: credit decisions, customer-facing features, English illaadha documents-nu peru sollappattadhu.

**3. Proof of concept (vaaram 3-la irundhu 6).** 300 masked historical files, rendu senior officers-oda label panna golden set. Structured-output model call-um Pydantic validation-um vechu extraction; ovvoru figure-kum oru citation. Mudhal result: critical fields-la 93%, 95% gate-ku keezha, perumbaalum nizhal vizhundha phone photos-naala. Oru image clean-up step-um oru low-confidence flag-um adha 96.4%-ku uyarthinadhu. Gate pass.

**4. Pilot-ku engineering (vaaram 6-la irundhu 8).** Lender-oda risk team Central India-la Azure-a approve pannirundhaanga, adhanaala models andha tenancy vazhiya, oru gateway-ku pinnaadi, oru fallback-oda use aachu. Containers-la FastAPI service, CI/CD, pipeline-la evals, masking-oda tracing, ovvoru view-kum edit-kum audit logs, Entra ID-oda sign-on, ovvoru officer-oda branch-a poruthu document access filter. Logs-la personal data mask aachu; erasure derived data-vukkum poachu.

**5. Pilot (vaaram 7-la irundhu 16).** Rendu branches-la 20 officers. Weekly demos, oru risk log. Oru branch-la blame bayathaala adoption nindruchu; written accountability-yum side-by-side review screen-um vechu fix pannom. Moonu change requests size panna: onnu swap-aa accept aachu, onnu phase 2-ku defer aachu, onnu decline aachu (credit decisions). Result: 24 nimidam median, 5.1% rework, 88% adoption.

**6. Production, handover (vaaram 17-la irundhu 26).** CEO-kkum CFO-kkum oru-page memo, full rollout approval-a vendru kudutthadhu. Branches-la moonu waves; caching-um tiering-um model costs-a all-in sumaar maasam ₹9 lakh-aa kuraichudhu. Runbooks, decision records, reverse shadowing-oda naalu vaara handover.

**Stage, decision, evidence:**

| Stage | Mukkiya decision | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Chatbot-la irundhu credit files-ku reframe | Interviews, shadowing, maasam 6,000 files |
| Scope | Credit decisions-um customer features-um exclude | Sign panna scope of work |
| POC | Image clean-up-um confidence flags-um serthom | Critical fields-la 93%-la irundhu 96.4% |
| Engineering | Approve panna Azure tenancy use pannom | Risk team-oda munnaadi approval |
| Pilot | Reviews-ku written accountability | Nindra branch-la adoption meendum vandhadhu |
| Change requests | Onnu swap, onnu defer, onnu decline | Size panna decisions log aachu |
| Production | Caching-um tiering-um | All-in sumaar maasam ₹9 lakh |
| Handover | Reverse shadowing | Client engineers ovvoru task-aiyum seidhaanga |

**Ungaloda sondha case study ezhudhuradhu.** Rendu moonu pages-la vaingunga. Problem-oda adhoda numbers-oda aarambinga. Table maadhiri, ovvoru stage-kum oru decision-um adhoda evidence-um kaattunga. Oru honest failure-um, enna maathinenga-nu-um serunga. Measured results-oda, oru lesson-oda mudinga. Reviewers-um interviewers-um neraya portfolios padippaanga; avanga nyaabagam vaikkiradhu real numbers, real trade-offs, nermaiyaana "naan vera enna pannirupen" irukkaradhu.

**Imagined client-a nambikkaiyaa aakkradhu.** Process-a neenga paaka kudiyadhu illa padikka kudiyadhu edhaavadhu oru vishayaththa adippadaiyaa vainga, example published hospital billing guides illa oru friend-oda velai. Ungaloda assumptions-um ovvoru number enga irundhu vandhadhu-num sollunga. POC-a real illa public data-la build pannunga, results-a ungaloda sondha golden set-la irundhu sollunga, guess-la illa.

## Oru worked example

Oru portfolio write-up-la illa interview-la FDE idha eppadi rendu nimidathukkulla summarise pannalaam:

"Chennai-la oru lender customer chatbot kettaanga. Discovery-la, real cost oru loan-ku 41 nimidam manual document work, maasam 6,000 loans, 7% rework-nu therinjudhu. Naan oru credit-file assistant-a propose panni scope pannen: citations-oda extraction-um oru draft summary-yum, ovvoru figure-aiyum officers review panna. POC mudhalla phone photos-naala accuracy gate-a miss pannidhu; image clean-up-um confidence flags-um adha fix pannidhu. Naan adha avanga Azure tenancy-la sign-on, branch-level access, audit logs, CI-la evals-oda deploy pannen. 10-vaara pilot-la, oru file-ku time 24 nimidama kuraindhadhu, rework 5.1%-aa kuraindhadhu, oru branch-la accountability concern-a fix panna apram adoption 88% aachu. 180 officers-ku rollout aachu, ippo client-oda team adha run panraanga. Naan vera enna pannirupen: security review-a vaaram 6-la illa, vaaram 1-la aarambichirupen."

Structure-a gavanichungga: numbers-oda problem, ungaloda decisions-um yen-nu-um, thappaa pona oru vishayam, adha eppadi handle pannenga, measured result, oru lesson.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

Indha engagement thavirththa thappugal, real engagements-a mulgadikkaravai:

- **Thevai-a vida kekkapatta-a build panradhu.** Discovery project-a maathichu.
- **Model metrics-a success-nu edukkiradhu.** Officer time-um rework-um thaan budget-a vendra measures.
- **Security-um sign-on-um pilot-ku apram vaikkiradhu.** Inga adhu pilot-la nadandhadhu, oru late review-a escalate pannanum-ngradha thavira.
- **Amaidhiyaa scope valaradhu.** Ovvoru request-um size panna decision-aa maarichu.
- **Handover-ku badhila oru handover document.** Client-oda engineers ovvoru task-aiyum thaangale seidhaanga.
