## Idhu enna

Executives-kitta neram kuraivu, priorities niraiya, panathukkum risk-kkum avanga thaan poruppu. Neenga anuppuradhoda mudhal lines-a padichuttu, mela padikkanumaa-nnu mudivu pannuvaanga. Avanga kitta nallaa communicate panradhu-nna anju pazhakkangal:

- **Answer first.** Conclusion allathu ungalukku vendiya decision mudhal sentence-la varanum. US Army-yoda writing guidance idhai thaan kekkudhu, idhai adikkadi BLUF-nu solluvaanga: bottom line up front.
- **Avanga mozhi.** Business results (time, cost, revenue, risk, customers), model names-o architectures-o illa.
- **Proof.** Othukkitta baseline-oda compare panna oru number, oru short demo, oru user-oda vaarthaigal.
- **Trade-offs, nermaiyaa.** Adhu evvalavu madhippullathu-nnu compare panna enna cost aagudhu, enna thappaa pogalaam, neenga enna recommend panreenga.
- **Oru thelivaana ask.** Avanga kitta irundhu enna venum, yaarkitta irundhu, eppo varaikkum.

```flow
title: Oru page memo

Headline | decision ore line-la
Headline -> Yen -> Evidence -> Risks -> Ask
```

Shape eppavume onnu thaan. Maarradhu content, andha content oru test-la thappikkanum: oru finance aal ovvoru number-aiyum check pannuvaanga.

Indha lesson, Chennai-la irukkura oru karpanai lender-oda CEO, CFO kooda nadakkura end-of-pilot meeting-a follow pannudhu. Indha path-la matha lessons-la varra adhe credit-file assistant thaan.

## Idhu eppadi velai seiyudhu

### Anju pagudhi structure

Endha update, email allathu memo-kkum ore pattern-a use pannunga:

1. *Headline*: mukkiyamaana point ore sentence-la, podhuvaa oru recommendation.
2. *Yen mukkiyam*: business effect, rupees, hours, customers allathu risk-la.
3. *Evidence*: rendu moonu facts, ovvonnum oru baseline-oda compare panni.
4. *Risks allathu trade-offs*: picture-a edhu maathalaam, adhukku neenga enna pannuveenga.
5. *Ask*: vendiya decision allathu help, adhukku owner yaaru, oru date.

Ovvoru calculation-aiyum appendix-la vainga, ovvonnukkum oru line, yaar venumnaalum oru nimishathula check pannalaam.

### Avanga numbers-a use pannunga, value-a cost kooda compare pannunga

Scope of work-la othukkitta KPI-aiyum baseline-aiyum use pannunga. Appuram client-kku sondhamaana oru figure vachu result-a panamaa maathunga, udhaaranamaa avanga HR team solra oru mani nerathoda loaded cost (salary-um benefits-um overheads-um). Neenga kandupidikkura figure-a challenge pannuvaanga; avanga finance team-oda figure-a panna maattaanga.

Appuram CFO panradhukku munnaadiye CFO-voda arithmetic-a neengale pannunga:

- **Ore unit, ore period.** Value varushathukku-nna, cost-um varushathukku thaan irukkanum. "Varushathukku ₹50 lakh value" pakkathula "otta maasam ₹9 lakh" vachaa, cost varushathukku ₹1.08 crore, value-a vida rettippukkum mela-ngradhu marainjudum.
- **Net value.** Varusha value kazhichu varusha running cost. Mukkiyamaana number idhu thaan.
- **Payback.** Inimel oru one-off cost irundhaa (training, rollout visits), adhai monthly net value-aala divide panni, adhu thirumba vara ethana maasam aagum-nu paarunga. Pilot-kku erkanave selavu panna panam eppadiyum poyaachu, adhanaala adhu rollout decision-la varakkoodaadhu.
- **Break-even.** Value-um cost-um samamaa aagura point, udhaaranamaa "ovvoru file-um kuranjadhu 13 nimisham michappaduthura varaikkum case nikkum". Executive edhai kavanikkanum-nu adhu solludhu.
- **Ovvoru benefit-aiyum oru thadavai mattum count pannunga.** Free aana officer hours-um "pudhu hiring illa"-ngradhum adikkadi ore benefit-a rendu thadavai solradhu thaan. Onnai edunga.
- **Use pannaa thaan hours count aagum.** Free aana nerathukku yaaraavadhu oru plan vachirundhaa thaan adhukku pana madhippu. Andha plan-aiyum adhoda owner-aiyum name pannunga.

India-kku oru gotcha: niraiya AI services-oda dollar prices-oda compare pannaa inga salaries kuraivu. Hours saved mela mattum build panna case, online-la neenga padikkura US case studies-a vida India-la perumbaalum romba melisaa irukkum. Adhai seekkirame check pannunga, quality, speed, growth-layum value thedunga.

### Technical results-a translate pannunga

| Technical statement | Executive version |
| --- | --- |
| "Critical fields-la 96.4% accuracy" | "Sumaar 28 figures-la onnukku correction venum; low-confidence figures flag aagum, decision-kku munnaadi officers ovvoru figure-aiyum adhoda source kooda check panraanga" |
| "p95 latency 2 seconds" | "Irubadhula pathombadhu badhilgal rendu seconds-kkulla vandhudum" |
| "Caching-oda oru chinna model-kku maarinom" | "Quality-la endha maatramum illaama running cost maasam ₹14 lakh-la irundhu ₹9 lakh-aa kurainjudhu" |
| "Recall at 5 of 0.91" | "Pathula onbadhu kelvigalukku, adhu padikkura anju documents-la sariyaana document irukku" |

Translate panna rendu rules. Mudhalla, friendly-aana number mattum illaama consequence-aiyum safeguard-aiyum serunga: oru thappukku enna cost, adhai edhu pidikkudhu-nnu executive-kku theriyanum. Rendaavadhu, oru metric-a round panni adhu kudukkaadha promise-aa maathaadheenga. "Pathula onbadhu thadavai sariyaana document-a kandupidikkudhu"-nnaa 90% sariyaana badhilgal maadhiri kekkum; recall at 5 solradhu sariyaana document anju candidates-la onnaa irundhuchu-nnu mattum thaan.

### Uncertainty pathi nermaiyaa irunga

Pilots podhuvaa romba aarvamaana branches-la, nallaa prepare aana users kooda nadakkum. Oru range kuduthu cautious pakkathukku plan pannunga: "Pilot oru file-kku 24 nimisham vandhuchu, aanaa pilot branches average-a vida mela irundhuchu, adhanaala full rollout-la 24-la irundhu 26 nimisham edhirpaarkkurom, 26-kku plan panrom." Appuram planning figure-layum case nikkum-nu kaattunga. Ore optimistic number, CFO ottai kandupidikka azhaikkum; break-even point irukkura range, neenga erkanave paarthutteenga-nnu kaattum.

### News moshamaa irukkumbodhu

Executives-kku eppavume successes pathi sollitte iruppaanga. Oru miss-a neenga eppadi solreenga-ngradhu thaan avangalukku nyaabagam irukkum. Moonu rules:

- **Mudhal line, kadaisi illa.** "Pilot adhoda target-a miss pannidichu" headline-la varanum. Adhai soften panna nalla parts-la aarambikkaadheenga.
- **Surprises vendaam.** Executive adhai steering meeting-kku munnaadi unga kitta irundhu kekkanum, meeting-la avanga staff kitta irundhu illa.
- **Options, appuram oru recommendation.** Rendu moonu choices, ovvonnukkum adhoda cost-um adhu enna tharum-num, neenga recommend panradhu edhu-nnum. Recommendation illaadha oru miss, unga problem-a room-laye busy-yaana aal kaiyila kudukkudhu.

Udhaaranamaa, Mumbai-la oru insurer, claim emails-a automatic-aa route panradhai pilot pannuchu-nnu vachukkonga, oru aal illaama 70% route aaganum-nu target:

> **Headline.** Pilot 52% claim emails-a automatic-aa route pannuchu, 70% target-a vida kuraivu. Niruthuradhukku badhilaa scope-a surukka recommend panrom.
>
> **Yen.** English emails, volume-la sumaar 55%, 78% vandhuchu; Hindi allathu Marathi-a English kooda kalandha emails 21% thaan vandhuchu.
>
> **Options.** (1) Ippove niruthuradhu: inimel cost illa. (2) English emails-kku mattum live poradhu: andha group-kku target-a vida mela, naalu vaarathula live. (3) Mixed-language emails-kku pilot-a ettu vaaram extend panradhu: cost adhigam, result nichayam illa.
>
> **Ask.** Indha vaarame option 2-a approve pannunga; adutha quarterly review-kku oru mixed-language plan kondu varuvom.

Numbers niruthu-nnu sollumbodhu stop-a recommend panradhum velaiyoda oru pagudhi thaan. Adhu thaan unga adutha recommendation-aiyum namba vaikkudhu.

### Executives-kkaana demos

Anju nimisham, oru kadhai: oru real case, before-um after-um, user-oda view, oru safeguard-a kaattura oru moment. Rehearse pannunga. Network fail aanaa oru recorded backup vachukkonga. Annaikku kaalaiyila try pannaadha oru demo case-a kaattave kaattaadheenga.

### Meeting-kku munnaadi

- **Oru pre-read anuppunga.** Memo-vai onnu rendu naal munnaadiye anuppunga, appo meeting padikkuradhula illaama decision-la pogum.
- **Avanga staff-a brief pannunga.** Meeting-kku munnaadi CFO-voda finance analyst-kku appendix-aiyum, CEO-voda chief of staff-kku ask-aiyum walk through pannunga. Avanga kelvigal gaps enga irukku-nnu kaattum, avanga team erkanave check panna numbers-a executive nambuvaanga.
- **Velippadaiyaana kelvigalukku prepare aagunga.** Full scale-la cost enna? Thappaa ponaa enna aagum? Enga data safe-aa? Enga aalunga enna ninaikkuraanga? Competitors kooda compare pannaa eppadi? Ovvonnukkum oru line badhil vachukkonga, kettaa detail.
- **60-second version-a prepare pannunga.** Decision, baseline-oda compare panna main result, net value, periya risk-um adhoda mitigation-um, idhai sathamaa, oru nimishathukkulla sollunga. Mudiyalai-nna memo innum clear-aa illa. Executives perumbaalum promise pannadhai vida kuraivaana neram thaan kuduppaanga.

### Kashtamaana kelvigalai handle panradhu

Ketta kelvikku oru sentence-la badhil sollunga, appuram detail offer pannunga: "Aamaa, data India-la irukkura enga Azure region-laye irukku. Useful-aa irundhaa architecture page-a kaatturen." Theriyalai-nna adhai sollittu, eppo badhil solluveenga-nnu oru neram kudunga. Oru CFO munnaadi guess panradhu, oru gap-a othukkuradhai vida adhigamaa trust-a izhakka vaikkum.

**Baseline-a challenge pannumbodhu.** Adhai eppadi measure panneenga, endha sample-la, endha period-la, scope of work-la yaaru adhai othukkittaanga-nnu sollunga. Appuram thirumba measure panna offer pannunga: "Pilot branches vazhakkathukku maaraanadhu-nnu ninaichaa, indha vaarame vera rendu branches-la 50 files-a time panni value-a thirumba solrom." Neenga thirumba check panna ready-aa irukkura baseline-a avanga ethukkuvaanga.

**Competitors pathi kettaa.** Oru published case study allathu annual report maadhiri public information-la irundhu source panna mudinjadhai vachu mattum badhil sollunga. Illai-nna kandupidichu solren-nu sollunga. Oru competitor-oda results-a nyaabagathula irundho kelvippattadhula irundho quote pannave koodaadhu; andha room-la oru thappaana claim, ella unmaiyaana claims-aiyum namba mudiyaadhadhaa aakkidum.

## Oru worked example

Pilot mudivula, lender-oda CEO-vum CFO-vum ungalukku 15 nimisham kudukkuraanga. Pilot facts: rendu branches-la 20 officers, 10 vaaram; oru loan file-kku median time 41-la irundhu 24 nimishamaa kurainjudhu; credit committee thiruppi anuppuna files 7%-la irundhu 5.1%-aa kurainjudhu. Lender 180 officers vachu maasathukku 6,000 files handle pannudhu, full scale-la system-a otta ellaam serthu maasam sumaar ₹9 lakh aagum. Lender-oda HR team oru officer hour-a loaded-aa sumaar ₹600-nu vaikkudhu. Idhu karpanai case-oda figures, method-a kaatta use panrom.

### Mudhal draft, adhu yen fail aagudhu

Value lines-oda mudhal draft sonnadhu: "Maasathukku sumaar 1,700 officer hours free aagudhu, varushathukku sumaar ₹50 lakh. Cost maasam sumaar ₹9 lakh." Mundhina naal brief panna finance analyst oru nimishathula rendu problems-a kandupidichaanga. Maasam ₹9 lakh-na varushathukku ₹1.08 crore, adhanaala memo thiruppi tharren-nu promise pannadhai vida rettippukkum mela kettuchu. Adhoda ₹50 lakh, lender-la yaarume othukkaadha oru hourly cost mela ninnuchu. Andha draft room-la fail aagiyirukkum.

### Revise panna memo

> **Recommendation: credit-file assistant-a 180 officers ellaarukkum moonu monthly waves-la rollout pannunga.**
>
> **Result.** 20 officers kooda 10 vaara pilot-la, oru loan file-kku median time 41-la irundhu 24 nimishamaa kurainjudhu, credit committee thiruppi anuppuna files 7%-la irundhu 5.1%-aa kurainjudhu. Officers ovvoru figure-aiyum adhoda source kooda check panraanga; credit decisions avanga kittaye irukku.
>
> **Value-um cost-um.** Oru file-kku 26 nimisham-ngra enga planning figure-la, michamaagura nerathoda madhippu maasam sumaar ₹9 lakh, idhu running cost-a cover pannudhu. Thiruppi anuppura files kuraiyuradhu maasam sumaar ₹1.4 lakh serkkudhu. Net value: planning figure-la varushathukku sumaar ₹16 lakh, full rollout pilot-a match pannaa sumaar ₹31 lakh. Free aagura nerathai operations head pudhu hiring-kku badhilaa adutha varusha applications growth-kku use pannuvaanga.
>
> **Risks.** Perumbaalum photo edutha documents irukkura branches-la gains kuraivaa irukkalaam; oru image clean-up step-aalayum branch visits-aalayum mitigate panrom. Ovvoru file-um kuranjadhu 13 nimisham michappaduthura varaikkum case nikkum. Idhai maasaa maasam report pannuvom, adhukku keezha ponaa adutha wave-a pause pannuvom.
>
> **Innaikke vendiya decision:** rollout budget-a approve panni operations-la oru owner-a name pannunga.

### Appendix

| Line | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Oru file-kku michamaagura neram | 41 kazhichu 26 = 15 nimisham (planning figure; pilot 17 kuduthuchu) |
| Free aagura officer hours | 6,000 files × 15 min ÷ 60 = maasam 1,500 hours |

| Net value | ₹10.4 lakh − ₹9 lakh = maasam sumaar ₹1.4 lakh, varusham ₹16 lakh (pilot-oda 17 nimishathula maasam ₹2.6 lakh) |
| Break-even | (₹9 lakh − ₹1.4 lakh) ÷ ₹600 = sumaar 1,270 hours, adhaavadhu oru file-kku sumaar 13 nimisham |

### Room-la

Demo oru real file-aiyum, officer-oda review screen-aiyum, flag aana oru low-confidence figure correct aagradhaiyum kaattudhu. Pilot 24 nimisham vandhappo plan yen 26 nimisham use pannudhu-nnu CFO kekkuraanga; pilot branches average-a vida mela irundhuchu-nnu neenga vilakkureenga. Break-even line-a monthly report-la serkka solraanga, andha condition-oda approve panraanga. Meeting pathu nimisham thaan aagudhu, yenna arguments ellaam mundhina naale avanga analyst kooda nadandhu mudinjudhu.

Edhu velai senjudhu-nnu kavaninga. Michamaagura neram running cost-a mattum thaan cover pannudhu, gain quality-layum growth-layum irundhu varudhu-nnu case nermaiyaa solludhu. Executives oru thelivaana gate irukkura, adakkamaana, nallaa check panna oru case-a approve pannaanga, oru stage-gate plan idhukkaaga thaan design pannappattadhu.

## Podhuvaana thappugal

- **Technology-la aarambikkuradhu.** Result-layum decision-layum aarambinga; architecture appendix-la, venumnaa mattum.
- **Translate pannaadha model metrics.** Adhai time, money, risk allathu customers-aa maathunga, safeguard-aiyum serunga.
- **Value-um cost-um vera vera units-la.** "Varushathukku" vs "maasathukku" oru nashtamaana case-a maraikkum. Eppavume net value-a kaattunga.
- **Oru benefit-a rendu thadavai count panradhu.** Free aana hours-um thavirtha hiring-um perumbaalum adhe rupees thaan.
- **Adhigamaa promise panradhu.** Oru range, cautious pakkam oru planning figure, oru break-even point, ore best-case number-a vida nalladhu.
- **Bad news-a pudhaikkuradhu.** Oru miss mudhal line-la varanum, options-um recommendation-um kooda.
- **Room-la surprises.** Mudhalla oru pre-read anuppi avanga staff-a brief pannunga.
- **Ask illa.** Oru owner, oru date-oda decision kekkalai-nna, adhu aridhaa thaan kidaikkum.

## Practice

Google-oda free technical writing course-la kuranjadhu mudhal unit-a edunga, adhu executive writing-kku neradiyaa apply aagum.

1. Anju pagudhi structure-a use panni unga use case-kku oru page recommendation memo ezhudhunga, ovvoru number-kkum oru line, oru net value line, oru break-even line irukkura appendix-oda.
2. Pilot adhoda target-a miss pannidichu-nnu vachu adhe memo-vai ezhudhunga: headline, cost-oda options, unga recommendation.
3. Anju nimisha demo kadhaikku script ezhudhunga: oru real case, before-um after-um, oru safeguard.
4. Oru CFO kekkura anju kelvigalai list pannunga, unga baseline-kku oru challenge udpada, ovvonnukkum oru line badhil ezhudhunga.

**Neenga mudichutteenga-nnaa** unga recommendation-a, adhoda main evidence-a, net value-a 60 seconds-kkulla, sathamaa, slides illaama solla mudiyum.
