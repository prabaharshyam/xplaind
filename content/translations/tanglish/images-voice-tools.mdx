## Idhu enna

Perumbaalaana makkal generative AI-a oru chat box-aa thaan paakkuraanga. Aana adhe technology ippo pictures, sound, video-kooda work pannudhu, adhula neraya innum dhinamum use panra apps-kulla irukku: unga phone camera, office suite, video-call app, chat assistant.

| Images | Speech | Meetings | Video, music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Create, edit, bill photo-va padikka | Transcribe, translate, padichu kaattu | Record, transcribe, summarise | Chinna clips, soundtracks (innum aarambam) |

| Vagai | Enna pannum | Dhinasari example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Image creation** | Description-la irundhu picture uruvaakkum | Chinna business-ku festival poster |
| **Image editing** | Background-a eduthudum, picture-a neettum, lighting-a sari pannum | Clean white background-la product photo |
| **Image reading** | Photo-la irukkura text-a describe illa padikkum | Maruthuvam strip, bill, timetable padikka |
| **Speech to text** | Audio-va ezhuththa maathum | Interview illa lecture-a transcribe panna |
| **Text to speech** | Text-a iyalbaana voice-la padichu kaattum | Hindi illa Tamil-la announcement |
| **Meeting assistants** | Calls-la serndhu, transcribe panni, actions-oda summarise pannum | Team call-la irundhu notes, action items |
| **Video and music** | Chinna clips, soundtracks generate pannum | Pathu vinadi product teaser |

Text-um images-um padikkura models-a **vision-language models**-nu solluvaanga. Kaiyezhuthu list-a photo edutthu, adha spreadsheet-aa maathu-nu assistant-kitta kekka idhu thaan kaaranam.

Indha lesson Anitha-va follow pannum. Avanga Tiruchirappalli-la oru home bakery nadathuraanga, Diwali campaign-ku indha tools-a use panraanga. Mudiyum podhu, ovvoru tool-um edhula nalla, edhula thappu, adha responsible-aa use panra rules enna-nu theriyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Images create panradhu

Image generators prompt-nu solra ezhuthu description-la irundhu work pannum. Nalla prompts specific-aa irukkum:

```text
  Weak:   "Diwali sweets poster"

  Strong: subject    a gift box of Indian sweets: kaju katli, ladoo
          setting    on a wooden table, diyas glowing behind
          style      warm, festive photo style
          colours    gold and maroon
          layout     square, empty space at the top for text
```

Image tools-ku innum picture-kulla irukkura **exact text**, **kaigal**, **specific real people** kashtam, sila requests-a maruppum. Vikkara illa advertise panra edhukkaagavum use pannina, commercial use pathi tool-oda rules-a check pannunga.

### Images padikkradhu

Kaiyezhuthu list, bill, maruthuvam strip, train timetable-a photo edutthu, assistant-a padikka illa explain panna sollunga. Neraya typing migum. Aana chinna, ilaicha, kaiyezhuthu text adikkadi thappaa padikkapadum, adhanaala mukkiyamaana edhaiyum original-oda check pannunga.

### Speech to text, translation

Transcription theliva English-ku romba nalla, Indian languages-kum, mixed speech-kum vegamaa munneerudhu. Innum ithula thappu varum:

- strong accents, vegamaa pesradhu;
- traffic, busy kadai maadhiri background noise;
- Hinglish, vera mixed-language speech;
- peyargal, idangal, technical words.

Interviews, lectures, voice notes-ku useful. Peyargal-ayum numbers-ayum eppavum check pannunga.

### Meeting assistants

Idhu video call-la serndhu, transcribe panni, action items-oda summary ezhudhum. Moonu rules:

1. Meeting record aagudhu-nu meeting-la **ellarukkum sollunga**.
2. Endha tools endha meetings-la serala-nu **unga organisation rules-a follow pannunga**.
3. Share panradhukku munnaadi **summary-a check pannunga**; adhu decisions-a miss pannalaam illa thappaa solalaam.

### Voice generation

Text-to-speech palla Indian languages-la iyalbaana announcements uruvaakkum, kadaigal, schools, public notices-ku super. Aana consent illaama **oru real person-oda voice-a clone panradhu** ethical illa, neraya cases-la illegal. Cloned voices thaan deepfakes lesson-la solra scams-oda mukkiya tool.

### Responsible-aa use panradhu

| Pannunga | Pannaadheenga |
| --- | --- |
| Mukkiyamaana text-a images-la neengalae serthukkonga | Image tool ezhudhuna text-a nambaadheenga |
| News, ads maadhiri mukkiyamaana idathula AI-made images-a label pannunga | Oru artist-oda style-a AI copy pannina adha unga original art-nu solladheenga |
| Makkalai record panradhukku munnaadi consent vaanggunga | Meetings-a ragasiyamaa record pannaadheenga |
| Images-la podhuvaana aatkala use pannunga | Real people avanga pannaadhadha pannura maadhiri images uruvaakkaadheenga |
| Private photos-a approve aagaadha tools-la podaadheenga | Matravanga private photos, recordings-a upload pannaadheenga |

## Oru worked example

Anitha Tiruchirappalli-la oru home bakery nadathuraanga. Instagram-layum WhatsApp-layum Diwali sweet boxes-a promote panna virumburaanga, adhukku avangalukku oru maalai thaan irukku.

**Poster.** Avanga describe panraanga: "A festive square poster with a gift box of Indian sweets, warm gold and maroon colours, diyas in the background, space at the top for text." Image tool naalu options tharudhu. Onnai edukkuraanga. Adhu serthadhu "Happy Diwalli"-nu thappaa ezhuthirukku, adhanaala adha eduthuttu, words, prices, phone number-a oru free design app-la avangalae serkkuraanga.

**Caption.** Box-la enna irukku, prices enna-nu Tamil-la oru voice note record pannuraanga, AI tool vachu transcribe panni, assistant-kitta English-layum Tamil-layum oru short caption-a maathu-nu kekkuraanga. Transcription thappaa ezhudhuna rendu Tamil words-a, oru sweet-oda peyar-um serthu, sari pannuraanga.

**Supplier bill.** Supplier-oda kaiyezhuthu invoice-a phone-la photo edutthu, items-a spreadsheet-la podu-nu assistant-kitta kekkuraanga. Ovvoru line-yum paper-oda check pannuraanga. Oru quantity 75-a illa 15-nu padichirukku.

**Maruththa oru suggestion.** Oru friend "innum nerya likes kidaikkum"-nu poster-la oru well-known actor-oda mugatha podalaam-nu solraanga. Anitha maruthuraanga: avangalukku permission illa, customers-a emaathum, tool-oda rules-um thadai pannudhu.

| Task | Tool | Edhu thappaachu | Avanga fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poster | Image generator | "Diwali"-a thappaa ezhudhinadhu | Text-a avangalae serthaanga |
| Caption | Speech to text, assistant | Rendu Tamil words, oru sweet peyar | Kaiyaala edit pannaanga |
| Supplier list | Image reading | 75-a 15-nu padichadhu | Ovvoru line-yum check pannaanga |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **AI images-kulla irukkura text-a nambradhu.** Mukkiyamaana words-a neengalae serthukkonga.
- **Makkalukku sollaama meetings-a record panradhu.** Eppavum arivichittu, rules-a follow pannunga.
- **Real people-oda mugam, voice-a consent illaama use panradhu.** Vilaiyaattukku-nu kooda, avangalukku kedu, law-a meeralaam.
- **Transcripts exact-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Peyargal, numbers, Indian-language words-a check pannunga.
- Vikkara illa advertise panra images-ku **commercial-use rules-a ignore panradhu.**
- Matravanga private photos-a approve aagaadha tools-la **upload panradhu.**
