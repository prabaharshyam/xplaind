## Idhu enna

Niraiya perukku generative AI oru chat box-aa thaan arimugam. Adhae technology ippo pictures, sound, video koodavum velai seiyudhu, adhula niraiya aerkanavae neenga use panra apps-kkulla irukku: unga phone camera, keyboard, office suite, video-call app, chat assistant.

| Vagai | Enna seiyum | Andraada example | Innum enga fail aagudhu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Image creation** | Oru description-la irundhu picture uruvaakkum | Oru chinna business-kku festival poster | Exact vaarthaigal, prices, nunukkamaana details; Indian mozhi text-a check pannunga |
| **Image editing** | Background-a edukkum, light-a maathum, picture-a neettum | Clean background-la oru product photo | Neenga appadiyae vaikka sonnadha maathidum |
| **Image reading** | Oru photo-va describe pannum allathu adhula irukkura text-a padikkum | Oru bill, marundhu strip, timetable | Kaiyezhuthu, mangalaana print, 1, 7 maadhiri orae maadhiri irukkura digits |
| **Speech to text** | Audio-va ezhuthaa maathum | Oru voice note allathu lecture-a transcribe panradhu | Peyargal, numbers, noise, kalandha mozhigal |
| **Text to speech** | Text-a iyalbaana kuralla sathamaa padikkum | Hindi allathu Tamil-la oru kadai announcement | Peyargal, sila vaarthaigalla vithiyaasamaana azhutham |
| **Meeting assistants** | Oru call-a transcribe panni action items-oda summarise pannum | Oru team call-la irundhu notes | Support illaadha mozhigal, ittukkattuna allathu vittuppona decisions |
| **Video and music** | Chinna clips pannum, perumbaalum sound-oda | Sila vinaadi product teaser | Neelam, clips muzhukka orae mugam allathu product-a vaikkiradhu, cost |

Text-oda serthu images-aiyum padikkira models-kku **vision-language models**-nu peru. Kaiyaala ezhudhuna oru list-a photo eduthu, adha spreadsheet-aa maatha oru assistant-kitta solla mudiyuradhukku idhu thaan kaaranam.

Indha lesson muzhukka nyaabagam vachukka vendiya orae idea: **Indha tools ovvonnum mudinja maadhiri theriyura onna thaan unga kaiyila kudukkudhu.** Poster print panna maadhiri theriyum, transcript type panna maadhiri theriyum, summary minutes maadhiri theriyum. Ovvoru tool-um enga fail aagum-nu therinju, sariyaa anga check panradhu thaan skill. Appuram edhuvum unga phone-a vittu poradhukku munnaadi rights, privacy, labels-a handle pannanum.

```flow

dir: LR

Risky parts-a check pannunga -> Rights-a check pannunga | real aalunga, brands, kuralgal, consent
Rights-a check pannunga -> Privacy-a check pannunga | ID numbers, phone numbers, mathavanga
Privacy-a check pannunga -> Label panni share pannunga | platform kettaa AI content-a declare pannunga
```

Indha lesson, Tiruchirappalli-la oru home bakery nadathura Anitha-va follow pannudhu; avanga oru Diwali campaign-kku indha tools-a use panraanga.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Indha tools yen andha idangalla fail aagudhu

Image, speech models periya alavula examples-la irundhu kathukkudhu. Edhaavadhu uruvaakkumbodhu, adhu paarthadha vachu romba likely-aana onna tharudhu. Adhanaala thaan motha picture-um sentence-um podhuvaa nallaa irukku: saadhaaranamaana vishayangalai predict panradhu easy.

Aridhaana, exact details vera maadhiri. Oru image model ezhuthugalai shapes-aa varaiyudhu, adhanaala adhu kuraivaa paartha oru vaarthaiyila oru ezhuthu vittuppogalaam allathu koodudhalaa varalaam; Hindi, Tamil maadhiri serndha ezhuthugalum uyirkkurigalum irukkura script-la thappu panna innum niraiya vazhi irukku. Oru speech model oru sathathukku romba likely-aana vaarthaiya edukkudhu, adhanaala "fifteen", "fifty", allathu adhu kettae irukkaadha oru kudumba-p peyar easy-aa maaridum. Oru meeting summary oru decision-oda romba likely-aana shape-a edukkudhu, adhanaala yaarumae edukkaadha oru decision-a kooda adhu sollalaam.

Idhula irundhu varra practical rule: **Draft-kku AI-a use pannunga, exact details-a ovvoru thadavaiyum neengalae check pannunga**: images-la irukkura vaarthaigal, peyargal, numbers, prices, dates, yaar edhukku othukkittaanga.

### Images uruvaakkuradhu, edit panradhu

Neenga picture-a oru prompt-la describe panreenga: subject, setting, style, colours, adhu endha shape-kku, udhaaranamaa Instagram-kku square. Text varra idathula kaali idam vidunga. "AI image generation and editing with Gemini (Nano Banana) and Midjourney" lesson prompting-a aazhamaa solli tharudhu.

**Images-la text.** Mundhaiya models-a vida pudhu models chinna English text-a romba nallaa ezhudhudhu. Udhaaranamaa, thannoda Nano Banana Pro model pala mozhigalla padikka koodiya text-a render pannudhu-nnu Google solludhu, aanaa adhoda sondha limits list-layae chinna text-um exact spelling-um innum irukku. Hindi, Tamil allathu vera Indian script text-a ezhuthu ezhuthaa test pannunga, yenna oru thappaana uyirkkuriya miss panradhu easy. Default rule: **Picture-a generate pannunga, vaarthaigalai type pannunga.** Headlines, prices, dates, phone numbers-a Canva maadhiri oru design app-la podunga, Hindi, Tamil allathu vera Indian mozhi text-a nallaa padikka therinja orutharai check panna sollunga.

**Editing.** Oru real photo-va edit panna oru tool-kitta solradhu ("light-a koottu, plain white background, product-a maathaadhae") pudhu image generate panradha vida pala neram nalladhu. Neenga vikkira edhukkum real product-a kaattunga. Unga cake maadhiri illaadha oru cake-oda generate panna "photo" customers-a aemaathum.

### Documents photos-a padikkiradhu

Oru bill, kaiyaala ezhudhuna list allathu timetable-a photo eduthu, adha padikka allathu oru table-aa maatha oru assistant-kitta sollunga. Idhu niraiya typing-a michappaduthum. Chinna, mangalaana allathu kaiyaala ezhudhuna text adikkadi thappaa padikkappadum, 1, 7 allathu 5, 6 maadhiri digits easy-aa kuzhambum.

Idha safe-aa aakkura rendu pazhakkangal:

- **Kanakku pottu check pannunga.** Ovvoru line-layum quantity-a rate-aala multiply panni amounts-a kootta assistant-kitta sollunga. Match aagaadha oru line, allathu paper-la irundhu vithiyaasamaana oru total, neraa thappaa padicha idatha kaattum.
- **Upload panradhukku munnaadi crop pannunga.** Neenga share panna ninaichadha vida oru photo-vula niraiya irukkalaam: oru Aadhaar number, oru bank account, vera orutharoda phone number, oru medical detail. Andha parts-a mudhalla crop pannunga allathu maraiinga. Oru form Aadhaar kettaa, UIDAI-la irundhu oru masked Aadhaar use pannunga, adhu kadaisi naalu digits mattum thaan kaattum. Work-la, documents-kku unga organisation approve panna tools-a mattum use pannunga.

### Speech to text, translation

Thelivaana English-kku transcription romba nallaa irukku, Indian mozhigalukku vegamaa improve aagudhu. Innum idhula thappu pannum:

- strong accents, vegamaa pesuradhu;
- traffic, busy-aana kadai maadhiri background noise;
- Hinglish, matha kalandha mozhi speech;
- peyargal, idangal, numbers, technical vaarthaigal.

Aarambikkiradhukku munnaadiyae thappugalai kuraikkalaam: amaidhiyaana room-la phone-kku pakkathula record pannunga, tool-la sariyaana mozhiya select pannunga, mukkiyamaana numbers-a medhuvaa sollunga (udhaaranamaa, "four hundred and fifty, four five zero"), aboorvamaana peyargalai spell pannunga. Transcribe pannadhukku appuram, ovvoru peyarukkum number-kkum transcript-a audio-voda oppittu padinga.

**Translation**-kku adhoda sondha trap irukku: grammar-padi sariyaa irundhaalum local readers-kku viraippaa allathu thappaa kekkalaam. Oru quick check: adha original mozhikkae thirumba translate panni compare pannunga. Oru notice allathu advert maadhiri public-aana edhukkum, andha mozhiya thaaimozhiyaa pesura orutharai padikka sollunga.

### Voice generation

Text-to-speech pala Indian mozhigalla iyalbaana announcements-a uruvaakkum, idhu kadaigal, schools, public notices-kku udhavum. Tool-oda stock voices-a, allathu unga sondha kurala use pannunga. **Oru real aaloda kurala consent illaama clone panradhu** vera vishayam. 2024-la Bombay High Court, Arijit Singh case-la oru interim order-la, andha singer-oda kurala copy panra tools-a kudukkak koodaadhu-nnu peyar sollappatta pala AI platforms-a thaduthuchu; avaroda kurala avaroda personality rights-oda pagudhiyaa paarthuchu. "Deepfakes and misinformation" lesson-la paarkkura scams-oda mukkiyamaana karuviyum cloned voices thaan.

### Meeting assistants

Ivai oru video call-la serndhu, adha transcribe panni, action items-oda oru summary ezhudhum. Onnu serradhukku munnaadi:

1. **Ellaarkittayum solli oppudhal vaangunga**, call record aagudhu, summarise aagudhu-nnu.
2. Endha tools endha meetings-la seralaam-ngradhula **unga organisation-oda rules-aiyum** client-oda rules-aiyum follow pannunga, recording enga store aagudhu-nnu therinjukkonga.
3. **Mozhiya check pannunga.** Niraiya meeting note-takers oru chinna list mozhigalai mattum thaan support pannudhu, oru meeting-kku oru mozhiya thaan edhirpaarkkudhu. Oru Hindi allathu Hinglish call-kku munnaadi tool support panra mozhigalai check pannunga.
4. **Share panradhukku munnaadi summary-a check pannunga.** Decisions, numbers, dates, owners-a pesunadhoda compare pannunga; confident-aana oru summary oru condition-a miss pannalaam allathu oru agreement-a ittukkattalaam.

### Video, music

Sound-oda chinna video clips ippo mainstream assistants-la kidaikkudhu. Udhaaranamaa, Google-oda Gemini app thannoda Veo models-oda chinna clips pannudhu, perumbaalum paid plans-la, oru naalaikku chinna limit-oda. Sila vinaadi neela clips, clips muzhukka orae mugam allathu product-a vaikkiradhula sirammam, oru usable shot-kku niraiya attempts edhirpaarunga. Background music generators images maadhiriyae rights kelvigalai kilappudhu. "Create cinematic AI videos with Google Veo and Flow" lesson video-va muzhusaa cover pannudhu.

### Rights, labels, ownership

**Labels.** 10 February 2026-la notify pannappattu 20 February 2026-la irundhu amalla irukkura India-voda IT Rules amendment, "synthetically generated information"-a cover pannudhu: real maadhiri theriyura, AI uruvaakkuna allathu AI maathuna images, audio, video. Platforms andha maadhiri content-a label pannanum, periya social media platforms upload pannumbodhu users-kitta adha declare panna sollanum. Oru platform kettaa, nermaiyaa declare pannunga. Rules maarikkittae irukku, adhanaala oru periya campaign-kku munnaadi latest-a check pannunga.

**Ownership.** Muzhukka AI uruvaakkuna oru image yaarukku sondham-nu India-la mudivaagala. Computer-generated works pathina Copyright Act clause 1994-la vandhadhu, innaikku tools varradhukku romba munnaadi. DPIIT amaicha oru committee December 2025-la generative AI, copyright pathina oru working paper-oda mudhal pagudhiya veliyittuchu; adhu AI training-a cover pannudhu, AI outputs-oda ownership-a adutha pagudhikku vittirukku. Idhu mudivaagura varai, muzhukka AI uruvaakkuna image-a unga logo-vaa allathu trademark-aa use pannaadheenga: mathavanga copy panradha ungalaala thadukka mudiyaama pogalaam. Ideas-kku AI-a use pannunga, appuram oru designer final logo-va uruvaakkanum allathu niraiyavae rework pannanum; register panna plan panra edhukkum legal advice vaangunga.

**Commercial use.** Adhu uruvaakkuradha vachu vikkiradhu allathu advertise panradhu pathi ovvoru tool-kkum adhoda sondha terms irukku. Oru advert-la oru image-a use panradhukku munnaadi adha padinga.

| Pannunga | Pannaadheenga |
| --- | --- |
| Mukkiyamaana text-a images-la neengalae type pannunga | Image tool ezhudhuna text-a ovvoru ezhuthaiyum check pannaama publish pannaadheenga |
| Oru platform kettaa AI content-a declare pannunga | Uyiroda irukkura oru artist-oda style-a AI copy pannadha unga sondha art-aa kaattaadheenga |
| Makkalai record panradhukku munnaadi oppudhal vaangunga | Calls-a ragasiyamaa record pannaadheenga |
| Stock voices allathu unga sondha kurala use pannunga | Oru real aaloda kural allathu mugatha consent illaama clone pannaadheenga |
| Photos-la irundhu ID numbers-aiyum mathavanga details-aiyum crop pannunga | Mathavangaloda private photos allathu recordings-a upload pannaadheenga |

## Oru worked example

Anitha, Tiruchirappalli-la oru home bakery nadathuraanga. Instagram-layum WhatsApp-layum Diwali sweet boxes-a promote pannanum, avangalukku orae oru maalai thaan irukku.

**Poster.** Avanga thannoda real sweet box-a photo eduthu, adha edit panna image tool-kitta solraanga: "square poster, box-a maathaadhae, adha oru mara table mela vai, pinnaadi diyas eriyattum, gold, maroon colours, mela oru Tamil Diwali vaazhthu". Tool-a test panna thaan vaazhtha kekkuraanga, appuram naalu results-la onna edukkuraanga. Vaazhthula oru thappaana uyirkkuri irukku, adhanaala adha eduthuttu, vaazhthu, price, than phone number-a Canva-la type panraanga. Tamil nallaa padikka therinja avanga thangachi final poster-a check panraanga. Post-la AI-made content irukkaa-nnu Instagram kekkumbodhu, aamaam-nu solraanga.

**Caption.** Box-a describe panni Tamil-la oru voice note record panraanga, niraiya per panra maadhiri price-a English-la solraanga, adha oru AI tool-la transcribe panni, Tamil-layum English-layum oru chinna caption-kku assistant-kitta kekkuraanga. Transcript, adhirasam sweet-a vera oru vaarthaiyaa maathiduchu, "four hundred and fifty rupees"-a ₹415-aa maathiduchu. Transcript-a than price list-oda oppittu padichu rendaiyum pidikkiraanga.

**Supplier bill.** Supplier-oda personal mobile number-a maraichadhukku appuram, avaroda kaiyaala ezhudhuna invoice-a photo eduthu, oru table panna assistant-kitta solraanga. Paper bill-la irukkura figures ivai, udhaaranamaa:

| Item | Quantity | Rate (₹) | Amount (₹) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cashews | 5 kg | 950 | 4,750 |
| Sugar | 75 kg | 44 | 3,300 |
| Ghee | 10 kg | 620 | 6,200 |
| **Total** | | | **14,250** |

Assistant sugar quantity-a 15 kg-aa padichudhu, aanaa amount-a ₹3,300-aavae vachudhu. Ovvoru line-layum quantity-a rate-aala multiply panna solraanga: 15 × 44 = 660, idhu 3,300-oda match aagala, adhanaala paper-a check panni 75 kg-aa thiruthuraanga. Ippo 75 × 44 = 3,300, moonu amounts-um bill-oda total ₹14,250-kku sariyaa varudhu.

**Avanga marutha oru suggestion.** "Adhiga likes kidaikkum"-nu oru friend poster-la oru prabala actor-oda mugatha poda solraanga. Anitha vendaam-nu solraanga: avangakitta permission illa, customers-a aemaathum, actor-oda personality rights-a meeralaam, tool-oda rules-um thadai pannudhu.

| Task | Enna thappaa pochu | Avanga eppadi pidichaanga |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poster | AI text-la thappaana Tamil uyirkkuri | Text-a thaanae type pannaanga; nallaa padikka therinjavanga check pannaanga |
| Caption | Oru sweet peyarum price-um thappaa kettuchu | Transcript-a price list-oda oppittu padichaanga |
| Supplier bill | 75 kg-a 15 kg-aa padichudhu | Quantity × rate, amount-oda match aagala |
| Actor-oda mugam | Suggest pannaanga, pannala | Panradhukku munnaadi consent, rights check |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Mudinja maadhiri theriyura draft-a mudinjadhaa ninaikkiradhu.** Neettaana poster, transcript allathu summary-layum thappaana price allathu peyar irukkalaam. Exact details-a ovvoru thadavaiyum check pannunga.
- **Oru spelling-a sari panna innum irupadhu images kekkuradhu.** Thirumba generate panradhu text-a nambagamaa aridhaa thaan sari pannum. Neengalae type pannunga.
