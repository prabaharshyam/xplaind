## Idhu enna

"Human in the loop-la vainga" AI product design-la romba common-aana advice, adhey romba thadavai mosamaa seiyappadura onnum. Oru manushan information illaama, oru manikku 500 AI decisions-la "approve" click pannanum-na, adhu oversight illa. Adhu oru peyar potta rubber stamp.

**Human in the loop**-na makkal oru AI feature work aaguradhula oru pagudhi, adhoda output-a use panravanga mattum illa. Naalu common roles irukku:

| Role | Aal enna pannuvaanga | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Review** | Use aagradhukku munnaadi AI output-a check pannuvaanga | Support agent AI draft panna reply-a anuppuradhukku munnaadi padippaanga |
| **Approve** | AI propose panna action-a authorise pannuvaanga | Manager AI suggest panna refund-a approve pannuvaanga |
| **Handle exceptions** | Kashtamaana illa uruthi illaadha cases-a edhuppaanga | AI routine claims-a handle pannum; adjusters vinodhamaanadha edhuppaanga |
| **Audit** | Nadandhadhukku appuram oru sample-a check pannuvaanga | Quality lead vaaram 5% automated decisions-a review pannuvaanga |

Unmaiyaana product kelvi "human irukkanuma?" illa, **engae, edhu cases-ku, enna information-oda, evvalavu vegamaa**.

Indha lesson-la Bengaluru-la oru lending app-la product manager Arjun-a follow pannuvom, avanga AI-assisted loan review-la credit officers engae irukkanum-nu design pannuraanga. Mudiyum podhu, vegamaana, arthamulla human roles-a design pannalaam, evidence valara valara avai eppadi maarum-nu plan pannalaam.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Risk-um confidence-um vachu mudivu pannunga

Unga case types-a rendu kelvila map pannunga: oru thappu evvalavu selavaanadhu, AI sariyaa irukka evvalavu vaaippu?

| Thappoda selavu | AI confidence kammi | AI confidence adhigam |
|---|---|---|
| Adhigam | Expert **approve** pannuvaanga, illa AI-a use pannaadheenga | Ovvoru case-um **approve** illa **review** |
| Kammi | **Review**, illa **exception**-aa route pannunga | **Automate** pannunga, oru sample-a **audit** pannunga |

Vinodhamaana cases, unga evaluation set-la edhukkum othu varaadhavai, edhaiyum paarkkaama experts kitta pogum.

### Review nimishatha design pannunga

Nalla review screen check panradhai vegamaa, unmaiyaa aakkum:

1. **Output-oda sources pakkathula**, adhanaala reviewer vera systems-a open pannanum-nu avasiyam illa.
2. **Check pannanum-nu irukkuradhu highlight aagum**: amounts, names, dates, AI-ku uruthi illaadha fields.
3. Approve, edit, reject-ku **ovvonnukkum oru action**.

Mudivu panna reviewers vera systems-la thedi alaiyanum-na, oru vaarathula check panradhai niruthiduvaanga.

### Automation bias-a kavanamaa paarunga

Makkal machine suggestions-a accept pannura pokku irukku, especially busy-aa, sorvaa irukkumbodhu. Idhuku **automation bias**-nu per. Edhirkka:

- Ovvoru answer-aiyum ore confidence-oda kaattaama, uncertainty-a theliva kaattunga.
- Mukkiya fields-ku, oru "approve all" button-ku badhila, reviewer active-aa confirm pannanum-nu vainga, example income figure-a tap panradhu.
- **Override rates**-a measure pannunga. Zero-kku kitta irukkura override rate, AI perfect-nu artham aagalam, illa yaarum unmaiyaa check pannala-nu artham aagalam.
- Appappo, kavanatha check panna, thelivaa oru thappu irukkura theriyaadha test cases-a serthu paarunga.

### Capacity-a plan pannunga

Reviews-ku neram aagum. Estimate pannunga:

```text
  daily volume needing review  x  minutes per review  =  minutes a day
        600 applications        x        3 min        =  1,800 min = 30 hours
```

Team-kitta andha hours irukkaa-nu paarunga. Illannaa queues kuvidhum, makkal avasarappaduvaanga, review quality silent-aa vizhundhudum.

### Accountability-a theliva paarunga

Approve panna AI output thappaa pochchunna yaaru poruppu? Perumbaalum approver, avanga kitta thevaiyaana information-um neramum irundhaa mattum adhu nyaayam. Ezhudhi vainga.

### Loop-a improve panna use pannunga

Ovvoru edit-um rejection-um, AI engae fail aagudhu-nu ungalukku irukkura best data. Avatha evaluation set-kum fixes-kum feed pannunga. System-a improve pannaadha loop oru extra velai thaan.

### Evidence-oda oversight-a maathunga

| Stage | Kammi risk cases | Adhiga risk cases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | Ovvonnayum approve illa review | Ovvonnayum approve |
| Quality, sollunga, moonu maadham nikkum pinnadi | Sample-a audit-ku nagarthunga | Innum ovvonnayum approve |
| Thodarndhu | Audit, quality kuraindhaa alerts-oda | Approve; orupodhum muzhusaa automate pannaadheenga |

Strict-aa aarambinga. Measure panna evidence irundhaa mattum relax pannunga, ovvoru maatraththaiyum document pannunga.

### User thaan human-aa irukkum podhu

Consumer products-la, user perumbaalum reviewer: avanga edit panra email draft, accept panra suggestion. Accept panradhu mattum easy option-aa irukkaama, edit panradhum athe alavu vegamaa irukkura maadhiri design pannunga.

## Oru worked example

Bengaluru-la oru lending app AI-a loan application reviews-a pre-fill panna use pannudhu: bank statements-la irundhu income-a edukkudhu, risks-a flag pannudhu, approve, reject, illa refer-nu suggest pannudhu. Product manager Arun human role-a design pannuraanga.

**Decisions makkal kitta thaan irukkum.** Credit officers ovvoru application-aiyum approve illa reject pannuvaanga; AI suggest mattum pannum. Lender-oda credit policy, ovvoru lending decision-kkum fairness-ukkum, regulator-kkum, oru peyar potta aal-a accountable-aa vechirukkum.

**Review screen** kaattum:

- suggest panna decision-um moonu mukkiya reasons-um;
- extract panna monthly income, adhu vandha bank statement lines highlight aagi;
- risk flags sivappula, example recent bounce aana EMI.

Decision buttons unlock aagradhukku munnaadi officers income figure-a tap panni confirm pannanum. Andha oru sinna step "paarkkaama approve"-a thadukkudhu.

**Overrides track aagudhu**, officer-um reason-um padi. Mudhal maadham, officers 14% AI suggestions-a override pannuraanga. Romba common reason, "salary credited in two parts", extraction step-la split salary credits illai-nu kaattudhu. Engineers fix pannuraanga.

**Capacity:** oru naalaikku 600 applications, ovvonnukkum 3 nimishama-na 30 officer-hours, team-oda capacity-aana 40-kkulla.

**Audit:** oru senior credit manager vaaram approve aana files-la 5%-a review pannuraanga.

| Measure | AI-ku munnaadi | Moonu maadham pinnaadi |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oru application-ku review time | 9 nimishangal | 3 nimishangal |
| Override rate | Bodhum illa | Maadham 1-la 14%, maadham 3-la 6% |
| Approvals-la default rate | Baseline | Manual process-oda same |
| Yaar mudivu pannuvaanga | Officer | Officer |

Review moonu madangu vegamaachu, quality nikkudhu, decisions adha poruppu edukkura makkal kittayae irukku.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Peyar alavukku mattum human.** Information-um neramum illannaa review oru rubber stamp.
- **Ellaathaiyum eppavum review panradhu.** Evidence valara valara kammi risk cases-a audit-ku nagarthunga.
- **Capacity-a ignore panradhu.** Review queues valarum, quality kuraiyum.
- **Theliva illaadha accountability.** Approve aana outputs-ku yaaru badhil solluvaanga-nu ezhudhunga.
- **Overrides-a vaste panradhu.** Improvement-ku adhu thaan best signal.
- **Zero override rate-a nalla seithi-nu edukkradhu.** Yaarum check pannala-nu artham aagalam.

## Practice pannunga

Keezha link panni irukkura PAIR Guidebook-la feedback, control chapters-a padinga, apram Human-AI Interaction guidelines-a.

1. **Cases-a map pannunga.** Neenga therndhedutha oru AI feature-ku, case types-a thappoda selavu, AI confidence vachu map panni, ovvonnayum automate, review, approve, exception, audit-la onnula assign pannunga.
2. **Screen-a sketch pannunga.** Sources, highlighted fields, one-click actions-oda review screen-a varaiyunga.
3. **Capacity-yum maatramum plan pannunga.** Review capacity-a estimate panni, eppo oversight-a relax pannalaam-nu rule ezhudhunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Unga feature-la makkal engae irukkaanga, enna paakkuraanga, evvalavu neram aagudhu, evidence-oda adhu eppadi maarum-nu kaattha mudiyum.
