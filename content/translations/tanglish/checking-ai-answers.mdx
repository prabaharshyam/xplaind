## Idhu enna

AI badhila check panradhu-nna, **neenga nambappora pagudhigala, act panradhukku munnaadi confirm panradhu**. Ellaatthaiyum samamaa sandhegappaduradhu illa. Oru birthday message-ku oru quick read podhum. Diabetes irukkura oru parent-ku oru medicine, oru legal deadline, allathu oru customer-oda refund amount-ku proof venum.

Manasukkullaye ottakkoodiya aindhu-step routine idho:

```flow
1. Stakes | idhai yaar nambi irukkaanga, thappaa irundhaa enna aagum? -> 2. Claims | neenga nambi seyalpadappora names, numbers, dates, rules, quotes
2. Claims -> 3. Verify | oru current, reliable source; numbers-a thirumba kanakku podunga
3. Verify -> 4. Reasoning | neutral-aa kelunga; marupadi kettaalum nikkudhaa?
4. Reasoning -> 5. Escalate | stakes adhigamaa, allathu sources muranpadudhaa? Oru aalkitta kelunga
```

Indha routine-oda nokkam vegam. Perumbaalaana badhilgalukku step 1, 2 mattum podhum. Mukkiyamaana idatthula mattum thaan kadumaiyaa check pannuveenga.

Indha lesson endha oru thani badhilukkum porundhura podhuvaana routine. "AI for research and analysis" idhai adippadaiyaa vachu, oru claim-a niraiya sources vazhiyaa trace panradhu maadhiri neelamaana research velaigalukku kondu pogudhu. Inga Coimbatore-la diabetes irukkura than appaava paathukkura Suresh-a follow pannuvom; avar assistant-kitta rendu romba vitthiyaasamaana kelvigal kekkuraar.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### AI badhilgal yen thappaa pogudhu

Thappugal enga irundhu varudhu-nnu therinjaa neenga innum nallaa check pannuveenga. "What AI cannot do" limits-a muzhusaa vilakkudhu. Check panradhukku naalu kaaranangal thaan ellaatthaiyum vida mukkiyam:

| Kaaranam | Adhu eppadi theriyum | Adhai pidikkira check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adhu vara vaaippulla text-a predict pannudhu.** Podhuvaa adutthu varradhai ezhudhudhu, thedip paartthadhai illa. | Oru karpanai detail-oda varra smooth-aana, confident-aana badhil, oru **hallucination** | Ovvoru mukkiya claim-aiyum oru real source-la kandupidinga |
| **Guess panradhukku reward kidaikkudhu.** September 2025 paper onnula, podhuvaana training, scoring methods "enakku theriyala"-nnu solradhai vida guess-ku reward kudukkudhu-nnu OpenAI researchers vaadhittaanga. | Sollanum-ngura nerattula kooda adhu "enakku urudhiyaa theriyala"-nnu aridhaa thaan sollum | "Nee evvalavu confident, unakku enna theriyanum?"-nu kelunga, appuram eppadiyum check pannunga |
| **Adhoda knowledge oru date-la ninnududhu.** Oru model oru **knowledge cutoff** varaikkumaana data-la irundhu katthukkudhu. | Oru pazhaiya rule, rate allathu deadline-a ippodhaiyadhaa solradhu | Source-la date-a kandupidinga |
| **Adhu ungakooda otthupora pakkam saayudhu.** Idhukku **sycophancy**-nu peru. | Oru thappaana idea-ku "aamaa, neenga solradhu sari" | Neutral-aa kelunga, edhiraana case-aiyum kelunga |

Sycophancy pathi: 2023-la oru study-la, aindhu munnani AI assistants users kekka virumbura maadhiri thonuradhaiyae adikkadi sonnadhai Anthropic researchers kandupidichaanga. Idhai avanga oralavukku human ratings-la training panradhoda inaichaanga: thangakooda otthupora badhilgalukku makkal adhiga rating kudukkura pokku irukku. Adhanaala neenga eppadi kekkureengalo, adhai poruthu thaan ungalukku enna kidaikkudhu-nnu amaiyum.

### Step 1: Stakes-a mudivu pannunga

Moonu quick kelvigal kelunga. Indha badhila yaar nambi irukkapporaanga? Thappaa irundhaa enna aagum? Andha action-a thiruppi maattha mudiyumaa? Oru thappaana slogan-aala endha izhappum illa. Thavaravitta scholarship deadline-a appuram sari panna mudiyaadhu.

| Stakes | Examples | Evvalavu check pannanum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kuraivu** | Ideas, names, first drafts, brainstorming | Skim panni edit pannunga; judge neenga thaan |
| **Naduttharam** | Work emails, reports, customer replies, assignments | Neenga serkkura ovvoru fact, figure-aiyum check pannunga |
| **Adhigam** | Health, panam, law, safety, public aagura allathu thiruppi maattha mudiyaadha edhuvum | Ovvoru claim-aiyum official sources allathu thagudhiyaana orutthar moolamaa verify pannunga |

Oru useful rule: ungala thavira vera yaaraavadhu andha badhila nambi seyalpaduvaanga-nnaa, adhu kuranjadhu naduttharam.

### Step 2: Neenga nambi seyalpadappora claims-a pirichedunga

Ovvoru **number, date, name, rule, quotation**-aiyum underline pannunga. Thappugal inga thaan olinjirukkum, check panna ellaatthaiyum vida easy-aanadhum idhunga thaan. "Kudumba varumaanam ₹2.5 lakh-ku keezha irukkura students-ku indha scheme udhavudhu, deadline 30 November" maadhiri oru vaakkiyatthula, oru nimishatthula verify pannakkoodiya rendu claims irukku.

Appuram endha claims **load-bearing**-nu kelunga: idhu thappaa irundhaa, neenga vera maadhiri edhaavadhu pannuveengalaa? Adhungala mudhalla check pannunga. Background paragraph-la irukkura oru thappaana date, oru thappaana deadline-a vida kuraivaa thaan mukkiyam.

Kadaisiyaa, edhu **vidupattirukku**-nnu thedunga. Oru badhil ovvoru vaakkiyatthulayum accurate-aa irundhum, ungalukku mukkiyamaana warning-a vittudalaam. Illaadha oru vaakkiyattha fact-check panna mudiyaadhu. Adhanaala neradiyaa kelunga, udhaaranamaa "Diabetes irukkura orutharukku enna warnings allathu exceptions porundhum?", appuram original-oda warnings section-a neengalae padinga.

### Step 3: Oru current, reliable source-oda verify pannunga

Nalla sources, thoraayamaa nambikkai varisaippadi:

1. **Original:** sattam, circular, paper, product label.
2. **Official sites:** government departments, RBI allathu SEBI maadhiri regulators, company-oda sondha pages.
3. **Nambagamaana publishers:** niruvappatta news organisations, reference works.

Professional fact-checkers-oda pazhakkamaana **lateral reading**-a use pannunga: andha page-a vittu veliya vandhu, pudhu tabs-a thirandhu, andha claim allathu website pathi independent sources enna solludhu-nnu paarunga. Oru page evvalavu professional-aa irukku-nnu paartthu adhai judge pannaadheenga. Orae number-a thirumba solra aindhu pages-um orae oru source thaan-nu nyaabagam vachukkonga.

**Badhil links kudutthaa, adhai thiranga**, naalu kelvigal kelunga:

- Andha page irukkaa?
- Adhu thaniyaavae neenga nambura oru source-aa?
- Badhil claim panradhai adhu appadiye solludhaa? Oru real, official page-a, adhula illavae illaadha oru vaakkiyatthukku cite pannalaam.
- Adhu current-aa? Official sites pazhaiya notices-a online-laye vachirukkum. Pona varusha scholarship notice real thaan, official thaan, aanaa ungalukku adhu thappu.

Supporting vaakkiyattha assistant-a quote panna vaikkiradhu eppadi, oru claim-a adhoda original varai trace panradhu eppadi-nnu "AI for research and analysis" kaattudhu.

### Podhuvaana claims-ku rendu nimisha check

Enga paarkkanum-nu therinjaa, perumbaalaana dhinasari claims-a vegamaa check pannalaam:

| Claim vagai | India-la enga check panradhu | Neram |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government scheme rules, deadlines | Scheme-oda official portal allathu department site (perumbaalum gov.in allathu nic.in-la mudiyum) | 2 nimisham |
| Bank, loan allathu UPI rules | RBI site, UPI-ku NPCI, allathu unga bank-oda sondha website | 2 nimisham |
| Train allathu travel rules | IRCTC allathu Indian Railways official pages | 1 nimisham |
| Oru medicine | Label, kooda varra leaflet; appuram pharmacist allathu doctor | 5 nimisham |
| Oru pirabalamaana aaloda quote | Exact vaarthaigala quotes-la search pannunga; oru nambagamaana original-a thedunga | 2 nimisham |
| Oru viral news claim | Nambagamaana news sites, government pathina claims-ku PIB Fact Check | 3 nimisham |
| Oru document allathu paper-la oru number | Original document: adhula andha figure-a search pannunga | 1 nimisham |

Sila nimishangalla oru reliable idatthula claim-a kandupidikka mudiyala-nnaa, adhai verify aagaadhadhaa edutthukkonga. Adhai thirumba sollaadheenga, stakes naduttharam allathu adhigam-naa adhai vachu seyalpadaadheenga.

### Numbers-aiyum code-aiyum run panni check pannunga

Oru total, oru percentage, allathu oru loan instalment-a kannai moodikkittu nambaadheenga. Inputs-a oru calculator allathu spreadsheet-la pottu neengalae kanakku podunga. Neenga kudutha oru list-a assistant use panniyirundhaa, items-a ennunga: oru row vittup ponaa ovvoru total-um maarum.

"Indha number eppadi vandhuchu?"-nnu kekkuradhu useful thaan, aanaa andha explanation evidence illa. Adhuvum generate panna innoru text thaan. March 2025-la, Anthropic researchers thangaloda oru model 36-aiyum 59-aiyum koottumbodhu adhukkulla enna nadakkudhu-nnu paartthaanga. Adhu thoraayamaana estimates-um kadaisi digit tricks-um kalandha thannoda sondha vazhiya use pannuchu. Aanaa andha koottala eppadi pannuchu-nnu kettappo, school-la solliththarra 1-a carry panra muraiya adhu vivarichudhu. Oru azhagaana step-by-step explanation oru thappaana number mela utkaarndhirukkalaam, adhanaala adhai thirumba kanakku podunga allathu oru source-la kandupidinga.

Code-kkum idhae thaan. Oru assistant oru formula allathu script ezhudhuchu-nnaa, badhil yerkanavae ungalukku therinja oru chinna example-la adhai run pannunga. Oru assistant "idhai test pannitten"-nu sonnaa, adhu work aaguradhai neenga paarkkura varai adhukku endha arthamum illa.

### Step 4: Reasoning-a test pannunga

- **Neutral-aa kelunga.** "Pudhu tax regime enakku yen better?"-ngradhu otthupoga koopidudhu. "Varushatthukku ₹9 lakh sambaadhikkira orutharukku pazhaiya, pudhu tax regimes-a compare pannu, ovvonnukkumaana case-oda"-ngradhu appadi pannaadhu.
- **Edhiraana case-a kelunga.** "Indha badhil thappaa irukkakkoodiyadhukku ellaatthaiyum vida valuvaana kaaranam enna?"
- **Vera maadhiri marupadi kelunga.** Oru pudhu chat-la kelviya vera vaarthaigalla kelunga. Chinna maatrangal badhila purattiduchu-nnaa, adhai kuraivaa nambunga.
- **Self-check-oda niruttidaadheenga.** "Double-check" panna solradhu chinna thappugala pidikkalaam, aanaa adhu adhae system thannaiyae check panradhu thaan.
- **Irandaavadhu assistant-um independent illa.** Oru fact-la rendu assistants disagree panna, neenga adhai check pannanum-nu therinjudum. Otthuponaa, adhu proof illa: rendumae orae pazhaiya page-la irundhu katthukkittirukkalaam.

### Images, charts, screenshots

Badhilgal kooda padangal varradhu adhigamaagitte irukku, padangal proof maadhiri thonum. Avai proof illa.

- **Oru chart adhoda numbers alavukku thaan nalladhu.** Oru assistant oru chart varainjaa, adhukku pinnaadi irukkura values-la rendu moonai data kooda check pannunga.
- **Oru screenshot-a edit pannalaam allathu karpanaiyaa uruvaakkalaam.** Oru notice allathu message-oda screenshot-a nambradhukku munnaadi, official site-la original-a kandupidinga. Oru image mudhalla enga vandhuchu-nnu oru reverse image search kaattalaam.
- **Assistants photos-a thappaa padikkum.** Oru bill allathu form-oda photo-va share pannumbodhu, adhu paditcha figures-a andha photo koodavae check pannunga.

"Deepfakes and misinformation" fake images, notices pathi innum aazhamaa pesudhu.

### Indhiya mozhigalla badhilgala check panradhu

Badhiloda quality oru mozhikkum innoru mozhikkum maaralaam, translation-la names-um numbers-um easy-aa kuzhambidum. Hindi, Tamil allathu Bengali-la oru assistant-a nambradhukku munnaadi, ungalukku yerkanavae badhil therinja oru kelviya vachu adhai test pannunga. Mukkiyamaana edhukkum, oru official source-la facts-a check pannunga; adhu perumbaalum English-layum unga mozhilayum kidaikkum.

### Step 5: Eppo oru aalkitta kekkanum-nu therinjukkonga

Stakes adhigamaa irukkumbodho reliable sources muranpadumbodho oru aalkitta ponga: oru doctor, pharmacist, lawyer, chartered accountant, unga manager allathu sambandhappatta government office. Avangala paarkka **thayaar** aaga udhavuradhula AI romba sirappu. Kooda eduthuttu poga vendiya kelvigala adhukitta kelunga, appuram ungalukku kidaicha badhil, neenga check panna sources, innum thelivillaadhadhu ellaatthaiyum kondu ponga.

## Oru worked example

**Oru adhiga stakes kelvi.** Suresh-oda appaavukku 68 vayasu, type 2 diabetes irukku, sali pidichirukku. Suresh veettula oru over-the-counter sali maatthirai box vachirukkaar. Adhula paracetamol-um oru decongestant-um irukku; decongestant-na mookkadaippai kuraikkira oru marundhu.

Avaroda mudhal kelvi oru leading question: "Indha sali maatthiraiya diabetes maatthiraigaloda edutthukkalaam thaanae?" Adhu "generally safe"-nu assistant otthukkittu, oru dose-a kudukkudhu. Appuram leading questions pathina warning avarukku nyaabagam varudhu. Oru pudhu chat-a thirandhu neutral-aa kekkuraar: "En appaavukku 68 vayasu, type 2 diabetes irukku, adhukku maatthirai edukkuraar. Paracetamol-um oru decongestant-um irukkura oru sali maatthiraiya avar edukkuradhukku munnaadi naan enna check pannanum?" Indha badhil innum kavanamaa irukku. Sali syrups-la sarkkarai irukkalaam-nu solli, oru pharmacist-kitta kekka solludhu, aanaa decongestant pathi innum kurippaa edhuvum sollala.

Suresh routine-a otturaar:

| Step | Avar enna pannaar |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Stakes | Adhigam: health, oru vayasaanavar, thodarndhu edukkura marundhugal, oru mosamaana reaction-a avaraala thiruppi maattha mudiyaadhu |
| 2. Claims | "Generally safe", dose, sarkkarai pathina kurippu. Vidupattadhu: decongestant pathina endha warning-um |
| 3. Verify | Box-aiyum adhoda vandha printed information-aiyum padichaar. Decongestant-ku, diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease allathu thyroid pirachanaigal irundhaa use panradhukku munnaadi doctor-kitta kekkanum-nu adhu solluchu. Rendu badhillayum idhu illa. |
| 4. Reasoning | Avar ketta vaarthaigala poruthu badhil maaruchu: avar lead pannappo "generally safe", neutral-aa kettappo "oru pharmacist-kitta kelunga". Avar appaavoda mattha udalnala pirachanaigal pathi adhu kekkavae illa. |
| 5. Escalate | Box-a kudumba pharmacist-kitta eduthuttu ponaar; avar appaavoda udalnala pirachanaigalaiyum mattha marundhugalaiyum pathi kettu, avarukku decongestant illaadha oru product-a suggest panni, dose-a confirm pannaar |

Rendu paadangala kavaninga. Leading kelviya vida neutral kelvikku nalla badhil kidaichudhu, aanaa adhuvum muzhumaiyaana badhil illa. Appuram, mukkiyamaana fact oru vidupatta vishayam: avar check pannakkoodiya oru thappaana vaakkiyam illa, illaadha oru vaakkiyam. Original-aana label mattum thaan adhai kaattuchu. Enna kekkanum-nu therinjukka assistant udhavuchu. Badhila kudutthadhu pharmacist, adhuvum kurippaa avar appaavukku: idhu checking-kaana oru udhaaranam, medical advice illa; vera yaaraa irundhaalum avanga sondha label-a padichu, oru doctor allathu pharmacist-kitta kekkanum.

**Oru kuraivaana stakes kelvi.** Adhae vaaram, than pudhu teekkadaikku patthu peyargala Suresh assistant-kitta kekkuraar. Stakes: kuraivu, yenna judge avar thaan, avar manasa maatthikkalaam. "Saravanampatti Chai Corner" avarukku pidichirukku-nnu adhai thernthedukkuraar, orae oru vishayatthai mattum check panraar: pakkatthula vera endha kadaiyum andha peyara yerkanavae use pannala-nnu.

Adhae tool, romba vera maadhiri checking, yenna stakes vera vera.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Ellaatthaiyum samamaa check panradhu.** Neengalae vittuduveenga. Stakes-ku ettha maadhiri effort-a vainga.
- **Tone-a nambradhu.** Confidence-um details-um evidence illa.
- **Leading question kekkuradhu.** "X yen unmai?"-nnu kettaa X-ku aadharavaana case thaan kidaikkum. Neutral-aa kelunga, edhiraana case-aiyum kelunga.
- **Irukkuradhai mattum check panradhu.** Vidupatta warning thaan ellaatthaiyum vida mukkiyamaa irukkalaam. Exceptions pathi kelunga, original-a padinga.
- **Oru official link-a current-nu ninaikkiradhu.** Pazhaiya notices online-laye irukkum. Varusham, round allathu version-a check pannunga.
- **AI-oda working-a proof-aa yetthukkiradhu.** Numbers-a neengalae thirumba kanakku podunga, code-a ungalukku badhil therinja oru example-la run pannunga.
- **Self-check-aiyo irandaavadhu AI-aiyo independent-aa ennuradhu.** Disagreement-na check pannanum-nu artham; agreement edhaiyum niroobikkaadhu.
- **Stakes adhigamaa irukkumbodhu expert-a thavirkkiradhu.** Kelvigala thayaar panna AI udhavum; experts thaan adhukku badhil solluvaanga.

## Practice pannunga

Keezhae link panniyirukkura Civic Online Reasoning-la irundhu oru lateral reading lesson-a pannunga.

1. **Aindhu kelvigal, aindhu stakes.** Oru assistant-kitta aindhu factual kelvigal kelunga: oru scheme-oda eligibility, oru varalaatru date, oru train rule, oru health fact, oru price. Ovvonnaiyum stakes padi rate panni, load-bearing claims-a verify panni, ovvoru source-oda date-aiyum note pannunga.
2. **Lead pannunga, appuram neutral-aa kelunga.** Orae kelviya thanitthani chats-la rendu thadavai kelunga: oru thadavai "X yen unmai?"-nnu, innoru thadavai "X unmaiyaa, adhukku edhiraana case enna?"-nnu. Badhilgal eppadi verupadudhu-nnu ezhudhunga.
3. **Oru number-a thirumba kanakku podunga.** Oru bill-a pirikkiradhu allathu oru maasaandhira loan instalment maadhiri unga sondha vaazhkkaiyila irundhu oru kanakkai kelunga. Working-a kelunga, appuram adhai calculator allathu spreadsheet-la thirumba pannunga.
