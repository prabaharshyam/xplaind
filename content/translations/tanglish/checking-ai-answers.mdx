## Idhu enna

AI answer-a check panradhu-na, **neenga act panna munnaadi nambi seiyura pagudhigala confirm panradhu**. Ellathayum samamaa sandhegapadradhu illa. Birthday message-ku oru vegamaana padippu podhum. Oru medicine dose, oru legal deadline, oru customer-oda refund amount-ku proof thevai.

Manasula odura aindhu step routine:

```flow
1. Stakes | idhu thappaa irundhaa enna aagum? -> 2. Claims | names, numbers, dates, rules, quotes-a underline pannunga
2. Claims -> 3. Verify | andha claims-a nambagamaana sources-oda check pannunga
3. Verify -> 4. Reasoning | artham aagudhaa? Thirumba kettaalum nikkudhaa?
4. Reasoning -> 5. Escalate | stakes adhigama, sources mothudhaa? Oru aal-a kelunga
```

Routine-oda nokkam vegam. Perumbaalaana answers-ku step 1, 2 podhum; mukkiyam-na mattum thaan aazhama check pannuveenga.

Indha lesson-la Coimbatore-la sakkarai noyaali appa-va paathukkura Suresh-a follow pannuvom, avan assistant-kitta kekkura romba vera vera rendu kelvigal. Lesson mudiyum podhu evvalavu check pannanum, enna check pannanum, enga check pannanum-nu theriyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Mudhalla stakes

Enna thappaa pogalaam-nu paathu, ungaloda effort-a match pannunga:

| Stakes | Examples | Evvalavu check pannanum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kuraivu** | Ideas, names, first drafts, brainstorming | Pozhudhu paathu edit pannunga; check thevai illa |
| **Naduthara** | Work emails, reports, customer replies, assignments | Neenga serkkura ovvoru fact-aiyum figure-aiyum check pannunga |
| **Adhigam** | Health, panam, law, safety, public illa thirumba maathamudiyaadha edhaiyum | Ovvoru claim-aiyum official sources illa qualified aal-oda verify pannunga |

### Check panna mudiyura claims-a kandupidinga

Ovvoru **number, date, name, rule, quotation**-aiyum underline pannunga. Thappugal inga thaan olinjirukkum, check panna easy-um idhu thaan. "Family income ₹2.5 lakh-ku kizhae irukkura students-ku scheme help pannum, deadline 30 November" maadhiri oru vaakkiyathula oru nimishathula verify panna rendu claims irukku.

### Nambagamaana sources use pannunga

Nalla sources, nambikkai-yin ottu-mothamaana order-la:

1. **Original:** law, circular, paper, product label.
2. **Official sites:** government departments, RBI, SEBI maadhiri regulators, company-oda sondha pages.
3. **Mathippulla publishers:** established news organisations, reference works.

**Lateral reading** use pannunga: pudhu tabs open panni, oru claim-a pathi illa website-a pathi independent sources enna solluraanga-nu paarunga; page evvalavu professional-aa irukku-nu paathu judge pannaama.

### AI-oda sondha sources-a check pannunga

Answer links cite pannaa, adha open pannunga. Moonu vishayam confirm pannunga: page irukku, source nambagamaanadhu, answer sollradha page unmaiyilae solludhu. Karpanaiyaana illa porundhaadha citations romba common.

### Reasoning-a test pannunga

- Kelunga: "Indha number-a eppadi kandupidichinga?"
- Kelunga: "Edhu indha answer-a thappaakkum?"
- Ore kelviya vera vaarthaigalla kelunga. Sirusaa maatrathukkae answer maaritaa, kuraivaa nambunga.

### Warning signs-a paarunga

| AI answer-la red flag | Example |
|---|---|
| Source illaama romba thulliyamaana numbers | "exactly 73.4%" |
| Real makkaloda quotes, vera enga-um kidaikkaadhadhu | |
| Romba convenient-aa irukkura rules | "no documents needed" |
| Neenga edhu sonnaalum otthukkum | "Yes, you're right" |
| Vegamaa maarura topics-la pazhaya info | prices, rules, dates |

### Common claims-ku rendu nimisha check

Edhu enga paakkanum-nu theriyuma-na, perumbaalaana dhinasari claims-a vegamaa check pannalaam:

| Claim vagai | India-la enga check pannalaam | Neram |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government scheme rules, deadlines | Scheme-oda official portal illa department site (perumbaalum gov.in illa nic.in-la mudiyum) | 2 nimisham |
| Bank, loan, UPI rules | RBI site, UPI-ku NPCI, illa ungaloda bank-oda sondha website | 2 nimisham |
| Train illa travel rules | IRCTC illa Indian Railways official pages | 1 nimisham |
| Oru medicine | Label-um leaflet-um, apram pharmacist illa doctor | 5 nimisham |
| Oru prasiddha aal-oda quote | Exact vaarthaigala quotes-la search pannunga; mathippulla original-a paarunga | 2 nimisham |
| Viral news claim | Mathippulla news sites, government pathi claims-ku PIB Fact Check | 3 nimisham |
| Document illa paper-la oru number | Original document: andha figure-a search pannunga | 1 nimisham |

Oru sila nimishathula nambagamaana idathula claim kidaikkala-na, verify aagala-nu eduthukkunga. Adha thirumba sollaadheenga, stakes naduthara illa adhigama irundhaa adhu mela act pannaadheenga.

### Indian languages-la answers-a check panradhu

Assistants perumbaalum English-la thaan romba accurate, yenna training-la English text thaan adhigam paathirukku. Hindi, Tamil, Bengali-la kettaa, facts kuraivaa irukkalaam, vaarthaigal thulliyam kammiyaa irukkalaam. Mukkiyamaana edhukkum, ore kelviya English-layum kettu compare pannunga, illa facts-a edhaavadhu oru language-la official source-la check pannunga.

### Eppo oru aal-a kekkanum

Oru doctor, pharmacist, lawyer, chartered accountant, ungaloda manager, illa sambandhappatta government office. Avangalukku sariyaana kelvigala **ready panna** AI romba nallaa help pannum.

## Oru worked example

**Adhigam stakes kelvi.** Suresh-oda appa-ku type 2 diabetes, ippo cold pudichirukku. Oru common over-the-counter cold medicine-a avanga diabetes tablets-oda edukkalaamaa-nu Suresh assistant-a kekkuraanga.

Answer "generally safe"-nu solli, oru dose kudukkudhu, sila cold syrups-la sakkarai irukkum-nu serkkudhu.

Suresh routine-a odudhuraanga:

| Step | Avanga enna pannaanga |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Stakes | Adhigam: health, vayasaanavar, regular medicines |
| 2. Claims | "Generally safe", dose, sakkarai pathi note |
| 3. Verify | Medicine-oda label-um leaflet-um padichaanga: oru ingredient-ku diabetes irukkuravangalukku caution irukku. Assistant adha sollala. |
| 4. Reasoning | "Generally safe" vague; appa-oda matha conditions pathi adhu kekkala |
| 5. Escalate | Family pharmacist-a koopitaanga, avar vera product recommend panni dose-a confirm pannaar |

Enna kekkanum-nu assistant help pannuchu. Answer-a pharmacist thandhaar.

**Kuraivu stakes kelvi.** Adhe vaaram, Suresh pudhu tea stall-ku pathu peyargal assistant-a kekkuraanga. Stakes: kuraivu. "Saravanampatti Chai Corner"-a pidichadhaala select panraanga, oru vishayam mattum check panraanga: vera yaar kadaiyum brand-um andha peyara use pannala.

Ore tool, romba vera maadhiri checking, yenna stakes vera.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Ellathayum samamaa check panradhu.** Neenga vittuduveenga. Effort-a stakes-ku ettha alavula vainga.
- **Tone-a nambradhu.** Confidence-um detail-um proof illa.
- **Citations-a open pannaama irukkradhu.** Links poliyaa, udaindhadhaa, claim-kku sambandham illaadhadhaa irukkalaam.
- **AI-a thannai check pannikka sollitu niruthuradhu.** Thavarugala pudikka useful, aana independent illa.
- **Stakes adhigama irukkum podhu expert-a vittudradhu.** AI kelvigal ready panna help pannum; experts badhil solluvaanga.
- **Vegamaa maarura facts-ku pazhaya answers use panradhu.** Prices, rules, deadlines maarum; ungaloda source-oda date-a check pannunga.

## Practice pannunga

Keezha link panni irukkura Civic Online Reasoning-la oru lateral reading lesson pannunga.

1. **Aindhu kelvigal, aindhu stakes.** Oru assistant-a aindhu factual kelvigal kelunga: oru scheme-oda eligibility, oru historical date, oru train rule, oru health fact, oru price. Ovvonnayum stakes-padi rate panni, mukkiyamaana claims-a verify pannunga.
2. **Citations-a audit pannunga.** Sources kettu ovvoru link-aiyum open pannunga. Edhu illaama irundhuchu, edhu thappu, edhu claim-a support pannala-nu record pannunga.
3. **Ungaloda rule-a ezhudhunga.** Kuraivu, naduthara, adhigam stakes-ku ungaloda sondha oru-vari checking rule-a ezhudhi, desk pakkathula vainga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Neenga thappu-nu pudicha oru AI answer-a, adha nirubicha source-oda kaattalaam.
