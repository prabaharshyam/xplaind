## Idhu enna

AI projects fail aagura oru podhuvaana kaaranam mosamaana model illa. Thappaana problem-a choose pannradhu thaan. Teams arithaa varra problems-ku, oru simple rule-aalaye solve panna mudiyum problems-ku, illa oru mistake-um thaanga mudiyaadha idathula, periya demos-a build pannuvaanga.

**AI-ku oru problem-a choose panradhu**-na, oru team-a commit panradhukku munnaadi, oru candidate use case-a sila kelvigaloda check panradhu:

| Kelvi | Edhai check panreenga |
| --- | --- |
| **Problem real-aa, value irukkaa?** | Aadi aadi varum, valikkum, alakka mudiyum |
| **AI sariyaana tool-aa?** | Oru rule, form, nalla search, illa process maatram idhai innum simple-aa solve pannaadhu |
| **AI thappaa ponaa enna aagum?** | Users mistakes-a kavanichu cheap-aa fix pannalaam; ovvoru vagai error-oda cost-um theriyum |
| **Data, context irukkaa?** | AI-ku thevaiyaana information-a access panna mudiyum |
| **Success-a alakka mudiyumaa?** | Ippo oru baseline-um, theliva oru metric-um irukku |

```flow
title: Nalla AI problems moonum serara idathula irukkum
Value-um frequency-um irukku -> Oru nalla AI problem
Mistakes cheap, illa check panna easy -> Oru nalla AI problem
AI sariyaana tool -> Oru nalla AI problem
```

Indha lesson Pune-la oru ed-tech company-la product manager Nikhil-a follow pannum; avanga leadership "ellaa idathulayum AI" venum-nu kekkuraanga. Lesson mudiyum podhu, oru kavarchiyaana AI idea-va theliva kaaranangaloda reject panni, AI clear-aa udhavura onna pick panna mudiyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### AI-la illa, users-la irundhu aarambinga

Users kitta avanga velai pathi pesunga. Neram, panam, satisfaction enga ponadhu-nu kandupidinga: dhinamum oru mani neram edukkura task, vaaram 200 thadavai support pathil solra kelvi, late-aa varradhaala yaarum padikkaadha report. Appuram thaan AI udhavumaa-nu kelunga.

### AI eppo fit aagum, eppo aagaadhu

| AI fit aagum podhu | AI fit aagaadha podhu |
| --- | --- |
| Inputs vagai vagaiyaa, unstructured: free text, documents, images, speech | Oru simple rule-ae podhumaana accurate |
| Task padikkradhu, ezhudhradhu, summarise, classify, search | Review illaama ovvoru answer-um exact-aa sariyaa irukkanum, tax due calculate panradhu, legal document file panradhu maadhiri |
| Oru aal check panna 'podhumaana' answer kooda neraya neram meechchapaduthum | Thevaiyaana information system-ku kidaikkaadhu |
| Scale-la personalisation mukkiyam | AI thappaa irukkum podhu users-aala therinjukka mudiyaadhu, errors-um costly |

### Errors-ku rendu pakkam irukku

Ovvoru AI mudivum rendu vidhathula thappaagalaam, rendukkum perumbaalum ore cost irukkaadhu:

| Use case | False alarm cost | Miss cost | Edhu mosam |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Payment-la fraud alert | Kovam vandha customer, oru OTP | Panam poidum | Perumbaalum miss |
| Resume screener | Weak candidate-ku selavaana neram | Nalla candidate anyaayamaa reject aavaanga | False rejection |
| Spam filter | Oru real email spam-la poidum | Inbox-la spam | Neraya neram false alarm |
| Medical triage | Thevai illaadha test | Periya noy miss aagidum | Miss, romba |

Edhu error mosam-nu mudivu panradhu design-a shape pannum: thresholds enga vaikkanum, eppo oru manushan review pannanum, product user-ku enna solanum.

### Naalu periya risks

Product teams ovvoru idea-vayum naalu risks-la judge pannuvaanga:

1. **Value:** users-ku venumaa, avanga choose pannuvaangalaa?
2. **Usability:** eppadi use panradhu-nu avangalaala purinjukka mudiyumaa?
3. **Feasibility:** ettra quality, speed, cost-la namma build panna mudiyumaa?
4. **Viability:** business-ku work aagumaa, legal, brand, cost risk-um serthu?

AI **feasibility**-la innum uncertainty serkkum: try panra varaikkum quality theriyaadhu. Real data-la oru vegamaana prototype-oda munnaadiye test pannunga.

### Score podunga, compare pannunga

Candidates-a pakkathula pakkathula vechchu, ovvonnukkum 1 (weak)-la irundhu 5 (strong) varaikkum score podunga:

| Idea | Value | Frequency | Error tolerance | Data | Feasible | Low risk | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 5 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 24 |
| B | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 15 |
| C | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **28: vegamaana win** |

Prototype panna onnu rendu pick pannunga, matha ideas-a yen reject pannineenga-nu ezhudhi vainga.

## Oru worked example

Nikhil Pune-la, 9 to 12 classes-ku online coaching nadathura oru ed-tech company-la product manager. Leadership adutha quarter-kulla "ellaa idathulayum AI" venum-nu kekkuraanga. Avan anju candidate ideas-a list panni score podraan:

| Idea | Value | Frequency | Thappaa ponaa | Data | Mudivu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Students-ku doubt-solving chat | High | Daily | Students-ku theriyum, learning-ku risky | Course content irukku | **Prototype**, course content-la ground panni, report panna answers-a teacher review pannattum |
| 2. Descriptive answers-a AI grading | Teachers-ku high | Weekly | Marks-ayum fairness-ayum nerukka paadhikkum; parents dispute pannuvaanga | Munnaadi grade panna answers irukku | **Appuram**, teachers ovvoru grade-ayum review pannanum |
| 3. Parents-ku auto-ezhudhina weekly progress emails | Medium | Weekly | Kammi: kandupidikka easy, haani kammi | Ellaam system-la irukku | **Vegamaana win**: oru template-um sila AI ezhudhina lines-um |
| 4. Edhu students dropout aavaanga-nu predict panradhu | High | Monthly | Students-a kalangappaduthalaam | Kaaranangal pathi data weak | **Ippo vendaam** |
| 5. Demand-padi fees set panna AI | Theliva illa | Arithu | Legal, nambikkai risk | Konjam | **Illa**: pricing analyst oru spreadsheet-oda nallaa panraanga |

Avan features list kudukkaama indha oru page table-a present panraan. Leadership rendu prototypes-um oru quick win-um ok pannuraanga, ovvoru "illa"-kkum kaaranam document aagudhu.

Moonu maadham kazhichu, progress emails parents teachers-a call panradha sumaar moonuil onnu kammi pannirukku, doubt-solving prototype rendu batches-oda pilot-la irukku. Avan reject panna ideas amaidhiyaa team-oda neratha thinnala.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Technology-la irundhu aarambikkradhu.** User pain-la irundhu aarambinga.
- **Simple solutions-a ignore panradhu.** Rules, forms, nalla search podhumaa irundha adhu cheap-um, adhigam reliable-um.
- **Thappaa ponaa enna aagum-nu kekkaama vidradhu.** Ovvoru error-oda cost-um design-a decide pannum.
- **Data irukku-nu assume panradhu.** Access-um quality-yum munnaadiye check pannunga.
- **Baseline illama irukkradhu.** Ippo task-a alakkala-naa, AI udhavuchu-nu prove panna mudiyaadhu.
- **Ellathukkum aama-nu solradhu.** Theliva oru "illa, yenna-na" oru product manager-oda mikka value-aana outputs-la onnu.
