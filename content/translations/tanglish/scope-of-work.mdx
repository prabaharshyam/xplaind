## Idhu enna

**Scope of work (SOW)**-na discovery-a oru plan-aa maathura agreement. Idhu perumbaalum sila pakkangal, client-oda business owner-um ungal pakkamum sign pannuvaanga. Oru nalla AI SOW-la irukkum:

- **Objective**: problem-um, baseline-um target-um irukkura primary KPI-um.
- **In scope**: neenga enna build pannuveenga, enna seiveenga, kurippaa.
- **Out of scope**: neenga enna seiyamaatteenga, adhe alavukku thelivaa.
- **Deliverables**: ovvoru concrete output (deploy panna service, oru eval report, oru runbook, training sessions).
- **Milestones**: dates, proof of concept, pilot, production-kku idaiyila irukkura stage gates-oda.
- **Acceptance criteria**: ovvoru deliverable-kum test panna koodiya conditions.
- **Assumptions, dependencies**: neenga client-a edhukku nambi irukkeenga (oru date-kkulla data access, oru IT contact, sign-on set-up, subject experts-oda neram).
- **Roles**: yaaru mudivu pannuvaanga, yaaru review pannuvaanga, yaaru support pannuvaanga.
- **Change process**: pudhu requests-a eppadi assess panni approve pannuvaanga.
- **Risks**: therinja risks, owners-oda.

| Scope of work: sila pakkangal, business owner sign pannuvaar | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Oru file-ku 41-la irundhu 22 min; rework 7%-ku mela illa |
| **In scope** | 24 fields, oru draft summary, oru review screen, ... |
| **Out of scope** | Credit decisions, customers, non-English, LOS |
| **Deliverables** | Service, eval report, pilot report, runbook, ... |
| **Milestones** | POC gate vaaram 4, pilot gate vaaram 14, apram production |
| **Acceptance** | 300 files-la 10 critical fields-la kurainjadhu 95%; citations |
| **Dependencies** | 10 July-kkulla file access; oru senior officer vaaraththukku 4 manineram |
| **Roles** | Yaaru mudivu panna, review panna, support panna |
| **Changes** | Log panni, 3 naalla size panni, ezhuthula approve |
| **Risks** | Peyar kuripitta owners-oda |

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

**Acceptance criteria-va tests-aa ezhudhunga.** "Assistant accurate-aa badhil solludhu"-nu accept illa reject panna mudiyaadhu. "Otthukkitta 150-kelvi golden set-la, kurainjadhu 85% rendu credit officers-aala sariyaanavai-nu judge pannapadanum, 100% badhilgalum oru source document-a cite pannanum"-nu panna mudiyum.

**AI uncertainty pathi thelivaa irungha.** Model outputs probabilistic. Define panna test set-la quality bar-a, thodarndhu irukkura human review-a, oru gate-la bar meetaaththaa enna nadakkum-nu (neettikkalaam, scope-a kuraikkalaam, illa niruththalaam) sollunga.

**Edhu veliyila-nu peyar sollunga.** "Out of scope: core banking system-oda integration; Tamil-language documents; mobile app; customer-facing use." Perumbaalaana disputes yaarum ezhudhi vaikkaadha vishayangalaala varum.

**Dependencies-a date-oda ezhudhunga.** "Client 15 July-kkulla document store-ku read access tharanum; ovvoru vaaram thaamadham milestone 2-a oru vaaram nagarthum."

**Surukkamaa, elimaiyaa vainga.** Business owner pathinaindhu nimishathula padikka mudiyanum. Technical details-a oru appendix-la podunga.

**Sariyaana signatures vaanginga.** "Aamaa, idhu mudinjidichchu"-nu solla koodiya aal sign pannanum.

**Mugamillaadha, testable vaarthaigal:**

| Section | Mugamillaadhadhu | Testable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | "AI-yoda efficiency-a improve panradhu" | "20 pilot officers-ku oru file-ku median neram 41-la irundhu 22 nimishathukku" |
| Acceptance | "Nallaa work aagudhu" | "Kadandha 300 files-la 10 critical fields-la kurainjadhu 95%, officers-oda entries-oda oppittu" |
| Out of scope | Sollaporadhu illa | "Credit decisions; customer-facing features; non-English documents" |
| Dependency | "Client data tharuvaanga" | "10 July-kkulla rendu varusha anonymised files-ku read access; thaamadhangal milestone 2-a vaaram-ku vaaram nagarthum" |
| Change process | Sollaporadhu illa | "Log panni, moonu velai naatkalukkulla size panni, head of operations-aala ezhuthula approve pannapadum" |

**Draft-a review panradhu.** SOW-va discovery meetings-la illaadha oruththarukku kudunga. Moonu kelvigal kelunga: enna deliver aagum, enna aagaadhu, ovvoru deliverable-um eppadi accept pannapadum? Ovvoru thayakkamum oru ambiguity-a kaattum. Sign pannuvadharku munnaadi adhai sari pannunga, yenna ovvonnum acceptance neraththula oru future argument.

**Miss aana gate-ku plan panradhu.** Oru gate-oda criteria meetaaththaa enna nadakkum-nu munnaadiye mudivu pannunga. Options: oru fix plan-oda sirriya neettippu, work aagura alavukku scope-a kuraikkuradhu, illa oru write-up-oda niruththuradhu. Ellaarum nambikkaiyoda irukkumbodhe idhai otthukkittaa, appuram varra kashtamaana pechu amaidhiyaagavum vegamaagavum mudiyum.

## Oru worked example

Chennai lender-oda credit-file assistant-ku SOW-la irundhu oru extract:

> **Objective.** 20 pilot officers-ku oru loan file-ku median officer neratha 41 nimishathula (baseline, June) irundhu 22 nimishathukku kuraikkanum, credit committee rework 7%-ku mela poga koodaadhu.
>
> **In scope.** Bank statements, salary slips, bureau reports-la irundhu (English, PDF, phone photos) otthukkitta 24 fields-a extract panradhu; lender-oda template-la oru draft credit summary; officers-ku oru review screen; lender-oda Azure tenancy-la deployment; eval, pilot reports; runbook; rendu training sessions.
>
> **Out of scope.** Credit decisions; customer-facing features; English-a thavira vera mozhigalla irukkura documents; file export-a thavira loan origination system-oda integration.
>
> **Acceptance (proof of concept, vaaram 4).** 300 kadandha files-la, 10 critical fields-la kurainjadhu 95% field accuracy, officers-oda entries-oda oppittu; ellaa summaries-um source pages-a cite pannanum.
>
> **Dependencies.** 10 July-kkulla rendu varusha anonymised files-ku read access; vaaraththukku 4 manineram oru senior credit officer.
>
> **Change process.** Pudhu requests log pannapattu, moonu velai naatkalukkulla size pannapattu, velai aarambikka munnaadi head of operations-aala ezhuthula approve pannapadum.

**Eppadi payan thandhudhu.** Moonu vaaram aana apram, collections team adhe tool-a ketkaanga. SOW scope-aiyum change process-aiyum peyar kuripittadhaala, andha request log pannapattu, size pannapattu, pilot mudinja apram-ku schedule pannapattu, credit-file velaiya amaidhiyaa thaamadhappaduthaama.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Mugamillaadha acceptance.** "Nallaa work aagudhu" mudivillaadha vivaadhatthai azhaikkum. Tests-aiyum data-vaiyum define pannunga.
- **Edhu veliyila-nu sollaama irukkradhu.** Ezhudhaadha assumptions disputes-aa maarum.
- **Date illaadha dependencies.** Appo client-oda thaamadhangal ungal missed deadlines aagidum.
- **Test set illaama accuracy-a vaakkuruthi tharadhu.** Quality-a eppavum otthukkitta dataset-oda, judging method-oda kattungal.
- **Thappaana aal sign pannuradhu.** Velaiya accept panna pora aal thaan SOW-la sign pannanum.

## Practice pannunga

Atlassian-oda project scope guide-aiyum, GOV.UK-oda user stories guide-aiyum padinga.

1. Unga discovery, KPI exercises-la irundhu use case-ku, mela irukkura ovvoru section-oda rendu pakka SOW ezhudhunga.
2. Moonu deliverables-ku acceptance criteria-va, vera yaaraavadhu run panna koodiya tests-aa ezhudhunga.
3. Oru nanbarodu maathi, avangala oru ambiguity-a kandupidikka sollunga; adhai sari pannunga.

**Eppo mudinjadhunnu sollalaam:** Andha room-la illaadha oruththarukku unga SOW-va kuduththu, enna deliver aagum, enna aagaadhu, eppadi accept pannapadumnu avanga thulliyamaa sollumbadi seiya mudiyum.
