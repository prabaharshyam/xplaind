## Idhu enna

AI product manager loops-la perumbaalum irukkum:

- **Product sense / design**: "X-ku oru AI feature design pannunga" illa "Y-a AI-vodu improve pannunga."
- **Metrics-um evaluation-um**: "Success-a eppadi measure pannuveenga?", "AI launch-ku podhumaana nalladhu-nu eppadi therinjukkuveenga?"
- **Technical depth**: engineers-oda velai seiya podhumaana alavu: RAG vs fine-tuning, latency-um cost trade-offs-um, models yen hallucinate pannum, agent-na enna.
- **Strategy-um business-um**: build vs buy, pricing, competition, unit economics.
- **Responsible AI**: bias, privacy, safety, regulation, AI-a eppo use pannakoodaadhu.
- **Behavioural**: judgement, influence, learning-a kaattura pazhaya situations.

| Round | Enna kepaanga |
|---|---|
| Product sense | "X-ku oru AI feature design pannunga" |
| Metrics-um evals-um | "Podhumaana nalladhu-nu eppadi theriyum?" |
| Technical depth | "RAG-aa fine-tuning-aa?" |
| Strategy | Build-aa buy-aa, pricing, unit economics |
| Responsible AI | Bias, privacy, safety, AI-a eppo use pannakoodaadhu |
| Behavioural | Numbers-oda STAR stories |

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

**AI product design questions-ku oru structure.**

1. *Clarify*: user, goal, constraints (market, platform, languages, regulation).
2. *User problems*: list pannunga, reasons-oda onnu select pannunga (frequency, pain, value).
3. *AI sariyaana tool-aa?* Yen-nu sollunga, neenga yosicha simple options enna-nu sollunga.
4. *Solution*: experience-a step by step, AI thappaa irukkum podhu enna nadakkum-nu serthu (sources, confidence, correction, hand-over).
5. *Eppadi work aagudhu*: surukkamaa: thevaiyaana data, retrieval irukkaa illaiyaa, model choice, human oversight.
6. *Metrics*: oru primary metric, guardrail metrics, oru offline evaluation plan, online signals.
7. *Risks*: quality, cost, privacy, fairness, misuse, mitigations.
8. *Launch plan*: prototype, pilot, rollout, enna nadandhaa niruththuveenga.

**Metrics questions.** Model quality (golden set-la accuracy, faithfulness)-a, product outcomes (task completion, save aana neram, retention, revenue)-a, guardrails (complaints, escalations, oru user-ku selavu)-a thaniyaa pirichunga. Launch bar-a experts-oda, data-voda eppadi set pannuveenga-nu explain pannunga.

**Technical questions.** Thelivaa, nermaiyaa irunga: jargon-a vida trade-offs-a explain pannunga. "Prompting-oda retrieval-oda aarambippom, yenna facts vaaram vaaram maarum; fine-tuning pazhaya knowledge-a uraya vechchidum."

**Responsible AI questions.** Harm-a kandupidichum, ippadiyum ship panna mudiyum-nu kaattunga: yaarukku paadhippu varalam, adha eppadi test pannuveenga, makkal enga control-la irukkanum, edha build pannaama vidveenga.

**Behavioural.** Numbers-oda STAR stories ready pannunga: oru launch, oru failure, engineering-oda oru disagreement, oru stakeholder-kku "no" sonna oru neram, poorna data illaama edutha oru decision.

**Sathama practice pannunga**, neram vechchu, oru friend-oda illa unga-a record panni. Interviewers "sariyaana" badhilai vida structure-um judgement-um thaan judge pannuvaanga.

**Ettu steps neraththula eppadi poradhu.** 25 nimisha design round-la, oru balanced answer ippadi irukkum:

| Step | Nimisham | Interviewer edha kekkuraanga |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Clarify | 2 | Mudhalla users, goals, constraints pathi kekkureengalaa? |
| 2. User problems | 3 | Reasons-oda oru problem-a select panreengalaa? |
| 3. AI sariyaana tool-aa? | 2 | Simple options-a yosikkreengalaa? |
| 4. Solution | 6 | AI thappaa irukkum podhu serthu, experience thelivaa irukkaa? |
| 5. Eppadi work aagudhu | 3 | Parts theriyumaa: data, retrieval, model, oversight? |
| 6. Metrics | 4 | Primary metric, guardrails, offline-um online evaluation-um |
| 7. Risks | 3 | Quality, cost, privacy, fairness, misuse, mitigations-oda |
| 8. Launch plan | 2 | Pilot, rollout, enna nadandhaa niruththuveenga |

Clock-a paarunga. Neraya candidates solution-kku pathinaaru nimisham selavu panni, metrics-kum risks-kum varavae maattaanga, AI PM judgement romba theriyura idam adhu thaan.

## Oru worked example

Question: "Oru popular Indian food-delivery app-ku oru AI feature design pannunga."

Kavya-oda oru strong answer, surukkamaa:

*Clarify*: metros-layum tier-2 cities-layum users, neraya languages, phone-first; goal order frequency-aa satisfaction-aa?

*Problem*: avanga enna order pannanum-nu thervu panradhu, delivery issues, dietary needs-nu list panni, "post-order issues" (item missing, delays)-a select panraanga: aadi aadi varudhu, kashtam, support-ku selavu adhigam.

*Yen AI*: complaints palavidha free text-um photos-um; AI adhai purinjukkittu, routine cases-a udane theerkkalaam.

*Solution*: in-app support assistant, complaint-um photo-vum padikkum, order data-va check pannum, oru limit-kulla refunds-a thaanaave kudukkum, veru maadhiri irundhaa hand-over pannum, Hindi, Tamil, English-la work aagum, confirm panradhukku munnaadi enna nadakkum-nu thelivaa kaattum.

*Metrics*: primary, theerkkuradhukku aagura median neram; guardrails, oru order-ku refund selavu, thirumba varra complaints, fraud rate, satisfaction; 500 labelled pazhaya complaints-la offline evaluation, launch bar-a support team-oda set pannuvaanga.

*Risks*: refund fraud (limits, photo checks, account history), thappaana refusals (easy hand-over), cost (classification-ku chinna model, kashtamaana cases-ku periya model).

*Launch*: rendu cities-la 5% orders, control-oda compare pannunga, metrics nilaichchaa expand pannunga.

Interviewer-oda follow-up: "Refund cost 20% adhigamaanaa?" Kavya automation-a kuraikkradhukku munnaadi, pudhu accounts-ku limits-a irukkamaakkuradhaiyum fraud patterns-a check panradhaiyum pathi pesuraanga.

| Step | Kavya-oda choice | Yen nalla score aagudhu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Post-order issues | Aadi aadi varudhu, kashtam, selavu adhigam; thelivaana reason sonnaanga |
| Yen AI | Palavidha free text-um photos-um | Rules-um forms-um indha variety-a cover pannaadhu |
| Failure design | Confirm panradhukku munnaadi outcome-a kaattum; easy hand-over | AI thappaa pogum-nu plan panraanga |
| Metrics | Median time to resolution, cost-um fraud guardrails-um | AI-a business outcomes-oda inaikkudhu |
| Launch | Rendu cities-la 5% orders, control-oda | Alakkappattadhu, thirumba maathikkalaam |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Solution-ku thaavuradhu.** Mudhalla clarify pannunga, oru problem-a select pannunga.
- **AI-ku AI-ae.** AI yen simple options-a vida nalladhu-nu sollunga.
- **Failure-a ignore panradhu.** AI thappaa irukkum podhu enna nadakkum-nu design pannunga.
- **Model metrics mattum.** Success-a user, business outcomes-oda, guardrails-oda inainga.
- **Purinjukkaama jargon.** Trade-offs-a simple-aa explain pannunga.

## Practice pannunga

PAIR Guidebook-ayum SVPG-oda naalu periya risks-ayum, Tech Interview Handbook-oda behavioural guide-ayum thirumba paarunga.

1. Ettu-step structure-a vachchu, moonu design questions-ku, ovvondrukkum 25 nimishathula, sathama badhil sollunga (udhaaranathukku: oru government services app-ku, oru hospital-oda patient app-ku, oru stock-broking app-ku AI).
2. Ovvondrukkum primary metric, rendu guardrails, launch bar-a ezhudhunga.
3. Numbers-oda anju STAR stories ready pannunga, ovvonnayum rendu nimishathukkulla practice pannunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Paarthirukkaadha oru AI product design question-ku 25 nimishathula thelivaana structure, metrics, risks-oda badhil sollamudiyum.
