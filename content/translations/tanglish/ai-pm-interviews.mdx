## Idhu enna

Oru AI product manager interview, endha AI feature-a build pannanumnu decide panna mudiyumaa, adhu podhumaana alavu nallaa irukkunnu prove panna mudiyumaa, real users vandhappuram adha safe-aavum affordable-aavum vachukka mudiyumaanu check pannudhu. Perumbaalum idhu oru classic PM interview maadhiri thaan irukkum. Vithiyaasam ennanna AI output probabilistic: adhae feature perumbaalaana neram sariyaa irukkum, sila neram confident-aa thappaa irukkum. Adhanaala classic PM answers adikkadi vittudura moonu pazhakkangalai interviewers gavanippaanga:

1. **Thappaana badhilgalukku design pannunga.** Model thappaa sonnaa user enna paarppaanga, oru manushan-kitta eppadi povaanga?
2. **Launch-kku munnaadi quality-a alanga.** Real cases-la oru eval-um munnaadiyae agree panna launch bar-um, "demo nallaa irundhuchu" illa.
3. **Unit economics therinjukkonga.** Ovvoru request-kkum tokens-la panam aagudhu, ovvoru thappaana action-um adha vida adhigamaa cost aagalaam.

Rounds company-kku company maarum, adhoda order-um fixed illa. Podhuvaa varra oru set:

| Round | Typical kelvi | Nalla badhil enna kaattum |
|---|---|---|
| Product sense | "Oru bus-ticket app-kku AI feature design pannunga." | Thernthedutha oru user problem, AI yen porundhudhunnu oru kaaranam, failure-kku oru plan |
| Metrics and evals | "Unga eval 93% solludhu. Launch pannuveengalaa?" | Goal-oda inaincha metric, launch bar, error severity |
| Technical depth | "Retrieval-aa fine-tuning-aa?" | Elimaiyaana trade-offs: freshness, cost, latency, effort |
| Strategy | "API mela build panradhaa, namma sondha model-a train panradhaa?" | Cost per request, differentiation, vendor risk |
| Responsible AI | "Idhu yaarai baadhikkalaam?" | Per sonna harms, adhukkaana tests, manushanga enga control-la irukkaanga |
| Behavioural | "Thappaa pona oru launch pathi sollunga." | Real numbers-um oru lesson-um irukkura STAR story |

Sila companies oru take-home assignment-um anuppuvaanga, adha keezha paarkkalaam. Bengaluru-la AI PM interviews-kku prepare pannikittu irukkura Kavya-va, numbers-oda oru muzhu design answer vazhiyaa indha lesson follow pannudhu.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Design kelvigalukku oru structure

1. *Clarify*: user, business goal, languages, phones, connectivity, regulation maadhiri constraints. Interviewer goal-a thernthedukkalainnaa, oru assumption-a sollittu aduthadhukku ponga.
2. *User problems*: moonu naalu list pannunga, onna thernthedunga, yennu sollunga (evvalavu adikkadi nadakkudhu, evvalavu valikkudhu, adhoda madhippu enna).
3. *AI sariyaana tool-aa?* Neenga yosicha simple option-a sollunga, form, rules, illanna better search maadhiri, adhu yen podhaadhunnu sollunga.
4. *Experience*: step by step, AI thappaa sonnaa enna nadakkumngradhum serthu: sources-a kaattunga, action edukkuradhukku munnaadi confirm pannunga, users correct panna vidunga, oru manushan-kitta hand over pannunga.
5. *Eppadi work aagudhu*: thevaiyaana data, retrieval venumaa vendaamaa, endha step-kku endha model, enga oru manushan review panraaru.
6. *Metrics*: oru primary metric, supporting metrics, guardrails, launch bar.
7. *Risks*: quality, cost, privacy, fairness, misuse, ovvonnukkum oru mitigation.
8. *Launch plan*: offline eval, shadow mode, control group-oda oru chinna pilot, ungala niruththa vaikkira result.

25 nimisha design round-la, oru balanced answer nerathai ippadi selavu pannum:

| Step | Nimishangal | Interviewer enna gavanippaanga |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Clarify | 2 | Ideas-kku munnaadi users, goal, constraints |
| 2. User problems | 3 | Kaaranangalode thernthedutha oru problem |
| 3. AI sariyaana tool-aa? | 2 | Nermaiyaa yosicha oru simple option |
| 4. Experience | 6 | AI thappaa solra neramum serthu oru thelivaana flow |
| 5. Eppadi work aagudhu | 3 | Data, retrieval, model choice, oversight |
| 6. Metrics | 4 | Goal-la irundhu varra oru metric, oru launch bar |
| 7. Risks | 3 | Mitigations-oda specific harms |
| 8. Launch plan | 2 | Pilot, control group, stop rules |

Niraiya candidates experience-layae padhinanju nimisham selavu pannittu metrics-kko risks-kko varavae maattaanga, aanaa AI PM judgement adhigamaa theriyuradhu anga thaan. Step 4-kku appuram clock-a oru thadavai paarunga.

### Metrics: goal-la irundhu aarambinga

Metrics-a moonu layers-la, indha order-la thernthedunga:

- **Primary (product outcome)**: goal niraiveruchunnu prove panra user result, udhaaranamaa repeat orders, complete panna applications, michamaana neram.
- **Supporting (AI behaviour)**: deflection rate (oru manushan illaamalae close aana cases-oda share), escalation rate (oru manushan-kitta hand over pannadhoda share), sample panna set-la correct-resolution rate.
- **Guardrails**: mosamaagak koodaadha vishayangal, complaints, thappaana actions, cost per request, languages alladhu regions muzhusum fairness maadhiri.

Idhula irukkura trap, oru supporting metric-a primary-aa promote panradhu. Assistant oru manushan-kitta poradha kashtamaakkinaalae deflection rate eridum. Ungalaiyae kettukkonga: "Users nilaimai mosamaagumbodhum indha number era mudiyumaa?" Aamaannaa, adhu unga primary metric illa.

### Evals-um launch bar-um

Eval-naa thirumba thirumba oattakkoodiya oru test: real cases-oda oru fixed set, ovvonnukkum oru sariyaana outcome, ovvoru thadavaiyum orae maadhiri score panradhu. Interview-la naalu vishayam sollunga:

1. **Cases enga irundhu varudhu.** Pazhaiya real cases, kashtamaanadhaiyum, neenga support panra ovvoru language-aiyum, aboorvamaana aanaa serious-aana types-aiyum serthu sample pannadhu.
2. **Yaar label panraanga.** Senior support agents maadhiri domain experts, oru case-kku rendu labellers, disagreements-a oru lead theerthu vaippaaru. Avanga agreement rate, indha task manushangalukkae evvalavu kashtamnu sollum.
3. **Periya alavula eppadi score panreenga.** Oru LLM-as-judge (rubric vachu outputs-a grade panra oru model) aayirakkanakkaana outputs-a score panna mudiyum, aanaa oru sample-a kaiyaala check pannunga, judge manushangalode othuppona mattum launch pannunga.
4. **Bar-a testing-kku munnaadiyae vainga.** Oru target score, innaikku irukkura baseline-oda (perumbaalum human team) oppittu, adhoda per solli vacha critical errors-kku zero tolerance. Meedhi 7%-la manushangala baadhikkira errors irundhaa, overall 93%-kku endha arthamum illa.

Appuram launch-oda ovvoru step-aiyum indha bar thaan gate pannum:

```flow
dir: LR
title: Eval-la irundhu rollout varaikkum
Offline eval | label panna real cases -[bar-a thottaa]-> Shadow mode | AI suggest pannum, manushanga decide pannuvaanga
Offline eval -[bar-a thodalainnaa]-> Fix panni re-test
Shadow mode -[manushangalode othupponaa]-> Pilot | 5% users, control group
Pilot -[metrics nilaichaa]-> Wider rollout
Pilot -[guardrail meerinaa]-> Roll back
```

### Cost per request

Interview-la exact prices thevaiyilla, aanaa kanakkoda shape therinjirukkanum:

> cost per request = (input tokens x input price + output tokens x output price) / 1,000,000

Neenga route panra models muzhusukkum idha blend pannunga (easy cases-kku oru chinna, malivaana model, hard cases-kku periya onnu), daily volume-aala perukkunga, rendu vishayathoda oppidunga: human baseline-oda cost, AI-oda mistakes-oda cost. Rendaavadhu oppeettai thaan perumbaalaana candidates miss pannuvaanga, adhu yen mukkiyamnu worked example kaattudhu. Oru real price-a sonnaa, adhu enga irundhu vandhadhu, endha thedhiyila check panneenganu sollunga; prices adikkadi maarum.

### Technical depth

Neenga code pannanumnu yaarum edhirpaarkka maattaanga. Trade-offs-a elimaiyaana vaarthaigalla explain pannanumnu edhirpaarppaanga. Udhaaranamaa: "Scheme rules sila vaarangalukku oru thadavai maarudhu, adhanaala current documents mela citations-oda retrieval use pannuven. Fine-tuning pazhaiya rules-a model-kkulla freeze pannidum, ovvoru change-kkum retraining venum." Vazhakkamaa varra matha topics: models yen hallucinate pannudhu, latency versus quality, agent-naa enna, adhukku yen limits venum, oru context window enna panna vidudhu.

### Responsible AI, Indhiya rules-a manasula vachu

Harm-a per solli-kittae ship panna mudiyumnu kaattunga. Yaar baadhikkappadalaam? Adha eppadi test pannuveenga? Enga oru manushan decide panraaru? Edha build panna marupeenga? Vazhakkamaana case studies-a thodura rules-a therinjukkonga:

- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** digital personal data-va cover pannudhu. Adhoda Rules November 2025-la notify aachu, notice, consent udpada perumbaalaana duties-kku 18-month lead time koduthirukkaanga, adhanaala customer data use panra endha design-layum consent-um purpose limits-um irukkanum.
- **Investment advice**-a SEBI regulate pannudhu: paid advice-kku podhuvaa investment adviser-aa registration venum, exemptions romba kurugiyadhu. Oru broking app-oda assistant-kku safe default, recommend panradhu illa, explain panradhu.
- **Languages-um devices-um** oru fairness issue: overall average-la mattum illaama, ovvoru language-kkum slow connections-kkum thaniyaa quality-a test pannunga.

### Pushback-a handle panradhu

"Costs 20% eriduchu" illanna "Users adha nambalai" maadhiri follow-ups, pressure-la neenga eppadi yosikkireenganu test pannudhu. Oru nalla shape: problem-a thirumba sollunga, ungakitta enna data irukkunnu kelunga alladhu assume pannunga, action edukkuradhukku munnaadi diagnose pannunga (volume-aa, mix-aa, leakage-aa?), orae oru specific change pannunga, endha result unga manasa maathumnu sollunga. Mudhal push-layae unga design-a kaividaadheenga, data illaama adha defend-um pannaadheenga.

### Behavioural stories

Anju STAR stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result) prepare pannunga: oru launch, oru failure, engineering-oda oru disagreement, neenga no sonna oru neram, muzhumaiyillaadha data-vode edutha oru decision. PM-kku maarura orutharoda strong AI story-oda shape idhu. Numbers illustrative thaan; unga sondha real numbers-a use pannunga.

- *Situation*: Chennai-la oru insurer-la claims staff, vaarathukku sumaar 2,000 emails-a sort panna naalu naal selavu pannaanga.
- *Task*: Avasaramaana hospital cases-a thappaa route pannaama, AI vachu emails-a route panra oru pilot-kku naan owner.
- *Action*: Rendu senior claims staff 300 pazhaiya emails-a label pannaanga. Naanga mudhallayae bar-a vachom: 90% sariyaana routing, avasaramaana cases-la onnu kooda thappaa route aagakkoodaadhu. Rendu vaaram shadow mode-la oattinom.
- *Result*: Perumbaalaana emails-kku sorting neram adhae naalukku kuranjadhu. Tamil-um English-um kalandha emails 70% neram thaan sariyaa route aachunnu shadow mode kaattuchu, adhanaala model improve aagura varaikkum avaigalai manushangakitta anuppinom.

Pudicha failure thaan story-a nambakkoodiyadhaa aakkudhu.

### Take-home assignments

Sila companies veettula solve panna oru case anuppuvaanga: oru chinna PRD (product requirements document), irukkura oru product-oda teardown, illanna deck-oda oru strategy kelvi, podhuvaa adhukkappuram oru live presentation-um kelvigalum irukkum. Avanga evvalavu neram edhirpaarkkuraanganu recruiter-kitta kettu adhukku pakkathulayae irunga. Oru strong submission chinnadhaa, decision-a munnaadi vachu irukkum: user-um problem-um, neenga reject panna options-oda oru recommendation, metrics-um launch bar-um, risks, assumptions-oda oru list. Draft panna AI tools use panradhu sagajam, aanaa ovvoru line-aiyum live-aa defend pannanum.

## Oru worked example

Kelvi: "Oru popular Indhiya food-delivery app-kku oru AI feature design pannunga."

Kavya-voda badhil, surukkamaa:

*Clarify*: "Users metros-layum chinna nagarangallayum irukkaanga, niraiya languages use panraanga, perumbaalum phones. Goal order frequency-aa, satisfaction-aa?" Interviewer "Neengalae choose pannunga"nu solraanga. Kavya: "Oru mosamaana order vandha customers-a thakka vachukkaradhu thaan goal-nu assume panren, yenna avanga thaan vittuppoga adhiga vaaippu irukku."

*Problem*: enna saappidanumnu thernthedukkuradhu, dietary needs, post-order issues-a list pannittu, post-order issues-a (missing items, wrong items, delays) thernthedukkuraanga: adikkadi nadakkudhu, valikkudhu, support-kku selavu adhigam.

*Yen AI*: "Complaints pala languages-la free text-aavum photos-aavum varudhu. Oru form users-a fixed categories-kkulla thalludhu, appavum photo-va padikka oru agent venum. AI rendaiyum padichu routine cases-a udanae resolve panna mudiyum."

*Experience*: assistant complaint-aiyum photo-vaiyum padikkudhu, order data-va check pannudhu, oru limit varaikkum refund offer pannudhu. User confirm panradhukku munnaadi enna nadakkumnu sariyaa kaattudhu. Hindi, Tamil, English-la work aagudhu, vithiyaasamaana edhuvum orae tap-la oru manushan-kitta pogum.

*Metrics*: clarify pannumbodhu vacha assumption inga thaan balan tharudhu.

- Primary: oru issue report panni 14 days-kkulla thirumba order panna customers-oda share. Idhu avanga sonna goal-la irundhu naeraa varudhu.
- Supporting: median time to resolution, deflection rate, escalation rate.
- Guardrails: wrong-refund rate (oru expert approve pannaadha refunds), wrong-denial rate, refund cost per order, satisfaction, adhae metrics language vaariyaa pirichu.

*Launch bar*: rendu senior support agents 500 pazhaiya complaints-a sariyaana outcome-oda label panraanga, disagreements-a support lead theerthu vaikkiraaru. Testing-kku munnaadiyae agree panna bar: adhae set-la human team-oda correct-resolution rate-a match panradhu. Thaniyaa, foreign object, allergen maadhiri food-safety complaints-la oru automatic denial kooda irukkakkoodaadhu; avai eppavum oru manushan-kitta thaan pogum.

*Cost*: Kavya oru back-of-envelope kanakku podraanga, ovvoru number-um illustration-kkaana assumption thaannu thelivaa solraanga:

| Item | Assumption | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume | Oru naalaikku 50,000 complaints, 60% AI handle pannudhu | Oru naalaikku 30,000 AI conversations |
| Tokens | Oru conversation-kku 6,000 input, 600 output | |
| Chinna model, 80% cases | Input, output-kku per million tokens $0.25, $1.50-nu vachukkonga | Oru conversation-kku $0.0024 |
| Periya model, 20% cases | Per million $2, $12-nu vachukkonga | Oru conversation-kku $0.0192 |
| Blended model cost | Oru dollar-kku sumaar ₹90 | Sumaar ₹0.52, retries-um checks-um serthu ₹1-nu vachukkalaam |
| Oru naalaikku model cost | 30,000 x ₹1 | ₹30,000 |
| Human baseline | Oru ticket-kku ₹35-nu vachukkonga | Adhae 30,000 tickets-kku ₹10.5 lakh |
| Wrong refunds | 30,000-la 3%-kku thappaana refund, average ₹150-nu vachukkonga | Oru naalaikku ₹1.35 lakh |

Avanga mudivu: "Token cost chinnadhu. Wrong-refund rate-la ovvoru oru point etramum oru naalaikku sumaar ₹45,000 cost aagudhu, muzhu model bill-a vida adhigam. Adhanaala launch decision wrong-refund rate-a poruthu thaan irukku, naan romba gavanamaa paarkkappora guardrail adhu thaan." Indha oru insight thaan oru AI PM answer-a oru generic answer-la irundhu pirikkudhu.

*Risks*: refund fraud (pudhu accounts-kku limits, photo checks, account history), thappaana denials (orae tap-la hand-over), language vaariyaa quality gaps (thani eval slices), photos, addresses-oda privacy (complaint-kku thevaiyaanadha mattum vachukkaradhu).

*Launch*: mudhalla shadow mode, appuram rendu nagarangalla 5% orders, oru control group-oda oppittu. Wrong-refund rate-o food-safety handling-o adhoda bar-a udachaa niruthunga.

Interviewer push back panraanga: "Pilot-la refund costs 20% eriduchu. Ippo enna?" Kavya action edukkuradhukku munnaadi diagnose panraanga: "Idhu adhiga complaints-aa, vera mix-aa, illanna leakage-aa? Refunds-a account age, complaint type vaariyaa pirichu, control group-oda oppiduven. Etram pudhu accounts-la irundhaa, avangaloda auto-refund limit-a kuraichu oru photo kattaayamaakkuven. 14-day repeat-order rate adhukku eedu kattura alavu eriyirundhaa automation-a kuraikka maatten. Control group-layum adhae etram therinjaa en manasa maathikkuven, yenna appo kaaranam AI illa."

## Podhuvaana thappugal

- **Naeraa solution-kku kudhikkiradhu.** Mudhalla clarify pannunga, goal pathi oru assumption sollunga, oru problem-a thernthedunga.
- **AI-kkaagavae AI.** Neenga yosicha simple option enna, adhu yen podhaadhunnu sollunga.
- **Thappaana badhilgalukku plan illaadhadhu.** Ovvoru design-layum action-kku munnaadi confirmation-um, oru manushan-kitta poga easy-aana vazhiyum venum.
- **Game pannakkoodiya primary metric.** Users kashtappadumbodhum chats, sessions, deflection eralaam. Primary metric goal-la irundhu varanum.
- **Context illaadha "95% accurate".** Endha cases-la, endha baseline-oda oppittu, endha errors eppavumae ethukka mudiyaadhadhunnu sollunga.
- **Results vandhappuram vacha launch bar.** Testing-kku munnaadiyae domain experts-oda bar-a agree pannunga, illanna adhukku arthamae illa.
- **Errors-oda cost illaadha token price.** Kanakkula model cost perumbaalum ellaathaiyum vida chinna number.
- **"Latest model use pannuvom."** Periya model oru quality plan illa; eval thaan.
- **Groups-a maraikkira averages.** Ovvoru language, region, device-kkum quality-a check pannunga.
- **Puridhal illaadha jargon.** Trade-off-a elimaiyaana vaarthaigalla explain pannunga, illanna andha term-a vittudunga.

## Practice

AI errors-a suthi design panna PAIR Guidebook-aiyum, product judgement-kku SVPG-oda four big risks-aiyum, Tech Interview Handbook-oda behavioural guide-aiyum thirumba paarunga.

1. Ettu steps-a use panni, moonu design kelvigalukku sathamaa badhil sollunga, ovvonnukkum 25 nimisham. Oru government services app-kku, oru hospital-oda patient app-kku, oru stock-broking app-kku AI feature try pannunga. Ungalaiyae record panni, step 4-kku appuram clock-a check pannunga.
2. Ovvonnukkum, sonna goal, primary metric, rendu guardrails, oru critical error-oda launch bar, unga assumptions-a label panna oru cost-per-request kanakku ezhudhunga.
3. Ovvoru badhilukkum oru follow-up-oda push back panna oru friend-kitta sollunga, diagnose-then-act shape-a practice pannunga.
4. Numbers-oda anju STAR stories prepare pannunga, kuranjadhu onnaavadhu AI alladhu data decision pathi irukkanum, ovvonnaiyum rendu nimishathukkulla sollunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Paarkkaadha oru AI product design kelvikku 25 nimishathula thelivaana structure, goal-la irundhu varra oru metric, oru launch bar, oru cost estimate, pushback-kku oru amaidhiyaana badhil idhellaam serthu badhil solla mudiyum.
