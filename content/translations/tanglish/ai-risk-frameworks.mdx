## Idhu enna

AI risk frameworks, AI risks-a eppadi kandupidikkanum, manage pannanum, avai control-la irukku-nnu eppadi prove pannanum-nu oru organisation-kku solludhu. Sila law, sila voluntary, onnu certify pannakkoodiyadhu. Indhiya teams podhuvaa anju frameworks-a sandhikkiraanga.

| Framework | Vagai | Ippodhaiya version (28 November 2026 annaikku check pannadhu) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU AI Act | Law, fines-oda | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744-aala amend aanadhu, 27 July 2026-la irundhu force-la irukku |
| NIST AI Risk Management Framework | Voluntary | AI RMF 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1, January 2023), koodave Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, July 2024). 1.0 revise aagittu irukku-nnu NIST solludhu; pudhu version edhuvum innum publish aagala |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Certify pannakkoodiya standard | ISO/IEC 42001:2023, first edition. Europe idha 2026-la endha maatramum illaama EN ISO/IEC 42001:2026-aa adopt pannucchu |
| Indhiyaavoda AI Governance Guidelines | Guidance | MeitY 5 November 2025 annaikku release pannucchu |
| RBI FREE-AI, model risk guidance | Regulated finance-kkaana guidance | FREE-AI committee report, 13 August 2025; draft model risk management guidance, 24 June 2026 (final version edhuvum kidaikkala) |

**EU AI Act** AI-a endha model use pannudhu-nnu vachu illa, adhu edhukku use aagudhu-nnu vachu pirikkudhu.

- **Banned practices**, social scoring, vulnerable aatkala exploit panra manipulation maadhiri. Omnibus, "nudifier" apps-kkum AI-generated child sexual abuse material-kkum oru ban-a serkkudhu.
- **High-risk uses**, Annex III-la list aanavai: workers-a hire panradhum manage panradhum, thaninabargaloda credit scoring, education access, essential services, sila biometrics, innum sila; koodave medical devices maadhiri regulated products-oda safety part-aa irukkura AI (Annex I). Duties: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, users-kkaana instructions, human oversight, appuram accuracy, robustness, security.
- **Transparency duties** (Article 50): oru chatbot, aatkal oru AI kooda pesuraanga-nnu avangalukku sollanum, generated illa manipulated content mark pannappadanum.
- **Minimal risk**: spam filters, drafting aids. Staff-kku basic AI literacy-a thavira koodudhalaa edhuvum illa.

General-purpose models-a uruvaakkuravangalukku thani duties irukku. Enga base aagiyirundhaalum, thannoda system EU market-la vachirukkura illa thannoda output EU-la use aagura endha company-kkum indha Act pogum. Fines, banned practices-kku 35 million euros illa worldwide turnover-la 7% varaikkum, matha perumbaalaana breaches-kku 15 million euros illa 3% varaikkum pogum.

**Dates mukkiyam**, yenna indha Act kattam kattamaa switch on aagudhu:

| Date | Enna apply aaga aarambikkudhu |
| --- | --- |
| 1 August 2024 | Act force-kku varudhu |
| 2 February 2025 | Banned practices; staff-kku AI literacy duty |
| 2 August 2025 | General-purpose AI models-kkaana duties; EU governance bodies |
| 2 August 2026 | Article 50 transparency duties, chatbot disclosure maadhiri |
| 2 December 2026 | Pudhu nudifier ban; 2 August 2026-kku munnaadiye market-la irukkura generative systems-kku marking grace period mudiyudhu |
| 2 December 2027 | Annex III uses-kku, hiring, credit maadhiri, high-risk duties (2 August 2026-la irundhu thalli vachadhu) |
| 2 August 2028 | Regulated products-kkulla irukkura AI-kku high-risk duties (2 August 2027-la irundhu thalli vachadhu) |

Indha delay oru holiday illa: buyers ippave evidence kekkuraanga, oru varusha quarterly fairness reports-a deadline-kku oru maasam munnaadi uruvaakka mudiyaadhu.

**NIST AI RMF** niraiya buyers share panra oru vocabulary. Adhoda naalu functions: **govern** (policies, roles, culture), **map** (context, risks), **measure** (tests, metrics), **manage** (prioritise panni act panradhu). Large language models mela build panra teams adhoda **Generative AI Profile**-kku map panraanga; adhu confabulation (nambikkaiyaa solra thappaana output), data privacy, information security, harmful bias, human-AI configuration (aatkal system-a alavukku meeri nambuuradhu) maadhiri 12 risks-a list pannudhu.

**ISO/IEC 42001** oru AI management system-a describe pannudhu: policy, roles, risk assessment, impact assessment, controls, internal audit, improvement. Accredited bodies idhukku edhiraa certify panraanga (ISO/IEC 42006:2025 avangaloda rules-a set pannudhu), ISO/IEC 42005:2025 impact assessment-kku guide pannudhu. Oru certificate unga process velai seiyudhu-nnu kaattum; endha oru model-um fair-aa illa safe-aa irukku-nnu adhu prove pannaadhu.

**Indhiyaa**-vula AI-kku-nnu thani law illa. MeitY-oda guidelines ezhu principles-a ("sutras") set pannudhu, people first, fairness and equity, accountability, understandable by design maadhiri; oru AI Governance Group, AI Safety Institute-oda testing, oru national AI incident database-a recommend pannudhu. Binding rules erkanave irukkura laws-la irundhu varudhu: amend aana IT Rules (20 February 2026-la irundhu) synthetic content-kku label podanum-nu solludhu, DPDP Act personal data-va cover pannudhu, adhoda Rules-la perumbaalaana duties May 2027-la aarambikkudhu. Finance-la, RBI-oda FREE-AI report 26 non-binding recommendations kodutthucchu, board approve panna oru AI policy, AI models-oda oru inventory maadhiri. June 2026 draft model risk guidance final aanadhum, banks-kittaiyum NBFCs-kittaiyum inventories, independent validation, human oversight-a require pannum.

Indha lesson-la, Bengaluru-la oru HR-tech company thannoda resume-screening assistant-a thannoda mudhal European client-kku thayaar panradha follow panrom.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### 1. Model-a illa, use-a classify pannunga

System enna decide pannudhu illa influence pannudhu, yaaraip patthi, oru thappu evvalavu mosamaa irukkum-nu kelunga. Adhe model emails draft pannumbodhu minimal risk, job applicants-a rank pannumbodhu high risk.

```flow
title: EU AI Act classification-oda mudhal pass
Use-a describe pannunga -> Banned practice-aa?
Banned practice-aa? -[aamaa]-> Niruthunga | redesign pannunga
Banned practice-aa? -[illai]-> Regulated product-oda safety part-aa?
Regulated product-oda safety part-aa? -[aamaa, third-party checks thevai]-> High risk | muzhu duties
Regulated product-oda safety part-aa? -[illai]-> Annex III area-vaa?
Annex III area-vaa? -[aamaa]-> Aatkala profile pannudhaa?
Aatkala profile pannudhaa? -[aamaa]-> High risk
Aatkala profile pannudhaa? -[illai]-> Narrow illa preparatory task mattumaa?
Narrow illa preparatory task mattumaa? -[illai]-> High risk
Narrow illa preparatory task mattumaa? -[aamaa]-> High risk illa | document panni register pannunga
Annex III area-vaa? -[illai]-> Aatkal kooda pesudhaa illa content generate pannudhaa?
Aatkal kooda pesudhaa illa content generate pannudhaa? -[aamaa]-> Transparency duties
Aatkal kooda pesudhaa illa content generate pannudhaa? -[illai]-> Minimal risk
```

Inga gotcha Article 6(3). Oru Annex III system high risk-la irundhu thappikkiradhu, adhu oru narrow procedural illa preparatory task pannaa, mudinja human work-a improve pannaa, illa past decisions-la irundhu vilagaradha human review-kku flag pannaa mattum thaan, adhuvum decision-a materially influence pannaama irundhaa. Aatkala profile panra system, candidates illa borrowers-a score panradhu maadhiri, final call yaaru eduthaalum eppavume high risk thaan. Exception-a nambi irundhaa, yen-nu document pannanum, appavum EU database-la register pannanum.

### 2. Neenga provider-aa deployer-aa-nnu mudivu pannunga

High-risk systems-kku, yaaru enna pannanum-nu idhu thaan mudivu pannudhu.

| Role | Yaaru | Mukkiya duties |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider | Adha build panni thannoda per-la market panravanga | Design duties (risk management, data governance, logging, documentation), quality management system, conformity assessment, CE marking, EU database registration, post-market monitoring, incident reporting, EU-kku veliya base aagiyirundhaa oru EU authorised representative |
| Deployer | Adha thannoda sondha velaiyila use panravanga | Instructions-padi use panradhu, adhigaaram ulla trained overseers, kuranjadhu aaru maasam vachirukkura logs, workers-kkum baadhikkappadura aatkalukkum solradhu; adhu oru public body illa public-service provider-aa irundhaa, illa credit score pannaa illa life illa health insurance-kku price pottaa, oru fundamental-rights impact assessment |

Hiring, credit udpada perumbaalaana Annex III uses-kku conformity assessment internal thaan: provider thaane check panni document pannikkiraanga, notified body edhuvum illaama. System-a rebrand panra, adhoda purpose-a maatthura, illa adha substantially modify panra oru deployer provider aagiduraanga.

### 3. Ore risk register vachukkonga

Ovvoru risk-kkum (wrong output, bias, privacy breach, prompt injection, over-reliance, misuse), likelihood, impact, controls, oru owner, date potta evidence-a record pannunga. Ore register ella framework-kkum map aagum: oru "unfair ranking" row, EU AI Act-la data governance-um accuracy-um, NIST-la map-um measure-um, ISO/IEC 42001-la oru risk assessment. Adha data-vaa vachukkonga, oru serious risk-kku control, owner illa fresh evidence illai-nnaa oru script review-a fail pannattum:

```python
from datetime import date

LEVEL = {"low": 1, "medium": 2, "high": 3}
RISKS = [
    {"id": "R1", "risk": "Unfair ranking by college tier", "likelihood": "medium", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["college names removed from input", "quarterly fairness eval"],
     "owner": "Head of data science", "evidence_date": date(2026, 9, 30)},
    {"id": "R2", "risk": "Candidate data sent to a vendor that trains on it", "likelihood": "low", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["zero-retention contract"], "owner": "", "evidence_date": None},
    {"id": "R3", "risk": "Recruiters accept shortlists without reading them", "likelihood": "high", "impact": "high",
     "controls": [], "owner": "Head of product", "evidence_date": date(2026, 5, 2)},
]

def review(risks, today=None, max_age_days=120):
    today = today or date.today()          # a default of date.today() would freeze at import time
    findings = []
    for r in risks:
        score = LEVEL[r["likelihood"]] * LEVEL[r["impact"]]   # 1 to 9
        if score < 3:
            continue
        if not r["controls"]:
            findings.append(f"{r['id']}: score {score} with no controls")
        if not r["owner"]:
            findings.append(f"{r['id']}: no owner")
        if not r["evidence_date"] or (today - r["evidence_date"]).days > max_age_days:
            findings.append(f"{r['id']}: evidence missing or older than {max_age_days} days")
    return findings

for f in review(RISKS, today=date(2026, 11, 28)):
    print(f)
```

28 November 2026 annaikku idhu print pannudhu:

```text
R2: no owner
R2: evidence missing or older than 120 days
R3: score 9 with no controls
R3: evidence missing or older than 120 days
```

R1 pass aagudhu: controls, oru owner, 59 naal munnaadiyaana evidence irukku. Ovvoru control-um run aagura edho onna point pannanum (oru eval, oru CI test, oru alert, oru scheduled review); edhaiyume point pannaadha control verum oru ennam thaan.

### 4. Group vaariyaa fairness-a test pannunga, neenga plan pannaadha groups udpada

Oru labelled test set-la groups-kku idaiyila selection rates-a compare pannunga. Oru common flag **four-fifths rule**: top group-oda rate-la 80%-kku keezha select aagura oru group-a investigate pannanum. Idhu US employment guidance-la irundhu varudhu (1978 Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures), anga idhu oru rule of thumb thaan, EU AI Act-laiyo Indhiya law-laiyo idhu legal test illa. Idha oru alarm-aa paarunga, verdict-aa illa.

```python
from collections import defaultdict

def by_group(rows, key):
    """rows: dicts with candidate fields plus 'selected' and 'qualified' (bools, from the labelled set)."""
    g = defaultdict(lambda: {"n": 0, "sel": 0, "qual": 0, "qual_sel": 0})
    for r in rows:
        s = g[key(r)]
        s["n"] += 1; s["sel"] += r["selected"]; s["qual"] += r["qualified"]
        s["qual_sel"] += r["selected"] and r["qualified"]
    rates = {k: v["sel"] / v["n"] for k, v in g.items()}
    top = max(rates.values())
    for k, v in sorted(g.items()):
        ratio = rates[k] / top if top else 1.0
        tpr = v["qual_sel"] / v["qual"] if v["qual"] else float("nan")
        note = "  <- below 0.8, investigate" if ratio < 0.8 else ""
        note += "  (small group)" if v["n"] < 100 else ""
        print(f"{k:<22} n={v['n']:<4} selected {rates[k]:>4.0%}  qualified picked {tpr:>4.0%}  ratio {ratio:.2f}{note}")
```

"Qualified picked"-naa model shortlist panna qualified candidates-oda share; selection rates samamaa irundhaalum, oru group-la adhigamaana qualified aatkala miss panra model-a adhu maraikkalaam. `key` argument-a vachu tier-um gender-um maadhiri combinations-a test pannalaam. Moonu traps experienced teams-aiye maattividudhu:

- **Proxies.** Oru column-a remove pannaa adhoda signal pogaadhu. PIN code, city, school board, free text-la English fluency, illa resume-la irukkura gap years, region, caste, class illa gender-kku badhilaa nikkalaam. Group oru input illai-nnaalum outcomes-a group vaariyaa test pannunga.
- **Intersections.** Oru model ovvoru attribute-kkum thaniyaa fair-aa theriyalaam, aanaa oru combination-kku unfair-aa irukkalaam, tier-3 colleges-la irundhu varra pengal maadhiri.
- **Small groups.** 60 perla irundhu edutha oru rate chance-aalaye pala points nagarum. Mudivukku varradhukku munnaadi innum adhiga cases collect pannunga.

Indhiyaavula gender, state illa region, input language, college tier, urban vs rural-a check pannunga.

### 5. Effort-a tier-kku ettha maadhiri vainga

Ovvoru system-oda tier-aiyum record pannunga, use maarumbodhu thirumba paarunga, udhaaranamaa oru internal tool customers-kkaaga vishayangala decide panna aarambikkumbodhu.

| Tier | Vachirukka vendiya artefacts |
| --- | --- |
| Minimal | Oru page system card, normal security review, staff AI training |
| Transparency | Andha card, koodave disclosure text-um content marking-um, product-la test pannadhu |
| High risk | Muzhu register, technical documentation, schedule-padi group vaariyaa fairness evals, oversight records-um override rates-um, logs, incident drills, post-market monitoring plan |

Evidence-a engineering-oda side effect aakkunga: ovvoru eval report-aiyum adhoda date, model version-oda save pannunga, logs-a query panra maadhiri vainga, approvals-a record pannunga, design nyaabagatthula fresh-aa irukkumbodhe system card-a ezhudhunga.

## Oru worked example

Bengaluru-la oru HR-tech company Indhiya employers-kku oru resume-screening assistant-a vikkudhu. November 2026-la Netherlands-la oru retailer store staff-a shortlist panna adha use panna virumbuuraanga. Keezha irukkura numbers illustration-kku mattum thaan.

**Classify.** Applicants-a screen panradhu oru Annex III employment use, thaninabargala score panradhu profiling, adhanaala final call yaaru eduthaalum idhu high risk. Duties 2 December 2027-la irundhu apply aagum, aanaa client sign panradhukku munnaadiye evidence kekkuraanga.

**Roles-a piringa.** Bengaluru company thaan provider; retailer thaan deployer.

| Bengaluru company panna vendiyadhu | Dutch retailer panna vendiyadhu |
| --- | --- |
| Technical documentation-um oru quality management system-um | Trained recruiters moolamaa, instructions-padi use panradhu |
| Internal conformity assessment, EU declaration of conformity, CE marking | Unmaiyaana human oversight, tool-oda ranking-a reject panra adhigaarathoda |
| EU database registration-um oru EU authorised representative-um | Thannoda control-la irukkura logs-a kuranjadhu aaru maasam vachirukkiradhu |
| System-kkullaye build panna logging; instructions for use | Tool use aagudhu-nnu thannoda works council-kkum candidates-kkum solradhu |
| Post-market monitoring-um serious incident reporting-um | Serious incidents-aiyum risks-aiyum provider-kku report panradhu |

**Register-a build pannunga.** Team 14 risks-a list pannudhu, idhellaam udpada:

- **Unfair ranking**, gender, college illa region vachu: scoring-kku munnaadi names, photos, addresses remove pannappadudhu; group vaariyaa quarterly fairness evals.
- **Recruiters-oda over-reliance:** shortlists kaaranangaloda varudhu, ovvoru rejection-um review aagudhu, override rates monitor aagudhu.
- **Data protection:** EU candidates-kku GDPR notices-um retention-um, hiring cycle mudinjadhum deletion, May 2027-kku munnaadi DPDP Rules-a meet panna oru plan.

**Measure pannunga.** Label panna 400 pazhaya applicants-la, tier-1 college candidates 30%-laiyum tier-3 candidates 19%-laiyum shortlist aanaanga: 11-point gap, ratio 0.63. Scoring input-la irundhu college names-a remove pannadhukku appuram:

```text
tier-1                 n=200  selected  30%  qualified picked  55%  ratio 1.00
tier-3                 n=200  selected  28%  qualified picked  52%  ratio 0.93
```

Ippo gap 2 points. Tier-um gender-um vachu piricchaa:

```text
tier-1, men            n=120  selected  30%  qualified picked  55%  ratio 0.98
tier-1, women          n=80   selected  30%  qualified picked  55%  ratio 0.98  (small group)
tier-3, men            n=140  selected  31%  qualified picked  57%  ratio 1.00
tier-3, women          n=60   selected  22%  qualified picked  40%  ratio 0.71  <- below 0.8, investigate  (small group)
```

Tier-3 colleges-la irundhu varra pengal four-fifths-kku keezha vizhuraanga, aanaa andha group-la 60 per mattum thaan irukkaanga. Team oru owner-odaiyum oru date-odaiyum oru open finding-a log pannudhu, innum 300 labelled cases collect pannudhu, gap years, kadaisi job-oda city maadhiri proxies-a check pannudhu.

Retailer andha pack-a accept panraanga: system card, instructions for use, group vaariyaa eval reports, logging design, register. Adhe pack Indhiya enterprise clients kooda nadakkura risk reviews-aiyum surukkudhu.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Build mudinjadhukku appuram ezhudhura governance.** Fairness tests, logging, oversight design-aiye shape pannanum.
- **Indhiyaavula irukkom-nu EU AI Act-a ignore panradhu.** System illa adhoda output EU-la use aanaa idhu apply aagum.
- **Delay-a relief-aa ninaikkiradhu.** December 2027 legal date; buyers ippave evidence kekkuraanga.
- **Technology-a vachu classify panradhu.** "Idhu verum chatbot thaan"-nu sonnaa edhuvume sollala; output edhukku use aagudhu-ngiradhu thaan tier-a mudivu pannudhu.
- **"Oru human thaan decide panraanga"-nnu nambuuradhu.** Profiling oru system-a high risk-laye vaikkudhu, orumurai kooda override pannaadha reviewer oversight illa.
- **Client thaan provider-nnu assume panradhu.** Neenga build panni unga per-la vitthaa, provider duties ungalodadhu.
- **Ore oru murai, overall numbers-la mattum check panna fairness.** Group vaariyaavum combinations vaariyaavum, oru schedule-padi measure pannunga.
- **Four-fifths-a law-nnu ninaikkiradhu, illa oru column-a drop panradha fix-nnu ninaikkiradhu.** Onnu oru US rule of thumb; innonnu proxies-a appadiye vittududhu.

## Framework overkill-aa irukkura neram

- **Team productivity tools**, sensitive illaadha data-la: oru short card-um normal security-um podhum.
- **Prototypes**, real users-aiyo real decisions-aiyo orubodhum reach pannaadhavai.
- **Internal drafting tools**, oru aal eppavume output-a review panni adhukku poruppetthukkira idatthula.

Aatkalaip patthi vishayangala decide panra illa strong-aa influence panra systems-kku muzhu set-a vachukkonga: hiring, lending, insurance, health, education, public services.
