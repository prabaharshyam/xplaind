## Idhu enna

AI governance frameworks, AI risks-a eppadi identify pannanum, manage pannanum, accountable-aa irukkanum-nu organisations-ku sollum. Indian teams-ku naalu mukkiyam:

- **EU AI Act**: AI uses-a risk padi classify panra oru law. Sila uses banned (authorities social scoring panradhu maadhiri). **High-risk** uses, hiring-la AI, credit scoring, education access, essential services, sila safety components maadhiri, obligations-a kondu varum: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy, robustness. Limited-risk uses-ku (chatbots, generated content) transparency duties. General-purpose AI models-oda providers-ku avanga sondha obligations. Company enga irundhaalum, EU market-la vacha illa EU-la use aana systems-ku idhu apply aagum, obligations pala varushangal-la padi padiyaa varum.
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**: naalu functions-oda oru voluntary US framework: **govern** (policies, roles, culture), **map** (context-um risks-um), **measure** (testing-um metrics-um), **manage** (prioritise panni action edukkuradhu). Idhu oru common vocabulary-aa romba use aagudhu.
- **ISO/IEC 42001**: oru AI management system-ku international standard. Security-ku ISO 27001 maadhiri, organisations idha vachu audit aagi certify aagalaam.
- **India-oda AI Governance Guidelines** (MeitY, November 2025): safe, trusted AI-ku principles, makkal mudhalla, fairness, accountability, transparency maadhiri, institutions-ukkum industry-kkum recommendations-oda. Idhu thani AI law-a uruvaakkala; IT Act, DPDP Act maadhiri ippo irukkura laws-aiyum sector regulators-aiyum nambudhu. RBI maadhiri regulators avanga sectors-ku sondha guidance kuduththirukkaanga.

| EU AI Act tier | Examples | Idhu enna artham |
|---|---|---|
| Banned | Authorities social scoring | Anumathi illa |
| High risk | Hiring, credit, education access, essential services | Muzhu obligations |
| Limited | Chatbots, generated content | Transparency duties |
| Minimal | Spam filters, drafting aids | Extra onnum illa |

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

**Use case-a classify pannunga.** System enna mudivu pannudhu illa influence pannudhu, yaarukku, oru thappu evvalavu serious? Internal emails-ku drafting assistant low risk; loan applicants-a illa job candidates-a rank panra oru model EU AI Act-la high risk, ella idathulayum sensitive.

**Risk register build pannunga.** Ovvoru risk-kum (thappaana output, bias, privacy breach, security attack, over-reliance, misuse), likelihood, impact, controls, owner-a record pannunga. Frameworks overlap aagum; oru register ellaathukkum map aagalaam.

**Controls-a requirements-ku map pannunga.** Typical controls, avai enga varum:

- documented purpose-um limits-um (oru AI system card);
- data governance: sources, quality checks, personal data handling;
- groups-layum languages-layum fairness-oda serntha evaluation;
- override panna unmaiyaana authority irukkura human oversight;
- logging-um audit trails-um;
- users-ku transparency ("neenga oru AI assistant-kitta pesureenga");
- incident response-um post-market monitoring-um;
- models-ukkum prompts-ukkum change management.

**Intentions illa, evidence.** Auditors-um risk committees-um artefacts-a virumbuvaanga: eval reports, group padi test results, logs, approval records, training records. Idha normal engineering-oda pagudhiyaa produce pannunga.

**Proportionality.** Low-risk systems-ku light documentation podhum; high-risk-ku muzhu set thevai. Low-risk tools-a over-govern panna effort veen, adoption-um kuraiyum.

**Frameworks compare:**

| Framework | Type | Enga kadikkum | Mukkiya ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EU AI Act | Law | EU-la use aagura systems | Risk tier padi obligations |
| NIST AI RMF | Voluntary framework | Romba use aagura vocabulary, US buyers | Govern, map, measure, manage |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Certify panna mudiyura standard | Enterprise procurement | Oru audit aana AI management system |
| India-oda AI Governance Guidelines | Principles-um recommendations-um | Indian deployments, sector rules-oda | Safe, trusted, accountable AI |

**Oru risk register row, muzhusaa:**

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Risk | College tier padi candidates-a unfair-aa rank panradhu |
| Likelihood and impact | Naduthara likelihood, high impact |
| Controls | Scoring input-la irundhu college names-a neekkunga; ovvoru quarter-um group padi fairness eval |
| Owner | Head of data science |
| Evidence | Quarterly fairness report; neekkapatta fields-a kaattura input schema |
| Mapped to | EU AI Act data governance, accuracy duties; NIST measure, manage |

**Pogira pokkulayae evidence build panradhu.** Romba cheap-aana compliance, nalla engineering-oda side effect. Ovvoru eval report-aiyum adhoda date-oda, version-oda save pannunga, logs-a query panna mudiyura padi vainga, approvals-a record pannunga, design fresh-aa irukkum podhae system card-a ezhudhunga. Maadhangal kazhichu oru auditor-ku idha thirumba build panna try pannaa romba neram edukkum, kammiyaa nambikkai tharum.

**Oru script check panna mudiyura register.** Risks-a data-vaa vainga, score pannunga, oru serious risk-ku controls, owner, sameebaththiya evidence illaina review-a fail pannunga:

```python
from datetime import date

LEVEL = {"low": 1, "medium": 2, "high": 3}
RISKS = [
    {"id": "R1", "risk": "Unfair ranking by college tier", "likelihood": "medium", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["remove college names from input", "quarterly fairness eval"],
     "owner": "Head of data science", "evidence": "fairness-2026-Q3.pdf", "evidence_date": date(2026, 9, 30)},
    {"id": "R2", "risk": "Candidate data sent to a vendor that trains on it", "likelihood": "low", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["zero-retention contract"], "owner": "", "evidence": None, "evidence_date": None},
]

def review(risks, today=date.today(), max_age_days=120):
    findings = []
    for r in risks:
        score = LEVEL[r["likelihood"]] * LEVEL[r["impact"]]
        if score >= 3:
            if not r["controls"]:
                findings.append(f"{r['id']}: score {score} with no controls")
            if not r["owner"]:
                findings.append(f"{r['id']}: no owner")
            if not r["evidence_date"] or (today - r["evidence_date"]).days > max_age_days:
                findings.append(f"{r['id']}: evidence missing or older than {max_age_days} days")
    return findings
```

**Code-la fairness checks.** Oru screening illa ranking system-ku, labelled test set-la groups-a vachu outcomes-a compare pannunga. Rendu simple numbers romba help pannum:

```python
from collections import defaultdict

def by_group(rows):
    """rows: dicts with 'group', 'selected' (bool) and 'correct' (bool)."""
    g = defaultdict(lambda: {"n": 0, "selected": 0, "correct": 0})
    for r in rows:
        s = g[r["group"]]
        s["n"] += 1; s["selected"] += r["selected"]; s["correct"] += r["correct"]
    rates = {k: v["selected"] / v["n"] for k, v in g.items()}
    top = max(rates.values())
    for k, v in g.items():
        ratio = rates[k] / top if top else 1
        flag = "  <- below four-fifths" if ratio < 0.8 else ""
        print(f"{k:<16} n={v['n']:<4} selected {rates[k]:.0%}  accuracy {v['correct'] / v['n']:.0%}  ratio {ratio:.2f}{flag}")
```

India-la check panna worth-aana groups-la adhikka gender, state illa region, input-oda language, college tier, urban vs rural irukkum. Sinna groups noisy rates-a tharum, adhanaala conclusions edukkradhukku munnaadi ovvoru group-kum podhumaana cases collect pannunga, selection rates-oda serthu group padi accuracy-yum paarunga.

**Risks-a tests-oda link pannunga.** Register-la ovvoru control-um odura edhavadhu onnai point pannanum: oru eval, oru CI test, oru dashboard alert, oru scheduled review. Oru control-a entha onnai kaattavum mudiyalainaa, adhu oru intention, control illa.

**Proportionality, practice-la.** Ovvoru system-kum tier-a ezhudhi vainga, andha tier-ku enna documentation thevai-nu. Low-risk tools-ku oru-page card-um light register-um; high-risk-ku muzhu register, fairness evals, human oversight records, incident drills. Use maarum podhu tier-a thirumba paarunga, example oru internal tool customers-ku mudivu edukka aarambichaa.

## Oru worked example

Bengaluru-la oru HR-tech company, Indian employers-ku oru resume-screening assistant-a vikkudhu, adhoda mudhal European client venumnu virumbudhu.

Classification: job candidates-a screen panradhu EU AI Act-la high-risk use, India-layum sensitive.

Team 14 risks-oda oru register build pannudhu. Examples: gender, college, region padi unfair ranking (controls: groups-la fairness evaluation, scoring-ku munnaadi names, photos, addresses-a neekkuradhu, quarterly bias audits); recruiters-oda over-reliance (controls: tool reasons-oda oru shortlist suggest pannum, recruiters ovvoru rejection-aiyum review pannanum, override rates monitor aagum); data protection (controls: DPDP, GDPR-padi consent-um retention-um, hiring cycle-ku appuram deletion).

Ovvoru control-aiyum EU AI Act-oda high-risk obligations-ukkum NIST functions-ukkum map pannuraanga, technical documentation, oru system card, oru logging design produce pannuraanga. Oru fairness evaluation, adhe skills irukkura tier-3 colleges candidates-ku kammi shortlist rate-a kaattudhu; team scoring input-la irundhu college names-a neekkudhu, gap 11 points-la irundhu 2-aa kuraiyudhu.

European client-oda procurement team documentation pack-a othukkudhu. Adhe pack Indian enterprise clients-oda reviews-aiyum kuraikkudhu.

| Finding | Maatram | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adhe skills irukkura tier-3 college candidates-ku kammi shortlist rate | Scoring input-la irundhu college names neekkapattadhu | Gap 11 points-la irundhu 2-aa kuraindhadhu |
| Recruiters review illaama shortlists-a othukkuraanga | Ovvoru rejection-um review aaganum; override rates monitor | Unmaiyaana oversight |
| Data eppavum vachirukkaanga | Hiring cycle-ku appuram deletion | DPDP, GDPR retention-a satisfy pannum |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Build-ku appuram frameworks-a paperwork-aa paakkuradhu.** Fairness testing, oversight maadhiri controls design-a shape pannanum.
- **Ellaathukkum oru level governance.** Effort-a risk-oda match pannunga.
- **India-la irukkaradhunaala EU AI Act-a ignore panradhu.** EU-la use aagura systems-ku idhu apply aagum.
- **Overall accuracy-la mattum fairness-a test panradhu.** Group padi measure pannunga, language-um region-um serthu.
- **Peyaralavula mattum oversight.** Override panna mudiyaadha illa pannaadha human oversight illa.
- **Odura onnaiyum kaattaadha controls.** Ovvoru control-aiyum oru test, eval, alert-oda link pannunga.
- **Launch-la oru thadava mattum fairness check.** Data-vum users-um maarum; schedule-padi thirumba pannunga.
- **Sinna groups-la irundhu conclusions edukkuradhu.** Mudhalla podhumaana cases collect pannunga.

## Framework overkill-aa irukkura neram

- **Sensitive illaadha data-la personal illa team productivity tools**: oru sinna card-um normal security practice-um podhum.
- **Unmaiyaana users-a thodaadha prototypes.**
- **Kuraiyaana impact irukkura internal drafting tools**, oru aal eppavum output-a review panni own pannum idathula.

Makkal pathi mudivu edukkura illa strong-aa influence panra systems-ku muzhu register, mapping, evidence use pannunga: hiring, lending, insurance, health, education, public services.

## Demo-la irundhu production-ku

- **Register-a repository-la vainga**, ovvoru release-ku munnaadiyum review check-a odunga.
- **Group padi fairness evals-a schedule pannunga**, results evidence-aa store aagattum.
- **Ovvoru serious risk-kum oru owner peyar sollunga**, register-a quarter-ku oru thadava review pannunga.
- **Ovvoru system-oda tier-aiyum**, adhukku enna thevai-nu record pannunga.
- **Controls-a oru thadava map pannunga**, ungaloda buyers-um regulators-um use panra frameworks-ku, mapping-a thirumba use pannunga.

## Practice pannunga

EU AI Act summary, NIST AI RMF overview-a padinga, AI Incident Database-la sila cases-a paarunga.

1. Neenga therinja moonu AI use cases-a (internal chatbot, loan pre-screening tool, exam proctoring tool) EU AI Act risk tier padi, reasons-oda classify pannunga.
2. Adhula onnukku kuraindhadhu ettu risks-oda, controls-um owners-um-oda oru risk register ezhudhunga.
3. Ovvoru control-aiyum oru EU AI Act obligation-ukkum oru NIST AI RMF function-ukkum map pannunga.
4. Ungaloda systems-la onnukku labelled set-la by_group check-a odi, numbers enna kaattudhu-nu oru paragraph ezhudhunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Ungaloda system edhu tier-la varudhu, adhukku enna thevai, ungakitta enna evidence irukku-nu oru risk committee-kku explain panna mudiyum.
