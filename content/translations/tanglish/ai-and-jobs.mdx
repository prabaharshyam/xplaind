## Idhu enna

"AI en velaiya edukkumaa?" India-la AI pathi makkal romba kekkura kelvi, final-year students-la irundhu senior managers varaikkum. Nermaiyaa sonna, idhu unga job title-a pathi illa, unga **tasks**-a pathi.

Oru job-na tasks-oda kattu. Customer-support executive routine queries-ku badhil solraanga, kovama irukkura customers-a handle panraanga, couriers-oda coordinate panraanga, notes ezhudhuraanga. Oru teacher lessons plan panraanga, ideas-a explain panraanga, homework mark panraanga, class-a paathukuraanga. Indha tasks-la sila-vatrula AI romba nalladha, sila-vatrula help pannum, sila-vatrula use-ae illa.

```flow
title: Oru job-na tasks-oda kattu
Oru job -> Task A | routine, text based
Oru job -> Task B | check pannanum
Oru job -> Task C | nambikkai, judgement
Oru job -> Task D | physical, kuzhappamaana idam
Task A -> AI-ku exposed
Task B -> AI help pannum
Task C -> Safe
Task D -> Safe
```

International Labour Organization (ILO) generative AI-yum velaiyum pathi panna research-la, **clerical work** (data entry, routine drafting, record keeping) thaan romba exposed-nu kandupidichaanga. Matha perumbaalaana occupations-la, AI oru pagudhi velaiya help pannura **augmentation** nadakka thaan vaaippu adhigam; oru aal-ku badhila AI muzhu velaiya seiyura **automation** illa.

Indha lesson-la Arun-a follow pannuvom. Hyderabad-la customer-support executive, avanga company oru AI assistant-a konduvaraanga. Lesson mudiyum podhu, entha job-aiyum tasks-a pirichu, ovvonnu evvalavu exposed-nu judge panni, useful-aa irukka oru plan podalaam.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Exposure-na tasks pathi

Entha job-aiyum judge panna, adhoda tasks-a list pannunga, ovvonnukkum moonu kelvi kelunga:

1. Indha task-a AI innaiku, podhumaana quality-la seiyuma?
2. Oru aal check panna, AI oru pagudhi seiyuma?
3. AI-kitta illaadha vishayam thevaiyaa: udambu, nambikkai, accountability, kuzhappamaana nilaimaiyil judgement?

Routine-aana, rules follow panra, perumbaalum text illa numbers irukkura tasks thaan romba exposed. Makkal, physical work, poruppu irukkura tasks thaan kammi exposed.

| Task vagai | Examples | Exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine text, data | Data entry, standard emails, simple translation, form filling | Adhigam |
| Drafting, summarising | Reports, meeting notes, code-oda first drafts | Adhigam, aana output-a check pannanum |
| Judgement-oda analysis | Supplier-a select panradhu, oru problem-a diagnose panradhu | Naduthara: AI help pannum, oru aal decide pannum |
| Makkal, nambikkai | Negotiate panradhu, counselling, team-a manage panradhu, client-ku sell panradhu | Kammi |
| Physical work | Electrical repairs, nursing care, construction | Kammi |

### Oru role-ku moonu vidha mudivugal

Tasks-a ellaam serthu paartha, oru role moonu vazhila onnula pogum:

- **Automated.** Perumbaalaana tasks-a AI-aala seiyalaam, adhanaala kammiyaana aalgal podhum. Idhu perumbaalum routine, text-based, rules follow panra velaikku nadakkum.
- **Augmented.** AI sila pagudhi-a paathukum, makkal innum adhigam, vegamaa illa nallaa seiyaraanga. Professional jobs-ku idhu thaan romba common mudivu.
- **Created.** AI-ya sutthi pudhu roles varum: AI applications build panra engineers, businesses-kulla AI-a deploy panravanga, adha test panra evaluators, AI product managers, automation specialists, trainers.

| AI evvalavu job-a seiyum | Kammi | Naduthara | Adhigam |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enna nadakkum | Perumbaalum safe | Augmented | Automated |
| Examples | Nurse, sales | Analyst, coder | Data entry |

### Headlines-a vida change yen mella nadakkudhu

AI oru task-a seiyum-nu irundhaalum, organisations adopt panna neram edukkum. Process maathanum, staff-a train pannanum, regulators-a satisfy pannanum, quality check pannanum, customer data-va protect pannanum, selavukku justify pannanum. Nambikkai mella thaan valarum, especially banking, healthcare, government-la. Adhanaala maatram oru raathirila varaadhu; varushakkanakkula, team team-aa varum.

### India-la idhukku enna artham

India-la periya IT services, business-process, back-office workforce irukku. Adhula routine coding, support, data processing maadhiri sila velaigal exposed. Adhe workforce ulagam muzhukka irukkura companies-ku AI build panni deploy panna nalla position-layum irukku, India job postings-la AI skills-ku demand vegamaa valarndhutte irukku. Rendumae oru neraththula unmai.

### Useful-aa irukkradhu

Ella mudivu-layum naalu vishayam help pannum:

1. **Ippo irukkura job-la AI-a nallaa use pannunga.** AI tools-a nallaa use panravanga, use pannaadha colleagues-a vida adhigam mudippaanga.
2. **Judgement, relationships, poruppu pakkam nagarunga.** Indha tasks makkal kittayae irukkum.
3. **AI-ku complement-aana skills valarthukkunga.** AI output-a check panradhu, domain knowledge, clear-aa communicate panradhu, data-vodu velai seiyradhu.
4. **AI-focused role** unga-ku set aanaa, adha consider pannunga. Xplaind-la irukkura paths ovvoru role-ku enna thevai-nu kaattum.

## Oru worked example

Arun Hyderabad-la oru e-commerce company-la customer support-la velai seiyraanga. Company oru AI assistant-a konduvaraanga, adhu chat-la order-status, return-policy kelvikku badhil solludhu.

Avanga vaaram munnaadiyum pinnaadiyum, neraththula evvalavu share-nu paarunga:

| Task | Munnaadi | Aaru maadham aprom |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine status, return queries | 60% | 10% (assistant escalate panradhu mattum) |
| Complex complaints | 25% | 45% |
| Warehouses, couriers-oda coordinate panradhu | 15% | 20% |
| Assistant-oda answers-a review panni improve panradhu | 0% | 25% |

Assistant perumbaalaana routine queries-a eduthukitta, adhanaala Arun-oda team sirusaachu; aalgal vittu poi, adhukku pudhusa edukkaama thaan, layoffs-naala illa. Arun-oda velai maraiyala; nagarndhudhu. Ippo avanga neram perumbaalum kashtamaana cases-layum assistant-a nallaa aakkuradhulayum pogudhu: adhu pazhaya return policy-a sonnaa, sariyaana answer-a adhoda knowledge base-la ezhudhuraanga.

Company-oda internal AI tools course-a eduthaanga, assistant eppo escalate pannanum-nu eppadi decide pannudhu-nu kathukkitaanga, aaru maadham kazhichu "support automation specialist"-nu pudhu role-ku poitaanga, assistant-a, adhoda workflows-a improve panraanga.

Avanga colleague Divya assistant-a use panna maruthaanga. Avanga velai innum automate aagaadha, kurainjutte irukkura sila cases-ku mattum aachu. Rendu perukkum idaiyila difference thiramai illa. Arun valarndhutte irukkura tasks pakkam nagarndhaanga, adhu thaan.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Job titles-la yosikkiradhu.** "Accountants replace aaiduvaanga"-nu sonna adhula onnum theriyaadhu. Job-a tasks-a pirichu, ovvonnayum paarunga.
- **Rendu extreme-a nambradhu.** "AI ella jobs-aiyum edukkum", "edhuvum maaraadhu" rendumae perumbaalaana roles-ku thappu. Maatram thaan nadakka vaaippu adhigam.
- **Enna aagudhu-nu kaathirukkradhu.** Ippo AI use panna kathukkuradhu, unga role automate aanaalum, augment aanaalum, onnume aagaadhaalum help pannum.
- **Pudhu roles-a ignore panradhu.** Neraya pudhu AI roles-ku domain knowledge-um AI skills-um thaan thevai, computer science degree illa.
- **Judgement illaama tools mattum kathukkradhu.** Employers AI output-a check panni result-ku poruppu edukka theriyaravangala virumbuvaanga, prompts type panna theriyaravangala mattum illa.
- **Confident-aana predictions-a nambradhu.** Job losses, gains-ku exact numbers yaarukkum theriyaadhu. Appadi sollravangala jaakkirathaiyaa paarunga.

## Practice pannunga

Keezha link panni irukkura, generative AI-yum jobs-um pathi ILO-oda research summary-a padinga, apram OpenAI Academy-la AI at work pathi oru session try pannunga.

1. **Unga job-a map pannunga.** Ippo irukkura illa aaga virumbura job-oda mukkiya tasks-a, ovvonnukkum approx neraththula evvalavu share-nu serthu list pannunga. Ovvonnayum "automate aagalaam", "help kidaikkum", "onnum aagaadhu"-nu mark pannunga.
2. **Romba help kidaikkura task-a test pannunga.** Indha vaaram andha task-a AI assistant-oda seinga. Evvalavu neram micham aachu-nu, enna check illa fix panna vendi irundhuchu-nu note pannunga.
3. **Oru skill select pannunga.** Adutha moonu maadhathula AI-kooda serthu unga value-va adhigam aakkura oru skill-a, adhukku mudhalla use panna pora resource-a peru sollunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Ungaloda job-a tasks-a pirikka mudiyum, edhu maarum-nu, yen-nu explain panna mudiyum, munnaadi irukka unga plan-a sollamudiyum.
