## Idhu enna

"AI en velaiya parichudumaa?" India-la final-year students-la irundhu senior managers varai makkal AI pathi adhigamaa kekkura kelvigalla idhuvum onnu. Nermaiyaana badhil: adhu unga job title-a poruthadhu illai, unga **tasks**-a poruthadhu.

Oru job-na pala tasks-oda thoguppu. Oru customer-support executive routine queries-kku badhil solraaru, kobamaana customers-a samaadhaanappaduthuraaru, couriers pinnaadi follow up panraaru, notes ezhudhuraaru. Oru teacher lessons plan panraanga, ideas-a vilakkuraanga, homework thiruthuraanga, oru class-a paathukkuraanga. Idhula sila tasks-la AI romba nallaa pannum, siladhula udhavum, siladhukku perisaa payan illa.

Indha lesson moonu vaarthaigal mela nikkudhu:

- **Exposure**-na innaikku oru task-a AI evvalavu seiya mudiyum allathu adhukku evvalavu udhava mudiyum-ngradhu. Idhu enna possible-nu kaattura alavu, prediction illa.
- **Automation**-na oru aalukku badhilaa AI andha task-a seiyudhu.
- **Augmentation**-na AI task-oda oru pagudhiya seiyudhu, andha aal innum adhigamaa, vegamaa allathu nallaa seiyuraanga.

```flow
title: Oru job-na tasks-oda thoguppu
Oru job -> Task A | routine, text saarndhadhu
Oru job -> Task B | check panna vendiya drafting
Oru job -> Task C | trust, judgement
Oru job -> Task D | physical, kuzhappamaanadhu
Task A -> High exposure | perumbagudhiya AI seiyum
Task B -> High exposure, check pannanum | AI draft pannum, oru aal check pannuvaanga
Task C -> Low exposure | AI thayaar pannum, oru aal mudivu pannuvaanga
Task D -> Low exposure, physical | idathula oru aal venum
```

Exposure pathina sirandha global evidence International Labour Organization (ILO)-la irundhu varudhu. Poland-oda research institute NASK kooda serndhu May 2025-la veliyitta adhoda refined index, kittathatta 30,000 work tasks-kku score pottuchu. Ulagam muzhukka sumaar **naalula oru worker** generative AI-kku edho oru alavu exposure irukkura occupation-la irukkaanga, aanaa **3.3%** per mattum thaan highest-exposure group-la irukkaanga-nnu adhu kandupidichudhu. **Clerical velai** (data entry, routine drafting, record keeping) thaan adhigamaa exposed. Aangalai vida pengal adhigamaa exposed: pengaloda jobs-la 4.7% highest group-la varudhu, aangalukku 2.4%. ILO-voda mukkiya mudivu: aalunga innum thevaippadradhaala, perumbaalaana jobs redundant aagradhai vida **transform** aaga thaan vaaippu adhigam.

Indha lesson, Hyderabad-la customer-support executive-aa irukkura Arun-a follow pannudhu; avaroda company oru AI assistant-a kondu varudhu. Kadaisiyila endha job-aiyum tasks-aa pirikkavum, ovvonnum evvalavu exposed-nu judge pannavum, job headlines-a critical-aa padikkavum, useful-aa irukka oru plan podavum ungalaala mudiyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Exposure-na tasks pathinadhu

Endha job-aiyum judge panna, adhoda tasks-a thoraayamaana nera share-oda list pannunga, ovvonnukkum moonu kelvi kelunga:

1. Innaikku indha task-a AI podhumaana quality-la seiya mudiyumaa?
2. Oru aal check panna, AI idhula oru pagudhiya seiya mudiyumaa?
3. AI-kitta illaadha vishayangal idhukku venumaa: oru udambu, trust, poruppu (accountability), allathu kuzhappamaana soozhnilaiyila judgement?

| Task vagai | Udhaaranangal | Exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine text, data | Data entry, standard emails, simple translation, form filling | High |
| Drafting, summarising | Reports, meeting notes, code-oda first drafts | High, aanaa output-a check pannanum |
| Judgement koodiya analysis | Oru supplier-a thernthedukkuradhu, oru problem-a diagnose panradhu | Medium: AI udhavum, oru aal mudivu pannuvaanga |
| Manushangalum trust-um | Negotiate panradhu, counselling, oru team-a manage panradhu, oru client-kku sell panradhu | Low |
| Physical velai | Electrical repairs, nursing care, construction | Low |

### Exposure-na job loss illa

Oru exposed task-a, vera sila vishayangalum unmaiyaa irundhaa thaan AI-kitta kuduppaanga. Experts naalu filters-a check pannuvaanga:

```flow
title: Oru exposed task unmaiyilaeye AI-kitta pogumaa?
Exposed task -> Quality podhumaa?
Quality podhumaa? -[illai]-> Manushangakittaye irukkum | AI konjam udhavalaam
Quality podhumaa? -[aamaa]-> Oru aalai vida malivaa?
Oru aalai vida malivaa? -[illai]-> Manushangakittaye irukkum
Oru aalai vida malivaa? -[aamaa]-> Thappu nadandhaa safe-aa?
Thappu nadandhaa safe-aa? -[illai]-> AI draft pannum, oru aal check pannuvaanga
Thappu nadandhaa safe-aa? -[aamaa]-> Anumadhiyum trust-um irukkaa?
Anumadhiyum trust-um irukkaa? -[illai]-> AI draft pannum, oru aal check pannuvaanga
Anumadhiyum trust-um irukkaa? -[aamaa]-> AI-kitta kudukkappadum
```

Niraiya naadugalai vida India-la cost innum mukkiyam, yenna inga aalungalukku aagura selavu kuraivu. London-la automate panna thagundha oru task, Lucknow-la kaiyaala seiyuradhu innum malivaa irukkalaam. Risk-um mukkiyam: oru thappaana order-status reply oru erichal thaan, aanaa oru thappaana loan decision allathu marundhu dose appadi illa. Rules-um trust-um medhuvaa thaan nagarum, mukkiyamaa banking, healthcare, government-la; anga oru kurippitta aal poruppaa irukkanum-nu regulators-um customers-um edhirpaarppaanga.

Organisations-kku neramum venum. Process-a maathanum, staff-kku train pannanum, customer data-va paadhukaakkanum, savings-a prove pannanum. Adhanaala maatram podhuvaa orae raathiriyila illaama, varushakkanakkula, team team-aa thaan varum.

### Oru role-kku moonu outcomes

Exposed tasks-oda share mattum oru role-oda outcome-a mudivu pannaadhu. Exposed velaiya AI eduthukkitta piragu enna micham irukku, velaiyoda alavu koodudhaa-nnu adhai poruthadhu.

- **Automated.** Kittathatta ellaa tasks-um exposed, micham irukkuradhu konjam thaan, adhanaala role marainjudum allathu vera oru role kooda merge aagidum. Idhu mukkiyamaa pure routine roles-kku nadakkum, forms-a mattum type panra oru data-entry operator maadhiri.
- **Augmented.** Niraiya tasks exposed-aa irukkalaam, aanaa micham irukkuradhu thaan job-oda core: judgement, trust, poruppu. Aalunga andha core-la adhiga neram selavazhippaanga. Idhu thaan romba common-aana outcome.
- **Created.** AI-a suthi pudhu roles varudhu: AI applications-a build panra engineers, businesses-kkulla adhai deploy panravanga, adhai test panra evaluators, workflows-a redesign panra automation specialists, AI product managers.

Expert-oda gotcha: **oru role augment aanaalum team surungalaam.** Udhaaranamaa, 10 per irukkura oru team than nerathula 60%-a routine queries-la selavazhikkudhu-nnu vachukkunga, oru assistant adhula perumbaalaanadhai eduthukkudhu. Queries ennikkai appadiye irundhaa, adhe velaikku 6 allathu 7 per podhumaa irukkalaam. Free aana nerathai valara, vegamaa badhil solla, allathu pudhu services kudukka company use pannaa, 10 peraiyum vachukkalaam. Exposure mattum illa, demand-aiyum gavaninga.

### Yaarukku laabam: experience-um skill-um

AI help ellaaraiyum orae alavu uyarthaadhu. Oru periya field study-la, economists Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li, Lindsey Raymond, business software vikkira oru Fortune 500 company-la oru AI assistant rollout aanappo 5,000-kkum adhigamaana customer-support agents-a follow pannaanga. Working-paper version-la, assistant vachirundha agents average-aa oru mani nerathukku sumaar **14%** adhiga issues-a solve pannaanga. Romba pudhusaana, kuraivaana skill irundha agents-kku sumaar **moonula oru pangu** laabam kidaichudhu; romba experienced aanavangalukku konjam thaan. Indha study 2025-la Quarterly Journal of Economics-la publish aachu.

Kaaranathai purinjukkiradhu useful. Adhiga productive aana agents-oda practices-a assistant beginners-kku parappuchu-nnu authors solraanga. Experts erkanavae appadi thaan velai senjaanga. Adhanaala routine velaiyila pudhu staff-kkum experienced staff-kkum idaiyila irukkura gap-a AI kuraikkum, experience-oda value assistant handle panna mudiyaadha cases pakkam nagarum.

### Entry-level nerukkadi

Adhe pattern thaan students-kku periya risk-a uruvaakkudhu. AI nallaa seiyura routine tasks thaan freshers munnaadi kathukkitta tasks.

Stanford Digital Economy Lab researchers (Brynjolfsson, Bharat Chandar, Ruyu Chen) millions kanakkaana workers-oda US payroll data-va study pannaanga. Avanga paper-oda August 2026 version-la, adhigamaa AI-exposed aana jobs-la 22-la irundhu 25 vayasu varai irukkura workers-oda employment, kuraivaa exposed aana jobs-la irukkura adhe vayasu workers kooda serndhu valarndhirundhaa enga irukkanumo adhai vida sumaar **19%** keezha irundhuchu; authors-oda mudhal wave data-la idhu 15%-aa irundhuchu. Naalu details mukkiyam:

- Indha sarivu perumbaalum **kuraivaana hiring**-aala vandhuchu, ilam workers adhigamaa velaiya vittu ponadhaala illa.
- Adhe jobs-la irundha vayasula mootha workers nilaiyaa irundhaanga allathu valarndhaanga.
- Paper-oda mundhaiya versions-la, maatram jobs ennikkaiyila thaan vandhuchu, sambalathula illa.
- AI-a tasks-a **automate** panna use panra jobs-la thaan sarivu adhigamaa irundhuchu, **augment** panna use panra jobs-la illa.

Gavanamaa padinga. Idhu US data; AI thaan indha sarivukku kaaranam-nu idhu proof illa, oru early warning-nu thaan authors solraanga. AI kooda thodarbudaiya, economy muzhukka paravalaana job displacement edhaiyum thaanga paarkkalai-nnum avanga solraanga. Aanaa indha direction Indian freshers-kku oru thelivaana signal: judgement-a neenga seekkiramae kaattanum, yenna routine velaiyila adhai kathukka ungalukku sambalam kudukkura employers kuraivaa iruppaanga. Juniors-a hire panradhaiyae muzhusaa niruthura teams thangalukkae keduthal pannikkiraanga, yenna innaikku juniors thaan naalaikku seniors.

### India-la idhoda artham

India-la periya IT services, business-process, back-office workforce irukku, adhoda entry-level velaiyila niraiya, adhaavadhu routine coding, testing, support, data processing, exposed-aa irukku. October 2025-la NITI Aayog, NASSCOM, BCG kooda serndhu thayaaricha *Roadmap for Job Creation in the AI Economy*-a veliyittuchu. India-voda tech services sector-kku 2031-kkulla ganisamaana job losses-oda achuruthal irukku-nnu adhu echarikkudhu, aanaa adutha anju varushathula 4 million varai pudhu jobs-a uruvaakkura vaaippum irukku-nnu solludhu; oru National AI Talent Mission-aiyum propose pannudhu. Idhellaam possibilities, forecasts illa. Endha pakkam pogum-ngradhu aalungalum companies-um evvalavu vegamaa reskill panraanga-ngradhai niraiya poruthadhu.

### Oru headline-a eppadi padikkiradhu

Perumbaalaana AI-and-jobs headlines anju vagai evidence-la edho onnu mela nikkudhu. Ovvonnum vera vera kelvikku badhil solludhu.

| Evidence vagai | Udhaaranam | Adhu enna sollum | Adhu enna sollaadhu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exposure index | ILO, May 2025 | Endha tasks-la AI udhava mudiyum | Jobs pogumaa, eppo |
| Field study | Support-agent study, 2025 | Oru company-la oru tool enna pannuchu | Unga company-layum appadiye nadakkumaa |
| Payroll data | Stanford young-worker study | Ippo hiring-kku enna nadakkudhu | Kaaranam, urudhiyaa |
| Employer survey | WEF Future of Jobs Report, January 2025 | 1,000-kkum adhigamaana employers 2030-kkulla enna edhirpaarkkuraanga (170 million jobs uruvaagum, 92 million displace aagum) | Adhu nadakkumaa-ngradhu; headline totals-la AI mattum illa, ellaa kaaranangalum kalandhirukku |
| Scenario report | NITI Aayog, October 2025 | Oru sector-kku outcomes-oda range | Unmaiyila enna nadakkum |

"AI X jobs-a azhichudum"-nu oru headline sonnaa, adhu endha vagai-nnu kelunga. Exposure-na loss illa, survey-na measurement illa, scenario-na promise illa.

### Unga company-la gavanikka vendiya signals

Maatram unga role-a thodradhukku munnaadi podhuvaa chinna signals-aa theriyum. Idhellaam gavaninga:

- Unga team-oda tasks mela oru AI tool-oda pilot, "time saved" allathu "tickets deflected"-kku targets kooda.
- Unga role-la vittu poravanga idathukku pudhu aal edukkaama irukkuradhu, allathu orae oru role-kku mattum hiring freeze.
- Team size appadiye irukkumbodhu ovvoruthharukkumaana targets koodradhu.
- "AI output-a review panradhu", "knowledge base-a maintain panradhu" maadhiri pudhu tasks job descriptions-la varradhu.
- Reviews-la managers "productivity per head" pathi pesuradhu.

Idhula edhuvum unga job pogudhu-nnu artham illa. Ovvonnum task mix maarudhu-nnu thaan artham; mudhalla nagaravangalukku thaan thangaloda pudhu tasks-a thernthedukkura vaaippu kidaikkum.

### Useful-aa irukkiradhu

Kittathatta ellaa outcome-layum naalu moves udhavum:

1. **Mudhalla unga sondha velaiyila AI-a use pannunga.** Neenga adikkadi seiyura, routine-aana, check panna easy-aana, thappu nadandhaalum periya izhappu illaadha oru task-la aarambinga. Unga periya task-kku judgement thevaippattaa adhula aarambikkaadheenga.
2. **Check panradhula nallaa aagunga.** Tool enga fail aagudhu-nnu kathukkittu, adhoda errors-a pidinga. Prompts type panna mattum therinjavangalai vida, AI output-kku poruppethukkakkoodiyavangalukku thaan value adhigam.
3. **Judgement, relationships, poruppu pakkam nagarunga.** Exceptions, escalations, customers, decisions-kku neengalae munvaanga.
4. **Ungalukku porundhinaa oru AI-focused role-a yosinga.** Niraiya roles-kku computer science degree illa, domain knowledge-um AI skills-um thaan venum. Ovvoru role-kkum enna thevai-nnu Xplaind-la irukkura paths kaattum.

### Unga manager kitta pesuradhu

Oru chinna conversation kelunga, evidence kondu ponga: "Rendu vaaramaa routine replies-kku assistant-a try pannen. Oru naalaikku sumaar oru mani neram michamaachu, rendu thappaana policy badhilgalaiyum pidichen. Andha nerathai escalations-layum assistant-oda badhilgalai improve panradhulayum selavazhikkanum-nu aasaippadren." Neenga tool-a use panreenga, adhai check panna mudiyum, free aana nerathukku oru plan vachirukkeenga-nnu idhu kaattudhu. Yaaraavadhu sollum varai kaathirukkiradhai vida idhu romba strong-aana position.

## Oru worked example

Arun, Hyderabad-la oru e-commerce company-la 12 per irukkura team-la customer support-la velai seiyuraar. Order-status, return-policy kelvigalukku chat-la badhil solra oru AI assistant-a company kondu varudhu.

Idho avaroda vaaram, munnaadiyum pinnaadiyum, nerathoda share-aa. Indha numbers oru illustration thaan, measure pannadhu illa.

| Task | Munnaadi | Aaru maasathukku appuram |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine status, return queries | 60% | 10% (assistant escalate panradhu mattum) |
| Complex complaints | 25% | 45% |
| Warehouses, couriers kooda coordinate panradhu | 15% | 20% |
| Assistant-oda badhilgalai review panni improve panradhu | 0% | 25% |

Arun-oda vaarathula arubadhu sadhaveedham exposed, irundhaalum avaroda role automate aagala, augment aachu. Micham irundhadhu (complaints, coordination, exceptions pathina judgement) thaan job-oda core, adhu valarndhuchu. Assistant oru pazhaiya return policy-a sonnappo, Arun sariyaana badhilai adhoda knowledge base-la ezhudhinaar. Adhoda mistakes pathi avar serthu vachirundha evidence-a use panni, assistant-oda escalations-a review panna vidunga-nnu avaroda manager kittayum kettaar.

Team unmaiyilaeye surunguchu. Order volume appadiye irundhuchu, adhanaala varushathula naalu per vittu ponappo, oruthharukku mattum thaan replacement eduthaanga; team 12-la irundhu 9-aa aachu. Yaaraiyum layoff pannala, aanaa pudhu support executives-a hire panradhu medhuvaachu.

Company-oda internal AI tools course-a avar eduthaar, assistant eppo escalate pannanum-nu eppadi decide pannudhu-nnu kathukkittaar, **support automation specialist**-ngra pudhu role-kku ponaar: assistant-aiyum adhai suthi irukkura workflows-aiyum improve panradhu thaan andha velai.

Kashtamaana case Sana-vodadhu: andha varusham hire panna orae fresher, ezhaavadhu maasathula join pannaanga. Arun nootrukkanakkaana easy-aana queries-la velaiya kathukkittaar. Sana-kku appadi kittathatta edhuvum kidaikkala, yenna adhaiyellaam ippo assistant handle pannudhu. Mudhal sila vaarangalla avangalukku complex complaints mattum thaan kuduthaanga, avanga romba kashtappattaanga. Team idhai moonu vazhiyila sari pannuchu:

- Sondhamaa escalations edukkuradhukku munnaadi, team-laye adhiga experience irukkura agent Kiran kooda rendu vaaram escalations-la **shadowing** pannaanga.
- Dhinamum oru senior kooda serndhu **20 assistant badhilgalai review panni**, thappaanadhai mark pannaanga; easy-aana queries-kku badhil solradhai vida idhu policies-a vegamaa kathu kuduthuchu.
- "Idhu yen escalate aachu"-ngra oru shared log-a team vachirundhuchu, adhanaala ovvoru tricky case-um adutha aalukku oru paadamaa aachu.

Teams-kkaana paadam: AI training ground-a eduthuttaa, neenga venumnae oru pudhu training ground-a build pannanum.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Job titles-la yosikkiradhu.** "Accountants replace aagiduvaanga"-nnu solradhu edhaiyum sollaadhu. Job-a tasks-aa pirichu, ovvonnaiyum paarunga.
- **Exposure-a job loss-aa ninaikkiradhu.** Exposure-na AI enna seiya mudiyum-ngradhu. Unmaiyila enna nadakkum-nu quality, cost, risk, rules, demand thaan mudivu pannum.
- **Rendu extreme-la onnai nambradhu.** "AI ellaa jobs-aiyum eduthukkum"-num "onnum maaraadhu"-num perumbaalaana roles-kku thappu thaan. Maatram thaan vara vaaippu adhigam, sila neram kuraivaana aalungaloda.
- **Team-a marandhudradhu.** Unga role augment aanaalum unga team hiring-a niruthalaam. Unga sondha tasks mattum illa, signals-aiyum gavaninga.
- **Periya task-la aarambikkiradhu.** Routine-aana, adikkadi varra, check panna easy-aana task-la aarambinga; adhiga neram edukkura task-kku judgement thevaippattaa adhula illa.
- **Kathukkiradhai AI-kitta vidradhu.** AI badhilgalai check pannaama copy panra freshers, employers ippo sambalam kudukkura judgement-a build pannavae maattaanga.
- **Confident-aa solra predictions-a nambradhu.** Edhirkaala job losses, gains pathina exact numbers yaarukkum theriyaadhu. Oru number endha vagai evidence-la irundhu varudhu-nnu kelunga.

## Practice pannunga

Keezha link panniyirukkura ILO-voda 2025 index summary-a padinga, OpenAI Academy-la AI at work pathina oru session-a try pannunga.

1. **Unga sondha job-a map pannunga.** Unga ippodhaiya allathu target job-oda mukkiya tasks-a, ovvonnukkum thoraayamaana nera share-oda, mottham 100% varra maadhiri list pannunga. Ovvonnaiyum high, medium allathu low exposure-nu mark pannunga, micham irukkuradhukku endha outcome (automated allathu augmented) porundhudhu-nnu sollunga.
2. **Oru task-a test pannunga.** Indha vaaram, ungaloda romba routine-aana, check panna easy-aana task-a oru AI assistant kooda pannunga. Evvalavu neram michamaachu, neenga sari panna vendiyirundha ovvoru error-aiyum note pannunga.
3. **Signals-a kandupidinga.** Mela sonna signals-la unga company-layo neenga join panna virumbura companies-layo theriyura edhaiyum ezhudhi vainga.
4. **Oru skill-a thernthedunga.** Adutha moonu maasathula AI kooda serndhu ungalai innum valuable aakkura oru skill-aiyum, adhukku neenga use pannappora mudhal resource-aiyum sollunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nnu eppo sollalaam:** unga sondha job-a tasks-aa pirikka mudiyum, edhu maara vaaippirukku yen-nu explain panna mudiyum, unga team-kku enna aagalaam-nu solla mudiyum, munnaadi irukka unga plan-a vivarikka mudiyum.
