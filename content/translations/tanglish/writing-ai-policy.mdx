## Idhu enna

Oru **AI policy**, oru organisation AI-a eppadi use pannudhu, eppadi build pannudhu-nnu rules podudhu. Company AI policy-a follow panradhu, AI-a safe-aa use panradhu pathina lessons employee pakkathai cover pannudhu: unga data-vai sort pannunga, licensed tool-a use pannunga, output-a check pannunga. Indha lesson mejaiyoda marupakkam. Endha tools approved, ovvonnum enna data paarkkalaam, risky uses-kku yaar sign off panraanga, edhaavadhu thappaa ponaa enna nadakkum-nu neenga thaan mudivu panreenga.

Oru employee oru rule-a purinjukkittaa podhum. Aanaa ezhudhuravarukku, ovvoru rule-um moonu tests-a pass pannanum:

1. **Oru pudhu employee oru nimishathula adhai oru real task-kku apply panna mudiyumaa?** "AI-a poruppaa use pannunga" fail. "Client code IDE assistant-kku mattum thaan poganum, opt-out list-la irukkura clients-kku adhuvum koodaadhu" pass.
2. **Oru veliyaal-kitta adhai defend panna mudiyumaa?** Oru client-oda security reviewer, oru auditor, allathu oru incident-kku appuram oru regulator.
3. **Adhu oru legal route-a vittu vaikkudhaa?** Safe-aana orey badhil "pannaadheenga"-naa, makkal unsafe-aana oru vazhiyai kandupidichuduvaanga.

Oru nalla mudhal policy **rendu mudhal naalu pakkam**, lawyers-kkaaga illaama employees-kkaaga ezhudhappattadhu, koodave oru pakka summary. AI tools-a **use** panra ellaaraiyum, AI features-a **build** panra teams-aiyum adhu cover pannum. Copy pannikka oru template idho; lesson-oda meedhi pagudhi ovvoru row-aiyum vilakkudhu.

| Section | Neenga enna mudivu panreenga | Oru sensible default | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope, principles | Yaarai cover pannudhu; 3 mudhal 5 commitments | Ellaa staff, contractors; poruppu manushangakitta thaan | Leadership sign pannum |
| Approved tools | Endha tools, endha settings-oda | Checklist-a pass panna tools mattum, per vaariyaa list | IT security |
| Tool vaariyaa data | Ovvoru tool-um endha data class-a paarkkalaam | Oru pakka matrix, koodave oru client opt-out list | Security, legal |
| Uses | Encouraged, restricted, prohibited | Ovvoru rule-kkum oru kaaranam, oru approved alternative | Policy owner |
| Exceptions | Eppadi kekkuradhu, evvalavu seekkiram badhil | Oru form, 3 working days-la badhil, log pannappadum | Policy owner |
| AI build panradhu | Launch-kku munnaadi enna irukkanum | Risk assessment, evaluation, oversight, monitoring | AI review group |
| Disclosure | AI involve aanadhai eppo sollanum | Chatbots thaanga AI-nu sollanum; real maadhiri irukkura AI media-kku label | Product, legal |
| Incidents | Enna report pannanum, yaarkitta, evvalavu seekkiram | 1 mani nerathukkulla security-kitta, nermaiyaana reports-kku blame illa | Security |
| Training, review | Ellaarum enna kathukkanum; policy eppo maarum | Real tasks-oda 30 nimisham; 6 maasathukku oru thadavai review | Policy owner |

Pune-la delivery head-aa irukkura Neha-vai neenga follow pannappoureenga; oru client-oda security review avanga company-a mudhal AI policy ezhudha vaikkudhu. Kadaisiyila oru policy-a draft pannavum, real tasks-la test pannavum, makkal adhai follow panraangalaa-nnu kaattavum ungalaala mudiyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Step 1: Erkanave enna nadakkudhu-nnu kandupidinga

Makkal erkanave enna use panraanga-nnu theriyura varai edhaiyum ezhudhaadheenga. Approve pannaadha use-a **shadow AI**-nu solvaanga, irukkuradhe theriyaadha oru tool pathi sensible-aa oru rule ezhudha mudiyaadhu. Naalu sources, romba cheap-aanadhula irundhu romba rich-aanadhu varai:

- **Licence consoles.** Company erkanave oru assistant-kku pay pannudhu-nnaa, adhoda admin page-la ethanai seats active-nu theriyum.
- **Network, proxy logs.** Therinja AI websites-kku ethanai visits-nu IT count panna mudiyum. Endha tools-nu theriyum, enna paste pannaanga-nnu theriyaadhu.
- **Expense claims, card statements.** Personal AI subscriptions-a thirumba claim panradhu unmaiyaana thevaikku oru strong sign.
- **Oru chinna anonymous survey.** Endha tools, endha tasks-kku, enna maadhiri data-oda, oru approved tool-la irundhu ungalukku enna venum?

Discovery-la kandupidikkira edhukkum thandanai illai-nnu ezhuthula promise pannunga, illai-nnaa neenga romba paarkka vendiya use-aiye makkal maraichuduvaanga.

Idhoda result oru **AI inventory**: use-la irukkura ovvoru AI tool, feature-um, adhoda owner, adhu paarkkura data, vendor, oru risk level, approval status-oda. Adhai update-aa vechukkonga. Oru client-oda security reviewer mudhalla kekkuradhu idhai thaan.

### Step 2: Yaar mudivu panraanga-nnu fix pannunga

Ovvoru mudivukkum oru aalai name pannunga:

| Mudivu | Yaar mudivu panraanga | Target turnaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oru pudhu tool-a approve panradhu | IT security, contract-kku legal kooda serndhu | 2 mudhal 3 vaaram |
| Client-facing bot maadhiri oru restricted use-a approve panradhu | Policy owner, allathu high risk-kku oru chinna AI review group | 1 mudhal 2 vaaram |
| Oru thadavaikkaana exception kudukkiradhu | Policy owner | 3 working days |
| Oru incident-a veliye report pannanumaa-nnu mudivu panradhu | Security, legal serndhu | Naatkal illa, mani nerangal |
| Policy-a maathuradhu | Policy owner, leadership sign pannum | 6 maasathukku oru thadavai, allathu oru trigger-kku appuram |

Indha nerangalai policy-laye ezhudhunga. Oru maasam kaanaama pora oru request, kekkuradhaiye niruththa makkalukku kathukkudukkum.

### Step 3: Checklist-oda tools-a approve pannunga

"Company data-la train pannaadha maadhiri configure panniyirukku"-ngradhu oru review-oda oru line thaan, muzhu review illa. Oru tool-a approve panradhukku munnaadi idhukkellaam ezhuthula badhil vaangunga:

1. **Training.** User maathakkoodiya oru setting moolamaa mattum illaama, contract moolamaave unga data training-la irundhu vilakkappattirukkaa?
2. **Retention.** Prompts, files, outputs evvalavu naal vechiruppaanga, oru admin adhai delete panna mudiyumaa?
3. **Location.** Data enga store, process aagudhu? Sila client contracts-la adhu India-kkullayo kurippitta regions-layo thaan irukkanum-nu irukkum.
4. **Access.** Vendor-la yaar unga prompts-a padikka mudiyum, vera endha companies (sub-processors) unga data-vai handle panraanga?
5. **Admin control.** Single sign-on, velaiyai vittu poravangalai remove panradhu, export pannakkoodiya audit logs, risky features-a off panna switches.
6. **Contract.** Confidentiality, oru data processing agreement, oru breach pathi vendor ungalukku evvalavu seekkiram sollanum.
7. **Maranjirukkura data paths.** Web search, plugins, email allathu drives-kkaana connectors, memory, agent modes, ovvonnum data-vai oru pudhu idathukku anuppudhu. Ungalukku thevaiyaanadhai mattum approve pannunga.

Badhilgalai inventory-la record panni, varushaa varusham allathu vendor than terms-a maathumbodhu thirumba review pannunga.

Appuram mundhaiya lessons-la vandha data classes-a vechu **data-by-tool matrix**-a ezhudhunga:

| Tool | Public | Internal | Confidential (client) | Personal data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise assistant | Aamaa | Aamaa | Client documents, aanaa code illa; opt-out clients-kku illa | Illa |
| Approved IDE-la irukkura code assistant | Aamaa | Aamaa | Client code; opt-out clients-kku illa | Illa |
| HR system-laye irukkura AI features | Aamaa | Aamaa | Illa | HR data, HR kaaranangalukku mattum |
| Free tools udpada vera endha tool-um | Aamaa | Illa | Illa | Illa |

**Client opt-out list** contracts-a oru engineer check pannakkoodiya onnaa maathudhu: endha clients third-party AI processing-a thadai panraanga, allathu kurippitta locations-la mattum allow panraanga. Legal adhai vechirukkum; ovvoru project start-layum delivery adhai check pannum.

### Step 4: Uses-a risk vaariyaa pirichu, makkal apply pannakkoodiya rules ezhudhunga

| Encouraged | Restricted (approval venum) | Prohibited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved tools-la drafting, summarising, research | Customer-facing chatbots, AI features | Unapproved tools-la confidential allathu personal data |
| Coding help, generated code-kku review-um licence check-um serthu | Hiring, appraisal, credit maadhiri manushanga pathina mudivugal | Real people-oda consent illaama senja deepfakes |
| Internal documents-a translate panradhu | Company systems-la action edukkakkoodiya agents | Manushanga mela legal effect irukkura muzhukka automated mudivugal |
| Ellaarkittayum sollittu, approved tool-la internal meetings-a transcribe panradhu | External calls-a record panradhu, marupakkam othukkittaa mattum | Endha prompt-layum secrets, passwords, API keys |

2026-la moonu pudhu rows-kku gavanam thevai:

- **Agents.** Email anuppa, code merge panna allathu panam move pannakkoodiya oru agent-kku, velaiyai mudikka thevaiyaana kuranja access, undo panna kashtamaana edhukkum munnaadi human approval, ovvoru action-kkum oru log venum. Oru chat tool-a approve pannaa adhoda agent mode-aiyum approve pannadhaa arththam illa.
- **Meeting transcription.** Oru sensible default: aarambathulaye ellaarkittayum sollunga, yaaru venumnaalum object pannalaam, transcript-a meeting-oda content maadhiriye treat pannunga.
- **Output-oda ownership.** Generated code, unga client thadai panra oru licence-la irukkura open-source code maadhiriye irukkalaam. Endha third-party code-kkum neenga use panra licence scanning-aiye run pannunga, AI-assisted work-a yaar own panraanga-nnu client contracts-la check pannunga.

**Disclosure defaults:** chatbots thangaloda mudhal message-laye thaanga AI-nu sollanum; real maadhiri irukkura AI images, audio, video-kku label podanum; contract allathu client kettaa, AI draft panna client deliverables-kku label podanum.

Ovvoru rule-aiyum naalu pagudhiyaa ezhudhunga: **rule, kaaranam, approved alternative, example.** Udhaaranamaa: "IDE assistant thavira vera endha tool-layum client code-a paste pannaadheenga. Kaaranam: niraiya client contracts adhai thadai pannudhu. Badhilaa: oru made-up snippet-oda pattern-a describe pannunga. Example: client-oda code-a paste pannaama, oru REST API-a eppadi paginate panradhu-nnu kelunga."

### Step 5: Veliyila irundhu varra rules-a serunga

Unga policy sattathaiyo unga contracts-aiyo vida loose-aa irukka mudiyaadhu. Idhu November 2026 nilavarappadi oru summary, legal advice illa; ippodhaiya text-a counsel-kitta confirm pannikkonga.

| Source | Adhu enna kekkudhu | Unga policy enna solludhu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client contracts | Perumbaalum third-party processing, locations allathu AI use-a limit pannum | Client opt-out list, project start-la check pannappadum |
| CERT-In directions (April 2022) | Data breaches, data leaks udpada listed cyber incidents-a gavanicha 6 mani nerathukkulla CERT-In-kku report pannanum | Staff 1 mani nerathukkulla security-kitta report pannanum; CERT-In pathi security-um legal-um mudivu pannum |
| DPDP Act, Rules (November 2025-la notify aanadhu) | May 2027-la irundhu perumbaalaana business kadamaigal apply aagum; oru personal data breach pathi baadhikkappattavangalukku thaamadham illaama solradhum, 72 mani nerathukkulla Data Protection Board-kku oru detailed report kudukkiradhum idhula adangum | Personal data sonna purpose-kku mattum; incident route erkanave legal-a reach pannudhu |
| IT Rules amendment (20 February 2026-la irundhu amalla) | Real maadhiri irukkura AI-made audio, images, video-kku platforms label podanum | Neenga oru platform nadathunaa legal review pannum; matha ellaarum default-aa andha maadhiri media-kku label podanum |
| EU AI Act | Oru non-EU firm-oda AI system EU-la offer pannappattaa allathu adhoda output anga use aanaa apply aagum. Chatbot disclosure kadamaigal 2 August 2026-la irundhu; oru 2026 amendment, hiring, credit maadhiri high-risk uses-kkaana kadamaigalai 2 December 2027-kku thalli vechudhu | Ungalukku EU users allathu clients irundhaa, legal unga uses-a Act-oda map pannum |

Vazhakkamaa panra thappu: veli deadline-a vida neelamaana oru internal deadline. "24 mani nerathukkulla report pannunga"-ngradhu 6 mani nera kadamaiyai mudiyaadhadhaa aakkidum. Internal clock thaan ellaathaiyum vida kurugiyadhaa irukkanum.

```flow
dir: LR
title: Oru AI incident, gavanikkiradhula irundhu close panradhu varai
Yaaro oru problem-a gavanikkiraanga -> 1 mani nerathukkulla security-kitta report | Nermaiyaana reports-kku blame illa
1 mani nerathukkulla security-kitta report -> Security, legal triage
Security, legal triage -[listed cyber incident allathu personal data]-> Veli reports | 6 mani nerathukkulla CERT-In; thevaikku etha maadhiri DPDP, client notices
Security, legal triage -[internal mattum]-> Fix panni record pannu
Veli reports -> Fix panni record pannu
Fix panni record pannu -> Policy allathu tool settings-a update pannu
```

Principles-kku, MeitY-oda India's AI Governance Guidelines (November 2025)-aiyum NIST AI Risk Management Framework-aiyum use pannunga. Indha guidelines pudhu AI sattam edhaiyum uruvaakkala; irukkura sattangalaiyum sector regulators-aiyum thaan nambudhu, adhanaala banking, insurance allathu health-la unga regulator-oda rules adhukku mela varum.

### Step 6: Neenga build panra AI features-kku oru launch gate vainga

Oru AI feature launch aagradhukku munnaadi, risk-kku etha maadhiri oru package kelunga: oru risk assessment, evaluation results, oru human oversight plan, monitoring, oru named group-oda approval. Oru internal summariser-kku oru pakkam podhum. Oru customer-facing credit assistant-kku accuracy, fairness-kkaana evaluation, adhai overrule pannakkoodiya oru aal, adhu thappaa irukkumbodhu enna panradhu-nnu oru plan venum.

### Step 7: Launch pannunga, alavidunga, review pannunga

Policy-aiyum approved tools-aiyum **orey naalla** launch pannunga; tools illaadha policy oru ban maadhiri thaan theriyum. Appuram:

- **Train** pannunga, ovvoru team-oda real tasks-a vechu 30 nimishathula.
- **Measure** pannunga maasaa maasam: approved tools-oda active users, unapproved AI sites-kku traffic, exception requests, incident reports, oru moonu-task quiz.
- **Signals-a padinga.** Aarambathulaye sila incident reports vandhaa route velai seiyudhu-nnu arththam; zero-naa perumbaalum maranjirukkura thappugal.
- **Alavukku etha maadhiri enforce pannunga.** Nermaiyaana thappugalukku coaching; incidents-a maraikkiradhukkum, thirumba thirumba venumne panra breaches-kkum HR process. Approved tool velaiyai sariyaa seiya aarambicha appuram thaan unapproved sites-a block pannunga.
- **Review** pannunga aaru maasathukku oru thadavai, appuram oru pudhu tool, oru pudhu sattam allathu oru serious incident-kku appuramum.

## Oru worked example

Idhu oru illustrative case. Method-a kaatturadhukkaaga company-um numbers-um made up.

Pune-la 300 per irukkura oru software services company-kitta AI policy edhuvum illa. Oru security review-la, oru periya client kekkuraanga: "Unga company-la AI use eppadi govern pannappadudhu, endha tools enga code-a paarthirukku?" Rendaavadhu paadhikku yaarukkum badhil theriyala. Adhai fix panna delivery head Neha-kku, CTO, legal counsel kooda serndhu aaru vaaram kidaikkudhu.

**Discovery (weeks 1, 2).** Oru anonymous survey-kku 210 replies varudhu; 150 per velaiyila AI use panraanga. Naalu chatbot sites regular-aa use aagradhu proxy logs-la theriyudhu. Client code-a personal accounts-la paste pannadhai rendu teams othukkiraanga. Yaarukkum thandanai illa, andha clients-kitta solla vendiya kadamai edhaavadhu irukkaa-nnu legal avanga contracts-a check pannudhu.

**Tool checks (weeks 2 mudhal 4).** Enterprise assistant checklist-a pass pannudhu. Code assistant-oda vendor prompts-a 30 naal vechirukku, India-kku veliyila process pannudhu. Rendu clients-oda contracts India-only processing kekkudhu, adhanaala avanga opt-out list-kku poraanga, avanga teams oru local code-search tool use panraanga. Assistant-oda email connector thaniyaa review aagura varai off-aave irukku.

**Oru kashtamaana request.** Than pull requests-a thaane merge panra oru AI agent venum-nu oru team kekkudhu. Neha adhai restricted-nu classify panraanga: agent pull requests open pannalaam, tests run pannalaam, aanaa merge panradhu oru human thaan, production-kku adhukku endha access-um illa.

**Avanga enna ezhudhinaanga** (moonu pakkam, CEO sign pannadhu):

| Section | Avanga enna ezhudhinaanga |
| --- | --- |
| Approved tools | Enterprise assistant, IDE code assistant, settings list pannappattadhu; connectors off |
| Tool vaariyaa data | Matrix, koodave 2 clients-oda opt-out list |
| Restricted | Client-facing AI features-kku oru risk assessment-um client-oda written approval-um venum; agents-kku least access, merge panradhu human |
| Prohibited | Client work-kku personal AI accounts; prompts-la secrets; unapproved tools-la client allathu personal data |
| Incidents | 1 mani nerathukkulla security-kitta report; CERT-In, client notices pathi security-um legal-um mudivu pannum |
| Exceptions, review | 3 working days-la badhil varra oru form; 6 maasathukku oru thadavai review |

**Rendu maasam kazhichu:**

| Measure | Munnaadi | Appuram | Eppadi alandhaanga |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Kandupidikkiradhukku munnaadiye ezhudhuradhu.** Makkal erkanave use panra tools-a kandukkaadha policy-a makkalum kandukka maattaanga.
- **Vera company-oda policy-a copy panradhu.** Avanga tools, clients, sattangal ungalodadhu illa; structure-a eduthukkonga, rules-a illa.
- **Orey oru setting-a vechu tool-a approve panradhu.** "Enga data-la train pannaadhu"-ngradhu retention, location, vendor access, connectors pathi edhuvum sollaadhu.
- **Veli deadline-a vida neelamaana internal deadline.** 24 mani nera internal rule, 6 mani nera CERT-In kadamaiyai udaichudum.
- **Route illaadha bans-um rules-um.** Makkal kannukku theriyaama personal accounts-la AI use pannitte iruppaanga. Ovvoru "pannaadheenga"-kkum oru approved alternative-um, sila naatkalla badhil varra oru exception route-um venum.
- **Thelivillaadha vaarthaigal.** Examples illaadha "appropriate", "responsibly", "sensitive" ovvoru reader-kkum vera vera arththam tharum.
- **System illaama verum oru document.** Owner illa, pazhaiya inventory, review date illai-nnaa, oru varushathukkulla policy thappaa aayidum.

## Practice pannunga

Keezhe link panniyirukkura NIST AI RMF-oda Govern function-aiyum, OWASP checklist-la AI asset inventory pathina section-aiyum padinga. PRS-oda summary 2023-la pass aana DPDP sattathai cover pannudhu; 2025 Rules appuram thaan vandhuchu. CERT-In directions-aiyum, EU AI Act summary-la high-risk uses-aiyum oru paarvai paarunga.

1. **Discover.** Ungalukku therinja oru organisation-kku, use-la irukkura anju AI tools, ovvonnum paarkkura data, adhoda owner-a list panni, ovvonnaiyum eppadi confirm pannuveenga-nnu ezhudhunga.
2. **Draft.** Indha lesson-oda aarambathula irukkura template-a rendu mudhal moonu pakkathula fill pannunga, oru data-by-tool matrix-um, 1 mani nera internal deadline-oda oru incident route-um serthu.
3. **Oru tool-a check pannunga.** Oru real AI tool-kku adhoda public terms-a vechu ezhu checklist kelvigalukkum badhil ezhudhunga, endha badhilgal kidaikkalai-nnu note pannunga.
4. **Test pannunga.** Draft-aiyum moonu real tasks-aiyum vera vera teams-la irukkura rendu perkitta kudunga. Avanga thappaa purinjukkitta ovvoru rule-aiyum fix pannunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nnu eppo sollalaam:** unga policy-a oru pudhu employee-kitta kuduthaa, moonu tasks-kku endha tool, endha data use pannalaam-nu avanga sariyaa sollanum, appuram oru client-oda security reviewer avanga data-vai paartha ovvoru tool-aiyum kandupidikka mudiyanum.
