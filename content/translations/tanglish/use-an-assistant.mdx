## Idhu enna

AI-a kathukka vegamaana vazhi, unmaiyaana oru velaikku use panradhu. Chat assistant-a oru **capable pudhu colleague** maadhiri nadathinaa romba useful: adhukku podhuvaa neraya theriyum, aana unga situation, customers, company pathi onnume theriyaadhu. Neenga theliva brief pannuveenga, adhu kudukkuradha review pannuveenga, use panradhukku munnaadi mukkiyamaanadha check pannuveenga.

```flow
dir: LR
Brief | who, task, context, format -> Review | is it what I asked for? Give feedback
Review -[iterate]-> Brief
Review -> Check | are the facts that matter right?
Check -> Use | send it, with my name on it
```

Indha lesson idha oru thadava, sariyaa, unga sondha velai illa padippu task-la panradhu pathi: drafting, summarising, explaining, planning illa brainstorming. Adhoda check oru chinna project, adhanaala mudiyum podhu moonu prompts, moonu answers, neenga check panna claims list irukkum.

Rohit-a follow pannuvinga. Avar Chennai-la oru broadband company-la customer support-la irukkaar, oru assistant vachu help article ezhudhuraar.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Nalla task-a edunga

| Nalla mudhal tasks | Mosamaana mudhal tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Neenga anuppa vendiya email illa message | Legal illa tax deadline |
| Neenda article illa meeting-oda summary | Maruthuvam dose |
| Paadhi therinja topic-oda explanation | Neengale judge panna mudiyaadha edhuvum |
| Event illa oru vaarathukku plan | Thirumba maathamudiyaadha decision |
| Prepare panna interview questions | Approve aagaadha tool-la confidential data-oda edhuvum |

Nalla tasks, draft time migukkura, nalladha, mosamaanadha easy-aa sollalaam. Output-a judge panna mudinjaa vegamaa kathukkalaam.

### Mulumaiyaana prompt ezhudhunga

Mulumaiyaana prompt-la naalu parts irukku:

| Part | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Neenga yaar, adhu yaarukku** | "I'm a final-year B.Com student preparing for a campus interview at a bank." |
| **Task** | "List ten questions they are likely to ask." |
| **Useful context** | "It's a public sector bank, for a probationary officer role. I did an internship at a credit co-operative." |
| **Format** | "As a numbered list, with a one-line tip for each." |

### Iterate pannunga

Mudhal answer oru draft. Maatrangal kelunga: chinnadhaa, innum specific-aa, vera tone, innum examples, cliches kammi. Enna thappu-nu sollunga. Oru perfect prompt ezhudha try panradhai vida rendu moonu rounds perumbaalum nallaa irukkum.

### Ore task-ku vera vera prompts try pannunga

Idhu thaan lesson-oda irudhayam. Moonu versions ezhudhunga:

1. **One-line prompt**, perumbaalaana makkal aarambikkira maadhiri.
2. **Naalu parts-um irukkura detailed prompt.**
3. **Assistant-a munnaadi ungalai kelvigal kekka solra detailed prompt:** "Ezhudhradhukku munnaadi, moonu kelvigal ennai kelu."

Results perumbaalum romba vera irukkum. Compare panradhu, edhu tips list-a vida prompting pathi adhigam kathukkudukkum.

### Claims-a check pannunga

Answer-la irukkura **factual claims**-a kandupidinga: numbers, dates, peyargal, rules, product details. Kuraindhapatcham mukkiyamaanadhai nambikkaiyaana source-oda check pannunga: official website, original document, textbook, illa expert.

```flow
Answer | "Routers should be restarted every week." A claim -> Source | ask the network team, read the router manual
Source -> Result | "Only restart when there's a problem."
Result -> Fix the line
```

Mukkiyamaana edhaiyaavadhu check panna mudiyala-na, adhai nambaadheenga.

### Edhai share pannareenga-nu gavanichukonga

Unga organisation approve pannaadha tools-la personal data, passwords, confidential work documents-a paste pannaadheenga. "Use AI safely at work" lesson idha detail-aa solludhu.

### Answers yen maarudhu

Ore kelviya rendu thadava kettaa, vera answers varalaam. Assistants ovvoru adutha word-aiyum konjam randomness-oda edukkum, wording-la chinna maatram result-a maathum. Edhaavadhu oru answer-a nambaama facts-a check panna idhuvum oru kaaranam.

## Oru worked example

Rohit Chennai-la oru broadband company-la customer support-la velai paakuraar. Avar manager oru chinna help article ezhudha sollraar: "En Wi-Fi yen slow-aa irukku?"

**Prompt 1, one line:**

> "Write an article about slow Wi-Fi."

Result 600 words, generic, avar company vikkaadha products-a mention panradhu.

**Prompt 2, detailed:**

> "I work for a broadband provider in Chennai. Write a 250-word help article for home customers titled 'Why is my Wi-Fi slow?' Cover router placement, too many devices, and when to restart the router. Friendly, simple English, with numbered steps. Do not mention brands."

Result use panra alavukku close-aa irukku.

**Prompt 3, detailed, munnaadi kelvigal:**

Prompt 2 maadhiriye, "Before writing, ask me three questions about our customers and router models."-nu serthu. Assistant customers-kitta endha router models irukku, endha plan speeds common, customers router settings-ku login panna mudiyuma-nu kekkudhu. Rohit badhil sollraar. Ippo article company kudukkura dual-band router-a pathiyum, vegamaana band-ku eppadi maaradhu-nu-m solludhu.

**Claims-a check panradhu.** Article "routers-a vaaram oru thadava restart pannanum"-nu solludhu. Rohit network team-a kekkuraar, avanga problems varum pothu mattum restart pannina podhum, vaaram restart panninaa thevaiyillaadha support calls varum-nu sollraanga. Avar andha line-a sari pannuraar.

| Prompt | Length | Accuracy | Customers-ku fit | Use panninaara? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. One line | 600 words | Vera brands-a mention panradhu | Mosam | Illa |
| 2. Detailed | 250 words | Oru thappaana claim | Nalla | Konjam |
| 3. Detailed plus questions | 250 words | Oru thappaana claim, apram sari | Romba nalla | Aama, review-ku apram |

Final article manager review-ku pogudhu, adutha vaaram publish aagudhu.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **One-line prompts.** Yaar, task, context, format kudunga.
- **Mudhal answer-a appadiyae edukkaradhu.** Specific feedback-oda iterate pannunga.
- **Edhaiyum check pannaama irukkaradhu.** Mukkiyamaana claims-a kandupidichu verify pannunga.
- **Approve aagaadha tools-la confidential illa personal data paste panradhu.**
- **Neengale judge panna mudiyaadha task-a edukkaradhu.** Nalladhu, mosamaanadhu theriyura idathula aarambinga.
- **Oru answer-a nambradhu.** Mukkiyamaa irundha, vera maadhiri thirumba kelunga illa source-a check pannunga.

## Practice pannunga

Keezha irukkura AI Fluency course-a edunga; AI-oda velai paakkum pothu delegation, description, discernment, diligence cover pannudhu.

Indha lesson-oda check oru chinna project:

1. **Oru task-a edunga**, unga velai illa padippula irundhu.
2. **Adhukku moonu prompts ezhudhunga:** one-line prompt, detailed prompt, ungalai munnaadi kelvigal kekkura detailed prompt.
3. **Ovvoru answer-layum oru claim-a check pannunga**, neenga nambura source-oda.
4. **Ezhudhi vainga:** edhu prompt nalla work aachu, yen, edhu sari, edhu thappu, eppadi therinjudhu.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Moonu prompts, adhoda answers, neenga check panna claims, adutha thadava enna maathuveenga-nu kaatta mudiyum.
