## Idhu enna

Oru **design round** ungalukku oru open problem kudukkum, udhaaranamaa "oru bank-kku support assistant design pannunga" illa "10 million documents mela question answering design pannunga". Oru interviewer iruppaanga, perumbaalum 45-la irundhu 60 nimisham. Introductions-um, kadaisiyila neenga kekkura kelvigalum poga, design-kku podhuvaa sumaar 35 nimisham micham irukkum. Neenga whiteboard-laiyo shared document-laiyo varaiveenga, interviewer naduvula "what if...?"-nnu interrupt pannuvaanga.

Oru nalla design-la enna irukkanum-ngradhu "AI system design" lesson-la irukku: components, request flow, failure tables, latency budgets. Indha lesson interview pathinadhu: neram eppadi selavaagudhu, interviewer enna ezhudhikkuraanga, oru strong answer numbers-oda serthu eppadi irukkum.

### Interviewer edha score panraanga

Niraiya companies structured interviews use panraanga: ovvoru candidate-kkum ore maadhiri kelvi, ezhudhi vecha oru rubric-la rating, ovvoru rating-aiyum support panra notes. Google-oda public re:Work guides indha approach-a vivarikkudhu. Adhanaala interviewer oru mangalaana impression-a uruvaakkala. Avanga oru scorecard-a fill panraanga, ovvoru heading-kkum keezha avangalukku evidence venum, perumbaalum neenga sonna vaarthaigale. Headings company-kku company maarum, aanaa perumbaalaanadhu ippadi irukkum:

| Heading | Strong rating-kkaana evidence | Weak rating-kkaana evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scoping | Varaiyuradhukku munnaadi users, scale, thappaana answer-oda cost, data rules pathi kettaanga | Mudhal nimishathulaye tools pera solla aarambichaanga |
| Design | Muzhu system seekkirame board-la; ovvoru part-um indha problem-kku yen-nu justify pannaanga | Technologies-a list pannaanga; ovvonnum yen irukku-nnu solla mudiyala |
| Depth | Riskiest part-la aazhamaa ponaanga, adhoda trade-offs therinjirundhuchu | Ovvoru box-laiyum sama alavu, melottamaana neram |
| Production thinking | Load, cost, latency-kku numbers; failures; oru test set, launch bar | Happy path-laye ninnuttaanga |
| Communication | Signpost pannaanga, check in pannaanga, "what if"-kku padhattam illaama adapt aanaanga | Neenda silences, illa mudhal design-a vidaappidiyaa defend pannaanga |

Practical paadam: interviewer ezhudhikkakkoodiya vishayangalai sollunga. "Naan 2 million consumers-nu assume panren, adhanaala peak-la oru second-kku sumaar 33 model calls"-nnu sonnaa, adhu evidence. Adhaiye manasukkulla yosichaa, adhu evidence illa.

### Company type-a poruthu rounds eppadi maarum

- **Periya product companies** podhuvaa generic-aana, scale adhigamaa irukkura prompts kuduthu, oru fixed rubric-la rate pannuvaanga. Thelivaana structure-um numbers-um dhaan romba mukkiyam.
- **Startups**-kku, adutha maasam version one-a neenga build panna mudiyumaa-nnu dhaan kavalai. "Edha cut pannuveenga?"-nnum, cost pathiyum kelvigalai edhirpaarunga. Silar indha round-kku badhilaa oru take-home illa live build veppaanga.
- **Forward deployed, client-facing roles** perumbaalum round-a oru customer conversation maadhiri nadathuvaanga. Udhaaranamaa, Palantir-oda loop pathina candidate reports oru "decomposition" round-a vivarikkudhu: adhula interviewer thelivillaadha problem irukkura oru customer maadhiri nadichu, neenga adha eppadi pirikkureenga-nnu score pannuvaanga. Innum adhigamaa kelvi kelunga, oru chinna first deliverable-a propose pannunga.
- **Indhiyaala IT services firms, global capability centres** perumbaalum prompts-a oru client-oda illa parent company-oda rules-a suthi amaippaanga: data residency, erkanave irukkura vendor stack, oru fixed budget. Andha constraints-a noise-aa paakkaama, problem-oda core-aa paarunga.

Idhu endha maadhiri round-nu recruiter-kitta kelunga. Adhu oru saadhaarana kelvi dhaan.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Ore time plan, adha eppadi resize panradhu

Ovvoru thadavaiyum ore plan-a use pannunga. Appo clock background-la odittu irukkum, unga gavanam problem melaye irukkum.

| Nimishangal | Phase | Eppo mudinjadhu-nnu sollalaam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 mudhal 5 | Clarify | Users, scale, thappaana answer-oda cost, languages, data rules, success metric ellaam board-la ezhudhiyaachu |
| 5 mudhal 10 | High level | User-la irundhu reply varai boxes, arrows; interviewer othukkittaanga |
| 10 mudhal 28 | Deep dives | Rendu moonu risky parts cover aachu, oru quick estimate-oda |
| 28 mudhal 31 | Failures | Nadakka adhiga vaaippulla moonu failures, ovvonnukkum adhoda guard |
| 31 mudhal 34 | Evals, ops | Test set, launch bar, rollout, neenga gavanikkappora andha oru metric |
| 34 mudhal 35 | Trade-offs | Pathu madangu scale-la, illa innum neram irundhaa, enna maathuveenga |

Design-kku 30 nimisham mattum kidaichaa, deep dives-a 13 nimishamaa kuraichu, matha ovvoru phase-aiyum appadiye vechukkunga. 45 kidaichaa, koodudhal nerathai deep dives-kkum failures-kkum kudunga, clarify-kku orupodhum illa. 20-vadhu nimishathukku vandhum innum mudhal deep dive-laye irundhaa, "Chunking-a inga park pannittu evaluation-kku poren"-nnu sollittu nagarunga. Oru phase-a perfect-aa cover panradha vida, ella phase-aiyum surukkamaa cover panradhu better.

### Mudhal anju nimisham

Design-a maathura kelvigalai kelunga, nerathai nirappura kelvigalai illa. Oru useful test: badhil oru box-aiyo oru number-aiyo maathaadhu-nnaa, andha kelviya kekkaadheenga. Romba strong-aana kelvigal:

- **Answer yaarukku pogudhu, thappaana answer-kku enna cost?** Staff padikkura thappaana answer oru thollai dhaan. Neradiyaa oru customer-kko doctor-kko pora thappaana answer oru legal problem aagalaam.
- **Evvalavu traffic, eppo?** Daily users, appuram peak pattern: bill day, exam results, festival sales.
- **Edhu live systems-la irundhu varanum?** Balances, order status, outages oru tool call-la irundhu dhaan varanum, model-oda memory-la irundhu orupodhum illa.
- **Languages, channel, data rules.** WhatsApp-la Tamil, English-ngradhu, web app-la English-la irundhu vera design. Data-va enga store pannalaam-nu kelunga.

Appuram unga assumptions-a numbers-aa solli, thappaa irundhaa correct panna solli interviewer-kitta kelunga. Avanga perumbaalum "fine, go ahead"-nnu solluvaanga; adhu dhaan neenga nagarradhukkaana licence.

### Yosikkaamale therinjirukka vendiya estimates

Interviewers-kku exact figures vendaam. Neenga orders of magnitude-la, sathamaa, oru nimishathukkulla reason panradha dhaan paakkanum.

| Alavu | Rule of thumb |
| --- | --- |
| English tokens | Oru token-kku sumaar 4 characters illa oru word-oda mukkaal pangu; 1,000 words-naa sumaar 1,300 tokens |
| Indian languages | Adhe arthathukku perumbaalum adhiga tokens thevaippadum, tokenizer-a poruthu; oru multiple-a solli, adha measure pannuven-nu sollunga |
| Oru naalla seconds | 86,400; quick kanakkukku 100,000-nu vechukkunga |
| Peak-la oru second-kku model calls | Daily conversations-a busy hours-aala divide panni, oru peak factor-aala (perumbaalum 2 mudhal 3) multiply panni, oru conversation-kku turns-aala multiply panni, 3,600-aala divide pannunga |
| Oru conversation-kku cost | Tokens in-a input price-aalaiyum, tokens out-a output price-aalaiyum multiply panni koottunga (prices oru million tokens-kku), appuram turns-aala multiply pannunga |
| Memory-la model weights | 16-bit-la oru parameter-kku sumaar 2 bytes, adhanaala oru 7-billion-parameter model-kku sumaar 14 GB thevai, koodave adhu process panra conversation-kkum memory venum |

Price oru assumption, adhukku oru date irukku-nnu eppavum sollunga. Prices adikkadi maarum, adhu therinja candidate experienced-aa theriyuvaanga.

### Conversation-a drive panradhu

- **Signpost pannunga.** "Mudhalla high level panren, appuram retrieval-laiyum outages-laiyum aazhamaa poren, yenna anga dhaan thappu nadakkalaam." Ippo interviewer-kku unga plan theriyum, seekkirame ungalai redirect panna mudiyum.
- **Ovvoru phase maarumbodhum check in pannunga.** "Neenga nenachadhukku idhu match aagudhaa, illa engayaavadhu innum aazhamaa poganumaa?" Interviewer kudukkura hints ellaam instructions dhaan. Evaluation pathi rendu thadavai kettaanga-nnaa, anga ponga.
- **Sathamaa yosinga, aanaa sentences-aa.** Ezhudhumbodhu pathu second silence paravaayilla. Muppadhu second silence-la room kai nazhuvidum.
- **"What if"-kku naalu moves-la badhil sollunga.** Adha oru number-aa restate pannunga, endha part mudhalla udaiyum-nu sollunga, andha part-a mattum maathunga, andha maatrathoda cost-a sollunga.
- **Thappugalai velippadaiyaa sari pannunga.** "Oru conversation-kku oru model call-nu sonnen; adhu oru message-kku onnu, adhanaala naalu madangu. Number-a thirumba kanakku poduren." Unga thappa neengale pidikkuradhu nalla signal.
- **"Enakku theriyaadhu"-nnu useful-aa sollunga.** "Andha provider-oda exact rate limit enakku theriyaadhu, adhanaala week one-laye check pannuven; adhu namma peak-a vida kuraivaa irundhaa, fix oru queue illa rendaavadhu region."

## Oru worked example

Prompt: "Tamil, English-la billing, outage kelvigalukku badhil solra, oru state electricity board-kkaana WhatsApp assistant-a design pannunga." Keezha irukkura numbers candidate sonna assumptions dhaan, oru unmaiyaana board pathina facts illa.

**0 mudhal 5 nimisham, pesuna maadhiriye.**

> **Candidate:** Edhaiyum varaiyuradhukku munnaadi, sila kelvigal. Board ethana consumers-kku serve pannudhu, traffic spike aagumaa?
>
> **Interviewer:** Sumaar 2 million. Bills anuppunadhukku appuramum, outages-appovum spike aagum.
>
> **Candidate:** Answers consumers-kku mattum dhaan pogudhaa, avanga sondha account pathi kekkalaamaa?
>
> **Interviewer:** Aamaa. Avanga bill-a check panna mudiyanum.
>
> **Candidate:** Appo oru identity check venum, adhanaala consumer number-a oru one-time code vechu verify pannuven. Outage status oru internal system-la irukku, adha naan call pannalaamaa? Data India-laye irukkanumaa?
>
> **Interviewer:** Rendukkum aamaa.
>
> **Candidate:** Assumptions solren. Saadhaarana naalla 1% consumers message panraanga, adhanaala 20,000 conversations. Bill illa outage naalla adhukku anju madangu, 100,000. Oru conversation-kku consumer-kitta irundhu naalu messages. Success-naa sariyaana tariff answers, helpline calls kuraiyuradhu. Idha vechaye pogalaamaa?

**5 mudhal 10 nimisham: high level.** Oru provider moolamaa WhatsApp Business Platform, appuram oru API service, appuram intent routing naalu paths-kku: bill lookup, outage status (code check-kku appuram tool calls), tariff kelvigal (rendu languages-laiyum tariff orders, FAQs mela retrieval, citations-oda), complaints (oru reference number-oda log panni staff-kitta oppadaikkappadum). Indian region-la host panna oru model reply-a ezhudhum.

**10 mudhal 28 nimisham: estimate, appuram deep dives.**

> **Candidate:** Quick numbers. Oru peak day-naa sumaar 10 busy hours-la 100,000 conversations, worst hour average-a vida sumaar moonu madangu. Adhanaala oru mani nerathula 30,000 conversations, adha naalu messages-aala multiply panna, oru second-kku sumaar 33 model calls. Ovvoru call-um sumaar 3,000 tokens in, idhula Tamil-kku adhiga tokens aaguradhaiyum kanakkula eduthirukken, 250 out. Adhu peak-la oru nimishathukku sumaar 6.5 million tokens. Mudhalla provider-oda rate limit-a adhukku edhiraa check pannuven.

| Item | Assumption | Mudivu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | Input, output tokens-kku oru million-kku $0.10, $0.40-nu vechukkuvom, oru dollar-kku ₹94 | Oru conversation-kku sumaar ₹0.15 |
| WhatsApp | Meta-oda rate card illa, third-party reports padi 1 October 2026-la irundhu India-la oru service message-kku sumaar ₹0.115 (silar ₹0.145 varai solraanga), oru number-kku maasam 1,000 free-kku appuram, GST-kku munnaadi (November 2026-la check pannadhu) | Oru conversation-kku sumaar ₹0.46, naalu replies |
| Maasam | 26 saadhaarana naal, 4 peak naal, adhanaala 9.2 lakh conversations | Model-kku sumaar ₹1.4 lakh, WhatsApp-kku ₹4.2 lakh, GST, provider fees-kku munnaadi |

> **Candidate:** Appo, andha reported rate unmaiyaa irundhaa, channel-kku model-a vida sumaar moonu madangu cost aagalaam. Rates adikkadi maarum, adhanaala Meta-oda rate card-la confirm pannuven. Idhula irundhu rendu vishayam varudhu. Cheaper model-a vida, conversations-a surukkuradhu dhaan adhigamaa save pannum. Outage kelvigalukku model-e thevaiyilla: tool status-a return pannum, oru fixed Tamil illa English reply adha nirappidum.

Appuram deep dives cover panradhu: bilingual retrieval (tariff tables-a tables-aave extract panradhu, rendu languages-laiyum chunks, ovvoru chunk-laiyum tariff order date), appuram outage answers live tool-la irundhu mattum, ovvoru area-kkum rendu nimisham pola cache pannadhu, yenna aayirakkanakkaana per ore nerathula keppaanga. Latency budget muzhu reply-kkum dhaan, yenna ovvoru reply-um oru complete message-aa pogudhu: 100 replies-la 95-kku sumaar 5 seconds.

**28 mudhal 35 nimisham.** Failures: thappaana tariff (citations, endha source-um match aagalai-nnaa refusal, flag aana answers-a vaaraa vaaram review), injection (complaint logging thavira write tools illa), provider outage (helpline number-oda oru fixed message). Evaluation: rendu languages-laiyum 300 unmaiyaana kelvigal, intent vaariyaa score pannadhu, board kooda othukkitta oru launch bar; mudhalla oru district-la launch panni, anga helpline call volume-a gavanikkanum.

**Interruptions.**

> **Interviewer:** Outage system down aanaa?
>
> **Candidate:** Appo outage answers dhaan udaiyura part. Kadaisiyaa therinja status-a adhoda timestamp-oda kaattuven, helpline number kuduppen, on-call-kku alert pannuven. Restoration time-a model guess panna vidamaatten.
>
> **Interviewer:** Traffic pathu madangu aanaa?
>
> **Candidate:** Adhu peak-la oru second-kku sumaar 330 calls, oru nimishathukku 65 million tokens. Rate limit dhaan mudhalla udaiyum. Peak traffic-la perumbaalaanadhu outage kelvigal-naa, fixed-reply path andha model calls-a neekkidum. Appuram prompt-oda fixed part-a cache pannuven, michatha oru "seekkiram reply panrom" message-oda queue pannuven. Cost: peak-oda uchathula replies medhuvaa varum.

| Phase | Scorecard evidence-a edhu vaangi thandhadhu |
| --- | --- |
| Clarify | Answers yaarukku pogudhu, edhu live systems-la irundhu varanum-nu kettaanga; numbers sonnaanga |
| Estimate | Peak-la oru second-kku 33 calls, design-aiye maathuna oru cost split |
| Deep dive | Model illaadha outage path; date pota tariff chunks-oda bilingual retrieval |
| What if | Udaiyura part-a sonnaanga, adha mattum maathunaanga, cost-a sonnaanga |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Answer yaarukku pogudhu-nnu kekkave illaadhadhu.** Staff-facing, customer-facing systems-kku vera vera guards venum, interviewer andha kelvikkaaga kaathuttu iruppaanga.
- **Oru lookup podhum-ngra idathula agent-a design panradhu.** Bill amount-um outage status-um database reads dhaan. Anga oru model-a sertha cost, delay, thappaa poga oru vazhi ellaam koodum.
- **Pinnaadi assumptions illaadha numbers.** "Maasam sumaar ₹5 lakh"-kku, adha uruvaakkuna volume, tokens, prices-a solra varai arthame illa.
- **Model-kku mattum price poduradhu.** Channel, one-time codes, human review, hosting-kku tokens-a vida adhigamaa cost aagalaam.
- **Hints-a noise-aa paakkuradhu.** Interviewer ore vishayatha rendu thadavai kettaa, missing evidence enga irukku-nnu ungalukku solraanga.
- **Manappaadam panna architecture-a oppikkiradhu.** Ovvoru prompt-laiyum design-a valaikka vendiya onnu rendu constraints irukkum. Unga design oru bank-kkum oru electricity board-kkum ore maadhiri irundhaa, avatra miss pannitteenga.
- **"What if"-kooda vaakkuvaadham panradhu.** Adhu oru pudhu requirement, unga judgement-kku savaal illa. Adapt aagunga, appuram cost-a sollunga.

Oru design answer-a timer vechu record pannunga. Aarukku score pannunga, ovvonnukkum oru point: varaiyuradhukku munnaadi clarify panneenga; numbers sonneenga; deep dives-kku munnaadi high level; failures-a guards-oda sonneenga; oru test set-um launch bar-um kudutheenga; oru "what if"-kku adhoda cost-oda badhil sonneenga. Unga nimishanga unmaiyila enga pochu-nnu note pannunga. Rendu thadavai interrupt panra oru partner kooda practice panradhu innum better; avangalukku neenga interviewer-aa irundhaa, oru thelivaana answer eppadi irukkum-nu ungalukke puriyum.
