## Idhu enna

Indian AI deployments-la naalu regimes adhikka varum:

- **India-oda Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**, adhoda Rules 14 November 2025-la notify aachu, obligations pathinettu maadhathula padi padiyaa varudhu. India-la irukkura makkal-oda digital personal data-va idhu govern pannum.
- **EU-oda General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, EU-la irukkura makkal-oda personal data-va handle pannum podhu apply aagum, example European customers-ku serve panra oru Indian company.
- **SOC 2**, oru law illa, oru service provider-oda security controls pathi independent audit report. Enterprise buyers, especially US-la, vendors-kitta adhikka idha kekkuvaanga.
- **HIPAA**, US health privacy law, US healthcare clients-ku US patients-oda health information-a process panra Indian firms-a thodum.

EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ISO/IEC 42001 maadhiri AI-specific frameworks risk, transparency, oversight pathi duties serkkum; mathha paths-la irukkura governance lessons adha pesum.

Indha lesson oru velai seiyura engineer-oda map, legal advice illa. Eppavum client-oda legal, privacy team-a serkkunga.

```flow

Clinic audio -> Model provider | processor terms, region, training illa

```

| Users yaaru | Apply aagura rules |
|---|---|
| India | DPDP Act |

| Oru US hospital | HIPAA, business associate agreement-oda |
| Enterprise buyers | SOC 2 |

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

**DPDP Act mukkiyamaanadhu.** Data yen, eppadi process aagudhu-nu mudivu panra organisation thaan **data fiduciary**; adhoda sarbaa process panra vendors **data processors**. Processing-ku oru lawful ground thevai, mukkiyamaa suyamaa, specific-aa, therinju, thelivaa irukkura consent, oru clear notice-ku appuram kudukkapattadhu, kudukka evvalavu easy-o thirumba edukkavum avvalavu easy-aa; Act sila legitimate uses-aiyum list pannudhu. Data-va sollappatta purpose-kku mattum use pannalaam, thevai illaama aana appuram erase pannanum. Makkalukku avanga data-va access, correct, erase panra rights-um, oru grievance process-um irukku. Fiduciaries reasonable security safeguards edukkanum, personal data breaches-a Data Protection Board-kum paadhikkappatta makkalukkum report pannanum. Kuzhandhaigal data-ku verifiable parental consent thevai, tracking illa targeted advertising-ku use panna koodaadhu. Periya illa sensitive fiduciaries significant data fiduciaries-aa classify aagalaam, audits, impact assessments maadhiri extra duties-oda. Data-va paadhukkaama vittaa penalties ₹250 crore varaikkum.

**GDPR mukkiyamaanadhu.** Idhe maadhiri ideas, adhigamaa details-oda: aaru lawful bases, purpose limitation, data minimisation, access, erasure ulla rights, high-risk processing-ku data protection impact assessments, EU-ku veliya transfers-ku rules, global turnover-la 4% varaikkum fines.

**SOC 2.** Oru auditor, provider-oda controls-a security-ku, optional-aa availability, confidentiality, processing integrity, privacy-ku criteria-vukku edhiraa test pannum. Type I report oru point in time-la design-a check pannum; Type II report oru kaalaththula, adhikka pala maadhangal, controls work aanadha check pannum. Neengalum, ungaloda stack-la irukkura model provider maadhiri ovvoru vendor-um idhu vachirukkeengala-nu clients kekkuvaanga.

**HIPAA.** Covered entities (providers, insurers), adhoda business associates vachirukkura health information-a paadhukkum. Oru vendor andha maadhiri data-va thoduradhukku munnaadi oru business associate agreement-la kaiyezhuththu poodanum, minimum thevaiyaanadha mattum use pannanum.

**Design-ku idhu enna artham.**

- Personal data-oda ovvoru flow-vaiyum map pannunga: source, purpose, enga store aagudhu, entha vendors paakkuraanga, evvalavu naal irukkum.
- Minimise pannunga: task-ku thevaiyaana fields mattum models-ku anuppunga; meedhiya mask pannunga.
- Model vendors-oda terms-a check pannunga: data training-ku use aagudha, store aagudha, enga?
- Retention, deletion: ovvoru store-kum periods set pannunga, erasure requests index, logs, backups-a reach pannum padi pannunga.
- Access control, audit logs, breach response plans.

**Regimes pakkam pakkamaa:**

| Regime | Idhu enna | Yaarai bind pannum | Typical AI design impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DPDP Act | Indian data protection law | Indian personal data-oda fiduciaries-um processors-um | Notice-um consent-um, purpose limits, erasure, breach reporting |
| GDPR | EU data protection law | EU residents-oda data-va handle panra yaarum | Lawful basis, impact assessments, transfer rules |
| SOC 2 | Independent controls audit report | Buyers kekkura service providers | Access control, logging, change management, vendor reviews |
| HIPAA | US health privacy law | Covered entities-um business associates-um | BAA, minimum necessary data, HIPAA-eligible services-um othukkitta hosting region-um |

**Privacy officer-kitta pesuradhu.** Opinions illa, data flow map-a konduvaanga. Ovvoru flow-kum, enna data nagarudhu, yen, enga store aagudhu, evvalavu naalaikku, entha vendor paakkuraanga, eppadi delete aagudhu-nu sollunga. Privacy officers ovvoru thadavaiyum adhe sila kelvigal thaan kekkuvaanga: purpose, lawful ground, minimisation, retention, security, rights, breach handling. Ezhudhina badhilgal irundhaa, oru maasa emails-a rendu mani neram meeting-aa maathum.

**Check panna mudiyura oru data flow map.** Ovvoru flow-vaiyum data-vaa ezhudhunga, privacy officer kandupidikkura gaps-a oru script kandupidikkattum:

```python
FLOWS = [
    {"name": "chat messages to model", "data": ["message text"], "purpose": "answer the question",
     "lawful_ground": "consent", "store": "none at vendor (zero retention)", "vendor": "model provider",
     "retention_days": 0, "region": "check vendor terms"},
    {"name": "conversation logs", "data": ["masked message text", "user id"], "purpose": "quality review",
     "lawful_ground": "consent", "store": "Postgres, Mumbai", "vendor": None, "retention_days": 30},
    {"name": "search index", "data": ["policy documents"], "purpose": "retrieval",
     "lawful_ground": "not personal data", "store": "vector DB, Mumbai", "vendor": "hosted DB",
     "retention_days": None},
]
REQUIRED = ["purpose", "lawful_ground", "store", "retention_days"]

def gaps(flows):
    out = []
    for f in flows:
        out += [f"{f['name']}: missing {k}" for k in REQUIRED if k not in f]
        if f.get("lawful_ground") != "not personal data" and f.get("retention_days") is None:
            out.append(f"{f['name']}: personal data with no retention period")
        if f.get("vendor") and "region" not in f and "Mumbai" not in str(f.get("store")):
            out.append(f"{f['name']}: vendor region not recorded")
    return out

print(gaps(FLOWS) or "no gaps")
```

Map-a repository-la vainga, ovvoru pudhu feature-oda review pannunga, privacy officer-ku print panna version-a kudunga.

**Code-la retention-um erasure-um.** Ovvoru system-kum thevaiyaana rendu jobs:

```python
def nightly_retention(db):
    db.execute("DELETE FROM conversation_logs WHERE created_at < now() - interval '30 days'")
    db.execute("DELETE FROM uploads WHERE created_at < now() - interval '90 days'")

def erase_user(user_id, db, index, cache):
    db.execute("DELETE FROM conversation_logs WHERE user_id = %s", (user_id,))
    db.execute("DELETE FROM memories WHERE user_id = %s", (user_id,))
    index.delete(filter={"owner": user_id})          # documents the user uploaded
    cache.delete_pattern(f"user:{user_id}:*")
    db.execute("INSERT INTO erasure_log (user_id_hash, at) VALUES (sha256(%s::bytea), now())", (user_id,))
```

Erasure log, identity-a clear-aa vachukkaama, oru request nadathappattadha record pannum. Backups avanga sondha cycle-la expire aagum; adha privacy notice-la sollunga.

**DPDP Rules.** Act-oda Rules 2025-la notify aachu, duties padi padiyaa amalukku varudhu. Adhula notices, consent managers, security safeguards, breach reporting timelines, kuzhandhaigal data pathi innum details irukku. Launch-ku munnaadi ippo irukkura dates-um text-um check pannunga.

**EU-ku serve pannaa.** GDPR-ku appuram, EU AI Act AI systems-a risk padi pirikkum. Perumbaalaana chat, content tools-ku transparency duties irukku (makkal AI-kitta pesuraanga-nu sollanum, generate panna content-a label pannanum), hiring, credit scoring, education assessment maadhiri high-risk uses-ku kanamaana duties: risk management, data governance, human oversight, logging, documentation. Adhoda obligations padi padiyaa apply aagum; ungaloda launch date-la edhu apply aagudhu-nu check pannunga.

**Vendor terms maarum.** Model providers data-use, retention terms-a update pannuvaanga. Neenga review panna terms-oda version-yum date-yum data flow map-la record pannunga, varushathukku oru thadava, ethavadhu change notice vandhaalum thirumba check pannunga.

## Oru worked example

Pune-la oru health-tech company, India clinics-ukkum oru US hospital network-kkum, doctor-patient conversations-la irundhu clinical notes draft panra oru AI scribe build panradhu.

Privacy review oru data flow map tharudhu: clinic-la irundhu audio, transcript, draft note, doctor-oda edits, clinic-oda records-la final note, logs.

India-kku (DPDP): clinic data fiduciary, company oru contract-padi data processor. Patients registration-la oru notice paathu consent kudukkuraanga, thirumba edukka oru simple vazhi-oda. Note sign aana 7 naal kazhichu audio delete aagum. Erasure requests transcripts-aiyum drafts-aiyum delete pannum.

US hospital-kku (HIPAA): oru business associate agreement sign aagudhu, US patient data US cloud region-la irukkum, model provider adhoda sondha HIPAA-eligible terms-ku keezha mattum use aagum.

Buyers-ku podhuvaa: company oru SOC 2 Type II audit-a aarambikkudhu, model provider-oda SOC 2 report-a adhoda vendor file-la vachukkudhu. Logs-la names-um identifiers-um mask aagum. Design review moonu vaaram aachu, ovvoru kelvikkum documented badhil irundhadhaala deal mudinjadhu.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Check pannaama "Naanga data store pannala".** Logs, traces, caches, backups, vendors ellaam data-va store pannum.
- **Vendor terms-a ignore panradhu.** Model provider-oda retention-um training terms-um ungaloda compliance-oda pagudhi.
- **Pinnaadi use aagum-nu ellaathaiyum collect panradhu.** Purpose limitation-um minimisation-um venaam-nu solludhu.
- **Derived data vazhiyaa erasure path illaadhadhu.** Source document-a delete pannitu, adhoda chunks, embeddings, logs-a vittaa adhu erasure illa.
- **Engineers legal opinions solradhu.** System-a precise-aa describe pannunga; law-va lawyers interpret pannattum.
- **Index, logs, caches-a miss panra erasure.** Ovvoru store-aiyum map pannunga.
- **Yaarum update pannaadha data flow map.** Repository-la vachu check pannunga.
- **Vendor terms eppavum maaraadhu-nu nenaikkuradhu.** Neenga check panna date-a record pannunga.

## Compliance velai lighter-aa irukkura neram

- **Personal data-vae illaadha**, public government circulars mela oru bot maadhiri: accuracy-yilum security-yilum kavanam podunga.
- **Synthetic data-oda prototype**, unmaiyaana users-a thodaadhadhu.
- **Personal illaadha company data-la internal tools**, contracts-um security policy-yum innum apply aagum, aana privacy law perumbaalum aagaadhu.

Unmaiyaana users-oda names, contact details, messages, health, panam, illa kuzhandhaigal data irukkura edhaiyum, muzhu process thevai-nu eduthukkunga.

## Demo-la irundhu production-ku

- **Data flow map-a code-la vainga**, gap check-a CI-la odunga.
- **Retention jobs-a dhinam raathiri odunga**, fail aanaa alert pannunga.
- **Erasure-a end to end test pannunga**, ovvoru quarter-um oru test user-oda.
- **Breach response plan vainga**, names, timelines, templates-oda.
- **Vendor terms-a thirumba check pannunga**, varushathukku oru thadavaiyum, ovvoru change notice-kum.

## Practice pannunga

DPDP Bill pathi PRS-oda summary, GDPR.eu-oda overview, AICPA page-oda SOC 2 section-a padinga.

1. Neenga build panna oru AI system-ku data flow map varainga: personal data pogura ovvoru idamum, yen, evvalavu naalaikku, entha vendor paakkuraanga.
2. Ovvoru flow-kum, privacy officer kekkura DPDP kelvi (purpose, consent, retention, security, erasure), ungaloda badhil-a ezhudhunga.
3. Oru model provider-oda data usage, retention terms-a padichu, oru client-ku aindhu line-la summarise pannunga.
4. Ungaloda system-ku FLOWS list-a ezhudhunga, check report panra ovvoru gap-aiyum sari pannunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Oru privacy officer-a ungaloda data flow map vazhiyaa nadathi kootitu poga mudiyum, oru erasure request oru aal-a ovvoru store-layum irundhu eppadi neekkudhu-nu kaattalaam.
