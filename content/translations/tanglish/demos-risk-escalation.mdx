## Idhu enna

Oru engagement-a healthy-aa vachchukka moonu habits:

- **Vaaram-oru demo**: real cases-la velai seiyra software-a irubadhu-la irundhu muppadhu nimisham kaattunga, slides illa. Enna maarichu, users enna sonnaanga, adutha enna.
- **Risk log**: enna thappaa pogalaam, evvalavu likely, evvalavu serious, yaar owner, enna panraanga-nu oru short, uyirulla list.
- **Escalation**: working level-la neraththukku theerkka mudiyaadha blocker-a, adhigaaram adhigam irukkaravanga kitta, mudhalla, options-oda kondu pogradhu.

```flow
title: Ovvoru vaaramum
Demo | real software, real masked cases, oru failure kaattappattadhu -> Risk log review, 5 nimisham | risk, likelihood, impact, owner, action, due, status
Risk log review, 5 nimisham -> Status note | AMBER: progress, risks, asks
Status note -> Oru risk milestone-a thakka pogudhu, owner maattikittaaraa?
Oru risk milestone-a thakka pogudhu, owner maattikittaaraa? -[aamaa]-> Escalation | facts, impact, options, recommendation, oru date-kulla decision
```

Indha lesson, Chennai-la oru lender-oda credit-summary pilot-oda aaraavadhu vaarathai follow pannum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

**Demo rules.** Real (masked) data-la real system-a kaattunga. Edhaavadhu thappaa poi, adhai eppadi handle pannom-nu serunga; adhu credibility-a valarkkum. Users-um business owner-um varattum, IT contact mattum illa. Client-kitta venum-nu decisions-oda, adutha vaara plan-oda mudinga. Call mudinja apram decisions-a ezhuthula record pannunga.

**Risk log.** Ovvoru risk-kum: clear statement ("Rural branches-la irundhu varra bank statement photos padikka mudiyaadha alavukku blurry-aa irukkalaam"), likelihood, impact (low, medium, high), owner (peru sollakkoodiya oru aal, perumbaalum client side-la), action, due date, status. Weekly call-la, short-aa review pannunga. Mudhal vaaramae pre-mortem-oda aarambinga: moonu maadhathula project fail aaiduchu-nu yosichu, yen-nu list pannunga.

Typical AI engagement risks: data access delays, data quality edhirpaarthadhai vida mosam, mukkiya subject expert kidaikkaadhadhu, security review late, users adopt pannaadhadhu, model provider maatrangal illa limits, scope valardhu.

**Escalation.** Oru risk oru milestone-a thakka pogudhu, owner-aala theerkka mudiyala, illa working team-ku mela oru decision thevai-nu irundhaa escalate pannunga. Mudhalla, facts-oda, options-oda pannunga:

"Data access moonu vaaram late. Last year-oda archived files-la aarambikkalana, proof-of-concept gate 15 August-la irundhu 5 September-ku nagarum; adhai IT indha vaaramae share panna mudiyum. Archive-a recommend panrom. Head of operations-kitta irundhu velli-kizhamaikkulla decision thevai."

Escalation-na kutram sollradhu illa. Involve aanavangala copy-la serunga, tone-a neutral-aa vachchukkunga, help panna munvaanga.

**One-line status.** Progress, risks, asks-ku oru oru vaakkiyam-oda green, amber, illa red status-a ovvoru weekly meeting-kum apram anuppunga; meetings illaamalae leaders-a informed-aa vachchirukkum.

**Risk log extract:**

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Owner | Action | Due | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Production-oda security review schedule aagala | High | High | IT security lead | CIO-oda office-kku escalate pannaachu | Week 7 | Open |
| Rendu branches 50%-kku keezha adoption | Medium | Medium | Regional operations heads | Branch visits | Week 7 | Open |
| Rural branches-la irundhu blurry statement photos | Medium | Medium | Product lead | Manual check-ku flag; branches-ku guidance | Week 8 | Mitigated |

**Pre-mortem nadathradhu.** Mudhal vaaram, core team-a irubadhu nimishathukku serunga. Sollunga: "Ippo moonu maadham aprom, project fail aaiduchu. Yen-nu ezhudhunga." Reasons-a amaidhiyaa collect pannunga, apram group pannunga. Ovvoru group-um owner-oda, action-oda oru risk aagum. Idha panra teams, apram nadakkura perumbaalaana risks-a, innum action edukka neram irukkum podhae kandupidichidraanga.

**Nambikkai valarkkura demos.** Pona vaarathula irundhu enna maarichu-nu aarambinga, real cases-la kaattunga, apram edhaavadhu thappaa poi system illa team adhai eppadi handle pannichu-nu oru vishayam kaattunga. Thevaiyaana decisions-oda, adutha vaara plan-oda mudinga, adhe naalae decisions-oda short ezhuthu summary anuppunga. Polished paths mattum kaattura team-a vida, failures-a kaattura team-a clients romba nambuvaanga.

## Oru worked example

Chennai lender pilot-oda 6-vadhu vaaram. Unga weekly update:

> **Status: Amber.** Median file time 27 nimisham (baseline 41, target 22); rework 5.4%; adoption 78%.
> **Demo:** pudhu side-by-side review screen; oru failure kaattinom, kai-yezhuthula thiruththangal irundha salary slip, adhai system manual check-ku sariyaa flag pannichu.
> **Top risks:**
> 1. *High*: production environment-oda security review schedule aagala; production-ku adhu week 12-kulla thevai. Owner: IT security lead. Action: innaiku CIO-oda office-kku escalate pannaachu.
> 2. *Medium*: rendu branches 50%-kku keezha adoption. Owner: regional operations heads. Action: adutha vaaram visits.
> **Decisions needed:** regional summary template-a Budhan-kizhamai-kulla approve pannunga.

Security escalation, review-a week 9-kku schedule panna vachchudhu, production gate-a block pannirukkum munnaadi moonu vaaram munnadiyae. Final review-la, head of operations, weekly updates thaan timeline-a nambinadhukku kaaranam-nu solraanga.

| Week-6 update-la irundha item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Status amber: file time 27 nimisham, target 22 | Extra meetings illaama leaders-a informed-aa vachchudhu |
| Security review CIO-oda office-kku escalate | Week 9-kku schedule aachu, production-a block panna irundhadhukku moonu vaaram munnadi |
| Regional template decision Budhan-kizhamai-kulla kekkapattadhu | Neraththukku approve aachu |

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Slide demos.** Software-a kaattunga; slides problems-a maraikkum.
- **Review panna padaadha risk logs.** Oru thadava ezhudhi marandha list yaaraiyum paadhukaakkaadhu.
- **Owners illaadha risks.** "Team" edhaiyum own pannaadhu. Oru aal peru podunga.
- **Late escalation.** Blocker adicha naalae solradhu complain panradhu, escalate panradhu illa.
- **Escalation-a kutram sollra maadhiri panradhu.** Facts, impact, options, clear ask.

## Practice pannunga

Atlassian-oda risk register, pre-mortem pages-a padinga.

1. Unga use case-ku 20 nimisha pre-mortem nadathunga (thaniyaa illa friend-oda), results-a owners, actions-oda oru risk log-aa maathunga.
2. Oru risk red aana vaarathukku, weekly one-paragraph update ezhudhunga.
3. Andha risk-ku rendu options-oda, recommend panna oru decision-oda escalation note ezhudhunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Peru sollappatta owners-oda oru risk log-um, busy-aana oru executive rendu nimishathula act panna mudiyra escalation note-um kaattalaam.
