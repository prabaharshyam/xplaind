## Idhu enna

Velaila AI-a safe-aa use panradhu, ovvoru thadavayum neenga kekkura moonu kelvigal-la adangum:

```flow
dir: LR
Before: what am I putting in? | is this data allowed in this tool? -> After: what am I getting out? | is it accurate and appropriate?
After: what am I getting out? -> Always: who is responsible? | you are, for what you send or decide
```

Velaila nadakkura perumbaalaana AI incidents periya hacks illa. Time pressure-la irukkura saadharana aatkal saadharana vishayangalai pannuradhu: Friday maalai client-oda contract-a personal chatbot account-la paste panradhu, AI ezhudhuna figure-a check pannaama manager-ku forward panradhu. Indha lesson andha thappugal-a thadukkura pazhakkangalai valarkkum.

Meena-va follow pannuvinga. Avanga Hyderabad-la oru chinna consulting firm-la associate, oru urgent request-a client-oda nambikkaya udaikkaama handle panraanga. Mudiyum podhu, velaila AI tool-la edhai paste pannalaam, edhai paste pannaakoodaadhu-nu decide panni, yen-nu explain panna mudiyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Unga tools-a therinjukkonga

Neraya companies AI assistant-oda **enterprise** version-a license pannuvaanga. Idhula perumbaalum data eppadi store aagudhu, yaar paarkkalaam, models train panna use aagudha-nu agreements irukkum. Free illa personal accounts-oda terms romba vera irukkalaam, example-ku conversations-a vechukkittu service-a improve panna use pannalaam.

| | Personal free account | Company-approved enterprise tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terms-a yaar otthukkittaanga | Neenga, "accept" click panni | Unga company, oru contract-la |
| Unga data training-ku use aagumaa? | Settings-a poruthu aagalaam | Perumbaalum illa, agreement-padi |
| Unga company paarkka, control panna mudiyumaa? | Mudiyaadhu | Mudiyum: admin controls, logs |
| Confidential velaikku sariyaa? | Illa | Aama, policy-kulla |

Unga company tools approve pannirundha, velaikku adhai use pannunga. Edhuvum approve pannala-na, ovvoru AI tool-ayum confidential information-ku approve aagaadhadhu-nu paarunga.

### Paste panradhukku munnaadi information-a sort pannunga

| Category | Examples | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Perumbaalum paravaayilla** | Public articles, unga company-oda public website text, unga sondha podhuvaana ezhuthu, karpanai examples | Edha tool-layum, aana output-a check pannunga |
| **Approved tools mattum** | Internal documents, release aagaadha plans, meeting notes | Unga company approve panna tools mattum |
| **Mulumaiyaa thavirunga**, oru specific tool-um use-um approve aagala-na | Peyar-oda phone number, Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, health information maadhiri customer illa employee **personal data** (PII); passwords, keys; confidentiality agreements-la irukkura client documents | Paste pannaadheenga |

**PII**-na personally identifiable information: oru aal-a identify panra edha details-um. India-oda **Digital Personal Data Protection Act**, organisations personal data-va lawful purpose-kku mattum, perumbaalum aal consent kuduthadhukku mattum use pannanum, reasonable security safeguards-oda kaapaathanum-nu solludhu. Indha duties-la perumbaalum 2027 May-la irundhu apply aagum. Approve aagaadha AI tool-la customer details-a paste pannina, adhu apply aana apram indha law-a meeralaam, ippovae unga company-oda client contracts-a meeralaam.

### Thevaiyillaadhadha eduthudunga

Pala neram sensitive part illaamalae help kidaikkum:

```text
  Before:  "Draft a reply to Mr Suresh Iyer (98xxxxxx45, policy
            MH-2291-0087) about his delayed claim of Rs 1,84,000."

  After:   "Draft a polite reply to a customer whose health insurance
            claim is delayed by two weeks. Apologise, explain we need
            one more hospital document, and give a new date."
```

Rendavadhu prompt-um ore alavukku nalla draft tharum. Unmaiyaana peyar, details-a unga sondha email system-la neengalae serthukkonga.

Data-va eduthuda vera vazhigal:

- Peyargala "Customer A", "Supplier B"-nu maathunga.
- Account numbers, amounts, dates-a eduthudunga illa round pannunga.
- Mulu document-a paste pannaama, situation-a unga sondha vaarthaigal-la summarise pannunga.
- Podhuvaana kelvi kelunga: "Vendor contract-la edhellaam clauses-la risk irukkum?"

### Veliya varradhai check pannunga

AI thappa irukkalaam, pazhasaa irukkalaam, tone-la thappa irukkalaam. Output-a use panradhukku munnaadi check pannunga:

- mukkiyamaana facts, figures;
- ungalaiyo company-yo kashtapaduthura edhuvum;
- adhu unga company-oda unmaiyaana policies, prices, promises-a prathibalikkudha-nu.

### Result-oda owner neenga

AI ezhudhuna report section-la thappaana figure irundhu, adha manager-ku anuppina, adhu ungal thappu. Velaila "AI ezhudhuchu" excuse illa, irukkavum koodaadhu.

### Policy-a follow pannunga, illa kelunga

Unga company-oda AI policy irundhu, adha padinga. Illa-na manager-a kelunga, appo varaikkum mela solra jaagrathaiyaana rules-a follow pannunga.

## Oru worked example

Meena Hyderabad-la oru chinna consulting firm-la associate. Friday maalai 7 manikku, oru client 40-page vendor contract-a email panni, Monday kaalaikulla risky clauses-oda oru-page summary kekkuraanga.

Avanga firm innum edha AI tool-aiyum approve pannala. Vegamaana vazhi, mulu contract-a personal chatbot account-la paste panni summary kekkaradhu. Avanga niruthi, moonu kelvigalai odi paakkuraanga:

| Kelvi | Meena-oda badhil |
| --- | --- |
| Naan enna ulla podaren? | Client-oda vendor-um pricing-um irukkura oru confidential contract. Client-oda firm agreement-la documents-a third parties-kitta share panna koodaadhu-nu irukku. |
| Enna veliya varum? | Adhu line by line check pannanum, appavum. |
| Yaar responsible? | Avanga-um, firm-um, client-kku. |

Adhanaala avanga:

1. Contract-a avangale padichu, liability, termination, payment, data protection clauses-a note pannuraanga.
2. Chatbot-a structure-kku mattum use panraanga: confidential edhaiyum paste pannaama, "Oru typical vendor services agreement-la buyer-ku edhellaam clauses romba risk-aa irukkum?"-nu kekkuraanga. Ettu clause types-oda useful checklist kidaikkudhu.
3. Andha checklist-a vachu summary-a avangalae ezhudhuraanga, adhu oru vithiyaasamaana auto-renewal clause-a kandupidikka help pannuchu.
4. Monday, indha maadhiri velaikku firm oru tool-a approve pannanum-nu manager-kitta sollraanga.

Manager otthukkuraar. Firm pinnaadi client work-ku oru enterprise assistant-a license panni, clients-oda documents-la use panradhukku munnaadi avangalodaya consent-a kekkudhu.

Paste pannadha vida Meena-oda summary-ku adhigam time aachu, aana client-oda nambikkaya kaapaathi, AI-kitta irundhu unmaiyaana help-um vaangittaanga.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Work documents-ku personal AI accounts use panradhu.** Oru thadava aanaalum, data company control-ku veliya pogudhu.
- **"Idhu oru thadava mattum"-nu personal data paste panradhu.** Adha eduthudunga illa maathunga; draft ore alavukku nalla irukkum.
- **AI output-a nallaa padikkaama forward panradhu.** Figures, peyargal, promises-a check pannunga.
- **Policy illa-na rules illa-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Laws-um client contracts-um innum apply aagum.
- **AI use-a maraikkiradhu.** Manager-kitta openaa irundha ungalai kaapaathum, team muzhuvadhum kathukka help pannum.
- **Paarkkaamalae screenshots paste panradhu.** Screenshots-la neenga share panna ninaikkaadha peyargal, emails, numbers irukkalaam.

## Practice pannunga

Ethics of AI course-la privacy, responsibility chapters-a padinga, keezha link irukku.

1. **Classify pannunga.** Velaila illa college-la neenga handle panra aindhu information-a list panni, ovvonnayum paste pannalaam, approved tools mattum, illa eppavum vendaam-nu classify pannunga.
2. **Strip panni try pannunga.** Oru real task-a edunga, ovvoru peyar, number, confidential detail-ayum eduthuttu AI tool-la pannunga. Appavum useful help kidaichudha?
3. **Policy-a kandupidinga.** Unga organisation-oda AI policy-a kandupidinga, illa adhu pathi manager-kitta kekka moonu kelvigal ezhudhunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Unga workplace-la AI tool-la edhai paste pannalaam, edhai pannaakoodaadhu-nu, result-ku avanga yen poruppu-nu oru colleague-kku explain panna mudiyum.
