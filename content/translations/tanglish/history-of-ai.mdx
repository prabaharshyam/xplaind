## Idhu enna

Artificial intelligence pudhusa theriyum, aana adhu namma perumbaalaana amma appa-vai vida pazhasu. Andha peyar **1956**-la, United States-la Dartmouth College-la nadandha oru summer workshop-kaaga vaikkapattadhu. Anga researchers seekiram machines-a manushan maadhiri reason panna vaikkalaam-nu nambinaanga. "Seekiram"-nu solradhula thappaa ponaanga, aana avanga aarambicha field ezhubadhu varushama odikittu irukku.

AI aarvam, emaatram-nu alaigal-la ponadhu, moonu periya approaches-um irundhudhu:

| Varusham | Enna nadandhudhu | Era |
|---|---|---|
| 1956 | "Artificial intelligence"-nu peyar vaikkapattadhu | Rules |
| 1980-gal | Expert systems boom, appuram oru AI winter | Rules |
| 1997 | Chess world champion thorkadikkapattaar | Rules |
| 2012 | Deep learning images-ku work aachu | Learning |
| 2017 | Transformer design publish aachu | Learning |
| 2022 | Chat assistants makkal kitta vandhudhu | Large models |

| Era | Main idea | Typical examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aatkal ezhudhuna rules** (1950-gal-la irundhu 1980-gal varaikkum) | Experts logic-a kaiyaala ezhudhuvaanga | Chess programs, diagnosis-ku expert systems |
| **Data-la irundhu kathukradhu** (1990-gal-la irundhu 2010-gal varaikkum) | Programs examples-la patterns-a kandupidikkum | Spam filters, recommendations, credit scores, image recognition |
| **Romba periya data-la large models** (2017-la irundhu) | Romba periya neural networks, adhigamaana text, images-la irundhu podhuvaana skills kathukum | Chat assistants, image generators, coding assistants |

Indha kadhai therinjaa rendu practical vazhila help aagum. AI-a yaarukkum konjam nimishathula explain panna mudiyum, innaiku solra claims-a nalla judge panna mudiyum, yenna ovvoru era-layum munnaadi over-promise pannirukkaanga.

Indha lesson full-aa oru example-a follow pannum: India-la oru bank mupadhu varushama loan applications-a eppadi check pannirukku.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Rules era

Aarambathula AI knowledge-a rules-aa ezhudha try pannuchu: "patient-ku fever-um rash-um irundha, measles-a yosinga". 1970-gal, 1980-gal-la, medicine, chemistry, business-ku nooru, aayiram kanakkaana rules-oda **expert systems** build pannaanga.

Kuruginna areas-la work aachu, aana easy-aa odanjudhu. Ulagathula ezhudha mudiyaadha alavukku exceptions irukku, oru hospital-ku ezhudhuna rules innoru hospital-la fail aachu. Rendu thadava promises results-a vida munnaadi poi, funding kuraiyichu. Indha slow kaalangalukku, sumaar 1970-gal-layum 1980-gal kadaisiyilum, **AI winters**-nu peyar.

### Learning era

Periya maatram: rules-a ezhudhradha niruthittu, adhai kathukkavaikka aarambicchaanga. Computer-ku answers-oda neraya examples kudunga, adhu pattern-a thaane kandupidikkum. Idhu thaan **machine learning**.

- Spam filters spam, not spam-nu mark panna latchakkanakkaana emails-la irundhu kathukichu.
- Recommendation systems makkal enna click pannaanga, enna vaangunaanga-nu paathu avangalukku enna pidikkum-nu kathukichu.
- Credit scores edhu maadhiri patterns thirumba kattuvaanga-nu predict pannum-nu kathukichu.

1997-la IBM-oda Deep Blue, world chess champion Garry Kasparov-a jeyichudhu. Adhu perumbaalum fast search-um kaiyaala tune panna evaluation-um use pannuchu, aana intelligence thevai-nu nenacha oru task-la computers nalla manushangala jeyikkalaam-nu kaattuchu.

2010-gal-la, neraya layers irukkura periya neural networks use panra **deep learning**, images-um speech-um recognise pannuradhula periya munnetram kuduthuchu. 2012-la oru deep network oru mukkiyamaana image-recognition competition-la romba periya vithiyaasathula jeyichudhu, field neural networks-a nokki thirumbuchu. Idhu possible aanadhukku rendu kaaranam: romba adhigamaana data, apram **GPUs**-nu solra fast chips, adhu arithmetic-a parallel-aa panna mudiyum.

### Large model era

**2017**-la Google-la researchers **transformer**-nu oru pudhu model design-a publish pannaanga. Adhu neenda text-a nalla handle pannum, periya datasets-la efficient-aa train pannalaam. Companies romba periya alavu text-la, adutha word-a predict pannuradhu-nu ore task-oda, periya periya transformer models-a train pannaanga.

Periya size-la, indha models ezhudha, summarise panna, translate panna, kelvikku badhil sola, code ezhudha mudinjudhu. Idha yaarum directly program pannala. Idha vachu build panna chat assistants **2022 kadaisi**-la makkal kitta vandhappo, konja vaaranga-lae latchakkanakkaana makkal AI-a directly use panna aarambichaanga.

### Yen vegamaa aachu

```flow
More data | the internet -> Bigger, better models
More computing | GPUs, data centres -> Bigger, better models
Better designs | transformers -> Bigger, better models
Bigger, better models -> Useful products, more users -> Large investment -> More computing
```

Moonu vishayam serndhu valarndhu, onnai onnu valarthudhu. Nalla results investment-a ilukkudhu, adhu adhigam computing-ku paysa tharudhu, adhu innum nalla results tharudhu.

### Maaradhadhu

Ovvoru era-layum over-promise pannaanga. 1960-gal-la experts irubadhu varushathukkulla human-level AI varum-nu sonnaanga. Ippodhaiya models munnaadi irundha ellathaiyum vida romba capable, aana appavum confident-aa thappu pannum, adha pinaadi lessons-la paarpom. History-la irundhu oru nalla pazhakkam: innaiku systems enna panna mudiyum-nu paathu asandhu pongalaam, seekiram enna pannum-nu confident-aa solra predictions-a sandhegam-oda paarunga.

## Oru worked example

India-la oru bank personal loan application-a varushangala eppadi check pannirukku-nu yosinga.

| Kaalam | Eppadi work aachu | Edhu era |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1990-gal | Loan officer oru rulebook use pannaar: minimum income, oru guarantor, loan-kum income-kum oru fixed ratio, documents-a kaiyaala check panradhu | Rules |
| 2000-gal, 2010-gal | Credit bureaus-um scoring models-um latchakkanakkaana pazhaya loans-la irundhu, edhu pattern thirumba kattuvaanga-nu kathukichu; decisions vegamaavum, consistent-aavum aachu | Learning |
| Innaiku | Language model applicant-oda bank statements-um salary slips-um padichu, credit score pakkathula, officer-ku vinadigal-la oru-page summary ezhudhudhu | Large models |

Ovvoru step-um munnaadi step mela build aachu. Rulebook maraiyala; adhoda rules system-kulla checks-aa aachu. Credit score maraiyala; summary adhu pakkathula irukku.

Moonu kaalathulayum, kashtamaana cases-la final decision oru aal thaan edukkaraanga. Maariyadhu, andha aal evvalavu padikkanum, kanakku podanum, check pannanum-ngradhu. Tools oru manushan-oda decision sutthi irukkura velaiya maathradhu, idhu AI history-la perumbaalum thirumba thirumba nadakkudhu.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **AI chat assistants-oda thaan aarambichudhu-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Idhu ezhubadhu varushama irukkura field; chat assistants adhoda latest, romba theriyura vadivam.
- **Progress orey maadhiri nadakkum-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Adhu vedippugal-aa vandhudhu, naduvula neenda slow kaalangal irundhudhu.
- **Ovvoru prediction-ayum nambradhu.** Experts timelines pathi rendu pakkamum adikkadi thappaa sonnirukkaanga.
- **Terms-a kuzhappikkiradhu.** AI periya field, machine learning adhukku oru approach, language models machine learning-oda oru vagai.
- **Pazhaya approaches sethu pochu-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Rules-um classical machine learning-um innum banks, factories, apps-kulla odudhu, perumbaalum language models pakkathulaye.

## Practice pannunga

Our World in Data-oda AI-oda brief history-a padinga, keezha link irukku, apram Elements of AI-oda first chapter-a padinga.

1. **Kadhaiya sollunga.** Aindhu vaakkiyathula, 1956-la irundhu innaikku varaikkum AI-oda kadhaiya, notes illaama, oru friend illa family member-kitta sollunga.
2. **Unga tools-a era-vaariyaa pirichunga.** Indha vaaram use panna moonu AI tools-a list pannunga: maps, spam filter, chat assistant, payment app-oda fraud alert maadhiri. Ovvonnum perumbaalum endha era approach use pannudhu-nu sollunga.
3. **Pazhaya prediction-a judge pannunga.** Pathu varushathukku munnaadi AI pathi sonna oru prediction-a kandupidichu, adhu nadandhudha-nu decide pannunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** 2017-ku apram AI yen ivvalavu vegamaa munneeruchu-nu, notes illaama, oru nimishathula explain panna mudiyum.
