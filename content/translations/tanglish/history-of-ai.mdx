## Idhu enna

Artificial intelligence pudhusaa thonum, aana namma perumbaalaanavangaloda parents-a vida pazhasu. **1950**-la British mathematician Alan Turing, machines yosikka mudiyumaa-nnu kettaaru. **1956**-la America-la Dartmouth College-la nadandha oru summer workshop-kkaaga "artificial intelligence"-ngura phrase uruvaachu. Indha field ippo ezhubadhu varushathukku mela odikkittu irukku.

Surukkamaa kadhai idhu thaan: mudhalla manushanga intelligence-a rules-aa ezhudhi vachaanga, appuram examples-la irundhu rules-a computers-e kathukka vittaanga, appuram romba adhigamaana text, images, sound-la romba periya models-a train pannaanga. Samebathula andha models problems-a reason panni solve pannavum, actions edukkavum aarambichirukku.

| Approach | Mukkiya idea | Edhula nalladhu | Eppo udaiyudhu | Innaikkum enga use aagudhu |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Manushanga ezhudhuna rules** (1950-galla irundhu 1980-gal varai romba strong) | Experts logic-a if-then rules-aavum search steps-aavum ezhudhuraanga | Oru narrow, fixed area-la thelivaana, check pannakkoodiya mudivugal | Yaarum rule ezhudhaadha oru case varumbodhu | Eligibility checks, billing, tax rules, safety checks |
| **Data-la irundhu kathukkuradhu** (1990-galla irundhu 2010-gal varai) | Badhil therinja niraya examples-la irundhu program pattern-a kandupidikkudhu | Oru thelivaa define panna prediction-a, oru naalaikku million thadavai | Pudhu data, training data-la irundhu vithyaasamaa irukkumbodhu | Spam filters, fraud alerts, credit scores, recommendations |
| **Periya data-la large models** (2017-la irundhu) | Romba periya oru neural network, romba adhigamaana text, images, audio-la irundhu general skills-a kathukkudhu | Padikkiradhu, ezhudhuradhu, coding, pechu, samebathula planning-um tools use panradhum | Oru fluent guess thappaa irundhu, adha confident-aa sollumbodhu | Chat assistants, coding assistants, agents |

Indha varushangala thoraayamaa padinga. Oru era-na perumbaalaana pudhu velai use panna approach-nu artham, kandippaana ellai illa. Pazhaya approaches eppavum maranju poradhilla. IBM-oda chess computer Deep Blue 1997-la world champion-a thorkadichudhu, aana rules approach-a thaan use pannuchu. Banks innum avangaloda pudhu models pakkathulaye rule checks-a oduraanga.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Kelviyum peyarum (1950 mudhal 1956 varai)

Turing "yosikkiradhu"-a define panna try pannala. Adhukku badhilaa, **imitation game**-nu avar sonna oru test-a propose pannaaru, ippo adha Turing test-nu solrom. Oru judge, oru aal koodavum oru machine koodavum text-la chat panraaru. Edhu yaaru-nnu judge-aala nambagamaa solla mudiyalana, andha machine intelligent-aa theriyura oru vishayatha panniyirukku.

Innaikku irukkura chat assistants, oru aalla irundhu pirichu solla kashtamaa irukkura maadhiri pesudhu, aanaalum adippadai thappugal pannudhu. Manushan maadhiri pesuradhum, sariyaa irukkuradhum vera vera vishayam.

1955-la John McCarthy-um avaroda moonu colleagues-um Dartmouth workshop-a propose pannaanga. Machines language use pannavum problems solve pannavum vaikkiradhula oru "significant advance", oru summer serndhu velai panna varalaam-nu avanga ezhudhunaanga. Adhu nambikkaiyaana niraya timelines-la mudhalaavadhu.

### Rules era

Aarambakaala AI-kitta rendu mukkiya tools irundhuchu. **Rules**, udhaaranamaa "patient-kku fever-um rash-um irundha, measles-aa irukkalaam-nu yosi". Appuram **search**, adhaavadhu niraya possible moves alladhu steps-a try panni, oru score vachu best-a therndhedukkiradhu.

1970-gallayum 1980-gallayum, **expert systems** oru expert-oda arivai noothukkanakkaana, aayirakkanakkaana rules-aa adaichu vachudhu. Romba famous-aanadhu MYCIN, blood infections-kku antibiotics suggest panna 1970-galla Stanford University-la build pannadhu. Oru test-la, specialists adhoda advice-a Stanford-oda sondha doctors-oda advice alavukku acceptable-nu rate pannaanga, aanaalum patients mela adhu vazhakkamaa use aagave illa. Adha hospital velaikkulla porutthuradhum, adhu thappu panna yaar poruppu-nnu mudivu panradhum kashtamaa irundhuchu.

Innaikku Andhra Pradesh-la irukkura oru graamathula pirandha Raj Reddy, thodarchiyaana pechai purinjukkira computers-kku munnodiyaa irundhaaru. Computing-oda uyarndha virudhaana 1994 Turing Award-a, expert systems-oda oru leader-aana Edward Feigenbaum kooda avar pagirndhukkittaaru.

Rules yen udainjudhu? Velaiyila rules adhigamaa irukkura endha system-layum idhe moonu kaaranangala paarppeenga:

- **Experts-aala avangalukku therinjadhu ellaathaiyum ezhudha mudiyaadhu.** Niraya skill judgement thaan, judgement nerthiyaana rules-aa maaraadhu.
- **Ulagathula exceptions-kku mudive illa.** Oru hospital-kkaaga ezhudhuna rules innoru hospital-la fail aachu.
- **Rules valara valara modhudhu.** Oru rule serttha innonnu amaidhiyaa udaiyalaam, adhanaala updates medhuvaavum risky-aavum aagudhu.

1997-la Deep Blue, world chess champion Garry Kasparov-a thorkadichudhu. Adhu ovvoru secondum egappatta positions-a search panni, perumbaalum manushanga tune panna methods-oda avatrukku score pottuchu. Adhu rules-um search-um uchathula irundha neram. Aana chess, fixed rules irukkura oru moodiya ulagam. Oru hospital-o bank-o appadi illa.

Rendu thadavai, promises results-a vida munnaadi oduchu, funding kuranjudhu. 1973-la mathematician James Lighthill-oda oru report, AI adhoda periya goals-a adaiyala-nnu UK-oda Science Research Council-kitta solluchu, research panam varandu pochu. 1980-galoda kadaisiyila expert-system business sarinjudhu. Indha medhuvaana kaalangala **AI winters**-nu solrom: periya promises, narrow results, appuram panam vera pakkam poiduchu.

### Neural networks: thannoda nerathukkaaga kaathirundha oru pazhaya idea

Rules koodave innoru idea amaidhiyaa odikkittu irundhuchu. 1950-galoda kadaisiyila Frank Rosenblatt, **perceptron**-a build pannaaru: thannoda inputs mela irukkura weights-a adjust panni examples-la irundhu kathukkira oru simple artificial neuron. 1969-la Marvin Minsky-um Seymour Papert-um ezhudhuna oru famous puthagam, appadippatta neurons-oda ore oru layer evlo limited-nu kaattuchu, aarvam kuranjudhu.

Theervu, niraya layers-a adukkuradhu. 1986-la David Rumelhart, Geoffrey Hinton, Ronald Williams **backpropagation**-a popular aakkunaanga, niraya layers-a ore nerathula train panra oru vazhi. Network oru guess pannudhu, adhu evlo thappu-nnu alakkudhu, andha error-a pinnaadi anuppi ovvoru weight-aiyum konjam adjust pannudhu. 2024-la Hinton-um John Hopfield-um, neural networks-oda machine learning-a saathiyamaakkuna kandupidippugalukkaaga Physics Nobel Prize-a pagirndhukkittaanga.

Appo 1990-galla neural networks yen jeyikkala? Appo irundha computers kodukka mudinjadha vida romba adhigamaana examples-um kanakkum adhukku thevaippattuchu. Data-um hardware-um ready aaguradhukku romba munnaadiye idea ready-aa irundhuchu.

### Learning era

1990-gal, 2000-galla vandha periya maatram: rules ezhudhuradha niruthittu, avatrai kathukka aarambichadhu. Badhil therinja niraya examples-a computer-kitta kodunga, pattern-a adhuve kandupidikkattum. Idhu thaan **machine learning**. Internet pudhu alavula data-va uruvaakki idhukku udhavuchu.

- Spam-no spam illa-nno mark panna million kanakkaana emails-la irundhu spam filters kathukkichu.
- Aalunga edha click pannaanga, edha vaangunaanga-nnu vachu, avangalukku enna pidikkum-nu recommendation systems kathukkichu.
- Pazhaya loans-la endha patterns repayment-a predict pannuchu-nnu credit scores kathukkichu.

Engineer-oda velai, rules ezhudhuradhula irundhu nalla examples segarikkiradhukku maaruchu. Balaveenangalum maaruchu: oru model-kku labelled examples venum, podhuvaa ore oru task thaan pannum, pudhu data vithyaasamaa irundha amaidhiyaa fail aagum, examples-la irukkura endha bias-aiyum copy pannum.

2012-la ippo AlexNet-nu theriyura oru deep neural network, photos-la objects-a adaiyaalam kaanura ImageNet-ngura oru periya contest-la kalandhukkichu. Adhoda top five guesses-la sariyaana badhil 15.3% thadavai illa. Adutha best entry-kku 26.2% thadavai illa. Andha idaiveli field-a **deep learning** pakkam thiruppuchu. Moonu vishayangal sandhichudhu: pazhaya backpropagation idea, oru millionukku mela labelled photos, **GPUs**, adhaavadhu games-kkaaga build panna, thevaiyaana kanakka parallel-aa panra graphics chips.

2016-la DeepMind-oda AlphaGo, Go board game-oda top player Lee Sedol-a naalukku onnu-nnu thorkadichudhu. Adhu learning-aiyum search-aiyum serthuchu: approaches onna onnu replace panradhilla, kalakkudhu.

### Large model era

**2017**-la Google researchers **transformer**-ngura oru pudhu design-a publish pannaanga. Rendu ideas adha special aakkuchu. Mudhalla, **attention**: ovvoru vaarthaiyum arthatha kandupidikka passage-la irukkura matha ella vaarthaigalaiyum paarkkudhu, adhanaala "river bank"-layum "bank loan"-layum "bank" vera vera maadhiri padikkappadudhu. Rendaavadhu, adhu vaarthai vaarthaiyaa illaama muzhu passage-aiyum ore nerathula padikkudhu, adhu GPUs-kku porundhudhu, periya datasets-la training-a practical aakkudhu.

Appuram companies transformers-a ore oru simple task-la train pannaanga: adutha vaarthaiya predict panradhu. Idhukku labels thevaiyilla, yenna endha text-um ekkanave oru worked example thaan. Adhu learning era-oda periya limit-a, adhaavadhu data-vukku label podura selava, neekkuchu.

2020-la, model size, data, computing power moonum serndhu valarumbodhu indha models smooth-aavum predictable-aavum improve aagudhu-nnu researchers kandupidichaanga. Idha **scaling**-nu solrom. Idhu AI selava oru kanakku potta bet-aa maathuchu. Adhe varusham, 175 billion adjustable weights irukkura GPT-3, prompt-la irukkura sila examples-a vachae pudhu tasks pannuchu.

Text predict panna mattum train panna oru model, neenga type panradha thodarndhu ezhudhum, avlo thaan. Adha oru assistant aakka, companies nalla example answers-layum, adhoda replies-kku aalunga kodutha ratings-layum adha innum train pannaanga. Ippadi build panna ChatGPT 30 November 2022-la release aanappo, adhu sumaar rendu maasathula 100 million monthly users-a adainjudhu-nnu UBS bank-oda analysts estimate pannaanga.

### 2023 mudhal 2026 varai: paarkkura, reason panra, act panra models

2023-la irundhu kadhai moonu dhisaiyila nagarndhirukku, innaikku neenga use panra tools-a idhu thaan vilakkudhu.

- **Paarkka, ketkka, pesa.** Models text koodave images-aiyum voice-aiyum edutthukka aarambichudhu. May 2024-la OpenAI-oda GPT-4o, text, audio, images-a ethukkichu, pechula oru aal edutthukkira alavu nerathula sathamaa badhil solla mudinjudhu.
- **Reason panna.** September 2024-la OpenAI, o1-a release pannuchu: badhil solradhukku munnaadi oru problem-a adhiga neram work panna train panna model. Idhu oru pudhu lever-a serthuchu: training-la mattum illaama, badhil solra nerathulayum computing power-a selavu panradhu. January 2025-la Chinese company DeepSeek, R1-a release pannuchu: yaar venumnaalum download pannakkoodiya open weights irukkura oru reasoning model. Reasoning, maths, code, multi-step problems-kku udhavudhu, aana adhu medhuvaanadhu, ovvoru badhilukkum adhiga selavu, adhanaala simple kelvigalukku adhu waste.
- **Act panna.** October 2024-la Anthropic, adhoda model-a oru computer screen-a operate panna vittuchu, pointer-a nagartthi type panna. November 2024-la adhu Model Context Protocol-a release pannuchu: assistants-a tools-odavum data-vodavum connect panra oru open standard. 2026-kkulla periya labs, thangaloda sondha sandboxed computer-la romba neram velai panra **agents**-a kodutthaanga, neenga illaadhappovum, oru schedule-layum kooda. Neelamaana tasks-la avai innum thappu pannudhu, adhanaala avai enna pannalaam-nu aalunga limit poduraanga, results-a check panraanga.

AI science-aiyum maathuchu: 2024 Chemistry Nobel Prize-la paadhi, proteins-oda shape-a predict panra AlphaFold-kkaaga Google DeepMind-oda Demis Hassabis-kkum John Jumper-kkum pochu.

India nerdiyaa indha kadhaikkulla vandhuchu. 7 March 2024-la Union Cabinet, computing, datasets, Indian models, skills-kkaaga ₹10,371.92 crore-oda IndiaAI Mission-a approve pannuchu. February 2026-la India, New Delhi-la AI Impact Summit-a nadathuchu, adhu New Delhi Declaration-oda mudinjudhu. Bengaluru company Sarvam AI, Indian languages-kkaaga build panna Sarvam-30B, Sarvam-105B-ngura rendu models-a arimugappaduthi, appuram avatrai open models-aa release pannuchu.

### Yen vegamaachu

```flow
title: AI-a vegappaduthuna loop
Adhiga data | internet, labels thevaiyilla -> Periya, sirandha models
Adhiga computing | GPUs, data centres -> Periya, sirandha models
Sirandha designs | transformers, reasoning -> Periya, sirandha models
Periya, sirandha models -> Useful products | million kanakkaana users
Useful products -> Periya investment
Periya investment -> Adhiga computing
```

Moonu inputs serndhu valarndhu, onnukkonnu oottikkichu. Sirandha results investment-a izhuthuchu, adhu adhiga computing-kku panam kodutthuchu, adhu sirandha results-a kodutthuchu.

Scaling-kku limits irukku, avatrai therinjukkiradhu thaan oru informed view-a hype-la irundhu pirikkudhu:

- **Selavu.** Size-la ovvoru periya step-kkum pala madangu adhiga computing thevaippattirukku.
- **Data.** Nalla, public, manushanga ezhudhuna text-oda supply limited, adhu computing valarra vegathula valaraadhu.
- **Ottura selavu.** Oru periya model oru thadavai mattum illa, adhu kodukkura ovvoru badhilukkum adhiga selavaagum.

Samebathiya gains vera levers-la irundhum varradhukku idhu oru kaaranam: kashtamaana kelvigalla adhiga neram yosikkiradhu, search alladhu code runner maadhiri tools. AI idhe vegathula improve aagikkitte irukkum-nu yaaraavadhu sonna, endha input thodarndhu valarum, yaar panam koduppaanga-nnu kelunga.

### Edhu maarala

Ovvoru era-um over-promise pannuchu. Dartmouth proposal ore summer-la oru periya advance-a edhirpaarthuchu. 1960-la economist-um AI munnodiyumaana Herbert Simon, irubadhu varushathukkulla oru aal panra endha velaiyaiyum machines panna mudiyum-nu ezhudhunaaru. Innaikku systems munnaadi irundha edhaiyum vida romba capable, aanaalum innum confident-aa thappu pannudhu.

Endha pudhu claim-kkum history moonu kelvigala kodukkudhu:

1. **Innaikku adhu enna pannudhu**, ennoda tasks maadhiri tasks-la, vikkiravar illaadha vera yaaro check panni?
2. **Andha claim edha nambi irukku**: adhiga data, adhiga computing, adhiga panam? Adhu thodarndhu valarumaa?
3. **Adhu thappaa irundha enna aagum**, yaar check panraanga?

## Oru worked example

Muppadhu varushathula oru loan application-a follow pannunga. Pune-la teacher-aa irukkura Meera, moonu varushathukku ₹3 lakh personal loan-kku apply panraanga. Inga irukkura ella numbers-um vilakkathukkaaga mattum.

Interest rate varushathukku 14%-nu vachukkonga. Avangaloda monthly instalment (EMI) sumaar ₹10,253 varudhu. Avanga ekkanave oru scooter loan-kku maasam ₹8,000 kattuuraanga, avanga take-home pay ₹45,000. Avangaloda ella EMIs-um serndhu take-home pay-la 50%-kku keezha irukkanum-nu bank-oda sondha rulebook solludhu-nnu vachukkonga. Avanga total ₹18,253, sambalathula sumaar 41%, adhanaala andha rule-la pass aaguraanga.

| 1990-gal | Oru officer rulebook-a kaiyaala apply panraaru: income, oru guarantor, EMI-to-pay ratio, documents | Ovvoru mudivukkum thelivaana kaaranangal | Salary slip illaadha oru nalla self-employed applicant-a thiruppi anuppudhu |
| 2000-gal, 2010-gal | Oru credit bureau score-um, million kanakkaana pazhaya loans-la irundhu kathukkitta oru scoring model-um | Periya alavula vegamaana, ore maadhiriyaana mudivugal | Credit history illaadha first-time borrowers-oda thadumaarudhu |
| Innaikku, sila lenders-kitta | Oru language model bank statements-aiyum salary slips-aiyum padichu, officer-kku oru page summary ezhudhudhu | Kuzhappamaana documents-a sila secondula padikkudhu | Oru number-a thappaa padichu, adha confident-aa sollalaam |
| 2026, oru bank serkkalaam | Oru agent, Meera-kitta missing documents kettu, checklist-a fill panni, vithyaasamaa theriyura edhaiyum flag pannudhu | Officer-kku manikkanakkaana follow-up-a michappaduthudhu | Yaarum check pannalana oru thappaana reading-a vachu act pannalaam |

Ippo oru failure pidipaduradha paarunga. Oru thadavai mattum vandha ₹60,000 Diwali bonus-a summary monthly income-aa enni, ₹1,05,000-nu report pannudhu-nnu vachukkonga. Appo avanga EMIs income-la verum 17% maadhiri theriyum. Rule check innum avangaloda salary slips-a thaan use pannudhu, adhula ₹45,000 thaan irukku, adhanaala officer andha mismatch-a paarkkuraaru. Pudhu layer padikkiradha vegamaakkuchu; pazhaya layers adhoda thappa pidichudhu.

Ovvoru step-um mundhaiyadhoda mela build aachu. Rulebook automatic checks aachu. Credit score, summary pakkathula ukkaarndhirukku. Ovvoru kaalathulayum kashtamaana cases-la final mudivai oru aal thaan edukkuraaru; maarunadhu, avar evlo padikkiradhu, kanakku poduradhu, thurathuradha kaiyaala panraaru-nguradhu thaan. Oru manusha mudivai suthi tools velaiya maathuradhu, indha pattern AI history muzhukka thirumba thirumba varudhu.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **AI chat assistants-oda aarambichudhu-nnu ninaikkiradhu.** Idhu ezhubadhu varusha pazhaya field. Chat assistants adhoda pudhu, romba kannukku theriyura vadivam.
- **Eras-a kandippaana dates-aa paarkkiradhu.** Deep Blue 1997-la jeyichudhu, aana rules approach-a thaan use pannuchu. Eras andha kaalathoda mukkiya approach-a vivarikkudhu, pazhaya approaches odikkitte irukku.
- **Neural networks pudhusu-nnu ninaikkiradhu.** Indha idea 1950-galoda kadaisiyila irundhu irukku. 2010-kku appuram vandhadhu, periya networks-a work panna vaikka podhumaana data-um computing power-um thaan.
- **Progress seeraa irukku-nnu ninaikkiradhu.** Adhu thideer thideernu vandhuchu, promises results-a vida romba munnaadi oduna ovvoru thadavaiyum winters vandhuchu.
- **Perisaa irundha thaan eppavum badhil-nu ninaikkiradhu.** Selavu, data, ottura selavu scaling-a limit pannudhu, adhanaala thaan reasoning-um tools-um pudhu levers aachu.
- **Ovvoru prediction-aiyum nambhuradhu.** Experts timelines pathi adikkadi thappaa irundhirukkaanga, rendu pakkamum. Oru system innaikku enna pannudhu-nnu vachu judge pannunga.
- **Terms-a kuzhappikkiradhu.** AI-na field, machine learning-na adhula oru approach, language models-na oru vagai machine learning, agent-na tools-um oru goal-um kodukkappatta oru language model.
- **Pazhaya approaches sethuppochu-nnu ninaikkiradhu.** Rules-um classical machine learning-um innum banks-layum apps-layum odudhu, perumbaalum oru language model-a suthi irukkura checks-aa.

## Practice pannunga

Keezha link panniyirukkura Our World in Data-oda AI-oda surukkamaana history-aiyum, Elements of AI-oda mudhal chapter-aiyum padinga.

1. **Kadhaiya sollunga.** 1950-la irundhu 2026 varaikkum AI-oda kadhaiya anju vaakkiyathula, notes illaama, oru friend-kittayo family member-kittayo sollunga. Oru AI winter-aiyum, 2023-kku appuram maaruna oru vishayathaiyum serthukkonga.
2. **Unga tools-a approach vachu piringa.** Indha vaaram neenga use panna moonu AI tools-a list pannunga, maps, oru spam filter, oru payment app-oda fraud alert maadhiri. Ovvonnum mukkiyamaa endha approach-a use pannudhu, edhu adha fail aakkum-nu sollunga.
3. **Oru pazhaya prediction-a judge pannunga.** Pathu varushathukku mela munnaadi AI pathi sonna oru prediction-a kandupidinga. Adhu nadandhuchaa, endha input-a (data, computing, designs) adhu thappaa kanichudhu?

**Mudinjadhu-nnu eppo sollalaam:** 2017-kku appuram AI yen ivlo vegamaa progress aachu-nnu notes illaama oru nimishathula explain panna mudiyum, andha progress-kku irukkura oru limit-a solla mudiyum.
