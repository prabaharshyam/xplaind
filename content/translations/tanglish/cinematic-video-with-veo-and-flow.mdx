## Idhu enna

**Veo** enbadhu Google DeepMind-oda **text-to-video** models kudumbam. Oru shot-a describe pannaa, allathu images kuduthaa, adhu oru short, realistic clip-a generate pannum. Veo 3 (2025)-la irundhu clips **synchronised sound**-oda varudhu: picture-kku porundhura ambience, effects, pesura varigal.

**Flow** enbadhu Google-oda AI filmmaking studio. Neenga oru director maadhiri velai seireenga: shots-a generate panreenga, characters-aiyum products-aiyum marupadi use pannakkoodiya references-aa vachirukkeenga, seekkiramaa mudinjidura shots-a extend panreenga, clips-a scenes-aa adukkureenga.

Flow-kkulla irukkura models adikkadi maarum. 28 November 2026 nilavarappadi, Google-oda Flow help pages ippadi solradhaa report aagiyirukku:

- **Gemini Omni Flash 1.2**, 5 November 2026 annaikku release aanadhu; pudhu projects-kku Google suggest panra rendu models-la idhu onnu. Idhu Veo model illa. Idhu 3-la irundhu 15 seconds varai clips pannum; lip-sync aana dialogue, neenga thaerndhedukkura voices, erkanave irukkura clips-a edit panradhu ellaam idhula irukku.
- **Veo 3.1 Lite** innonnu; thodarndhu irukkapporra orae Veo model idhu thaan. Idhu sound-oda 4, 6 allathu 8 seconds clips pannum, 720p-la Omni-a vida oru second-kku kuraivaana credits-la.
- **Veo 3.1 Fast**, **Veo 3.1 Quality**, **Omni Flash 1.1** moonum 5 December 2026 annaikku Flow-la irundhu retire aagudhu. Inga link panniyirukkura videos udpada niraiya tutorials innum avatrai thaan kaattudhu. Avangaloda directing advice innum porundhum; peyargalum vilaigalum mattum thaan maariyirukku.

### Model oru clip-a eppadi uruvaakkudhu

Veo oru **diffusion model**: Veo 3 latent diffusion use pannudhu-nnu adhoda model card solludhu, Veo 3.1 Lite Veo 3 mela build aanadhu. Adhu video-voda oru compressed form-la random noise-la irundhu aarambichu, unga prompt-um images-um vazhikaatta, nerathula onnoda onnu porundhura frames varra varaikkum noise-a konjam konjamaa neekkum. Oru director-kku idhula irundhu rendu vishayam theriyudhu:

- Edhu *paarkka* sariyaa irukkum-nu thonudho adhai thaan model predict pannum, edhu unmaiyo adhai illa. Neenga kaattalai-nnaa, unga nija saree allathu logo eppadi irukkum-nu adhukku theriya vazhiye illa.
- Ovvoru generation-um oru pudhu random draw, adhanaala adhae prompt ovvoru thadavaiyum vera take-a kudukkum. Adhanaala pala takes edunga, reference images-a nambunga.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Videos-aa illa, shots-aa yosinga

Ovvoru generation-um sila seconds-oda oru shot; oru 30-second ad-na editor-la inaicha anju, aaru shots. Mudhalla oru **shot list** ezhudhunga: enna paarkkurom, camera, sound, ethana seconds. Adhu thaan unga budget-um kooda, yenna credits oru second-kko oru clip-kko charge aagudhu.

Oru 30-second Reel-kku nalla default: oru establishing shot, rendu moonu detail shots, oruthar product-a use panra oru shot, oru title card. Generate panra shots-a 4-la irundhu 8 seconds-kkulla vainga: avai kuraivaa selavaagum, kuraivaa fail aagum, easy-aa onnaa cut aagum.

### Oru cinematographer pesura maadhiri prompts ezhudhunga

Oru strong prompt orae oru action irukkura oru shot-a describe pannum. Adhu aaru vishayangalai cover pannum:

| Element | Enna sollanum | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | yaar allathu enna, maaraadha details-oda | an elderly weaver with silver hair, cream kurta |
| Action | oru thelivaana action | slides the shuttle across the loom |
| Setting | idam, neram, vaanilai | a dim workshop in Varanasi, morning light through a window |
| Camera | shot size, angle, movement, lens | close-up, slow dolly in, 50 mm, shallow depth of field |
| Lighting, look | mood, colour, texture | warm window light, low contrast, gentle film grain |
| Sound | ambience, effects, dialogue | clack of the loom, distant temple bells, no music |

"Make it look like a movie"-ngradhukku model-kku endha arthamum illa. Adhukku badhilaa craft-a peyar solli kurippidunga: lens (35 mm, anamorphic), movement (slow dolly in, handheld, drone push), lighting (low-key, practical lamps, golden hour), texture (film grain, 24 fps motion blur). Ungalukku *vendaadhadhu* enna-nnum sollunga: "no text on screen", "no music", "no people".

Dialogue-kku, oru shot-la ovvoru speaker-kkum quotes-kkulla oru short line kudunga, yaar pesuraanga-nnum sollunga. Flow-la Omni Flash-um ingredients-um use pannumbodhu, prompt box-kku keezha oru generation-kku oru voice-a thaerndheduthu, prompt-la adhai kurippidalaam; adhanaala oru character adhae voice-laye pesum.

### Mudhalla aspect ratio-va thaerndhedunga

Instagram Reels, Stories, YouTube Shorts, WhatsApp Status-kku portrait 9:16 use pannunga; YouTube videos, websites, presentations-kku landscape 16:9. Flow-oda help page Veo 3.1 Lite-kku rendaiyum list pannudhu, Omni Flash 1.2-layum rendum irukku-nnu reports solludhu; unga mode-la 9:16 irukkaa-nnu check pannunga. Mudhal take-kku munnaadiye adhai set panni, ellaa shots-kkum adhaiye vainga: landscape-a appuram portrait-aa crop pannaa frame-la sumaar moonula rendu pangu poyidum, pala neram unga subject-oda mugathoda serndhu.

### Generate panra moonu vazhigalai use pannunga

- **Text to video:** prompt mattum. Exact thotram mukkiyam illaadha establishing shots-kkum moods-kkum nalladhu.
- **Frames to video:** neenga mudhal frame-a kudukkureenga, venumnaa kadaisi frame-aiyum. Composition-um, movement-oda aarambamum mudivum unga control-la irukkum. Product shots-kkum continuity-a sari pannavum best.
- **Ingredients to video** (references-nnum solvaanga): oru character, oru product, oru style frame maadhiri pala images kudukkureenga, model avatrai consistent-aa vachirukkum. Shots muzhukka adhae aal allathu adhae product venumnaa best.

Rendu finishing tools-um udhavum. **Extend** seekkiram mudinjidura oru clip-kku seconds-a serkkum, **editing** (Omni Flash 1.2) erkanave irukkura oru clip-a instruction vachu maathum, udhaaranamaa "make it dusk".

### Shot-kku aetha model-a thaerndhedunga

| Shot-kku enna venum | Mudhalla try panna vendiya model, November 2026 nilavarappadi | Yen |
|---|---|---|
| Maaraama irukka vendiya character allathu product | ingredients-oda Omni Flash 1.2 | pala references edukkum, clips-a edit panna mudiyum |
| 8 seconds-a vida neelamaana shot | Omni Flash 1.2 | paid plans-la 15 seconds varai, adhiga rate-la |
| Dialogue | ingredients-um thaerndhedutha voice-um kooda Omni Flash 1.2 | lip-sync; oru generation-kku oru thaerndhedutha voice |
| Simple establishing allathu mood shot, 8 seconds varai | Veo 3.1 Lite | oru second-kku kuraivaana cost, sound serndhae varum |
| Oru idea-va malivaa test panna | 360p-la Omni Flash 1.2 | oru second-kku 1 credit |

Idhai oru starting point-aa eduthukkunga: oru muzhu ad-kku commit panradhukku munnaadi, oru shot-a rendu models-layum run panni compare pannunga.

### Characters-aiyum products-aiyum consistent-aa vainga

AI video-la consistency thaan romba kashtamaana pagudhi; amateur velaiyaiyum professional velaiyaiyum ellaathaiyum vida adhigamaa pirikkiradhum adhu thaan.

1. Ovvoru character-kkum product-kkum **oru strong reference image-a uruvaakkunga**. Nija product-a seeraana daylight-la photo edunga. Oru character-kku, thelivaana, munpakkam paarkkura oru image-a generate pannunga (Flow-la Google-oda image models-um irukku; AI image generation pathina B2 lesson-a paarunga).
2. Adhu varra **ovvoru shot-layum adhae reference-a attach pannunga**, ingredient-aavo first frame-aavo.
3. **Adhae short description-a ovvoru prompt-layum paste pannunga**, vaarthaikku vaarthai: "Meera, 25, short curly hair, mustard kurta, silver jhumkas".
4. **Frames vachu shots-a inainga.** Shot 4-oda kadaisi frame-a shot 5-oda mudhal frame-aa use pannunga.
5. **Ovvoru retry-kkum oru vishayathai mattum maathunga.** Rendu moonu takes eduthu best-a vachukkunga. Oru take fail aanaa, oru vishayathai maathunga; prompt, model, reference moonaiyum orae nerathula maathinaa, edhu udhavuchu-nnu solla mudiyaadhu. Adjectives-a vida specific instructions nallaa velai seiyum: "hands stay on the loom", "no text on screen".

### Models edhula weak-nu therinjukkunga

Complex motion, muzhukka sariyaana text, edits muzhukka full consistency idhellaam innum kashtam thaan-nu Gemini Omni Flash-kkaana Google-oda model card solludhu. Matha rows podhuvaana practice. Idhukku aetha maadhiri plan pannunga.

| Weakness | Enna theriyum | Workaround |
|---|---|---|
| On-screen text, logos | spelling thappaana words, valanja letters | ellaa text, prices, logos-aiyum editor-la serunga |
| Complex motion | vegamaana action-la kai kaal valaiyum, objects onnoda onnu kalakkum | oru shot-kku oru simple action, medhuvaana moves |
| Hands, fine detail | visithiramaana viralgal, objects-oda kalakkura kaigal | simple hand moves; extra takes-kku budget vainga |
| Crowds | mugangal blur aagum, repeat aagum | munnaadi konjam per, pinnaadi oru soft crowd |
| Shots-kku naduvula drift | oru dress allathu mugam colour allathu shape maarum | adhae reference, description-a reuse pannunga; frames to video use pannunga |
| Neelamaana dialogue | lip-sync vilagum, words repeat aagum, voice maarum | oru shot-kku oru short line, oru fixed voice |

### Generator-kku veliyae edit pannunga

CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere allathu Flow-oda scene tools-la clips-a cut pannunga. Music, voice-over, ellaa text, logo, title card-aiyum anga serunga. Loudness-a samamaakkunga, yenna ovvoru clip-oda sound-um thanithaniyaa mix aagiyirukkum.

Monthly credits ovvoru billing cycle-layum reset aagum, adutha maasathukku pogaadhu.

### Publish panradhu: labels, watermarks, rights

Oru ad live aagradhukku munnaadi idhaiyellaam check pannunga (28 November 2026 nilavarappadi).

- **Watermarks.** Ovvoru Flow clip-layum **SynthID** irukku; adhu Google-oda tools kandupidikkakkoodiya oru invisible watermark. Neenga Indhiyaala irundhaa oru visible watermark automatic-aa podappadum-nnum Flow-oda help page solludhu; anga AI Ultra subscribers mattum thaan adhai off panna mudiyum-nu press reports solludhu. Adhu mukkiyamaana edhaiyum maraikkaadha maadhiri shots-a frame pannunga, adhai orupodhum crop allathu blur pannaadheenga.
- **Indhiyaavoda IT Rules.** 20 February 2026-la irundhu amalukku vandha amendments "synthetically generated" audio, video-va cover pannudhu. Periya social media platforms oru upload synthetic-aa-nnu users kitta kekkanum, andha badhilai check pannanum, synthetic content-a thelivaa label pannanum, labels allathu metadata-va neekka vidakkoodaadhu. Unga pangu: unmaiyaa badhil sollunga, platform-oda AI label-a use pannunga.
- **Instagram, Facebook.** Photorealistic AI video allathu realistic AI audio post pannumbodhu thannoda AI disclosure tool-a use panna sollalaam, oru "AI info" label-a serkkalaam-nu Meta solludhu.
- **Advertising code.** Indhiyaavoda advertising self-regulator ASCI, ads-la AI content-a label panradhu pathina guidelines-a September 2026-la veliyittuchu; avai sumaar moonu maasam kazhichu amalukku varanum. Avai risk-based: AI content oru buyer-a mislead panna vaaippirukkura idathula "Audio/Video created using AI" maadhiri oru label thevai; consent illaama panna fake endorsements allathu deepfakes label irundhaalum anumadhi illa.
- **Manushanga, brands.** Public figures vendaam, matha companies-oda logos vendaam; oru reference-la endha nija mugam irundhaalum written consent venum.
- **Music.** Personal Reel-la allow aagura oru track ads-kku license aagaama irukkalaam. Advertising-kku clear aana music-a use pannunga, udhaaranamaa Meta-voda Sound Collection (adhoda terms Meta-voda apps-kkulla commercial use-a allow pannudhu) allathu paid ads-a cover panra oru stock licence.
- **Commercial use.** Neenga generate panradhukku Google ownership claim panradhillai-nnu Google solludhu; outputs-a eppadi use pannalaam-ngradhai adhoda Terms of Service thaan govern pannudhu-nnu Flow-oda help page solludhu. Paid campaign-kku munnaadi ippodhaiya terms-a padinga.

## Oru worked example

Rohan oru chinna Banarasi handloom label-kku marketing paarkkuraaru. Andha label-aala oru film crew-kku selavu panna mudiyaadhu, aanaa Diwali-kku munnaadi avarukku oru 30-second Instagram Reel venum. Avarkitta Google AI Pro irukku (maasam ₹1,950, 1,000 Flow credits, 28 November 2026 nilavarappadi). Indha numbers planning-kkaana oru illustration thaan, measure panna results illa.

**Step 1: shot list,** ellaamae portrait 9:16-la.

| Shot | Enna paarkkurom | Mode | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Workshop theruvula vidiyarkaalai, oru cycle kadandhu pogudhu, koyil mani | text to video | 6 s |
| 2 | Loom-la weaver-oda kaigaloda close-up, shuttle-oda clack sattham | ingredients (weaver) | 5 s |
| 3 | Loom-la gold, red silk threads, slow dolly in | ingredients (saree photo) | 5 s |
| 4 | Bengaluru flat-la Meera box-a thirakkuraanga, cardboard sarasarappu | ingredients (Meera, saree) | 6 s |
| 5 | Mirror munnaadi Meera saree-a kattuuraanga | ingredients (Meera, saree) | 5 s |
| 6 | Title card: brand name, "Handwoven in Varanasi" | editor-la uruvaakkinadhu | 3 s |

Generate panra anju shots mottham 27 seconds; title card editor-la uruvaakkappadudhu.

**Step 2: references.** Nija saree-a daylight-la flat-aa vachu photo edukkuraaru, adhanaala ad unmaiyaana pattern-a kaattum. Meera-ngra oru karpanaiyaana ilam pennukku oru front-facing image-aiyum, weaver-kku onnaiyum generate panraaru; ovvonnukkum oru fixed description, adhai ovvoru prompt-layum paste panraaru.

**Step 3: malivaana drafts.** Framing-aiyum movement-aiyum check panna, 360p-la Omni Flash 1.2 vachu ovvoru shot-kkum oru draft: 27 seconds × 1 credit = **27 credits**.

**Step 4: final takes.** 720p-la ovvoru shot-kkum moonu takes: 27 seconds × 3 credits × 3 takes = **243 credits**.

**Step 5: fixes.** Shot 2-la weaver-kku oru extra viral irukku. Shot 5-la saree border red-la irundhu maroon-aa maaridudhu.

- Shot 2: prompt-a appadiye vachu, "two hands, natural anatomy, hands stay on the loom"-a serthu, innum rendu takes edukkuraaru: 5 × 3 × 2 = **30 credits**.
- Shot 5: innum adjectives serthaa udhavaadhu, yenna avar endha red-a solraaru-nnu model-kku theriya vazhi illa. Border sariyaa irukkura shot 4-la irundhu oru still-a eduthu, shot 5-a andha still-la irundhu **frames to video**-kku maathuraaru. Rendu takes: 5 × 3 × 2 = **30 credits**.

**Total:** 27 + 243 + 30 + 30 = **330 credits**, maasathoda 1,000-la sumaar moonula oru pangu, allathu plan vilaiyila thoraayamaa ₹640 (330 ÷ 1,000 × ₹1,950). Free account-la oru naalaikku 50 credits vachu idhukku aezhu naal aagum; AI Plus-oda maasam 200 credits mattum idhukku podhaadhu.

Veo 3.1 Lite malivaa irukkumaa? Shot 1-kku, aamaa: 6 seconds-kku 10 credits, 720p-la Omni Flash 1.2-la 18. Rohan shot 1-a rendulayum try panraaru, aanaa reference shots-a Omni Flash 1.2-laye vachukkuraaru.

**Step 6: edit, publish.** Best takes-a CapCut-la cut panni, loudness-a samamaakki, ads-kku license irukkura oru sitar track-a serkkuraaru. Brand name-um "Handwoven in Varanasi"-um editor text-aa pogudhu. Visible watermark plain background mela irukku. Upload pannumbodhu Instagram-oda AI kelvikku yes-nu badhil solraaru; label-oda nija sarees-a vachu AI-la panna film idhu-nnu caption solludhu.

Brand references-aiyum descriptions-aiyum vachukkudhu, adhanaala adhoda adutha festival posts avatrula irundhae aarambikkum.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

**Muzhu video-kkum orae vague prompt.** Model oru shot-a thaan pannum, oru film-a illa. Oru shot list plan pannunga, oru shot-kku oru action.

**Shots-kku naduvula descriptions-a maathuradhu.** "A woman in a red saree"-um "a lady in maroon"-um rendu vera vera aalungalaa aayiduvaanga. Adhae exact wording-aiyum adhae references-aiyum reuse pannunga.

**Oru shot-la romba adhigam.** Pala actions, crowds, vegamaana kaigal artefacts-a undaakkum. Shots-a simple-aavum medhuvaavum vainga; cuts vachu energy serunga.

**Model-kitta text, prices, logos kekkuradhu.** Letters valanjidum. Ellaa text-aiyum editor-la serunga.

**Thappaana aspect ratio.** Reel-kkaaga crop panna oru landscape clip frame-la perumbagudhiya izhandhudum. Mudhal take-kku munnaadiye 9:16 allathu 16:9-a thaerndhedunga.

**Oru mosamaana take-kku appuram muzhu prompt-aiyum rewrite panradhu.** Velai senjadhai izhandhuduveenga. Oru vishayathai maathunga, allathu mode-a maathunga, udhaaranamaa sariyaana oru still-la irundhu frames to video.

**Oru pazhaiya tutorial-oda models allathu prices-a nambuuradhu.** Neenga aarambikkira naalla Flow-oda model menu-vaiyum costs-aiyum check pannunga.

**AI-nu maraikkiradhu, allathu ungalukku rights illaadhadhai use panradhu.** Watermark-a appadiye vainga, platform-oda AI kelvikku unmaiyaa badhil sollunga, license aana music-aiyum consent kudutha mugangalaiyum mattum use pannunga.
