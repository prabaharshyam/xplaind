## Idhu enna

**Deepfake**-na oru real person sollaadhadha illa pannaadhadha pannura maadhiri kaattura AI-made illa AI-altered media. Oru film star trading app-a promote panra video. Nadakkaadha oru politician-oda speech. Unga magan "naan kashtathula irukken, udane paisa venum"-nu solra cloned voice.

**Misinformation**-na thappaana illa emaathura information, makkal theriyndhu share pannaalum, theriyaama share pannaalum. AI convincing fakes-a periya alavula, kammi selavula uruvaakka vechudhu: fake notices, fake screenshots, fake news articles, fake voices.

| AI ippo edhellaam fake pannum | Eppadi |
|---|---|
| Mugam | Illaadha oru aal-a uruvaakkum, illa real mugatha maathum |
| Ithazhgal | Real mugatha pudhu words-oda sync pannum |
| Voice | Konja vinaadi audio-la irundhu clone pannum |
| Documents | Fake circulars, letters, receipts |
| Text | Fake news articles, posts |

India-la deepfakes perumbaalum celebrity faces use panra investment scams-la, amma appa, thatha paatti-a target panra voice-clone calls-la, WhatsApp-la share aagura fake government notices-la varudhu.

Indha lesson Lakshmi-oda appa-va follow pannum. Avar Thanjavur-la retire aana teacher, ore vaaraththula oru deepfake video-vayum, oru voice-clone call-ayum sandhikkiraar. Mudiyum podhu, fakes-a kannala kandupidikkaradhu yen podhaadhu, adhukku badhila edha check pannanum, India-la enga report pannanum-nu theriyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Fakes eppadi uruvaakkapaduthu

Konja varushathukku munnaadi oru film studio thevaipatta vishayangala AI tools ippo pannidum:

- Illaadha aatkaloda **mugangalai uruvaakkum**.
- Oru video-la innoru aal-oda udambu mela **oru mugatha maathum**.
- Pudhu audio-ku **ithazhgalai sync pannum**, adhanaala oru real person pudhu words solra maadhiri theriyum.
- Oru chinna recording-la irundhu, sila neram public video illa voice note-la irundhu eduthha konja vinaadi-la irundhu, **oru voice-a clone pannum**.

### Kannala kandupidikkaradhu yen kashtam aagudhu

Pazhaya fakes-la kandupidikkara signs irundhudhu: odd blinking, blurry teeth, porundhaadha lighting, vinodhamaana kaigal, robot voice. Mosamaana fakes-la ippavum varum, aana nalla fakes ippo casual-aa paartha thaan pass aagidum, mukkiyamaa chinna phone screen-la. Detect Fakes maadhiri research projects, nalla fakes-a kandupidikkarathula makkal perumbaalum chance-a vida konjam thaan nallaa irukkaanga-nu kaattudhu.

Adhanaala useful kelvi "idhu fake maadhiri theriyudha?"-nu illa, "**context nikkudha?**"-nu thaan.

### Edhu nalla work aagum: context-a check pannunga

| Check | Ungalai kelungal | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | Yaar mudhalla post pannaanga? Adhu andha aal-oda official account-la, illa nambagamaana news site-la irukkaa? | Forwarded WhatsApp message-la mattum irukkura "news clip" |
| **Plausibility** | Indha aal unmaiyaave idha solluvaaraa? Vegamaa act panna, pay panna, invest panna, share panna thalluthaa? | Oru famous businessman maasathukku 30% returns-nu promise panradhu |
| **Corroboration** | Nambagamaana outlets report panraangalaa? Fact-checkers enna solraanga? | Claim-a search panna, news reports adhai scam-nu sollradhu |
| **Pause** | Oru manineram kaathirundha enakku enna nashtam? | Perumbaalum onnum illa, aana vegamaa act pannina neraya nashtam aagalaam |

Government of India pathiya claims-ku, **PIB Fact Check** adhai verify illa debunk pannum. Independent fact-checkers-um regular-aa publish panraanga.

### Voice-clone scams

Idhu oru script-a follow pannum:

```flow
1. Urgent call or voice note from a "relative" | "I've had an accident" or "I've been arrested" -> 2. Secrecy | "Don't tell Amma"
2. Secrecy -> 3. Pressure | "Send it now, there's no time"
3. Pressure -> 4. Payment | UPI ID, gift card or bank transfer
4. Payment -[your defence]-> Hang up -> Call back on the known number -> Ask for the family code word
```

Moonu pazhakkangal unga family-a kaapaathum:

1. Edhaiyum pay panradhukku munnaadi, andha aal-oda **therinja number-ku hang up panni thirumba call pannunga**.
2. Unmaiyaana emergencies-kku **oru family code word-a otthukkonga**.
3. **Pressure-la eppavum pay pannaadheenga.** Unmaiyaana emergencies rendu nimisha check-a thaangum.

### Report pannunga

India-la cyber fraud-a national cybercrime portal-la illa **1930** helpline-ku call panni report pannunga, fake content-a platform-kum report pannunga. Vegamaa report pannunga: konja manineram munnaadi anuppina paisa-va banks sila neram freeze panna mudiyum.

### Rules

India-oda IT Rules, platforms-a report pannina unlawful content-ku, impersonation serthu, act panna sollum. 2026 February 20-la irundhu amalla irukkura oru amendment, AI-generated audio, images, video-ku clear label irukkanum-nu-m, government illa court order vandhadhum moonu manineramkulla platforms unlawful content-a remove pannanum-nu-m solludhu. Rules maarikitte irukkum, aana mela solra pazhakkangal rules enna sonnaalum work aagum.

## Oru worked example

Lakshmi-oda appa Ramanathan Thanjavur-la retire aana teacher, pala WhatsApp groups-la active-aa irukkaar. Oru maasathukku munnaadi Lakshmi avar kooda irubadhu nimisham ukkaarndhu scams pathi pesinaanga. Indha vaaram, andha pechu irandu thadava payan thandhudhu.

**Thingal: video.** Oru friend, oru well-known business leader "government-approved" investment app-a, "guaranteed 30% monthly returns"-oda recommend panra video-va forward panraar. Video unmaiyaa theriyudhu: voice, mugam, office background.

Ramanathan-ku context checks nyaabagam varudhu. Business leader-oda peyar-a app peyaroda search pannuraar. Pala news sites andha video-va theriyndha deepfake scam-nu report panradhum, company adhai maruthu statement vittiruppadhum paakkuraar. App-a install pannala, news link-a group-ku reply pannuraar.

**Budhan: call.** Avar phone adikkudhu. Peran Karthik maadhiriye kekkudhu: "Thatha, bike-la accident aayiduchu, hospital-kaaga indha UPI ID-ku ₹50,000 anuppunga, Amma-kitta sollaadheenga."

Avar idhayam padaikkudhu. Appuram script-a gavanikkuraar: urgent, ragasiyam, ippove pay pannu. Thirumba call pannuren-nu sollittu, hang up panni, Karthik-oda number-a dial pannuraar. Karthik Chennai-la irukkura college hostel-la irundhu, romba nallaa irukkaan-nu pesuraan.

| Message | Warning signs | Avar enna check pannaar | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Investment video | Guaranteed periya returns, "government-approved", forward mattum | Leader-ukkum app-ukkum news-la search pannaar | Theriyndha deepfake scam; group-ku warn pannaar |
| Peran-oda call | Urgent, ragasiyam, ippove pay, pudhu UPI ID | Hang up panni Karthik-a directly call pannaar | Karthik safe-aa irundhaan; scam report aachu |

Caller-oda number-a cybercrime portal-la report panni, friends group-la oru warning post pannuraar. Family ippo emergencies-ku oru code word use panradhu.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Glitches kandupidikkaradhai nambradhu.** Nalla fakes-la illa. Source-um context-um check pannunga.
- **Urgency-la act panradhu.** Scammers ungalai venumne arasarappaduthuvaanga. Konjam nillunga, verify pannunga.
- **Check pannaama forward panradhu.** Nalla ennathoda share pannaalum, adhu kedu paravum.
- **Therinja voice, mugatha nambradhu.** Rendumey fake pannalaam. Therinja number-la thirumba call pannunga.
- **Report pannaama irukkaradhu.** Reports adhe scam matravangala adaiyaama thada udhavum, vegamaa report pannina paisa meetkavum udhavalaam.
- **Vayasanavangalai mattum target panraanga-nu nenaikkiradhu.** Students-um professionals-um kooda job, loan, investment fakes-la maattikkuraanga.

## Practice pannunga

Detect Fakes project-a try pannunga, keezha link irukku, PIB Fact Check-oda recent posts-a paarunga, oru lateral reading lesson-a pannunga.

1. **Ungalai test pannikkonga.** Detect Fakes test-a eduthu, score-a note pannunga. Perumbaalum makkal aachariyappaduvaanga.
2. **Oru viral claim-a check pannunga.** Unga family WhatsApp groups-la irundhu oru viral claim-a edutthu, lateral reading illa fact-checker vachu check pannunga.
3. **Unga family-a kaapaathunga.** Unga family-kooda oru emergency code word-a otthukkonga, oru vayasaana relative-ku "hang up and call back" rule-a avanga mozhila sollikodunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Therinja voice-la vara urgent paisa request-a eppadi handle pannanum-nu, oru vayasaana relative-ku avanga mozhila explain panna mudiyum.
