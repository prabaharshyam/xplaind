## Idhu enna

Perumbaalaana makkalukku AI assistants romba neram micham panra idam writing thaan. Language model adhoda mulaiyil text uruvaakkura machine, adhanaala naalu dhinasari writing velaigalla romba nallaa irukkum:

| Vela | Neenga enna kudukkanum | Enna kidaikkum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Drafting** | Rough notes, reader, goal | Email, report section, post-oda first version |
| **Rewriting** | Ungaloda text-um eppadi maathanum-nu | Kuraivaa, clear-aa, anbaa, formal-aa illa simple-aa |
| **Translating** | Text-um target language-um | Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, vera languages-la illa experts illaadhavangalukku plain English-la oru version |
| **Summarising** | Neenda document, thread, transcript | Key points, decisions, actions |

Naalu velaila-yum **neengathaan author**. Enna solla-nu neenga decide panreenga, check panreenga, poruppu edukkureenga. Assistant vegamaa ezhudhura junior writer, email-la sign panravar illa.

```flow
dir: LR
Ungaloda notes | facts, reader, goal -> AI draft | vegamaa oru fluent first version
AI draft | vegamaa oru fluent first version -> Ungaloda check | facts, tone, promises, ungaloda voice: mukkiyamaana step
Ungaloda check -> Neenga anuppureenga | ungaloda peyar
```

Indha lesson-la Kolkata-la logistics company-la operations lead Fatima-va follow pannuvom, avanga kuzhappamaana oru meeting-a client update-aa maathuraanga. Lesson mudiyum podhu, check pannaadha edhaiyum anuppaama, vegamaa draft, rewrite, translate, summarise panna mudiyum.

## Idhu eppadi work aagudhu

### Unga notes-la irundhu drafting

Nalla drafting prompt-la moonu pagudhi irukkum: **ungaloda points, reader, reader enna seiyanum-nu virumburinga.** Mukkiyam-na length limit-um serunga.

> "Here are my notes from the vendor meeting. Draft an email to my manager summarising the three options and recommending option 2. She is busy, so keep it under 150 words and put the recommendation first."

Reader-um goal-um illaama, generic text kidaikkum, adha neenga mudhalla irundhu marubadiyum ezhudhanum.

### Ungaloda voice-a vechukkradhu

Machine ezhudhina maadhiri irukkura text-a readers kandupidichiduvaanga: "I hope this email finds you well", "delve into", moonu adjectives-oda neenda lists. Adha thavirkka:

1. Ungaloda pazhaya oru rendu messages-a paste panni, adhe style-la ezhudha sollunga.
2. Plain, short sentences kelunga.
3. Neenga ennaikkum sollaadha phrases-a delete pannunga.

### Rewriting

Facts munnaadiyae ungaludhu, adhanaala rewriting perumbaalum drafting-a vida payanullathu:

- "Idha konjam anbaa, aana urudhiyaa maathu."
- "Technical background illaadha customer-ku idha explain pannu."
- "Idha paadhi length-ku kuraichu; ovvoru number-aiyum vechukko."

### Translating

Nalla assistants Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, vera Indian languages-a konjam nallaa handle pannum, aana quality maarum, especially formal writing, legal terms, regional usage-la. Official letters, health illa legal info, neraya customers-ku messages maadhiri mukkiyamaana edhukkum, nallaa theriyura oruthar check pannanum.

### Summarising

Enna vagai summary venum-nu sariyaa sollunga. "Idha summarise pannu"-nu sonna, theliva illaadha oru paragraph kidaikkum. Idhu neenga act panna mudiyura onnu tharum:

> "From this transcript, list: decisions made, open questions, and actions with owner and deadline. Quote the exact words wherever a deadline was agreed."

Summaries mukkiya details-a vittudalaam, deadlines-a mellusaa maathalaam, sollaadha points-a karpanai pannalaam. Neenga act panna pora edhaiyum source-oda check pannunga.

### Poruppu, privacy

Neenga anuppina, neenga sonnadhu thaan. Anuppradhukku munnaadi check pannunga:

| Check | Yen |
| --- | --- |
| Names, titles | Models peyar-a maathum, thappaa ezhudhum |
| Numbers, dates | Amaidhiyaa nadakkura romba common thappu |
| Promises, commitments | Draft neenga seiya mudiyaadha edhaiyaavadhu promise pannalaam |
| Tone | Mosamaana seidhiku santhosham-aana text padikkave sari illa |
| Confidential content | Andha data-ku unga organisation approve panna tools mattum use pannunga |

## Oru worked example

Fatima Kolkata-la logistics company-la operations lead. Oru mukkiyamaana client-ku pora shipment late aaguthu. Avanga kitta oru mani neram meeting transcript-um, adhu pathi 40 emails-um irukku, client innaiku update venum-nu ketkuraanga.

**Summarise.** Company approve panna assistant-la transcript-a paste panni kekkuraanga: "Summarise into decisions made, open issues, and actions with owner and deadline. Quote the exact words where deadlines were agreed." Rendu nimishathula oru page summary kidaikkudhu.

Moonu deadlines-a quotes-oda check panraanga. Oru summary line "Friday"-nu sollum, aana quote panna vaarthaigal "end of next week". Adha sari panraanga. Andha oru check thaan kaalaila romba mathippulla nimisham.

**Draft.** Adutha: "Draft an update email to the client in a calm, professional tone. Apologise for the delay without blaming our partner, give the new delivery date of 14 November, and offer a call this week. Under 150 words." Style example-aa avanga pazhaya oru email-a paste panraanga.

**Edit.** Draft nallaa irukku. Form letter maadhiri irukkura "We sincerely regret any inconvenience this may have caused"-a eduthuduraanga, client-oda festival-season stock pathi oru personal line serkuraanga, date-a innoru thadava check panraanga.

| Step | AI-oda neram | Vazhakkamaana neram |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transcript, emails padichu decisions-a kandupidikkradhu | 10 nimisham (summary-um checks-um) | 60 nimisham |
| Client email ezhudhuradhu | 15 nimisham (draft-um edits-um) | 45 nimisham |
| Mothham | 25 nimisham | sumaar 1 mani 45 nimisham |

Neram micham panninaanga, mukkiyamaana ellaathaiyum check pannaanga: deadline, date, tone.

## Podhuvaa panra thappugal

- **Padikkaama anuppradhu.** Thappugalum vichithiramaana phrases-um ungaloda peyaroda poidum.
- **Bland-aana, machine maadhiri text.** Style example kudunga, ungala maadhiri irukkura varaikkum edit pannunga.
- **Decisions-ku summaries-a nambradhu.** Neenga act panna pora details-a, especially deadlines, numbers-a, source-oda check pannunga.
- **Mukkiyamaana messages-ku check pannaadha translations.** Nallaa theriyura oruthar-a padikka sollunga.
- **Confidential documents-a approve aagaadha tools-la paste panradhu.** Unga organisation-oda rules-a follow pannunga.
- **Romba neenda output.** Word limit kelunga. Kuraivaa irukkradhu perumbaalum nallaa, especially busy readers-ku.

## Practice pannunga

Unga school illa velai idam allow panra edhaavadhu assistant-a use pannunga, nalla pazhakkangalukku keezha link panni irukkura AI Fluency course-a paarunga.

1. **Draft pannunga, edit pannunga.** Ungaloda rough notes-la irundhu oru real email-a draft panni, ungala maadhiri irukkura varaikkum edit pannunga. Evvalavu maatrangal pannineenga, endha vagai-nu (facts, tone, style) count pannunga.
2. **Summarise pannunga, check pannunga.** Oru neenda article illa report-a decisions, numbers, open questions-aa summarise pannunga. Moonu points-a original-oda check pannunga.
3. **Translate pannunga, mark pannunga.** Oru sirusaana notice-a ungaluku nallaa theriyura oru Indian language-ku translate panni, ovvoru thappaiyum, ottaadha phrase-aiyum mark pannunga.

**Mudinjadhu-nu eppo sollalaam:** Oru draft-a, ungaloda edits-a, neenga check panna oru summary-a kaattalaam, check pannaama neenga ennaikkum anuppaadha vishayangala sollalaam.
