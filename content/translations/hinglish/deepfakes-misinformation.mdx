## Ye kya hai

**Deepfake** AI se bana ya badla hua media hota hai, jisse lagta hai ki koi asli insaan woh bol ya kar raha hai jo usne kiya hi nahi. Kisi film star ka trading app promote karte hue video. Kisi neta ka aisa bhaashan jo kabhi hua hi nahi. Aapke bete ki cloned awaaz jo kehti hai ki woh musibat mein hai aur abhi paise chahiye.

**Misinformation** galat ya bhatkaane waali jaankari hai, chahe log use jaan-boojhkar share karein ya anjaane mein. AI se convincing fakes bade level par banana sasta ho gaya hai: fake notices, fake screenshots, fake news articles aur fake voices.

| AI aaj kya fake kar sakta hai | Kaise |
|---|---|
| Chehra | Aisa insaan banao jo kabhi tha hi nahi, ya asli chehra badal do |
| Hont | Asli chehre ko naye words ke saath sync karo |
| Awaaz | Kuch seconds ke audio se clone karo |
| Documents | Fake circulars, letters, receipts |
| Text | Fake news articles aur posts |

India mein deepfakes sabse zyada celebrity chehron waale investment scams, mummy-papa aur dada-dadi ko nishaana banaane waali voice-clone calls, aur WhatsApp par share hone waale fake government notices mein dikhte hain.

Ye lesson Lakshmi ke papa ke saath chalta hai, jo Thanjavur ke retired teacher hain, aur ek hi hafte mein ek deepfake video aur ek voice-clone call ka saamna karte hain. End tak aap jaan jaoge ki fakes ko aankh se pakadna kaafi kyun nahi hai, uski jagah kya check karna hai, aur India mein kahan report karna hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Fakes kaise bante hain

AI tools ab aise kaam kar sakte hain jinke liye kuch saal pehle film studio chahiye hota tha:

- **Aise logon ke chehre generate karna** jo exist hi nahi karte.
- **Video mein ek chehra dusre insaan ke sharir par laga dena.**
- **Hont ko naye audio ke saath sync karna**, taaki asli insaan naye words bolta lage.
- **Chhoti recording se awaaz clone karna**, kabhi-kabhi sirf kuch seconds jo kisi public video ya voice note se liye gaye hon.

### Aankh se pakadna mushkil kyun hota ja raha hai

Purane fakes mein pehchaan ke nishaan hote the: ajeeb blinking, dhundhle daant, mismatched lighting, ajeeb haath, robot jaisi awaaz. Sasti fakes mein ye ab bhi dikhte hain, par sabse achhe fakes ab casual jaanch pass kar lete hain, khaaskar chhoti phone screen par. Detect Fakes jaise research projects dikhate hain ki achhe fakes pakadne mein log aksar andaaze se zyada behtar nahi hote.

Toh kaam ka sawaal "kya ye fake dikhta hai?" nahi, balki "**kya context sahi baithta hai?**" hai.

### Kya behtar kaam karta hai: context check karo

| Check | Khud se poochho | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Source** | Pehle kisne post kiya? Kya ye us insaan ke official account ya kisi bharosemand news site par hai? | Aisi "news clip" jo sirf forwarded WhatsApp message mein hai |
| **Plausibility** | Kya ye insaan sach mein ye kahega? Kya ye mujhe jaldi kuch karne, pay karne, invest karne ya share karne ke liye dhakel raha hai? | Koi mashhoor businessman 30% monthly returns ka waada kar raha hai |
| **Corroboration** | Kya bharosemand outlets ise report karte hain? Fact-checkers kya kehte hain? | Claim search karne par news reports ise scam bata rahi hain |
| **Pause** | Ek ghanta ruk jaane se mujhe kya nuksaan hai? | Aam taur par kuch nahi, jabki jaldbaazi mehengi pad sakti hai |

Government of India ke baare mein claims ke liye **PIB Fact Check** unhe verify ya galat sabit karta hai. Independent fact-checkers bhi niyamit roop se publish karte hain.

### Voice-clone scams

Ye ek script par chalte hain:

```flow
1. "Rishtedaar" ki urgent call ya voice note | "Mera accident ho gaya" ya "Mujhe arrest kar liya gaya" -> 2. Raaz | "Mummy ko mat batana"
2. Raaz -> 3. Dabaav | "Abhi bhejo, time nahi hai"
3. Dabaav -> 4. Payment | UPI ID, gift card ya bank transfer
4. Payment -[aapka bachaav]-> Call kaat do -> Jaane-pehchaane number par wapas call karo -> Family code word poochho
```

Teen aadatein aapke parivaar ko bachaati hain:

1. Kuch bhi pay karne se pehle call kaat do aur us insaan ke **jaane-pehchaane number par wapas call karo.**
2. Asli emergencies ke liye **family code word tay karo.**
3. **Dabaav mein kabhi pay mat karo.** Asli emergency do minute ki jaanch jhel leti hai.

### Report karo

India mein cyber fraud national cybercrime portal par ya **1930** helpline par call karke report karo, aur fake content platform ko report karo. Jaldi report karo: bank kabhi-kabhi pichhle kuch ghanton mein bheje gaye paise freeze kar sakte hain.

### Rules

India ke IT Rules platforms se maangte hain ki unlawful content, impersonation sahit, report hone par us par action lein. 20 February 2026 se lagu ek amendment ye bhi kehta hai ki AI-generated audio, images aur video par saaf label ho, aur platforms government ya court ke order ke teen ghante ke andar unlawful content hataayein. Rules badalte rehte hain, par upar ki aadatein rules kuch bhi kahein tab bhi kaam karti hain.

## Ek worked example

Lakshmi ke papa Ramanathan, Thanjavur ke retired teacher hain aur kai WhatsApp groups mein active rehte hain. Ek mahine pehle Lakshmi unke saath bees minute baithi aur scams ke baare mein baat ki. Is hafte us baat ka do baar fayda hua.

**Monday: video.** Ek dost ek jaane-maane business leader ka video forward karta hai, jo ek "government-approved" investment app recommend kar raha hai jisme "guaranteed 30% monthly returns" hain. Video asli lagta hai: awaaz, chehra, office ka background.

Ramanathan ko context checks yaad aate hain. Woh leader ka naam aur app ka naam saath mein search karte hain. Kai news sites us video ko jaana-pehchaana deepfake scam bata rahi hain, aur company ne iske inkaar mein statement bhi nikaala hai. Woh app install nahi karte, aur group mein news link ke saath jawab dete hain.

**Wednesday: call.** Unka phone bajta hai. Bilkul unke potey Karthik jaisi awaaz: "Thatha, bike se accident ho gaya hai, hospital ke liye is UPI ID par ₹50,000 bhej do, Amma ko mat batana."

Unka dil tezi se dhadakta hai. Phir woh script pehchaan lete hain: urgent, raaz, abhi pay karo. Woh kehte hain ki wapas call karenge, call kaat dete hain, aur Karthik ka number milaate hain. Karthik Chennai ke college hostel se uthata hai, bilkul theek.

| Message | Warning signs | Unhone kya check kiya | Nateeja |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Investment video | Guaranteed bade returns, "government-approved", sirf forwarded | Leader aur app ke liye news search ki | Jaana-pehchaana deepfake scam; group ko warn kiya |
| Potey ki call | Urgent, raaz, abhi pay karo, naya UPI ID | Call kaat kar Karthik ko seedha call kiya | Karthik safe tha; scam report kiya |

Woh caller ka number cybercrime portal par report karte hain aur dosto ke group mein warning daalte hain. Ab family emergencies ke liye code word use karti hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Glitches pakadne par bharosa karna.** Achhe fakes mein koi glitch nahi hoti. Uski jagah source aur context check karo.
- **Jaldbaazi mein kaam karna.** Scammers jaan-boojhkar jaldi machaate hain. Ruko aur verify karo.
- **Check kiye bina forward karna.** Achhi neeyat se bhi share karne se nuksaan failta hai.
- **Jaani-pehchaani awaaz ya chehre par bharosa karna.** Dono fake ho sakte hain. Jaane-pehchaane number par wapas call karo.
- **Report na karna.** Reports se wahi scam dusron tak pahunchne se rukta hai, aur jaldi report karne se paise wapas milne mein madad ho sakti hai.
- **Ye maan lena ki sirf buzurg nishaane par hain.** Students aur professionals bhi job, loan aur investment ke fakes mein fas jaate hain.

## Practice karo

Detect Fakes project try karo (neeche link hai), PIB Fact Check ki recent posts dekho, aur ek lateral reading lesson karo.

1. **Khud ko test karo.** Detect Fakes test do aur apna score likho. Zyadatar log hairaan rehte hain.
2. **Ek viral claim check karo.** Apne family WhatsApp groups se ek viral claim lo aur use lateral reading ya fact-checker se check karo.
3. **Apne parivaar ko bachao.** Apne parivaar ke saath emergency code word tay karo aur ek buzurg rishtedaar ko unki bhasha mein "hang up and call back" rule sikhao.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap kisi buzurg rishtedaar ko, unki bhasha mein, samjha sako ki jaani-pehchaani awaaz se aaye urgent paise ke request ko kaise sambhaalna hai.
