## Ye kya hai

AI image tools words se tasveerein banate hain. Zyadatar **diffusion models** hote hain: ye random noise se shuru karte hain aur aapke prompt ke hisaab se use step by step hataate hain, jab tak image na ban jaaye. Kaafi tools **reference images** bhi le sakte hain, jisse aap photo edit kar sakte ho ya kisi insaan ya product ko kai pictures mein ek jaisa rakh sakte ho.

Do tools zyadatar zaroorat poori kar dete hain:

- **Gemini ka image model**, jiska nickname **Nano Banana** hai, Gemini app aur Google ke AI Studio mein. Ye khaaskar **plain words mein editing** ("make it night", "swap the shirt colour") aur edits ke beech chehre aur objects ko consistent rakhne mein achha hai.
- **Midjourney**, jo khoobsurat, artistic images aur strong look ke liye jaana jaata hai, aur website se chalta hai. Isme aspect ratio, result kitna stylised ho, aur variations ke liye settings hain.

```flow
Prompt | subject, details, setting, camera, light, style, aspect ratio -[words se nayi image]-> Generate
Generate -> 4 mein se best chuno -> Upscale
Prompt -[aapki photo + ek instruction]-> Edit | "bags hatao, warmer light, person ko waisa hi rakho"
4 mein se best chuno -[reference ki tarah dobara use karo]-> Ek jaisi images ka set
```

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Photographer ya illustrator ki tarah prompt likho.** Vague prompts se generic images aati hain. Woh cheezein batao jo ek professional tay karta hai:

| Hissa | Kis sawaal ka jawab | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | exactly kaun ya kya | a smiling Malayali grandmother holding a plate of appam |
| Details | kapde, materials, colours | white mundu with a gold border, banana-leaf plate |
| Setting | kahan aur kab | a sunlit Kerala kitchen with copper pots |
| Composition | framing aur angle | medium shot, eye level, subject on the right third |
| Lighting | quality aur direction | soft morning light from a window |
| Style ya medium | photo, illustration, 3D, watercolour | editorial food photography |
| Format | kahan use hogi | 4:5 for Instagram, 16:9 for YouTube |

**Regenerate karne ki jagah edit karo.** Gemini mein photo upload karo aur ek baar mein ek badlaav batao. Ye bhi batao ki kya waisa hi rehna chahiye ("keep the face and pose unchanged"). Kai chhote edits ek bade edit se behtar hote hain.

**Midjourney ki kaam ki settings.** `--ar 16:9` aspect ratio set karta hai. Ek stylize setting tay karti hai ki Midjourney apna artistic look kitna jode. Variations aur remix se aap us result ko nudge kar sakte ho jo lagbhag pasand hai, aur aap image aur style references bhi de sakte ho. Current documentation dekho, kyunki version names aur options badalte rehte hain.

**Consistency.** Kisi character, product ya mascot ke liye ek strong reference image banao, phir har nayi image mein use reference ki tarah dobara use karo aur likha hua description wahi rakho.

**Image se video tak.** Strong still images AI video ka bhi starting point hoti hain. Veo aur Flow waali guide mein aap image ko shot ke pehle frame ki tarah, ya character aur product reference ki tarah use karoge, toh achhi image par lagaya time do baar kaam aata hai.

**Images mein text.** Naye models chhota text pehle se bahut behtar likhte hain, par lamba text ab bhi galat ho jaata hai. Posts aur thumbnails ki headlines ke liye aksar Canva ya Figma mein text jodna tez padta hai.

**Resolution aur upscaling.** Generators ek tay size mein images banate hain. Print, banners ya bade screens ke liye tool ka upscale option ya alag upscaler use karo, aur publish karne se pehle full size par haath, text aur logos jaise fine details check karo; phone par na dikhne waali chhoti galtiyan print mein dikh jaati hain.

**Rights aur labels.** Commercial use ke liye har tool ke terms dekho; paid plans mein aksar ijazat hoti hai. Kaafi platforms ab AI se bani ya bahut edit ki hui images ko label karne ko kehte hain, aur Google apne models ki images mein ek invisible SynthID watermark jodta hai. AI use ke baare mein khul kar batana customers ka bharosa badhaata hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Lakshmi Kochi mein home bakery chalati hain aur unhein ek naye Instagram page ke liye images chahiye, par woh har hafte photographer afford nahi kar sakti.

Woh apne best-selling plum cake ki photo apne phone se kitchen table par kheenchti hain. Gemini mein woh ek-ek karke teen edits karti hain: "replace the background with a rustic wooden table and a cup of coffee", "warmer, softer light like early morning", aur "add a sprig of rosemary on the plate, keep the cake exactly the same". Cake sach mein unka hi rehta hai, jo customers ke saath imaandaari ke liye zaroori hai.

Ek festive banner ke liye woh Midjourney use karti hain: "Christmas window display of a small Kerala bakery at dusk, fairy lights, star lanterns, plum cakes on the shelf, warm glow, cinematic, --ar 16:9". Woh chaar results mein se ek chunti hain aur use upscale karti hain.

Woh Gemini mein ek mascot design karti hain, ek khush cartoon baker, phir us image ko teen aur posts ke liye reference ki tarah use karti hain taaki woh har baar ek jaisa dikhe. Headlines Canva mein lagti hain.

Page pehle din se professional dikhta hai. Unka rule: asli products edited photos hote hain, kabhi generated nahi, taaki customers ko bilkul wahi mile jo woh dekhte hain.

## Aam galtiyaan

**Ek-word prompts.** "A cake" se stock-photo jaisa cake milta hai. Wahi shot describe karo jo aapke dimaag mein hai.

**Ek edit mein kai cheezein badalna.** Gemini ek ya do saaf badlaav paanch ek saath se kaafi behtar follow karta hai.

**Asli products ke "generated photos".** Customers wahi expect karte hain jo woh dekhte hain. Apne products ki asli photos edit karo.

**Asli logon ya brands ka use.** Celebrities, bina ijazat ke asli customers, ya doosri companies ke logos ki images mat banao.

**Zinda artist ka style paise ke liye copy karna.** Artist ka naam lene ki jagah woh khoobiyan describe karo jo aapko pasand hain.

**Aspect ratio ignore karna.** Square mein bani image YouTube thumbnail mein buri tarah crop hoti hai. Generate karne se pehle format tay karo.

**Pehla result maan lena.** Kuch options generate karo, compare karo, aur sabse achhe ko chhote, specific badlaavon se refine karo.
