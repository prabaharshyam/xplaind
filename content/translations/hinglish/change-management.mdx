## Ye kya hai

**Change management** logon ko purane tareeke se naye tareeke par aane mein madad karne ka kaam hai. AI systems ke liye ye cover karta hai:

- ye samjhana ki badlaav kyun ho raha hai aur har insaan ke liye iska kya matlab hai;
- design aur testing mein users ko shaamil karna;
- unke apne real cases use karne wali training;
- shuru ke hafton mein support, jab aadatein banti hain;
- adoption measure karna aur jo logon ko rok raha hai uspar action lena.

ADKAR naam ka ek common model kehta hai ki har insaan ko kyun ki **awareness**, hissa lene ka **desire**, kaise ka **knowledge**, practice mein karne ki **ability**, aur tikne ke liye **reinforcement** chahiye. Adoption ruk jaaye toh dhoondho ki inmein se kaunsa missing hai.

| ADKAR | Har insaan ko kya chahiye | Aap kya karte ho |
|---|---|---|
| **A**wareness | Badlaav kyun? | Leaders wajah aur kya badal raha hai samjhate hain |
| **D**esire | Kya main chahta hoon? | Jobs, blame aur extra kaam par imaandaar jawab |
| **K**nowledge | Ye kaise kaam karta hai? | Unke apne cases par 45 minute ki training |
| **A**bility | Kya main roz kar sakta hoon? | Champions, ek support channel, shuru ki jhanjhat theek karo |
| **R**einforcement | Kya ye tikega? | Results share karo, processes aur targets update karo |

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Chintaon ko naam do.** Typical wali: "Kya ye mujhe replace kar dega?", "AI galat hua toh kya mujhe blame milega?", "Kya iske output ko check karna extra kaam hai?", "Kya mere kaam par nazar rakhi ja rahi hai?" Leaders ki maujoodgi mein imaandaari se jawab do. Agar roles badlenge toh bataao kaise.

**Accountability saaf karo.** AI-assisted decision galat ho toh zimmedaar kaun hai? Aam taur par woh jisne use review aur approve kiya, jaise pehle. Ye likhit mein rakho aur dekho ki review step unhe sahi check karne ke liye zaroori cheezein deta hai.

**Champions ko shaamil karo.** Har team se izzat paane waale users chuno jo early versions test karein aur design ko shape karein. Unka feedback system ko behtar banata hai aur unka samarthan kisi bhi announcement se behtar apne saathiyon ko manata hai.

**Real kaam par train karo.** Unki apni queue ke cases se chhote sessions (45 minute): kaise use karna hai, kis mein achha hai, kahan fail hota hai, kaise check karna hai, feedback kaise dena hai. System ke galat hone ke examples bhi rakho.

**Shuru ke hafton mein support do.** Sawaalon ke liye ek dikhta channel, har team mein ek champion, shuru ki jhanjhat par jaldi fixes. Ignore karne par shuru ki frustrations pakki raay ban jaati hain.

**Measure karo aur action lo.** Per person aur per team usage, feedback aur use na karne ki wajah track karo. Kam use karne waalon se baat karo; unki wajah aksar theek ho sakti hain (ek slow step, ek missing field, ek manager jisne kabhi zikr hi nahi kiya).

**Reinforce karo.** Results share karo (bacha hua time, kam rework), teams ko credit do, aur processes aur targets update karo taaki naya tareeka hi normal tareeka ban jaaye.

**Chintayein aur imaandaar jawab:**

| Chinta | Jo jawab madad karta hai |
| --- | --- |
| "Kya ye mujhe replace kar dega?" | Saaf batao ki roles mein kya badalta hai, agar kuch badalta hai, aur kya rehta hai; leaders jawab dein, sirf project team nahi |
| "Galat hone par kya mujhe blame milega?" | Likhit accountability: officers flagged aur critical fields ka review own karte hain, tool sources side by side dikhata hai |
| "Kya ye extra kaam hai?" | Milte-julte team ke saathiyon se file ke pehle aur baad ka measured time dikhao |
| "Kya mujh par nazar rakhi ja rahi hai?" | Batao ki kya measure hota hai, kyun, aur kaun dekhta hai; adoption team ke hisaab se measure karo, individuals ko rank karne ke liye nahi |

**Adoption dashboard.** Har team aur har hafte ke liye dikhao: tool se hone waale eligible kaam ka share, per item median time, rework rate, aur top feedback reasons. Liye gaye actions ke liye ek chhota note column jodo. Jab koi team tay level se neeche aaye, jaise 50%, toh usi hafte koi us team ke paas jaaye. Dashboard ye dhoondhne ke liye hai ki madad kahan chahiye, teams ko blame karne ke liye nahi.

**Aisi training jo tikti hai.** Team ki apni queue ke cases, jinmein ek aisa jahan system galat ho, aur demo dekhne ke bajay session ke dauraan logon ko khud try karne do. End mein bataao ki sawaal kahan poochne hain aur unka champion kaun hai. Jinse chhoot gayi unke liye session dobara karo, kyunki jo log training skip karte hain, aksar wahi baad mein kehte hain ki tool kaam nahi karta.

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai lender mein, pilot ke teen hafte baad, Anna Nagar branch mein adoption 92% hai aur Tambaram mein 41%.

Aap Tambaram jaate ho aur officers se baat karte ho. Teen wajah saamne aati hain:

- Branch manager ne kaha tha ki kisi bhi figure ki galti "unki zimmedaari hai, AI ho ya na ho", aur kai officers ne tay kiya ki sab kuch dobara type karna zyada safe hai.
- Do officers se training chhoot gayi thi.
- Draft summary mein English headings the jo unke regional credit head ke pasandeeda format se alag the.

Actions: head of operations ek note bhejte hain ki officers flagged aur critical fields ka review karne ke liye accountable hain, aur side-by-side view check karne ka approved tareeka hai; branch manager ek session mein shaamil hote hain jahan officers dikhate hain ki woh fields kaise verify karte hain. Ek repeat training hoti hai. Summary template ko ek regional variant milta hai. Do hafte mein Tambaram 85% par pahunch jaata hai, aur uska median file time 37 se 25 minute par aa jaata hai.

| Branch measure | Visit se pehle | Do hafte baad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tambaram mein adoption | 41% | 85% |
| Tambaram mein median file time | 37 minute | 25 minute |
| Jo wajah theek hui | Blame wala message, chhoot gayi training, template mismatch | Saaf kiya gaya note, repeat training, regional template |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Email se launch.** Ek announcement adoption nahi hai.
- **Job wale sawaal se bachna.** Chuppi afwahein paida karti hai. Imaandaari se batao ki kya badal raha hai.
- **Generic demos par training.** Logon ke apne cases use karo, failures ke saath.
- **Accountability ka saaf na hona.** Agar log blame se darte hain toh woh tool par bharosa aur use nahi karenge.
- **Low adopters ke paas na jaana.** Wajah local hoti hain aur aksar theek karna aasaan hota hai.

## Practice karo

Prosci ka ADKAR model ka description padho.

1. Apne use case ke liye teen groups (front-line staff, unke managers, risk ya compliance) ki mumkin chintayein list karo aur batao ki har ek ko kaise address karoge.
2. Real cases use karne wala 45 minute ka training session plan karo, jisme ek case aisa ho jahan system galat ho.
3. Ek adoption dashboard design karo: har team ke liye kya measure karoge, aur agar koi team 50% se neeche aaye toh kya karoge.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap ek atki hui team ke liye samjha sako ki ADKAR ka kaunsa element missing hai aur use theek karne ke liye is hafte kya karoge.
