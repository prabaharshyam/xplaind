## Ye kya hai

AI projects ke fail hone ki ek aam wajah kharab model nahi hoti. Wajah hoti hai galat problem chunna. Teams aisi problems ke liye shaandaar demos banati hain jo kabhi-kabhar hi aati thi, jinhe simple rule hal kar deta, ya jahan ek bhi galti manzoor nahi thi.

**AI ke liye problem chunna** ka matlab hai ki team ko jutaane se pehle candidate use case ko kuch sawaalon par parkho:

| Sawaal | Aap kya check kar rahe ho |
| --- | --- |
| **Kya problem asli aur valuable hai?** | Ye baar-baar aati hai, dard deti hai aur naapi ja sakti hai |
| **Kya AI sahi tool hai?** | Koi rule, form, behtar search ya process badlaav ise zyada simple tareeke se hal nahi karta |
| **AI galat ho toh kya hota hai?** | Users galtiyan dekh kar sasti mein theek kar sakte hain; aapko har tarah ki galti ka cost pata hai |
| **Kya data aur context hai?** | AI ko jo jaankari chahiye, woh use mil sakti hai |
| **Kya success naapi ja sakti hai?** | Aaj ka baseline hai aur saaf metric hai |

```flow
title: Achhi AI problems wahan hoti hain jahan teeno milte hain
Valuable aur baar-baar -> Achhi AI problem
Galtiyan sasti ya check karne mein aasaan -> Achhi AI problem
AI sahi tool hai -> Achhi AI problem
```

Ye lesson Nikhil ke saath chalta hai, jo Pune ki ek ed-tech company mein product manager hain aur jinki leadership "AI everywhere" maang rahi hai. Lesson ke end tak aap kisi lubhaavne AI idea ko saaf wajahon ke saath reject kar paoge, aur aisa idea chun paoge jahan AI saaf madad karta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### AI se nahi, users se shuru karo

Users se unke kaam ke baare mein baat karo. Dhoondho ki time, paisa ya satisfaction kahan jaa raha hai: woh kaam jo roz ek ghanta leta hai, woh sawaal jiska jawab support hafte mein 200 baar deta hai, woh report jo koi nahi padhta kyunki woh bahut der se aati hai. Uske baad hi poochho ki AI madad karta hai ya nahi.

### AI kab fit hota hai aur kab nahi

| AI aksar fit hota hai jab | AI aksar fit nahi hota jab |
| --- | --- |
| Inputs alag-alag aur unstructured hon: free text, documents, images, speech | Ek simple rule kaafi accurate ho |
| Kaam padhna, likhna, summarise karna, classify karna ya search karna ho | Har jawab bina review ke bilkul sahi chahiye ho, jaise tax ki rakam nikaalna ya legal document file karna |
| Aisa "kaafi achha" jawab jise insaan check kare, phir bhi bahut time bachaata ho | Zaroori jaankari system ko available na ho |
| Bade scale par personalisation matter karta ho | Users pakad nahi sakte ki AI kab galat hai, aur galtiyan mehengi hon |

### Galtiyon ke do pehlu

Har AI faisla do tarah se galat ho sakta hai, aur dono ka cost shayad hi kabhi barabar hota hai:

| Use case | False alarm ka cost | Miss ka cost | Kaunsi galti zyada buri |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Payment par fraud alert | Naraaz customer, ek OTP | Paisa khona | Aksar miss |
| Resume screener | Kamzor candidate par time lagna | Achhe candidate ka anuchit reject hona | Galat reject |
| Spam filter | Asli email spam mein jaana | Inbox mein spam | Aksar false alarm |
| Medical triage | Ek gair-zaroori test | Gambhir bimari ka chhoot jaana | Miss, bahut zyada |

Kaunsi galti zyada buri hai, ye tay karna design ko shape deta hai: thresholds kahan lagaye, kab insaan review kare, aur product user ko kya bataye.

### Chaar bade risks

Product teams har idea ko chaar risks par judge karti hain:

1. **Value:** kya users ise chahenge aur chunenge?
2. **Usability:** kya woh samajh paayenge ki ise kaise use karna hai?
3. **Feasibility:** kya hum ise theek quality, speed aur cost par bana sakte hain?
4. **Viability:** kya ye business ke liye chalta hai, legal, brand aur cost risk milakar?

AI **feasibility** mein extra uncertainty jodta hai: quality aksar try kiye bina pata hi nahi chalti. Real data par quick prototype se ise jaldi test karo.

### Score karo aur compare karo

Candidates ko saath-saath rakho aur har ek ko 1 (kamzor) se 5 (mazboot) tak score do:

| Idea | Value | Frequency | Error tolerance | Data | Feasible | Low risk | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 5 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 24 |
| B | 4 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 2 | 15 |
| C | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 | **28: quick win** |

Prototype ke liye ek ya do chuno, aur likh lo ki baaki ko kyun reject kiya.

## Ek example se samjho

Nikhil Pune ki ek ed-tech company mein product manager hain, jo classes 9 se 12 ke liye online coaching chalati hai. Leadership unse agle quarter tak "AI everywhere" maangti hai. Woh paanch candidate ideas likhte hain aur unhe score karte hain:

| Idea | Value | Frequency | Galat hua toh | Data | Faisla |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Students ke liye doubt-solving chat | High | Roz | Students ko dikhta hai, learning ke liye risky | Course content available | **Prototype**, course content par grounded, report kiye gaye answers par teacher review ke saath |
| 2. Descriptive answers ki AI grading | Teachers ke liye high | Hafte mein | Seedha marks aur fairness par asar; parents vivad karenge | Pehle ke graded answers hain | **Baad mein**, har grade teachers review karein |
| 3. Parents ko auto-likhe weekly progress emails | Medium | Hafte mein | Low: pakadna aasaan, nuksaan kam | Sab system mein hai | **Quick win**: template plus AI ki likhi kuch lines |
| 4. Predict karna ki kaunse students chhod denge | High | Mahine mein | Students par thappa lag sakta hai | Wajahon ka data kamzor hai | **Abhi nahi** |
| 5. Demand ke hisaab se AI se fees tay karna | Saaf nahi | Kabhi-kabhar | Legal aur trust risk | Thoda | **Nahi**: spreadsheet waala pricing analyst theek kaam kar leta hai |

Woh features ki list ke bajaye ye ek page ki table pesh karte hain. Leadership do prototypes aur ek quick win par maan jaati hai, aur har "nahi" ki wajah likhi hui rehti hai.

Teen mahine baad, progress emails se teachers ko aane waale parents ke calls lagbhag ek tihaai kam ho gaye hain, aur doubt-solving prototype do batches ke saath pilot mein hai. Jin ideas ko unhone mana kiya, unhone team ka time chupchaap nahi khaya.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Technology se shuru karna.** User ki takleef se shuru karo.
- **Simple solutions ko ignore karna.** Jab rules, forms aur behtar search kaafi hon, toh woh sasti aur zyada bharosemand hoti hain.
- **Ye na poochhna ki galat hua toh kya hoga.** Har galti ka cost design tay karta hai.
- **Maan lena ki data hai.** Access aur quality jaldi check karo.
- **Baseline nahi.** Aaj agar kaam naapte nahi, toh saabit nahi kar sakte ki AI ne madad ki.
- **Har cheez par haan kehna.** Saaf "nahi, kyunki" product manager ke sabse kimti outputs mein se ek hai.

## Practice karo

PAIR Guidebook ka user needs waala chapter padho (neeche link hai), aur SVPG ka "Four Big Risks".

1. **List.** Kisi product ya team ke liye, jise aap jaante ho, paanch AI ideas likho.
2. **Score.** Har ek ko value, frequency, error tolerance, data, feasibility aur risk par score karo, aur note karo ki kaunsi tarah ki galti zyada buri hai.
3. **Recommend.** Aadhe page ki recommendation likho: kise prototype karna hai, kise reject, aur kyun.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap kisi lubhaavne AI idea ko saaf wajahon ke saath reject kar sako aur aisa idea chun sako jahan AI saaf madad karta hai.
