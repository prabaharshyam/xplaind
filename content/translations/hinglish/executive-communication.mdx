## Ye kya hai

Executives ke paas time kam, priorities bahut aur paise aur risk ki zimmedaari hoti hai. Unse achhe se communicate karne ka matlab hai:

- **Answer first.** Conclusion ya zaroori decision se shuru karo, phir support.
- **Unki language.** Business results (time, cost, revenue, risk, customers), model names ya architectures nahi.
- **Proof.** Baseline ke saath ek number, ek chhota demo, ek user ke shabd.
- **Trade-offs, honestly.** Kitna kharcha hai, kya galat ho sakta hai, aap kya recommend karte ho.
- **Ek clear ask.** Aapko unse kya chahiye, kab tak.

| One-page memo | |
|---|---|
| **Headline** | Recommendation: saare 180 officers ko rollout karo |
| **Why** | Mahine mein lagbhag 1,700 officer hours free hote hain |
| **Evidence** | Ek file par 41 se 24 minute; rework 7% se 5.1% |
| **Risks** | Photo khinche hue documents; mitigations bataye gaye |
| **Ask** | Budget approve karo aur owner ka naam do, aaj hi |

```flow
dir: LR
Headline -> Why -> Evidence -> Risks -> Ask
```

Appendix mein har number kaise nikla, ek line mein dikhate hain.

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek lender ke CEO aur CFO ke saath pilot ke end ki meeting ko follow karta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Structure.** Kisi bhi update ya email ke liye ek simple pattern:

1. *Headline*: mukhya baat ek sentence mein.
2. *Why it matters*: business effect.
3. *Evidence*: do ya teen facts.
4. *Risks ya trade-offs*: kya tasveer badal sakta hai.
5. *Ask*: zaroori decision ya madad, date ke saath.

**Jo numbers woh pehchaante hain.** Scope of work mein agree hue KPI aur baseline use karo, rupees, hours ya customers mein. "Mahine mein lagbhag 1,700 officer hours bachte hain, maujooda salary costs par saal mein lagbhag ₹50 lakh" achha lagta hai; "field extraction par 96.4% F1" translate kiye bina nahi lagta.

**Executives ke liye demos.** Paanch minute, ek story: ek real case, before aur after, user ka view, aur ek moment jo safeguard dikhaye. Rehearse karo. Network fail hone par recorded backup rakho.

**Aam sawaalon ke liye taiyaar raho.** Full scale par kitna cost? Galat hone par kya hoga? Kya hamara data safe hai? Hamare log kya sochte hain? Competitors jo kar rahe hain usse ye kaise compare karta hai? Har ek ka ek-line jawab ready rakho, detail maangne par.

**Chhote documents.** Decisions ke liye 30-slide deck se one-page memo behtar hai. Agar slides chahiye hi, toh ek slide par ek idea aur headline hi slide title.

**Uncertainty ke baare mein honest raho.** "Full rollout par hum ek file par 20 se 25 minute expect karte hain; pilot branches average se upar thi, isliye hum 25 ka plan karte hain" ek optimistic number se zyada bharosemand hai.

**Technical results ko translate karna:**

| Technical statement | Executive version |
| --- | --- |
| "Critical fields par 96.4% accuracy" | "Officers lagbhag tees mein se ek figure theek karte hain; har figure ka check karne ke liye ek source hai" |
| "p95 latency 2 seconds" | "Sabse busy ghante mein bhi answers lagbhag do second mein aate hain" |
| "Chhote model aur caching par shift hue" | "Running cost ₹14 lakh se ₹9 lakh mahina ho gaya, quality mein koi badlaav nahi" |
| "Recall at 5 of 0.91" | "Ye das mein se nau baar sahi document dhoondh leta hai" |

**60-second version.** Recommendation aur uska evidence ek minute ke andar zor se bolne ki practice karo. Jo decision chahiye, baseline ke against mukhya result, scale par value, cost, aur sabse bada risk apne mitigation ke saath, sab cover karo. Agar ek minute mein nahi bol sakte, toh memo abhi clear nahi hai. Executives aksar waade se kam time dete hain, aur pehla minute baaki sab tay karta hai.

**Mushkil sawaalon ko handle karna.** Poochhe gaye sawaal ka jawab ek sentence mein do, phir detail offer karo: "Haan, data hamare Azure region mein India mein hi rehta hai. Chaho toh architecture page dikha sakta hoon." Agar pata nahi, toh bolo aur batao kab jawab doge. CFO ke saamne guess karne se zyada bharosa gap maan lene se bachta hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Pilot ke end mein lender ke CEO aur CFO aapko 15 minute dete hain. Aapka one-page memo:

> **Recommendation: credit-file assistant ko teen mahine mein saare 180 officers ko rollout karo.**
>
> **Result.** 20 officers ke saath 10 hafte ke pilot mein, ek loan file par median time 41 se 24 minute hua, aur credit committee dwara wapas bheji gayi files 7% se 5.1% hui.
>
> **Full scale par value.** Mahine mein lagbhag 1,700 officer hours free (6,000 files mein har ek par 17 minute), saal mein lagbhag ₹50 lakh, jise operations head naye hiring ke bina 25% zyada applications handle karne mein use karne ka plan rakhte hain.
>
> **Cost.** Full scale par chalane mein lagbhag ₹9 lakh mahina, model costs aur support milakar.
>
> **Risks.** Jin branches mein zyadatar photo khinche documents hain wahan adoption kam ho sakta hai; image clean-up step aur branch visits se mitigate. Saare credit decisions officers ke paas hi rehte hain.
>
> **Aaj ka zaroori decision:** rollout budget approve karo aur operations mein ek owner ka naam do.

Demo mein ek real file, officer ki review screen aur ek flag hua low-confidence figure theek hote dikhta hai. CFO poochhta hai ₹50 lakh kaise nikla; aap appendix mein ek-line formula dikhate ho. Approval dus minute mein mil jaata hai.

| Memo element | Kya kaha |
| --- | --- |
| Recommendation | Teen mahine mein saare 180 officers ko rollout |
| Result | Ek file par 41 se 24 minute; rework 7% se 5.1% |
| Value | Mahine mein lagbhag 1,700 officer hours, saal mein lagbhag ₹50 lakh |
| Cost | Full scale par lagbhag ₹9 lakh mahina |
| Ask | Aaj budget approve karo aur owner ka naam do; dus minute mein approve hua |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Technology se shuru karna.** Result aur decision se shuru karo.
- **Bina translate kiye model metrics.** Unhe time, paisa, risk ya customers mein badlo.
- **Aasaan decisions ke liye lambe decks.** Aksar ek page kaafi hai.
- **Overpromise karna.** Ranges aur downside ke plans ek best-case number se behtar hain.
- **Ask na karna.** Decision maangoge nahi, toh shaayad hi milega.

## Practice karo

Google ke free technical writing course ki kam se kam pehli unit lo, jo seedha executive writing par lagu hoti hai.

1. Apne use case ke liye paanch-part structure mein one-page recommendation memo likho, har number kisi source tak trace ho.
2. Paanch minute ke demo ki story script karo: ek real case, before aur after, ek safeguard.
3. Paanch sawaal likho jo CFO poochhega aur har ek ka ek-line jawab likho.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap slides ke bina, zor se, 60 second ke andar apni recommendation aur uska mukhya evidence de sako.
