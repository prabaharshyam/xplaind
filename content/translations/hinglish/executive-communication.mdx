## Ye kya hai

Executives ke paas time kam hota hai, priorities bahut, aur paise va risk ki zimmedaari. Aap jo bhejte ho uski pehli lines padhkar wo tay karte hain ki aage padhein ya nahi. Unse achhi tarah baat karne ka matlab hai paanch aadatein:

- **Answer first.** Conclusion ya jo decision aapko chahiye, wo pehle sentence mein jaata hai. US Army ki writing guidance yahi maangti hai, aur ise aksar BLUF kehte hain: bottom line up front.
- **Unki bhaasha.** Business results (time, cost, revenue, risk, customers), model names ya architectures nahi.
- **Proof.** Tay baseline ke mukaable ek number, ek chhota demo, kisi user ke shabd.
- **Trade-offs, imaandaari se.** Iski keemat kya hai aur ye kitne ka hai, kya galat ho sakta hai, aap kya recommend karte ho.
- **Saaf ask.** Unse kya chahiye, kisse, kab tak.

```flow
title: Ek panne ka memo
dir: LR
Headline | decision ek line mein
Headline -> Kyun -> Evidence -> Risks -> Ask
Ask | decision, owner, taareekh
```

Shape hamesha ek jaisa rehta hai. Badalta hai content, aur content ko ek test paas karna hota hai: finance ka koi aadmi har number check karega.

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek kaalpanik lender ke CEO aur CFO ke saath pilot ke ant ki meeting ko follow karta hai, wahi credit-file assistant jo is path ke doosre lessons mein aata hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Paanch hisson waala structure

Har update, email ya memo ke liye ek hi pattern use karo:

1. *Headline*: mukhya baat ek sentence mein, aam taur par ek recommendation.
2. *Kyun maayne rakhta hai*: business asar, rupaye, ghante, customers ya risk mein.
3. *Evidence*: do-teen facts, har ek baseline ke mukaable.
4. *Risks ya trade-offs*: kya cheez tasveer badal sakti hai, aur aap uske liye kya karoge.
5. *Ask*: kaun-sa decision ya madad chahiye, uska owner kaun hai, aur ek taareekh.

Har calculation appendix mein rakho, har ek ki ek line, taaki koi bhi use ek minute mein check kar sake.

### Unke numbers use karo, aur value ki cost se tulna karo

Scope of work mein tay KPI aur baseline use karo. Phir result ko aise figure se paise mein badlo jo client ka apna ho, jaise unki HR team ka bataaya ek ghante ka loaded cost (salary ke saath benefits aur overheads). Aapka banaaya figure challenge hoga; unki apni finance team ka figure nahi hoga.

Phir CFO se pehle CFO waala hisaab khud kar lo:

- **Ek unit, ek period.** Agar value saal ki hai, toh cost bhi saal ki honi chahiye. "Saal ki ₹50 lakh value" ke bagal mein "chalaane ka mahine ka ₹9 lakh" ye baat chhupa deta hai ki cost saal ka ₹1.08 crore hai, value ke dogune se bhi zyada.
- **Net value.** Saal ki value mein se saal ki running cost ghataao. Yahi wo number hai jo maayne rakhta hai.
- **Payback.** Agar ab se aage koi one-off cost hai (training, rollout visits), toh use monthly net value se divide karo, pata chalega kitne mahine mein paisa waapas aayega. Pilot par jo paisa pehle hi kharch ho chuka, wo har haal mein ja chuka hai, isliye wo rollout decision mein nahi aata.
- **Break-even.** Wo point jahaan value aur cost baraabar hote hain, jaise "case tab tak tika hai jab tak har file kam se kam 13 minute bachaati hai". Isse executive ko pata chalta hai ki kis cheez par nazar rakhni hai.
- **Har benefit ek hi baar gino.** Khaali hue officer hours aur "nayi hiring nahi" aksar ek hi benefit ko do baar bataana hota hai. Ek chuno.
- **Hours tabhi gine jaate hain jab wo use hon.** Khaali hue time ki paise mein value tabhi hai jab kisi ke paas uska plan ho. Plan aur uske owner ka naam batao.

India ke liye ek gotcha: kai AI services ki dollar prices ke mukaable yahaan salaries kam hain. Sirf bache ghanton par bana case, online padhi US case studies ke mukaable India mein aksar bahut patla hota hai. Ise jaldi check karo, aur quality, speed aur growth mein bhi value dhoondho.

### Technical results ko translate karo

| Technical statement | Executive version |
| --- | --- |
| "Critical fields par 96.4% accuracy" | "Kareeb 28 mein se ek figure ko correction chahiye; low-confidence figures flag hote hain, aur decision se pehle officers har figure ko uske source se check karte hain" |
| "p95 latency 2 seconds" | "Bees mein se unnees jawaab do seconds ke andar aa jaate hain" |
| "Caching ke saath ek chhote model par chale gaye" | "Quality mein bina badlaav ke running cost mahine ke ₹14 lakh se ghatkar ₹9 lakh ho gayi" |
| "Recall at 5 of 0.91" | "Das mein se nau sawaalon ke liye sahi document un paanch mein hota hai jo ye padhta hai" |

Translate karne ke do rules. Pehla, sirf friendly number nahi, consequence aur safeguard bhi jodo: executive ko pata hona chahiye ki ek galti ki keemat kya hai aur use kaun pakadta hai. Doosra, kisi metric ko round karke aisa waada mat banao jo wo karta hi nahi. "Das mein se nau baar sahi document dhoondh leta hai" sunne mein 90% sahi jawaab jaisa lagta hai; recall at 5 sirf ye kehta hai ki sahi document paanch candidates mein tha.

### Uncertainty ke baare mein imaandaar raho

Pilots aam taur par sabse utsaahi branches mein, sabse taiyaar users ke saath chalte hain. Ek range do aur saavdhaan sire ke hisaab se plan karo: "Pilot mein har file par 24 minute aaye, lekin pilot branches average se upar thi, isliye full rollout par hum 24 se 26 minute ki ummeed karte hain aur 26 ke hisaab se plan karte hain." Phir dikhao ki planning figure par bhi case tika rehta hai. Ek optimistic number CFO ko chhed dhoondhne ka nyota deta hai; break-even point waali range dikhaati hai ki aap pehle hi dekh chuke ho.

### Jab khabar buri ho

Executives ko har samay successes ke baare mein bataaya jaata hai. Aap miss ko kaise batate ho, wahi unhe yaad rehta hai. Teen rules:

- **Pehli line mein, aakhiri mein nahi.** "Pilot apna target miss kar gaya" headline mein jaata hai. Use naram karne ke liye achhi baaton se shuru mat karo.
- **Koi surprise nahi.** Executive ko ye aapse sunna chahiye, steering meeting se pehle, meeting mein apne hi staff se nahi.
- **Options, phir ek recommendation.** Do-teen choices, har ek ki cost aur usse kya milega, aur jo aap recommend karte ho. Bina recommendation waala miss aapki problem room ke sabse busy aadmi ko thama deta hai.

Jaise, maan lo Mumbai ki ek insurer ne claim emails ki automatic routing ka pilot kiya, target tha 70% emails bina kisi insaan ke route hon:

> **Headline.** Pilot ne 52% claim emails automatic route kiye, 70% target se kam. Hum rokne ki jagah scope chhota karne ki recommendation dete hain.
>
> **Kyun.** English emails, volume ka kareeb 55%, 78% tak pahunche; Hindi ya Marathi ko English ke saath milaane waale emails 21% tak pahunche.
>
> **Options.** (1) Abhi rok do: aage koi cost nahi. (2) Sirf English emails ke liye live jaao: us group ke liye target se upar, chaar hafte mein live. (3) Mixed-language emails ke liye pilot aath hafte badhaao: zyada cost, nateeja pakka nahi.
>
> **Ask.** Isi hafte option 2 approve karein; agle quarterly review mein hum mixed-language plan laayenge.

Jab numbers rukne ko kahein tab stop recommend karna bhi kaam ka hissa hai. Yahi aapki agli recommendation ko bharosemand banaata hai.

### Executives ke liye demos

Paanch minute, ek kahaani: ek asli case, before aur after, user ka view, aur ek pal jismein koi safeguard dikhe. Rehearse karo. Network fail ho jaaye toh recorded backup rakho. Aisa demo case kabhi mat dikhao jo aapne usi subah try na kiya ho.

### Meeting se pehle

- **Pre-read bhejo.** Memo ek-do din pehle bhejo, taaki meeting padhne mein nahi, decision par jaaye.
- **Unke staff ko brief karo.** Meeting se pehle CFO ke finance analyst ko appendix aur CEO ke chief of staff ko ask samjha do. Unke sawaal dikhaate hain ki gaps kahaan hain, aur executive un numbers par bharosa karta hai jinhe uski apni team pehle hi check kar chuki ho.
- **Zaahir sawaalon ki taiyaari karo.** Full scale par cost kya hai? Galat hone par kya hoga? Kya hamaara data safe hai? Hamaare log kya sochte hain? Competitors se tulna kaisi hai? Har ek ka ek line ka jawaab rakho, poochhne par detail.
- **60-second version taiyaar karo.** Decision, baseline ke mukaable main result, net value, aur sabse bada risk uski mitigation ke saath, zor se, ek minute ke andar bolo. Nahi bol paaye toh memo abhi saaf nahi hai. Executives aksar waade se kam time dete hain.

### Mushkil sawaal sambhaalna

Jo poochha gaya uska jawaab ek sentence mein do, phir detail offer karo: "Haan, data India mein hamaare Azure region mein hi rehta hai. Zaroorat ho toh main architecture page dikha sakta hoon." Nahi pata toh saaf bolo aur batao ki kab tak jawaab doge. CFO ke saamne andaaza lagaana, koi gap maan lene se zyada bharosa khota hai.

**Jab baseline ko challenge kiya jaaye.** Batao ki use kaise measure kiya, kis sample par, kis period mein, aur scope of work mein kisne use manzoor kiya. Phir dobaara measure karne ka offer do: "Agar aapko lagta hai ki pilot branches asaamaanya thi, toh hum isi hafte do aur branches mein 50 files time karke value dobaara bata sakte hain." Jis baseline ko aap dobaara check karne ko taiyaar ho, use wo maan lenge.

**Jab competitors ke baare mein poochha jaaye.** Sirf wahi batao jo public information se source kar sako, jaise koi published case study ya annual report. Nahi toh bolo ki pata karke bataoge. Kisi competitor ke results yaad se ya suni-sunaai baat se kabhi quote mat karo; us room mein ek galat daava har sachche daave ki saakh khatam kar deta hai.

## Ek worked example

Pilot ke ant mein lender ke CEO aur CFO aapko 15 minute dete hain. Pilot ke facts: do branches mein 20 officers, 10 hafte; har loan file par median time 41 se ghatkar 24 minute; credit committee se waapas bheji gayi files 7% se ghatkar 5.1%. Lender 180 officers ke saath mahine mein 6,000 files sambhaalta hai, aur full scale par system chalaane mein sab milaakar mahine ka kareeb ₹9 lakh lagta hai. Lender ki HR team ek officer hour ko loaded kareeb ₹600 maanti hai. Ye kaalpanik case ke figures hain, method dikhaane ke liye.

### Pehla draft, aur wo kyun fail hota hai

Value waali lines ke pehle draft mein likha tha: "Mahine mein kareeb 1,700 officer hours khaali hote hain, saal ka lagbhag ₹50 lakh. Cost mahine ka kareeb ₹9 lakh." Ek din pehle brief kiye gaye finance analyst ne ek minute mein do problems pakad li. Mahine ka ₹9 lakh yaani saal ka ₹1.08 crore, toh memo jitna lautaane ka waada kar raha tha uske dogune se zyada maang raha tha. Aur ₹50 lakh ek aisi hourly cost par tika tha jise lender mein kisi ne manzoor nahi kiya tha. Wo draft room mein fail ho jaata.

### Sudhra hua memo

> **Recommendation: credit-file assistant ko sabhi 180 officers ke liye teen monthly waves mein roll out karein.**
>
> **Result.** 20 officers ke saath 10 hafte ke pilot mein har loan file par median time 41 se ghatkar 24 minute hua, aur credit committee se waapas bheji gayi files 7% se ghatkar 5.1% hui. Officers har figure ko uske source se check karte hain; credit decisions unhi ke paas rehte hain.
>
> **Value aur cost.** Har file par 26 minute ke hamaare planning figure par, bache time ki value mahine ki kareeb ₹9 lakh hai, jo running cost ko cover karti hai. Kam waapas bheji gayi files mahine ka kareeb ₹1.4 lakh jodti hain. Net value: planning figure par saal ka kareeb ₹16 lakh, aur agar full rollout pilot jaisa raha toh kareeb ₹31 lakh. Operations head khaali hue time ko nayi hiring ki jagah agle saal applications ki growth ke liye use karenge.
>
> **Risks.** Jin branches mein zyaadatar photo kheenche hue documents hain, wahaan faayda kam ho sakta hai; image clean-up step aur branch visits se mitigate kiya jaayega. Case tab tak tika hai jab tak har file kam se kam 13 minute bachaati hai. Hum ise har mahine report karenge aur iske neeche jaane par agli wave rok denge.
>
> **Aaj chahiye decision:** rollout budget approve karein aur operations mein ek owner ka naam dein.

### Appendix

| Line | Calculation |
| --- | --- |
| Har file par bacha time | 41 ghata 26 = 15 minute (planning figure; pilot mein 17 aaya) |
| Khaali hue officer hours | 6,000 files × 15 min ÷ 60 = mahine mein 1,500 hours |
| Hours ki value | 1,500 × ₹600 = mahine ka ₹9.0 lakh |
| Kam waapas bheji gayi files | 6,000 × (7% − 5.1%) = mahine mein 114 × har ek ke 2 hours (lender ka estimate) × ₹600 = mahine ka kareeb ₹1.4 lakh |
| Running cost | Sab milaakar mahine ka ₹9 lakh, har file par kareeb ₹150 |
| Net value | ₹10.4 lakh − ₹9 lakh = mahine ka kareeb ₹1.4 lakh, saal ka ₹16 lakh (pilot ke 17 minute par mahine ka ₹2.6 lakh) |
| Break-even | (₹9 lakh − ₹1.4 lakh) ÷ ₹600 = kareeb 1,270 hours, yaani har file par kareeb 13 minute |

### Room mein

Demo ek asli file, officer ki review screen aur flag hue ek low-confidence figure ko correct hote dikhaata hai. CFO poochhti hain ki pilot mein 24 minute aaye toh plan 26 minute kyun use karta hai; aap samjhaate ho ki pilot branches average se upar thi. Wo monthly report mein break-even line jodne ko kehti hain, aur usi shart ke saath approve kar deti hain. Meeting das minute mein khatam hoti hai, kyunki behas ek din pehle unke analyst ke saath ho chuki thi.

Dhyaan do ki kis cheez ne kaam kiya. Case imaandaari se batata hai ki bacha time sirf running cost nikaalta hai, aur faayda quality aur growth se aata hai. Executives ne ek saaf gate waala, sadha hua, achhi tarah check kiya gaya case approve kiya, jiske liye stage-gate plan bana hi hota hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Technology se shuru karna.** Result aur decision se shuru karo; architecture appendix mein, agar zaroori ho toh.
- **Bina translate kiye model metrics.** Unhe time, paise, risk ya customers mein badlo, aur safeguard bhi jodo.
- **Value aur cost alag-alag units mein.** "Saal ka" banaam "mahine ka" ghaate waale case ko chhupa deta hai. Hamesha net value dikhao.
- **Ek benefit ko do baar ginna.** Khaali hue hours aur tali hui hiring aksar wahi rupaye hote hain.
- **Zaroorat se zyada waada.** Ek range, saavdhaan sire par planning figure aur ek break-even point, ek best-case number se behtar hain.
- **Buri khabar dabaana.** Miss pehli line mein jaata hai, options aur recommendation ke saath.
- **Room mein surprises.** Pehle pre-read bhejo aur unke staff ko brief karo.
- **Koi ask nahi.** Agar aap owner aur taareekh ke saath decision nahi maangte, toh wo shaayad hi milta hai.

## Practice

Google ke free technical writing course ki kam se kam pehli unit karo, jo seedhe executive writing par laagu hoti hai.

1. Paanch hisson waale structure se apne use case ke liye ek panne ka recommendation memo likho, aise appendix ke saath jismein har number ki ek line ho aur ek net value aur ek break-even line ho.
2. Wahi memo aise likho jaise pilot apna target miss kar gaya ho: headline, cost ke saath options, aur aapki recommendation.
3. Paanch minute ki demo kahaani ka script likho: ek asli case, before aur after, ek safeguard.
4. Wo paanch sawaal list karo jo ek CFO poochhega, aapke baseline ko challenge karne waala sawaal bhi, aur har ek ka ek line ka jawaab likho.

**Aap tab poore ho jab** apni recommendation, uska main evidence aur uski net value 60 seconds ke andar, zor se, bina slides ke bata sako.
