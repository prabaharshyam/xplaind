## Ye kya hai

Zyaadatar log generative AI se ek chat box ke roop mein milte hain. Wahi technology ab pictures, sound aur video ke saath bhi kaam karti hai, aur iska bada hissa pehle se un apps mein hai jo aap use karte ho: aapka phone camera aur keyboard, aapka office suite, aapka video-call app aur aapka chat assistant.

| Kism | Kya karta hai | Roz ka example | Abhi bhi kahaan fail hota hai |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Image creation** | Description se picture banaata hai | Kisi chhote business ka festival poster | Exact shabd, prices, baareek details; koi bhi Indian-language text check karo |
| **Image editing** | Background hataata hai, light badalta hai, picture ko badhaata hai | Clean background par product photo | Jo cheezein rakhne ko kaha, unhe bhi badal deta hai |
| **Image reading** | Photo describe karta hai ya usmein likha text padhta hai | Bill, dawaai ka strip, timetable | Handwriting, pheeki print, 1 aur 7 jaise milte-julte digits |
| **Speech to text** | Audio ko likhe text mein badalta hai | Voice note ya lecture ko transcribe karna | Naam, numbers, noise, mili-juli bhaashaayein |
| **Text to speech** | Text ko natural awaaz mein bolkar padhta hai | Hindi ya Tamil mein dukaan ki announcement | Naamon aur kuch shabdon par ajeeb zor |
| **Meeting assistants** | Call ko transcribe karke action items ke saath summary banaate hain | Team call ke notes | Unsupported bhaashaayein, gadhe hue ya chhoote decisions |
| **Video and music** | Chhote clips banaate hain, aksar sound ke saath | Kuch seconds ka product teaser | Lambaai, clips mein ek hi chehra ya product banaaye rakhna, cost |

Jo models text ke saath images bhi padhte hain, unhe **vision-language models** kehte hain. Inhi ki wajah se aap haath se likhi list ki photo kheenchkar assistant se use spreadsheet banaane ko keh sakte ho.

Is lesson mein saath rakhne waala ek idea: **inmein se har tool aapko kuch aisa deta hai jo poora taiyaar dikhta hai.** Poster chhapa hua lagta hai, transcript type kiya hua lagta hai, summary minutes jaisi lagti hai. Skill ye jaanna hai ki har tool kahaan fail hota hai aur theek wahin check karna, phir kuch bhi aapke phone se baahar jaane se pehle rights, privacy aur labels sambhaalna.

```flow
title: AI se bani koi bhi cheez share karne se pehle
dir: LR
Banao | image, transcript, voice ya notes -> Risky hisse check karo | shabd, naam, numbers, prices
Risky hisse check karo -> Rights check karo | asli log, brands, awaazein, consent
Rights check karo -> Privacy check karo | ID numbers, phone numbers, doosre log
Privacy check karo -> Label karke share karo | platform pooche toh AI content declare karo
```

Ye lesson Anitha ke saath chalta hai, jo Tiruchirappalli mein home bakery chalaati hain aur Diwali campaign ke liye in tools ko use karti hain.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Ye tools wahin kyun fail hote hain jahaan hote hain

Image aur speech models examples ke bahut bade collections se seekhte hain. Jab wo kuch banaate hain, toh jo dekha hai uske hisaab se sabse likely cheez banaate hain. Isiliye poori picture ya sentence aam taur par achha hota hai: aam cheezon ka andaaza lagaana aasaan hai.

Kam dikhne waali, exact details alag hain. Image model aksharon ko shapes ki tarah banaata hai, isliye jo shabd usne kam dekha hai usmein koi akshar chhoot sakta hai ya zyaada aa sakta hai, aur Hindi ya Tamil jaisi jude aksharon aur maatraaon waali script mein galti ke aur raaste hain. Speech model kisi awaaz ke liye sabse likely shabd chunta hai, isliye "fifteen" aur "fifty", ya koi aisa surname jo usne kabhi nahi suna, aasaani se badal jaate hain. Meeting summary kisi decision ka sabse likely roop chunti hai, isliye wo aisa decision bhi likh sakti hai jo kisi ne liya hi nahi.

Isse practical rule nikalta hai: **Draft ke liye AI use karo, aur exact details har baar khud check karo**: images mein likhe shabd, naam, numbers, prices, dates aur kisne kis baat par haan kaha.

### Images banaana aur edit karna

Aap picture ko prompt mein describe karte ho: subject, setting, style, colours, aur wo kis shape ke liye hai, jaise Instagram ke liye square. Jahaan text aayega wahaan khaali jagah chhodo. "AI image generation and editing with Gemini (Nano Banana) and Midjourney" lesson prompting ko gehraai se sikhaata hai.

**Images mein text.** Naye models chhota English text pehle ke models se kahin behtar likhte hain. Example ke liye, Google kehta hai ki uska Nano Banana Pro model kai bhaashaaon mein padhne laayak text render karta hai, par uski apni limits ki list mein abhi bhi chhota text aur exact spelling shaamil hain. Koi bhi Hindi, Tamil ya doosri Indian-script text akshar-akshar test karo, kyunki galat maatra aasaani se chhoot jaati hai. Default rule: **Picture generate karo, shabd type karo.** Headlines, prices, dates aur phone numbers Canva jaise design app se daalo, aur koi bhi Hindi, Tamil ya doosri Indian-language text kisi achhe padhne waale se check karwao.

**Editing.** Kisi tool se asli photo edit karwaana ("light tez karo, plain white background, product ko mat badlo") aksar nayi image generate karne se behtar hota hai. Jo bhi aap bechte ho, uske liye asli product dikhaao. Cake ki aisi generated "photo" jo aapke cake jaisi nahi dikhti, customers ko gumraah karti hai.

### Documents ki photos padhna

Kisi bill, haath se likhi list ya timetable ki photo kheencho aur assistant se use padhne ya table banaane ko kaho. Isse bahut typing bachti hai. Chhota, pheeka ya haath se likha text aksar galat padha jaata hai, aur 1 aur 7, ya 5 aur 6 jaise digits aasaani se gadbada jaate hain.

Do aadatein ise safe banaati hain:

- **Hisaab se check karo.** Assistant se har line par quantity ko rate se multiply karwao aur amounts judwao. Jo line match na kare, ya jo total paper se alag ho, wo seedhe galat padhi gayi jagah dikhaata hai.
- **Upload se pehle crop karo.** Ek photo mein usse kahin zyaada ho sakta hai jitna aap share karna chaahte the: Aadhaar number, bank account, kisi aur ka phone number, koi medical detail. Un hisson ko pehle crop karo ya dhako. Jab koi form Aadhaar maange, toh UIDAI se masked Aadhaar use karo, jismein sirf aakhri chaar digits dikhte hain. Work par documents ke liye sirf wahi tools use karo jo aapki organisation ne approve kiye hain.

### Speech to text aur translation

Saaf English ke liye transcription bahut achha hai aur Indian languages ke liye tezi se sudhar raha hai. Phir bhi inmein galtiyaan karta hai:

- strong accents aur tez bolna;
- traffic ya bhari hui dukaan jaisa background noise;
- Hinglish aur doosri mili-juli bhaasha waali speech;
- naam, jagahein, numbers aur technical shabd.

Shuru karne se pehle hi galtiyaan kam ki ja sakti hain: shaant kamre mein phone ke paas record karo, tool mein sahi bhaasha chuno, zaroori numbers dheere bolo (jaise, "four hundred and fifty, four five zero"), aur anokhe naamon ki spelling bolo. Transcribe hone ke baad, har naam aur number ke liye transcript ko audio se milaakar padho.

**Translation** ka apna jaal hai: wo grammar ke hisaab se sahi hokar bhi local readers ko atpata ya galat lag sakta hai. Ek quick check: use waapas original bhaasha mein translate karke compare karo. Notice ya advert jaisi kisi bhi public cheez ke liye, us bhaasha ke native speaker se padhwao.

### Voice generation

Text-to-speech kai Indian languages mein natural announcements banaata hai, jo dukaanon, schools aur public notices ke kaam aata hai. Tool ki stock voices use karo, ya apni khud ki awaaz. **Kisi asli insaan ki awaaz bina consent clone karna** alag baat hai. 2024 mein Bombay High Court ne Arijit Singh ke case mein ek interim order mein kai naam liye gaye AI platforms ko singer ki awaaz copy karne waale tools dene se roka, aur unki awaaz ko unke personality rights ka hissa maana. Cloned voices "Deepfakes and misinformation" lesson mein bataaye gaye scams ka bhi mukhya hathiyaar hain.

### Meeting assistants

Ye video call mein judte hain, use transcribe karte hain aur action items ke saath summary likhte hain. Kisi ko jodne se pehle:

1. **Sabko batao aur unki haan lo** ki call record aur summarise ho rahi hai.
2. Kaun-se tools kin meetings mein jud sakte hain, is par **apni organisation ke rules** aur client ke rules maano, aur jaano ki recording kahaan store hoti hai.
3. **Bhaasha check karo.** Kai meeting note-takers bhaashaaon ki chhoti list hi support karte hain aur ek meeting mein ek hi bhaasha ki ummeed karte hain. Hindi ya Hinglish call se pehle tool ki supported languages check karo.
4. **Share karne se pehle summary check karo.** Decisions, numbers, dates aur owners ko jo kaha gaya tha usse milaao; confident dikhne waali summary koi shart chhod sakti hai ya koi agreement gadh sakti hai.

### Video aur music

Sound waale chhote video clips ab mainstream assistants mein milte hain. Example ke liye, Google ka Gemini app apne Veo models se chhote clips banaata hai, zyaadatar paid plans par aur roz ki chhoti limit ke saath. Kuch seconds lambe clips, clips mein ek hi chehra ya product banaaye rakhne mein dikkat, aur ek kaam ke shot ke liye kai koshishon ki ummeed rakho. Background music generators bhi images jaise hi rights ke sawaal uthaate hain. "Create cinematic AI videos with Google Veo and Flow" lesson video ko theek se cover karta hai.

### Aazmaane ke liye tools (November 2026 tak)

Free tiers aur limits aksar badalte hain, isliye bharosa karne se pehle har tool ka apna page check karo.

| Kaam | Aazmaane ke options | Kis baat ka dhyaan rakhein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Images banaana ya edit karna | Gemini app (free daily quota), ChatGPT (limited free images) | Commercial-use terms; images mein text |
| Images par text daalna | Canva ya koi bhi design app | Indian-script text kisi achhe padhne waale se check karwao |
| Indian-language speech aur translation | Government of India ka Bhashini app (free); Sarvam AI ke speech models, uske developer platform se | Pehle apne accent aur shabdon par test karo |
| Jaldi voice notes se text | Aapke phone keyboard mein voice typing | Numbers aur naam |
| Meeting notes | Google Meet aur Microsoft Teams ke note-takers (paid plans ya add-on licences) | Supported languages; consent; storage |

### Rights, labels aur ownership

**Labels.** India ka IT Rules amendment, jo 10 February 2026 ko notify hua aur 20 February 2026 se laagu hai, "synthetically generated information" ko cover karta hai: AI se bani ya AI se badli gayi images, audio aur video jo asli lagein. Platforms ko aise content par label lagaana hoga, aur bade social media platforms ko upload ke waqt users se ise declare karwaana hoga. Jab platform pooche, imaandaari se declare karo. Rules badalte rehte hain, isliye kisi bade campaign se pehle latest check karo.

**Ownership.** Poori tarah AI se bani image ka maalik kaun hai, ye India mein tay nahi hai. Computer-generated works par Copyright Act ka clause 1994 ka hai, aaj ke tools se bahut pehle ka, aur DPIIT ki banaayi ek committee ne December 2025 mein generative AI aur copyright par working paper ka pehla hissa chhaapa, jo AI training ko cover karta hai aur AI outputs ki ownership ko baad ke hisse ke liye chhodta hai. Jab tak ye tay nahi hota, poori tarah AI se bani image ko apna logo ya trademark mat banao: ho sakta hai aap doosron ko use copy karne se na rok paao. Ideas ke liye AI use karo, phir final logo kisi designer se banwao ya kaafi had tak rework karwao, aur jo bhi register karne ka plan ho uske liye legal advice lo.

**Commercial use.** Har tool ki apni terms hoti hain ki uski banaayi cheez se bechna ya advertising karna kaise chalega. Kisi advert mein image use karne se pehle unhe padho.

| Karo | Mat karo |
| --- | --- |
| Zaroori text images par khud type karo | Image tool ka likha text har akshar check kiye bina publish mat karo |
| Jab platform pooche, AI content declare karo | Kisi jeevit artist ke style ki AI copies ko apna art bataakar mat chalaao |
| Logon ko record karne se pehle unki haan lo | Calls chupke se record mat karo |
| Stock voices ya apni awaaz use karo | Kisi asli insaan ki awaaz ya chehra bina consent clone mat karo |
| Photos se ID numbers aur doosron ki details crop karo | Doosron ki private photos ya recordings upload mat karo |

## Ek example se samjho

Anitha, Tiruchirappalli mein home bakery chalaati hain. Wo Instagram aur WhatsApp par Diwali sweet boxes promote karna chaahti hain, aur unke paas ek hi shaam hai.

**Poster.** Wo apne asli sweet box ki photo kheenchti hain aur image tool se use edit karne ko kehti hain: "square poster, box ko mat badlo, use lakdi ki table par rakho aur peeche jalte diye, gold aur maroon colours, aur upar Tamil mein Diwali ki shubhkaamna". Shubhkaamna wo tool ko test karne ke liye maangti hain, phir chaar results mein se ek chunti hain. Shubhkaamna mein ek maatra galat hai, toh wo use hataakar shubhkaamna, price aur apna phone number Canva mein type karti hain. Unki behen, jo Tamil achhe se padhti hai, final poster check karti hai. Jab Instagram poochta hai ki post mein AI-made content hai ya nahi, toh wo haan kehti hain.

**Caption.** Wo box ke baare mein Tamil mein ek voice note record karti hain, price English mein bolti hain jaise bahut log bolte hain, use AI tool se transcribe karti hain, aur assistant se Tamil aur English mein chhota caption maangti hain. Transcript ne adhirasam mithaai ko kisi aur shabd mein badal diya, aur "four hundred and fifty rupees" ko ₹415 kar diya. Wo transcript ko apni price list se milaakar padhti hain aur dono pakad leti hain.

**Supplier ka bill.** Supplier ka personal mobile number dhakne ke baad, wo uske haath se likhe invoice ki photo kheenchti hain aur assistant se table banaane ko kehti hain. Paper bill par ye figures hain, example ke liye:

| Item | Quantity | Rate (₹) | Amount (₹) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cashews | 5 kg | 950 | 4,750 |
| Sugar | 75 kg | 44 | 3,300 |
| Ghee | 10 kg | 620 | 6,200 |
| **Total** | | | **14,250** |

Assistant ne sugar ki quantity 15 kg padhi par amount ₹3,300 hi rakha. Wo usse har line par quantity ko rate se multiply karwaati hain: 15 × 44 = 660, jo 3,300 se match nahi karta, toh wo paper dekhkar use 75 kg kar deti hain. Ab 75 × 44 = 3,300 aur teenon amounts judkar bill ke total ₹14,250 ke baraabar aate hain.

**Ek suggestion jo unhone thukra diya.** Ek dost "zyaada likes ke liye" poster par kisi mashhoor actor ka chehra lagaane ko kehti hai. Anitha mana kar deti hain: unke paas permission nahi hai, isse customers gumraah honge, ye actor ke personality rights tod sakta hai, aur tool ke rules iski ijaazat nahi dete.

| Task | Kya galat hua | Unhone kaise pakda |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poster | AI text mein Tamil ki galat maatra | Text khud type kiya; achhe padhne waale ne check kiya |
| Caption | Ek mithaai ka naam aur price galat suna gaya | Transcript ko price list se milaakar padha |
| Supplier ka bill | 75 kg ko 15 kg padha | Quantity × rate, amount se match nahi hua |
| Actor ka chehra | Suggest hua, bana nahi | Banaane se pehle consent aur rights check |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Taiyaar dikhne waale draft ko taiyaar maan lena.** Saaf-suthre poster, transcript ya summary mein bhi galat price ya naam ho sakta hai. Exact details har baar check karo.
- **Ek spelling theek karne ke liye bees aur images maangna.** Dobaara generate karne se text bharose se kam hi theek hota hai. Khud type karo.
- **Poore documents upload karna.** Bills aur forms ki photos mein aksar Aadhaar numbers, phone numbers ya bank details hote hain. Pehle crop karo.
- **Meeting assistant ko aisi bhaasha mein use karna jo wo support nahi karta.** English-only tool mein Hinglish call confident par galat summary deti hai.
- **Native reader ke bina machine translation publish karna.** Wo sahi ho sakta hai par customers ko galat lag sakta hai.
- **Poori tarah AI se bani image ko logo banaana.** Ho sakta hai aap use protect na kar paao.
- Jab platform pooche tab **AI-content declaration chhod dena.**
- **"Bas maze ke liye" awaaz clone karna.** Kisi asli insaan ki awaaz ya chehre ke liye, mazaak mein bhi, unka consent chahiye.

## Practice karo

Neeche linked Microsoft Learn ka generative AI fundamentals par chhota module karo, aur Gemini mein images banaane par Google ka help page padho.

1. **Ek poster banao.** Kisi asli ya kaalpanik event ke liye poster background banao, phir shabd khud jodo, jismein ek line aapki apni bhaasha mein ho. Kisi achhe padhne waale se check karwao.
2. **Transcription test karo.** Apni bhaasha mein ek minute ka voice note record karo jismein teen naam aur teen numbers hon. Use do alag tools se transcribe karo aur har ek ke liye naamon aur numbers mein galtiyaan gino.
3. **Ek bill padho.** Kisi chhape ya haath se likhe bill ki photo kheencho, personal details dhako, aur assistant se use table banwao. Galat padhi line dhoondhne ke liye quantity × rate check use karo.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap bata sako** ki aapne chat ke alaawa kaun-se teen AI tools use kiye, har ek ne kaun-si exact detail galat ki, aapne use kaise pakda, aur ek cheez jo aap unke saath nahi karoge.
