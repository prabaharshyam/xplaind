## Ye kya hai

Zyadatar log generative AI se chat box ke roop mein milte hain. Par wahi technology ab pictures, sound aur video ke saath bhi kaam karti hai, aur uska bahut hissa un apps ke andar pehle se hai jo aap roz use karte ho: phone camera, office suite, video-call app aur chat assistant.

| Images | Speech | Meetings | Video aur music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Banao, edit karo, bill ki photo padho | Transcribe, translate, zor se padhna | Record, transcribe, summarise | Chhote clips, soundtracks (abhi shuruaati) |

| Kism | Ye kya karta hai | Roz ka example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Image creation** | Description se picture banata hai | Chhote business ke liye festival poster |
| **Image editing** | Background hataata hai, picture badhaata hai, lighting theek karta hai | Saaf safed background par product photo |
| **Image reading** | Photo ko describe karta hai ya usmein text padhta hai | Dawai ki strip, bill ya timetable padhna |
| **Speech to text** | Audio ko likhe hue text mein badalta hai | Interview ya lecture transcribe karna |
| **Text to speech** | Text ko natural awaaz mein zor se padhta hai | Hindi ya Tamil mein announcement |
| **Meeting assistants** | Calls mein judte hain, transcribe karte hain aur actions ke saath summarise karte hain | Team call ke notes aur action items |
| **Video aur music** | Chhote clips aur soundtracks generate karte hain | Das second ka product teaser |

Jo models text ke saath images bhi padhte hain unhe **vision-language models** kehte hain. Inhi ki wajah se aap haath se likhi list ki photo khinchkar assistant se spreadsheet banwa sakte ho.

Ye lesson Anitha ke saath chalta hai, jo Tiruchirappalli mein home bakery chalati hai, aur in tools ko Diwali campaign ke liye use karti hai. End tak aap jaan jaoge ki har tool kisme achha hai, kahan fail hota hai, aur unhe zimmedaari se use karne ke rules kya hain.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Images banana

Image generators likhe hue description se kaam karte hain, jise prompt kehte hain. Achhe prompts specific hote hain:

```text
  Weak:   "Diwali sweets poster"

  Strong: subject    a gift box of Indian sweets: kaju katli, ladoo
          setting    on a wooden table, diyas glowing behind
          style      warm, festive photo style
          colours    gold and maroon
          layout     square, empty space at the top for text
```

Image tools ab bhi picture ke andar **exact text**, **haath** aur **khaas asli logon** par struggle karte hain, aur kuch requests mana bhi kar sakte hain. Jo cheez aap bechoge ya jiska advertisement karoge, uske liye tool ke commercial use ke rules check karo.

### Images padhna

Haath se likhi list, bill, dawai ki strip ya train timetable ki photo lo aur assistant se use padhne ya samjhaane ko kaho. Isse bahut typing bachti hai. Par chhota, dhundhla ya haath se likha text aksar galat padha jaata hai, isliye jo bhi zaroori ho use original se milao.

### Speech to text aur translation

Saaf English ke liye transcription bahut achhi hai aur Indian languages aur mixed speech ke liye tezi se behtar ho rahi hai. Abhi bhi ye in mein galtiyaan karti hai:

- tez accents aur tez bolna;
- background noise, jaise traffic ya bheed waali dukaan;
- Hinglish aur doosri mixed-language speech;
- naam, jagah aur technical words.

Interviews, lectures aur voice notes ke liye ye kaam ki hai. Naam aur numbers hamesha check karo.

### Meeting assistants

Ye video call mein judte hain, use transcribe karte hain aur action items ke saath summary likhte hain. Teen rules:

1. Meeting mein **sabko batao** ki ye record ho rahi hai.
2. Kaunsa tool kaunsi meeting mein aa sakta hai, ispar **apne organisation ke rules follow karo.**
3. Share karne se pehle **summary check karo**; woh faisle chhod sakta hai ya galat likh sakta hai.

### Voice generation

Text-to-speech kai Indian languages mein natural announcements bana sakta hai, jo dukaanon, schools aur public notices ke liye badhiya hai. Par **kisi asli insaan ki awaaz clone karna** consent ke bina galat hai aur kai cases mein illegal bhi. Cloned voices deepfakes waale lesson mein bataye scams ka mukhya hathiyaar hain.

### Zimmedaari se use

| Karo | Mat karo |
| --- | --- |
| Zaroori text images mein khud daalo | Image tool ke likhe text par bharosa mat karo |
| Jahan zaroori ho, jaise news ya ads, wahan AI-made images par label lagao | Kisi artist ki style ki AI copies ko apni original art ki tarah mat dikhao |
| Logon ko record karne se pehle consent lo | Meetings chhupkar record mat karo |
| Images mein generic log use karo | Asli logon ko woh karte hue mat dikhao jo unhone kiya hi nahi |
| Private photos ko unapproved tools se door rakho | Doosron ki private photos ya recordings upload mat karo |

## Ek worked example

Anitha Tiruchirappalli mein home bakery chalati hai. Woh Instagram aur WhatsApp par Diwali sweet boxes ka promotion karna chahti hai, aur uske paas ek shaam hai.

**Poster.** Woh describe karti hai: "Ek festive square poster, jisme Indian sweets ka gift box, warm gold aur maroon colours, peeche diyas, aur upar text ke liye jagah." Image tool chaar options deta hai. Woh ek chunti hai. Usne jo text daala tha usme "Happy Diwalli" likha tha, galat spelling, toh woh use hata deti hai aur words, prices aur apna phone number khud ek free design app mein daalti hai.

**Caption.** Woh Tamil mein box ke contents aur prices batate hue ek voice note record karti hai, AI tool se transcribe karti hai, aur assistant se use English aur Tamil mein chhote caption mein badalne ko kehti hai. Transcription ne do Tamil words galat likhe the, ek mithai ka naam bhi, woh theek kar deti hai.

**Supplier bill.** Woh supplier ke haath se likhe invoice ki phone photo leti hai aur assistant se items ko spreadsheet mein daalne ko kehti hai. Har line paper se milaati hai. Ek quantity 75 ki jagah 15 padhi gayi thi.

**Ek suggestion jo woh mana karti hai.** Ek dost kehta hai ki "zyada likes ke liye" poster par kisi mashhoor actor ka chehra laga do. Anitha mana kar deti hai: uske paas permission nahi hai, ye customers ko bhatkaayega, aur tool ke rules bhi mana karte hain.

| Kaam | Tool | Kya galat kiya | Uska fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Poster | Image generator | "Diwali" ki spelling galat | Text khud daala |
| Caption | Speech to text plus assistant | Do Tamil words, ek mithai ka naam | Haath se edit kiya |
| Supplier list | Image reading | 75 ko 15 padha | Har line check ki |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **AI images ke andar ke text par bharosa karna.** Zaroori words khud daalo.
- **Logon ko bataye bina meetings record karna.** Hamesha announce karo aur rules follow karo.
- **Consent ke bina asli logon ke chehre ya awaaz use karna.** Mazaak mein bhi ye unhe nuksaan pahuncha sakta hai aur kanoon tod sakta hai.
- **Ye maan lena ki transcripts bilkul sahi hain.** Naam, numbers aur Indian-language words check karo.
- **Commercial-use rules ko ignore karna**, un images ke liye jinhe aap bechoge ya jinse advertisement karoge.
- **Doosron ki private photos** unapproved tools par upload karna.
