## Ye kya hai

Ek **design round** aapko ek open problem deta hai, jaise "ek bank ke liye support assistant design karo" ya "10 million documents par question answering design karo", aur ek interviewer hota hai, aksar 45 se 60 minute ke liye. Shuru ke introductions aur aakhir mein aapke sawaalon ke baad, design ke liye aamtaur par lagbhag 35 minute bachte hain. Aap whiteboard ya shared document par banaate ho, aur interviewer beech mein "what if...?" se interrupt karta hai.

Ek achhe design mein kya hota hai, ye "AI system design" lesson mein hai: components, request flow, failure tables, latency budgets. Ye lesson khud interview ke baare mein hai: time kaise bantta hai, interviewer kya likhta hai, aur ek strong answer numbers ke saath kaisa sunaai deta hai.

### Interviewer kya score karta hai

Kai companies structured interviews use karti hain: har candidate se ek hi tarah ka sawaal, ek likhe hue rubric par rating, aur har rating ke peeche notes. Google ki public re:Work guides isi approach ko samjhaati hain. Toh interviewer koi dhundhla impression nahi bana raha. Wo ek scorecard bhar raha hai, aur use har heading ke neeche evidence chahiye, aksar aapke apne shabd. Headings company ke hisaab se badalti hain, par zyaadatar aisi dikhti hain:

| Heading | Strong rating ka evidence | Weak rating ka evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scoping | Draw karne se pehle users, scale, galat answer ki cost aur data rules ke baare mein poocha | Pehle hi minute mein tools ke naam ginaane lage |
| Design | Poora system jaldi board par; har part is problem ke liye justify kiya | Technologies ki list di; bata nahi paaye ki har ek kyun hai |
| Depth | Sabse risky part mein gehraai se gaye aur uske trade-offs jaante the | Har box par barabar, oopari-oopari time |
| Production thinking | Load, cost aur latency ke numbers; failures; ek test set aur launch bar | Happy path par hi ruk gaye |
| Communication | Signpost kiya, check in kiya, "what if" par shaanti se adapt kiya | Lambi chuppiyaan, ya pehle design ko zid se defend kiya |

Practical sabak: aisi baatein bolo jo interviewer likh sake. "Main 2 million consumers maan raha hoon, toh peak par lagbhag 33 model calls har second" ye evidence hai. Yahi baat man mein sochna evidence nahi hai.

### Company ke type ke hisaab se rounds kaise alag hote hain

- **Badi product companies** aksar generic, scale waale prompts deti hain aur ek fixed rubric par rate karti hain. Saaf structure aur numbers sabse zyada maayne rakhte hain.
- **Startups** ko ye dekhna hota hai ki kya aap agle mahine version one bana sakte ho. "Aap kya cut karoge?" aur cost par sawaal expect karo. Kuch is round ki jagah take-home ya live build rakhte hain.
- **Forward deployed aur client-facing roles** mein round aksar customer conversation ki tarah chalta hai. Misaal ke liye, Palantir ke loop par candidate reports ek "decomposition" round batati hain, jismein interviewer ek vague problem waale customer ka role nibhaata hai aur score karta hai ki aap use kaise todte ho. Zyada sawaal poochho, aur ek chhota first deliverable propose karo.
- **India mein IT services firms aur global capability centres** aksar prompts ko kisi client ya parent company ke rules ke aaspaas banaate hain: data residency, pehle se maujood vendor stack, ek fixed budget. In constraints ko noise mat samjho, yahi problem ka core hain.

Recruiter se pooch lo ki round kis tarah ka hai. Ye ek normal sawaal hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Ek time plan, aur use resize kaise karein

Har baar ek hi plan use karo, taaki clock background mein chalta rahe aur aapka dhyaan problem par rahe.

| Minute | Phase | Kab poora maana jaaye |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 se 5 | Clarify | Users, scale, galat answer ki cost, languages, data rules aur success metric board par likhe hain |
| 5 se 10 | High level | User se reply tak boxes aur arrows, interviewer ke saath agreed |
| 10 se 28 | Deep dives | Do-teen risky parts cover hue, ek quick estimate ke saath |
| 28 se 31 | Failures | Teen sabse likely failures, har ek ka guard |
| 31 se 34 | Evals aur ops | Test set, launch bar, rollout, wo ek metric jis par aap nazar rakhoge |
| 34 se 35 | Trade-offs | Das guna scale par, ya zyada time mile toh, aap kya badloge |

Agar design ke liye sirf 30 minute milein, toh deep dives ko 13 minute kar do aur baaki har phase rakho. Agar 45 milein, toh extra time deep dives aur failures ko do, clarify ko kabhi nahi. Agar 20ve minute par bhi aap pehle deep dive mein ho, toh kaho "chunking ko main yahin park karta hoon aur evaluation par chalta hoon" aur aage badho. Har phase ko thoda-thoda cover karna, ek ko perfect cover karne se behtar hai.

### Pehle paanch minute

Wo sawaal poochho jo design badlein, wo nahi jo sirf time bharein. Ek kaam ka test: agar jawaab se koi box ya koi number nahi badlega, toh mat poochho. Sabse strong sawaal ye hain:

- **Answer kise milta hai, aur galat answer ki cost kya hai?** Staff ke padhne waala galat answer ek pareshaani hai. Seedhe customer ya doctor ko bheja gaya galat answer legal problem ban sakta hai.
- **Kitna traffic, aur kab?** Daily users, aur peak pattern: bill day, exam results, festival sales.
- **Kya live systems se aana chahiye?** Balances, order status aur outages ek tool call se aane chahiye, model ki memory se kabhi nahi.
- **Languages, channel aur data rules.** WhatsApp par Tamil aur English, web app par English se alag design hai. Poochho ki data kahaan store ho sakta hai.

Phir apne assumptions numbers mein batao aur interviewer se kaho ki galat hon toh theek kar dein. Wo aksar kehte hain "fine, go ahead", aur yahi aapka aage badhne ka licence hai.

### Estimates jin par sochna na pade

Interviewers ko exact figures nahi chahiye. Wo dekhna chahte hain ki aap orders of magnitude mein, bolkar, ek minute se kam mein reason karte ho.

| Cheez | Rule of thumb |
| --- | --- |
| English tokens | Har token mein lagbhag 4 characters ya ek word ka teen-chauthaai; 1,000 words lagbhag 1,300 tokens |
| Indian languages | Usi matlab ke liye aksar zyada tokens lagte hain, tokenizer par depend karta hai; ek multiple batao aur kaho ki aap measure karoge |
| Ek din ke seconds | 86,400, quick hisaab ke liye 100,000 maan lo |
| Peak par har second ke model calls | Daily conversations ko busy hours se divide karo, ek peak factor (aksar 2 se 3) se multiply karo, har conversation ke turns se multiply karo, 3,600 se divide karo |
| Har conversation ki cost | Tokens in guna input price, plus tokens out guna output price, per million tokens, guna turns |
| Memory mein model weights | 16-bit par har parameter ke lagbhag 2 bytes, toh 7-billion-parameter model ko lagbhag 14 GB chahiye, plus us conversation ke liye memory jo wo process kar raha hai |

Hamesha bolo ki price ek assumption hai aur uski ek date hai. Prices aksar badalte hain, aur jo candidate ye jaanta hai wo experienced lagta hai.

### Conversation ko drive karna

- **Signpost karo.** "Main pehle high level karunga, phir retrieval aur outages mein gehraai se jaaunga, kyunki gadbad wahin ho sakti hai." Ab interviewer ko aapka plan pata hai aur wo jaldi redirect kar sakta hai.
- **Har phase badalne par check in karo.** "Kya ye aapke soche hue se match karta hai, ya kahin aur gehraai mein jaaoon?" Interviewer ke hints instructions hain. Agar wo evaluation ke baare mein do baar poochhein, toh wahaan jaao.
- **Bolkar socho, par sentences mein.** Likhte hue das second ki chuppi theek hai. Tees second ki chuppi mein room haath se nikal jaata hai.
- **"What if" ka jawaab chaar moves mein do.** Use ek number ke roop mein dobaara bolo, batao kaun-sa part pehle tootega, sirf wahi part badlo, badlaav ki cost batao.
- **Galtiyon se khulkar recover karo.** "Maine kaha tha har conversation mein ek model call; asal mein har message par ek hai, toh uska chaar guna. Main number dobaara nikaalta hoon." Apni galti khud pakadna achha signal hai.
- **"Mujhe nahi pata" kaam ke tareeke se bolo.** "Mujhe us provider ki exact rate limit nahi pata, toh main week one mein check karunga; agar wo hamaare peak se kam hai, toh fix ek queue ya doosra region hai."

## Ek example se samjho

Prompt: "Ek state electricity board ke liye WhatsApp assistant design karo jo billing aur outage sawaalon ke jawaab Tamil aur English mein de." Neeche ke numbers candidate ke bataaye assumptions hain, kisi asli board ke facts nahi.

**Minute 0 se 5, jaise bola gaya.**

> **Candidate:** Kuch bhi draw karne se pehle, kuch sawaal. Board kitne consumers ko serve karta hai, aur kya traffic spike hota hai?
>
> **Interviewer:** Lagbhag 2 million. Bills jaane ke baad aur outages ke dauraan spike hota hai.
>
> **Candidate:** Kya answers sirf consumers ko jaate hain, aur kya wo apne account ke baare mein pooch sakte hain?
>
> **Interviewer:** Haan. Unhe apna bill check kar paana chahiye.
>
> **Candidate:** Toh mujhe identity check chahiye, main consumer number ko one-time code se verify karunga. Outage status ek internal system mein hai jise main call kar sakta hoon? Aur kya data India mein hi rehna chahiye?
>
> **Interviewer:** Dono ka jawaab haan.
>
> **Candidate:** Main assumptions batata hoon. Normal din par 1% consumers message karte hain, toh 20,000 conversations. Bill ya outage waale din uska paanch guna, 100,000. Har conversation mein consumer ke chaar messages. Success ka matlab hai sahi tariff answers aur kam helpline calls. Inhi ke saath chaloon?

**Minute 5 se 10: high level.** Ek provider ke through WhatsApp Business Platform, phir ek API service, phir intent routing chaar paths mein: bill lookup aur outage status (code check ke baad tool calls), tariff sawaal (dono languages mein tariff orders aur FAQs par retrieval, citations ke saath), aur complaints (ek reference number ke saath log hokar staff ko saunpi jaati hain). Indian region mein hosted ek model reply likhta hai.

**Minute 10 se 28: estimate, phir deep dives.**

> **Candidate:** Quick numbers. Ek peak day mein lagbhag 10 busy hours mein 100,000 conversations, aur sabse kharaab ghanta average ka lagbhag teen guna. Yaani ek ghante mein 30,000 conversations, guna chaar messages, toh lagbhag 33 model calls har second. Har call mein lagbhag 3,000 tokens in, jismein Tamil ke zyada tokens bhi gine hain, aur 250 out. Yaani peak par lagbhag 6.5 million tokens har minute. Main sabse pehle provider ki rate limit iske against check karunga.

| Item | Assumption | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | Maan lo input aur output tokens ke $0.10 aur $0.40 per million, ₹94 ka ek dollar | Lagbhag ₹0.15 har conversation |
| WhatsApp | Meta ka rate card nahi, third-party reports ke hisaab se 1 October 2026 se India mein har service message ka lagbhag ₹0.115 (kuch ₹0.145 tak batate hain), har number par mahine ke 1,000 free ke baad, GST se pehle (November 2026 mein check kiya) | Lagbhag ₹0.46 har conversation, chaar replies |
| Mahina | 26 normal din aur 4 peak din, toh 9.2 lakh conversations | Model ka lagbhag ₹1.4 lakh, WhatsApp ka ₹4.2 lakh, GST aur provider fees se pehle |

> **Candidate:** Toh agar wo reported rate sahi hai, toh channel model se lagbhag teen guna mehenga pad sakta hai. Rates aksar badalte hain, isliye main Meta ke rate card par confirm karunga. Isse do baatein nikalti hain. Saste model se zyada bachat chhoti conversations se hoti hai. Aur outage sawaalon ko model ki zaroorat hi nahi: tool status lautaata hai aur ek fixed Tamil ya English reply use bhar deta hai.

Phir deep dives cover karte hain bilingual retrieval (tariff tables ko tables ke roop mein hi extract karna, dono languages mein chunks, har chunk par tariff order ki date), aur outage answers jo sirf live tool se aate hain, har area ke liye kuch minute cache kiye hue, kyunki hazaaron log ek saath poochhte hain. Latency budget poore reply ke liye hai, kyunki har reply ek complete message ke roop mein jaata hai: 100 mein se 95 replies ke liye lagbhag 5 seconds.

**Minute 28 se 35.** Failures: galat tariff (citations, koi source match na ho toh refusal, flag hue answers ka weekly review), injection (complaint logging ke alaawa koi write tools nahi), provider outage (helpline number ke saath ek fixed message). Evaluation: dono languages mein 300 asli sawaal, intent ke hisaab se scored, board ke saath agreed ek launch bar; pehle ek district mein launch karo aur wahaan helpline call volume par nazar rakho.

**Interruptions.**

> **Interviewer:** Agar outage system hi down ho toh?
>
> **Candidate:** Tab outage answers waala part toot-ta hai. Main aakhri known status uske timestamp ke saath dikhaaunga, helpline number doonga aur on-call ko alert karunga. Main model ko restoration time ka andaaza nahi lagaane doonga.
>
> **Interviewer:** Agar traffic das guna ho jaaye toh?
>
> **Candidate:** Tab peak par lagbhag 330 calls har second aur 65 million tokens har minute. Sabse pehle rate limit toot-ti hai. Agar peak traffic ka zyaadatar hissa outage sawaal hain, toh fixed-reply path wo model calls hata deta hai. Phir main prompt ka fixed part cache karunga, aur baaki ko "hum jaldi reply kar rahe hain" message ke saath queue karunga. Cost ye hai ki peak ke bilkul upar replies dheeme honge.

| Phase | Kis cheez se scorecard evidence mila |
| --- | --- |
| Clarify | Poocha ki answers kise milte hain aur kya live systems se aana chahiye; numbers bataaye |
| Estimate | Peak par 33 calls har second, aur ek cost split jisne design badal diya |
| Deep dive | Bina model waala outage path; dated tariff chunks ke saath bilingual retrieval |
| What if | Tootne waala part bataaya, sirf wahi badla, cost bataai |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Kabhi na poochhna ki answer kise milta hai.** Staff-facing aur customer-facing systems ko alag guards chahiye, aur interviewer isi sawaal ka intezaar kar raha hota hai.
- **Jahaan lookup kaafi ho wahaan agent design karna.** Bill amount aur outage status database reads hain. Wahaan model jodne se cost, delay aur galat hone ka ek raasta badhta hai.
- **Bina assumptions ke numbers.** "Lagbhag ₹5 lakh har mahina" ka koi matlab nahi jab tak aap wo volume, tokens aur prices na batao jinse ye nikla.
- **Sirf model ki price lagaana.** Channel, one-time codes, human review aur hosting, tokens se zyada mehenge pad sakte hain.
- **Hints ko noise samajhna.** Jab interviewer ek hi baat do baar pooche, toh wo bata raha hai ki missing evidence kahaan hai.
- **Rata hua architecture sunaana.** Har prompt mein ek-do constraints hote hain jinse design mudna chahiye. Agar aapka design bank aur electricity board ke liye ek jaisa dikhta hai, toh aapne wo miss kar diye.
- **"What if" se behes karna.** Ye ek nayi requirement hai, aapke judgement ko challenge nahi. Adapt karo, phir cost batao.

## Practice karo

Neeche link ki gayi Chip Huyen ki ML interviews book ke wo hisse padho jo batate hain ki interview rounds kaise chalte hain, aur apne pehle timed run se pehle "AI system design" lesson ka content revise karo.

Is lesson ka check ek project hai. Ye das prompts bolkar practice karo. Do ko poore 35 minute ke designs ki tarah karo, aur baaki ko paanch minute ke spoken answers ki tarah.

1. Ek bank ke credit card customers ke liye Hindi aur English mein support assistant design karo. (design)
2. Per-team access rights ke saath 10 million internal documents par question answering design karo. (design)
3. Ek naye, saste model par switch ko aap kaise evaluate karoge?
4. Hamaare AI feature ki cost is mahine double ho gayi. Aap kaise pata karoge ki kyun, aur use aadha kaise karoge?
5. Support tickets ko 40 categories mein baantne ke liye prompting, RAG ya fine-tuning?
6. Customer emails padhne waale assistant mein prompt injection kaise rokoge?
7. Train tickets book karne waala agent design karo. Wo bina poochhe kya kar sakta hai?
8. Ek construction firm ke liye 200 page ke tender documents ko aap kaise summarise karoge?
9. Kisi aise AI project ke baare mein batao jiska first version fail hua.
10. Aap kaise tay karte ho ki ek assistant launch ke liye ready hai?

Ek design answer timer ke saath record karo. Use chhah mein se score karo, har ek ka ek point: draw karne se pehle clarify kiya; numbers bataaye; deep dives se pehle high level; failures ko guards ke saath bataaya; test set aur launch bar diya; ek "what if" ka jawaab uski cost ke saath diya. Note karo ki aapke minute asal mein kahaan gaye. Kisi partner ke saath practice karna jo do baar interrupt kare, aur bhi achha hai, aur unke liye interviewer banne se aap seekhte ho ki ek saaf answer kaisa sunaai deta hai.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** ek andekha AI system 35 minute mein, bolkar, design kar sako, rubric par chhah mein se kam se kam paanch score ke saath, aur har number ek bataaye hue assumption se juda ho.
