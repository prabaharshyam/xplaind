## Ye kya hai

AI product manager loops mein aam taur par ye hote hain:

- **Product sense / design**: "X ke liye AI feature design karo" ya "Y ko AI se behtar banao."
- **Metrics aur evaluation**: "Success kaise naapoge?", "Kaise pata chalega ki AI launch ke liye kaafi achha hai?"
- **Technical depth**: engineers ke saath kaam karne laayak: RAG versus fine-tuning, latency aur cost trade-offs, models hallucinate kyun karte hain, agent kya hota hai.
- **Strategy aur business**: build versus buy, pricing, competition, unit economics.
- **Responsible AI**: bias, privacy, safety, regulation, AI kab use nahi karna chahiye.
- **Behavioural**: pichhli situations jo judgement, influence aur learning dikhayein.

| Round | Kya poochte hain |
|---|---|
| Product sense | "X ke liye AI feature design karo" |
| Metrics aur evals | "Kaise pata chale ki kaafi achha hai?" |
| Technical depth | "RAG ya fine-tuning?" |
| Strategy | Build ya buy, pricing, unit economics |
| Responsible AI | Bias, privacy, safety, AI kab use nahi karna |
| Behavioural | Numbers ke saath STAR stories |

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**AI product design questions ke liye ek structure.**

1. *Clarify*: user, goal, constraints (market, platform, languages, regulation).
2. *User problems*: list banao, reasons ke saath ek chuno (frequency, pain, value).
3. *Kya AI sahi tool hai?* Batao kyun, aur kaunse simple options par aapne socha.
4. *Solution*: experience step by step, ye bhi ki AI galat ho toh kya hota hai (sources, confidence, correction, hand-over).
5. *Kaise kaam karta hai*: sankshep mein: kaunsa data chahiye, retrieval hai ya nahi, model choice, human oversight.
6. *Metrics*: ek primary metric, guardrail metrics, ek offline evaluation plan aur online signals.
7. *Risks*: quality, cost, privacy, fairness, misuse, aur mitigations.
8. *Launch plan*: prototype, pilot, rollout, aur kya hone par aap ruk jaoge.

**Metrics ke sawaal.** Model quality (golden set par accuracy, faithfulness) ko product outcomes (task completion, bacha hua time, retention, revenue) aur guardrails (complaints, escalations, cost per user) se alag rakho. Samjhao ki launch bar aapne experts aur data ke saath kaise set kiya.

**Technical sawaal.** Saaf aur honest raho: jargon nahi, trade-offs samjhao. "Hum prompting aur retrieval se shuru karenge kyunki facts har hafte badalte hain; fine-tuning purani knowledge ko freeze kar deta."

**Responsible AI ke sawaal.** Dikhao ki aap harm pehchaan sakte ho aur phir bhi ship kar sakte ho: kise nuksaan ho sakta hai, aap use kaise test karoge, insaan kahan control mein rahenge, aap kya nahi banaoge.

**Behavioural.** Numbers ke saath STAR stories taiyaar karo: ek launch, ek failure, engineering ke saath ek disagreement, ek baar jab aapne stakeholder ko no kaha, adhoore data ke saath liya gaya ek decision.

**Zor se bolkar practice karo**, time lagakar, kisi dost ke saath ya khud ko record karke. Interviewers "sahi" jawab se zyada structure aur judgement dekhte hain.

**Aath steps time mein kaise baantein.** 25 minute ke design round mein, ek balanced answer aisa dikhta hai:

| Step | Minutes | Interviewer kya sunta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Clarify | 2 | Kya aap pehle users, goals aur constraints ke baare mein poochte ho? |
| 2. User problems | 3 | Kya aap reasons ke saath ek problem chunte ho? |
| 3. Kya AI sahi tool hai? | 2 | Kya aap simple options par vichaar karte ho? |
| 4. Solution | 6 | Kya experience saaf hai, tab bhi jab AI galat ho? |
| 5. Kaise kaam karta hai | 3 | Kya aapko parts pata hain: data, retrieval, model, oversight? |
| 6. Metrics | 4 | Primary metric, guardrails, offline aur online evaluation |
| 7. Risks | 3 | Quality, cost, privacy, fairness, misuse, mitigations ke saath |
| 8. Launch plan | 2 | Pilot, rollout, aur kya hone par aap ruk jaoge |

Ghadi par nazar rakho. Kai candidates solution par pandrah minute laga dete hain aur metrics ya risks tak pahunchte hi nahi, jabki AI PM judgement sabse zyada wahin dikhta hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Question: "Ek popular Indian food-delivery app ke liye AI feature design karo."

Kavya ka ek strong answer, sankshep mein:

*Clarify*: metros aur tier-2 cities ke users, kai languages, phone-first; goal order frequency hai ya satisfaction?

*Problem*: wo list banati hai ki kya order karna hai ye chunna, delivery issues aur dietary needs, aur "post-order issues" (missing items, delays) chunti hai: aksar hote hain, takleef dete hain, support ke liye mehnge hain.

*Kyun AI*: complaints alag-alag free text aur photos mein aati hain; AI unhe samajh sakta hai aur routine cases turant suljha sakta hai.

*Solution*: in-app support assistant jo complaint aur photo padhta hai, order data check karta hai, ek limit ke andar refunds apne aap deta hai, kuch bhi unusual ho toh hand-over karta hai, Hindi, Tamil aur English mein kaam karta hai, aur confirm karne se pehle saaf dikhata hai ki kya hone waala hai.

*Metrics*: primary, resolution ka median time; guardrails, refund cost per order, repeat complaints, fraud rate, satisfaction; 500 labelled pichhli complaints par offline evaluation, launch bar support team ke saath set kiya.

*Risks*: refund fraud (limits, photo checks, account history), galat refusals (aasaan hand-over), cost (classification ke liye chhota model, mushkil cases ke liye bada).

*Launch*: do cities mein 5% orders, control se compare karo, metrics tikein toh expand karo.

Interviewer ka follow-up: "Agar refund cost 20% badh jaye?" Kavya batati hai ki automation kaatne se pehle naye accounts ke liye limits kasna aur fraud patterns check karna.

| Step | Kavya ka choice | Achha score kyun milta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | Post-order issues | Aksar hote hain, takleef dete hain, mehnge hain; saaf wajah di |
| Kyun AI | Alag-alag free text aur photos | Rules aur forms itni variety cover nahi kar paate |
| Failure design | Confirm karne se pehle outcome dikhata hai; aasaan hand-over | AI ke galat hone ki planning hai |
| Metrics | Median time to resolution, cost aur fraud guardrails ke saath | AI ko business outcomes se jodta hai |
| Launch | Do cities mein 5% orders, control ke saath | Naapa hua, palatne laayak |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Seedhe solution par koodna.** Pehle clarify karo aur ek problem chuno.
- **AI sirf AI ke liye.** Batao ki AI simple options se behtar kyun hai.
- **Failure ko ignore karna.** Design karo ki AI galat ho toh kya hota hai.
- **Sirf model metrics.** Success ko user aur business outcomes se jodo, guardrails ke saath.
- **Bina samjhe jargon.** Trade-offs saaf shabdon mein samjhao.

## Practice karo

PAIR Guidebook aur SVPG ke four big risks, aur Tech Interview Handbook ka behavioural guide dobara dekho.

1. Aath-step structure use karke teen design questions ke jawab zor se bolkar do, har ek 25 minute mein (jaise: government services app ke liye, hospital ke patient app ke liye, stock-broking app ke liye AI).
2. Har ek ke liye primary metric, do guardrails aur launch bar likho.
3. Numbers ke saath paanch STAR stories taiyaar karo, aur har ek ko do minute ke andar practice karo.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap ek anjaan AI product design question ka jawab 25 minute mein saaf structure, metrics aur risks ke saath de sako.
