## Ye kya hai

AI product manager interview ye check karta hai ki kya aap tay kar sakte ho ki kaun sa AI feature banana hai, saabit kar sakte ho ki wo kaafi achha hai, aur real users aane ke baad use safe aur affordable rakh sakte ho. Iska zyadatar hissa classic PM interview jaisa hi dikhta hai. Fark ye hai ki AI output probabilistic hota hai: wahi feature zyadatar samay sahi hota hai aur kabhi-kabhi poore bharose se galat. Isliye interviewers teen aadaton par dhyaan dete hain jo classic PM answers aksar chhod dete hain:

1. **Galat jawabon ke liye design karo.** Model galat ho toh user kya dekhta hai, aur wo kisi insaan tak kaise pahunchta hai?
2. **Launch se pehle quality naapo.** Real cases par ek eval aur pehle se tay launch bar, "demo achha dikha" nahi.
3. **Unit economics jaano.** Har request mein tokens ka paisa lagta hai, aur har galat action usse zyada mehnga pad sakta hai.

Rounds company ke hisaab se badalte hain, aur unka order fixed nahi hota. Ek aam set:

| Round | Typical sawaal | Achha jawab kya dikhata hai |
|---|---|---|
| Product sense | "Ek bus-ticket app ke liye AI feature design karo." | Ek chuni hui user problem, AI kyun fit hai iska kaaran, aur failure ke liye ek plan |
| Metrics and evals | "Aapka eval 93% kehta hai. Launch karoge?" | Goal se juda metric, launch bar, error severity |
| Technical depth | "Retrieval ya fine-tuning?" | Seedhe trade-offs: freshness, cost, latency, effort |
| Strategy | "API par build karein ya apna model train karein?" | Cost per request, differentiation, vendor risk |
| Responsible AI | "Isse kise nuksaan ho sakta hai?" | Naam lekar bataye harms, unke tests, kahan insaan control mein rehte hain |
| Behavioural | "Kisi aise launch ke baare mein batao jo galat gaya." | Real numbers aur ek seekh ke saath STAR story |

Kuch companies take-home assignment bhi bhejti hain, jiski baat neeche hai. Ye lesson Kavya ke saath chalta hai, jo Bengaluru mein AI PM interviews ki taiyaari kar rahi hai, numbers ke saath ek poore design answer tak.

## Ye kaise kaam karta hai

### Design sawaalon ke liye ek structure

1. *Clarify*: user, business goal, aur languages, phones, connectivity aur regulation jaise constraints. Agar interviewer goal nahi chunta, toh ek assumption batao aur aage badho.
2. *User problems*: teen ya chaar list karo, ek chuno, aur batao kyun (kitni baar hoti hai, kitni takleef deti hai, uski keemat kya hai).
3. *Kya AI sahi tool hai?* Jis simple option par aapne socha uska naam lo, jaise form, rules ya behtar search, aur batao wo kyun kam padta hai.
4. *Experience*: step by step, ismein ye bhi ki AI galat ho toh kya hota hai: sources dikhao, action se pehle confirm karo, users ko sudhaarne do, kisi insaan ko hand over karo.
5. *Ye kaise kaam karta hai*: kaun sa data chahiye, retrieval hai ya nahi, kis step ke liye kaun sa model, kahan koi insaan review karta hai.
6. *Metrics*: ek primary metric, supporting metrics, guardrails, aur launch bar.
7. *Risks*: quality, cost, privacy, fairness aur misuse, har ek ke saath ek mitigation.
8. *Launch plan*: offline eval, shadow mode, control group ke saath ek chhota pilot, aur wo result jis par aap ruk jaoge.

25 minute ke design round mein, ek balanced answer samay aise baant-ta hai:

| Step | Minute | Interviewer kis par dhyaan deta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Clarify | 2 | Ideas se pehle users, goal aur constraints |
| 2. User problems | 3 | Kaaranon ke saath chuni gayi ek problem |
| 3. Kya AI sahi tool hai? | 2 | Imaandaari se socha gaya ek simple option |
| 4. Experience | 6 | Ek saaf flow, ismein ye bhi ki AI galat ho tab kya |
| 5. Ye kaise kaam karta hai | 3 | Data, retrieval, model choice, oversight |
| 6. Metrics | 4 | Goal se nikalne wala metric, aur ek launch bar |
| 7. Risks | 3 | Mitigations ke saath specific harms |
| 8. Launch plan | 2 | Pilot, control group, stop rules |

Bahut se candidates experience par hi pandrah minute laga dete hain aur metrics ya risks tak pahunchte hi nahi, jabki AI PM judgement sabse zyada wahin dikhta hai. Step 4 ke baad ek baar ghadi dekh lo.

### Metrics: goal se shuru karo

Metrics teen layers mein, isi order mein chuno:

- **Primary (product outcome)**: wo user result jo saabit kare ki goal poora hua, jaise repeat orders, poori hui applications ya bacha hua samay.
- **Supporting (AI behaviour)**: deflection rate (bina insaan ke band hue cases ka share), escalation rate (insaan ko saunpe gaye cases ka share), ek sampled set par correct-resolution rate.
- **Guardrails**: wo cheezein jo kharab nahi honi chahiye, jaise complaints, galat actions, cost per request, aur languages ya regions ke beech fairness.

Jaal ye hai ki kisi supporting metric ko primary bana diya jaaye. Agar assistant bas insaan tak pahunchna mushkil kar de, toh bhi deflection rate badh jaata hai. Khud se poocho: "Kya ye number badh sakta hai jabki users ka haal kharab ho?" Agar haan, toh wo aapka primary metric nahi hai.

### Evals aur launch bar

Eval ek dohraya ja sakne wala test hai: real cases ka ek fixed set, har ek ka ek sahi outcome, aur har baar ek hi tareeke se score. Interview mein chaar baatein kaho:

1. **Cases kahan se aate hain.** Purane real cases, aise sample kiye hue ki unmein mushkil cases, aapki support ki har language, aur rare lekin serious types shaamil hon.
2. **Unhe label kaun karta hai.** Domain experts, jaise senior support agents, har case par do labellers aur asahmati suljhaane ke liye ek lead. Unka agreement rate batata hai ki ye task insaanon ke liye bhi kitna mushkil hai.
3. **Bade paimaane par score kaise karte ho.** Ek LLM-as-judge (ek model jo rubric ke hisaab se outputs ko grade karta hai) hazaaron outputs score kar sakta hai, lekin ek sample haath se check karo aur launch tabhi karo jab judge insaanon se sahmat ho.
4. **Bar khud, testing se pehle tay.** Ek target score, aaj ke baseline se tulna karke (aksar human team), saath mein naam lekar bataye gaye critical errors ke liye zero tolerance. Agar baaki 7% mein wo errors hain jo logon ko nuksaan pahunchaate hain, toh overall 93% ka koi matlab nahi.

Phir yahi bar launch ke har step ka gate banta hai:

```flow
dir: LR
title: Eval se rollout tak
Offline eval | labelled real cases -[bar poora]-> Shadow mode | AI sujhaata hai, insaan tay karte hain
Offline eval -[bar adhoora]-> Fix karo aur re-test
Shadow mode -[insaanon se sahmat]-> Pilot | 5% users, control group
Pilot -[metrics tike rahe]-> Wider rollout
Pilot -[guardrail toota]-> Roll back
```

### Cost per request

Interview mein exact prices ki zaroorat nahi, lekin hisaab ka shape pata hona chahiye:

> cost per request = (input tokens x input price + output tokens x output price) / 1,000,000

Ise un models par blend karo jin par aap route karte ho (aasaan cases ke liye ek chhota, sasta model, mushkil cases ke liye bada), daily volume se guna karo, aur do cheezon se tulna karo: human baseline ki cost aur AI ki galtiyon ki cost. Doosri tulna hi zyadatar candidates chook jaate hain, aur worked example dikhata hai ki ye kyun maayne rakhti hai. Agar koi real price batate ho, toh batao wo kahan se hai aur kis taareekh ko check kiya; prices aksar badalte hain.

### Technical depth

Aapse code karne ki ummeed nahi hai. Aapse trade-offs seedhe shabdon mein samjhaane ki ummeed hai. Jaise: "Scheme ke rules har kuch hafton mein badalte hain, isliye main current documents par citations ke saath retrieval use karunga. Fine-tuning purane rules ko model mein freeze kar dega aur har badlaav par retraining chahiye hogi." Doosre aam topics: models hallucinate kyun karte hain, latency versus quality, agent kya hai aur use limits kyun chahiye, aur ek context window kya karne deta hai.

### Responsible AI, Indian rules ko dhyaan mein rakhkar

Dikhao ki aap harm ka naam le sakte ho aur phir bhi ship kar sakte ho. Kise nuksaan ho sakta hai? Use kaise test karoge? Kahan koi insaan faisla karta hai? Aap kya banaane se mana karoge? Un rules ko jaano jo aam case studies ko chhoote hain:

- **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** digital personal data ko cover karta hai. Iske Rules November 2025 mein notify hue, aur notice aur consent sameet zyadatar duties ko 18-month lead time diya gaya, isliye customer data use karne wale har design mein consent aur purpose limits hone chahiye.
- **Investment advice** ko SEBI regulate karta hai: paid advice ke liye aam taur par investment adviser ke roop mein registration chahiye, aur exemptions bahut seemit hain. Broking app ke assistant ke liye safe default hai samjhaana, recommend karna nahi.
- **Languages aur devices** fairness ka mudda hain: quality ko har language aur slow connections ke liye alag se test karo, sirf overall average par nahi.

### Pushback sambhaalna

"Costs 20% badh gayin" ya "Users is par bharosa nahi karte" jaise follow-ups test karte hain ki aap dabaav mein kaise sochte ho. Ek achha shape: problem dobaara kaho, poocho ya maan lo ki aapke paas kaun sa data hai, action se pehle diagnose karo (volume, mix ya leakage?), ek specific badlaav karo, aur batao ki kaun sa result aapki raay badal dega. Pehle hi push par apna design mat chhodo, aur bina data ke uska bachaav bhi mat karo.

### Behavioural stories

Paanch STAR stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result) taiyaar karo: ek launch, ek failure, engineering se ek asahmati, ek baar jab aapne no kaha, aur adhoore data ke saath liya gaya ek decision. PM mein aa rahe kisi vyakti ki strong AI story ka shape ye hai. Numbers illustrative hain; apne asli numbers use karo.

- *Situation*: Chennai mein ek insurer ke claims staff har hafte kareeb 2,000 emails chhaantne mein chaar din lagaate the.
- *Task*: Mere zimme ek pilot tha, AI se emails route karna, bina urgent hospital cases ko galat route kiye.
- *Action*: Do senior claims staff ne 300 purane emails label kiye. Humne pehle bar tay kiya: 90% sahi routing aur urgent cases mein ek bhi galat routing nahi. Humne do hafte shadow mode mein chalaaya.
- *Result*: Zyadatar emails ke liye chhaantne ka samay usi din tak aa gaya. Shadow mode ne dikhaaya ki Tamil aur English mile emails sirf 70% baar sahi route hue, isliye model sudharne tak humne wo insaanon ko bheje.

Pakda gaya failure hi story ko bharosemand banata hai.

### Take-home assignments

Kuch companies ghar par solve karne ke liye ek case bhejti hain: ek chhota PRD (product requirements document), kisi maujooda product ka teardown, ya deck ke saath ek strategy sawaal, jiske baad aam taur par ek live presentation aur sawaal hote hain. Recruiter se poocho ki wo kitna samay expect karte hain aur uske aas-paas hi raho. Ek strong submission chhota aur decision par kendrit hota hai: user aur problem, reject kiye gaye options ke saath ek recommendation, metrics aur launch bar, risks, aur assumptions ki ek list. Draft karne ke liye AI tools use karna aam hai, lekin har line ka bachaav aapko live karna hoga.

## Ek worked example

Sawaal: "Ek popular Indian food-delivery app ke liye ek AI feature design karo."

Kavya ka jawab, sankshep mein:

*Clarify*: "Users metros aur chhote shehron mein hain, kai languages use karte hain aur zyadatar phones. Goal order frequency hai ya satisfaction?" Interviewer kehta hai "aap chuno". Kavya: "Main maanungi ki goal un customers ko roke rakhna hai jinka order kharab aaya, kyunki unke chhodne ki sambhaavna sabse zyada hai."

*Problem*: wo kya khaana hai ye chunna, dietary needs aur post-order issues list karti hai, aur post-order issues (missing items, wrong items, delays) chunti hai: baar-baar hote hain, takleef dete hain, aur support ke liye mehnge hain.

*AI kyun*: "Complaints kai languages mein free text aur photos ke roop mein aati hain. Ek form users ko fixed categories mein dhakelta hai aur photo padhne ke liye phir bhi ek agent chahiye. AI dono padh sakta hai aur routine cases turant resolve kar sakta hai."

*Experience*: assistant complaint aur photo padhta hai, order data check karta hai, aur ek limit tak refund offer karta hai. User ke confirm karne se pehle ye theek-theek dikhata hai ki kya hoga. Ye Hindi, Tamil aur English mein kaam karta hai, aur kuch bhi asaamanya ek tap mein kisi insaan tak jaata hai.

*Metrics*: yahin clarify karte samay liya gaya assumption kaam aata hai.

- Primary: un customers ka share jinhone issue report kiya aur 14 days ke andar phir order kiya. Ye seedhe uske bataye goal se nikalta hai.
- Supporting: median time to resolution, deflection rate, escalation rate.
- Guardrails: wrong-refund rate (wo refunds jo koi expert approve nahi karta), wrong-denial rate, refund cost per order, satisfaction, aur yahi metrics language ke hisaab se alag-alag.

*Launch bar*: do senior support agents 500 purani complaints ko sahi outcome ke saath label karte hain, aur support lead asahmati suljhaata hai. Testing se pehle tay bar hai usi set par human team ke correct-resolution rate ki barabari karna. Alag se, foreign object ya allergen jaisi food-safety complaints par ek bhi automatic denial nahi hona chahiye; wo hamesha kisi insaan ke paas jaati hain.

*Cost*: Kavya ek back-of-envelope hisaab lagaati hai aur saaf kehti hai ki har number samjhaane ke liye ek assumption hai:

| Item | Assumption | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume | Ek din mein 50,000 complaints, 60% AI sambhaalta hai | Ek din mein 30,000 AI conversations |
| Tokens | Har conversation mein 6,000 input aur 600 output | |
| Chhota model, 80% cases | Maan lo input aur output ke liye per million tokens $0.25 aur $1.50 | Har conversation par $0.0024 |
| Bada model, 20% cases | Maan lo per million $2 aur $12 | Har conversation par $0.0192 |
| Blended model cost | Ek dollar mein kareeb ₹90 | Kareeb ₹0.52, retries aur checks ke saath ₹1 maan lo |
| Ek din ki model cost | 30,000 x ₹1 | ₹30,000 |
| Human baseline | Maan lo har ticket par ₹35 | Unhi 30,000 tickets ke liye ₹10.5 lakh |
| Wrong refunds | Maan lo 30,000 mein se 3% ko galat refund, average ₹150 | Ek din mein ₹1.35 lakh |

Uska nishkarsh: "Token cost chhoti hai. Wrong-refund rate mein har ek point ki badhat se ek din mein kareeb ₹45,000 lagte hain, poore model bill se zyada. Isliye launch ka faisla wrong-refund rate par tika hai, aur yahi wo guardrail hai jis par main sabse kareeb se nazar rakhungi." Yahi ek insight AI PM answer ko ek generic answer se alag karti hai.

*Risks*: refund fraud (naye accounts ke liye limits, photo checks, account history), galat denials (ek tap mein hand-over), language ke hisaab se quality gaps (alag eval slices), aur photos aur addresses ki privacy (sirf utna rakho jitna complaint ko chahiye).

*Launch*: pehle shadow mode, phir do shehron mein 5% orders, ek control group ke saamne. Agar wrong-refund rate ya food-safety handling apna bar tode toh ruk jao.

Interviewer push back karta hai: "Pilot mein refund costs 20% badh gayin. Ab kya?" Kavya action se pehle diagnose karti hai: "Kya zyada complaints hain, alag mix hai, ya leakage? Main refunds ko account age aur complaint type ke hisaab se todungi, aur control group se tulna karungi. Agar badhat naye accounts mein hai, toh main unki auto-refund limit kam karungi aur photo zaroori karungi. Agar 14-day repeat-order rate itna badha hai ki iska kharcha nikal aaye, toh main automation kam nahi karungi. Agar control group mein bhi waisi hi badhat dikhe toh main apni raay badal loongi, kyunki tab wajah AI nahi hai."

## Aam galtiyan

- **Seedhe solution par koodna.** Pehle clarify karo, goal ke baare mein ek assumption batao, aur ek problem chuno.
- **AI sirf AI ke liye.** Batao ki aapne kaun sa simple option socha aur wo kyun kam padta hai.
- **Galat jawabon ke liye koi plan nahi.** Har design mein action se pehle confirmation aur kisi insaan tak pahunchne ka aasaan raasta chahiye.
- **Game kiya ja sakne wala primary metric.** Chats, sessions aur deflection badh sakte hain jabki users pareshaan hon. Primary metric goal se nikalna chahiye.
- **Bina context ke "95% accurate".** Batao kin cases par, kis baseline ke muqaable, aur kaun se errors kabhi manzoor nahi.
- **Results ke baad tay kiya gaya launch bar.** Testing se pehle domain experts ke saath bar tay karo, warna uska koi matlab nahi.
- **Errors ki cost ke bina token price.** Hisaab mein model cost aksar sabse chhota number hota hai.
- **"Hum latest model use karenge."** Bada model quality plan nahi hai; eval hai.
- **Groups ko chhipaane wale averages.** Har language, region aur device ke liye quality check karo.
- **Bina samajh ke jargon.** Trade-off seedhe shabdon mein samjhao, ya term chhod do.

## Practice

AI errors ke hisaab se design karne ke liye PAIR Guidebook, product judgement ke liye SVPG ke four big risks, aur Tech Interview Handbook ka behavioural guide phir se dekho.

1. Aath steps use karke teen design sawaalon ka jawab bolkar do, har ek 25 minute. Ek government services app, ek hospital ke patient app aur ek stock-broking app ke liye AI feature try karo. Khud ko record karo aur step 4 ke baad ghadi check karo.
2. Har ek ke liye likho: bataya gaya goal, primary metric, do guardrails, ek critical error ke saath launch bar, aur apne assumptions label karke ek cost-per-request hisaab.
3. Kisi dost se kaho ki har jawab par ek follow-up ke saath push back kare, aur diagnose-then-act shape ki practice karo.
4. Numbers ke saath paanch STAR stories taiyaar karo, kam se kam ek kisi AI ya data decision par, aur har ek do minute se kam mein sunao.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** kisi andekhe AI product design sawaal ka 25 minute mein jawab de sako, ek saaf structure, goal se nikalne wale metric, ek launch bar, ek cost estimate aur pushback par ek shaant jawab ke saath.
