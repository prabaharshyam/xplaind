## Ye kya hai

**Discovery** engagement ka pehla phase hai, aam taur par ek se teen hafte, kuch bhi build hone se pehle. Iska goal hai saajhi samajh:

- Kaunsi problem matter karti hai, kiske liye, aur kitni?
- Aaj kaam kaise hota hai, step by step?
- Time, cost aur errors asal mein kahan jaate hain?
- Kaunsa data maujood hai, kahan, aur kis haalat mein?
- Kaunse constraints lagu hain: rules, systems, budgets, log?
- Kya AI sahi tool hai, ya ek form, report ya process badlaav kaafi hoga?

Output chhota aur concrete hota hai: ek process map, numbers ke saath problem statement, value aur feasibility se rank kiye use cases ki shortlist, aur risks aur open questions.

```flow
title: Discovery, do hafte mein
Week 1: interviews aur shadowing | past tense mein 5-10 interviews, aadha din shadowing -> Process map | trigger, steps, outcome; kaun, system, minute, failure points, judgement ya rote
Process map -> Week 2: rough numbers | volume, per item time, rework -> Use cases score karo
Use cases score karo -> Play-back: "Humne ye suna" -> Client sudharta hai -> Tay pehla use case + baseline
```

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek non-banking lender ko follow karta hai jisne customer chatbot maanga tha aur jise kuch aur chahiye tha.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Kaam karne waalon se baat karo.** Leaders process ko waisa batate hain jaisa design hua; front-line staff waisa jaisa woh hai. Dono ka interview karo. Alag-alag roles mein 30 se 45 minute ki paanch se das baatcheet ka target rakho.

**Past ke baare mein poochho, future par raay nahi.** "Aapne jo aakhri claim process kiya uske baare mein batao" "Kya AI assistant aapki madad karega?" se behtar hai. Log ye andaaza lagane mein kamzor hain ki woh kya use karenge, aur jo unhone kiya woh batane mein achhe.

**Kaam dekho.** Kisi ke saath ek ghanta baitho. Har system jo woh kholte hain, har copy-paste, saathi se poochha har sawaal, har intezaar note karo. Shadowing woh dhoondhti hai jo interviews chhod dete hain.

**Process ko map karo.** Trigger se outcome tak ke steps banao, har ek kaun karta hai, kaunse systems, kitna time aur failure points kahan hain. Mark karo ki kahan judgement chahiye aur kahan sirf rote kaam hai.

**Rough hi sahi, measure karo.** Mahine ka volume, per item time, error ya rework rates, backlog. Jahan ho sake real data maango; staff ke estimates ek shuruaat hain.

**Use cases score karo.** Har candidate ke liye: value (bachaye hue ghante ya errors, revenue), feasibility (data maujood hai, AI is tarah ke task mein achha hai, galtiyon ki tolerance), risk (regulatory, reputational) aur effort. Aise tasks ko favour karo jo baar-baar hote hain, takleef dete hain, jinmein data bahut hai aur jinka output insaan check kar sakta hai.

**Play back karo.** Jo suna woh client ko unhi ke shabdon mein dikhao, aur unhe aapko sudharne do. Bharosa yahin banta hai.

**Use cases score karna:**

| Use case | Value | Feasibility | Risk | Effort | Rank |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Document extraction aur draft credit summary | High: mahine ke lagbhag 4,000 officer-hours, 7% rework | High: decisions ke saath do saal ki files | Medium: officer har figure review karta hai | Medium | 1 |
| Collections call notes summary | Medium | High | Low | Low | 2 |
| Customer chatbot | Low: call centre queries pehle se achhe se sambhaal raha hai | Medium | Medium: customer-facing | Medium | 3 |

**Achhe interview sawaal:**

| Iski jagah | Ye poochho |
| --- | --- |
| "Kya AI aapki madad karega?" | "Aapne jo aakhri application process ki uske baare mein batao." |
| "Kya ye process slow hai?" | "Aakhri wali mein kitna time laga, aur aap kahan ruke?" |
| "Aapko kaunse features chahiye?" | "Pichhle hafte aapne ek system se doosre mein kya copy kiya?" |
| "Kya errors hote hain?" | "Aakhri baar kab kuch wapas bheja gaya, aur kyun?" |

**Play-back likhna.** Ek page: client ke shabdon mein problem, process map, teen zaroori numbers, wajah ke saath ranked use cases, risks aur open questions, aur ek proposed pehla step uske baseline ke saath. Meeting se pehle bhejo, phir saath mein padho aur sudhaar maango. Jo play-back client edit karta hai, use woh apna maanta hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai ka ek non-banking lender "customers ke liye AI chatbot" maangta hai. Aap pehle do hafte ke discovery ka proposal dete ho.

Head of operations, do team leads, chaar credit officers aur do collections agents ke saath interviews, aur loan-processing team ko aadha din shadow karne par pata chalta hai:

- Customer queries zyadatar maujooda call centre achhe se sambhaal leta hai.
- Asli dard loan processing mein hai: credit officers har application par lagbhag 40 minute bank statements, salary slips aur bureau reports padhne, figures spreadsheet mein dobara type karne aur summary note likhne mein lagate hain. Mahine mein 6,000 applications ke saath, ye lagbhag 4,000 officer-hours hain.
- Dobara type kiye figures ki errors ki wajah se 7% applications credit committee wapas bhej deti hai.
- Documents PDFs aur phone photos ki shakal mein aate hain; lender ke paas final decisions ke saath do saal ki purani applications hain.

Aapka play-back kehta hai ki customer chatbot se nahi, document extraction aur draft credit summary se shuru karo, jisme officer har figure review kare. Baseline 40 minute aur 7% rework hai. Head of operations maan jaate hain, aur chatbot ka idea baad ki list mein chala jaata hai.

| Finding | Evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Customer queries pehle se achhe se sambhal rahi hain | Call-centre interviews aur data |
| Officers har application par lagbhag 40 minute lagate hain | Shadowing aur team-lead estimates |
| Mahine mein 6,000 applications, lagbhag 4,000 officer-hours | Operations data |
| 7% applications retyping errors ki wajah se wapas | Credit committee records |
| Recommendation | Extraction aur draft summary se shuru karo; chatbot baad ki list mein |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Pehli meeting mein solutioning.** Pehle suno; evidence milne ke baad propose karo.
- **Sirf managers se baat karna.** Kaagaz par ka process aur asal mein chalta process alag hote hain.
- **Hypothetical sawaal poochna.** Aakhri real case ke baare mein poochho.
- **Numbers na hona.** Volume aur per item time ke bina aap use cases rank nahi kar sakte aur baad mein value prove nahi kar sakte.
- **Non-AI fixes ko ignore karna.** Kabhi-kabhi ek template ya database view solve kar deta hai. Ye kehne se credibility banti hai.

## Practice karo

GOV.UK ke discovery aur user research ke guides padho.

1. Ek aisa process chuno jo aap observe kar sako (college admission office, kisi dukaan ki billing, dost ki job). Sirf past-tense sawaalon se do logon ka interview karo.
2. Process map banao, steps, log, systems, time aur failure points ke saath.
3. Teen use cases likho, har ek ko value, feasibility, risk aur effort par score karo, aur ek page ka play-back likho.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap ek process map aur har use case ke liye kam se kam ek number ke saath ranked use-case list present kar sako, aur samjha sako ki top wala pehle kyun hai.
