## Ye kya hai

Paramparik software predictable hota hai: wahi input toh wahi output, aur galat ho toh woh bug hai. AI ka output badalta rehta hai, aur woh apni prakriti se kabhi-kabhi galat hota hai. **Anishchit output ke liye UX** isi sachchai ke hisaab se design karna hai.

Lakshya hai **calibrated trust**:

```flow
dir: LR
Under-trust | users ek galti ke baad kaam ka tool chhod dete hain: value bekaar gayi -> Calibrated trust | sahi hone ki sambhaavna par bharosa, pakka na ho toh check, jaldi sambhalna: yahi lakshya hai
Calibrated trust -> Over-trust | har jawab maan lete hain, galtiyaan customers tak pahuncha dete hain: asli nuksaan
```

Research par aadharit guidelines, jaise Microsoft ki Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction aur Google ki People + AI Guidebook, kuch mukhya patterns par ekmat hain. Ye lesson unhe aise faislon mein badalta hai jo aap screen par le sako.

Hum Chennai ki ek insurance company ki product manager Divya ke saath chalenge, jab woh ek AI coverage assistant ko dobara design karti hain kyunki agents ne customers ko galat jawab de diye the. Lesson ke end tak aap aisi screen design kar paoge jisme user kuch hi seconds mein galat AI jawab ko notice kare aur sambhal jaaye.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Patterns

| Pattern | Kaisa dikhta hai | Kyun kaam karta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ummeedein tay karo** | "Aapke policy documents ke sawaalon ke jawab deta hai. Legal advice nahi deta." | Users ko pata hota hai kis cheez par bharosa karein |
| **Dikhao ki ye AI hai** | Jahan users jaanna chahein wahan saaf label | Users apne check karne ki savdhaani usi hisaab se rakhte hain |
| **Sources dikhao** | Numbered citations; ek par tap karo toh exact passage dikhe | Users check kar sakte hain, aur galat jawab alag se dikh jaate hain |
| **Uncertainty ka signal do** | "Low confidence: kripya amount check karein"; highlight kiye fields | Dhyaan wahan jaata hai jahan chahiye |
| **Correction aasaan banao** | Editable drafts, kaaran ke saath one-click "ye galat hai", undo | Galtiyaan theek karna sasta ho jaata hai, aur feedback se system sudharta hai |
| **Insaan ke haath mein control rakho** | Bhejne se pehle preview; paise, messages, deletions se pehle confirm | Consequences ke liye insaani faisla chahiye |
| **Gracefully fail ho** | "Mujhe saaf jawab nahi mila. Underwriting helpdesk try karein." | Na khaali screen, na confident andaaze |
| **Progress dikhao** | Streaming text; "3 documents check ho rahe hain..." | Bina feedback ke intezaar toota hua lagta hai |

### Confidence ka signal savdhaani se do

"93.7% confident" jaise sateek numbers vaigyanik lagte hain aur galat bharosa bulate hain, khaaskar jab number theek se calibrate na ho. Behtar tareeke:

- Un specific fields ko highlight karo jin par system ko pakka nahi hai;
- Seedhe shabd use karo: "Mujhe is amount par pakka nahi hai";
- Behaviour badlo, jaise retrieval kamzor ho toh jawab dene se mana karke kisi insaan ki or bhejna.

### Indian products mein patterns

Kai Indian users AI se WhatsApp chats, voice assistants aur regional-language apps mein milte hain, jahan screens chhoti aur dhyaan kam hota hai. Patterns phir bhi lagu hote hain, chhote roop mein. WhatsApp bot jawab ke ant mein likh sakta hai "Source: Policy section 4.2. Agar ye theek na lage toh WRONG reply karein." Voice assistant keh sakta hai "Mujhe pakka nahi hai. Kya main aapko agent se jod doon?" Regional-language app source passage usi bhasha mein dikha sakta hai jisme jawab hai. Chhoti screens par sources aur aasaan correction zyada zaroori ho jaate hain, kam nahi, kyunki users ek baar mein zyada context nahi dekh paate.

### Pehli galti ke liye design karo

Users jaldi tay kar lete hain ki feature par bharosa karna hai ya nahi. Agar pehli galti aise tool se aaye jo pakka dikh raha tha, toh bharosa toot jaata hai. Agar tool ne shuru se apni limits aur sources dikhaye the, toh pehli galti ka asar bahut kam hota hai: user ne use pakad liya, aur product ne isme madad ki.

### Asli errors ke saath test karo

Usability tests aamtaur par dekhte hain ki users koi task poora kar paate hain ya nahi. AI features ke liye aise cases bhi rakho jahan AI **jaanboojhkar galat** ho, aur dekho ki users notice karte hain aur sambhalte hain ya nahi. Design ki asli pariksha yahi hai.

```text
  Test plan for an AI answer screen
  8 questions:  6 with correct AI answers, 2 with planted errors
  Measure:      did the user spot the error?  how long did it take?
                did they know what to do next?
```

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai ki ek insurance company apne agent app mein AI assistant jodti hai, taaki agents customers se baat karte hue policy coverage ke sawaalon ke jawab pa sakein.

**Version one** ek confident paragraph jawab dikhata hai, bina sources, bina limits aur problems report karne ka koi tareeka nahi. Aath sawaalon wale usability test mein do jawab jaanboojhkar galat the. Agents ne shayad hi kabhi check kiya, aur role-play mein **dono** galat jawab customers ko de diye gaye.

**Version two**, jise product manager Divya ne dobara design kiya:

| Change | Agent ko ab kya dikhta hai |
| --- | --- |
| Ummeedein | Header ki ek line: "AI jawab policy wordings se deta hai. Customers ko salaah dene se pehle check karein." |
| Sources | Har jawab mein numbered citations; ek par tap karo toh exact clause dikhe |
| Graceful failure | Jab retrieval ko sirf kamzor matches milein, jawab ki shuruaat "Mujhe saaf jawab nahi mila" se hoti hai aur underwriting helpdesk sujhaya jaata hai |
| Uncertainty | Amounts aur exclusions highlight hote hain taaki agent confirm kare |
| Correction | Teen kaaranon wala "galat jawab" button, jise product team har hafte dekhti hai |

**Agla test:**

| Measure | Version one | Version two |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Agents ne kitne planted galat jawab pakde | Har session mein ausat 2 mein se 0 | Sabhi sessions mein 8 mein se 7 |
| Agents ka satisfaction score | 5 mein se 3.1 | 5 mein se 4.2 |
| Pehli asli galti ke baad usage | Gira | Badha |

Agents ne version two par **zyada** bharosa kiya, kam nahi, kyunki unhe control mehsoos hua aur woh dekh sakte the ki jawab kyun diya gaya. Production mein pehli asli galtiyon ke baad bhi usage girne ki jagah badhta raha.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Ye chhupana ki ye AI hai.** Users apna bharosa is aadhaar par tay karte hain ki unhe kya pata hai.
- **Bina sources ke confident jawab.** Users check nahi kar paate, isliye zaroorat se zyada bharosa karte hain.
- **Nakli sateekta.** "93.7% confident" galat bharosa bulata hai; seedhe signals use karo.
- **Theek karne mein mushkil outputs.** Final, bina edit wale outputs users ko galtiyaan maan lene ki or dhakelte hain.
- **Sirf safal cases test karna.** Dekho ki AI galat ho toh users kya karte hain.
- **Khaali failures.** Agle kadam ke bina "kuch galat ho gaya" users ko atka deta hai.

## Practice karo

Neeche link ki Microsoft ki Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction padho, aur explainability aur trust par PAIR ka chapter bhi.

1. **Audit.** Koi AI feature chuno jo aap use karte ho aur use paanch guidelines ke saamne parkho. Note karo kahan fail hota hai.
2. **Sketch.** Kaagaz par dobara design ki hui screen banao, jisme ummeedein, sources, uncertainty signals aur aasaan correction hon.
3. **Test.** Apna sketch do logon ko dikhao, ek sahi aur ek galat AI jawab ke saath, aur note karo ki woh galat wala pakadte hain ya nahi.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap aisa design dikha sako jahan user galat AI jawab ko kuch hi seconds mein notice karke sambhal jaaye.
