## Ye kya hai

Puraane tarah ka software predictable hota hai: wahi input toh wahi output, aur jab wo galat ho, toh wo ek bug hai jise aap fix kar sakte ho. AI ka output badalta rehta hai, aur wo apni prakriti se kabhi-kabhi galat hota hai, tab bhi jab kuch toota nahi hai. **Anishchit output ke liye UX** isi sachchaai ke hisaab se design karna hai.

Goal hai **calibrated trust**: users har case mein AI par utna hi bharosa karein jitne ka wo haqdaar hai.

```flow
dir: LR
Under-trust | users ek galti ke baad kaam ka tool nazarandaaz kar dete hain: value bekaar gayi -> Calibrated trust | sahi hone ki sambhaavna ho toh bharosa, pakka na ho toh check, jaldi sambhalna
Calibrated trust -> Over-trust | har jawaab maan lena aur galtiyaan aage badha dena: asli nuksaan
```

Over-trust ka ek naam hai: **automation bias**, yaani machine ke output ko bina check kiye maan lene ki aadat, jo tab aur mazboot hoti hai jab log busy hon ya machine aamtaur par sahi dikhti ho. Human in the loop design karne waala lesson ise gehraai se samjhaata hai. Yahaan baat seedhi hai: jo screen pakki dikhti hai wo logon ko check karna chhodne ki aadat daalti hai, aur jo screen apna evidence dikhaati hai wo check karna tez bana deti hai.

Research par aadhaarit do guides is field ko aakaar deti hain. Microsoft ki Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction 18 guidelines ko is hisaab se group karti hai ki wo kab laagu hoti hain: **shuruaat mein**, **interaction ke dauraan**, **jab system galat ho**, aur **samay ke saath**. Google ki People + AI Guidebook bhi aisi hi baatein cover karti hai, explainability aur trust par ek chapter ke saath. Ye lesson unhe aise faislon mein badalta hai jo aap screen par le sako, aur wo hissa jodta hai jise designers aksar chhod dete hain: "ye galat ho sakta hai" waala signal asal mein aata kahaan se hai.

Aap Divya ko follow karoge, jo Chennai ki ek insurance company mein product manager hai, jab wo agents ke customers tak galat answers pahunchaane ke baad ek AI coverage assistant ko redesign karti hai.

## Ye kaise kaam karta hai

### Patterns, is hisaab se ki wo kab laagu hote hain

Ye table Microsoft ke chaar phases ko follow karti hai, taaki aap isse kisi bhi AI feature ko pehle use se chhathe mahine tak audit kar sako.

| Phase | Pattern | Kaisa dikhta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shuruaat mein | **Expectations set karo** | "Aapki policy wordings ke baare mein sawaalon ke jawaab deta hai. Legal advice nahi deta." |
| Shuruaat mein | **Dikhao ki ye AI hai** | Wahaan ek saaf label jahaan users tay karte hain ki answer par bharosa karein ya nahi |
| Interaction ke dauraan | **Check kiye hue sources dikhao** | Numbered citations; kisi par tap karo toh exact passage dikhta hai, mel khaata sentence highlight hota hai |
| Interaction ke dauraan | **Uncertainty ka signal do** | Khaas field par "Is amount ko check karein", shabdon mein aur ek icon ke saath |
| Interaction ke dauraan | **Progress dikhao** | Streaming text, ya "3 documents check ho rahe hain..." |
| Jab galat ho | **Correction aasaan banao** | Edit ho sakne waale drafts, kaaran ke saath ek tap waala "Wrong answer", undo |
| Jab galat ho | **Saleeke se fail karo** | "Mujhe saaf jawaab nahi mila. Underwriting helpdesk se poochhein." |
| Jab galat ho | **Control logon ke haath mein rakho** | Bhejne se pehle preview; paise, messages ya deletions se pehle confirm |
| Samay ke saath | **Seekho aur batao** | Feedback ka har hafte review; coverage ya behaviour badalne par "What's new" |

Pehli do rows sasti hain, phir bhi teams unhe chhod deti hain. "Interaction ke dauraan" waali rows tabhi kaam karti hain jab system bata sake ki wo kab unsure hai, aur wahi agla section hai.

### Uncertainty signal kahaan se aata hai

Ek designer ek minute mein peela "please check" badge bana sakta hai. Phir ek engineer ko tay karna padta hai ki use kab dikhaana hai. Agar koi tay nahi karta, toh badge ya toh har cheez par dikhta hai (aur users use nazarandaaz karna seekh jaate hain) ya kisi par nahi. Teams asal mein ye signals use karti hain:

| Signal | Kaise kaam karta hai | Kya pakadta hai | Kya chhoot jaata hai |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retrieval score | Sabse achhe se mel khaane waale passage ka score us threshold se neeche hai jise aap labelled questions par tune karte ho | Aise sawaal jinhe documents cover nahi karte | Aise passages jo milte-julte dikhte hain par galat hain, jaise kisi doosre plan ka clause |
| Citation support check | Kya cited passage mein answer ki amount, date ya yes or no hai? Numbers ke liye exact match; wording ke liye ek doosra checking step | Gadhe hue numbers, aise citations jo claim ko support nahi karte | Aise claims jinhe itne dheele dhang se paraphrase kiya gaya ki wo match nahi hote |
| Rule aur schema checks | Amount allowed range mein hai, plan customer ke plan se mel khaata hai, zaroori fields maujood hain | Naamumkin ya mel na khaane waali values | Sahi lagne waali par galat values |
| Runs ke beech sahmati | Wahi sawaal do-teen baar poochho, ya do models se poochho; jab key fields mel na khaayein toh flag karo | Asthir, andaaze waale answers | Aise answers jo har baar ek hi tarah se galat hote hain |
| Self-reported confidence | Model se poochho "tum kitne sure ho?" | Akele mein bahut kam | Aamtaur par overconfident |

Aakhri row hi jaal hai. Xiong aur unke saathiyon ki 2024 ki ek study mein paaya gaya ki jin language models se unka confidence batane ko kaha gaya, wo bahut zyada overconfident the, aur values zyaadatar 80% se 100% ke beech thi. Kai answers ki tulna karne waale methods ne behtar kiya. Model ke apne confidence ko kamzor evidence maano, kabhi bhi akela signal nahi.

Signals ko milaakar display modes ka ek chhota set banao, jo code mein tay ho, model par na chhoda jaaye:

```flow
title: Signals se us tak jo user dekhta hai
Sawaal -> Passages retrieve karo
Passages retrieve karo -[top score threshold se neeche]-> Koi answer nahi | ye batao aur helpdesk ka raasta do
Passages retrieve karo -[achha match]-> Draft answer
Draft answer -[amount cited clause mein nahi]-> Flag ke saath answer | Is amount ko check karein
Draft answer -[saare checks pass]-> Sources ke saath answer
```

Amounts ke liye us faisle ka ek minimal version aisa dikhta hai. 0.6 ka threshold bas ek udaaharan hai; aap apna threshold kuch sau labelled questions ko score karke aur ye chunkar set karte ho ki kamzor matches kahaan se shuru hote hain.

```python
import re

def numbers(text):
    return set(re.findall(r"\d[\d,]*", text))

def display_mode(answer, cited_passage, top_score, threshold=0.6):
    if top_score < threshold:
        return "no_answer"          # say so and offer the helpdesk
    if not numbers(answer) <= numbers(cited_passage):
        return "check_amount"       # an amount is not in the cited clause
    return "answer"                 # show with sources

clause = "Room rent is covered up to Rs 5,000 per day."
print(display_mode("Room rent is covered up to Rs 5,000 per day.", clause, 0.82))  # answer
print(display_mode("Room rent is covered up to Rs 7,500 per day.", clause, 0.82))  # check_amount
print(display_mode("Dental implants are covered.", clause, 0.41))                  # no_answer
```

Asli systems "5000", "5,000" aur "five thousand" ko normalise bhi karte hain, par shape wahi rehta hai: saste checks, jo screen banne se pehle tay ho jaate hain.

### Citations ko dikhaane se pehle check karo

Yahaan citations sabse mazboot pattern hain, aur wo ulta bhi pad sakte hain. Ding aur unke saathiyon ki 2025 ki ek study mein, citations hone par logon ne chatbot answers par zyada bharosa kiya, tab bhi jab citations random chune gaye the. Bharosa tabhi ghata jab logon ne unhe sach mein check kiya. Isliye jo citation answer ko support nahi karta wo koi citation na hone se bhi bura hai: wo galat answer ko verified jaisa dikha deta hai.

Isse teen rules nikalte hain:

- **Dikhaane se pehle verify karo.** Citation support check chalao. Agar cited passage mein mukhya claim nahi hai, toh citation hata do aur claim ko flag karo.
- **Document se nahi, passage se link karo.** "Policy wording, page 14" se check karna dheema ho jaata hai. Clause ko kholo, mel khaane waale sentence ko highlight karke.
- **Paanch se ek achha citation behtar.** Ding aur unke saathiyon ne paaya ki ek citation ne lagbhag utna hi bharosa badhaaya jitna paanch ne. Extra links checking badhaaye bina bas clutter badhaate hain.

### Percentages ya sirf colour nahi, shabdon mein kaho

"93.7% confident" jaise sateek numbers scientific lagte hain aur galat bharose ko bulaava dete hain, aur unke peechhe ka number shaayad hi kabhi achhe se calibrated hota hai. Users kisi percentage par action bhi nahi le sakte: kya customer ko batane ke liye 81% kaafi hai? Behtar options:

- flag ko usi khaas field par lagao: "Is amount ko check karein", poore answer par koi golmol banner nahi;
- kisi action se jude seedhe shabd use karo: "Mujhe is limit ke baare mein pakka nahi pata. Clause 4.2 mein confirm karein";
- signals kamzor hon toh behaviour badlo: jawaab mat do, aur kisi insaan ki taraf bhejo.

Sirf colour par bharosa mat karo. Colour-blind users ko laal ya peela border shaayad na dikhe, aur accessibility standard WCAG 2.1 kehta hai ki colour jaankaari dikhaane ka akela tareeka nahi hona chahiye. Colour ke saath ek icon aur shabd jodo, aur pakka karo ki screen reader "check karne ki zaroorat hai" announce kare. Voice product mein ise bolkar kaho: "Mujhe is amount ke baare mein pakka nahi pata."

### Friction ko stakes ke hisaab se rakho

Aap jo bhi check jodte ho, usmein kuch seconds lagte hain. Har cheez par confirm step laga do toh log bina padhe click karke aage badh jaate hain, jo phir se automation bias hi hai. Friction wahaan rakho jahaan galtiyaan sabse mehngi padti hain:

| Stakes | Udaaharan | Design |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low | Agent ke apne notes ke liye ek call ka summary | AI label aur aasaan edit; koi zabardasti ke checks nahi |
| Medium | Coverage ka answer jo agent customer ko dohraata hai | Har answer par check kiya hua source, highlight ki hui amounts aur exclusions, "advise karne se pehle check karein" |
| High | Claim rejection, payment, koi legal ya medical statement | Ek confirm step jo key fields dikhaaye, human approval, ya bilkul koi AI answer nahi |

Ek kaam ka decision rule: poochho "Agar ye answer galat hai aur kisi ko pata nahi chalta, toh kaun bharega aur kitna?" Cost jitni badi, row utni upar.

### Indian products mein patterns

Kai Indian users AI se WhatsApp chats, voice assistants aur regional-language apps mein milte hain, jahaan screens chhoti hoti hain aur dhyaan kam. Patterns phir bhi laagu hote hain, chhote roop mein. Ek WhatsApp bot answer ko aise khatam kar sakta hai: "Source: Policy section 4.2. Agar ye sahi nahi lagta toh WRONG reply karein." Ek voice assistant keh sakta hai: "Mujhe pakka nahi pata. Kya main aapko kisi agent se jod doon?"

Language isse bhi mushkil problem khadi karti hai. Answer Tamil ya Hindi mein ho sakta hai, par policy wording aksar sirf English mein hoti hai, isliye Tamil bolne waala user citation check nahi kar paata. Dono dikhao: original English clause, mel khaane waala sentence highlight karke, aur ek translated excerpt jis par label ho "Machine translation. English text hi official wording hai." Dono mein amounts digits mein rakho, aur support check English original par chalao, kyunki translation chupchaap kisi number ya kisi "not" ko badal sakta hai.

### Logon ko batao ki ye AI hai: niyam

Labelling sirf achha design nahi, ek legal ummeed banta ja raha hai. EU AI Act ka Article 50, 2 August 2026 se, ye maangta hai ki logon se baat karne waale AI systems is tarah design hon ki logon ko pata chale ki wo AI se deal kar rahe hain, jab tak ye saaf na ho. EU users ko serve karne waali Indian companies ke liye ye maayne rakhta hai. India mein, Reserve Bank ki FREE-AI committee ki August 2025 ki report recommend karti hai ki financial firms customers ko batayein jab wo AI se deal kar rahe hon. Ye ek committee report hai, isliye ise binding rule maanne se pehle RBI ke maujooda directions check karo.

### Pehli galti ke liye design karo

Users jaldi tay kar lete hain ki kisi feature par bharosa karein ya nahi. Agar pehli galti aise tool se aati hai jo pakka dikhta tha, toh bharosa toot jaata hai aur kai log kabhi lautte nahi. Agar tool ne shuru se apni limits aur sources dikhaaye the, toh pehli galti ki keemat bahut kam hoti hai: user ne use pakad liya, aur product ne aisa karne mein uski madad ki.

### Planted errors ke saath test karo

Usability tests aamtaur par check karte hain ki users koi task poora kar paate hain ya nahi. AI features ke liye aise answers bhi rakho jo **jaanboojhkar galat** hon, aur dekho ki users dhyaan dete hain aur sambhalte hain ya nahi. Sabhi participants ke liye ek hi unit mein gino, taaki versions ki tulna ho sake.

```text
  Test plan for an AI answer screen
  Per participant: 8 questions, 6 correct AI answers, 2 planted errors
  4 participants:  8 planted errors in total
  Measure:         planted errors caught (out of 8), seconds to catch,
                   did they know what to do next?
```

Chaar logon ke saath aisa test badi design problems pakad leta hai. Ye kisi chhote sudhaar ko saabit nahi karta, isliye production numbers se confirm karo.

### Launch ke baad bharose ko measure karo

Calibration khisakta rehta hai. Jo log pehle hafte mein dhyaan se check karte the, wo chhathe hafte tak aankh moondkar rubber-stamp kar sakte hain. Teen numbers track karo, stakes ke hisaab se alag karke:

| Metric | Chinta waala pattern |
| --- | --- |
| High-stakes answers par citation open rate | Lagbhag zero, jabki accept rate bahut ooncha bana rahe: logon ne check karna chhod diya |
| Flag hue fields par edit ya override rate | Flag hue fields bina badle, kuch seconds mein accept: flags nazarandaaz ho rahe hain |
| Har 100 answers par wrong-answer reports | Ghat rahe hain jabki AI answers ke baare mein customer complaints badh rahi hain: logon ne report karna chhod diya, errors nahi ruke |

Inmein se koi bhi accuracy nahi hai. Inke saath answers ke ek random sample ka har hafte expert audit karo, taaki aapko asli error rate pata ho jisse bharose ko mel khaana chahiye.

## Ek worked example

Chennai ki ek insurance company apne agent app mein ek AI assistant jodti hai, taaki agents customers se baat karte samay policy coverage ke sawaalon ke jawaab de sakein. Neeche diye numbers samjhaane ke liye hain, upar bataaye gaye tarah ke ek chhote usability test se, production mein measure kiye gaye results nahi.

**Version one** ek confident paragraph answer dikhaata hai, jismein na sources hain, na limits, aur na problems report karne ka koi tareeka. Chaar agents mein se har ek ne 8 role-play sawaalon ke jawaab diye, jinmein se 2 mein planted galat answers the. Agents ne shaayad hi check kiya: **8 mein se 0** planted errors pakde gaye, aur sabhi 8 "customer" tak pahunch gaye.

**Version two**, jise product manager Divya ne redesign kiya:

| Badlaav | Agent ko ab kya dikhta hai | Iske peechhe ka signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Expectations | Header: "AI policy wordings se jawaab deta hai. Customers ko advise karne se pehle check karein." | Kisi ki zaroorat nahi |
| Check kiye hue sources | Har claim par ek citation; tap karne par clause khulta hai, mel khaata sentence highlight hota hai | Citation support check |
| Uncertainty | Un amounts aur exclusions par icon ke saath "Is amount ko check karein" jo clause mein nahi mile | Cited clause se number match |
| Saleeke se failure | "Mujhe saaf jawaab nahi mila" aur underwriting helpdesk ke liye ek button | Tune kiye gaye threshold se neeche retrieval score |
| Plan match | Answers sirf customer ke apne plan variant ki wording se | Plan code par rule check |
| Correction | Teen kaaranon waala ek "Wrong answer" button, har hafte review | User feedback |

Chaar alag agents ne wahi test liya. Unhone **8 mein se 7** planted errors pakde, aur unki satisfaction 5 mein se 3.1 se badhkar 4.2 ho gayi. Agents ne version two par **zyada** bharosa kiya, kam nahi, kyunki wo dekh paa rahe the ki har answer kyun diya gaya aur jab wo galat tha toh use theek kar paa rahe the.

Jo ek error chhoot gaya, wahi kaam ka hissa hai. Answer ne kaha ki ek procedure covered hai aur ek asli clause cite kiya jo sach mein yahi kehta tha, par wo plan ke premium variant se tha, customer ke basic variant se nahi. Citation check pass ho gaya kyunki text mel kha raha tha. Divya ne upar waala plan-match rule joda, isiliye wo table mein hai. Seekh: citation check saabit karta hai ki passage ye kehta hai, ye nahi ki passage yahaan laagu hota hai.

4 agents aur 8 planted errors waala test sudhaar ki disha dikhaata hai, uska size nahi. Ise jeet kehne se pehle, Divya ki team ne tay kiya ki pehle do mahinon mein production ko kya dikhaana chahiye: claim-amount answers par citation open rate zero se achha-khaasa upar bana rahe, flag hui amounts kam se kam kabhi-kabhi edit hon, aur wrong-answer reports ghatte samay AI answers se judi complaints na badhein.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Ye chhipaana ki ye AI hai.** Users jo jaante hain usi par bharosa calibrate karte hain, aur kuch markets mein labelling ab legal zimmedaari hai.
- **Bina kisi signal ke uncertainty badges.** Agar kisi ne tay nahi kiya ki flag kab dikhe, toh wo hamesha dikhta hai ya kabhi nahi.
- **Model ke apne confidence par bharosa karna.** Self-reported confidence aamtaur par ooncha hota hai, answer sahi ho ya galat; compute kiye hue checks use karo.
- **Bina check kiye citations.** Jo citation answer ko support nahi karta wo galat answer ko verified jaisa dikha deta hai.
- **Nakli sateekta.** "93.7% confident" galat bharose ko bulaava deta hai aur users ko action lene ke liye kuch nahi deta.
- **Sirf colour waale cues.** Akela laal border colour-blind users aur screen readers ke liye fail hota hai; ek icon aur shabd jodo.
- **Har jagah ek jaisa friction.** Low-stakes outputs par confirm steps logon ko high-stakes waalon par bhi click karke aage badhna sikha dete hain.
- **Aise outputs jinhe theek karna mushkil ho.** Final, edit na ho sakne waale outputs users ko galtiyaan maan lene par majboor karte hain.
- **Sirf success cases test karna.** Galat answers plant karo aur gino ki kitne users unhe pakadte hain.
- **Khaali failures.** Bina agle step ke "Kuch galat ho gaya" users ko atka deta hai.

## Practice

Neeche linked Microsoft ki Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction padho, aur explainability aur trust par PAIR ka chapter bhi.

1. **Audit.** Koi AI feature chuno jo aap use karte ho. Chaaron phases (shuruaat mein, interaction ke dauraan, jab galat ho, samay ke saath) mein se har ek ke liye, ek cheez note karo jo wo achha karta hai aur ek jagah jahaan wo fail hota hai.
2. **Signal dhoondho.** Apne redesign mein ek "please check" moment ke liye likho ki table ka kaun-sa signal use trigger karta hai, aur usse kya chhoot jaayega.
3. **Sketch.** Paper par ek redesigned screen ka sketch banao jismein expectations, ek check kiya hua source, ek field-level flag jo sirf colour par nirbhar na ho, aur ek tap waala correction ho. Uski stakes row mark karo aur batao ki friction kyun theek baithta hai.
4. **Test.** Apna sketch do logon ko ek sahi aur ek planted galat answer ke saath dikhao. Record karo ki wo kitne planted errors pakadte hain aur usmein kitna samay lagta hai.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** aisa design dikha sako jismein user ek planted galat answer ko kuch seconds mein pakadkar sambhal jaaye, aur us computed signal ka naam bata sako jiski wajah se screen ne use flag kiya.
