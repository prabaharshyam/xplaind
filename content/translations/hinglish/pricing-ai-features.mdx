## Ye kya hai

**AI feature ki pricing** ka matlab hai ye tay karna ki customers uske liye kaise pay karenge, aur check karna ki revenue uski costs ko achhe margin ke saath cover karta hai. Do facts ise aam software se alag banate hain:

- **Har use ki variable cost**: har request mein model tokens aur infrastructure ka paisa lagta hai. Heavy users light users se bahut zyada kharch karte hain.
- **Anishchit value**: customers abhi seekh rahe hain ki AI unke liye kitna worth hai.

| User | Mahine ki requests | Cost per request | Mahine ki cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 30 | ₹0.80 | ₹24 |
| Typical | 150 | ₹0.80 | ₹120 |
| Heavy | 2,000 | ₹0.80 | **₹1,600**: ₹999 subscription se zyada |

Har user ki monthly cost = requests x cost per request.

```flow
dir: LR
title: Flat price par ghata kyun ho sakta hai
Mahine ki requests x cost per request -> Us user ki cost
Us user ki cost -> ₹999 subscription se upar?
₹999 subscription se upar? -[haan, heavy user]-> Un par ghata
₹999 subscription se upar? -[nahi]-> Margin
```

Is lesson mein hum Bengaluru ki ek accounting SaaS company ke product manager Varun ke saath chalenge, jab woh invoice-reading feature ki pricing karta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Apni unit economics jaano.** Asli usage se cost per request aur har user ki monthly requests measure karo, light, typical aur heavy users ke liye. Cost per user = requests × cost per request. Use user ke diye hue paise se compare karo. Model costs waqt ke saath girti rahi hain, par features behtar hone par usage aksar zyada tezi se badhta hai.

**Pricing models.**

- **Bundled**: maujooda plan mein shaamil. Simple hai aur adoption badhata hai, par costs margin mein chhup jaati hain; fair-use limits chahiye.
- **Tiered**: ucha plans mein AI ya zyada AI shaamil ("Pro par mahine mein 100 AI summaries, Business par unlimited"). Common aur samajhne mein aasaan.
- **Add-on**: AI feature ke liye har user par alag fee. Value ka saaf signal; adoption kam.
- **Usage-based**: per document, per minute, per conversation ya per credit pay karo. Price ko cost se jodta hai; customers ke liye budget karna mushkil.
- **Outcome-based**: per resolved ticket, per qualified lead pay karo. Value se jodta hai; saaf measurement aur baanta hua risk chahiye.

Kai products inhe milakar use karte hain: included usage waala tier aur uske upar charges.

**Margins bachao.** Fair-use limits, model tiering (simple requests ke liye sasti models), caching, chhote outputs aur jo urgent nahi usse batch processing. Top 5% users par nazar rakho; woh aksar zyadatar cost chalate hain.

**Value par price karo, cost par nahi.** Cost floor tay karti hai. Value ceiling tay karti hai: bachaye hue ghante, kamaya hua revenue. Customers se value ke baare mein baat karo; sirf surveys se nahi, asli offers se prices test karo.

**Indian market ke notes.** Consumer aur chhote-business segments mein price sensitivity zyada hai; rupee pricing, UPI payments aur chhote pack sizes ("50 credits ke ₹199") madad karte hain. Enterprise clients usage bands ke saath predictable annual pricing pasand karte hain.

**Pricing models side by side:**

| Model | Example | Kiske liye achha | Kis baat se savdhaan |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bundled | Har plan mein AI shaamil | Adoption | Chhupi hui cost; fair-use limits chahiye |
| Tiered | Pro par mahine mein 100, Business par 2,000 | Saafpan, upgrades | Sahi included amounts chunna |
| Add-on | AI ke liye ₹299 per user per month | Saaf value signal | Kam adoption |
| Usage-based | ₹0.60 per invoice | Price cost ko follow karta hai | Customers ke liye budget karna mushkil |
| Outcome-based | Per resolved ticket | Price value ko follow karta hai | Sehmat measurement chahiye |

**Model cost ka badalna.** Per-token prices girte rahe hain, par behtar features ka matlab aksar zyada aur lambi requests hota hai. Unit economics ko har quarter asli usage se dobara compute karo, beta se nahi.

**Value ke baare mein customers se baat karna.** Price fix karne se pehle, Varun ki team ne das customers se poocha ki invoice entry mein unhe kitna time lagta hai aur kaun karta hai. Chhoti dukaanon ne kaha ki owner ek-do ghante per hafta lagata hai; accountants ne kaha ki unke staff ko har mahine poore din lagte hain. Value ka ye farak, cost ka farak nahi, accountants ke liye alag Business plan ko justify karta tha, jo khushi se zyada dete hain kyunki feature unka bahut zyada bachata hai.

**Browser mein try karo.** Alag-alag prices par AI feature ka margin nikaalo. Numbers apne se badlo.

```python
cost_per_use_inr = 0.60          # model, hosting and support per use; measure your own
uses_per_user_month = 120
for price in (49, 99, 149):
    cost = cost_per_use_inr * uses_per_user_month
    margin = (price - cost) / price
    print(f"₹{price}/month: cost ₹{cost:.0f}, margin {margin:.0%}")
```

## Ek example se samjho

Bengaluru ki ek SaaS company chhote businesses ko accounting software ₹999 per month mein bechti hai. Woh ek AI feature launch karti hai jo invoices padhta hai aur ledger entries suggest karta hai.

Beta mein measure kiya: average cost ₹0.80 per invoice. Light users mahine mein 30 invoices process karte hain (₹24), typical 150 (₹120), heavy 2,000 (₹1,600, poori subscription se zyada).

PM Varun ise free bundle karne ka sochta hai: average cost lagbhag ₹180 per customer hogi, margin bahut girega, aur heavy users ghate mein honge.

Woh ye chunta hai:

- ₹999 plan mein mahine ke 100 AI invoices included (adoption ke liye achha);
- uske upar 500 ke packs ₹299 mein;
- ₹2,499 ka Business plan jisme 2,000 included hain, un accountants ke liye jo kai clients sambhaalte hain.

Engineering saaf chhapi hui invoices ke liye chhota model aur photos aur haath ke likhe ke liye hi bada model use karke cost per invoice ₹0.45 tak le aati hai. Teen mahine baad, 62% customers feature use karte hain, 11% packs kharidte hain ya upgrade karte hain, aur feature ka gross margin har tier par positive hai.

| Customer type | Mahine ke invoices | ₹0.45 par cost | Deta hai | AI par margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 30 | ₹14 | ₹999 plan | Positive |
| Typical | 150 | ₹68 | ₹999 plan + ek pack (₹299) | Positive |
| Heavy (accountant) | 2,000 | ₹900 | ₹2,499 Business plan | Positive |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Flat price mein unlimited AI.** Heavy users aapko ghate mein daal sakte hain.
- **Cost per request ko ignore karna.** Prices set karne se pehle use measure karo.
- **Sirf cost par price karna.** Customers value ke liye pay karte hain; uska kuch hissa lo.
- **Complex credit systems.** Customers ko samajh aana chahiye ki woh kya khareed rahe hain.
- **Kabhi dobara na dekhna.** Costs aur usage badalte hain; pricing ko regularly review karo.

- **Competitor ki price copy karna.** Unki costs, customers aur value aapse alag hain.
