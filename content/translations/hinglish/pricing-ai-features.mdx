## Ye kya hai

**AI feature ki pricing** ka matlab hai tay karna ki customers uske liye kaise pay karenge, aur check karna ki revenue uski cost nikaalkar achha margin chhodta hai. Do baatein ise aam software se alag banaati hain:

- **Har istemaal par variable cost.** Aam software feature par ek aur customer ke click karne se lagbhag koi extra kharch nahi hota. AI feature har request par model tokens ka paisa deta hai. Heavy users ka kharch light users se kahin zyaada hota hai.
- **Value pakki nahi.** Customers abhi seekh rahe hain ki AI unke liye kitne ka hai, isliye copy karne ke liye koi tay "normal" price nahi hai.

Ye lesson Varun ki kahaani hai, jo Bengaluru ki ek accounting SaaS company mein product manager hai. Uski company chhote businesses ko ₹999 mahine ka plan (plus GST) bechti hai aur ek feature jod rahi hai jo invoices padhkar ledger entries suggest karta hai. Is lesson mein company ke saare numbers illustrative hain. Maan lo har invoice process karne mein ₹1.00 lagta hai; ye number kahaan se aata hai, aage dikhega.

| User | Mahine ke invoices | ₹1.00 prati invoice par cost | ₹999 ke plan se tulna |
|---|---|---|---|
| Light | 30 | ₹30 | Chhoti |
| Typical | 150 | ₹150 | Theek |
| Heavy (ek accountant) | 2,000 | **₹2,000** | Jitna pay karte hain uska doguna |

```flow
dir: LR
title: Flat price par ghaata kyun ho sakta hai
Mahine ke invoices × prati invoice cost -> Us user ki cost
Us user ki cost -> ₹999 plan price se upar?
₹999 plan price se upar? -[haan, heavy user]-> Un par ghaata hota hai
₹999 plan price se upar? -[nahi]-> Margin
```

Baaki lesson chaar sawaalon ka jawaab kram se deta hai: ek istemaal ki asli cost kya hai, kaun-sa pricing model fit hota hai, heavy users ko margin khaane se kaise rokein, aur customers kitna pay karenge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Step 1: ek request ki cost banao

Cost ka andaaza lagaane ke liye beta ki zaroorat nahi. Chaar numbers chahiye: andar jaane waale tokens, baahar aane waale tokens, prati token price aur exchange rate. Phir wo jodo jo model ka bill nahi dikhaata.

```flow
title: Cost per request banaana
Input tokens × input price -> Dollars mein model cost
Dikhne wala output aur thinking tokens × output price -> Dollars mein model cost
Dollars mein model cost -[× exchange rate]-> Rupees mein model cost
Rupees mein model cost -[× retry factor]-> Retries ke saath cost
Retries ke saath cost -[+ storage, logs, hosting]-> Cost per request
```

28 November 2026 tak, Anthropic ye API prices US dollars mein prati million tokens list karta hai (Haiku prices 100,000 tokens tak ke prompts par laagu hain):

| Model | Input | Output | Kiske liye achha |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claude Haiku 5.5 | $0.10 | $0.50 | Saaf, simple, high-volume tasks |
| Claude Sonnet 5.5 | $2 | $10 | Mushkil inputs, jaise photos aur handwriting |

Doosre providers bhi yahi structure (alag input aur output prices) alag numbers ke saath use karte hain. Unka current price page padho aur jo bhi number use karo uske saath check date likho.

Sonnet 5.5 par ek invoice ke liye Varun ka estimate (token counts illustrative hain; apne 50 asli invoices par khud naapo):

- Input: 1,500 tokens (instructions aur invoice image) × $2 prati million = $0.003.
- Output: 200 dikhne waale tokens (ledger entries) plus 400 thinking tokens = 600 × $10 prati million = $0.006.
- Model cost: $0.009. ₹94 prati dollar maanein to ye ₹0.85 hai.
- Retries: maan lo 10 mein se 1 invoice format check mein fail hokar dobaara chalta hai, to 1.1 se guna karo: ₹0.93.
- Baaki per-use costs, jaise storage, logs aur har invoice par banta hosting: maan lo ₹0.07.
- **Cost per invoice: lagbhag ₹1.00.**

### Step 2: chhupe hue thinking tokens par nazar rakho

Aaj ke zyaadatar models jawaab dene se pehle "sochte" hain. Un thinking tokens ka bill output ke roop mein, poore output price par banta hai, chahe user aur aapki app unhe kabhi na dekhein. Anthropic ke documentation ke mutaabik response mein sirf summary dikhe tab bhi aap poori thinking ke paise dete ho.

Beta bill se PM ke chaunkne ki sabse aam wajah yahi hai. Varun ke pehle beta hafte mein model apne default effort level par chala, jo Anthropic ke API par Sonnet 5.5 ke liye `high` hai. 200 dikhne waale tokens banaane ke liye usne har invoice par lagbhag 1,600 tokens socha. Output line 1,800 × $10 prati million = $0.018 ho gayi, aur har invoice ki poori cost badhkar lagbhag **₹2.24** ho gayi, estimate se doguna se bhi zyaada. Extraction ke liye kam effort level set karke, phir invoices ke ek test set par accuracy check karne se thinking waapas lagbhag 400 tokens par aur cost ₹1.00 par aa gayi.

Isse do rules nikalte hain. Thinking tokens alag se log karo (Anthropic unhe har response ke usage data mein report karta hai, aur OpenAI reasoning tokens ko aise hi ek field mein report karta hai). Aur maximum output length ko kabhi dikhne waale reply ke size par cap mat karo: thinking bhi usi limit mein gini jaati hai, isliye tight cap jawaab kaat deta hai aur retry aur mehenga padta hai.

### Step 3: GST, gateway fees aur rupee ka hisaab rakho

Teen India-specific cheezein chupchaap margin khiska deti hain.

- **GST.** India mein software subscriptions par 18% GST lagta hai. Agar aap "₹999 + GST" advertise karte ho, to customer ₹1,178.82 deta hai aur aapke paas ₹999 rehte hain. Agar "₹999 GST samet" advertise karte ho, to aapke paas sirf 999 ÷ 1.18 = ₹846.61 rehte hain, jitna aapne shaayad socha tha usse lagbhag 15% kam. Margin hamesha GST ke bina waale price par nikaalo, aur pricing page par saaf likho ki aapka matlab kaun-sa hai. GST mein registered business buyers aamtaur par GST waapas claim kar sakte hain; consumers nahi kar sakte, isliye consumer apps mein buyer ke liye inclusive price hi maayne rakhta hai.
- **Payment gateway fees.** Razorpay ka standard domestic rate har payment ka 2% plus us fee par GST hai (28 November 2026 ko check kiya). ₹1,178.82 ke payment par 2% lagbhag ₹24 hai, plus us par lagbhag ₹4 GST. Registered business aamtaur par fee par laga GST waapas claim kar sakta hai.
- **Exchange rate.** Zyaadatar model APIs US dollars mein bill karte hain. Mid-November 2025 mein rupee lagbhag ₹88.7 prati dollar tha aur mid-November 2026 mein lagbhag ₹93.5 se ₹94, May 2026 mein lagbhag ₹96 chhoone ke baad. Sirf isi badlaav ne ek saal mein wahi dollar bill rupees mein lagbhag 6% mehenga kar diya: $10,000 ka monthly bill lagbhag ₹8.9 lakh se ₹9.4 lakh ho gaya. Buffer rakhkar price karo, aur rupee hile to dobaara hisaab lagaao. Jab GST-registered Indian company koi foreign API kharidti hai, to aamtaur par reverse charge mein GST khud bharti hai aur waapas claim karti hai; details apne accountant se check karo.

### Step 4: pricing model chuno

| Model | Example (date na ho to illustrative) | Kiske liye achha | Kis baat ka dhyaan rakhein |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bundled | Har plan mein AI included | Adoption | Chhupi cost; fair-use limits chahiye |
| Tiered | Pro par mahine ke 200, Business par 2,500 | Saaf samajh, upgrades | Included maatra tay karna |
| Add-on | AI ke liye ₹299 prati user prati mahina | Saaf value signal | Kam adoption |
| Usage-based | ₹1.50 prati invoice | Price cost ke saath chalta hai | Customers ko budget banaana mushkil lagta hai |
| Outcome-based | Intercom Fin: $0.99 prati resolution (28 November 2026 ko check kiya) | Price value ke saath chalta hai | Success ki ek maani hui definition chahiye |

Zyaadatar products inhe milaate hain: included usage wala ek tier aur uske upar paid packs. Ye hybrid zyaadatar customers ke liye bill predictable rakhta hai aur phir bhi sabse heavy users se charge karta hai.

Outcome-based pricing aakarshak hai, par bahut kuch maangti hai. Intercom billable Fin resolution us conversation ko maanta hai jismein Fin ke aakhri jawaab ke baad customer aur koi madad nahi maangta. Dekho iska asar kya hota hai: har fail hui koshish ki cost vendor uthaata hai, isliye ye tabhi chalta hai jab aapka success rate ooncha aur stable ho. Ise chunne se pehle "outcome" ki aisi sateek definition likho jise aap aur customer dono logs se gin sakein.

### Step 5: aise credits design karo jin par customers bharosa karein

Agar aap packs ya credits bechte ho, to teen cheezein pehle hi tay karo.

- **Expiry.** Jo credits bina warning ke mahine ke aakhir mein expire ho jaate hain, wo dhokhe jaise lagte hain aur naaraaz support tickets banaate hain. 12 mahine valid packs behtar hain, ya bacha hua included usage ek mahine ke liye roll over karo.
- **Visibility.** App mein usage meter dikhao, aur included use ke 80% aur 100% par email aur product dono mein warn karo.
- **Breakage.** Breakage wo revenue hai jo aise credits se aata hai jinka paisa diya gaya par kabhi use nahi hue. Apna margin is par mat banao. Jin customers ko pata chalta hai ki wo unused credits ka paisa de rahe hain, wo chhod dete hain.

### Step 6: margin bachao

- **Per-account rate limits.** Har account par daily cap (jaise, ek normal heavy din ka 3 guna) kisi script ya abuse hue free trial ko ek raat mein mahine ka budget jalaane se rokta hai.
- **Model tiering.** Saaf, simple requests chhote model par bhejo aur sirf mushkil waali bade model par.
- **Caching.** Har request par cached prompt prefix (fixed instructions) ka poora price dene ke bajaay use reuse karo.
- Jo kaam urgent nahi hai uske liye **chhote outputs aur batch processing**.
- **Top 5% users par nazar rakho.** Neeche Varun ke mix mein 5% customers 55% cost banaate hain.

### Step 7: cost par nahi, value par price karo

Cost floor tay karti hai. Value ceiling tay karti hai: bache hue ghante, tali hui galtiyaan, badha hua revenue. Price fix karne se pehle Varun ki team ne das customers se poochha ki invoice entry mein kitna time lagta hai aur kaun karta hai. Chhoti dukaanon ne kaha hafte mein ek-do ghante, owner khud karta hai. Accountants ne kaha har mahine staff ke poore-poore din. Cost ka farq nahi, value ka yahi farq accountants ke liye alag Business plan ko sahi thehraata hai. Prices ko sirf surveys se nahi, asli offers (ek pricing page, ek sales quote) se test karo: log survey mein ek baat kehte hain aur pay kisi aur cheez ke liye karte hain.

Costs aur usage dono badalte hain. Token prices aamtaur par girte aaye hain, par behtar features zyaada aur lambi requests laate hain, aur rupee bhi hilta hai. Unit economics har quarter asli usage se dobaara nikaalo, beta se nahi.

**Apne browser mein try karo.** Ye tokens se cost per invoice aur har customer type ke liye bacha margin nikaalta hai. Numbers apne hisaab se badlo.

```python
# Illustrative token counts; prices are Anthropic list prices, USD per million tokens, checked 28 November 2026
INR_PER_USD = 94          # assumption; check today's rate
RETRY = 1.1               # 1 in 10 requests runs twice
OTHER_INR = 0.07          # storage, logs, hosting per request

def cost_per_invoice(tokens_in, visible_out, thinking, price_in, price_out):
    usd = tokens_in * price_in / 1e6 + (visible_out + thinking) * price_out / 1e6
    return usd * INR_PER_USD * RETRY + OTHER_INR

print(f"Sonnet 5.5, high effort: ₹{cost_per_invoice(1500, 200, 1600, 2, 10):.2f}")
print(f"Sonnet 5.5, low effort:  ₹{cost_per_invoice(1500, 200, 400, 2, 10):.2f}")
print(f"Haiku 5.5:               ₹{cost_per_invoice(1500, 200, 400, 0.10, 0.50):.2f}")

cost = 0.45               # blended cost after tiering, from the worked example
for name, invoices, pays in [("Light", 30, 999), ("Typical", 150, 999), ("Heavy", 2000, 2499)]:
    ai_cost = invoices * cost
    print(f"{name}: pays ₹{pays} before GST, AI cost ₹{ai_cost:.2f}, left ₹{pays - ai_cost:.2f}")
```

## Ek example se samjho

Varun ke paas beta cost hai: Sonnet 5.5 par low effort mein ₹1.00 prati invoice. Ab wo price tay karta hai.

**Average problem chhupa deta hai.** Maan lo customer base 50% light (mahine ke 30 invoices), 45% typical (150) aur 5% heavy (2,000) hai. Weighted average 0.5 × 30 + 0.45 × 150 + 0.05 × 2,000 = 182.5 invoices hai, yaani har customer par mahine ka lagbhag ₹183. ₹999 ke plan par ye affordable lagta hai. Par un 182.5 invoices mein se 100 heavy 5% ke hain, jo kul cost ka 55% hai, aur unmein se har ek ₹999 dekar ₹2,000 ki cost karwaata hai. Unlimited AI bundle karne par har accountant par mahine ka lagbhag ₹1,000 ka ghaata hoga.

**Wo ek hybrid chunta hai** (saare prices GST ke bina):

- **Pro, ₹999:** mahine ke 200 AI invoices included, jo light aur typical users ko cover karte hain aur adoption badhaate hain.
- **Packs:** 500 aur invoices ₹399 mein, 12 mahine valid, usage meter aur 80% par alert ke saath.
- **Business, ₹2,499:** 2,500 invoices included, plus accountants ke liye multi-client features.

Pack ka price soch-samajhkar rakha gaya hai. Pro par jis accountant ko 2,000 invoices chahiye, use chaar packs kharidne honge: ₹999 + 4 × ₹399 = ₹2,595. Business sasta hai aur zyaada deta hai, isliye heavy users ghaate waale plan par baithe rehne ke bajaay ek tier upar chale jaate hain.

Ye prices tabhi chalte hain jab cost per invoice kam ho. ₹1.00 par ek poora pack serve karne mein ₹399 ke saamne ₹500 lagte hain, aur 2,500 ki cap par Business customer ₹2,499 ke saamne ₹2,500 ki cost karwaata hai. Isliye Varun ₹0.50 ka cost target rakhta hai aur naye plans live hone se pehle model tiering ship karta hai.

**Engineering cost ghataati hai.** Maan lo 70% invoices saaf, printed PDFs hain. Ye Haiku 5.5 par jaate hain, har ek ki cost lagbhag ₹0.12 (wahi token counts, prati token lagbhag 20 guna sasta, plus wahi ₹0.07 ki baaki costs). Baaki 30%, photos aur handwriting, ₹1.00 par Sonnet 5.5 par hi rehte hain. Ek check Haiku ki galtiyaan pakadta hai, aur maan lo Haiku waale 10 mein se 1 invoice phir Sonnet 5.5 par ₹1.00 extra mein dobaara chalta hai. Blend:

- Haiku share: 0.70 × ₹0.12 = ₹0.08
- Sonnet share: 0.30 × ₹1.00 = ₹0.30
- Escalations: 70% ka dasvaan hissa, yaani saare invoices ka 7%, × ₹1.00 = ₹0.07
- **Blended cost: lagbhag ₹0.45 prati invoice**

| Customer type | Mahine ke invoices | Plan | Pay karte hain (GST se pehle) | ₹1.00 par AI cost | ₹0.45 par AI cost | ₹0.45 ki AI cost ke baad bacha |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Light | 30 | Pro | ₹999 | ₹30 | ₹13.50 | ₹985.50 |
| Typical | 150 | Pro | ₹999 | ₹150 | ₹67.50 | ₹931.50 |
| Heavy (accountant) | 2,000 | Business | ₹2,499 | ₹2,000 | ₹900 | ₹1,599 |

"Bacha" column profit nahi hai: isi se baaki product, support aur sales ka kharch nikalna hai, aur lagbhag 2% payment gateway ko jaata hai. ₹1.00 par heavy accountant sirf ₹499 chhodta; ₹0.45 par wo ₹1,599 chhodta hai, aur ek poora pack (₹399 ke saamne ₹225 ki cost) bhi profitable hai. Quarterly review ke liye Varun teen alerts set karta hai: cost per invoice ₹0.60 se upar, rupee ₹100 prati dollar se upar, aur koi bhi account jo apne plan ke included invoices ka 3 guna se zyaada use kare.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Flat price mein unlimited AI.** Sabse heavy 5% users aadhi se zyaada cost bana sakte hain aur jitni unki cost hai usse kam pay karte hain.
- **Dikhne waale reply ki length se pricing.** Thinking tokens output mein bill hote hain. Prices set karne se pehle unhe naapo, aur har task ke liye effort level set karo.
- **GST-inclusive price par margin nikaalna.** Uska 18% kabhi aapka nahi hota, aur gateway lagbhag 2% aur le leta hai.
- **Exchange rate ko nazarandaaz karna.** Dollar mein bill hone wala model rupee kamzor hone par mehenga ho jaata hai, usage mein bina kisi badlaav ke.
- **Beta usage se pricing.** Beta users utsaahi aur gine-chune hote hain. Launch ke baad usage, request length aur user mix badal jaate hain.
- **Per-account rate limit na hona.** Ek script ya abuse hua free trial ek raat mein mahine ka budget jala sakta hai.
- **Chupchaap expire hone waale credits.** Customers thaga hua mehsoos karte hain aur chhod dete hain. Meter dikhao, pehle se warn karo aur rollover do.
- **Sirf cost par pricing.** Customers value ke liye pay karte hain; uska kuch hissa capture karo.
- **Competitor ka price copy karna.** Unki costs, customers aur value aapse alag hain.
