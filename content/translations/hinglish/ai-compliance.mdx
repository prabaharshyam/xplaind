## Ye kya hai

Indian AI deployments mein chaar regimes sabse zyada aate hain:

- **India ka Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)**, jiske Rules 14 November 2025 ko notify hue aur obligations athaarah mahine mein charno mein lagu hoti hain. Ye India mein logon ke digital personal data ko govern karta hai.
- **EU ka General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, jo tab lagu hota hai jab aap EU mein logon ka personal data handle karte ho, jaise European customers ko serve karne waali Indian company.
- **SOC 2**, koi kanoon nahi, balki ek service provider ke security controls par independent audit report. Enterprise buyers, khaaskar US mein, aksar vendors se ye maangte hain.
- **HIPAA**, US ka health privacy law, jo un Indian firms tak pahunchta hai jo US healthcare clients ke liye US patients ki health information process karti hain.

EU AI Act, NIST AI Risk Management Framework aur ISO/IEC 42001 jaise AI-specific frameworks risk, transparency aur oversight ke baare mein duties jodte hain; doosre paths ke governance lessons unhein cover karte hain.

Ye lesson ek kaam karte engineer ka map hai, legal advice nahi. Hamesha client ki legal aur privacy team ko shaamil karo.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**DPDP Act ki zaroori baatein.** Jo organisation tay karti hai ki data kyun aur kaise process hoga, woh **data fiduciary** hai; uski taraf se process karne waale vendors **data processors** hain. Processing ke liye ek lawful ground chahiye, mukhya taur par consent jo free, specific, informed aur unambiguous ho, ek saaf notice ke baad diya gaya ho, aur waapas lena utna hi aasaan ho jitna dena; Act kuch legitimate uses bhi list karta hai. Data sirf bataye gaye purpose ke liye use ho sakta hai aur jab zaroorat na rahe to erase hona chahiye. Logon ke paas apna data access, correct aur erase karne ke rights hain aur ek grievance process ka bhi. Fiduciaries ko reasonable security safeguards lene hote hain aur personal data breaches ko Data Protection Board aur prabhavit logon ko report karna hota hai. Bachchon ke data ke liye verifiable parental consent chahiye aur use tracking ya targeted advertising ke liye use nahi kiya ja sakta. Bade ya sensitive fiduciaries ko significant data fiduciaries ke roop mein classify kiya ja sakta hai, audits aur impact assessments jaisi extra duties ke saath. Data ki suraksha mein fail hone par penalties ₹250 crore tak jaati hain.

**GDPR ki zaroori baatein.** Milte-julte vichar, zyada detail ke saath: chhe lawful bases, purpose limitation, data minimisation, access aur erasure jaise rights, high-risk processing ke liye data protection impact assessments, EU se bahar transfers ke rules, aur global turnover ka 4% tak fines.

**SOC 2.** Ek auditor provider ke controls ko security, aur optional taur par availability, confidentiality, processing integrity aur privacy ke criteria ke khilaf test karta hai. Type I report ek point in time par design check karti hai; Type II report check karti hai ki controls ek period mein, aam taur par kai mahine, kaam kiye. Clients poochte hain ki kya aapke paas, aur aapke stack ke har vendor ke paas, jaise model provider, ye hai.

**HIPAA.** Covered entities (providers, insurers) aur unke business associates ke paas rakhi health information ki raksha karta hai. Vendor ko aisa data chhoone se pehle business associate agreement par sign karna hota hai, aur sirf minimum zaroori hi use karna hota hai.

**Design ke liye iska kya matlab hai.**

- Personal data ke har flow ko map karo: source, purpose, kahan store, kaunse vendors dekhte hain, kitne din rehta hai.
- Minimise karo: models ko sirf task ke liye zaroori fields bhejo; baaki mask karo.
- Model vendors ke terms check karo: kya data training mein use hota hai, store hota hai, aur kahan?
- Retention aur deletion: har store ke liye periods set karo, aur erasure requests ko index, logs aur backups tak pahunchao.
- Access control, audit logs aur breach response plans.

**Regimes saath-saath:**

| Regime | Ye kya hai | Kispar bandhan | Typical AI design asar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DPDP Act | Indian data protection kanoon | Indian personal data ke fiduciaries aur processors | Notice aur consent, purpose limits, erasure, breach reporting |
| GDPR | EU data protection kanoon | EU residents ka data handle karne waala koi bhi | Lawful basis, impact assessments, transfer rules |
| SOC 2 | Independent controls audit report | Woh service providers jinke buyers ise maangte hain | Access control, logging, change management, vendor reviews |
| HIPAA | US health privacy kanoon | Covered entities aur business associates | BAA, minimum necessary data, HIPAA-eligible services aur tay hua hosting region |

**Privacy officer se baat karna.** Raay nahi, data flow map lekar jaao. Har flow ke liye batao ki kaunsa data move hota hai, kyun, kahan store hota hai, kitne din, kaunsa vendor dekhta hai aur kaise delete hota hai. Privacy officers har baar wahi chand sawaal poochte hain: purpose, lawful ground, minimisation, retention, security, rights aur breach handling. Likhe hue jawab ek mahine ke emails ko do ghante ki meeting bana dete hain.

**Aisa data flow map jise check kiya ja sake.** Har flow ko data ki tarah likho, aur ek script ko wo gaps dhoondhne do jo privacy officer dhoondhta:

```python
FLOWS = [
    {"name": "chat messages to model", "data": ["message text"], "purpose": "answer the question",
     "lawful_ground": "consent", "store": "none at vendor (zero retention)", "vendor": "model provider",
     "retention_days": 0, "region": "check vendor terms"},
    {"name": "conversation logs", "data": ["masked message text", "user id"], "purpose": "quality review",
     "lawful_ground": "consent", "store": "Postgres, Mumbai", "vendor": None, "retention_days": 30},
    {"name": "search index", "data": ["policy documents"], "purpose": "retrieval",
     "lawful_ground": "not personal data", "store": "vector DB, Mumbai", "vendor": "hosted DB",
     "retention_days": None},
]
REQUIRED = ["purpose", "lawful_ground", "store", "retention_days"]

def gaps(flows):
    out = []
    for f in flows:
        out += [f"{f['name']}: missing {k}" for k in REQUIRED if k not in f]
        if f.get("lawful_ground") != "not personal data" and f.get("retention_days") is None:
            out.append(f"{f['name']}: personal data with no retention period")
        if f.get("vendor") and "region" not in f and "Mumbai" not in str(f.get("store")):
            out.append(f"{f['name']}: vendor region not recorded")
    return out

print(gaps(FLOWS) or "no gaps")
```

Map ko repository mein rakho, har naye feature ke saath review karo, aur privacy officer ko printed version do.

**Code mein retention aur erasure.** Har system ko do jobs chahiye:

```python
def nightly_retention(db):
    db.execute("DELETE FROM conversation_logs WHERE created_at < now() - interval '30 days'")
    db.execute("DELETE FROM uploads WHERE created_at < now() - interval '90 days'")

def erase_user(user_id, db, index, cache):
    db.execute("DELETE FROM conversation_logs WHERE user_id = %s", (user_id,))
    db.execute("DELETE FROM memories WHERE user_id = %s", (user_id,))
    index.delete(filter={"owner": user_id})          # documents the user uploaded
    cache.delete_pattern(f"user:{user_id}:*")
    db.execute("INSERT INTO erasure_log (user_id_hash, at) VALUES (sha256(%s::bytea), now())", (user_id,))
```

Erasure log record karta hai ki request poori hui, bina pehchaan saaf rakhe. Backups apne cycle par expire hote hain; ye privacy notice mein likho.

**DPDP Rules.** Act ke Rules 2025 mein notify hue, duties charno mein lagu hoti hain. Ye notices, consent managers, security safeguards, breach reporting timelines aur bachchon ke data par aur detail jodte hain. Launch se pehle maujooda dates aur text check karo.

**Agar aap EU ko serve karte ho.** GDPR ke alawa, EU AI Act AI systems ko risk ke hisaab se baantta hai. Zyadatar chat aur content tools par transparency duties hain (logon ko batao ki woh AI se baat kar rahe hain, generated content ko label karo), jabki hiring, credit scoring ya education assessment jaise high-risk uses par bhaari duties hain: risk management, data governance, human oversight, logging aur documentation. Iski obligations charno mein lagu hoti hain; check karo ki aapki launch date par kaunsi lagu hain.

**Vendor terms badalte hain.** Model providers data-use aur retention terms update karte hain. Aapne jo terms review kiye, unka version aur date data flow map mein record karo, aur saal mein kam se kam ek baar aur har change notice par dobara check karo.

## Ek worked example

Pune ki ek health-tech company ek AI scribe banati hai jo doctor-patient conversations se clinical notes draft karta hai, India ke clinics aur ek US hospital network ke liye.

Privacy review ek data flow map banata hai: clinic se audio, transcript, draft note, doctor ki edits, clinic ke records mein final note, logs.

India ke liye (DPDP): clinic data fiduciary hai aur company ek contract ke tahat data processor. Patients ko registration par notice milta hai aur woh consent dete hain, waapas lene ke saade tareeke ke saath. Note sign hone ke 7 din baad audio delete hota hai. Erasure requests transcripts aur drafts delete karti hain.

US hospital ke liye (HIPAA): ek business associate agreement sign hota hai, US patient data US cloud region mein rehta hai, aur model provider sirf apne HIPAA-eligible terms ke tahat use hota hai.

Buyers ke liye aam taur par: company ek SOC 2 Type II audit shuru karti hai, aur model provider ki SOC 2 report apni vendor file mein rakhti hai. Logs mein names aur identifiers mask hote hain. Design review mein teen hafte lage, aur deal isliye band hui kyunki har sawaal ka documented jawab tha.

## Aam galtiyan

- **Bina check kiye "hum data store nahi karte".** Logs, traces, caches, backups aur vendors sab data store karte hain.
- **Vendor terms ko ignore karna.** Model provider ka retention aur training terms aapki compliance ka hissa hain.
- **Kaam aayega is soch mein sab kuch collect karna.** Purpose limitation aur minimisation mana karte hain.
- **Derived data ke through erasure path nahi.** Source document delete karna lekin uske chunks, embeddings aur logs nahi, erasure nahi hai.
- **Engineers ka legal raay dena.** System ko precisely describe karo; kanoon ka matlab lawyers ko nikalne do.
- **Erasure jo index, logs ya caches miss kar de.** Har store ko map karo.
- **Data flow map jise koi update nahi karta.** Use repository mein rakho aur check karo.
- **Maan lena ki vendor ke terms kabhi nahi badalte.** Jis din check kiya woh date record karo.

## Jab compliance kaam halka ho

- **Bilkul personal data nahi**, jaise public government circulars par ek bot: accuracy aur security par dhyaan do.
- **Synthetic data waala prototype** jo asli users ko kabhi nahi chhoota.
- **Non-personal company data par internal tools**, jahan contracts aur security policy phir bhi lagu hain lekin privacy law zyadatar nahi.

Asli users ke names, contact details, messages, health, paise ya bachchon ke data waali kisi bhi cheez ko poore process ki zaroorat waali maano.

## Demo se production tak
