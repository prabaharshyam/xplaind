## Ye kya hai

AI ke jawaab ko check karne ka matlab hai **jin hisson par aap bharosa karoge, unhe amal karne se pehle confirm karna**. Iska matlab sab par barabar shak karna nahi hai. Birthday message ke liye ek baar padh lena kaafi hai. Diabetes waale maata-pita ki dawa, koi legal deadline ya kisi customer ka refund amount, inke liye saboot chahiye.

Ye rahi paanch steps ki ek routine, jo aap man mein hi chala sakte ho:

```flow
1. Daanv | is par kaun bharosa kar raha hai, aur galat hua toh kya? -> 2. Daave | wo naam, numbers, dates, rules aur quotes jin par aap amal karoge
2. Daave -> 3. Verify | ek maujooda, bharosemand source; numbers dobaara calculate karo
3. Verify -> 4. Reasoning | neutral tareeke se poochho; dobaara poochhne par bhi tikta hai?
4. Reasoning -> 5. Escalate | daanv ooncha hai, ya sources asehmat hain? Kisi insaan se poochho
```

Is routine ka maqsad raftaar hai. Zyaadatar jawaabon ke liye sirf step 1 aur 2 kaafi hain. Kadi jaanch aap tabhi karte ho jab baat maayne rakhti ho.

Ye lesson kisi bhi ek jawaab ke liye aam routine hai. "AI for research and analysis" isi par aage badhkar lambe research kaamon ke liye hai, jaise kisi daave ko kai sources mein trace karna. Yahaan hum Suresh ke saath chalenge, jo Coimbatore mein apne diabetic pita ki dekhbhaal karte hain, aur assistant se do bahut alag sawaal poochhte hain.

## Ye kaise kaam karta hai

### AI ke jawaab galat kyun hote hain

Galtiyaan kahaan se aati hain, ye pata ho toh aap behtar check karte ho. "What AI cannot do" seemaaon ko poora samjhaata hai. Checking ke liye chaar kaaran sabse zyada maayne rakhte hain:

| Kaaran | Ye kaisa dikhta hai | Ise pakadne waala check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ye sambhaavit text predict karta hai.** Ye wo likhta hai jo aam taur par aage aata hai, wo nahi jo isne dhoondhkar dekha ho. | Ek smooth, confident jawaab jismein koi gadhi hui detail ho, yaani ek **hallucination** | Har aham daave ko kisi asli source mein dhoondho |
| **Andaaza lagaane ko reward milta hai.** September 2025 ke ek paper mein OpenAI ke researchers ne tark diya ki training aur scoring ke aam tareeke "mujhe nahi pata" ki bajaay andaaze ko reward karte hain. | Ye shaayad hi kabhi "mujhe pakka nahi pata" kehta hai, tab bhi jab ise kehna chahiye | Poochho "Tum kitne confident ho, aur tumhe kya jaanne ki zaroorat hogi?", phir bhi check zaroor karo |
| **Iska knowledge ek taareekh par ruk jaata hai.** Model ek **knowledge cutoff** tak ke data se seekhta hai. | Koi puraana rule, rate ya deadline jise maujooda bataaya jaaye | Source par taareekh dhoondho |
| **Ye aapse sehmat hone ki taraf jhukta hai.** Ise **sycophancy** kehte hain. | Kisi galat idea par "haan, aap sahi ho" | Neutral tareeke se poochho, aur ulta paksh bhi maango |

Sycophancy par: 2023 ki ek study mein Anthropic ke researchers ne paaya ki paanch bade AI assistants aksar users ko wahi bataate the jo wo sunna chaahte lagte the. Unhone ise kuch had tak insaanon ki ratings par training se joda: log un jawaabon ko zyaada rating dete hain jo unse sehmat hon, aisa rujhaan hai. Isliye aap jaise poochhte ho, usi se tay hota hai ki aapko kya milta hai.

### Step 1: Daanv tay karo

Teen chhote sawaal poochho. Is jawaab par kaun bharosa karega? Galat hua toh kya hoga? Kya us kaam ko palta ja sakta hai? Galat slogan se koi nuksaan nahi. Chhooti hui scholarship deadline baad mein theek nahi ho sakti.

| Daanv | Udaaharan | Kitna check karna hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kam** | Ideas, naam, first drafts, brainstorming | Sarsari nazar daalo aur edit karo; faisla aapka hai |
| **Madhyam** | Work emails, reports, customer replies, assignments | Jo bhi fact aur figure aap daalte ho, har ek check karo |
| **Ooncha** | Health, paisa, kaanoon, safety, kuch bhi jo public ho ya palta na ja sake | Har daave ko official sources ya kisi qualified insaan se verify karo |

Ek kaam ka rule: agar aapke alaawa koi aur is jawaab par amal karega, toh daanv kam se kam madhyam hai.

### Step 2: Jin daavon par aap amal karoge, unhe chuno

Har **number, date, naam, rule aur quotation** ko underline karo. Galtiyaan yahin chhipi hoti hain, aur check karne mein sabse aasaan bhi yahi hain. "Ye scheme un students ki madad karti hai jinki family income ₹2.5 lakh se kam hai, aur deadline 30 November hai" jaise ek vaakya mein do daave hain, jinhe aap ek minute mein verify kar sakte ho.

Phir poochho ki kaun-se daave **load-bearing** hain: agar ye galat hota, toh kya aap kuch alag karte? Unhe pehle check karo. Background paragraph mein galat taareekh, galat deadline se kam maayne rakhti hai.

Aakhir mein, dekho ki kya **chhoot gaya** hai. Koi jawaab har vaakya mein sahi hokar bhi wo warning chhod sakta hai jo aapke liye aham hai. Jo vaakya hai hi nahi, use aap fact-check nahi kar sakte. Isliye seedhe poochho, jaise "Diabetes waale kisi insaan par kaun-si warnings ya exceptions laagu hoti hain?", aur original ka warnings waala hissa khud padho.

### Step 3: Maujooda, bharosemand source se verify karo

Achhe sources, bharose ke mote-mote kram mein:

1. **Original:** kaanoon, circular, paper, product label.
2. **Official sites:** sarkaari vibhaag, RBI ya SEBI jaise regulators, company ke apne pages.
3. **Bharosemand publishers:** jaane-maane news organisations aur reference works.

**Lateral reading** karo, jo professional fact-checkers ki aadat hai: page chhodo, naye tabs kholo, aur dekho ki independent sources us daave ya website ke baare mein kya kehte hain. Kisi page ko is se mat parkho ki wo kitna professional dikhta hai. Yaad rakho ki ek hi number dohraane waale paanch pages bhi ek hi source hain.

**Agar jawaab mein links hain, toh unhe kholo**, aur chaar sawaal poochho:

- Kya wo page maujood hai?
- Kya ye aisa source hai jis par aap apne aap mein bharosa karoge?
- Kya ye theek wahi kehta hai jo jawaab daava karta hai? Kisi asli, official page ko aise vaakya ke liye cite kiya ja sakta hai jo usmein hai hi nahi.
- Kya ye current hai? Official sites puraane notices online rakhti hain. Pichhle saal ka scholarship notice asli hai, official hai, aur aapke liye galat hai.

"AI for research and analysis" dikhaata hai ki assistant se supporting vaakya quote kaise karwaayein, aur kisi daave ko uske original tak kaise trace karein.

### Aam daavon ke liye do minute ka check

Agar pata ho ki kahaan dekhna hai, toh zyaadatar rozmarra ke daave jaldi check ho jaate hain:

| Daave ka prakaar | India mein kahaan check karein | Samay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sarkaari scheme ke rules aur deadlines | Scheme ka official portal ya vibhaag ki site (aksar gov.in ya nic.in par khatam hoti hai) | 2 minute |
| Bank, loan ya UPI rules | RBI ki site, UPI ke liye NPCI, ya aapke bank ki apni website | 2 minute |
| Train ya travel rules | IRCTC ya Indian Railways ke official pages | 1 minute |
| Koi dawa | Label aur saath aaya leaflet, phir pharmacist ya doctor | 5 minute |
| Kisi mashhoor insaan ka quote | Theek wahi shabd quotes mein search karo; kisi bharosemand original ko dhoondho | 2 minute |
| Koi viral news daava | Bharosemand news sites, aur sarkaar se jude daavon ke liye PIB Fact Check | 3 minute |
| Kisi document ya paper mein koi number | Original document: usmein wo figure search karo | 1 minute |

Agar kuch minuton mein aapko wo daava kisi bharosemand jagah nahi milta, toh use unverified maano. Use aage mat dohraao, aur daanv madhyam ya ooncha ho toh us par amal mat karo.

### Numbers aur code ko chalaakar check karo

Koi total, percentage ya loan instalment kabhi aankh moondkar mat maano. Inputs ko calculator ya spreadsheet mein daalo aur khud hisaab lagaao. Agar assistant ne aapki di hui list use ki hai, toh items gino: ek row chhootne se har total badal jaata hai.

"Ye number kaise nikaala?" poochhna kaam ka hai, par explanation saboot nahi hai. Wo bhi generate kiya hua text hi hai. March 2025 mein Anthropic ke researchers ne apne ek model ke andar jhaankkar dekha jab wo 36 aur 59 jod raha tha. Usne mote andaazon aur aakhri ank waali tricks ka apna hi mila-jula tareeka use kiya. Phir bhi jab usse poochha gaya ki jod kaise kiya, toh usne school waala 1 haasil le jaane ka tareeka bataaya. Ek saaf-suthri, step-by-step explanation kisi galat number ke upar baithi ho sakti hai, isliye use dobaara calculate karo ya kisi source mein dhoondho.

Code ke saath bhi yahi hai. Agar assistant koi formula ya script likhta hai, toh use ek chhote example par chalaao jiska jawaab aapko pehle se pata ho. Assistant ka "maine ise test kar liya hai" tab tak kuch nahi, jab tak aapne use kaam karte na dekh liya ho.

### Step 4: Reasoning parkho

- **Neutral tareeke se poochho.** "Naya tax regime mere liye behtar kyun hai?" sehmati ko nyota deta hai. "₹9 lakh saalaana kamaane waale kisi insaan ke liye puraane aur naye tax regime ki tulna karo, dono ke paksh ke saath" aisa nahi karta.
- **Ulta paksh maango.** "Is jawaab ke galat hone ki sabse mazboot vajah kya hai?"
- **Dobaara, alag tareeke se poochho.** Nayi chat mein sawaal ko naye shabdon mein poochho. Agar chhote badlaavon se jawaab palat jaaye, toh us par kam bharosa karo.
- **Self-check par mat ruko.** Usse "double-check" karwaane se chhoti galtiyaan pakdi ja sakti hain, par ye wahi system hai jo khud ko check kar raha hai.
- **Doosra assistant bhi independent nahi hai.** Agar do assistants kisi fact par asehmat hon, toh aapko pata chal jaata hai ki check karna hai. Agar wo sehmat hon, toh wo saboot nahi hai: ho sakta hai dono ne wahi puraana page padhkar seekha ho.

### Warning signs par nazar rakho

| AI ke jawaab mein red flag | Udaaharan |
|---|---|
| Bina source ke bahut sateek numbers | "theek 73.4% applicants" |
| Asli logon ke quotes jo aapko kahin aur nahi milte | Kisi mantri ke naam ki ek line jo kisi news site par nahi hai |
| Rules jo kuch zyaada hi suvidhajanak lagein | "Koi documents nahi chahiye" |
| Aap jo bhi kaho, usse sehmat ho jaana | Aapke virodh karne ke baad "haan, aap sahi ho" |
| Tezi se badalne waale topic par, bina taareekh ke, pakka jawaab | Is saal ki limit, rate ya price |

### Images, charts aur screenshots

Jawaabon ke saath ab zyaada se zyaada tasveerein aati hain, aur tasveerein saboot jaisi lagti hain. Wo saboot nahi hain.

- **Chart utna hi achha hai jitne uske numbers.** Agar assistant koi chart banaata hai, toh uske peechhe ki do-teen values ko data se milaakar check karo.
- **Screenshot edit kiya ya gadha ja sakta hai.** Kisi notice ya message ke screenshot par bharosa karne se pehle, official site par original dhoondho. Reverse image search se pata chal sakta hai ki koi image pehli baar kahaan aayi thi.
- **Assistants photos galat padh lete hain.** Jab aap kisi bill ya form ki photo share karo, toh usne jo figures padhe, unhe photo se hi milaakar check karo.

"Deepfakes and misinformation" nakli images aur notices ke baare mein aur gehraai se bataata hai.

### Bhaarateeya bhaashaaon mein jawaab check karna

Jawaab ki quality ek bhaasha se doosri bhaasha mein alag ho sakti hai, aur translation mein naam aur numbers aasaani se bigad jaate hain. Hindi, Tamil ya Bengali mein kisi assistant par bharosa karne se pehle, use aise sawaal par test karo jiska jawaab aapko pehle se pata ho. Kisi bhi zaroori cheez ke liye facts kisi official source mein check karo, jo aksar English aur aapki bhaasha dono mein milta hai.

### Step 5: Jaano ki kab kisi insaan se poochhna hai

Jab daanv ooncha ho ya bharosemand sources asehmat hon, toh kisi insaan ke paas jaao: doctor, pharmacist, lawyer, chartered accountant, aapka manager ya sambandhit sarkaari daftar. Unke paas jaane ki **taiyaari** mein AI bahut badhiya hai. Usse saath le jaane waale sawaal maango, phir apne saath wo jawaab le jaao jo aapko mila, wo sources jo aapne check kiye, aur jo ab bhi saaf nahi hai.

## Ek worked example

**Oonche daanv waala sawaal.** Suresh ke pita 68 saal ke hain, unhe type 2 diabetes hai aur sardi lag gayi hai. Suresh ke ghar par ek over-the-counter sardi ki goli ka dibba hai. Usmein paracetamol aur ek decongestant hai, yaani band naak kholne waali dawa.

Unka pehla sawaal leading hai: "Ye sardi ki goli diabetes ki goliyon ke saath lena theek hai, hai na?" Assistant sehmati deta hai ki ye "generally safe" hai aur ek dose bata deta hai. Phir unhe leading sawaalon waali warning yaad aati hai. Wo nayi chat kholkar neutral tareeke se poochhte hain: "Mere pita 68 saal ke hain, unhe type 2 diabetes hai aur wo uski goliyaan lete hain. Paracetamol aur decongestant waali sardi ki goli lene se pehle mujhe kya check karna chahiye?" Ye jawaab zyaada saavdhaan hai. Ismein bataaya gaya hai ki sardi ke syrups mein cheeni ho sakti hai aur pharmacist se poochhne ka sujhaav hai, par decongestant ke baare mein ab bhi kuch khaas nahi kaha gaya.

Suresh routine chalaate hain:

| Step | Unhone kya kiya |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Daanv | Ooncha: health, ek buzurg, niyamit dawaiyaan, aur bura reaction ho jaaye toh wo use palat nahi sakte |
| 2. Daave | "Generally safe", dose, cheeni waali baat. Chhoota hua: decongestant ke baare mein koi bhi warning |
| 3. Verify | Unhone dibba aur uske saath aayi printed jaankaari padhi. Decongestant ke liye usmein likha tha ki agar diabetes, high blood pressure, heart disease ya thyroid ki samasya ho, toh use se pehle doctor se poochhein. Dono mein se kisi jawaab ne ye nahi kaha tha. |
| 4. Reasoning | Jawaab unke shabdon ke saath badal gaya: jab unhone lead kiya toh "generally safe", jab neutral poochha toh "pharmacist se poochho". Isne unke pita ki baaki beemaariyon ke baare mein kabhi nahi poochha. |
| 5. Escalate | Wo dibba lekar family pharmacist ke paas gaye, jinhone unke pita ki beemaariyon aur baaki dawaiyon ke baare mein poochha, unke liye bina decongestant waala product sujhaaya aur dose confirm ki |

Do sabak dekho. Neutral sawaal ko leading sawaal se behtar jawaab mila, par phir bhi poora nahi. Aur sabse aham fact ek chhooti hui baat thi: koi galat vaakya nahi jise wo check kar sakein, balki ek gaayab vaakya. Sirf original, yaani label, ne ise dikhaaya. Assistant ne unhe ye jaanne mein madad ki ki kya poochhna hai. Jawaab pharmacist ne diya, aur wo bhi khaas unke pita ke liye: ye checking ki ek misaal hai, medical advice nahi; koi aur ho toh use apna label khud padhna chahiye aur doctor ya pharmacist se poochhna chahiye.

**Kam daanv waala sawaal.** Usi hafte Suresh assistant se apne naye chai ke stall ke liye das naam maangte hain. Daanv: kam, kyunki faisla unhi ka hai aur wo apna man badal sakte hain. Wo "Saravanampatti Chai Corner" chunte hain kyunki unhe pasand hai, aur bas ek cheez check karte hain: ki aas-paas koi aur dukaan wo naam pehle se use na kar rahi ho.

Wahi tool, bilkul alag checking, kyunki daanv alag the.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Sab kuch barabar check karna.** Aap thakkar chhod doge. Mehnat daanv ke hisaab se rakho.
- **Tone par bharosa karna.** Confidence aur details saboot nahi hain.
- **Leading sawaal poochhna.** "X sach kyun hai?" poochhoge toh X ke paksh ka case milega. Neutral poochho aur ulta paksh maango.
- **Sirf wahi check karna jo likha hai.** Chhooti hui warning sabse zyaada maayne rakh sakti hai. Exceptions ke baare mein poochho aur original padho.
- **Official link ko current maan lena.** Puraane notices online rehte hain. Saal, round ya version check karo.
- **AI ke hisaab ko saboot maan lena.** Numbers khud dobaara calculate karo, aur code ko aise example par chalaao jiska jawaab aapko pata ho.
- **Self-check ya doosre AI ko independent ginna.** Asehmati batati hai ki check karna hai; sehmati kuch saabit nahi karti.
- **Daanv ooncha hone par expert ko chhod dena.** AI sawaal taiyaar karne mein madad karta hai; jawaab experts dete hain.

## Practice

Neeche link kiye gaye Civic Online Reasoning se lateral reading ka ek lesson karo.

1. **Paanch sawaal, paanch daanv.** Kisi assistant se paanch factual sawaal poochho: kisi scheme ki eligibility, koi aitihaasik taareekh, koi train rule, koi health fact aur koi price. Har ek ka daanv aanko, phir load-bearing daavon ko verify karo aur har source ki taareekh note karo.
2. **Pehle lead karo, phir neutral poochho.** Ek hi sawaal alag-alag chats mein do baar poochho: ek baar "X sach kyun hai?" aur ek baar "Kya X sach hai, aur iske khilaaf kya case hai?" Likho ki jawaab kaise alag the.
3. **Ek number dobaara calculate karo.** Apni zindagi se koi hisaab maango, jaise bill baantna ya mahine ki loan instalment. Usse working maango, phir use calculator ya spreadsheet mein khud dobaara karo.
