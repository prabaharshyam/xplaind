## Ye kya hai

AI answer check karne ka matlab hai **amal karne se pehle un hisson ko confirm karna jin par aap bharosa karoge**. Iska matlab har cheez par barabar shak karna nahi hai. Birthday message ko bas ek baar tez padhna kaafi hai. Medicine ki dose, legal deadline ya customer ke refund amount ke liye saboot chahiye.

Ye paanch-step ki routine hai jo aap dimaag mein chala sakte ho:

```flow
1. Stakes | agar ye galat hua toh kya hoga? -> 2. Claims | names, numbers, dates, rules, quotes par line khencho
2. Claims -> 3. Verify | un claims ko reliable sources se check karo
3. Verify -> 4. Reasoning | kya ye samajh mein aata hai? Dobara poochhne par bhi tikta hai?
4. Reasoning -> 5. Escalate | stakes zyada hain, ya sources asahmat hain? Kisi insaan se poochho
```

Routine ka maksad speed hai. Zyadatar answers ko sirf step 1 aur 2 chahiye; zyada tabhi check karte ho jab zaroori ho.

Is lesson mein hum Suresh ke saath chalenge, jo Coimbatore mein apne diabetic pitaji ki dekhbhaal karta hai, assistant se do bilkul alag sawaalon ke zariye. Lesson ke end tak aap jaan jaoge ki kitna check karna hai, kya check karna hai aur kahan.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Pehle stakes

Apni mehnat ko is hisaab se rakho ki kya galat ho sakta hai:

| Stakes | Examples | Kitna check karein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kam** | Ideas, names, pehle drafts, brainstorming | Skim karo aur edit karo; check ki zaroorat nahi |
| **Medium** | Work emails, reports, customer replies, assignments | Jo bhi fact aur figure daalo, har ek check karo |
| **Zyada** | Health, paisa, law, safety, jo bhi public ya wapas na hone waala ho | Har claim ko official sources ya qualified insaan se verify karo |

### Checkable claims dhoondho

Har **number, date, name, rule aur quotation** par line khencho. Galtiyan yahin chhupti hain, aur yahi check karne mein sabse aasaan bhi hain. "Scheme un students ko support karti hai jinki family income ₹2.5 lakh se kam hai, aur deadline 30 November hai" jaise sentence mein do claims hain jo aap ek minute mein verify kar sakte ho.

### Reliable sources use karo

Achhe sources, bharose ke mote kram mein:

1. **Original:** law, circular, paper, product label.
2. **Official sites:** government departments, RBI ya SEBI jaise regulators, company ke apne pages.
3. **Jaane-maane publishers:** established news organisations aur reference works.

**Lateral reading** use karo: naye tabs kholo aur dekho ki independent sources kisi claim ya website ke baare mein kya kehte hain, page ko uske professional dikhne se judge karne ki jagah.

### AI ke apne sources check karo

Agar answer links cite karta hai, toh unhe kholo. Teen cheezein confirm karo: page hai, source bharosemand hai, aur page sach mein wahi kehta hai jo answer claim karta hai. Banaye hue ya mel na khaate citations aam hain.

### Reasoning test karo

- Poochho: "Ye number kaise nikaala?"
- Poochho: "Kya cheez is answer ko galat bana sakti hai?"
- Wahi sawaal alag shabdon mein poochho. Agar chhote badlaav se answer palat jaaye, toh us par kam bharosa karo.

### Chetavani ke sanket dekho

| AI answer mein red flag | Example |
|---|---|
| Bina source ke bahut precise numbers | "exactly 73.4%" |
| Asli logon ke quotes jo aur kahin nahi milte | |
| Rules jo zyada hi convenient lagte hain | "koi documents nahi chahiye" |
| Aap jo sujhao woh us se sahmat ho jaata hai | "Yes, you're right" |
| Tezi se badalte topics par purani jaankari | prices, rules, dates |

### Aam claims ke liye do-minute ka check

Zyadatar roz ke claims tezi se check ho sakte hain agar pata ho ki kahan dekhna hai:

| Claim ka type | India mein kahan check karein | Time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Government scheme ke rules aur deadlines | Scheme ka official portal ya department ki site (aksar gov.in ya nic.in par khatam hoti hai) | 2 minute |
| Bank, loan ya UPI rules | RBI ki site, UPI ke liye NPCI, ya aapke bank ki apni website | 2 minute |
| Train ya travel rules | IRCTC ya Indian Railways ke official pages | 1 minute |
| Koi medicine | Label aur leaflet, phir pharmacist ya doctor | 5 minute |
| Kisi mashhoor insaan ka quote | Exact words quotes mein search karo; kisi jaane-maane original ko dhoondho | 2 minute |
| Viral news claim | Jaane-maane news sites, sarkaar ke baare mein claims ke liye PIB Fact Check | 3 minute |
| Document ya paper mein koi number | Original document: figure search karo | 1 minute |

Agar kuch minutes mein aap claim ko kisi reliable jagah par na dhoondh paao, toh use unverified maano. Use dohrao mat, aur medium ya zyada stakes mein us par amal mat karo.

### Indian languages mein answers check karna

Assistants aam taur par English mein sabse accurate hote hain, kyunki training mein unhone sabse zyada English text dekha. Agar aap Hindi, Tamil ya Bengali mein poochho, toh facts kamzor aur wording kam precise ho sakti hai. Kisi bhi zaroori cheez ke liye wahi sawaal English mein bhi poochho aur compare karo, ya kisi bhi language mein official source se facts check karo.

### Jaano kab insaan se poochhna hai

Doctor, pharmacist, lawyer, chartered accountant, aapka manager ya sambandhit government office. AI aapko unke liye sahi sawaal **taiyaar** karne mein shaandaar madad karta hai.

## Ek example se samjho

**Zyada stakes waala sawaal.** Suresh ke pitaji ko type 2 diabetes hai aur unhe sardi ho gayi hai. Suresh assistant se poochhta hai ki kya woh ek common over-the-counter cold medicine apni diabetes ki tablets ke saath le sakte hain.

Answer kehta hai ki ye "generally safe" hai, ek dose deta hai, aur jodta hai ki kuch cold syrups mein sugar hoti hai.

Suresh routine chalata hai:

| Step | Usne kya kiya |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Stakes | Zyada: health, bade umar ke insaan, regular medicines |
| 2. Claims | "Generally safe", dose, sugar waali baat |
| 3. Verify | Medicine ka label aur leaflet padha: ek ingredient par diabetes waalon ke liye caution likha hai. Assistant ne ye nahi bataya tha. |
| 4. Reasoning | "Generally safe" dhundhla hai; usne pitaji ki doosri conditions ke baare mein poochha hi nahi |
| 5. Escalate | Family pharmacist ko phone kiya, jisne doosra product recommend kiya aur dose confirm ki |

Assistant ne use ye jaanne mein madad ki ki kya poochhna hai. Jawab pharmacist ne diya.

**Kam stakes waala sawaal.** Usi hafte Suresh assistant se apni nayi chai ki dukaan ke liye das naam maangta hai. Stakes: kam. Woh "Saravanampatti Chai Corner" chunta hai kyunki use pasand hai, aur sirf ek cheez check karta hai: ki koi aur dukaan ya brand pehle se wahi naam use na kar raha ho.

Tool wahi, check bilkul alag, kyunki stakes alag the.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Sab kuch barabar check karna.** Aap haar maan loge. Mehnat ko stakes ke hisaab se rakho.
- **Tone par bharosa karna.** Confidence aur detail saboot nahi hain.
- **Citations na kholna.** Links nakli, toote hue ya claim se unrelated ho sakte hain.
- **AI se khud ko check karwa kar ruk jaana.** Slips pakadne ke liye kaam ka hai, par independent nahi.
- **Stakes zyada hone par expert ko skip karna.** AI sawaal taiyaar karne mein madad karta hai; jawab expert dete hain.
- **Tezi se badalte facts ke liye purane answers use karna.** Prices, rules aur deadlines badalte hain; apne source ki date check karo.

## Practice karo

Civic Online Reasoning ka ek lateral reading lesson karo, neeche linked.

1. **Paanch sawaal, paanch stakes.** Assistant se paanch factual sawaal poochho: kisi scheme ki eligibility, ek historical date, ek train rule, ek health fact aur ek price. Har ek ko stakes se rate karo aur jo claims maayne rakhte hain unhe verify karo.
2. **Citations ka audit karo.** Sources maango aur har link kholo. Note karo ki kaunse missing the, galat the ya claim ko support nahi karte the.
3. **Apna rule likho.** Kam, medium aur zyada stakes ke liye apna ek-line ka checking rule likho aur use apni desk ke paas rakho.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap ek aisa AI answer dikha sako jo aapne galat pakda, aur woh source jisne use saabit kiya.
