## Ye kya hai

Zyadatar logon ke liye writing woh jagah hai jahan AI assistants sabse zyada time bachate hain. Language model, apne core mein, text banane ki machine hai, isliye woh chaar roz ke writing kaamon mein bahut achha hai:

| Kaam | Aap use kya dete ho | Aapko kya milta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Drafting** | Rough notes, reader, goal | Email, report section ya post ka pehla version |
| **Rewriting** | Aapka text aur use kaise badalna hai | Chhota, saaf, narm, zyada formal ya aasaan |
| **Translating** | Text aur target language | Hindi, Tamil, Bengali aur doosri bhasha mein version, ya non-experts ke liye plain English |
| **Summarising** | Lamba document, thread ya transcript | Key points, decisions aur actions |

Chaaron mein **author aap hi rehte ho**. Kya kehna hai, aap tay karte ho, aap check karte ho, aur uski zimmedaari aapki hoti hai. Assistant ek tez junior writer hai, woh insaan nahi jo email par sign karta hai.

```flow
dir: LR
Aapke notes | facts, reader, goal -> AI draft | ek fluent pehla version, tezi se
AI draft | ek fluent pehla version, tezi se -> Aapka check | facts, tone, promises, aapki voice: yahi step maayne rakhta hai
Aapka check -> Aap bhejte ho | aapka naam isi par
```

Is lesson mein hum Fatima ke saath chalenge, jo Kolkata ki ek logistics company mein operations lead hain, jab woh ek uljhi meeting ko client update mein badalti hain. Lesson ke end tak aap bina kuch unchecked bheje, tezi se draft, rewrite, translate aur summarise kar paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Apne notes se drafting

Achhe drafting prompt ke teen hisse hote hain: **aapke points, reader, aur aap reader se kya karwana chahte ho.** Zaroori ho toh length limit jodo.

> "Ye vendor meeting ke mere notes hain. Mere manager ko ek email draft karo jo teen options ka summary de aur option 2 recommend kare. Woh busy hain, isliye 150 words ke andar rakho aur recommendation pehle rakho."

Reader aur goal ke bina aapko generic text milta hai, jise phir aap shuru se dobara likhte ho.

### Apni voice banaye rakhna

Readers ko machine-made text pata chal jaata hai: "I hope this email finds you well", "delve into", teen adjectives ki lambi lists. Isse bachne ke liye:

1. Apne ek-do pichhle messages paste karo aur assistant se style match karne ko kaho.
2. Saaf, chhote sentences maango.
3. Woh phrases hata do jo aap zor se kabhi nahi bologe.

### Rewriting

Rewriting aksar drafting se zyada kaam ki hoti hai, kyunki facts pehle se aapke hain:

- "Isse narm banao par firm rakho."
- "Ye ek customer ke liye samjhao jise technical background nahi hai."
- "Ise aadhi length mein kaato; har number rakho."

### Translating

Achhe assistants Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu aur doosri Indian languages kaafi theek handle karte hain, par quality badalti hai, khaaskar formal writing, legal terms aur regional usage mein. Kisi bhi zaroori cheez ke liye, jaise official letters, health ya legal jaankari, ya bahut saare customers ko messages, fluent speaker se check karwao.

### Summarising

Saaf kaho ki kaisa summary chahiye. "Isse summarise karo" aapko dhundhla paragraph deta hai. Ye aapko aisi cheez deta hai jis par kaam kiya ja sake:

> "Is transcript se ye list karo: liye gaye decisions, open questions, aur owner aur deadline ke saath actions. Jahan deadline tay hui, wahan exact words quote karo."

Summaries zaroori details chhod sakte hain, deadlines narm kar sakte hain ya aise points gadh sakte hain jo kabhi kahe hi nahi gaye. Jis par aap kaam karoge use source se check karo.

### Zimmedaari aur privacy

Aapne bheja toh aapne kaha. Bhejne se pehle ye check karo:

| Check | Kyun |
| --- | --- |
| Names aur titles | Models names badal dete hain ya galat likhte hain |
| Numbers aur dates | Sabse common chupchaap galti |
| Promises aur commitments | Draft aisi cheez ka promise kar sakta hai jo aap deliver nahi kar sakte |
| Tone | Buri khabar par khush text bura lagta hai |
| Confidential content | Us data ke liye sirf wohi tools use karo jo aapki organisation approve kare |

## Ek example se samjho

Fatima Kolkata ki ek logistics company mein operations lead hain. Ek zaroori client ki shipment late ho gayi hai. Unke paas ek ghante ki meeting ka transcript aur uske baare mein 40 emails hain, aur client ko aaj update chahiye.

**Summarise.** Company ke approved assistant mein woh transcript paste karti hain aur poochhti hain: "Decisions made, open issues, aur owner aur deadline ke saath actions mein summarise karo. Jahan deadlines tay hui, wahan exact words quote karo." Do minute mein unke paas ek page ka summary hai.

Woh teeno deadlines ko quotes se check karti hain. Summary ki ek line mein "Friday" hai, par quote kiye words "end of next week" hain. Woh use theek karti hain. Wahi ek check subah ka sabse kimti minute hai.

**Draft.** Agla: "Client ko shaant, professional tone mein ek update email draft karo. Hamare partner ko doshi thehraye bina delay ke liye maafi maango, 14 November ki nayi delivery date do, aur is hafte call offer karo. 150 words ke andar." Woh style example ke liye apna ek purana email paste karti hain.

**Edit.** Draft achha hai. Woh "We sincerely regret any inconvenience this may have caused" kaat deti hain, jo form letter jaisa lagta hai, client ke festival-season stock ke baare mein ek personal line jodti hain, aur date ek baar phir check karti hain.

| Step | AI ke saath time | Aam time |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transcript aur emails padhna, decisions dhoondhna | 10 minute (summary plus checks) | 60 minute |
| Client email likhna | 15 minute (draft plus edits) | 45 minute |
| Total | 25 minute | lagbhag 1 ghanta 45 minute |

Unhone time bachaya aur phir bhi har zaroori cheez check ki: deadline, date aur tone.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Bina padhe bhej dena.** Galtiyan aur ajeeb phrases aapke naam se chale jaate hain.
- **Bland, machine jaisa text.** Style example do aur tab tak edit karo jab tak woh aap jaisa na lage.
- **Decisions ke liye summaries par bharosa karna.** Jis par kaam karoge, woh details source se check karo, khaaskar deadlines aur numbers.
- **Zaroori messages ke liye unchecked translations.** Fluent speaker se padhwao.
- **Confidential documents ko unapproved tools mein paste karna.** Aapki organisation ke rules follow karo.
- **Bahut lamba output.** Word limit maango. Chhota aksar behtar hota hai, khaaskar busy readers ke liye.

## Practice karo

Koi bhi assistant use karo jo aapka school ya workplace allow karta ho, aur achhi aadaton ke liye neeche linked AI Fluency course.

1. **Draft aur edit.** Apne rough notes se ek asli email draft karo, phir use tab tak edit karo jab tak woh aap jaisa na lage. Ginti karo ki kitne changes kiye, aur kis tarah ke: facts, tone ya style.
2. **Summarise aur check.** Ek lambe article ya report ko decisions, numbers aur open questions mein summarise karo. Original se teen points check karo.
3. **Translate aur mark.** Ek chhoti notice ko apni achhi aati Indian language mein translate karo, aur har galti ya awkward phrase mark karo.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap ek draft, apne edits aur ek check kiya hua summary dikha sako, aur bata sako ki kaunsi cheezein aap kabhi bina check kiye nahi bhejoge.
