## Ye kya hai

Kuch bhi automate karne se pehle, AI ke saath ya uske bina, aapko pata hona chahiye ki kaam asal mein hota kaise hai. **Process map** ek diagram hai jo dikhata hai ki kaam kaise ek starting event se outcome tak behta hai. Har step ke liye ye dikhata hai:

- **kya** hota hai;
- **kaun** karta hai;
- **kaunsa system** ya tool use hota hai: email, Tally, portal, WhatsApp, Excel;
- **kitna time** lagta hai, intezaar sahit;
- **kahan** galtiyaan, dobara kaam ya deri hoti hai;
- **decision points**, jaise "kya amount ₹50,000 se zyada hai?".

```flow
dir: LR
Invoice aata hai | who: mail room, system: email -> Excel mein type hota hai | who: accounts, 4 min
Excel mein type hota hai -> Approve hua? | who: manager, 2 din intezaar: bottleneck
Approve hua? -[haan]-> Payment hua | who: accounts, system: Tally, 3 min
```

Ise aap kaagaz, whiteboard ya free diagram tool par draw kar sakte ho. Boxes aur arrows kaafi hain. Value drawing mein nahi hai; baatcheet mein hai aur un numbers mein jo usme jaate hain.

Ye lesson Ludhiana ke ek textile exporter ki accounts team ke saath chalta hai, jo map kar rahi hai ki supplier invoices ka payment kaise hota hai. End tak aap ek asli process map kar paoge, use rough napoge aur tay kar paoge ki pehle kya badalna hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### 1. Saaf kinaare waala ek process chuno

Aisa flow chuno jiska shuru aur ant saaf ho: "supplier invoice milne se payment hone tak", "accounts" nahi. Bade, dhundhle processes bade, dhundhle maps banate hain jinhe koi use nahi karta.

### 2. Asli process map karo, official nahi

Manager ka description aam taur par official version hota hai. Kaam karne waale asli wala jaante hain, uske workarounds aur intezaar ke saath. Unke saath baitho aur kaho ki **pichhle asli case** ko walk-through karein: "Kal ke invoices ke saath aapne kya kiya, dikhao." Jaise woh bolein, har step sticky note ya box mein likhte jaao.

Kaam ke sawaal:

- "Aap aage kya karte ho?"
- "Wo jaankari aapko kahan se milti hai?"
- "Isme kitna time lagta hai? Kitna intezaar karna padta hai?"
- "Jab galat ho jaata hai toh kya hota hai?"

### 3. Details jodo, khaaskar exceptions

Har step ke liye likho kaun, kaunsa system, kitna time aur kitni baar galat hota hai. Phir exceptions poochho: "GSTIN missing ho toh kya hota hai? Amount PO se match na kare toh?" Exceptions kam hote hain par dheere hote hain, aur aksar kul time ka bada hissa lete hain.

### 4. Rough napo

Perfect data ki zaroorat nahi. Priority tay karne aur baad mein fayda sabit karne ke liye rough numbers kaafi hain:

| Measure | Kaise nikaalein | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume | Pichhle mahine ke cases ginn lo | Mahine mein 600 invoices |
| Har item par hands-on time | Kuch asli cases ka time lo | Ek type karne mein 4 minute |
| Intezaar ka time | Emails ya system mein dates milao | Approval ke liye 2 din |
| Exception rate | Jinhe extra kaam chahiye woh cases ginn lo | 15% mein PO number missing |
| Error ya dobara-kaam rate | Corrections ginn lo | 3% galat type hue |

Ye numbers aapki **baseline** ban jaate hain.

### 5. Waste dhoondho

Aam nishaan:

- wahi data do ya zyada jagah type hona;
- approvals ya replies ka intezaar;
- emails ya folders mein jaankari dhoondhna;
- wahi cheez do baar check karna;
- aise systems ke beech haath se copy karna jo aapas mein baat kar sakte the.

### 6. Step by step tay karo

Har wasteful step ke liye paanch options mein se ek chuno:

```flow
Kya ye step bilkul zaroori hai? -[nahi]-> Hata do
Kya ye step bilkul zaroori hai? -[haan]-> Kya ise simple kar sakte hain?
Kya ise simple kar sakte hain? -[haan]-> Simple karo
Kya ise simple kar sakte hain? -[nahi]-> Kya rules saaf aur fixed hain?
Kya rules saaf aur fixed hain? -[haan]-> Rules se automate karo
Kya rules saaf aur fixed hain? -[nahi]-> Kya ye text padhna ya likhna hai?
Kya ye text padhna ya likhna hai? -[haan]-> AI step se automate karo | saath mein insaan ka check
Kya ye text padhna ya likhna hai? -[nahi]-> Abhi ke liye chhod do
```

Automate karne se pehle waste hataao. Gair-zaroori step ko automate karne se waste bas tez ho jaata hai.

## Ek worked example

Ludhiana ke ek textile exporter ki accounts team mein do assistants aur ek manager hain. Woh mahine mein lagbhag 600 supplier invoices process karte hain. Manager ko lagta hai ki process mein "har invoice par kuch minute" lagte hain.

Team pichhle Tuesday ke invoices lekar milkar asli process map karti hai:

| Step | Kaun | System | Time | Problems |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Invoice email, WhatsApp ya courier se aata hai; paper waale scan hote hain | Assistant | Email, phone, scanner | Har paper invoice par 5 min | Teen channels check karne padte hain |
| 2. Details Excel mein type hoti hain | Assistant | Excel | 4 min | Typing errors lagbhag 3% |
| 3. Purchase order check hota hai | Assistant | Tally | 3 min | 15% mein PO number nahi: purchase team ko phone call, lagbhag 20 min |
| 4. Manager approve karta hai | Manager | Email | Intezaar: average 2 din | Sabse badi deri |
| 5. Payment ke liye details dobara type hoti hain | Assistant | Tally | 3 min | Double data entry |
| 6. Payment har hafte schedule hota hai | Assistant | Bank portal | 1 min | Ek hafta aur ruk sakta hai |

**Findings.** Exceptions aur paper invoices ginne par har invoice par average lagbhag 15 minute ka hands-on kaam, yaani lagbhag mahine mein 150 ghante. Data do baar type hota hai. Aur sabse badi deri typing mein nahi hai: approval ke liye do din intezaar mein hai.

**Decisions.**

| Waste | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Aane ke teen channels | Simple karo: suppliers se kaho ki invoices ek hi address par email karein |
| Invoice details type karna | AI step se automate karo jo fields padhe, saath mein assistant ka check |
| Tally mein dobara type karna | Hata do: check hua data ek baar Tally mein push karo |
| Approval ka intezaar | Simple karo: ₹1 lakh se kam ke amounts ke liye WhatsApp par one-tap approval |
| Missing PO numbers | Exception hata do: bade suppliers se PO numbers zaroori karo |

Map owner ke saamne case banata hai, aur baseline numbers teen mahine mein fayda sabit karenge.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Manager ke description se map banana.** Kaam karne waalon se baat karo, haal ke asli case ke saath.
- **Exceptions ko ignore karna.** Zyadatar time wahin chhupa hota hai.
- **Numbers na hona.** Volume aur time ke bina na priority tay hoti hai na improvement sabit hota hai.
- **Waste ko automate karna.** Automate karne se pehle hataao aur simple karo.
- **Bahut bada process map karna.** Saaf shuru aur ant waala ek flow chuno.
- **Intezaar ka time bhool jaana.** Steps ke beech ki deri aksar steps se zyada maayne rakhti hai.

## Practice karo

Atlassian ki process mapping guide (neeche link hai) aur GOV.UK ki discovery phase guide padho.

1. **Interview karo.** Apna jaana hua ek process chuno, jaise coaching centre mein fee collection, leave approvals ya stock reordering. Jo use karta hai usse pichhle asli case ke baare mein interview lo.
2. **Map draw karo.** Har step ke liye kaun, system, time aur failure points ke saath, aur volume aur exceptions ke rough numbers ke saath draw karo.
3. **Tay karo.** Waste ke teen sabse bade sources mark karo aur har ek ke liye tay karo: hata do, simple karo, rules se automate karo, AI plus check se automate karo, ya chhod do.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap apna map numbers ke saath dikha sako aur samjha sako ki pehle kaunsa badlaav karoge aur kyun.
