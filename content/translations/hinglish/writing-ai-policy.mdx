## Ye kya hai

**AI policy** tay karti hai ki koi organisation AI ko kaise use aur build karegi. Company ki AI policy follow karne aur AI ko safely use karne waale lessons employee ki taraf ki baat karte hain: apna data sort karo, licensed tool use karo, output check karo. Ye lesson mez ki doosri taraf ka hai. Aap tay karte ho ki kaun-se tools approved hain, har tool kaun-sa data dekh sakta hai, risky uses par kaun sign off karega, aur kuch gadbad hone par kya hoga.

Employee ko bas rule samajhna hota hai. Likhne waale ko har rule teen tests mein pass karwaana hota hai:

1. **Kya ek naya employee ise ek minute mein kisi asli task par laagu kar sakta hai?** "AI ko zimmedaari se use karo" fail. "Client code sirf IDE assistant mein jaayega, aur opt-out list waale clients ke liye wahaan bhi nahi" pass.
2. **Kya aap ise kisi baahar waale ke saamne defend kar sakte ho?** Client ka security reviewer, koi auditor, ya kisi incident ke baad koi regulator.
3. **Kya ye koi legal raasta chhodta hai?** Agar safe jawaab sirf "mat karo" hai, toh log koi unsafe raasta dhoondh lete hain.

Ek achhi pehli policy **do se chaar pannon** ki hoti hai, lawyers ke liye nahi balki employees ke liye likhi, saath mein ek panne ka summary. Ye AI tools **use** karne waale har insaan ko aur AI features **build** karne waali teams ko cover karti hai. Ye raha copy karne ke liye ek template; baaki lesson har row ko samjhaata hai.

| Section | Aap kya tay karte ho | Ek sensible default | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scope aur principles | Ye kise cover karti hai; 3 se 5 commitments | Saara staff aur contractors; zimmedaari insaanon ki rehti hai | Leadership sign karti hai |
| Approved tools | Kaun-se tools, kin settings ke saath | Sirf checklist pass karne waale tools, naam se listed | IT security |
| Tool ke hisaab se data | Har tool kaun-si data class dekh sakta hai | Ek panne ka matrix, saath mein client opt-out list | Security aur legal |
| Uses | Encouraged, restricted, prohibited | Har rule ke saath ek wajah aur ek approved alternative | Policy owner |
| Exceptions | Kaise maangein, kitni jaldi jawaab | Ek form, 3 working days mein jawaab, logged | Policy owner |
| AI build karna | Launch se pehle kya hona chahiye | Risk assessment, evaluation, oversight, monitoring | AI review group |
| Disclosure | Kab batana hai ki AI shaamil tha | Chatbots bataayein ki wo AI hain; asli dikhne waale AI media par label | Product aur legal |
| Incidents | Kya report karna hai, kise, kitni jaldi | 1 ghante ke andar security ko, imaandaar reports par koi blame nahi | Security |
| Training aur review | Sabko kya seekhna hai; policy kab badlegi | Asli tasks ke saath 30 minute; har 6 mahine mein review | Policy owner |

Aap Pune ki delivery head Neha ke saath chaloge, jinki company ko ek client ke security review ki wajah se apni pehli AI policy likhni padti hai. Aakhir tak aap ek policy draft kar paoge, use asli tasks par test kar paoge aur dikha paoge ki log use follow kar rahe hain ya nahi.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Step 1: Pata karo ki pehle se kya chal raha hai

Jab tak ye pata na ho ki log pehle se kya use kar rahe hain, kuch mat likho. Bina approval waale use ko **shadow AI** kehte hain, aur jis tool ke hone ka aapko pata hi nahi, uske baare mein aap samajhdaari waala rule nahi likh sakte. Chaar sources, sabse saste se sabse zyada jaankaari waale tak:

- **Licence consoles.** Agar company pehle se kisi assistant ke paise de rahi hai, toh uska admin page dikhaata hai ki kitni seats active hain.
- **Network aur proxy logs.** IT jaane-maane AI websites par visits gin sakta hai. Isse pata chalta hai kaun-se tools, ye nahi ki kya paste kiya gaya.
- **Expense claims aur card statements.** Personal AI subscriptions ka claim asli zaroorat ka pakka sanket hai.
- **Ek chhota anonymous survey.** Kaun-se tools, kin tasks ke liye, kis tarah ke data ke saath, aur approved tool se aapko kya chahiye?

Likhkar waada karo ki discovery mein jo bhi milega us par kisi ko saza nahi hogi, warna log theek wahi use chhupa lenge jo aapko sabse zyada dekhna hai.

Nateeja hota hai ek **AI inventory**: use mein chal raha har AI tool aur feature, uske owner, wo jo data dekhta hai, vendor, risk level aur approval status ke saath. Ise up to date rakho. Client ka security reviewer sabse pehle yahi maangta hai.

### Step 2: Tay karo ki faisla kaun karega

Har faisle ke liye ek insaan ka naam likho:

| Faisla | Kaun tay karta hai | Target turnaround |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Naya tool approve karna | IT security, contract ke liye legal ke saath | 2 se 3 hafte |
| Client-facing bot jaisa koi restricted use approve karna | Policy owner, ya high risk ke liye ek chhota AI review group | 1 se 2 hafte |
| Ek baar ka exception dena | Policy owner | 3 working days |
| Tay karna ki incident baahar report karna hai ya nahi | Security aur legal milkar | Ghante, din nahi |
| Policy badalna | Policy owner, leadership ke sign ke saath | Har 6 mahine, ya kisi trigger ke baad |

Ye samay policy mein likho. Jo request ek mahine ke liye gaayab ho jaaye, wo logon ko poochhna band karna sikha deti hai.

### Step 3: Checklist se tools approve karo

"Company data par train na karne ke liye configured hai" review ki ek line hai, poora review nahi. Tool approve karne se pehle inke likhit jawaab lo:

1. **Training.** Kya aapka data contract se training se baahar hai, sirf aisi setting se nahi jise user badal sake?
2. **Retention.** Prompts, files aur outputs kitne samay tak rakhe jaate hain, aur kya admin unhe delete kar sakta hai?
3. **Location.** Data kahaan store aur process hota hai? Kuch client contracts mein zaroori hai ki wo India mein ya tay regions mein hi rahe.
4. **Access.** Vendor mein kaun aapke prompts padh sakta hai, aur kaun-si doosri companies (sub-processors) aapka data handle karti hain?
5. **Admin control.** Single sign-on, chhodkar jaane waalon ko hataana, export ho sakne waale audit logs, aur risky features band karne ke switches.
6. **Contract.** Confidentiality, ek data processing agreement, aur breach ke baare mein vendor ko aapko kitni jaldi batana hoga.
7. **Chhupe data paths.** Web search, plugins, email ya drives ke connectors, memory aur agent modes, har ek data ko kisi nayi jagah bhejta hai. Sirf utna approve karo jitna chahiye.

Jawaab inventory mein record karo aur har saal, ya jab vendor apni terms badle, dobaara review karo.

Phir pichhle lessons ki data classes se **data-by-tool matrix** likho:

| Tool | Public | Internal | Confidential (client) | Personal data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise assistant | Haan | Haan | Client documents, par code nahi; opt-out clients ke liye nahi | Nahi |
| Approved IDE mein code assistant | Haan | Haan | Client code; opt-out clients ke liye nahi | Nahi |
| HR system mein built-in AI features | Haan | Haan | Nahi | HR data, sirf HR kaamon ke liye |
| Koi bhi aur tool, free waale bhi | Haan | Nahi | Nahi | Nahi |

**Client opt-out list** contracts ko aisi cheez mein badal deti hai jise engineer check kar sake: kaun-se clients third-party AI processing mana karte hain, ya sirf kuch locations mein allow karte hain. Legal ise rakhta hai; delivery har project ki shuruaat mein ise check karti hai.

### Step 4: Uses ko risk ke hisaab se baanto aur aise rules likho jo log laagu kar sakein

| Encouraged | Restricted (approval chahiye) | Prohibited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved tools mein drafting, summarising aur research | Customer-facing chatbots aur AI features | Unapproved tools mein confidential ya personal data |
| Coding help, generated code ke review aur licence check ke saath | Logon ke baare mein faisle, jaise hiring, appraisal ya credit | Asli logon ke, unki consent ke bina bane deepfakes |
| Internal documents translate karna | Company systems mein kaam kar sakne waale agents | Logon par legal asar waale poori tarah automated faisle |
| Sabko batakar, approved tool mein internal meetings transcribe karna | External calls record karna, sirf doosri taraf ki sahmati se | Kisi bhi prompt mein secrets, passwords aur API keys |

2026 mein teen nayi rows par dhyaan dena hoga:

- **Agents.** Jo agent email bhej sakta hai, code merge kar sakta hai ya paise move kar sakta hai, use kaam ke liye zaroori sabse kam access, kisi bhi aise kaam se pehle human approval jise waapas karna mushkil ho, aur har action ka log chahiye. Chat tool approve karne ka matlab uska agent mode approve karna nahi hai.
- **Meeting transcription.** Ek sensible default: shuru mein sabko batao, kisi ko bhi aapatti karne do, aur transcript ko meeting ke content ki tarah hi treat karo.
- **Output ka ownership.** Generated code kisi aise open-source code jaisa ho sakta hai jiska licence aapka client mana karta hai. Jo licence scanning aap kisi bhi third-party code ke liye karte ho wahi chalao, aur client contracts mein check karo ki AI-assisted kaam ka owner kaun hai.

**Disclosure defaults:** chatbots apne pehle message mein bataayein ki wo AI hain; asli dikhne waali AI images, audio aur video par label ho; AI se draft hue client deliverables par label ho, jab contract ya client kahe.

Har rule chaar hisson mein likho: **rule, wajah, approved alternative, example.** Jaise: "IDE assistant ke alaawa kisi bhi tool mein client code paste mat karo. Wajah: kai client contracts ise mana karte hain. Iski jagah: ek banaaye hue snippet se pattern samjhao. Example: client ka code paste kiye bina poochho ki REST API ko paginate kaise karein."

### Step 5: Baahar ke rules andar jodo

Aapki policy kaanoon ya aapke contracts se dheeli nahi ho sakti. Ye November 2026 tak ka summary hai, legal advice nahi; maujooda text counsel se confirm karwaao.

| Source | Ye kya maangta hai | Aapki policy kya kehti hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Client contracts | Aksar third-party processing, locations ya AI use ko limit karte hain | Client opt-out list, project ki shuruaat mein check |
| CERT-In directions (April 2022) | Data breaches aur data leaks samet listed cyber incidents, pata chalne ke 6 ghante ke andar CERT-In ko report | Staff 1 ghante ke andar security ko report kare; CERT-In par security aur legal faisla karein |
| DPDP Act aur Rules (November 2025 mein notified) | May 2027 se zyaadatar business duties laagu, jinmein personal data breach ke baare mein prabhaavit logon ko bina deri batana aur 72 ghante ke andar Data Protection Board ko detailed report dena shaamil hai | Personal data sirf bataaye gaye purpose ke liye; incident route pehle se legal tak pahunchta hai |
| IT Rules amendment (20 February 2026 se laagu) | Platforms ko asli dikhne waale AI-made audio, images aur video par label lagaana hoga | Agar aap platform chalaate ho, toh legal review kare; baaki sab default roop se aise media par label lagaayein |
| EU AI Act | Non-EU firms par laagu jab unka AI system EU mein offer ho ya uska output wahaan use ho. Chatbot disclosure duties 2 August 2026 se; 2026 ke ek amendment ne hiring aur credit jaise high-risk uses ki duties 2 December 2027 tak khiska deen | Agar aapke EU users ya clients hain, toh legal aapke uses ko Act se map kare |

Sabse aam galti hai baahar waali deadline se lambi internal deadline. "24 ghante ke andar report karo" se 6 ghante ki duty naamumkin ho jaati hai. Internal ghadi sabse chhoti honi chahiye.

```flow
dir: LR
title: Ek AI incident, pata chalne se band hone tak
Kisi ko problem dikhti hai -> 1 ghante ke andar security ko report | Imaandaar reports par koi blame nahi
1 ghante ke andar security ko report -> Security aur legal triage
Security aur legal triage -[listed cyber incident ya personal data]-> Baahar ki reports | 6 ghante ke andar CERT-In; zaroorat ke hisaab se DPDP aur client notices
Security aur legal triage -[sirf internal]-> Fix karo aur record karo
Baahar ki reports -> Fix karo aur record karo
Fix karo aur record karo -> Policy ya tool settings update karo
```

Principles ke liye MeitY ki India's AI Governance Guidelines (November 2025) aur NIST AI Risk Management Framework use karo. Ye guidelines koi naya AI kaanoon nahi banaatin; ye maujooda kaanoonon aur sector regulators par tiki hain, isliye banking, insurance ya health mein aapke regulator ke rules inke upar laagu hote hain.

### Step 6: Aap jo AI features build karte ho, unke liye launch gate rakho

Kisi AI feature ke launch se pehle risk ke hisaab se ek package maango: risk assessment, evaluation results, human oversight plan, monitoring, aur kisi named group ka approval. Ek internal summariser ke liye ek panna kaafi hai. Customer-facing credit assistant ko accuracy aur fairness ka evaluation, use overrule kar sakne waala ek insaan, aur uske galat hone par kya karna hai iska plan chahiye.

### Step 7: Launch karo, naapo, review karo

Policy aur approved tools **ek hi din** launch karo; bina tools ki policy ban jaisi lagti hai. Phir:

- **Train** karo 30 minute mein, har team ke asli tasks se.
- **Measure** har mahine: approved tools ke active users, unapproved AI sites par traffic, exception requests, incident reports aur teen tasks waala quiz.
- **Signals padho.** Shuru mein kuch incident reports aayein toh matlab route kaam kar raha hai; zero ka matlab aksar chhupi hui galtiyaan.
- **Anupaat mein enforce karo.** Imaandaar galtiyon par coaching; incidents chhupaane ya baar-baar jaanboojhkar niyam todne par HR process. Unapproved sites tabhi block karo jab approved tool kaam theek se kar raha ho.
- **Review** har chhah mahine mein, aur kisi naye tool, naye kaanoon ya gambhir incident ke baad.

## Ek example se samjho

Ye ek illustrative case hai. Method dikhaane ke liye company aur numbers banaaye gaye hain.

Pune ki 300 logon waali ek software services company ke paas koi AI policy nahi hai. Ek security review ke dauraan ek bada client poochhta hai: "Aapki company mein AI use kaise govern hota hai, aur kin tools ne hamaara code dekha hai?" Doosre hisse ka jawaab kisi ke paas nahi hai. Delivery head Neha ko CTO aur legal counsel ke saath ise theek karne ke liye chhah hafte milte hain.

**Discovery (weeks 1 aur 2).** Ek anonymous survey mein 210 jawaab aate hain; 150 log kaam par AI use karte hain. Proxy logs mein chaar chatbot sites regular use mein dikhti hain. Do teams maanti hain ki unhone client code personal accounts mein paste kiya. Kisi ko saza nahi hoti, aur legal un clients ke contracts check karta hai ki unhe batane ki koi duty toh nahi.

**Tool checks (weeks 2 se 4).** Enterprise assistant checklist pass kar leta hai. Code assistant ka vendor prompts 30 din rakhta hai aur India ke baahar process karta hai. Do clients ke contracts mein India-only processing zaroori hai, isliye wo opt-out list par jaate hain aur unki teams ek local code-search tool use karti hain. Assistant ka email connector alag se review hone tak band rehta hai.

**Ek mushkil request.** Ek team aisa AI agent chaahti hai jo apne pull requests khud merge kare. Neha ise restricted rakhti hain: agent pull requests khol sakta hai aur tests chala sakta hai, par merge ek insaan karega, aur use production ka koi access nahi milega.

**Unhone kya likha** (teen panne, CEO ke sign ke saath):

| Section | Unhone kya likha |
| --- | --- |
| Approved tools | Enterprise assistant aur IDE code assistant, settings listed; connectors band |
| Tool ke hisaab se data | Matrix, saath mein 2 clients ki opt-out list |
| Restricted | Client-facing AI features ke liye risk assessment aur client ka written approval zaroori; agents ko least access aur merge insaan kare |
| Prohibited | Client ke kaam ke liye personal AI accounts; prompts mein secrets; unapproved tools mein client ya personal data |
| Incidents | 1 ghante ke andar security ko report; CERT-In aur client notices par security aur legal faisla karein |
| Exceptions aur review | 3 working days mein jawaab waala form; har 6 mahine mein review |

**Do mahine baad:**

| Measure | Pehle | Baad mein | Kaise naapa |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Approved tools ke weekly active users | 40 | 170 | Licence admin console |
| Unapproved chatbot sites par visits | Hafte mein lagbhag 900 | Hafte mein lagbhag 150 | Proxy logs |
| Exception requests | Koi nahi (koi route nahi) | 11, jinmein 9 approved | Request form |
| Report hue incidents | 0 | 2 | Security log |
| Client security review | Khula sawaal | Pass, inventory attach karke | Client ki report |

Dono incidents ye the: asli customer names waali ek test file assistant mein paste karna, aur ek prompt jismein client ki API key thi. Key ek ghante ke andar rotate kar di gayi, aur security aur legal ne usi din dono ko CERT-In aur client contract ke hisaab se check kiya. Dono isliye saamne aaye kyunki report karne par koi blame nahi tha.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Pata karne se pehle likhna.** Jo policy logon ke pehle se use ho rahe tools ko ignore karti hai, log bhi use ignore kar dete hain.
- **Kisi doosri company ki policy copy karna.** Unke tools, clients aur kaanoon aapke nahi hain; structure lo, rules nahi.
- **Ek setting dekhkar tool approve karna.** "Hamaare data par train nahi karta" retention, location, vendor access ya connectors ke baare mein kuch nahi batata.
- **Baahar waali deadline se lambi internal deadline.** 24 ghante ka internal rule 6 ghante ki CERT-In duty tod deta hai.
- **Bina route ke bans aur rules.** Log personal accounts par, nazar se door, AI use karte rehte hain. Har "mat karo" ke saath ek approved alternative aur dinon mein jawaab dene waala exception route chahiye.
- **Golmol shabd.** Bina examples ke "appropriate", "responsibly" aur "sensitive" ka har padhne waale ke liye alag matlab hota hai.
- **System ki jagah sirf ek document.** Koi owner nahi, puraani inventory aur koi review date nahi, toh policy ek saal mein galat ho jaati hai.

## Practice karo

Neeche linked NIST AI RMF ka Govern function aur OWASP checklist ka AI asset inventory waala section padho. PRS ka summary 2023 mein pass hue DPDP kaanoon ko cover karta hai; 2025 ke Rules baad mein aaye. CERT-In directions aur EU AI Act summary ke high-risk uses par ek nazar daalo.

1. **Discover.** Jis organisation ko aap jaante ho, uske liye use mein chal rahe paanch AI tools, har ek jo data dekhta hai aur uska owner list karo, aur likho ki har ek ko aap kaise confirm karoge.
2. **Draft.** Is lesson ki shuruaat waala template do se teen pannon mein bharo, ek data-by-tool matrix aur 1 ghante ki internal deadline waale incident route ke saath.
3. **Ek tool check karo.** Kisi asli AI tool ki public terms se checklist ke saaton sawaalon ke jawaab likho, aur note karo ki kaun-se jawaab nahi mile.
4. **Test karo.** Draft aur teen asli tasks alag-alag teams ke do logon ko do. Jo bhi rule wo galat samjhein, use theek karo.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab** aap apni policy kisi naye employee ko do aur wo teen tasks ke liye sahi-sahi bata de ki kaun-sa tool aur kaun-sa data use kar sakta hai, aur kisi client ka security reviewer har wo tool dhoondh sake jisne uska data dekha hai.
