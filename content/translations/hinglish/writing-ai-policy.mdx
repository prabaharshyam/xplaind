## Ye kya hai

Ek **AI policy** tay karti hai ki koi organisation AI ko kaise use aur build karegi. Iske bina log andaaza lagate hain: kuch log AI se poori tarah door rehkar peeche reh jaate hain, kuch client data free tools mein paste kar dete hain. Achhi policy safe raaste ko aasaan raasta bana deti hai.

Ek achhi pehli policy **do se chaar pages** ki hoti hai, vakeelon ke bajaye employees ke liye likhi hui, aur har chhe mahine mein review hoti hai. Isme aam taur par do parts hote hain:

| Part | Kiske liye | Kya cover karta hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI tools use karna** | Sabke liye | Kaunse tools, kaunsa data, kya review aur disclosure |
| **AI features banana** | Product aur engineering teams | Risk assessment, testing, oversight, monitoring aur launch se pehle approval |

| Policy ka part | Kya kehta hai |
|---|---|
| Principles | 3-5 chhote commitments |
| Approved tools | Naam ke saath, settings ke saath |
| Data classes | Public, internal, confidential, personal: kaunse tools kise dekh sakte hain |
| Use cases | Encouraged, restricted, prohibited |
| Review | Kaun accountable hai aur kya review hota hai |
| Disclosure | Customers ko AI use ke baare mein kab batana hai |
| Building AI | Risk assessment, phir evals, phir approval |
| Incidents | Report kaise karna hai, bina blame ke |
| Training | Sabke liye 30 minute, regular updates ke saath |

Ye lesson Neha ke saath chalta hai, jo Pune ki ek software services company mein delivery head hai, jab ek client ka security review company ko apni pehli AI policy likhne par majboor karta hai. End tak aap ek practical policy draft kar paoge jise ek naya employee real tasks par apply kar sake.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Principles

Teen se paanch chhote commitments jinse baaki sab nikalta hai, jaise:

1. AI-assisted kaam ke liye log accountable rahein.
2. Personal aur confidential data ki hifazat karo, clients ka bhi.
3. AI involve ho toh customers ke saath transparent raho.
4. Launch se pehle quality aur fairness test karo.
5. Problems ko jaldi aur bina blame ke report karo.

India ki AI Governance Guidelines aur NIST AI Risk Management Framework jaise frameworks principles ke liye achhe sources hain.

### Approved tools

Approved tools ke naam batao aur ye bhi ki wo kaise configure hain, jaise "company ka enterprise AI assistant, jo company data par train na hone ke liye configure hai". Saaf likho ki work data ke liye personal accounts use nahi karne hain.

### Data classes

| Class | Examples | Kaunse tools ise process kar sakte hain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Published material, public circulars | Koi bhi tool |
| Internal | Process notes, internal templates | Sirf approved tools |
| Confidential | Client code, contracts, unreleased results | Sirf approved tools, aur sirf jahan client contracts allow karein |
| Personal | Customer ya employee ka personal data | Sirf specifically approved systems; DPDP Act follow karta hai |

Personal data **DPDP Act** follow karta hai: use sirf un purposes ke liye karo jinke baare mein logon ko bataya gaya tha, consent jaise lawful ground par, aur zaroorat se zyada nahi.

### Use cases

| Encouraged | Restricted (approval chahiye) | Prohibited |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drafting, summarising, research | Customer-facing AI features | Customer data unapproved tools mein |
| Coding assistance, review ke saath | Logon ke baare mein decisions, jaise hiring ya credit | Asli logon ke deepfakes |
| Internal documents ka translation | Medical, legal ya financial advice | Bina review ke, logon par legal asar daalne waale poori tarah automated decisions |

Har prohibited use ke liye, jahan koi allowed alternative ho, use batao. Jo policy sirf "mat karo" kehti hai, wo use ko chhupa deti hai.

### Review aur accountability

Jo insaan AI output use karta hai, wahi uske liye zimmedaar hai. Customer-facing aur high-risk uses ke liye ek **named owner** aur documented review chahiye.

### Disclosure

Batao ki customers ya readers ko kab batana hai ki AI use hua: chatbots khud ko AI batayein, aur AI-assisted reports par label lage jahan clients ya rules maangte hain.

### AI features banana

Launch se pehle, risk ke hisaab se ek chhota package:

```flow
dir: LR
Idea -> Risk assessment -> Evaluation results -> Oversight plan -> Monitoring and incident plan -> Approval by a named group -> Launch
```

Low-risk internal summariser ke liye ek page kaafi hai; customer-facing credit assistant ke liye bahut zyada chahiye.

### Incidents

AI ki galti, data leak ya harmful output kaise report karna hai, kisko, aur kitni jaldi, jaise "24 ghante ke andar security team ko", aur **honest reports par koi blame nahi**. Chhupi hui galtiyan hi mehngi padti hain.

### Training aur updates

Saare staff ke liye ek chhoti training, ek named policy owner, aur ek review date. Tools aur laws tezi se badalte hain; do saal purani policy shaayad galat hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Pune ki 300 logon ki ek software services company ke paas koi AI policy nahi hai. Staff alag-alag tools use karta hai, kuch client code ke saath. Ek security review mein ek bada client poochta hai: "Aapki company mein AI use kaise govern hota hai?" Kisi ke paas achha jawab nahi hai.

Delivery head Neha CTO aur legal counsel ke saath do hafte mein teen-page ki policy draft karti hai:

| Section | Unhone kya likha |
| --- | --- |
| Principles | Accountability, pehle client confidentiality, transparency, quality aur fairness, problems report karo |
| Approved tools | Enterprise AI assistant aur approved IDE mein code assistant, dono company data par train na hone ke liye configured |
| Client code aur data | Sirf approved tools mein, aur sirf jahan client contract allow kare; opt-out karne waale clients ki list |
| Restricted | Client-facing AI features ke liye risk assessment aur client ka approval chahiye |
| Prohibited | Client ke kaam ke liye personal AI accounts; koi bhi customer personal data unapproved tools mein |
| Incidents | 24 ghante ke andar security team ko report; honest galtiyon par koi blame nahi |
| Training | Saare staff ke liye 30 minute; policy owner har chhe mahine review karta hai |

**Phir kya hua:**

| Measure | Policy se pehle | Ek mahine baad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Approved AI tools ka use | Kam; bahut log personal accounts par | Lagbhag teen guna zyada |
| Client security review | Khula sawaal | Pass hua |
| Reported incidents | Koi report nahi hua, kai ka shak | Do report hue aur jaldi fix hue |

Approved tools ka use teen guna ho gaya kyunki logon ko aakhirkaar pata chal gaya ki kya allowed hai. Report hue do incidents, ek test file jisme asli naam the aur ek prompt jisme client ki API key thi, ek din ke andar pakad liye gaye, kyunki report karne par koi blame nahi tha.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **AI ko poori tarah ban karna.** Log phir bhi use karte hain, unsafe tareeke se aur nazar se door.
- **Lambe legal documents jo koi nahi padhta.** Saaf likho, examples aur one-page summary ke saath.
- **Client contracts ko ignore karna.** Kai contracts client data ke processing par restrictions lagate hain.
- **Koi owner ya review date nahi.** Tools aur laws tezi se badalte hain.
- **Koi incident route nahi.** Phir galtiyan chhupi rehti hain.
- **Sirf "mat karo".** Hamesha batao ki kaam karne ka approved tareeka kya hai.

## Practice karo

Neeche link kiye hue NIST AI RMF ka govern function, DPDP Act par PRS ka summary, aur EU AI Act summary ki prohibited aur high-risk uses ki list skim karo.

1. **Draft karo.** Jis organisation ko aap jaante ho, uske liye upar ke saare sections ke saath do-page ki AI policy likho.
2. **Summarise karo.** Employees ke liye paanch dos aur paanch don'ts ka one-page summary likho.
3. **Test karo.** Alag teams ke do logon se ise padhne aur ek cheez batane ko kaho jo saaf nahi hai; use fix karo.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap apni policy ek naye employee ko de sako aur wo teen example tasks ke liye sahi bata de ki kaunsa tool aur kaunsa data use kar sakta hai.
