## Ye kya hai

AI product ke har idea ke saamne shuru mein do sawaal aate hain, aur inhe galat karna mehenga padta hai:

1. **Feasibility.** Kya AI ye kaam hamare real data par, theek cost aur speed par, kaafi achhe se kar sakta hai?
2. **Build versus buy.** Kya hum tayyar product kharidein, jo platform pehle se use karte hain usmein AI feature configure karein, ya khud banayein?

Teams aksar pehle sawaal ko chhod deti hain aur doosre par mahino behes karti hain. Behtar order ye hai: kuch dinon mein feasibility test karo, phir tay karo ki deliver kaise karna hai.

```flow
Idea -> 20-50 real examples par prototype | din, mahine nahi
20-50 real examples par prototype -[bahut door]-> Abhi feasible nahi | zyada context ya data chahiye, ya ruko
20-50 real examples par prototype -[kareeb]-> Feasible: ab tay karo deliver kaise karna hai
Feasible: ab tay karo deliver kaise karna hai -> Product kharido
Feasible: ab tay karo deliver kaise karna hai -> Platform configure karo
Feasible: ab tay karo deliver kaise karna hai -> Apna system banao
```

Ye lesson Sana ke saath chalta hai, jo Hyderabad ki ek hospital chain mein product manager hain. Woh test karti hain ki AI discharge summaries draft kar sakta hai ya nahi, phir tay karti hain ki deliver kaise karein. Lesson ke end tak aap ek quick feasibility test chala paoge aur build, configure ya buy ke faisle ka bachaav kar paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Din mein prototype

20 se 50 **real** examples lo, aur agar unmein personal data ho toh mask kar do. Task ko ek capable model aur simple prompt se try karo, haath se ya notebook mein. Ye hai **proof of concept**: chhota, tez aur imaandaar.

Phir outputs ko kisi **domain expert** se judge karwao, un engineers se nahi jinhone prompt banaya. Har output ko simple scale par rate karo:

| Rating | Matlab |
| --- | --- |
| Usable | Sahi hai, zyada se zyada chhote edits chahiye |
| Needs work | Idea sahi hai, bade edits chahiye |
| Unsafe ya wrong | Use karne par nuksaan karega |

- Agar results bahut door hain, toh task abhi feasible nahi ho sakta, ya zyada context ya data chahiye.
- Agar results kareeb hain, toh feasibility likely hai, aur asli kaam reliability, integration, safety aur evaluation ka hai.

### Cost aur speed ka andaaza

Prototype se naapo ki har request par kitne tokens lagte hain aur har jawab mein kitna time lagta hai. Isse expected volume se guna karo. Dekho ki ye budget mein aur user ke sabr mein fit hota hai ya nahi: doctor draft ke liye das second ruk lega, do minute nahi.

### Teen options

| Option | Ye kya hai | Taakat | Kamzori |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Product kharidna** | Use case ke liye tayyar tool, jaise meeting-notes tool ya document-processing service | Sabse tez; updates vendor sambhaalta hai | Customisation kam; vendor ka roadmap; aapka data vendor ke paas jaata hai |
| **Platform configure karna** | Jo software pehle se use karte ho, jaise CRM, help desk ya office suite, usmein AI feature | Tez; maujooda data aur permissions use hote hain | Flexibility kam; us platform se bandhe rehte ho |
| **Build karna** | Model APIs ya open models par apna system | Sabse zyada control aur differentiation | Engineers, evaluation aur chalu maintenance chahiye |

### Faisle ke factors

1. **Differentiation.** Kya ye aapke compete karne ka core hai? Jo aapko alag banata hai woh banao; commodity functions kharido.
2. **Data aur compliance.** Data kahan ja sakta hai? Kya vendor aapke security, privacy aur sector rules poore karta hai?
3. **Fit.** Kya ye aapki languages, document formats aur workflows sambhaalta hai?
4. **Total cost of ownership.** Do se teen saal mein licences ya usage, integration, aur ise chalaane waale log.
5. **Speed.** Aapko value kitni jaldi chahiye?
6. **Lock-in.** Baad mein switch karna kitna mushkil hoga?

### Hybrid normal hai

Zyadatar organisations aakhir mein mix karte hain: commodity tasks ke liye kharidte hain, jahan differentiate karte hain wahan banate hain, aur standard interfaces se options khule rakhte hain taaki baad mein models ya vendors badal sakein.

## Ek example se samjho

Hyderabad ki ek hospital chain chahti hai ki AI doctors ke notes aur lab reports se discharge summaries draft kare. Lambi shifts ke end mein doctors har summary par 20 se 30 minute lagate hain.

**Feasibility.** Product manager Sana 40 de-identified patient records jama karti hain. Capable model aur hospital ke summary template se woh 40 drafts banati hain. Teen senior doctors unhe rate karte hain:

| Rating | Drafts | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chhote edits ke saath usable | 31 | Zyadatar wording ke badlaav |
| Bade edits chahiye | 7 | Pehle ke notes ka context chhoot gaya |
| Unsafe | 2 | Dono mein medication ka badlaav chhoot gaya |

Nateeja: feasible, par sirf doctor ke mandatory review aur pharmacy record se automatic medication cross-check ke saath.

**Options:**

| Option | Sana ne kya paya | Nateeja |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product A kharidna | Unke hospital information system se judta nahi | Fit ki wajah se bahar |
| Product B kharidna | Data India ke bahar process karta hai; hospital ki legal team mana kar deti hai | Compliance ki wajah se bahar |
| Hospital system vendor ka add-on configure karna | Unke template ya medication checks ke hisaab se customise nahi ho sakta | Fit aur safety ki wajah se bahar |
| Build | Ek chhoti team unke maujooda cloud ke Indian region mein available model par banati hai, hospital system se jodti hai, medication cross-check aur doctor sign-off ke saath | Recommended |

**Cost.** Unka andaaza hai ki banane mein teen mahine lagenge, aur lagbhag 6,000 discharges per month ke volume par do saal ka total cost add-on ke licences se kam rahega. Discharge summaries ki quality is baat mein bhi matter karti hai ki hospital patient care mein kaise compete karta hai, isliye khud banane se asli differentiation milta hai.

Leadership build approve karti hai, saath mein ek saal baad market ka review, agar tab tak koi aisa product aa jaye jo unke rules poore kare.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Prototype chhod dena.** Feasibility ke andaaze aksar dono taraf galat nikalte hain.
- **Banane waalon se hi outputs judge karwana.** Domain experts se poochho.
- **Commodity features banana.** Jo aapko alag nahi banata, use kharido.
- **Data handling check kiye bina kharidna.** Compliance achhe products ko bhi bahar kar sakta hai.
- **Licence price ko kisi se compare hi na karna.** Banane mein bhi logon aur maintenance ka cost hota hai.
- **Lock-in bhool jaana.** Sign karne se pehle jaano ki aap nikloge kaise.

## Practice karo

SVPG ke four big risks dobara padho (neeche link hai), aur Hamel Husain ki field guide bhi.

1. **Prototype.** Ek AI task 20 real examples par try karo aur kisi domain jaanne waale se outputs rate karwao.
2. **Compare.** Do products aur ek platform feature dhoondho jo wahi task solve karne ka daava karte hon. Unhe fit, data handling, cost aur lock-in par compare karo.
3. **Recommend.** Do saal ke cost estimate ke saath ek page ki build, configure ya buy recommendation likho.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap expert se rate kiye gaye prototype results dikha sako aur chaar factors par apne build-versus-buy faisle ka bachaav kar sako.
