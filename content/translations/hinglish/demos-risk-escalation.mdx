## Ye kya hai

Teen aadatein jo engagement ko healthy rakhti hain:

- **Weekly demo**: real cases par working software ke 20 se 30 minute, slides nahi. Kya badla, users ne kya kaha, aage kya hai.
- **Risk log**: kya galat ho sakta hai, kitna likely aur serious hai, kiska owner kaun hai aur kya kiya ja raha hai, iski ek chhoti, jeeti-jaagti list.
- **Escalation**: jo blocker working level par time par solve nahi ho sakta, use jaldi aur options ke saath zyada authority waale insaan ke paas le jaana.

```flow
title: Har hafte
Demo | real software, real masked cases, ek failure dikhaya gaya -> Risk log review, 5 minute | risk, likelihood, impact, owner, action, due, status
Risk log review, 5 minute -> Status note | AMBER: progress, risks, asks
Status note -> Koi risk milestone se takrane waala hai, aur owner atka hai?
Koi risk milestone se takrane waala hai, aur owner atka hai? -[haan]-> Escalation | facts, asar, options, recommendation, tareekh tak decision
```

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek lender mein credit-summary pilot ke chhathe hafte ko follow karta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Demo ke rules.** Real (masked) data par real system dikhao. Kuch galat hua ho aur use kaise sambhaala gaya, woh bhi shaamil karo; isse credibility banti hai. Users aur business owner ko bulao, sirf IT contact ko nahi. End mein client se jo decisions chahiye aur agle hafte ka plan rakho. Call ke baad decisions likhit mein record karo.

**Risk log.** Har risk ke liye: ek saaf statement ("Rural branches ki bank statement photos padhne ke liye bahut blurry ho sakti hain"), likelihood aur impact (low, medium, high), owner (ek named insaan, aksar client side par), action aur due date, aur status. Weekly call mein ise chhota sa review karo. Ise pehle hafte se pre-mortem ke saath shuru karo: socho ki teen mahine mein project fail ho gaya hai aur kaaran list karo.

Typical AI engagement risks: data access mein deri, data quality umeed se kharab, key subject expert ka available na hona, security review late, users ka adopt na karna, model provider ke badlaav ya limits, scope ka badhna.

**Escalation.** Escalate karo jab koi risk milestone se takrane waala ho aur owner use solve na kar sake, ya jab working team se upar decision chahiye. Jaldi, facts aur options ke saath karo:

"Data access teen hafte late hai. Proof-of-concept gate 15 August se 5 September par khisak jaayega, jab tak hum pichhle saal ki archived files se shuru na karein, jo IT is hafte share kar sakta hai. Hum archive recommend karte hain. Head of operations se Friday tak decision chahiye."

Escalation blame nahi hai. Jo log involved hain unhe copy mein rakho, tone neutral rakho aur madad offer karo.

**Ek line mein status.** Har weekly meeting ke baad progress, risks aur asks par ek-ek vaakya ke saath green, amber ya red status bhejne se leaders bina meetings ke informed rehte hain.

**Risk log ka ek extract:**

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Owner | Action | Due | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Production ka security review schedule nahi hua | High | High | IT security lead | CIO ke office ko escalate kiya | Week 7 | Open |
| Do branches 50% se neeche adoption par | Medium | Medium | Regional operations heads | Branch visits | Week 7 | Open |
| Rural branches ki blurry statement photos | Medium | Medium | Product lead | Manual check ke liye flag; branches ko guidance | Week 8 | Mitigated |

**Pre-mortem chalana.** Pehle hafte core team ko bees minute ke liye bulao. Kaho: "Teen mahine baad project fail ho gaya hai. Likho kyun." Kaaran chupchaap collect karo, phir unhe group karo. Har group ek risk ban jaata hai jiska owner aur action hota hai. Jo teams ye karti hain, woh baad mein hone waale zyadatar risks ko tab hi pakad leti hain jab action lene ka time bacha hota hai.

**Aise demos jo bharosa banate hain.** Pichhle hafte se kya badla, usse shuru karo, real cases par dikhao, phir ek cheez dikhao jo galat hui aur system ya team ne use kaise sambhaala. End mein jo decisions chahiye aur agle hafte ka plan rakho, aur usi din decisions ka chhota likhit summary bhejo. Clients us team par kahin zyada bharosa karte hain jo apni failures dikhati hai, un se jo sirf polished paths dikhati hain.

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai lender pilot ka hafta 6. Aapka weekly update:

> **Status: Amber.** Median file time 27 minute (baseline 41, target 22); rework 5.4%; adoption 78%.
> **Demo:** naya side-by-side review screen; ek failure dikhayi, haath se edits waali ek salary slip jise system ne manual check ke liye sahi flag kiya.
> **Top risks:**
> 1. *High*: production environment ka security review schedule nahi hua; production ko ye hafta 12 tak chahiye. Owner: IT security lead. Action: aaj CIO ke office ko escalate kiya.
> 2. *Medium*: do branches 50% se neeche adoption par. Owner: regional operations heads. Action: agle hafte visits.
> **Decisions needed:** regional summary template ko Wednesday tak approve karo.

Security escalation se review hafta 9 ke liye schedule ho gaya, us se teen hafte pehle jab woh production gate ko block karta. Final review mein head of operations kehti hain ki weekly updates ki wajah se unhe timeline par bharosa tha.

| Week-6 update ka item | Nateeja |
| --- | --- |
| Status amber: file time 27 minute, target 22 | Extra meetings ke bina leaders informed rahe |
| Security review CIO ke office ko escalate | Week 9 ke liye schedule, production ko block karne se teen hafte pehle |
| Regional template decision Wednesday tak maanga | Time par approve hua |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Slide demos.** Software dikhao; slides problems chhupati hain.
- **Risk logs jo kabhi review nahi hote.** Ek baar likh kar bhula di gayi list kisi ko protect nahi karti.
- **Bina owners ke risks.** "Team" kisi cheez ki owner nahi hoti. Ek insaan ka naam likho.
- **Late escalation.** Jis din blocker lage usi din bolna shikayat hai, escalation nahi.
- **Escalation ko blame banana.** Facts, asar, options, ek saaf ask.

## Practice karo

Atlassian ke risk register aur pre-mortem ke pages padho.

1. Apne use case ke liye 20 minute ka pre-mortem chalao (akele ya dost ke saath) aur results ko owners aur actions ke saath risk log mein badlo.
2. Ek aise hafte ke liye weekly one-paragraph update likho jisme ek risk red ho gaya ho.
3. Us risk ke liye do options aur ek recommended decision ke saath escalation note likho.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap named owners wala risk log aur ek aisa escalation note dikha sako jis par ek busy executive do minute mein action le sake.
