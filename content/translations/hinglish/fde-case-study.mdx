## Ye kya hai

Ye Xplaind ke liye likha gaya ek worked engagement hai, jo is path ke lessons use karta hai. Client ek fictional mid-sized non-banking lender hai, Chennai mein, jiske Tamil Nadu aur Karnataka mein 180 credit officers hain. Engagement lagbhag chhe mahine chalta hai. Ise apne capstone write-up ke model ki tarah padho.

| Hafte | Stage | Kya hua |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Discovery aur KPI | "Ek chatbot" credit-file assistant ban gaya; baseline 41 min, 7% rework |
| 3-6 | Proof of concept | 300 files, ek golden set; image clean-up ke baad 93% se 96.4%; gate pass |
| 6-8 | Engineering | Azure Central India, gateway aur fallback, SSO, branch access, audit, CI mein evals |
| 7-16 | Pilot | 20 officers, weekly demos, risk log; 24 min, 5.1% rework, 88% adoption |
| 17-26 | Production aur handover | Memo approve, 3 rollout waves, ₹9 lakh mahina, reverse shadowing |

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek fictional mid-sized lender, jiske 180 credit officers hain, mein poore engagement ko follow karta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

Engagement is path ke stages follow karta hai, har ek apne evidence ke saath.

**1. Pehli call aur discovery (hafta 1 se 2).** Maang thi "customers ke liye chatbot". Interviews aur shadowing ne asli dard dikhaya: credit officers har loan file par lagbhag 41 minute bank statements, salary slips aur bureau reports padhne, figures dobara type karne aur summary likhne mein lagate the, aur 7% files galtiyon ke liye credit committee se wapas aati thi. Mahine mein 6,000 files. Play-back ne project ko reframe kiya, aur business owner, head of operations, maan gaye.

**2. KPI aur scope (hafta 2).** Primary KPI: har file par median officer time, baseline 41 minute, target 22. Guardrail: rework 7% se zyada nahi. Adoption track kiya gaya. Scope of work mein bataya gaya ki kya bahar hai: credit decisions, customer-facing features aur non-English documents.

**3. Proof of concept (hafta 3 se 6).** 300 masked historical files, do senior officers ke saath label kiya golden set. Structured-output model call aur Pydantic validation se extraction; har figure ke liye ek citation. Pehla result: critical fields par 93%, 95% gate se neeche, zyadatar parchhai wali phone photos ki wajah se. Ek image clean-up step aur low-confidence flag ne use 96.4% tak pahunchaya. Gate pass.

**4. Pilot ke liye engineering (hafta 6 se 8).** Lender ki risk team ne Central India mein Azure approve kiya tha, isliye models us tenancy ke through, ek gateway ke peeche aur ek fallback ke saath use hue. Containers mein FastAPI service CI/CD ke saath, pipeline mein evals, masking ke saath tracing, har view aur edit ke liye audit logs, Entra ID se sign-on, aur har officer ki branch ke hisaab se document access filter. Logs mein personal data mask hua; erasure derived data tak bhi gaya.

**5. Pilot (hafta 7 se 16).** Do branches mein 20 officers. Weekly demos aur ek risk log. Ek branch mein blame ke darr se adoption ruk gaya; written accountability aur side-by-side review screen se theek hua. Teen change requests ko size kiya gaya: ek swap ke roop mein accept hua, ek phase 2 ke liye defer hua, ek decline hua (credit decisions). Result: 24 minute median, 5.1% rework, 88% adoption.

**6. Production aur handover (hafta 17 se 26).** CEO aur CFO ko ek one-page memo ne full rollout ki approval dilayi. Branches mein teen waves; caching aur tiering se model costs ghatkar all-in lagbhag ₹9 lakh mahina. Runbooks, decision records aur reverse shadowing ke saath chaar hafte ka handover.

**Stage, decision aur evidence:**

| Stage | Mukhya decision | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | Chatbot se credit files par reframe | Interviews, shadowing, mahine mein 6,000 files |
| Scope | Credit decisions aur customer features bahar | Signed scope of work |
| POC | Image clean-up aur confidence flags jode | Critical fields par 93% se 96.4% |
| Engineering | Approved Azure tenancy use ki | Risk team ki pehle ki approval |
| Pilot | Reviews ke liye written accountability | Ruki hui branch mein adoption wapas aaya |
| Change requests | Ek swap, ek defer, ek decline | Size kiye hue decisions log hue |
| Production | Caching aur tiering | All-in lagbhag ₹9 lakh mahina |
| Handover | Reverse shadowing | Client engineers ne har task kiya |

**Apna case study likhna.** Do ya teen page mein rakho. Problem aur uske numbers se shuru karo. Table ki tarah har stage ke saath ek decision aur uska evidence dikhao. Ek honest failure aur aapne kya badla, woh shaamil karo. Measured results aur ek lesson par khatam karo. Reviewers aur interviewers bahut portfolios padhte hain; jo yaad rehte hain unmein real numbers, real trade-offs aur ek saaf "main alag kya karta" hota hai.

**Imagined client ko believable banana.** Process ko aise kisi cheez par based karo jo aap observe ya padh sakte ho, jaise published hospital billing guides ya kisi dost ki job. Apni assumptions batao aur har number kahan se aaya. POC ko real ya public data par banao, aur results apne golden set se report karo, andaaze se nahi.

## Ek example se samjho

Portfolio write-up ya interview mein FDE ise do minute ke andar kaise summarise kar sakta hai:

"Chennai ke ek lender ne customer chatbot maanga. Discovery ne dikhaya ki asli cost har loan par 41 minute ka manual document work tha, mahine mein 6,000 loans, 7% rework ke saath. Maine ek credit-file assistant propose kiya aur scope kiya: citations ke saath extraction aur ek draft summary, jisme officers har figure review karte hain. POC pehle accuracy gate se chuka phone photos ki wajah se; image clean-up aur confidence flags ne use theek kiya. Maine ise unke Azure tenancy mein sign-on, branch-level access, audit logs aur CI mein evals ke saath deploy kiya. 10 hafte ke pilot mein, har file ka time 24 minute hua, rework 5.1% tak gira aur ek branch ki accountability concern theek karne ke baad adoption 88% pahunch gaya. Ye 180 officers tak rollout hua aur ab client ki team ise chalati hai. Main alag kya karta: security review hafta 6 ki jagah hafta 1 mein shuru karta."

Structure dekho: numbers ke saath problem, aapke decisions aur kyun, ek cheez jo galat hui aur aapne kaise sambhaali, measured result aur ek lesson.

## Aam galtiyaan

Ye woh galtiyaan hain jinse ye engagement bacha, aur jo real engagements ko dubo deti hain:

- **Jo chaahiye tha uski jagah jo maanga gaya woh banana.** Discovery ne project badal diya.
- **Model metrics ko success maanna.** Officer time aur rework hi budget jeetne waale measures the.
- **Security aur sign-on ko pilot ke baad par chhodna.** Yahan woh pilot ke dauran chale, sirf ek late review ko escalate karna pada.
- **Chupchaap scope badhna.** Har request ek sized decision ban gayi.
- **Handover ki jagah ek handover document.** Client ke engineers ne har task khud kiya.

## Practice karo

Business context ke liye FDE role par a16z ka essay padho.

Is lesson ka check capstone write-up hai. Kisi Indian industry mein jo aap jaante ho (hospital chain, logistics company, state department, retailer) ek public dataset aur ek imagined client chuno. Kam se kam POC real mein banao.
