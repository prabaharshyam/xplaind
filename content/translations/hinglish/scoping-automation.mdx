## Ye kya hai

"Kya aap WhatsApp se aane waale hamaare orders automate kar sakte ho?" Sunne mein ek sawaal lagta hai. Ismein darjanon sawaal chhupe hain. Voice notes bhi? List mein na ho aisa product aaye toh? Result kaun check karega? Messages kaun dekh sakta hai? Chhah mahine baad ise kaun theek karega? Ek **automation scope** kaam shuru hone se pehle in sabka jawaab deta hai, taaki dono taraf ko pata ho ki unhe kya mil raha hai aur wo kis cheez ke paise de rahe hain.

Scope ek chhota written agreement hai, aamtaur par ek-do page ka, technical design nahi: owner har line padhkar haan ya na keh sake. Iske gyaarah hisse hain:

| Hissa | Kis sawaal ka jawaab deta hai |
| --- | --- |
| **Process aur boundaries** | Kahaan shuru hota hai, aur kahaan khatam? |
| **Inputs aur volumes** | Kya aata hai, kin formats mein, din mein kitne? |
| **Steps** | System kya karta hai, aur log ab bhi kya karte hain? |
| **Exceptions** | Jo cases system handle nahi kar sakta, unka kya hoga? |
| **Systems aur access** | Kaun-se accounts chahiye, kisse, kab tak? |
| **Data aur risks** | Kaun-sa personal data kahaan jaata hai? Kya galat ho sakta hai? |
| **Assumptions** | Price tika rahe, iske liye kya sach rehna chahiye? |
| **Success measures** | Kya behtar hoga, kis baseline ke muqaable? |
| **Acceptance aur sign-off** | Kaun-sa test, kis data par, aur "accepted" kaun kahega? |
| **Support aur changes** | Fixes kitne samay tak free hain, aur change kise maana jaayega? |
| **Price, payments aur timeline** | Kitna, kab pay hoga, kin dates tak? |

**In scope** wo kaam hai jiska aap vaada karte ho. **Out of scope** wo kaam hai jiske baare mein aapne likhkar kaha hai ki nahi karoge. **Change request** scope badalne ka ek written proposal hai, jismein time aur price par uska asar likha ho.

```flow
Discovery | process map, samples -> Draft scope -[client reads]-> Questions, edits
Questions, edits -> Draft scope
Draft scope -> Signed scope | in, out, acceptance, support, price
Signed scope -> Build -> Acceptance test -> Handover -> Support period -> Change requests
```

Ye lesson Indore ki ek freelance automation specialist Ritika ke saath chalta hai. Ek distributor ne unse "WhatsApp se aane waale orders automate karne" ko kaha hai. Lesson ke ant tak aap aisa scope likh paoge jis par owner sign kare, aise price ke saath jise aap defend kar sako.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Process map se shuru karo

Discovery waala map use karo. Start aur end ko concrete events ke roop mein likho, jaise neeche worked example mein hai. Phir har step ko **automated**, **assisted** (system draft karta hai, insaan approve karta hai) ya **manual** mark karo. Map aur ye decisions milkar scope bante hain.

Pehle version ke liye ek kaam ka default: agar kisi step ko undo karna mushkil hai, toh use automated nahi, assisted rakho. Baad mein, jab data dikha de ki ye safe hai, human check hataana aasaan hai; kisi mehngi galti ke baad use jodna aasaan nahi.

### Samples ikattha karo aur gino

Normal dinon ke lagbhag 40 real inputs maango (20 se 50 chal jaate hain), messy waale bhi, client ke pasandeeda examples nahi. Phir gino:

| Input type | 40 samples mein ginti | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typed text orders | 22 | In scope |
| Haath se likhi lists ki photos | 12 | In scope, staff review ke saath |
| Voice notes | 5 | Abhi ke liye out of scope; phase 2 |
| Doosre apps se forward kiye PDFs | 1 | Exception ke roop mein staff ko bheja jaayega |

Is sample size par ginti mota-mota andaaza hi hai. 40 mein baarah photos bataati hain ki kaam aur risk mein photos ka bada hissa hai; ek PDF bas itna batata hai ki PDFs aate hain, ye nahi ki kitne aam hain. Isliye staff se bhi poochho: "Aur kya aata hai, chaahe kabhi-kabhaar hi?"

Har exception ke liye chuno: use automatically handle karna hai, kisi insaan ko bhejna hai, ya baahar rakhna hai, aur jo chuna wo likh lo. "Insaan ko bhejo" ke liye naam waali ek queue aur naam waala ek insaan chahiye.

Samples mein asli customers ki details hoti hain: unhe aise folder mein rakho jo client ke control mein ho, aur ant mein apni copies delete kar do.

### Human checkpoints par sahmati banao

Payments, customers ko jaane waale messages aur deletions ke liye aamtaur par approval chahiye. Batao kaun approve karega, kis tool mein. Jis checkpoint ke liye kisi ke paas samay nahi, wo ek hafte ke andar skip hone lagega, isliye use tez banao: original message ke bagal mein extracted result, aur confirm karne ke liye ek button.

### Access ko dependency banao

Har system, uske liye zaroori account, use kaun dega aur kab tak, sab list karo. Technical problems se zyaada aksar access mein deri projects ko rokti hai.

WhatsApp ek aam example hai. Automation ke liye **WhatsApp Business Platform** chahiye, jo Meta ka API hai, aamtaur par kisi provider ke zariye; phone par free WhatsApp Business app kaafi nahi hai. Meta pehle business se business verification poora karne ko keh sakta hai. Providers bataate hain ki review mein kuch ghanton se lekar lagbhag do hafte lagte hain, kabhi-kabhi usse zyaada, aur koi guaranteed time nahi hai, isliye client ko ise pehle hafte mein hi shuru karna chahiye. Provider se ye bhi poochho ki number API se judne ke baad kya staff usi number par phone app chalaata reh sakta hai; Meta kuch cases mein, conditions ke saath, iski ijaazat deta hai.

Scope mein likho: "Agar access der se mila, toh go-live utne hi din aage khiskega."

### Data aur risks likho

Zyaadatar automations personal data move karte hain: naam, phone numbers, order history. India mein, Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) ise cover karta hai. Jo business tay karta hai ki data kis liye use hoga, wo **data fiduciary** hai. Aap, messaging provider aur AI provider uski taraf se data process karte ho, **data processors** ke roop mein, aur Act kehta hai ki fiduciary kisi processor ko sirf valid contract ke tahat use kar sakta hai. DPDP Rules November 2025 mein notify hue aur unki zyaadatar duties May 2027 se laagu hongi, isliye clients abhi se iske baare mein poochh rahe hain. Ye legal advice nahi hai; kisi bhi asaamaanya cheez ke liye client ko lawyer se poochhna chahiye.

Scope mein, seedhe shabdon mein jawaab do:

- system mein kaun-sa personal data aata hai, aur kisse;
- wo kahaan store aur process hota hai, kaun-sa AI provider aur kaun-sa region bhi;
- raw messages aur photos delete hone se pehle kitne samay tak rakhe jaate hain;
- unhe kaun dekh sakta hai;
- kya AI provider business API data par train karta hai (uski maujooda terms check karo aur date note karo).

Top teen se paanch risks list karo, har ek ke saath ek mitigation aur ek owner.

### Assumptions saaf likho

Assumption wo cheez hai jis par aap bharosa kar rahe ho par check nahi ki, jaise "client product list ko update rakhega" ya "billing import format nahi badlega". Agar koi assumption fail hua, toh extra kaam change request hai. Isse fixed price fair rehta hai.

### Pehle baseline measure karo, phir targets rakho

Jo saving aapne measure nahi ki, uska daava nahi kar sakte. Kuch normal dinon tak maujooda process ka time naapo aur aaj ki errors gino, phir unke muqaable targets rakho (ROI measure karne waala lesson inhe rupayon mein badalta hai).

### Acceptance ko test ke roop mein define karo

"System achha kaam karta hai" test nahi hai. Test karne laayak acceptance ke paanch hisse hote hain:

1. **Data:** kaun-se items, kis period ke, kisne chune. Client ko chunna chahiye, sirf in-scope items mein se.
2. **Answer key:** har item ka sahi result, test se pehle client ki taraf se taiyaar.
3. **Correct ki definition:** jaise, product code aur quantity dono billed invoice se match karein.
4. **Threshold:** jo number pahunchna hai.
5. **Sign-off:** accept karne ka adhikaar rakhne waala insaan, aur uske paas reply ke liye kitna samay hai.

Do gotchas. Pehla, line accuracy order accuracy nahi hai. Agar 90% lines sahi hain, ek order mein paanch lines hain aur errors baraabar phaili hain, toh sirf lagbhag 59% orders poori tarah sahi honge (0.9 ki power 5 lagbhag 0.59 hai). Batao ki aap kise measure kar rahe ho, aur human review ke liye uska kya matlab hai. Doosra, office manager test chala sakta hai, par accept sirf owner kar sakta hai. Owner ka naam likho aur reply window jodo, jaise: "Agar paanch working days ke andar failures ki koi written list nahi aati, toh test pass maana jaayega."

### Support aur changes

Support period batao, jaise go-live ke baad 30 din ke fixes, aur fix aur change ke beech lakeer kheencho:

- **Fix:** system wo nahi kar raha jo scope mein likha hai. Support ke dauraan free.
- **Change:** kuch bhi naya ya alag, jaise naya input format, field ya system, ya client ki taraf ka koi badlaav jaise billing software upgrade. Iska price change request ke roop mein lagega.

Project ke beech mein bina time ya price badle judne waali requests **scope creep** hain. Unhe absorb mat karo, mana bhi mat karo: har ek ko chhote quote ke saath phase 2 list par daalo.

### Effort ka estimate lagao

Kaam ko aise tasks mein todo jinka estimate aadhe-aadhe din mein laga sako, aur unhe jodo. Jo aapne nahi dekha uske liye contingency jodo, jaise 15 se 25%, samples kam the toh aur zyaada, aur apne day rate se multiply karo. Agar kisi hisse ka estimate bilkul nahi laga sakte, toh wo fixed price ke liye ready nahi hai: pehle ek chhota paid prototype becho.

### Pricing model aur payment milestones chuno

| Model | Kab sabse sahi | Surprises ka bojh kaun uthaata hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed price | Scope saaf hai aur samples dekh liye hain | Aap |
| Time and materials | Scope saaf nahi ya exploratory hai | Client |
| Monthly retainer | Support ke baad maintenance aur chhote changes | Dono, hours ki monthly cap ke andar |

Pehle client ke liye ek samajhdaar default hai milestones mein pay hone waala fixed price, jaise sign karte samay 40%, acceptance par 40% aur support khatam hone par 20%. Advance commitment dikhaata hai aur aapke shuruaati dinon ka kharch nikaalta hai, acceptance par payment client ko bachaata hai, aur aakhri hissa support ke dauraan aapko responsive rakhta hai. Batao ki prices mein GST shaamil hai ya nahi.

Monthly running costs bhi scope mein daalo (messaging provider, AI usage, hosting), jahaan ho sake client seedhe pay kare.

## Ek example se samjho

Sanjay Jain ke Indore waale distributor par Ritika ki discovery mein pata chalta hai: 300 retailers se din mein lagbhag 150 orders, mahine ke 26 working days. Ye WhatsApp par typed text, voice notes, Hindi aur English mein haath se likhi lists ki photos, aur kabhi-kabhaar doosre app se forward kiye PDF ke roop mein aate hain. Do staff har order ko aise billing software mein type karte hain jo CSV files import karta hai. Unki do hafte ki baseline: har order par lagbhag 3 minute, aur 2% orders galat quantity ke saath ship hote hain.

### Jo scope wo bhejti hain

**WhatsApp order entry, version 1, 2 December 2026**

- **Process:** tab shuru hota hai jab koi order message business WhatsApp number par pahunchta hai; tab khatam hota hai jab din ke confirmed orders ek CSV file mein aa jaate hain jise billing software import karta hai.
- **In:** business number par aane waale text aur photo orders; AI se retailer, products aur quantities extract karna, product list se match karke; ek review screen jahaan staff har order confirm ya correct karta hai; ek daily CSV export.
- **Out:** voice notes (phase 2), payments aur payment reminders, stock checks, retailers ko automatic replies, aur billing software mein koi bhi badlaav.
- **Exceptions:** anjaan products ya retailers, na padhi ja sakne waali photos aur forward kiye PDFs original message ke saath review queue mein jaate hain.
- **Access (client dega):** kisi provider ke zariye WhatsApp Business Platform account, Meta business verification 4 December tak shuru aur 14 December tak ready; codes ke saath product aur retailer lists, 4 December tak; ek CSV file jise billing software ne accept kiya ho.
- **Data:** retailers ke naam, phone numbers aur orders provider, automation server (India mein hosted) aur AI provider ke API se hokar jaate hain. Raw messages aur photos 90 din baad delete hote hain. Sirf do order staff aur owner log in kar sakte hain. Dono providers ke saath processor terms go-live se pehle confirm hongi.
- **Risks:** verification mein deri (owner: client; tab tak test number par build karna). Photos padhi nahi jaatin (owner: Ritika; unhe review mein bhejna aur rate har hafte report karna). Staff check karna band kar deta hai (owner: Sanjay Jain; har order par confirm click zaroori, saath mein weekly spot check).
- **Assumptions:** client product list update rakhega; CSV format nahi badlega; volumes din mein 250 orders se kam rahenge.
- **Success:** entry time har order par lagbhag 3 minute se ghatkar lagbhag 1 minute; galat quantities aaj ke 2% se zyaada nahi, lakshya 1%.
- **Acceptance:** October 2026 ke 200 in-scope text aur photo orders, client ke chune hue. Client ka accountant billed invoices se answer key 7 December tak taiyaar karega. Ek line tab correct hai jab product code aur quantity dono invoice se match karein. Pass: staff review se pehle kam se kam 90% lines correct, aur har exception review queue mein. Sign-off: Sanjay Jain, likhit mein, test ke paanch working days ke andar.
- **Support:** go-live ke baad 30 din ke fixes. Naye formats, fields ya systems, aur client ki taraf ke changes, ₹7,500 prati din par change requests hain.
- **Price aur timeline:** ₹90,000 fixed, GST alag, sign karte samay ₹36,000, acceptance par ₹36,000 aur support khatam hone par ₹18,000. Lagbhag ₹9,000 mahine ke running costs client dega. Acceptance test 28 December waale hafte mein; agar WhatsApp access 14 December tak ready ho toh go-live 4 January 2027 ko, nahi toh jitni deri utna baad mein.

### Do hafte baad

Owner retailers ko automatic payment reminders maangte hain. Scope mein ye out likhe hain, isliye Ritika inhe quote ke saath phase 2 list par daalti hain, aur samjhaati hain ki ye chhota kaam kyun nahi hai. Jis retailer ne pichhle 24 ghanton mein message nahi kiya, use reminder bhejne ke liye Meta-approved template use karna hoga, aur har delivered reminder ka charge lagta hai. Jaise, 300 retailers × 4 reminders yaani mahine mein 1,200 utility messages; lagbhag ₹0.115 prati message par (India ke liye third-party reports yahi figure bataati hain, November 2026 mein check kiya; Meta ke rate card par confirm karo), ye GST se pehle lagbhag ₹138 hai. Paisa kam hai; kaam kam nahi: template approval, retailers ki consent, aur aisi dukaan ko reminder bhejne ka risk jisne pay kar diya hai. Ye baatcheet do minute ki hai.

### Go-live ke teen hafte baad

Do requests aati hain. Jab kisi order mein 20 se zyaada items hote hain, CSV lines chhod deti hai: system scope tod raha hai, isliye ye free fix hai. Upgrade hua billing software naye columns maangta hai: ye client ki taraf ka change hai, isliye aadhe din ka change request.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Samples ke bina scope,** ya achhe dikhne ke liye chune samples ke saath.
- **Kya baahar hai, is par chuppi.** Bina likhe exclusions free kaam ban jaate hain.
- **Access ko baad mein sochna.** Akele Meta verification build ke kuch hisson se zyaada lamba khinch sakta hai.
- **Answer key ya named signer ke bina acceptance.** Test ek raay ban jaata hai.
- **Lines measure karna aur orders ka vaada karna.** 90% lines sahi hone par bhi, kai orders ko abhi bhi theek karna padta hai.
- **Data section nahi.** Pehla privacy sawaal hi go-live rok deta hai.
- **Fix aur change ke beech koi lakeer nahi.** Har request "ye toota hua hai" bankar aati hai.
- **Running costs chhupaana,** ya aisa scope likhna jise owner padh na sake.

## Practice karo

Neeche linked Atlassian ka project scope waala guide aur GOV.UK ka user stories waala guide padho.

1. **Samples.** Apne process map ke kisi idea ke liye 20 se 40 real ya realistic inputs ikattha karo aur gino, har type ke liye ek decision ke saath.
2. **Scope.** Saare gyaarah hisse ek-do page mein seedhe shabdon mein likho, aur Ritika ke scope se compare karo.
3. **Acceptance.** Paanch hisson waala test likho aur kisi aur se poochho ki wo ise kaise chalaayenge. Agar unke man mein koi sawaal hai, toh ye abhi poora nahi hua.
4. **Price.** Contingency ke saath ek effort table aur ek running-cost table banao, har price par date daalkar.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** apna scope business owner ko de sako aur wo likhkar sahmat hon ki kya in hai, kya out hai, success kaise measure hoga aur iski cost kitni hogi.
