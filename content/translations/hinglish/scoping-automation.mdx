## Ye kya hai

"Kya aap hamare WhatsApp ke orders automate kar sakte ho?" sunne mein ek sawaal lagta hai. Asal mein iske peeche darjanon sawaal chhupe hain: kaunse orders? Voice notes bhi? Anjaan products ka kya? Approve kaun karega? Chhe mahine baad theek kaun karega? **Automation scope** in sabka jawab kaam shuru hone se pehle de deta hai, taaki dono taraf ko pata ho ki kya mil raha hai.

Automation scope ek chhota agreement hota hai, aksar ek panne ka:

| Section | Kya batata hai | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Process aur seemayein** | Kahan se shuru (trigger) aur kahan khatam (outcome) | Order message aane se lekar billing ke liye CSV taiyaar hone tak |
| **Inputs** | Formats, sources, volumes | WhatsApp par text aur photos, roz kareeb 150 |
| **Automated aur manual steps** | Automation kya karta hai aur insaan kya karte rahenge | AI extract karta hai; staff har order confirm karta hai |
| **Exceptions** | Ajeeb cases mein kya hoga | Anjaan products original message ke saath staff ke paas jaate hain |
| **Systems aur access** | Apps, accounts, permissions, kaun dega aur kab tak | WhatsApp Business account, client 10 December tak set up karega |
| **Success measures** | Kya behtar hoga, baseline ke saath | Entry time 3 minute se ghatkar 1 minute se kam |
| **Acceptance test** | Dono taraf kaise check karein ki ye chal raha hai | 200 purane orders dobara chalaye; 90% lines match hui |
| **Ownership aur support** | Maintain kaun karega, support ki avadhi, change kise maana jaayega | 30 din ki fixes; changes tay day rate par |
| **Price aur timeline** | Client ke kaam ke liye | Fixed fee aur har mahine ke running costs |

```flow
Discovery | process map, samples -> Draft scope -[client padhta hai]-> Questions, edits
Questions, edits -> Draft scope
Draft scope -> Signed scope | in, out, acceptance, support, price
Signed scope -> Build -> Acceptance test -> Handover -> Support period -> Change requests
```

Is lesson mein hum Indore ke ek freelance automation specialist ke saath chalenge, jise ek distributor ne "WhatsApp se orders automate karne" ko kaha hai. Lesson ke end tak aap ek panne ka aisa scope likh paoge jis par koi business owner likhit mein haan keh sake.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Process map se shuru karo

Boundaries tay karne ke liye discovery wala map use karo. Har step ko **automated**, **assisted** (AI draft kare, insaan approve kare) ya **manual** mark karo. Scope yaani map aur us par liye gaye faisle.

### Inputs aur exceptions imaandaari se list karo

Kuch bhi tay karne se pehle **20 se 50 asli samples** maango. Formats aur exceptions gino:

| Input ka type | Samples mein hissa | Faisla |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typed text orders | 55% | Scope mein |
| Haath se likhi lists ki photos | 30% | Scope mein, staff review ke saath |
| Voice notes | 12% | Abhi scope se bahar; phase 2 |
| Doosre apps se forward hue PDFs | 3% | Exception ki tarah staff ko bheje jaayenge |

Har exception ke liye tay karo: use handle karo, kisi insaan ko bhejo, ya bahar rakho. Faisla likh lo.

### Human checkpoints tay karo

Kaunse steps mein approval chahiye? Payments, customers ko messages aur deletions mein aamtaur par chahiye. Approve kaun karega, aur kis tool mein? Jis checkpoint ke liye kisi ke paas samay nahi, use skip kar diya jaayega.

### Access ko dependency banao

Har system list karo, kis tarah ka account chahiye aur access kab tak milna hai. Technical dikkaton se zyada projects access ki deri se rukte hain. Access late ho, toh timeline khisakti hai, aur scope mein ye likha hona chahiye.

### Acceptance ko test ki tarah define karo

Vague: "system achha chalta hai". Testable: "Pichhle mahine ke 100 asli invoices mein se kam se kam 85 bina kisi insaani haath ke process hon aur saari fields accountant ki entries se match karein; har failure review queue mein dikhe." Aapke alawa koi aur bhi ise chala sake.

### Support aur changes

Support ki avadhi likho, jaise go-live ke baad 30 din ki fixes. **Change request** define karo: naya supplier format, naya system, naya feature. Ye bhi likho ki changes ki price kaise tay hogi. Jo requests project ke beech mein bina time ya price badle aa jaayein, woh **scope creep** hain; unhe quote ke saath phase 2 ki list mein daalo.

### Pricing models

| Model | Kab sahi hai | Risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fixed price | Scope saaf ho aur samples dekhe ja chuke hon | Achanak aayi dikkatein aap uthate ho |
| Time and materials | Scope unclear ya exploratory ho | Achanak aayi dikkatein client uthata hai |
| Monthly retainer | Lagaatar maintenance aur chhote changes | Saaf monthly limit chahiye |

Tool aur AI usage ke costs hamesha shaamil karo, aur likho ki kaun bharega.

## Ek example se samjho

Indore ke ek freelance automation specialist se ek distributor "WhatsApp se orders automate karne" ko kehta hai.

**Discovery** mein pata chalta hai ki 300 retailers se roz kareeb 150 orders aate hain, WhatsApp par text, voice notes aur haath se likhi lists ki photos ke roop mein, Hindi aur English mein. Staff unhe billing software mein type karta hai, jo CSV files import kar sakta hai.

**Ek panne ka scope:**

- **Andar:** business WhatsApp number se aaye text aur photo orders; retailer, products aur quantities ka AI extraction, jo product list se match ho; ek review screen jahan staff har order confirm kare; billing software ke liye roz ka CSV export.
- **Bahar:** voice notes (phase 2), payments, stock checks aur retailers ko automatic replies.
- **Exceptions:** Anjaan products ya retailers original message ke saath staff ke paas jaate hain.
- **Access:** Kisi provider ke zariye WhatsApp Business Platform account, jise client 10 December tak set up kare; product aur retailer lists sheets ke roop mein.
- **Success:** Har order ki entry ka time kareeb 3 minute se ghatkar 1 minute se kam; bheji gayi quantities mein errors aaj se zyada nahi.
- **Acceptance:** 200 purane orders dobara chalaye jaayein; staff review se pehle kam se kam 90% lines sahi match hon.
- **Support:** 30 din ki fixes; naye formats ya features change request ki tarah, tay day rate par.
- **Price:** fixed fee, aur WhatsApp provider aur AI usage ke monthly running costs, jo client bharega.

**Do hafte baad** owner kehta hai ki retailers ko automatic payment reminders bhi jod do. Idea achha hai, par scope mein nahi hai. Woh apni quote ke saath phase 2 ki list mein jaata hai. Chunki scope mein saaf likha tha ki kya bahar hai, baat do minute mein nipat jaati hai, behes nahi banti.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Samples ke bina scope tay karna.** Asli inputs mein exceptions dikhte hain.
- **Kya bahar hai, is par chup rehna.** Jo likha nahi, woh muft ka extra kaam ban jaata hai.
- **Access ko baad ki baat samajhna.** Deri ki sabse aam wajahon mein se ek yahi hai.
- **Vague acceptance.** Asli data par numbers wala test define karo.
- **Support ki shartein na likhna.** Aap hamesha ke liye bina paise ke support ban jaate ho.
- **Running costs chhupana.** Pehla provider bill aane par client ko lagta hai ki use dhokhe mein rakha gaya.

## Practice karo

Neeche link kiya Atlassian ka project scope guide aur GOV.UK ka user stories guide padho.

1. **Samples.** Apne process map se ek automation idea lo aur kam se kam 20 asli ya asli jaise input samples ikattha karo.
2. **Scope.** Upar ke har section ke saath ek panne ka scope likho.
3. **Acceptance.** Acceptance test aise likho ki koi aur use chala sake.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap apna scope business owner ko do aur woh likhit mein maan le ki kya andar hai, kya bahar hai aur success kaise naapi jaayegi.
