## Ye kya hai

**Workplace AI policy** employees ko batati hai ki kaam par AI tools kaise use kar sakte hain. Ye isliye hoti hai kyunki AI assistants use karna aasaan hai aur galat use karna bhi aasaan: customer phone numbers ki ek spreadsheet paste kar do, aur ek hi jhatke mein kanoon, client contract aur company ka bharosa teeno toot sakte hain.

Achhi policies chhoti aur practical hoti hain. Aam taur par ye saat cheezein cover karti hain:

| Section | Ye kya kehta hai | Example rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Approved tools** | Kaunse assistants aur features allowed hain | "Company-licensed assistant use karo; kaam ke liye personal accounts allowed nahi hain" |
| **Data rules** | AI tools mein kya jaa sakta hai aur kya nahi | "Customer ka personal data CRM ke built-in AI ke bahar nahi" |
| **Use cases** | Kahan AI encouraged hai aur kahan restricted | "Drafting aur summarising ke liye encouraged; final hiring decisions ke liye nahi" |
| **Review aur accuracy** | AI output kaun check karta hai, aur zimmedaar kaun hai | "Har figure aap check karte ho; jo bhejte ho woh aapki zimmedaari hai" |
| **Disclosure** | Logon ko kab batana hai ki AI use hua | "Report mostly AI se draft hui ho toh clients ko batao" |
| **Intellectual property** | AI-assisted kaam kiska hai, aur copying se kaise bachna hai | "Dusron ka copyrighted kaam rewrite karne ke liye paste mat karo" |
| **Reporting** | Galti ya problem kaise report karni hai | "24 ghante ke andar AI committee ko email karo" |

Ye lesson Karthik ke saath chalta hai, jo Chennai ki ek manufacturer mein sales mein kaam karta hai, aur apni company ke rules ke andar rehkar AI se proposal banata hai. End tak aap jaan jaoge ki achhi policy mein kya hota hai, apna data kaise sort karna hai, aur jab company ki koi policy hi na ho tab kya karna hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Companies ko parwaah kyun hoti hai

Jab aap consumer AI tool mein type karte ho, toh text store ho sakta hai, aur settings aur terms ke hisaab se service behtar karne mein use bhi ho sakta hai. Isse teen risk bante hain:

1. **Legal.** Unapproved tool mein customer ka personal data daalna India ke DPDP Act ko tod sakta hai, jo personal data ke use aur sharing ko limit karta hai.
2. **Contractual.** Client contracts aksar unki information third parties ke saath share karna mana karte hain.
3. **Confidentiality.** Strategy, unreleased financial results aur source code leak ho sakte hain.

Company-licensed enterprise versions ke saath aam taur par commitments aate hain: aapka data training ke liye use nahi hota, ek pata hui jagah par store hota hai, aur access control mein rehta hai.

```flow
Company data ke saath ek kaam -> Kaunsa tool?
Kaunsa tool? -[personal free account]-> Ruko | data se models train ho sakte hain, admin control nahi
Kaunsa tool? -[company-licensed tool]-> Aage badho | approved kaam, data training mein use nahi hota
```

| | Personal free account | Company-licensed tool |
|---|---|---|
| Terms | Aapne bina padhe click kar diye | Company ka sign kiya hua contract |
| Aapka data | Models train karne mein kaam aa sakta hai | Training mein use nahi hota |
| Control | Koi admin control nahi | Admin controls aur audit logs |
| Kuch leak ho jaaye toh | Company madad nahi kar sakti | Security team ko pata hai data kahan jaata hai |
| Kahan use karein | Work data ke liye nahi | Approved kaam ke liye |

### Apne data ko chaar categories mein baanto

Ye tay karne ka aasaan tareeka ki kya kahan jaa sakta hai:

| Category | Examples | Thumb rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | Published brochures, company website, public reports | Aam taur par kisi bhi tool mein theek |
| **Internal** | Roz ke internal documents, process notes, templates | Sirf approved tools |
| **Confidential** | Client contracts, unreleased results, strategy, pricing | Sirf approved tools, kabhi-kabhi bilkul nahi |
| **Personal aur sensitive** | Contact details ke saath customer ya employee ke naam, health, salaries, Aadhaar ya PAN numbers, bank details | Sabse sakht rules; aksar sirf kuch khaas approved systems mein |

Shak ho toh document ko ek category aur sakht maan lo.

### Jo zaroori nahi hai use hata do

Aksar sensitive hissa hataye bina bhi kaam ho jaata hai. Naam ki jagah "Customer A" likho, phone numbers aur account numbers hata do, financial figures round kar do, aur sirf wahi structure rakho jo chahiye. Assistant "Coimbatore ke ek mid-size client" ke baare mein utna hi achha likhta hai jitna asli company ke naam ke saath.

### Accountability

Kaam par aapka naam hai. Client report mein figure galat ho, toh "AI ne likha tha" bahaana nahi chalta. Policies aam taur par ye saaf likhti hain, aur managers aur clients waise bhi yahi maante hain.

### Jab policy hai hi nahi

Bahut si chhoti companies mein abhi AI policy nahi hai. Iska matlab ye nahi ki koi rule nahi: kanoon aur contracts tab bhi lagu hain. Policy aane tak:

1. Manager ya IT team se poochho ki kya allowed hai.
2. Sirf public material ya apne non-confidential notes use karo.
3. Naam, numbers aur identifiers hata do.
4. Personal data kabhi paste mat karo.
5. Use karne se pehle har cheez check karo.
6. Manager ko batao ki aap AI kaise use kar rahe ho.

## Ek worked example

Karthik, Chennai ki ek mid-sized machine-tools manufacturer mein sales mein kaam karta hai. Woh ek bade automotive client ke liye proposal banane mein AI assistant use karna chahta hai.

Uski company ki ek page ki policy kehti hai:

- Company-licensed assistant internal aur confidential information ke liye approved hai. Kaam ke liye personal free accounts allowed nahi hain.
- Customer ka personal data, jaise naam aur phone numbers, CRM ke built-in AI features ke alawa kahin enter nahi karna.
- Bhejne se pehle pricing finance team se check karwani hai.
- AI-assisted client documents ko account manager review karega.

Karthik ye karta hai:

| Step | Usne kya kiya | Kaunsa rule follow hua |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Material jama kiya | Client ki published requirements aur company ke standard proposal sections | Sirf internal aur public data |
| Notes saaf kiye | Paste karne se pehle client contact ka naam aur phone number hata diya | CRM ke bahar personal data nahi |
| Draft banaya | Licensed assistant se proposal aur pricing table ka draft banwaya | Approved tool |
| Pricing check ki | Table finance ko bheji, jisne galat base price par nikala gaya discount theek kiya | Pricing finance review karta hai |
| Review | Final proposal uske manager ne review kiya | Account manager review |

Galat discount se company ko lagbhag ₹4 lakh ka nuksaan hota. Use AI ne nahi, review step ne pakda.

Ek colleague kehta hai ki "free personal account tez hai", ussi se use karo. Karthik use policy samjhaata hai aur licensed tool kholna dikhaata hai. Policies tabhi chalti hain jab colleagues ek-dusre ko yaad dilaate hain.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Work data ke liye personal accounts use karna.** Chahe harmless lage, data company ke control se bahar chala jaata hai.
- **General assistants mein customer ka personal data paste karna.** Specific approved system use karo, ya personal details hata do.
- **"Policy nahi hai" ko "rules nahi hain" maan lena.** Data protection law, contracts aur confidentiality tab bhi lagu hain.
- **Manager se AI use chhupaana.** Kuch galat ho jaaye toh transparency aapko bachaati hai.
- **Review skip karna kyunki AI "aam taur par sahi" hota hai.** Pricing, contracts ya customer data ke liye "aam taur par sahi" kaafi nahi hai.
- **Policy ko ek baar padhne ki cheez maanna.** Tools badalte hain toh policies bhi badalti hain; updates check karte raho.

## Practice karo

AI Fluency course ka responsible use waala section dekho (neeche link hai), aur PRS ka India ke DPDP law ka summary padho.

1. **Apni policy dhoondho.** Apne organisation ya college ki AI policy dhoondho. Na ho toh paanch sawaal likho jo aap apne manager se poochhoge.
2. **Das documents classify karo.** Das aise documents likho jo aap aksar use karte ho, aur har ek ko public, internal, confidential ya personal mein daalo. Note karo ki har ek ke liye aap kaunse tools use kar sakte ho.
3. **Saaf karo aur karo.** Ek asli kaam lo, uske saare personal aur confidential details hata do, aur approved tool se karo.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap kisi colleague ko samjha sako ki aapke workplace par AI ke liye kaunse tools aur kaunsa data allowed hai, aur shak hone par kya karna hai.
