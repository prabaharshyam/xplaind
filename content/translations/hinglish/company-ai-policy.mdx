## Ye kya hai

**Workplace AI policy** employees ko batati hai ki kaam par AI tools kaise use karne hain. Ye isliye hai kyunki AI assistants ko use karna aasaan hai aur galat use karna bhi aasaan: customer phone numbers waali ek spreadsheet paste karne se client contract toot sakta hai, aapki company ki confidentiality duties toot sakti hain, aur jaise-jaise Bharat ka data law poori tarah laagu hota hai, law bhi.

Achhi policies chhoti aur practical hoti hain. Woh aamtaur par saat cheezein cover karti hain:

| Section | Kya kehta hai | Example rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Approved tools** | Kaun-se assistants, features, extensions aur notetakers allowed hain | "Company-licensed assistant use karo; kaam ke liye personal accounts allowed nahin" |
| **Data rules** | Har tool mein kya daal sakte hain aur kya nahin | "CRM ke built-in AI ke baahar customer personal data nahin" |
| **Use cases** | AI ko kahaan badhaava hai aur kahaan rok | "Drafting aur summarising ke liye badhaava; hiring decisions ke liye nahin" |
| **Review aur accuracy** | AI output kaun check karta hai, aur zimmedaar kaun hai | "Har figure aap check karo; jo bhejo uske owner aap ho" |
| **Disclosure** | Logon ko kab batana hai ki AI use hua | "Report kaafi had tak AI-drafted ho to clients ko batao" |
| **Intellectual property** | AI ki madad se bane kaam ka owner kaun, aur copying se bachna | "Doosron ka copyrighted kaam rewrite karwaane ke liye paste mat karo" |
| **Reporting** | Galti ya problem kaise report karein, aur kitni jaldi | "1 ghante ke andar IT security ko batao; imaandaar reports par saza nahin" |

Ek employee ke taur par aapko policy ratne ki zaroorat nahin. Har task se pehle teen sawaalon ka jawaab dena hai, aur pata hona chahiye ki policy mein har jawaab kahaan hai.

```flow
title: Kaam ke task par AI use karne se pehle teen sawaal
Kaam ka ek task -> Kya ye tool aur ye feature approved hai?
Kya ye tool aur ye feature approved hai? -[nahin]-> Ruko aur IT se poochho | ya sirf public material use karo
Kya ye tool aur ye feature approved hai? -[haan]-> Kya ye data isme daal sakte hain?
Kya ye data isme daal sakte hain? -[nahin]-> Use hatao, ya bataaya gaya system use karo
Kya ye data isme daal sakte hain? -[haan]-> Kya output ko review ya disclosure chahiye?
Use hatao, ya bataaya gaya system use karo -> Kya output ko review ya disclosure chahiye?
Kya output ko review ya disclosure chahiye? -> Check karo, review karwao, phir bhejo
```

Ye lesson Karthik ke saath chalta hai, jo Chennai mein ek manufacturer ke yahaan sales mein kaam karte hain, jab woh apni company ke rules ke andar ek proposal taiyaar karte hain. Aakhir tak aap jaan jaaoge ki achhi policy kya cover karti hai, apna data kaise sort karein, roz ke tools mein built-in AI ko kaise handle karein, galti ke baad kya karein, aur company mein policy hi na ho to kya karein.

## Ye kaise kaam karta hai

### Companies ko fikr kyun hai

Jab aap kisi consumer AI tool mein type karte ho, to woh text store ho sakta hai aur, settings aur vendor ke terms ke hisaab se, service behtar karne mein use ho sakta hai. Isse teen tarah ke risk bante hain:

1. **Legal.** Bharat ka Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) personal data ke use aur sharing ko limit karta hai. Iske Rules November 2025 mein notify hue, aur businesses ki zyaadatar duties May 2027 se laagu hongi. Tab tak, financial details, passwords aur health records jaise sensitive personal data par IT Act ke 2011 ke rules ab bhi laagu hain.
2. **Contractual.** Client contracts aksar unki information third parties se share karne par rok lagaate hain, ya sirf tay kiye gaye deshon mein allow karte hain. Ye aaj laagu hai.
3. **Confidentiality.** Strategy, unreleased financial results aur source code leak ho sakte hain. 2023 mein, engineers ke internal source code ek public chatbot mein paste karne ke baad Samsung ne staff ke public chatbots use karne par rok lagaayi.

Agar aap bank, NBFC, insurer ya hospital mein kaam karte ho, to aapka regulator upar se aur rules jodta hai, aur aapki compliance team unhein jaanti hai. Jaise, RBI ki FREE-AI committee report (August 2025) recommend karti hai ki regulated firms ke paas board-approved AI policy ho. Ye binding rule nahin, salaah hai, par dikhaati hai ki sector kis taraf ja raha hai.

Company licence ye badal deta hai ki rules kaun tay karta hai:

| | Personal free account | Company-licensed tool |
|---|---|---|
| Terms | Aapne bas click karke maan liye | Company ka sign kiya hua contract |
| Aapke data par training | Aapki settings aur vendor ke terms par depend karta hai | Company ka contract tay karta hai; IT se poochho usmein kya likha hai |
| Control | Koi admin control nahin | Single sign-on, admin settings aur audit logs |
| Kuchh leak ho jaaye to | Company na use dekh sakti hai, na theek kar sakti hai | Security team ko pata hota hai data kahaan gaya |
| Kiske liye use karein | Zyaada se zyaada public material | Approved kaam, data rules ke andar |

Licence koi blank cheque nahin hai. Ye usi tool aur settings par laagu hai jo company ne approve kiye, aur usi data par jo policy mein bataaya gaya hai.

### Apne data ko chaar categories mein sort karo

| Category | Examples | Rule of thumb |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Public** | Published brochures, company website, public reports | Aamtaur par kisi bhi tool mein theek |
| **Internal** | Process notes, templates, internal announcements | Sirf approved tools |
| **Confidential** | Client contracts, unreleased results, strategy, draft prices | Sirf approved tools, kabhi-kabhi bilkul nahin |
| **Personal aur sensitive** | Phone numbers ya emails ke saath naam, salaries, health, Aadhaar ya PAN numbers, bank details | Sabse sakht; aksar sirf ek tay system mein |

Teen cheezein jinhein log aksar galat sort karte hain:

- **Kisi client ke liye draft price list** confidential hai, public nahin. Ye public tabhi banti hai jab aur agar publish ho, aur client quote aamtaur par kabhi publish nahin hota.
- **Aapke CRM ya inbox ka screenshot** personal data hai agar usmein naam, numbers ya emails dikh rahe hon, chaahe screenshot kisi bhi cheez ko dikhaane ke liye liya gaya ho.
- **Aapke apne meeting notes** ki category wahi hoti hai jo unmein maujood sabse sensitive cheez ki hai. Client call ke notes aamtaur par confidential hote hain, aur personal agar unmein kisi ke contact details hon.

Shak ho to document ko ek category zyaada sakht mein daalo.

### Woh AI jise aapne jaanboojhkar nahin khola

Kai leaks chatbot kholne se nahin, balki un tools mein built-in AI se hote hain jo log pehle se use karte hain. Inmein se har ek ke liye apni policy check karo; agar woh kuchh nahin kehti, to koi feature on karne se pehle IT se poochho.

| Feature | Ye kya dekh sakta hai | Sensible default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Office suites aur email mein AI**, jaise "summarise this thread" ya "draft a reply" | Jin documents, sheets aur mail ki taraf ise point kiya jaaye, kabhi-kabhi aapki poori drive | Sirf wahi version use karo jo aapki company ne license karke on kiya hai, aur sirf data rules ke andar |
| **Browser extensions** jo rewrite, summarise ya translate karte hain | Aksar har page jo aap kholte ho, CRM, HR portal aur webmail samet | Sirf wahi extensions install karo jo IT ne approve kiye hain; baaki hata do |
| **AI meeting notetakers** | Call par sabki kahi har baat, saath mein naam aur share ki gayi screens | Shuruaat mein sabko batao aur kisi ko bhi mana karne do; external calls ke liye pehle doosri taraf se poochho |
| **Coding assistants** | Khuli files, aksar poori repository, aur kabhi-kabhi keys waali configuration files | Sirf approved assistant, sirf us code par jiski client ijaazat deta hai; jo files ye padhta hai unmein passwords aur keys mat rakho |

Notetakers par khaas dhyaan chahiye, kyunki ye sirf aapko nahin, doosre logon ko bhi record karte hain. Recording ke rules har desh mein alag hain. Jaise California mein, confidential baatcheet record karne ke liye usmein shaamil sabki consent chahiye, isliye US team ke saath client call par sirf shishtachaar ka nahin, legal sawaal bhi uth sakta hai. Kai client contracts bhi recording par rok lagaate hain. Transcript ko meeting jaisa hi maano: uski bhi wahi category aur wahi rules.

### Jo zaroori nahin, use hatao

Aksar aap sensitive hisse ke bina bhi task kar sakte ho. Naamon ki jagah roles likho ("client ke purchase head"), phone numbers aur account numbers hatao, jo figures maayne nahin rakhte unhein round karo, aur sirf wahi rakho jo task ko chahiye. Assistant "ek bade automotive client" ke baare mein bhi utna hi achha likhta hai jitna asli company ke baare mein. Worked example mein poora before aur after hai.

### Review, disclosure aur jawabdehi

Aapke kaam par aapka naam hai. Agar client report mein galat figure hai, to "AI ne likha tha" koi bahaana nahin. Policies aamtaur par ye saaf likhti hain, aur managers aur clients waise bhi yahi maante hain. Isse do aadatein nikalti hain:

- **Sabse zaroori cheezein pehle check karo:** prices, dates, naam, legal terms aur jo kuchh bhi aap promise kar rahe ho.
- **Disclose karo jab policy ya padhne waala iski ummeed kare.** Kuchh clients kehte hain ki deliverable kaafi had tak AI-drafted ho to unhein bataaya jaaye. Apne manager ko batana ki aap AI kaise use karte ho, kuchh gadbad hone par aapko bhi bachaata hai.

### Agar galti se kuchh paste ho jaaye

Ye galti har kisi se ek baar hoti hai: galat file, galat tab, ab bhi signed in personal account. Maayne rakhta hai agla ek ghanta.

1. **Ruko.** Aur kuchh mat bhejo, aur chatbot se use "bhool jaane" ko mat kaho. Jo pehle se store ho chuka hai, use woh undo nahin kar sakta.
2. **Facts note karo:** kaun-sa tool aur account, samay, aur theek-theek kya andar gaya.
3. **Turant report karo** IT ya security ko, apni policy mein diye channel se. Aapki company ko kuchh incidents pata chalne ke 6 ghante ke andar CERT-In, yaani Bharat ki cyber security agency, ko report karne pad sakte hain, isliye ek din ki deri ek chhoti problem ko us duty ke ullanghan mein badal sakti hai.
4. Report karne ke baad, ya jaise security kahe, **chat delete karo**. Delete karne se madad milti hai, par service apne terms ke tahat kuchh samay tak copy rakh sakti hai, isliye delete karna theek karne ke baraabar nahin.
5. Jo bhi **password, key ya access token** aapne paste kiya, use usi din **badlo**.

Achhi policies kehti hain ki imaandaar aur jaldi ki gayi reports par saza nahin hoti. Galti chhupaana hi use disciplinary matter banaata hai.

### Jab koi policy na ho

Kai chhoti companies mein abhi koi AI policy nahin hai. Iska matlab ye nahin ki koi rules nahin: laws aur contracts tab bhi laagu hain. Jab tak policy nahin aati:

1. Apne manager ya IT team se poochho ki kaun-se tools allowed hain.
2. Sirf public material ya apne non-confidential notes use karo.
3. Naam, numbers aur identifiers hatao.
4. Personal data, passwords ya keys kabhi paste mat karo.
5. Use karne se pehle sab kuchh check karo.
6. Apne manager ko batao ki aap AI kaise use kar rahe ho.

Aur bhi behtar, khud ek one-page draft do. Ye raha ek template jise apne hisaab se badal sakte ho. Ye shuruaat hai, legal advice nahin; final rules un par chhodo jinke paas IT aur legal ki zimmedaari hai.

```text
[Company] AI use policy (draft, version 1, [date])

1. Approved tools: [licensed assistant], with [settings]. No personal
   AI accounts for work. Ask IT before switching on any other AI
   feature, browser extension or notetaker.
2. Data: public and internal data in approved tools. Confidential
   client data only where the client contract allows it. Personal
   data only in [named systems]. Never passwords or keys.
3. Encouraged: drafting, summarising, research, formatting.
4. Needs approval: anything customer-facing, decisions about people
   (hiring, appraisal, credit), recording external calls.
5. Review: you check every fact and figure; [named role] reviews
   prices and anything sent to a client.
6. Disclosure: tell clients when a deliverable is largely AI-drafted,
   or when their contract asks.
7. Ownership: do not paste others' copyrighted work to rewrite it.
8. Mistakes: report to [contact] within 1 hour. Honest reports are
   not punished.
9. Owner: [name]. Reviewed every 6 months.
```

## Ek worked example

Ye ek illustrative case hai. Method dikhaane ke liye company, naam aur numbers banaaye gaye hain.

Karthik, Chennai mein ek mid-sized machine-tools manufacturer ke yahaan sales mein kaam karte hain. Woh ek bade automotive client ke liye proposal taiyaar karne mein AI use karna chaahte hain, jise ek naye plant ke liye paanch CNC machines chahiye.

Unki company ki one-page policy kehti hai:

- Company-licensed assistant internal aur confidential information ke liye approved hai. Kaam ke liye personal free accounts allowed nahin.
- Customer personal data, jaise naam aur phone numbers, sirf CRM aur uske built-in AI features mein jaata hai.
- Video-call tool ka AI notetaker internal calls ke liye approved hai. External calls ke liye pehle doosri taraf se poochho.
- Bhejne se pehle pricing ko finance team check kare, aur client documents ko account manager review kare.

**Step 1: Notes saaf karo.** Pehli call ke unke notes:

```text
Before: Spoke to Ramesh Natarajan (purchase head, 98765 43210) at
        Zentora Motors. Need 5 CNC machines for the new plant by March.
        Budget about Rs 2.6 crore. Unhappy with current vendor's
        service: engineers take 3 to 4 days to arrive.

After:  The client's purchase head needs 5 CNC machines for a new
        plant by March. Budget about Rs 2.6 crore. Unhappy with the
        current vendor's service: engineers take 3 to 4 days to arrive.
```

Naam aur phone number personal data hain, isliye woh CRM mein hi rehte hain. Budget aur client ki zarooratein confidential hain, jinke liye licensed assistant approved hai. Client ka naam draft mein kuchh nahin jodta, isliye woh bhi hat-ta hai.

**Step 2: Limits ke saath prompt.** Licensed assistant mein woh likhte hain:

```text
You are helping me draft a sales proposal for CNC machines. Using the
notes and our standard proposal sections below, draft three sections:
the client's needs, our proposed solution, and our service plan.
Formal Indian business English, under 600 words. Do not invent prices,
delivery dates or specifications. Write [CHECK] wherever a figure is
needed. Then build a pricing table from the price list below.
```

"Do not invent" waali line zaroori hai. Iske bina assistants aksar khaali jagahon ko aise figures se bhar dete hain jo sahi lagte hain par kisi ne maange nahin.

**Step 3: Review galti pakadta hai.** Quote ke do hisse hain: ₹2 crore ki paanch machines, aur ₹50 lakh ke spares aur installation, jin par company kabhi discount nahin deti. Assistant ne standard 8% discount poore ₹2.5 crore par laga diya. Finance ne pakda: ₹2 crore ka 8% ₹16 lakh hota hai, par draft mein ₹20 lakh tha. Is galti se ₹4 lakh haath se nikal jaate. Ise review step ne pakda, AI ne nahin.

**Step 4: Notetaker ka sawaal.** Follow-up video call par meeting tool apna AI notetaker on karne ka offer deta hai. Ye external call hai, isliye Karthik shuruaat mein poochhte hain. Purchase head mana kar dete hain: unki apni company supplier calls record karne par rok lagaati hai. Karthik haath se notes lete hain aur baad mein contact details CRM mein daal dete hain.

Baad mein ek colleague "kyunki ye tez hai" kehkar free personal account suggest karta hai. Karthik use dikhaate hain ki licensed tool kaise kholein, jismein ek minute se bhi kam lagta hai. Policies tab kaam karti hain jab colleagues ek-doosre ko yaad dilaate hain.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Kaam ke data ke liye personal accounts use karna.** Bhale ye nuksaan-rahit lage, ye data ko aapki company ke control se baahar kar deta hai.
- **Ye maan lena ki tool approved hai to uska har feature approved hai.** Connectors, extensions, notetakers aur agent modes, har ek data ko kisi nayi jagah bhej sakta hai. Har ek check karo.
- **General assistants mein customer personal data paste karna.** Bataaya gaya system use karo, ya pehle personal details hatao.
- **Bina poochhe notetaker on kar dena.** Ye call par sabko record karta hai, aur ho sakta hai doosri taraf iski ijaazat na de.
- **Galti se hua paste chhupaana, ya sirf chat delete karna.** Turant report karo; delete karne se woh undo nahin hota, aur deri se 6 ghante ki reporting duty toot sakti hai.
- **Ye maan lena ki "koi policy nahin" matlab "koi rules nahin".** Data protection law, contracts aur confidentiality tab bhi laagu hain.
- **Review chhod dena kyunki AI "aamtaur par sahi" hota hai.** Pricing, contracts ya customer data ke liye "aamtaur par sahi" kaafi nahin.
- **Policy ko ek baar padhne ki cheez samajhna.** Tools badalne ke saath policies badalti hain; updates check karte raho.

## Practice

Neeche linked AI Fluency course ka responsible use waala section karo, aur Bharat ke DPDP law par PRS ka summary padho. Woh 2023 mein pass hue Act ko cover karta hai; Rules November 2025 mein aaye.

1. **Apni policy dhoondho.** Apne organisation ya college ki AI policy dhoondho aur is hafte kiye ek task ke liye is lesson ki shuruaat waale teen sawaalon ke jawaab do. Agar koi policy nahin hai, to paanch sawaal likho jo aap apne manager se poochhoge.
2. **Das documents classify karo.** Das documents ki list banao jo aap aksar use karte ho, aur har ek ko public, internal, confidential ya personal mein classify karo. Note karo ki har ek ke liye kaun-se tools use kar sakte ho.
3. **Apne hidden AI ka audit karo.** Apne work laptop aur phone par har AI feature, browser extension aur notetaker ki list banao, aur har ek ko approved, unapproved ya unknown mark karo.
4. **Saaf karo aur karo.** Ek asli task lo, Karthik ki tarah saari personal aur confidential details hatao, aur use kisi approved tool mein karo.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** kisi colleague ko samjha sako ki aapke workplace par AI ke liye kaun-se tools, features aur data allowed hain, aur galti se paste hone ke baad pehle ek ghante mein kya karna hai.
