## Ye kya hai

Kaam par AI ko safely use karna teen sawaalon par tika hai jo aap har baar poochhte ho:

```flow
dir: LR
Pehle: main kya daal raha hoon? | kya ye data is tool mein allowed hai? -> Baad mein: mujhe kya mil raha hai? | kya ye sahi aur theek hai?
Baad mein: mujhe kya mil raha hai? -> Hamesha: zimmedaar kaun hai? | aap, jo bhejte ya tay karte ho uske liye
```

Workplaces par zyadatar AI incidents filmi hacks nahi hote. Ye time ke dabaav mein aam log aam kaam karte hain: Friday shaam ko client ka contract personal chatbot account mein paste karna, ya AI ka likha figure bina check kiye manager ko forward kar dena. Ye lesson un aadaton ke baare mein hai jo ye galtiyaan rokti hain.

Aap Meena ke saath chaloge, jo Hyderabad ki ek chhoti consulting firm mein associate hai, aur ek urgent request ko client ka bharosa todhe bina sambhaalti hai. End tak aap tay kar paoge ki kaam par AI tool mein kya paste kar sakte ho aur kya nahi, aur wajah bhi samjha paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Apne tools ko jaano

Kai companies AI assistant ka **enterprise** version license karti hain. Inke saath aam taur par agreements aate hain ki data kaise store hoga, kaun dekh sakta hai, aur kya use models train karne mein use kiya jaayega. Free ya personal accounts ke terms bahut alag ho sakte hain, jaise aapki conversations rakhna aur service behtar karne mein use karna.

| | Personal free account | Company-approved enterprise tool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terms kisne maane | Aapne, "accept" click karke | Aapki company ne, contract mein |
| Kya aapka data training mein use hota hai? | Ho sakta hai, settings par depend karta hai | Aam taur par nahi, agreement ke mutabik |
| Kya aapki company ise dekh ya control kar sakti hai? | Nahi | Haan: admin controls aur logs |
| Confidential kaam ke liye theek? | Nahi | Haan, policy ke andar |

Agar company ne tools approve kiye hain, toh kaam ke liye wahi use karo. Agar koi approve nahi hua, toh har AI tool ko confidential information ke liye unapproved maano.

### Paste karne se pehle information ko baanto

| Category | Examples | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Aam taur par theek** | Public articles, company ki public website ka text, aapki apni generic writing, banaye hue examples | Koi bhi tool, phir bhi output check karo |
| **Sirf approved tools** | Internal documents, unreleased plans, meeting notes | Sirf wahi tools jo company ne approve kiye |
| **Bilkul bachao**, jab tak koi khaas tool aur use approve na ho | Customer ya employee ka **personal data** (PII), jaise phone number ke saath naam, Aadhaar, PAN, bank details, health information; passwords aur keys; confidentiality agreements waale client documents | Paste mat karo |

**PII** matlab personally identifiable information: koi bhi details jo kisi insaan ki pehchaan batayein. India ka **Digital Personal Data Protection Act** organisations se maangta hai ki personal data sirf lawful purpose ke liye use karein, aam taur par aisa jiske liye insaan ne consent diya ho, aur use reasonable security safeguards se protect karein. Inme se zyadatar duties May 2027 se lagu hongi. Customer details ko unapproved AI tool mein paste karna lagu hone par is kanoon ko tod sakta hai, aur abhi bhi aapki company ke clients ke saath contracts tod sakta hai.

### Jo zaroori nahi use hata do

Aksar sensitive hissa hataye bina bhi madad mil jaati hai:

```text
  Before:  "Draft a reply to Mr Suresh Iyer (98xxxxxx45, policy
            MH-2291-0087) about his delayed claim of Rs 1,84,000."

  After:   "Draft a polite reply to a customer whose health insurance
            claim is delayed by two weeks. Apologise, explain we need
            one more hospital document, and give a new date."
```

Doosre prompt se bhi utna hi achha draft milta hai. Asli naam aur details aap khud apne email system mein daalte ho.

Data hataane ke aur tareeke:

- Naam ki jagah "Customer A" ya "Supplier B" likho.
- Account numbers, amounts aur dates hata do ya round kar do.
- Poora document paste karne ki jagah situation ko apne words mein summarise karo.
- General sawaal poochho: "Vendor contract mein aam taur par kaunsi clauses mein risk hota hai?"

### Jo bahar aaye use check karo

AI galat, purana ya tone mein galat ho sakta hai. Output use karne se pehle check karo:

- jo facts aur figures maayne rakhte hain;
- jo bhi aapko ya company ko sharminda kar sakta hai;
- ki woh aapki company ki asli policies, prices aur waadon ko dikhata hai.

### Nateeje ki zimmedaari lo

Agar AI ke likhe report section mein galat figure hai aur aapne use manager ko bhej diya, toh galti aapki hai. Kaam par "AI ne likha tha" bahaana nahi chalta, aur chalna bhi nahi chahiye.

### Policy follow karo, ya poochho

Agar company ki AI policy hai toh padho. Nahi hai toh manager se poochho, aur tab tak upar ke savdhaan rules follow karo.

## Ek worked example

Meena Hyderabad ki ek chhoti consulting firm mein associate hai. Friday shaam 7 baje client ek 40 page ka vendor contract email karta hai aur Monday subah tak risky clauses ka ek page ka summary maangta hai.

Uski firm ne abhi koi AI tool approve nahi kiya hai. Tez raasta ye hota ki poora contract apne personal chatbot account mein paste karke summary maange. Woh ruk jaati hai aur teen sawaal poochhti hai:

| Sawaal | Meena ka jawab |
| --- | --- |
| Main kya daal rahi hoon? | Ek confidential contract jisme client ke vendor aur pricing ka naam hai. Client ke saath firm ka agreement documents ko third parties ke saath share karna mana karta hai. |
| Mujhe kya milega? | Ek summary jise use waise bhi line by line check karna padta. |
| Zimmedaar kaun hai? | Woh hai, aur firm client ke prati hai. |

Toh uski jagah woh ye karti hai:

1. Contract khud padhti hai aur liability, termination, payment aur data protection ki clauses note karti hai.
2. Chatbot sirf structure ke liye use karti hai, ye poochhkar: "Typical vendor services agreement mein buyer ke liye kaunsi clauses mein sabse zyada risk hota hai?" bina kuch confidential paste kiye. Use aath clause types ki kaam ki checklist milti hai.
3. Us checklist se khud summary likhti hai, jisse use ek anokhi auto-renewal clause pakadne mein madad milti hai.
4. Monday ko manager ko batati hai ki firm ko is tarah ke kaam ke liye ek tool approve karna chahiye.

Manager maan jaata hai. Firm baad mein client ke kaam ke liye ek enterprise assistant license karti hai, aur clients ke documents par use karne se pehle unse consent maangti hai.

Meena ka summary paste karne se zyada time mein bana, par usne client ka bharosa rakha aur AI se asli madad bhi paayi.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Work documents ke liye personal AI accounts use karna.** Ek baar bhi karo toh data company ke control se bahar chala jaata hai.
- **"Bas ek baar" personal data paste karna.** Use hata do ya badal do; draft utna hi achha banega.
- **AI output ko dhyaan se padhe bina forward karna.** Figures, naam aur waade check karo.
- **Ye maan lena ki policy nahi toh rules nahi.** Kanoon aur client contracts tab bhi lagu hain.
- **AI use chhupaana.** Manager ke saath khul kar rehna aapko bachaata hai aur poori team ko seekhne mein madad karta hai.
- **Bina dekhe screenshots paste karna.** Screenshots mein aksar naam, emails aur numbers hote hain jo aap share karna nahi chahte the.

## Practice karo

Ethics of AI course ke privacy aur responsibility waale chapters karo (neeche link hai).

1. **Classify karo.** Kaam ya college mein aap jin paanch information ko sambhaalte ho unhe likho, aur har ek ko paste karne ke liye theek, sirf approved tools, ya kabhi nahi mein baanto.
2. **Hatao aur try karo.** Ek asli kaam lo aur har naam, number aur confidential detail hatakar AI tool se karo. Kya phir bhi kaam ki madad mili?
3. **Policy dhoondho.** Apne organisation ki AI policy dhoondho, ya teen sawaal likho jo aap manager se uske baare mein poochhoge.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap kisi colleague ko samjha sako ki aapke workplace par AI tool mein kya paste kar sakte hain aur kya nahi, aur nateeje ki zimmedaari unki hi kyun rehti hai.
