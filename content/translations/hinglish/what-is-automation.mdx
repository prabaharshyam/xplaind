## Ye kya hai

Apne kaam ke hafte ka sabse boring hissa socho: emails se details spreadsheet mein copy karna, ek hi reply bees baar bhejna, messages ko sahi insaan ko forward karna. **Workflow automation** woh software hai jo ye repeated sequence of steps aapke liye chalaata hai, un apps mein jo aap already use karte ho.

Har automation ke teen hisse hote hain:

```flow
dir: LR
title: Data har arrow ke saath aage jaata hai
Trigger: nayi email aati hai -> Step: use padho, fields nikaalo -> Step: sheet mein ek row jodo -> Result: team ko message bhejo
```

- Ek **trigger**: jo use shuru kare. Nayi email, submit hua form, sheet mein nayi row, din ka koi time, ya kisi doosre app ka message **webhook** ke through. Webhook ek web address hai jise ek app call karke doosre app ko data bhejta hai, jaise hi kuch hota hai.
- **Steps**, jinhe aksar **nodes**, actions ya modules kehte hain: aage kya hoga. Email padho, invoice number nikaalo, customer dhoondho, row jodo, message bhejo.
- **Aage jaata data**: har step ka output agle step ka input ban jaata hai.

**n8n**, **Zapier**, **Make** aur **Microsoft Power Automate** jaise tools se aap ye visually bana sakte ho, boxes ko jodkar, bahut kam ya bina code ke.

**AI steps** ek nayi cheez jodte hain: messy text ke saath judgement jaisi abilities. Ek AI step unstructured email padhkar fields nikaal sakta hai, request ko classify kar sakta hai, document ki summary bana sakta hai ya reply ka draft likh sakta hai. Isse automation saaf, rule-based kaam se aage badhkar us messy, text-bhare kaam tak pahunch jaata hai jisse zyadatar offices bhare hain.

Is lesson mein hum Kochi ki ek travel agency ke saath chalenge, jo apni booking enquiries automate karti hai. Lesson ke end tak aap kisi bhi automation ko trigger, steps aur checks ke roop mein bata paoge, aur achhe pehle candidates chun paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Rule-based automation

Classic kism: har input predictable hota hai aur har step ek fixed rule.

> "Form submit hone par, sheet mein ek row jodo aur team ko email bhejo."

Rule-based automations reliable hote hain, chalaane mein sasti hoti hain aur test karna aasaan hota hai. Unki kamzori ye hai ki input messy ho toh woh toot jaati hain: alag tarah se likhi email, koi field missing, ek typo.

### AI-assisted automation

Yahan ek AI step variety sambhaalta hai, jabki rules aur insaan risk sambhaalte hain:

```flow
accounts@ par aayi email -> AI step: ye kya hai?
AI step: ye kya hai? -[spam]-> Archive karo
AI step: ye kya hai? -[payment query]-> Accounts team ko forward karo
AI step: ye kya hai? -[invoice]-> AI step: vendor, amount, GSTIN, due date nikaalo
AI step: vendor, amount, GSTIN, due date nikaalo -> Saare fields mile aur Rs 1 lakh se kam?
Saare fields mile aur Rs 1 lakh se kam? -[haan]-> Sheet mein row jodo
Saare fields mile aur Rs 1 lakh se kam? -[nahi]-> Kisi insaan se check karwao
```

| | Rule-based steps | AI steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kismein achhe | Fixed, predictable input | Messy, alag-alag tarah ka text |
| Example | "Agar amount ₹50,000 se zyada hai, toh approval maango" | "Ye email padho aur invoice amount dhoondho" |
| Har run ka kharcha | Lagbhag kuch nahi | Model ka thoda sa charge |
| Galat ho sakta hai? | Sirf jab rule galat ho | Haan, kabhi-kabhi; checks jodo |
| Best use | Routing, calculations, data ek jagah se doosri jagah le jaana | Padhna, classify karna, summarise karna, draft karna |

Achhi automations dono ko milaati hain: AI padhta hai, rules decide karte hain, aur risky hisson ko insaan check karte hain.

### Kya automate karne layak hai

Aisa kaam dhoondho jo:

1. **Frequent** ho: roz ya har hafte, saal mein do baar nahi.
2. **Repetitive** ho: har baar wahi steps.
3. **Rule-based ya check karne mein aasaan** ho: koi insaan jaldi se result confirm kar sake.
4. **Digital** ho: data pehle se apps, emails ya files mein ho.
5. **Painful** ho: slow, galtiyon waala ya boring.

Rare, high-judgement ya bahut sensitive tasks aksar pehle candidate ke roop mein kharaab hote hain.

### Automation ko kya chahiye

- **Apps tak access**, aksar ready-made connectors se jo har app ki API use karte hain.
- **Permissions** jahan padhna aur likhna zaroori ho, usse zyada nahi.
- **Steps ki saaf definition**, isiliye pehle process ko map karna aata hai.
- **Ek owner** jo dekhe ki kab toota aur use theek kare.

## Ek example se samjho

Kochi ki ek chhoti travel agency ko email aur website form se roz lagbhag 40 booking enquiries aati hain. Abhi staff har ek ko spreadsheet mein copy karta hai, standard message se reply karta hai, aur sahi agent ko forward karta hai: domestic, international ya pilgrimage tours. Busy dinon mein enquiries ghanton bina jawab ke padi rehti hain aur kuch inbox mein kho jaati hain.

Woh n8n mein ye automation banate hain:

| # | Step | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Email ya website form se ek nayi enquiry aati hai | Trigger |
| 2 | Enquiry padho; naam, phone, destination, dates, travellers ki sankhya aur budget nikaalo; domestic, international ya pilgrimage mein classify karo | AI step |
| 3 | Bookings sheet mein ek row jodo | Rule |
| 4 | Customer ko expected response time ke saath ek vinamr acknowledgement bhejo | Rule, fixed template ke saath |
| 5 | Team chat par sahi agent ko details ke saath notify karo | Rule, step 2 ki class ke hisaab se |
| 6 | Agar AI ko phone number ya travel dates na mile, toh enquiry front desk ko bhejo taaki woh haath se sambhaale | Check |

**Ek mahine baad ke results:**

| Measure | Pehle | Baad mein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enquiries par staff ka time | Lagbhag 2.5 ghante roz | Lagbhag 30 minute roz, zyadatar flag hui enquiries |
| Pehle reply ka time | 2 se 6 ghante | 5 minute se kam |
| Kho gayi enquiries | Har hafte kuch | Koi nahi mili |
| Galat classify hui enquiries | Track nahi hoti thi | Lagbhag 30 mein 1, agents ne pakad li |

Automation ki owner front-desk lead hain. Jab website form mein "special requests" field judi, toh ek din ke liye extraction step dates miss karne laga; unhone flag hui enquiries ka badhna notice kiya aur step theek kar diya.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Toote hue process ko automate karna.** Pehle process ko theek aur simple karo, phir automate karo.
- **Sabse mushkil, sabse rare task se shuru karna.** Frequent, simple, check hone layak kaam se shuru karo.
- **Koi owner nahi.** Har automation kabhi na kabhi toot-ti hai; kisi ko dhyaan dena aur theek karna padta hai.
- **AI steps ko risky actions par bina check ke chhodna.** Payments, deletions aur sensitive messages ke liye ek insaan jodo.
- **Data rules bhool jaana.** Automations mein customer data par bhi privacy law lagu hota hai.
- **Automation ko zaroorat se zyada access dena.** Permissions utni hi rakho jitni har step use karta hai.

## Practice karo

Zapier ke automation basics waale guides dekho (link neeche hai), aur n8n ka quickstart page padho.

1. **List banao.** Paanch repetitive tasks likho jo aap ya aapki team har hafte karti hai. Har ek ke liye trigger, steps aur data kahan rehta hai, ye note karo.
2. **Score do.** Har ek ko frequency, repetitiveness, check karne ki aasaani aur pain ke liye 1 se 5 tak score do. Sabse zyada score waala chuno.
3. **Design karo.** Us task ke liye mark karo ki kaunse steps simple rules hain, kaunse mein AI step chahiye, aur insaan ko kahan check karna chahiye.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap apne kaam ki ek automation ko trigger, steps aur checks ke roop mein bata sako, aur keh sako ki woh achha pehla candidate kyun hai.
