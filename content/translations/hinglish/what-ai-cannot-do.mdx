## Ye kya hai

AI assistants draft kar sakte hain, summarise kar sakte hain, translate kar sakte hain, samjha sakte hain aur brainstorm kar sakte hain, aur kaafi achhe se. Lekin unki kuch limits bhi hain jo bhool jaana aasaan hai, kyunki woh itna fluently likhte hain. Confident aur achhi likhi hui baat sach lagti hai, chahe woh ho ya na ho.

Roz ke use mein ye chhe limits sabse zyada matter karti hain:

| Limit | Matlab | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Galat facts** | Woh galat information bhi poore confidence se bol sakta hai | Gadhi hui statistic ya aisa law jo hai hi nahi |
| **Recent events** | Training data jama hone ke baad kya hua, ye shayad na pata ho | Pichhle mahine announce hui nayi scheme |
| **Exact numbers** | Tool ke bina calculations aur dates mein kamzor ho sakta hai | Lambe invoice ka jod galat karna |
| **Judgement** | Uske apne koi values ya goals nahi; woh likely text banata hai | Kisi employee ko doosra mauka dena ya nahi |
| **Accountability** | Kisi decision ke liye use zimmedaar nahi thehraya ja sakta | Loan galat se reject ho jaaye toh jawab kaun dega |
| **Aapka context** | Aapki situation ke baare mein woh wahi jaanta hai jo aap batao | Aapki company ki leave policy |

Ye limits jaanne ka matlab AI se door rehna nahi hai. Matlab hai use sahi cheezon ke liye use karna.

| AI sabse zyada kahan madad karta hai | Aapko kahan check karna ya decide karna hai |
|---|---|
| Drafting aur rewriting | Facts, figures, dates |
| Apne text ki summary | Recent rules aur prices |
| Kisi topic ko samjhaana | Logon ke baare mein decisions |
| Options par brainstorming | Koi bhi cheez jiske liye aapko blame milega |

Is lesson mein hum Priya ke saath chalenge, jo Pune mein HR executive hain. Woh assistant ko ek aise kaam ke liye use karti hain jo woh achha karta hai, aur ek aise kaam ke liye jahan woh chupchaap fail hota hai. Lesson ke end tak aap har limit ka naam bata paoge aur uska tod nikaal paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Confident galtiyan kyun hoti hain

Language model apne training data ke patterns ke hisaab se sabse likely agle words likhta hai. Jab tak product use search tool ya aapke documents se na jode, woh kuch dhoondhta nahi. Jab facts nahi hote, toh woh default mein ruk kar "mujhe nahi pata" nahi bolta. Phir bhi woh sunne mein sahi lagne waala jawab bana deta hai. Isse **hallucination** kehte hain.

```flow
Sawaal | "Tax-free gratuity ki limit kya hai?" -> Model | training text ke patterns, kuch purane, kuch naye
Model -[current rule pata hai]-> Sahi jawab
Model -[purana figure seekha tha]-> Confident, par out of date
Model -[kabhi saaf dekha hi nahi]-> Confident, par gadha hua
```

Teeno raaston se aane waala likha hua text barabar confident hota hai. Tone se aap ye nahi bata sakte ki kaunsa raasta liya gaya.

### Knowledge cut-off

Model ek certain date tak ke data se seekhta hai, jise uska **knowledge cut-off** kehte hain. Search tool ke bina, use nayi sarkari scheme, haal ka price change ya is saal ke tax rules ka pata nahi ho sakta. Kuch assistants web search kar sakte hain, jo madad karta hai, par tabhi jab product aapke sawaal ke liye search use kare aur achhe sources dhoondh le.

### Private context

Model ko aapki company ki policies, aapke customer ki history ya aapke parivaar ke medical records tab tak nahi pata jab tak aap share na karo, aur unhein share karna shayad sahi bhi na ho. "Aapki leave policy" ke baare mein uska jawab typical policies par based ek andaaza hai.

### Numbers

Aajkal kaafi assistants maths ke liye calculator ya code tool use karte hain, jo reliable hai. Par hamesha nahi: kabhi-kabhi woh digits predict karke "dimaag mein" hisaab kar lete hain, jo lambe sums par galat ho sakta hai. Zaroori figures ke liye working dikhaane ko kaho aur khud check karo.

### Judgement aur accountability

Loan approve karna, kisi ko hire karna, kisi employee par action lena ya patient ko kya batana hai, ye sab values, laws aur context ka mamla hai. Decision ka jawab kisi ko dena padta hai. AI information taiyaar karne, options list karne aur risks batane mein madad kar sakta hai. Decide insaan ko karna hai. Is role mein insaan ko rakhna **human-in-the-loop** kehlaata hai.

### Consistency

Ek hi sawaal do baar poochho toh alag jawab mil sakte hain, kyunki assistants thodi randomness se words chunte hain. Ideas ke liye ye theek hai, even useful. Facts ke liye badalta jawab warning sign hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Priya, Pune ki ek manufacturing company mein HR executive hain. 22 saal baad retire ho rahi ek employee poochhti hain ki unki gratuity ka kitna hissa tax-free hoga.

**Task 1: ek fact.** Priya assistant se poochhti hain: "India mein maximum kitni gratuity tax-free hai?"

Assistant ek figure ke saath confident jawab deta hai. Priya ko yaad hai ki limit kuch saal pehle badhaayi gayi thi, aur woh pakki nahi ki ye figure current hai. Woh assistant se source maangti hain; woh ek general description deta hai, link nahi.

Woh Income Tax Department ki website aur apni company ke tax consultant ka recent circular dekhti hain, aur current limit dhoondh leti hain. Assistant ka figure purana tha.

**Task 2: ek draft.** Ab woh assistant ko us kaam ke liye use karti hain jo woh achha karta hai:

> "Write a warm, two-paragraph email to a retiring employee explaining how gratuity is calculated for 22 years of service, and that part of it is tax-free up to a limit. Leave the limit as [LIMIT] so I can fill it in."

Draft saaf aur pyaar bhara hai. Woh verified figure khud bhar deti hain aur employee ko badhaai dene waali ek line jod deti hain.

| Kaam | Kaunsi limit | Priya ne kya kiya |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tax-free gratuity limit | Galat facts, out of date | Official source check kiya; AI galat tha |
| Explanation email | Koi nahi: drafting uski khoobi hai | Draft use kiya, verified figure bhar diya |
| Early payment approve karna ya nahi | Judgement, accountability | Company policy aur apne manager ke decision ko follow kiya |

Tool ek hi, kaam teen: drafting par bharosa kiya, facts check kiye aur decision insaanon ke paas rakha.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Fluent likhe ko saboot maan lena.** Achhi likhi baat bhi galat ho sakti hai.
- **Search ke bina recent events poochhna.** Model ko shayad pata hi na ho.
- **Decision AI par chhod dena.** Woh information taiyaar kar sakta hai; decide aap karte ho aur jawab bhi aap dete ho.
- **Ye maan lena ki use aapki situation pata hai.** Use wahi pata hai jo aap batate ho.
- **Mental maths par bharosa karna.** Working dikhaane ko kaho, ya spreadsheet use karo.
- **Galtiyan karta hai, isliye use hi na karna.** Insaan bhi galtiyan karte hain. Use wahan karo jahan check karna aasaan ho.

## Practice karo

Elements of AI ka woh chapter padho jo batata hai AI kya hai (link neeche hai), aur generative AI par Microsoft ka chhota intro bhi.

1. **Recent facts test karo.** Kisi assistant se recent Indian news ya rules ke baare mein teen factual sawaal poochho, phir har jawab ko official source se check karo. Note karo kaunse galat ya outdated the.
2. **Consistency test karo.** Use ek hi opinion waala sawaal do baar poochho aur jawab compare karo.
3. **Apne kaam chhaanto.** Apne kaam ke teen aise tasks likho jahan AI draft ya summarise kar sakta hai, aur teen aise jahan insaan ko decide karna hai.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap AI assistants ki chaar limits bata sako aur apne testing se har ek ki ek misaal de sako.
