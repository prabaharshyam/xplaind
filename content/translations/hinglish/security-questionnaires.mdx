## Ye kya hai

**Security questionnaire**, jise vendor risk assessment bhi kehte hain, wo tareeka hai jisse koi enterprise kisi supplier ko data ya systems ka access dene se pehle uski jaanch karta hai. Ye client ki apni spreadsheet ho sakti hai; Cloud Security Alliance ka **CAIQ** (Consensus Assessments Initiative Questionnaire), AI systems ke liye uska naya **AI-CAIQ** ya Shared Assessments ka **SIG** jaisa koi standard set; ya koi vendor-risk portal jahaan aap jawaab type karte ho aur evidence upload karte ho.

Sawaal lagbhag har baar unhi cheezon ko cover karte hain: policies aur reports, access control, encryption, penetration tests, logging aur incident response, backups, aur data location, retention aur sub-processors. Ab ek AI section bhi hota hai: client data par training, model providers, prompt injection, human oversight aur change testing.

### Reports aur standards jinka naam reviewer lega

- **SOC 2** aapke controls par ek independent auditor ki report hai. **Type I** batati hai ki wo kisi ek samay par theek se design kiye gaye the; **Type II** batati hai ki wo kai mahinon ke review period mein kaam bhi karte rahe, isliye reviewers ise pasand karte hain. Agar wo period kaafi pehle khatam ho chuka hai, toh uske baad ke samay ko cover karne waale "bridge letter" ki maang ki ummeed rakho. SOC 2 ek attestation report hai, certificate nahi: "hamaare paas SOC 2 Type II report hai" likho, "hum SOC 2 certified hain" nahi.
- **ISO/IEC 27001** ek information security management system ko certify karta hai; **ISO/IEC 42001** ek AI management system ko certify karta hai, yaani koi company jo AI banaati ya use karti hai, use kaise govern karti hai.
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework** ek voluntary US framework hai. Iske against koi certified nahi hota; aap dikha sakte ho ki aapka process isse kaise map hota hai.

## Ye kaise kaam karta hai

### Answer library banao

Zyaadatar sawaal alag-alag clients mein dohraaye jaate hain. Approved answers ki ek reviewed library rakho, har jawaab ke saath ek **owner** (aamtaur par wo insaan jo us control ko chalaata hai), ek **last-reviewed date** aur **evidence links** (policies, SOC 2 report, penetration test summary, architecture diagrams, test results). Har naye questionnaire ka jawaab library se do, phir naye jawaab waapas usmein jod do. Jab architecture badle, jaise koi naya model provider ya tracing tool aaye, toh har prabhaavit jawaab usi hafte theek karo. Pichhle saal ke system ke baare mein jawaab, galat jawaab hai.

Baar-baar ke kaam ko aur kam karne ke liye, CAIQ jaisa koi standard questionnaire ek baar bhar lo aur client ke apna bhejne se pehle hi use offer kar do. Aur ek **trust centre** chalaao: ek public security summary, saath mein ek gated area jahaan approved clients non-disclosure agreement ke tahat SOC 2 report aur baaki evidence download karte hain.

### Aise jawaab likho jo ek follow-up jhel jaayein

Sirf "Yes" follow-ups ko bulaawa deta hai, aur overclaim contract breach ban sakta hai. Achha jawaab batata hai kya, kaise aur kahaan. Jab koi control sirf aanshik roop se laagu ho, toh "Partially" kaho: kya hai, kya nahi hai aur baaki ki taareekh. Sab kuch claim karne waale vendor ke muqaable, reviewers "abhi nahi, aur ye raha plan" par kahin zyada bharosa karte hain.

| Sawaal | Weak jawaab | Strong jawaab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Encryption at rest? | "Yes" | "AES-256, keys Azure Key Vault mein, Central India region; keys har saal rotate hoti hain" |
| Kya data training ke liye use hota hai? | "No" | "Provider X ki enterprise terms hamaare API data par training ko exclude karti hain; abuse checks ke liye wo requests 30 din rakhta hai; clause attached" |
| Prompt injection? | "Hum industry-standard AI security use karte hain" | "Hum risk kam karte hain: least-privilege tools, outbound actions ke liye approval, CI mein chalne waala injection test set; latest results attached" |
| Pen testing? | "Yes" | "Web app ka saalaana external test; AI endpoints 15 January 2027 ke liye book hue test mein shaamil hain" |

Yahaan provider, retention period aur taareekhein sirf udaaharan hain; apne waale apne contracts se likho.

### AI section

Lagbhag har AI questionnaire mein ye sawaal aane ki ummeed rakho, aur jawaab current rakho:

- **Providers aur terms:** kaun-se providers aur regions, aur contract training ke baare mein aur requests kitne samay tak rakhi jaati hain, is par kya kehta hai. Har provider ki terms aapne aakhiri baar kab padheen, uski taareekh record karo, kyunki terms badalti hain.
- **Bheja gaya data:** kaun-se fields model tak pahunchte hain, aur kya personal data pehle mask hota hai.
- **Har jagah retention:** prompts sirf app mein nahi, provider ke paas, aur aapke tracing tool, logs aur backups mein bhi rehte hain. Sabke liye jawaab do.
- **Prompt injection aur actions:** model kaun-se tools call kar sakta hai, kin permissions ke saath, aur kin actions ke liye kisi insaan ki approval chahiye.
- **Guardrails, audit logs aur kill switches,** test results ke saath.
- **Change control:** naya model ya prompt release se pehle kaise test hota hai.

### Sub-processors aur data flows

Har third party ko list karo jo client data ko chhooti hai: cloud, model provider, vector database, tracing tool, email service, aur jawaab draft karne ke liye aap jo bhi AI tool use karte ho. Har ek ka purpose aur data location batao. Contracts aksar client ko naye sub-processor par aapatti karne dete hain, isliye koi naya jodne ka matlab hai pehle client ko batana. Ek panne ka **data flow map** (data kahaan aata hai, har store aur service jisse wo guzarta hai, har ek ka region, aur use kaun dekh sakta hai) akele hi bahut-se sawaalon ka jawaab de deta hai.

### Indian regulatory sawaal

Kisi Indian bank ke apne rules uske questionnaire ka bada hissa tay karte hain. Zyaadatar legal duties bank par hoti hain, aur wo unhe contract ke zariye aap tak pahunchaata hai.

| Rule | Kis par laagu | Bank aamtaur par aapse kya poochhega | Taiyaar rakhne waala evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RBI (Commercial Banks – Managing Risks in Outsourcing) Directions, 2025, 28 November 2025 ko jaari, IT outsourcing par alag chapter ke saath | Bank par, jise aapke saath apne contract mein terms daalni hoti hain | Kya aap bank ke audits aur RBI ke inspection ko maanoge? Aapke subcontractors kaun hain? Aap exit kaise karoge aur data kaise lautaaoge? | Tay hue audit aur inspection clauses, subcontractor list, continuity aur exit plan |
| Commercial banks ke liye RBI ke cybersecurity aur technology risk directions, 31 July 2026 ko jaari | Bank par, jise cyber incidents detect hone ke 6 ghante ke andar RBI ke DAKSH platform par report karne hote hain | Aap humein incident ke baare mein kitni jaldi bataoge? Kya hum aapki penetration test report aur fixes dekh sakte hain? | Ghanton mein likha incident notice clause, latest test summary, fix tracker |
| CERT-In directions, 28 April 2022, IT Act ke section 70B(6) ke tahat | Seedhe aap par, aur bank par bhi: ye service providers, data centres, body corporates aur kai doosron ko cover karta hai | Kya aap listed incidents ko notice karne ke 6 ghante ke andar CERT-In ko report karte ho, ICT logs ko Indian jurisdiction mein rolling 180 din tak rakhte ho, aur clocks ko NIC ya NPL time servers se sync karte ho? Aapka point of contact kaun hai? | CERT-In step waala incident runbook, log retention settings, time server settings, naamit contact |
| Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, aur DPDP Rules, 2025 | Bank par Data Fiduciary ke roop mein; aap par uske Data Processor ke roop mein, jo sirf valid contract ke tahat engage hota hai | Aap kaun-sa personal data process karte ho, aur kyun? Kaun-se security safeguards use karte ho? Personal data breach ke baare mein humein kitni jaldi bataoge? Kya hamaare kehne par data erase karoge? | Data processing agreement, data inventory, safeguard list, erasure procedure |
| Storage of Payment System Data par RBI circular, 6 April 2018 | Payment system providers, aur jin vendors ko wo engage karte hain | Kya aapke system mein payment data aata hai? Wo kahaan store hota hai? | Data flow map jo dikhaaye ki payment data India mein hi rehta hai, ya aap tak pahunchta hi nahi |
| RBI ki FREE-AI committee report, 13 August 2025 | Abhi kisi par nahi: ye 7 principles ("sutras") aur 26 recommendations waali committee report hai, binding direction nahi | Kya aap hamaari AI inventory aur AI policy mein support kar sakte ho? Kya kisi decision ko explain aur trace kiya ja sakta hai? Loop mein insaan kahaan hai? | Model card, eval results, audit log schema, approval steps |

Paanch gotchas jo anubhavi reviewers jaante hain:

- **Koi RBI rule nahi kehta ki saara bank data India mein hi rehna chahiye.** Saaf legal rule payment system data ko cover karta hai. DPDP Act, allowed deshon ki list banaane ki bajaay, sarkaar ko un deshon mein transfers rokne deta hai jinka wo naam le. Phir bhi zyaadatar banks policy ke taur par Indian hosting maangte hain, toh iski ummeed rakho, par kabhi mat likho "RBI videsh mein koi bhi data rakhne se mana karta hai".
- **Act ke apne shabd use karo.** DPDP Act **Data Fiduciary** (bank), **Data Processor** (aap), **Data Principal** (customer) aur **personal data breach** ki baat karta hai. "Sub-processor" aur "controller" GDPR-style contracts se aate hain, jinhe bahut-se questionnaires copy karte hain. Sawaal ka jawaab waise hi do jaise poochha gaya hai, par legal positions Act ke terms mein batao.
- **Taareekhein jaano.** DPDP Rules November 2025 mein notify hue; security safeguards aur breach intimation samet zyaadatar duties May 2027 se laagu hoti hain. Abhi sign hone waale contracts us taareekh ke baad tak chalenge, isliye banks abhi se poochhte hain.
- **Clocks alag-alag logon ki hain.** Bank ki 6-ghante waali RBI report us samay se chalti hai jab bank incident detect karta hai, isliye aapke der se bataane par bank ki deadline chhoot jaati hai. Sirf usi notice window par haan kaho jo aapki on-call team Ravivaar raat 2 baje bhi nibha sake. DPDP Act ke tahat Data Fiduciary, Data Protection Board aur prabhaavit logon ko batata hai; aapka kaam bank ko jaldi batana hai.
- **Versions alag hote hain.** Small finance banks, NBFCs aur doosri regulated entities ke apne versions hote hain, aur puraane questionnaires IT services ki outsourcing par 2023 Master Direction ka hawaala de sakte hain. Asal baat ka jawaab do, wo version cite karo jo bank confirm kare, aur koi bhi compliance claim apne legal adviser se approve karwao. Banks aksar CERT-In-empanelled auditor se penetration tests chaahte hain, isliye book karne se pehle poochh lo.

### Evidence, drafting aur follow-up calls

Sensitive evidence, jaise poori SOC 2 report, sirf non-disclosure agreement ke tahat, expire hone waale link se share karo. Puraana pad jaane waala text paste karne ki bajaay living documents ka link do. Injection aur permission tests ke CI results ko ek chhote PDF ke roop mein export karne laayak rakho: AI evidence wo cheez hai jo reviewers ne sabse kam dekhi hai.

Ek model aapki library aur design notes se jawaab draft kar sakta hai. Teen rules. Wo sirf library aur attached documents use kare, aur jo library ids use keen unhe cite kare. Ek naamit insaan har jawaab approve kare, taaki koi managadhant control baahar na jaaye. Aur client ka questionnaire confidential hai: sirf usi approved provider aur region ke saath draft karo jo pehle se aapki sub-processor list mein hai. Kisi bank ka questionnaire personal chatbot account mein paste karna apne aap mein ek data-handling failure hai.

Follow-up calls par, system jaanne waale engineer aur data flow map ko saath laao, sirf usi ka jawaab do jo aap jaante ho, aur jo kuch check karna pada wo usi din bhej do.

Jawaabon ko aisi file mein store karo jise team code ki tarah review kare, aur bookkeeping ko script karo:

```python
import yaml
from datetime import date, timedelta

def load(path="security-answers.yaml"):
    with open(path) as f:
        return yaml.safe_load(f)

def stale(answers, today=None, max_age_days=180):
    today = today or date.today()
    cutoff = today - timedelta(days=max_age_days)
    return [(a["id"], a["owner"]) for a in answers
            if a["reviewed"] < cutoff or not a.get("evidence")]

def suggest(question, answers, top=3):
    words = set(question.lower().split())
    def overlap(a):
        text = a["question"] + " " + " ".join(a["tags"])
        return len(words & set(text.lower().split()))
    return [a["id"] for a in sorted(answers, key=overlap, reverse=True)[:top]]

answers = load()
print(stale(answers, today=date(2026, 11, 28)))
print(suggest("Are prompts retained in logs?", answers, top=1))
```

28 November 2026 ko chalaane par, `stale` ye print karta hai: `[('prompt-retention', 'ml-lead')]`. Wo jawaab 180 din se zyada puraana hai, uska koi evidence nahi hai, aur wo tracing settings ko cover karta hai jo aksar badalti hain. `suggest` ye print karta hai: `['prompt-retention']`. `stale` ko har mahine chalaao aur har owner ko uski list bhejo. Candidates dhoondhne ke liye `suggest`, ya badi library ke liye embeddings, use karo, par har match ko ek insaan check kare: milta-julta jawaab bhi galat ho sakta hai.

## Ek worked example

Ye example sirf samjhaane ke liye hai: counts aur taareekhein tareeka dikhaati hain, koi asli deal nahi.

Ek safal pilot ke baad, Bengaluru ke ek AI startup ko ek bade private bank se 240 sawaalon waali spreadsheet milti hai, jo do hafte mein deni hai. FDE, Meera, coordinate karti hai.

**Day 1: triage.** Library 170 sawaalon se match karti hai. Baaki 70 mein se 40 AI-specific hain aur 30 bank-specific. Har group ko ek owner aur due date milti hai.

**Days 2 se 6: AI sawaal.** Wo asli design documents (data flow map, gateway configuration, masking rules, guardrail tests, approval step, audit log schema) se jawaab deti hai, aur har jawaab kisi document section ka hawaala deta hai.

"Kya aap prompts retain karte ho?" check karte hue, use pata chalta hai ki tracing tool poore prompts India ke baahar rakhta hai; library ka jawaab puraana ho chuka tha. Team tracing ko ek Indian region mein le jaati hai aur pehle account numbers mask karti hai. Meera usi din jawaab, sub-processor list aur data flow map update karti hai.

**Days 3 se 9: bank-specific sawaal.** Startup ke legal adviser ke saath, wo unhe upar ki table waale rules se map karti hai:

- **Outsourcing:** startup audit aur inspection clauses maanta hai aur apne cloud, model provider aur tracing tool ko subcontractors ke roop mein list karta hai.
- **Incident notice:** bank 2 ghante ke andar notice maangta hai. Meera on-call lead se confirm karti hai, phir runbook mein CERT-In step ke bagal mein ek "bank ko batao" step jodti hai.
- **Logs:** retention Indian region mein 180 din par set hota hai.
- **DPDP:** jawaab startup ko bank ka Data Processor batate hain, aur draft data processing agreement aur erasure procedure attach karte hain.

**Days 10 se 12: gaps aur review.** Do asli gaps saamne aate hain: AI endpoints ka koi penetration test nahi, aur model misbehaviour ke liye koi incident plan nahi. Wo dono ke liye "Partially" jawaab deti hai, CERT-In-empanelled firm ke saath book hua test aur ek draft runbook attach karke. Ek naamit insaan har jawaab review karta hai.

**Outcome.** Bank do sharton ke saath maan jaata hai: 60 din ke andar test report, aur go-live se pehle final runbook. 70 naye jawaab owners aur taareekhon ke saath library mein jaate hain, taaki agla bank zyada matches ke saath shuru ho.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Overclaiming.** Jhootha "Yes" legal problem ban sakta hai. "Partially" kaho aur plan do.
- **Golmol AI jawaab.** "Hum industry-standard AI security use karte hain" ka koi matlab nahi. Providers, regions, terms aur controls ke naam batao.
- **Koi sub-processor ya data ki koi copy chhoot jaana,** jaise pichhle mahine joda gaya tracing tool jo prompts rakhta hai, ya jawaab draft karne mein use hua AI tool.
- **Bina owner ya review date waale jawaab,** jisse koi jawaab sach na rahe toh kisi ko pata nahi chalta.
- **AI se draft kiye jawaab bina check kiye bhejna.**
- **"SOC 2 certified" ya "NIST AI RMF certified" kehna.** SOC 2 ek audit report hai, aur NIST framework ka koi certification nahi hota.
- **Indian law ko dheele dhang se batana.** "Hum RBI compliant hain" bahut vague hai, aur "RBI kehta hai saara data India mein ho" galat hai. Batao kaun-sa rule, aap kya karte ho aur kaun-sa evidence ye dikhaata hai.
- **Aisi notice window ka waada karna jo on-call nibha na sake.**

## Jab poori library zaroorat se zyada hai

- **Aapke pehle ek-do questionnaires**: unka dhyaan se jawaab do, phir un jawaabon ko library mein badal do.
- **Chhote clients** jo email par kuch sawaal poochhte hain: seedhe jawaab do, utni hi sateekta se.
- **Internal projects** jo company ki apni security review process mein cover hote hain.

## Demo se production tak

- **Library ko version control mein rakho** aur puraane jawaabon ko har mahine aur har architecture change par flag karo.
- **Sub-processor list aur data flow map saath-saath maintain karo**, har service ke purpose aur location ke saath.
- **Ek Indian rules sheet rakho** (rule, taareekh, kis par laagu, aapka evidence) aur jab regulators naye directions jaari karein toh legal se use review karwao.
- **Har questionnaire track karo**: taareekh, bheja gaya answer version, waada kiye gaye follow-ups.

3. Apna data flow map banao aur har sub-processor ko purpose aur location ke saath list karo.
4. Apne system ke liye Indian rules table bharo, har row mein apna jawaab aur evidence.
