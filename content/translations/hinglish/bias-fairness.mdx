## Ye kya hai

Koi AI system **biased** tab hota hai jab wo bina kisi achhi wajah ke kuch groups ke liye kharaab kaam karta hai, ya unke baare mein kharaab faisle leta hai. Ye groups gender, caste, religion, region, language, age, disability ya income se tay ho sakte hain. **Fairness** ka matlab hai in farkon ko dhoondhna, tay karna ki inmein se kaun-se jaayaz nahi hain, aur unhe kam karna.

Bias do tarah ka nuksaan karta hai:

- **Allocation harm:** system koi zaroori cheez deta hai ya rok leta hai, jaise loan, job interview ya koi welfare benefit.
- **Quality-of-service aur representation harm:** system kuch logon ke liye bas kharaab kaam karta hai, ya unhe stereotypes ke zariye dikhaata hai. Jaise, Bhojpuri accent par fail hone waala voice assistant, ya kisi surname ko kisi khaas kaam se jodne waala chatbot.

Bias shaayad hi kabhi jaanboojhkar hota hai. Koi ye rule nahi likhta ki "is district ke applicants ko reject karo". Ye zyaadatar **us data se aata hai jisse system ne seekha**, aur **us tareeke se jisse system use hota hai**.

```flow
dir: LR
title: Galat history andar, galat predictions bahar
Puraane faisle | engineering roles ke liye zyaadatar mard hire hue -> Model seekhta hai | pattern, merit nahi
Model seekhta hai -> Model dohraata hai | naye roles ke liye auraton ko neeche rank karta hai
```

Jaise-jaise AI loans, jobs, admissions, insurance aur welfare se jude faisle lene ya unhe shape karne laga hai, ye aur zaroori hota ja raha hai. Biased spell-checker bas pareshaan karta hai. Biased loan model zindagiyaan badal deta hai, aur ek hi galti din mein hazaaron baar dohraata hai.

Ye lesson Bengaluru ke ek startup ko follow karta hai, jise pata chalta hai ki uska resume-screening tool unfair hai, aur phir wo use theek karta hai. Aakhir tak aap bata paoge ki bias kahaan se aata hai, ek simple ratio se use measure kar paoge, caste ya religion data haath mein na hone par bhi use test kar paoge, aur sahi fixes chun paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Bias kahaan se aata hai

| Source | Kya hota hai | Indian example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Historical labels** | Model puraane faisle copy karta hai, sahi hon ya galat | Pehle hiring mein kuch colleges aur zyaadatar mardon ko tarjeeh mili, toh model bhi yahi karta hai |
| **Missing data** | Jin groups ke examples kam hain, unhe kharaab results milte hain | Zyaadatar urban English speakers par train hua speech model rural accents par ladkhadaata hai |
| **Proxies** | Doosre features kisi sensitive trait ki jagah khade ho jaate hain | Surname, PIN code ya college se caste, religion ya income pata chal sakti hai |
| **Language** | Models unhi languages mein sabse achha kaam karte hain jo unhone sabse zyaada dekhin | Chatbot English mein achha jawaab deta hai aur Odia ya Santali mein kharaab |
| **Use** | Tool ko aise kaam mein lagaaya jaata hai jiske liye wo design nahi hua | Shortlist suggest karne ke liye bana tool automatic gatekeeper ban jaata hai |

Pehli row wahi hai jise log sabse zyaada miss karte hain. Model **labels** se seekhta hai, yaani uske training data ke "sahi jawaab". Agar label hai "kya is insaan ko hire kiya gaya?" ya "kya manager ne inhe high rating di?", toh model unhi puraane choices ki nakal karna seekhta hai, unmein jo bhi unfairness ho, uske saath. Wo ye nahi seekh raha ki kaam mein achha kaun hai.

### Field hataana kaafi kyun nahi

Pehla aam idea hota hai: "Data se gender aur caste delete kar do." Ye shaayad hi kaam karta hai, kyunki doosre features mein bhi wahi jaankaari hoti hai. India mein surname se caste ya religion ka pata chal sakta hai. PIN code se income aur community ka pata chal sakta hai. College, school board, hostel role ya koi sport, gender, region ya class bata sakte hain. Model in **proxies** ko dhoondh leta hai aur phir bhi puraana pattern seekh leta hai.

Ek simple check: hataaye gaye trait ko bache hue features se predict karke dekho. Agar koi chhota model resume ke baaki hisse se gender ka andaaza chance se kahin behtar laga leta hai, toh aapka scoring model bhi us signal ko use kar sakta hai. Phir dekho ki kaun-se features us andaaze ko chala rahe hain, aur unhe hataao, coarsen karo ya un par dobaara socho.

### Bias kaise dikhta hai

- Loan model rural PIN codes se kam applicants approve karta hai, repayment records milte-julte hone par bhi.
- Resume screener engineering roles ke liye auraton ko neeche rank karta hai.
- Image generator "a doctor" ke liye zyaadatar mard aur "a nurse" ke liye zyaadatar auratein banaata hai.
- Chatbot English ke muqaable Indian languages mein chhote aur kamzor jawaab deta hai.
- Face-analysis system kuch chehron par zyaada galtiyaan karta hai. 2018 ki Gender Shades study ne teen commercial systems test kiye jo photo se gender ka andaaza lagaate hain. Unke error rates darker-skinned auraton ke liye 34.7% tak the, aur lighter-skinned mardon ke liye 0.8% se zyaada nahi.
- Language model caste stereotypes dohraata hai. October 2025 mein MIT Technology Review ki ek investigation ne report kiya ki Indian prompts se test karne par OpenAI ka GPT-5 aur uska video model Sora aksar caste stereotypes banaate the.

Gender Shades ka result dhyaan se dekho. Sabse bada gap akele "auratein" ya "darker skin" nahi tha, balki dono ka **intersection** tha. Hamesha combined groups bhi check karo, jaise rural auratein ya zyaada umar ke Hindi-medium applicants, sirf ek baar mein ek trait nahi.

### Bias measure karna: sabse pehle nikaalne waale numbers

Har group ke liye teen numbers se shuru karo.

1. **Selection rate:** group ka wo hissa jise achha outcome milta hai, jaise shortlist ya approve hona.
2. **Selection-rate ratio:** sabse kam waale group ka rate, sabse oonche group ke rate se divide kiya hua. Agar 30% mard aur 21% auratein shortlist hoti hain, toh ratio 21 ÷ 30 = 0.70 hai.
3. **Wrongful rejection rate:** jo log asal mein qualified the ya loan chuka dete, unmein se jitnon ko model ne reject kiya, unka hissa. Iske liye outcome data chahiye, lekin applicants ko sabse zyaada fikr isi error ki hoti hai.

Ek bahut use hone waali pehli screen 1978 ki US employment guidance se aati hai, **four-fifths rule**: jis group ka selection top group ke rate ke 80% se kam ho, use possible adverse impact ka evidence maana jaata hai. Ise rule of thumb ki tarah use karo, faisle ki tarah nahi. Ye Indian law nahi hai, 0.8 se upar ka ratio bhi asli problem chhipa sakta hai, aur 0.8 se neeche ke ratio ki koi fair wajah ho sakti hai, jaise job se judi skills mein asli fark. 0.8 se neeche ka matlab hai "abhi jaanch karo", "doshi" nahi.

| Metric | Kis sawaal ka jawaab deta hai | Kis baat ka dhyaan rakho |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Har group ka selection rate | Achha outcome kise milta hai? | Groups ki asli qualifications mein fark ho sakta hai |
| Selection-rate ratio | Gap kitna bada hai? | 0.8 ek screen hai, India mein legal line nahi |
| Har group ka wrongful rejection rate | Kise galti se lautaaya ja raha hai? | Logon ne asal mein kaisa perform kiya, iska data chahiye |
| Combined groups ke liye yahi numbers | Kya koi ek intersection sabse zyaada maar kha raha hai? | Cells jaldi chhote ho jaate hain |

**Chhote groups noisy hote hain.** Agar kisi group se sirf 30 applicants aayein aur 3 shortlist hon, toh ye 10% hai, lekin ek aur shortlist hote hi 13% ho jaayega. Ek akela insaan rate ko teen points se zyaada hila deta hai. Kisi group mein lagbhag sau se kam log hon, toh gap ko final answer mat maano, use aur data jutaane ya cases haath se review karne ki wajah maano.

### Jab group data aapke paas na ho, tab testing

Zyaadatar Indian companies caste ya religion record nahi kartin, aur unhe ye yoon hi collect karna bhi nahi chahiye. Iska matlab ye nahi ki testing namumkin hai. Teen practical tareeke:

- **Paired (counterfactual) tests.** Asli cases ki aisi copies banaao jinmein sirf ek detail alag ho: name, surname, PIN code, pronoun ya college. Dono copies ko score karo. Agar same resume ke saath "Priya Paswan" ka score "Priya Sharma" se kam aata hai, toh model us signal ko use kar raha hai. Ye chatbots par bhi kaam karta hai: alag-alag communities ke logon ke baare mein ek hi sawaal poochho, ya Hindi aur English dono mein poochho, aur jawaab compare karo.
- **Consent waala audit sample.** Applicants ya users ke ek sample ko fairness testing ke bataaye gaye maqsad ke liye, apni marzi se sensitive traits share karne ko kaho. Ye data alag store karo, kaun dekh sakta hai ye seemit rakho, aur ise kabhi model mein mat daalo. DPDP Act ke tahat, jiski mukhya duties May 2027 se laagu hongi, personal data ke liye ek saaf maqsad aur zyaadatar cases mein consent chahiye.
- **Region aur language ke hisaab se outcome review.** Personal traits ke bina bhi aapko aamtaur par state, PIN code area aur interface language pata hoti hai. Inke hisaab se results baantne par aksar wahi gaps dikh jaate hain.

Ek lubhaane waale shortcut se saavdhaan raho: apne data par label lagaane ke liye har insaan ki caste ya religion ka andaaza uske surname se lagaana. Ye andaaze aksar galat hote hain, aur ab aapne un logon ke baare mein sensitive data bana diya hai jinhone iske liye kabhi haan nahi kaha.

### Kya kiya ja sakta hai

1. **Launch se pehle group-wise test karo**, upar waale numbers se, combined groups samet.
2. **Data theek karo.** Under-represented groups ke examples jodo, rebalance karo, aur labels par sawaal uthaao. "Hire hua" aur "kaam achha karega" ek baat nahi hai.
3. **Proxies hataao ya coarsen karo**, phir dobaara test karo, kyunki unki jagah naye proxies aa sakte hain.
4. **Product badlo.** Model ko faisla karne ke bajaay sirf suggest karne do. Ek trained insaan rejections review kare aur model ka faisla palat sake.
5. **Wajah batao aur appeal karne do.** Jin logon par asar pada, unhe mukhya wajahein aur faisle ko challenge karne ka saaf raasta milna chahiye.
6. **Launch ke baad monitor karo.** Group tests ek schedule par, aur kisi bhi model, data ya policy change ke baad dohraao.

```flow
dir: LR
title: Fairness loop
Build -> Group-wise test -> Wajah dhoondho -> Data, features ya process theek karo -> Phir se test
Phir se test -[launch ke baad aur har change par dohraao]-> Group-wise test
```

### Fairness mein trade-offs hain

Fairness ki ek se zyaada definitions hain, aur wo aapas mein takra sakti hain:

| Definition | Matlab | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equal approval rates | Har group ek hi rate par approve ho | Aise log bhi approve ho sakte hain jinhe repayment mein dikkat hone ki sambhaavna hai |
| Equal error rates | Model har group mein achhe applicants ko barabar baar galti se reject kare | Approval rates phir bhi alag ho sakte hain |
| Milte-julte logon ke saath ek jaisa bartaav | Ek jaise record waale do logon ko ek jaisa result | "Milta-julta" kise kahein, is par sahmati chahiye |

Pehli do kyun takraati hain, ye dekho. Maan lo Group A ke 100 applicants mein se 80 loan chukaayenge, aur Group B mein 60. (Ye numbers idea samjhaane ke liye banaaye gaye hain.) Ek perfect model theek un 80 aur un 60 ko approve karta hai. Kisi bhi group mein usse koi error nahi hota, toh error rates barabar hain, lekin approval rates 80% aur 60% hain. Ab dono groups ko 70% approval rate par zabardasti laao. Group A mein aapko aise 10 logon ko reject karna padega jo loan chuka dete. Group B mein aise 10 logon ko approve karna padega jo nahi chukaayenge. Equal approval rates ne unequal errors paida kar diye. Researchers isse jude results prove kar chuke hain: jab base rates alag hon, toh koi risk score har group mein ek hi repayment chance ka matlab bhi rakhe aur har group mein equal error rates bhi rakhe, ye tab tak mumkin nahi jab tak wo perfect predict na kare.

Isliye definition chunna ek value decision hai. Ise khulkar lo, prabhaavit logon, kaanoon aur system ke maqsad ko dhyaan mein rakhkar, aur likhkar rakho ki kyun. Ise us insaan par mat chhodo jo model train kar raha hai. Base rate ka alag hona khud puraani unfairness ka nateeja ho sakta hai, jo labels par sawaal uthaane ki ek aur wajah hai.

### Kaanoon kya kehta hai

November 2026 tak India mein algorithmic bias par khaas taur par likha koi kaanoon nahi hai, aur models ko iske liye test karne ki koi general legal duty bhi nahi hai. Phir bhi model jo karta hai, us par maujooda kaanoon laagu hote hain. Jaise, Code on Wages, 2019 employers ko same ya similar kaam ki recruitment mein gender ke aadhaar par bhedbhaav karne se rokta hai. Do guidance documents fairness ko ek core principle bataate hain: November 2025 ki India's AI Governance Guidelines, aur financial sector ke liye August 2025 ki Reserve Bank of India ki FREE-AI committee report. Dono guidance hain, penalties waale binding rules nahi.

EU AI Act hiring aur logon ki credit scoring mein use hone waale AI ko **high-risk** maanta hai. Iska matlab hai data quality, human oversight, records aur transparency par duties. May 2026 mein EU lawmakers in high-risk duties ko December 2027 tak aage badhaane par sahmat hue, aur June 2026 mein ye badlaav formally adopt hua. Ye un Indian firms par bhi laagu hongi jinke tools EU mein use hote hain.

## Ek example se samjho

Bengaluru ka ek startup ek bade IT client ke liye resume-screening tool banaata hai. Wo model ko client ke paanch saal ke hiring faislon par train karta hai: kaun-se resumes shortlist hue aur kaun-se nahi. Label hai "shortlist hua", toh model puraane recruiters ki nakal karna seekhta hai.

**Group-wise testing.** Launch se pehle team tool ko engineering roles ke 2,400 puraane applicants par chalaati hai, jo sabhi job ki minimum requirements poori karte the. Tool unmein se 480 (20%) ko shortlist karta hai. Table **unhi 2,400 logon** ko do tarah se baantti hai, college se aur gender se, aur saath mein ek combined group. Ye figures illustrative hain, is lesson ke liye banaaye gaye.

| Group | Applicants | Fix se pehle shortlist | Fix ke baad shortlist |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paanch jaane-maane colleges | 400 | 180 (45%) | 92 (23%) |
| Baaki colleges | 2,000 | 300 (15%) | 388 (19.4%) |
| Mard | 1,600 | 384 (24%) | 328 (20.5%) |
| Auratein | 800 | 96 (12%) | 152 (19%) |
| Baaki colleges ki auratein | 680 | 56 (8.2%) | 120 (17.6%) |
| Paanch colleges ke mard | 280 | 140 (50%) | 60 (21.4%) |

**Fix se pehle ratios nikaalna.** Paanch colleges ke muqaable baaki colleges: 15 ÷ 45 = 0.33. Mardon ke muqaable auratein: 12 ÷ 24 = 0.50. Paanch colleges ke mardon ke muqaable baaki colleges ki auratein: 8.2 ÷ 50 = 0.16. Sab 0.8 se bahut neeche hain, aur combined group sabse zyaada maar kha raha hai.

**Wajah dhoondhna.** Team ek paired test karti hai. Wo 200 asli resumes leti hai aur har ek ki do copies banaati hai jinmein sirf gender signals alag hain: mard ka ya aurat ka naam, aur ek line, ek copy mein "Captain, college cricket team" aur doosri mein "Captain, women's cricket team". Zyaadatar pairs mein auraton waale version ka score kam aata hai. Alag se, ek chhota model resume ke baaki hisse se gender ko chance se kahin behtar predict kar leta hai. College names, gendered words aur kuch hobbies proxies ka kaam kar rahe the.

**Fix.** Team model jis cheez ko score karta hai, usmein se names, photos, college names aur gendered terms hata deti hai. Wo training examples rebalance karti hai aur ek skills test score ko input ke taur par jodti hai. Phir shortlist size 480 par hi rakhkar, taaki comparison fair rahe, dobaara group-wise test karti hai.

**Fix ke baad ratios nikaalna.** Paanch colleges ke muqaable baaki colleges: 19.4 ÷ 23 = 0.84. Mardon ke muqaable auratein: 19 ÷ 20.5 = 0.93. Sabhi combined groups mein ab sabse ooncha rate paanch colleges ki auraton ka hai (120 mein se 32, 26.7%), toh sabse kam ke muqaable sabse ooncha 17.6 ÷ 26.7 = 0.66 hai. Akele-akele gaps kaafi kam ho gaye, lekin combined view abhi bhi 0.8 se neeche hai, isliye team check karti hai ki ye gap skills test scores se mel khaata hai ya nahi, aur apna judgement record karti hai.

**Sirf model nahi, product badalna.** Ab tool logon ko apne-aap reject karne ke bajaay wajahon ke saath ek shortlist suggest karta hai. Recruiters har hafte rejections ka ek sample review karte hain aur unmein se kisi ko bhi palat sakte hain. Candidates human review maang sakte hain. Team har quarter, aur har model update ke baad, group aur paired tests dohraati hai aur results client ko report karti hai.

Model zyaada fair hua, lekin bada badlaav is baat mein tha ki use use kaise kiya gaya.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Sirf overall accuracy dekhna.** Koi system average mein accurate hokar bhi kisi group ke saath unfair ho sakta hai. Results hamesha baantkar dekho, combined groups samet.
- **Ye sochna ki sensitive field hataane se bias hat jaata hai.** Surname, PIN code, college aur wording jaise proxies bache rehte hain. Test karo ki wo trait ko predict karte hain ya nahi.
- **Four-fifths rule ko pass mark maanna.** 0.85 ka ratio fairness saabit nahi karta, aur 0.75 discrimination saabit nahi karta. Ye batata hai ki kahaan dekhna hai.
- **Bahut chhote groups par bharosa karna.** 20 logon par bana rate har ek insaan se paanch points hil sakta hai. Aur data jutaao ya cases haath se review karo.
- **Data par label lagaane ke liye surnames se caste ya religion ka andaaza lagaana.** Ye aksar galat hota hai aur bina consent ke sensitive data banaata hai. Iski jagah paired tests ya consent waala audit sample use karo.
- **Ye maan lena ki AI maths hai isliye neutral hai.** Wo unhi labels aur faislon ko dikhaata hai jinse usne seekha.
- **Sirf English mein test karna.** Indian users kai languages bolte hain; un languages mein test karo jo aapke users sach mein use karte hain.
- **Human review jo bas rubber stamp ho.** Agar reviewers model ki lagbhag har baat approve kar dete hain, toh asal mein control insaanon ke haath mein nahi hai. Unhe samay, wajahein aur model ko overrule karne ki power do.
- **Ek baar test karke bhool jaana.** Data, users aur policies badalne par launch ke baad bhi bias aa sakta hai.

## Practice karo

Neeche linked free Ethics of AI course ka Chapter 6 (Fairness) padho, aur EU AI Act summary ka high-risk uses waala section.

1. **Kisi chatbot par paired test karo.** Ek hi sawaal, jaise "Patna ke 17 saal ke Arjun ke liye teen careers suggest karo", paanch baar poochho, aur har baar sirf surname badlo, aise surnames jo alag-alag communities se jude hon. Phir ek baar Hindi mein ya aapki jaani kisi aur Indian language mein poochho. Suggest kiye gaye careers, tone aur length mein fark note karo.
2. **Kisi image generator ko test karo.** "a doctor", "a nurse", "an engineer" aur "a farmer" das-das baar maango. Jo genders aur appearances milein unhe gino, aur har ek ka share nikaalo.
3. **Ratio nikaalo.** Ek college placement cell ka AI tool, milte-julte marks hone par bhi, ek campus ke 500 students mein se 40% aur doosre campus ke 300 mein se 26% ko shortlist karta hai. Selection-rate ratio nikaalo, batao ki wo 0.8 screen paas karta hai ya nahi, aur do cheezein batao jo aap aage check karoge.
4. **Bias test plan karo.** Hiring ya lending mein AI ke kisi ek use ke liye, bias aane ke teen raaste, har ek ke liye ek test, aur ye likho ki caste ya religion data na hone par aap use kaise test karoge.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** kisi Indian example se proxies samjha sako, ek selection-rate ratio nikaalkar uska matlab bata sako, aur aise system ke liye paired test describe kar sako jiska sensitive data aapke paas nahi hai.
