## Ye kya hai

AI system **biased** hota hai jab woh kuch groups ke liye kharab perform kare ya unke saath anyaay kare: gender, caste, religion, region, language, age, disability, income ya doosre traits ke hisaab se. **Fairness** un farqon ko dhoondhne aur kam karne ka kaam hai jahan woh justified nahi hain.

AI mein bias shaayad hi jaanbujhkar hota hai. Koi aisa rule nahi likhta ki "is district ke applicants ko reject karo". Ye aam taur par do jagah se aata hai: **jis data se system ne seekha**, aur **jis tareeke se system use hota hai**.

```flow
dir: LR
title: Anyaayi bhara itihaas andar, anyaayi bhare predictions baahar
Purane decisions | engineering roles ke liye zyadatar aadmi hire hue -> Model seekhta hai | "aadmi = achhe engineers": ek pattern, fact nahi
Model seekhta hai -> Model dohrata hai | nayi engineering roles ke liye women ko neeche rank karta hai
```

Ye tab aur zaroori ho jaata hai jab AI loans, jobs, school admissions, insurance aur welfare ke decisions leta ya unhe shape karta hai. Biased spell-checker khijaata hai. Biased loan model zindagiyan badal deta hai.

Is lesson mein hum Bengaluru ke ek startup ke saath chalenge jo dekhta hai ki uska resume-screening tool unfair hai, aur use theek karta hai. Lesson ke end tak aap bata paoge ki bias kahan se aata hai, use Indian settings mein pehchaan paoge aur bata paoge ki use kaise test karein.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Bias kahan se aata hai

| Source | Kya hota hai | Indian example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Historical data** | Model purane anyaayi bhare decisions ki nakal karta hai | Pehle hiring kuch colleges aur zyadatar aadmiyon ke paksh mein thi, toh model bhi wahi karta hai |
| **Missing data** | Jin groups ke kam examples hon unke results kharab aate hain | Shehri English speakers par train hua speech system gaon ke accents mein dikkat karta hai |
| **Proxies** | Doosre features kisi sensitive trait ki jagah le lete hain | Surname, PIN code ya college caste, religion ya income bata dete hain |
| **Language** | Model usi language mein sabse achha hota hai jo usne sabse zyada dekhi | Chatbot English mein achha jawab deta hai, Odia mein kharab |
| **Use** | Tool ko aise kaam ke liye use kiya jaata hai jiske liye woh bana nahi tha | Sujhaav dene ke liye bana assistant automatic gatekeeper ban jaata hai |

### Ek field hata dena kaafi kyun nahi hai

Pehla common khayal hota hai: "Bas data se gender aur caste hata do." Ye shaayad hi kaam karta hai, kyunki doosre features wahi jaankari le jaate hain. India mein surname caste ya religion bata sakta hai; PIN code income aur community bata sakta hai; college dono bata sakta hai. Model ye **proxies** dhoondh leta hai aur phir bhi purana pattern seekh leta hai.

### Bias kaise dikhta hai

- Loan model kuch districts ke kam applicants ko approve karta hai, repayment records milte-julte hone par bhi.
- Resume screener engineering roles ke liye women ko neeche rank karta hai.
- Image generator "a doctor" maangne par sirf aadmi dikhata hai aur "a nurse" par sirf auratein.
- Chatbot Indian languages mein English se chhote, kamzor jawab deta hai.
- Face-recognition system saanwale skin tones par zyada galtiyan karta hai.

### Kya kiya ja sakta hai

1. **Group-wise test karo.** Sirf overall nahi, har group ke liye accuracy aur outcomes measure karo. Bias averages mein chhupa rehta hai.
2. **Data sudhaaro.** Zyada representative examples jama karo, rebalance karo, aur nuksaan karne waale proxies kam karo.
3. **Logon ko in-charge rakho.** Jo decisions zindagi par asar daalte hain, unke liye human review rakho, model ko override karne ki taakat ke saath.
4. **Samjhao aur appeal ki ijaazat do.** Prabhaavit logon ko mukhya reasons samajh aane chahiye aur woh decision ko challenge kar sakein.
5. **Launch ke baad monitor karo.** Users aur data badalte hain; group tests regularly dohrao.

```flow
dir: LR
Banao -> Group-wise test karo -> Data ya rules sudhaaro -> Dobara test karo
Dobara test karo -[use mein monitor karo, har quarter]-> Banao
```

### Fairness mein trade-offs hote hain

Fairness ki ek se zyada definitions hain, aur woh takra sakti hain:

| Definition | Matlab | Trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equal approval rates | Har group utni hi rate se approve hota hai | Aise logon ko bhi approve kar sakta hai jinhe repayment mein dikkat ho sakti hai |
| Equal error rates | Model har group ke liye barabar baar galat hota hai | Approval rates phir bhi alag ho sakte hain |
| Milte-julte logon ke saath ek jaisa bartaav | Same record waale do logon ko same result milta hai | "Milte-julte" ka matlab kya hai, is par sahmati chahiye |

Ek chunna value decision hai, jo khule mein logon, law aur organisation ke purpose ko dhyaan mein rakhkar liya jaata hai. Ise us par chhodna theek nahi jo model train kar raha ho.

## Ek example se samjho

Bengaluru ka ek startup ek bade IT client ke liye resume-screening tool banata hai. Woh model ko client ke paanch saal ke hiring decisions par train karta hai: kaunse resumes shortlist hue aur kaunse nahi.

**Group-wise testing.** Launch se pehle team milte-julte skills aur experience waale candidates ke shortlist rates compare karti hai:

| Group (milte-julte skills aur experience) | Fix se pehle shortlist | Fix ke baad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paanch jaane-maane colleges ke graduates | 48% | 31% |
| Doosre colleges ke graduates | 14% | 27% |
| Aadmi, engineering roles | 33% | 29% |
| Auratein, engineering roles | 19% | 27% |

**Wajah dhoondhna.** Pehle ki hiring un paanch colleges aur zyadatar aadmiyon ke paksh mein thi, aur model ne wahi pattern seekh liya tha. Resumes mein college ke naam aur gendered words, jaise "women's hostel secretary", proxies ki tarah kaam kar rahe the.

**Fix.** Team model jo score karta hai usme se names, photos, college names aur gendered terms hata deti hai, training examples rebalance karti hai, aur group-wise phir test karti hai. Gaps kaafi kam ho jaate hain, par zero tak nahi.

**Sirf model nahi, product badalna.** Tool ab logon ko automatically reject karne ki jagah reasons ke saath ek shortlist sujhata hai. Recruiters har rejection review karte hain. Candidates human review maang sakte hain. Team har quarter group tests dohrati hai aur results client ko report karti hai.

Model zyada fair ho gaya, par bada badlaav ye tha ki use kaise use kiya jaata hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Sirf overall accuracy dekhna.** Ek system average mein accurate ho kar bhi kisi group ke saath anyaay kar sakta hai. Hamesha results ko todkar dekho.
- **Ye sochna ki sensitive field hatane se bias hat jaata hai.** Surname, PIN code aur college jaise proxies bache rehte hain.
- **Ye maan lena ki AI neutral hai kyunki woh maths hai.** Woh us data aur decisions ko dikhata hai jinse usne seekha.
- **Sirf English mein test karna.** Indian users kai languages bolte hain; unhi mein test karo jo aapke users bolte hain.
- **High-stakes decisions ko bina human review ya appeal ke automate karna.** Kisi insaan ko accountable rakho.
- **Ek baar test karke bhool jaana.** Data aur users badalne par bias launch ke baad bhi aa sakta hai.

## Practice karo

Free Ethics of AI course ka fairness waala chapter lo, neeche linked, aur EU AI Act summary ka high-risk uses waala section padho.

1. **Image generator test karo.** "a doctor", "a nurse", "an engineer" aur "a farmer" har ek dus baar maango. Jo genders aur appearances aaye unhe note karo.
2. **Languages compare karo.** Kisi chat assistant se wahi sawaal English mein aur apni jaani hui Indian language mein poochho, aur answers ki quality aur length compare karo.
3. **Bias test plan karo.** Hiring ya lending mein kisi ek AI use ke liye likho ki bias teen tareeke se kaise ghus sakta hai, aur har ek ke liye ek test.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap proxies ko ek Indian example se samjha sako aur bata sako ki kisi system ko group-wise bias ke liye kaise test karoge.
