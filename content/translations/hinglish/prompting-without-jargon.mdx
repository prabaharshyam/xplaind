## Ye kya hai

**Prompt** woh sab hai jo aap AI assistant ko type karte ho. Achhe prompts likhne ke liye khaas tricks, jaadui phrases ya technical words ki zaroorat nahi. Achha prompt ek saaf brief jaisa hota hai jo aap kisi kaabil naye colleague ko dete ho, jo aam taur par bahut kuch jaanta hai par aapki situation ke baare mein kuch nahi.

Chaar saadi aadatein zyadatar kaam kar deti hain:

```flow
dir: LR
1. Context | aap kaun, kiske liye, kyun, facts. Hamesha -> 2. Example | achha kaisa dikhta hai. Jab ho sake
2. Example -> 3. Format | length, structure, tone, language. Hamesha
3. Format -> 4. Feedback | padho, specific badlaav maango. Hamesha
```

Ye aadatein bahut fark daalti hain. Ek-line ka request generic jawab laata hai, kyunki assistant ko sab kuch andaaza lagana padta hai. Achha brief pehli koshish mein hi kaam-chalau cheez de deta hai.

Ye lesson Ravi ke saath chalta hai, jo Coimbatore mein tuition centre chalata hai, aur ek dhundhle request ko aise message mein badalta hai jo woh parents ko bhej sake. End tak aap chaaron aadatein bina sochhe use karoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Aadat 1: context do

Assistant nahi jaan sakta ki aap kaun ho, jawab kaun padhega, ya aapki situation ke facts kya hain. Use batao.

| Weak | Strong |
| --- | --- |
| "Write a leave application." | "I'm a nurse at a hospital in Madurai. Write a short, polite leave application to my head nurse for three days next week, 12 to 14 November, for my sister's wedding. I have already arranged for a colleague to cover my shifts." |

Strong version padhne waale, dates, wajah aur ek tasalli dene waali detail deta hai. Assistant ko inme se kuch gadhna nahi padta, jiska matlab ye bhi hai ki woh kuch bana dene ke kam chance mein hai.

### Aadat 2: example dikhao

Agar aapke paas koi purani email, report ya message hai jo aapko pasand aaya, toh private details hataakar use paste karo aur kaho "isi style mein likho". Style ko words mein describe karne se examples behtar kaam karte hain, kyunki model patterns copy karne mein bahut achha hai. Model ko input aur output ke kuch examples dena taaki woh pattern follow kare, **few-shot prompting** kehlaata hai.

```text
  Example 1:  Product: steel water bottle  ->  "Stays cold 24 hours. Built for Chennai summers."
  Example 2:  Product: cotton kurta         ->  "Breathes in the heat. Looks sharp at work."
  Now write:  Product: jute tote bag        ->  ?
```

### Aadat 3: format maango

Saaf batao ki aapko wapas kya chahiye. Format instructions likhna sasta hai aur bahut editing bachata hai:

- "five bullet points"
- "under 120 words"
- "a table with columns: item, cost, notes"
- "in simple Tamil, then the same in English"
- "formal but warm"
- "start with the recommendation"

### Aadat 4: specific feedback do

Pehla jawab ek draft hai. Use padho aur saaf batao kya badalna hai:

| Vague feedback | Specific feedback |
| --- | --- |
| "Make it better." | "Cut it to 80 words and drop the second paragraph." |
| "That's wrong." | "Our office closes at 6, not 7. Fix the timing." |
| "Too formal." | "Write it the way you'd text a colleague you like." |

Do-teen round ke specific feedback se aam taur par kaam ban jaata hai.

### Doosri kaam ki chaalein

- **Pehle use se sawaal poochhne ko kaho:** "Likhne se pehle mujhse jo jaanna ho woh poochho." Isse aksar feedback ka ek round bach jaata hai.
- **Options maango:** "Mujhe teen alag subject lines do."
- **Apni soch check karwao:** "Is plan mein kya galat ho sakta hai?"
- **Bade kaam ko steps mein todo:** pehle outline maango, uspar sehmat ho jao, phir har section maango.

### Kitna daal sakte ho

Har model ka ek **context window** hota hai: ek baar mein kitna text woh le sakta hai, aapka prompt, paste kiye documents aur ab tak ki conversation sahit. Aaj ke assistants bahut kuch rakh sakte hain, aksar sainkdon pages, par bahut lambi conversations bhatak sakti hain. Agar assistant aapke pehle ke instructions bhoolne lage, toh naya chat shuru karo aur mukhya baatein dobara likho.

### Kya share nahi karna

Passwords, Aadhaar, PAN ya bank details, aur confidential work information prompts se door rakho, jab tak aapke organisation ne us tool ko us tarah ke data ke liye approve na kiya ho.

## Ek worked example

Ravi Coimbatore mein classes 8 se 10 ke liye ek chhota tuition centre chalata hai. Use naye weekend batches announce karne ke liye WhatsApp message chahiye.

**Pehli koshish:**

> "Write a message about weekend classes."

Result 180 words ka hai, generic, "all subjects" aur "expert faculty" likha hai, aur paanch emojis hain. Kaam ka nahi.

**Doosri koshish, context aur format ke saath:**

> "I run a tuition centre in Coimbatore for classes 8 to 10, maths and science. Write a WhatsApp message to parents announcing new weekend batches starting 7 December, Saturday and Sunday, 9 to 11 am. Fees ₹1,500 a month. Seats limited to 15. Keep it under 80 words, friendly, with at most one emoji. Write it in English, then the same in simple Tamil."

Result kareeb hai. Ravi **specific feedback** deta hai:

> "Mention that the first class is a free trial, and put the phone number 98xxxxxx21 at the end of both versions."

Teesra version taiyaar hai. Bhejne se pehle woh apni behen se, jo Tamil padhaati hai, Tamil wording check karwaata hai. Woh ek word theek kar deti hai.

| Version | Kaunsi aadatein use hui | Kaam ka? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Koi nahi | Nahi: generic, bahut lamba |
| 2 | Context, format | Lagbhag: do details missing |
| 3 | Context, format, feedback | Haan, Tamil check ke baad |

Poore kaam mein lagbhag paanch minute lage, aur message ka har fact Ravi se aaya, assistant ki kalpana se nahi.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Ek-line ke requests.** Context aur format jodo; tees second lagte hain.
- **Pehla jawab maan lena.** Use draft samjho aur feedback do.
- **Vague feedback.** "Make it better" se "chhota karo, aur kam formal" kahin kam madad karta hai.
- **Style dikhane ki jagah describe karna.** Example ho toh paste karo.
- **Private data share karna.** Naam, numbers aur confidential details hataao, jab tak tool approved na ho.
- **Ye sochna ki achha prompt facts ki guarantee hai.** Achhe prompts jawab behtar karte hain; woh hamesha sahi nahi bana dete. Jo facts maayne rakhte hain unhe check karo.

## Practice karo

Anthropic ka free AI Fluency course lo (neeche link hai), ya OpenAI Academy ke beginner sessions dekho.

1. **Weak se shuru karo.** Apne hafte ka ek asli kaam chuno, jaise message, plan ya explanation. Ek-line ka prompt likho aur jawab save karo.
2. **Aadatein lagao.** Prompt ko context, example (agar ho) aur format ke saath dobara likho. Naya jawab save karo.
3. **Feedback do.** Do round ka specific feedback do aur teeno versions ko saath-saath compare karo.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap apna ek-line ka prompt aur behtar prompt saath-saath dikha sako, aur samjha sako ki fark kisne daala.
