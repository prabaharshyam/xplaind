## Ye kya hai

"Human in the loop rakho" AI product design ki sabse common salah hai, aur sabse zyada kharaab tareeke se ki jaane waali bhi. Jis insaan ko ek ghante mein 500 AI decisions par bina information ke "approve" click karna hai, woh oversight nahi hai. Woh naam likha hua rubber stamp hai.

**Human in the loop** ka matlab hai ki log AI feature ke kaam karne ka hissa hain, sirf uske output ke users nahi. Chaar common roles hain:

| Role | Insaan kya karta hai | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Review** | Use hone se pehle AI output check karta hai | Support agent bhejne se pehle AI ka drafted reply padhta hai |
| **Approve** | AI ke propose kiye action ko authorise karta hai | Manager AI ke suggest kiye refund ko approve karta hai |
| **Handle exceptions** | Mushkil ya uncertain cases leta hai | AI routine claims sambhaalta hai; adjusters unusual waale lete hain |
| **Audit** | Baad mein ek sample check karta hai | Quality lead har hafte 5% automated decisions review karta hai |

Asli product ka sawaal "kya insaan hona chahiye?" nahi hai, balki **kahan, kin cases ke liye, kaunsi information ke saath, aur kitni tezi se**.

Ye lesson Arjun ke saath chalta hai, jo Bengaluru ke ek lending app mein product manager hai, jab woh design karta hai ki AI-assisted loan review mein credit officers kahan baithein. End tak aap aise human roles design kar paoge jo tez aur meaningful hon, aur plan kar paoge ki evidence badhne par woh kaise badlenge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Risk aur confidence se decide karo

Apne case types ko do sawaalon par map karo: galti kitni mehngi hai, aur AI ke sahi hone ki kitni sambhaavna hai?

| Galti ki cost | AI confidence kam | AI confidence high |
|---|---|---|
| High | Expert se **approve** karwao, ya AI use mat karo | Har case **approve** ya **review** karo |
| Low | **Review**, ya **exception** ki tarah route karo | **Automate** karo, aur ek sample **audit** karo |

Unusual cases, jo aapke evaluation set mein kisi se mel nahi khaate, kuch bhi ho experts ke paas jaate hain.

### Review moment ko design karo

Achha review screen checking ko tez aur asli banata hai:

1. **Output uske sources ke saath**, taaki reviewer ko doosre systems na kholne padein.
2. **Jo check karna hai use highlight karo**: amounts, names, dates aur woh fields jinke baare mein AI ko shak tha.
3. Approve, edit aur reject ke liye **ek-ek action**.

Agar reviewers ko decide karne ke liye doosre systems mein dhoondhna padta hai, toh woh ek hafte mein check karna band kar dete hain.

### Automation bias par nazar rakho

Log machine ke suggestions ko accept karne ki taraf jhukte hain, khaaskar jab busy ya thake hon. Ise **automation bias** kehte hain. Iska muqabla karo:

- Uncertainty saaf dikhao, har answer ko same confidence ke saath pesh karne ki jagah.
- Key fields ke liye reviewer se active confirm karwao, jaise income figure par tap karna, ek "approve all" button ki jagah.
- **Override rates** naapo. Zero ke paas override rate ka matlab ho sakta hai ki AI perfect hai, ya ye ki koi asal mein check nahi kar raha.
- Kabhi-kabhi jaan-boojhkar galti waale known test cases daalo, dhyaan check karne ke liye.

### Capacity ka plan banao

Reviews mein time lagta hai. Andaaza lagao:

```text
  daily volume needing review  x  minutes per review  =  minutes a day
        600 applications        x        3 min        =  1,800 min = 30 hours
```

Pakka karo ki team ke paas utne ghante hain. Warna queues badhti hain, log jaldbaazi karte hain, aur review quality chupchaap gir jaati hai.

### Accountability saaf karo

Jab approve hua AI output galat nikle, toh zimmedaar kaun hai? Aam taur par approver, jo tabhi insaaf hai jab unke paas zaroori information aur time tha. Likh do.

### Loop ko improve karne ke liye use karo

Har edit aur rejection aapke paas sabse achha data hai ki AI kahan fail hota hai. Unhe evaluation set aur fixes mein daalo. Jo loop system ko behtar nahi karta woh bas extra kaam hai.

### Evidence ke saath oversight badlo

| Stage | Kam-risk cases | High-risk cases |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Launch | Har ek ko approve ya review | Har ek ko approve |
| Maano teen mahine quality tiki rahi | Sample audit par le jao | Phir bhi har ek ko approve |
| Aage chalte hue | Audit, quality girne par alerts ke saath | Approve; kabhi poori tarah automate mat karo |

Strict se shuru karo. Sirf measure kiye hue evidence ke saath dheel do, aur har badlaav document karo.

### Jab user hi insaan ho

Consumer products mein user aksar khud reviewer hota hai: email draft jo woh edit karta hai, suggestion jo woh accept karta hai. Aise design karo ki accept karna kabhi akela aasaan option na ho, aur edit karna utna hi tez ho.

## Ek worked example

Bengaluru ka ek lending app loan application reviews ko pre-fill karne ke liye AI use karta hai: woh bank statements se income nikaalta hai, risks flag karta hai, aur approve, reject ya refer suggest karta hai. Product manager Arjun human role design karta hai.

**Decisions logon ke paas rehte hain.** Credit officers har application approve ya reject karte hain; AI sirf suggest karta hai. Lender ki credit policy har lending decision ke liye ek named insaan ko accountable rakhti hai, fairness ke liye aur apne regulator ke liye.

**Review screen** dikhata hai:

- suggested decision aur teen main reasons;
- extract ki hui monthly income, jis bank statement ki lines se aayi woh highlight ke saath;
- risk flags laal rang mein, jaise haal hi mein bounce hui EMI.

Decision buttons unlock hone se pehle officers ko income figure par tap karke confirm karna padta hai. Ye ek chhota step "bina dekhe approve" ko rokta hai.

**Overrides track hote hain** officer aur reason ke hisaab se. Pehle mahine officers AI ke 14% suggestions override karte hain. Sabse common reason, "salary credited in two parts", dikhata hai ki extraction step mein split salary credits chhoot rahe the. Engineers fix karte hain.

**Capacity:** roz 600 applications, har ek 3 minute, yaani 30 officer-hours, jo team ki 40 ki capacity ke andar hai.

**Audit:** ek senior credit manager har hafte approved files ka 5% review karta hai.

| Measure | AI se pehle | Teen mahine baad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Har application ka review time | 9 minute | 3 minute |
| Override rate | Lagu nahi | Mahina 1 mein 14%, mahina 3 mein 6% |
| Approvals par default rate | Baseline | Manual process jaisa hi |
| Kaun decide karta hai | Officer | Officer |

Review teen guna tez hua, quality tiki rahi, aur decisions un logon ke paas rahe jo unke liye accountable hain.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Sirf naam ka human.** Information aur time ke bina review rubber stamp hai.
- **Hamesha sab kuch review karna.** Evidence badhne par kam-risk cases ko audit par le jao.
- **Capacity ko ignore karna.** Review queues badhti hain aur quality girti hai.
- **Unclear accountability.** Likh do ki approved outputs ka jawab kaun dega.
- **Overrides ko waste karna.** Ye improvement ke liye sabse achha signal hain.
- **Zero override rate ko achhi khabar maanna.** Iska matlab ho sakta hai ki koi check nahi kar raha.

## Practice karo

Neeche link kiye PAIR Guidebook ke feedback aur control chapters padho, aur Human-AI Interaction guidelines.

1. **Cases map karo.** Apne chune hue AI feature ke liye case types ko galti ki cost aur AI confidence se map karo, aur har ek ko automate, review, approve, exception ya audit mein se ek do.
2. **Screen sketch karo.** Sources, highlighted fields aur one-click actions ke saath review screen banao.
3. **Capacity aur badlaav ka plan banao.** Review capacity ka andaaza lagao, aur likho ki oversight kab dheela kiya ja sakta hai.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap dikha sako ki aapke feature mein log kahan baithte hain, woh kya dekhte hain, kitna time lagta hai aur evidence ke saath ye kaise badlega.
