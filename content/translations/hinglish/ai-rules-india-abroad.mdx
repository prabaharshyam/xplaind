## Ye kya hai

AI koi bina kanoon ki jagah nahi hai. Jab koi app AI se aapka loan decide karta hai, aapke messages padhta hai ya aapka chehra generate karta hai, toh kai rules pehle se lagoo hote hain. India ke logon ke liye teen sabse zaroori hain:

| Rule | Ye kya hai | Kispar asar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act)** | Digital personal data par India ka law, jiske rules November 2025 mein notify hue aur lagbhag atharah mahine mein phase-in honge | India ke logon ka digital personal data sambhaalne waali koi bhi organisation, AI systems mein bhi |
| **India ki AI Governance Guidelines** | Electronics aur IT Ministry ke safe aur trusted AI ke principles, November 2025 mein jaari | Developers, deployers aur regulators; zyadatar voluntary, maujooda laws par tiki hui |
| **EU AI Act** | Pehla broad AI law, jo AI uses ko risk ke hisaab se baantta hai | Wo companies jinka AI European Union mein use hota hai, Indian firms bhi |

Doosre maujooda laws bhi AI par lagoo rehte hain: Information Technology Act, consumer protection, fraud aur impersonation ke liye criminal law, aur banking, insurance aur health ke sector rules. Bank ke AI par RBI waise hi nazar rakhta hai jaise uske baaki systems par.

```flow
title: India mein AI app ko chhoone waale rules, sabse chaude se sabse sankre tak
AI Governance Guidelines | upar principles -> Sector rules | banks ke liye RBI, insurance ke liye IRDAI
Sector rules -> General laws | IT Act, consumer, criminal
General laws -> DPDP Act | aapka personal data
EU AI Act | agar product Europe mein use ho
```

Is lesson mein hum Deepa ke saath chalenge, jo Jaipur mein teacher hain, jab woh ek app se loan ke liye apply karti hain aur apne rights use karti hain. Lesson ke end tak aap saaf shabdon mein bata paoge ki in rules ka ek aam user ke liye kya matlab hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### DPDP Act saaf shabdon mein

DPDP Act us organisation ko jo tay karti hai ki aapka data kaise use hoga, **data fiduciary** kehta hai, aur aapko, yaani insaan ko, **data principal**. Mukhya duties aur rights:

- **Notice aur consent.** Organisations ko saaf batana hota hai ki kaunsa personal data kyun collect kar rahi hain, aur aam taur par uske liye aapka consent lena hota hai.
- **Kabhi bhi wapas lo.** Aap consent utni hi aasaani se wapas le sakte ho jitni aasaani se diya tha.
- **Dekho, sudhaaro, erase karwao.** Aap apne data ka summary maang sakte ho, use sudhaar sakte ho, aur jab zaroorat na rahe toh erase karwa sakte ho.
- **Security aur breaches.** Organisations ko aapka data protect karna hota hai, aur breach hone par aapko aur Data Protection Board of India ko batana hota hai.
- **Bachche.** Bachchon ke data ke liye maa-baap ka verifiable consent chahiye, aur bachchon ko track nahi kiya ja sakta na unhe advertising se target kiya ja sakta hai.
- **Penalties.** Badi penalties lagoo hoti hain, reasonable security safeguards na lene par ₹250 crore tak.
- **Shikayatein.** Aap pehle organisation ke grievance officer se shikayat karte ho, phir Data Protection Board se.

In duties aur rights mein se zyadatar May 2027 se lagoo hote hain, yaani rules ke 13 November 2025 ko notify hone ke atharah mahine baad. Tab tak company kanooni roop se bandhi na ho, par poochhna aksar phir bhi kaam karta hai, aur RBI jaise sector rules regulated lenders se pehle hi grievance officer rakhwate hain.

```flow
dir: LR
Aap data dete ho -[consent]-> Organisation sirf bataye hisaab se use karti hai
Aap consent wapas lete ho -> Organisation ruk jaati hai aur erase karti hai
Aap apna data dekhna chahte ho -> Organisation summary dikhati hai
Aap shikayat karte ho -> Grievance officer
Grievance officer -[solve nahi hua?]-> Data Protection Board of India
```

### India ki AI Governance Guidelines

Guidelines logon ko pehle rakhti hain aur trust, fairness, accountability, transparency aur safety ko badhaava deti hain. Woh AI policy ko coordinate karne ke liye naye institutions aur industry ke liye voluntary measures recommend karti hain, jaise transparency reports aur logon ke grievance uthaane ke tareeke. Zaroori baat, woh alag AI law **nahi** banatin. Woh risk sambhaalne ke liye maujooda laws aur sector regulators par nirbhar karti hain, aur innovation ko bhi badhaava deti hain.

### EU AI Act

EU AI Act AI uses ko risk levels mein baantta hai:

| Risk level | Examples | Kya zaroori hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unacceptable | Anyaayi bartaav tak le jaane waali social scoring, logon ko nuksaan pahunchane waale tareeke se manipulate karna | Banned |
| High | Hiring, credit scoring, education, essential services, kuch medical uses mein AI | Risk management, achha data, human oversight, records, transparency |
| Limited | Chatbots, AI-generated content | Logon ko batao ki woh AI se baat kar rahe hain; kuch generated content ko label karo |
| Minimal | Spam filters, games mein AI | Koi nayi duty nahi |

Jin Indian companies ke AI products Europe mein use hote hain, unhe ise follow karna hota hai, aur iska tareeka doosri jagah ke rules aur customer expectations ko bhi shape kar raha hai.

### Aapke liye iska matlab

- Agree karne se pehle padho ki app aapke data ke saath kya karne ka dawa karta hai.
- Jo permissions app ko nahi chahiye, unhe band karo.
- Apna data dekhne, sudhaarne aur delete karne ke apne rights use karo.
- Ummeed rakho ki kai situations mein aapko bataya jaayega ki aap AI se deal kar rahe ho.
- Agar AI ka koi decision aapko nuksaan pahunchaye, jaise loan ya job application reject hona, toh poochho ki woh kaise liya gaya aur appeal kaise karein.

## Ek example se samjho

Deepa, Jaipur ki ek school teacher, ko apni maa ki surgery ke liye ₹60,000 ka personal loan chahiye. Woh ek lending app download karti hain.

**Permissions.** App unke contacts, photos aur SMS ka access maangta hai. Woh notice padhti hain: contacts "services behtar banane ke liye" use hote hain. Ye dhundhla hai aur loan ke liye zaroori nahi, isliye woh mana kar deti hain. App phir bhi unki application process karta hai, jisse pata chalta hai ki permission kabhi zaroori thi hi nahi.

**Tez reject.** Do minute baad bina kisi reason ke woh reject ho jaati hain. Unhe automated decision ka shak hota hai. Woh app mein listed lender ke grievance officer ko likhti hain: "Kripya meri application par decision ke mukhya reasons samjhaiye aur kisi insaan se review karwaiye." Ek hafte ke andar lender mukhya reasons ke saath jawab deta hai, ek purani unpaid credit card dues ki entry, aur Deepa dikhati hain ki woh bhar di gayi thi, toh ek insaan unki file review karta hai. Loan approve ho jaata hai.

**Chhodna.** Ek mahine baad woh loan chuka deti hain aur app use karna band kar deti hain. Woh consent wapas leti hain aur apna data erase karne ko kehti hain. Company email se deletion confirm karti hai, jo woh save kar leti hain.

**Dost ki madad.** Jab unki dost ka data kisi doosre app ke leak mein dikhta hai, toh Deepa use check karne mein madad karti hain ki company ne kya disclose kiya hai, passwords badalne mein, aur ye note karne mein ki company jawab na de toh Data Protection Board mein shikayat kaise karein.

| Situation | Deepa ne kaunsa right ya rule use kiya |
| --- | --- |
| Dhundhli contacts permission | Notice aur consent; jo zaroori nahi use mana karo |
| Turant reject | Grievance officer ke zariye reasons aur human review maango |
| Use band karna | Consent wapas lo aur erasure request karo |
| Dost ka leak | Breach notice duties; Data Protection Board mein shikayat |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Har permission accept kar lena.** Kai apps zaroorat se zyada maangte hain. Jo zaroori nahi use mana karo.
- **Ye sochna ki AI unregulated hai.** Data protection, consumer, criminal aur sector laws pehle se lagoo hain.
- **Apne rights use na karna.** Aap apna data dekhne, sudhaarne aur delete karwane ko kah sakte ho, aur shikayat bhi kar sakte ho.
- **Ye maan lena ki EU rules India mein maayne nahi rakhte.** Europe ko serve karne waale Indian products ko unhe follow karna hota hai, aur woh global practice ko shape karte hain.
- **Automated rejection ko final maan lena.** Reasons aur human review maango.
- **Guidelines ko law samajhna.** India ki AI Governance Guidelines principles hain; bandhan waale rules maujooda laws aur regulators se aate hain.

## Practice karo

PRS Legislative Research ka DPDP law par summary padho, neeche linked, aur EU AI Act ka high-level summary.

1. **Do privacy notices padho.** Apne use ke do apps chuno. Likho ki har ek kaunsa data kyun collect karta hai, aur use delete kaise karein.
2. **Permissions saaf karo.** Apne phone par ek app ki permissions check karo aur jo zaroori nahi unhe band karo.
3. **Erasure request likho.** Teen sentences mein likho ki consent wapas lene aur apna data erase karwane ke liye aap company ko kya bhejoge.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap kisi dost ko apne personal data par teen rights, shikayat kahan karni hai, aur EU AI Act jo ek cheez ban karta hai woh samjha sako.
