## Ye kya hai

**Veo**, Google DeepMind ke **text-to-video** models ka parivaar hai. Aap ek shot describe karte ho, ya use images dete ho, aur woh ek chhoti, realistic clip banata hai. Veo 3 (2025) se clips **synchronised sound** ke saath aati hain: picture se mel khaati ambience, effects aur boli gayi lines.

**Flow**, Google ka AI filmmaking studio hai. Aap ek director ki tarah kaam karte ho: shots banate ho, characters aur products ko dobara use hone wale references ki tarah rakhte ho, jaldi khatam hone wale shots ko extend karte ho, aur clips ko scenes mein sajaate ho.

Flow ke andar ke models aksar badalte hain. 28 November 2026 tak, reports ke mutaabik Google ke Flow help pages ye kehte hain:

- **Gemini Omni Flash 1.2**, 5 November 2026 ko release hua, un do models mein se ek hai jo Google naye projects ke liye suggest karta hai. Ye Veo model nahi hai. Ye 3 se 15 seconds ki clips banata hai, lip-sync wale dialogue, aapki chuni hui voices, aur pehle se bani clips ki editing ke saath.
- **Veo 3.1 Lite** doosra hai, aur akela Veo model jo bana rahega. Ye sound ke saath 4, 6 ya 8 seconds ki clips banata hai, 720p par Omni se prati second kam credits mein.
- **Veo 3.1 Fast**, **Veo 3.1 Quality** aur **Omni Flash 1.1** Flow se 5 December 2026 ko retire ho rahe hain. Kai tutorials, jinmein yahan linked videos bhi hain, ab bhi inhi ko dikhaate hain. Unki directing advice ab bhi kaam ki hai; bas naam aur prices badal gaye hain.

### Model clip kaise banata hai

Veo ek **diffusion model** hai: uska model card kehta hai ki Veo 3 latent diffusion use karta hai, aur Veo 3.1 Lite, Veo 3 par bana hai. Woh video ke ek compressed roop mein random noise se shuru karta hai aur aapke prompt aur images ke hisaab se noise ko kadam-dar-kadam hataata hai, jab tak aise frames na ban jaayein jo samay ke saath aapas mein mel khaayein. Ek director ke liye isse do baatein nikalti hain:

- Model woh predict karta hai jo *dikhne* mein sahi lage, woh nahi jo sach hai. Jab tak aap dikhao nahi, use pata nahi ho sakta ki aapki asli saree ya logo kaisa dikhta hai.
- Har generation ek naya random draw hai, isliye ek hi prompt har baar alag take deta hai. To kai takes banao, aur reference images par bharosa karo.

```flow

Idea -> Shot list | 4 se 6 shots, har ek mein ek action
Shot list -> Reference images | character, product, style

Takes review karo -[achha take]-> Edit | kram, music, text, title card

Edit -> Label lagao aur publish karo
```

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Videos nahi, shots mein socho

Har generation kuch seconds ka ek shot hai; 30-second ki ad paanch ya chhah shots hoti hai jo editor mein jode jaate hain. Pehle ek **shot list** likho: kya dikhega, camera, sound aur kitne seconds. Yahi aapka budget bhi hai, kyunki credits prati second ya prati clip lagte hain.

30-second ki Reel ke liye ek achha default: ek establishing shot, do ya teen detail shots, product use karte kisi insaan ka ek shot, aur ek title card. Generate hone wale shots 4 se 8 seconds ke beech rakho: woh kam kharch karte hain, kam fail hote hain aur aasaani se judte hain.

### Prompts waise likho jaise cinematographer baat karta hai

Mazboot prompt ek shot ko ek action ke saath describe karta hai. Usmein chhah cheezein hoti hain:

| Element | Kya kehna hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | kaun ya kya, tay details ke saath | an elderly weaver with silver hair, cream kurta |
| Action | ek saaf action | slides the shuttle across the loom |
| Setting | jagah, samay, mausam | a dim workshop in Varanasi, morning light through a window |
| Camera | shot size, angle, movement, lens | close-up, slow dolly in, 50 mm, shallow depth of field |
| Lighting aur look | mood, colour, texture | warm window light, low contrast, gentle film grain |
| Sound | ambience, effects, dialogue | clack of the loom, distant temple bells, no music |

"Make it look like a movie" ka model ke liye koi matlab nahi hai. Uski jagah craft ka naam lo: lens (35 mm, anamorphic), movement (slow dolly in, handheld, drone push), lighting (low-key, practical lamps, golden hour), aur texture (film grain, 24 fps motion blur). Ye bhi batao ki aapko kya *nahi* chahiye: "no text on screen", "no music", "no people".

Dialogue ke liye, har shot mein har speaker ki quotes mein ek chhoti line do, aur batao ki kaun bol raha hai. Flow mein, Omni Flash aur ingredients ke saath, aap prompt box ke neeche har generation ke liye ek voice chun sakte ho aur prompt mein uska zikr kar sakte ho, taaki character ki voice wahi rahe.

### Pehle aspect ratio chuno

Instagram Reels aur Stories, YouTube Shorts aur WhatsApp Status ke liye portrait 9:16 use karo, aur YouTube videos, websites aur presentations ke liye landscape 16:9. Flow ka help page Veo 3.1 Lite ke liye dono list karta hai, aur reports kehti hain ki Omni Flash 1.2 bhi dono deta hai; check karo ki aapke mode mein 9:16 hai. Pehle take se pehle ise set karo aur har shot ke liye yahi rakho: baad mein landscape ko portrait mein crop karne se frame ka kareeb do-tihaai hissa chala jaata hai, aksar aapke subject ke chehre ke saath.

### Banane ke teen tareeke use karo

- **Text to video:** sirf prompt. Establishing shots aur moods ke liye achha, jahan exact look maayne nahi rakhta.
- **Frames to video:** aap pehla frame dete ho, aur chaaho to aakhri bhi. Composition aur movement ki shuruaat aur ant aapke control mein rehte hain. Product shots aur continuity theek karne ke liye sabse achha.
- **Ingredients to video** (ise references bhi kehte hain): aap kai images dete ho, jaise ek character, ek product aur ek style frame, aur model inhe ek jaisa rakhta hai. Shots mein wahi insaan ya wahi product rakhne ke liye sabse achha.

Do finishing tools bhi kaam aate hain. **Extend** jaldi khatam hone wali clip mein seconds jodta hai, aur **editing** (Omni Flash 1.2) kisi maujooda clip ko instruction se badalta hai, jaise "make it dusk".

### Shot ke hisaab se model chuno

| Shot ki zaroorat | Pehle try karne wala model, November 2026 tak | Kyun |
|---|---|---|
| Character ya product jise waisa hi rehna hai | ingredients ke saath Omni Flash 1.2 | kai references leta hai, clips edit kar sakta hai |
| 8 seconds se lamba shot | Omni Flash 1.2 | paid plans par 15 seconds tak, ooche rate par |
| Dialogue | ingredients aur chuni hui voice ke saath Omni Flash 1.2 | lip-sync; har generation mein ek chuni hui voice |
| Simple establishing ya mood shot, 8 seconds tak | Veo 3.1 Lite | prati second kam cost, sound saath mein |
| Kisi idea ka sasta test | 360p par Omni Flash 1.2 | prati second 1 credit |

Ise shuruaat maano: poori ad par commit karne se pehle ek shot dono models par chalao aur compare karo.

### Characters aur products ko ek jaisa rakho

AI video mein consistency sabse mushkil hissa hai, aur yahi amateur aur professional kaam ko sabse zyaada alag karta hai.

1. Har character aur product ki **ek mazboot reference image banao**. Asli product ki photo barabar daylight mein kheencho. Character ke liye, ek saaf, saamne dekhti image banao (Flow mein Google ke image models bhi hain; AI image generation wala B2 lesson dekho).
2. Jahan bhi woh aaye, **har shot mein wahi reference attach karo**, ingredient ya first frame ki tarah.
3. **Har prompt mein wahi chhoti description paste karo**, shabd-dar-shabd: "Meera, 25, short curly hair, mustard kurta, silver jhumkas".
4. **Frames se shots jodo.** Shot 4 ka aakhri frame shot 5 ke pehle frame ki tarah use karo.
5. **Har retry mein ek hi cheez badlo.** Do ya teen takes banao aur sabse achha rakho. Agar koi take fail ho, to ek cheez badlo; agar prompt, model aur reference ek saath badloge, to pata nahi chalega ki kisse fark pada. Adjectives se behtar specific instructions kaam karte hain: "hands stay on the loom", "no text on screen".

### Jaano ki models kismein kamzor hain

Gemini Omni Flash ke liye Google ka model card kehta hai ki complex motion, poori tarah sahi text aur edits mein poori consistency ab bhi mushkil hain. Baaki rows aam practice se hain. Inke hisaab se plan karo.

| Weakness | Kya dikhta hai | Workaround |
|---|---|---|
| On-screen text aur logos | galat spelling wale words, tedhe letters | saara text, prices aur logos editor mein jodo |
| Complex motion | tez action mein haath-pair mudte hain, objects aapas mein mil jaate hain | har shot mein ek simple action, dheeme moves |
| Hands aur fine detail | ajeeb ungliyaan, objects mein milte haath | simple hand moves; extra takes ka budget rakho |
| Crowds | chehre blur aur repeat hote hain | aage kam log, peeche ek soft crowd |
| Shots ke beech drift | dress ya chehre ka colour ya shape badal jaata hai | wahi reference aur description dobara use karo; frames to video use karo |
| Lamba dialogue | lip-sync khisakta hai, words repeat hote hain, voice badalti hai | har shot mein ek chhoti line, ek fixed voice |

### Generator ke baahar edit karo

Clips ko CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, Premiere ya Flow ke scene tools mein kaato. Music, voice-over, saara text, logo aur title card wahin jodo. Loudness barabar karo, kyunki har clip ka sound alag se mix hota hai.

### Apne credits ka budget banao

Flow har generation ke liye **credits** leta hai. Ye figures Google ke pages se reported hain, 28 November 2026 ko check kiye gaye; model chunte samay Flow jo cost dikhata hai, agar woh alag ho to wahi maano.

| Access | Bharat mein price | Flow credits |
|---|---|---|
| Free Google account | free | roz 50 credits, bache hue credits aage nahi jaate |
| Google AI Plus | ₹399 mahina | 200 credits mahina |
| Google AI Pro | ₹1,950 mahina | 1,000 credits mahina |

Har generation ki cost, usi din check ki gayi:

- **Omni Flash 1.2:** 360p par prati second 1 credit, 720p par prati second 3 credits. Free users 10 seconds tak ki clips bana sakte hain; 11 se 15 seconds aur extend karne ke liye paid plan chahiye, aur reports kehti hain ki 10 ke baad har second ki cost doguni hai.
- **Veo 3.1 Lite:** Ultra se neeche ke plans par, 720p par 4, 6 ya 8 seconds ki har clip ke 10 credits.

Monthly credits har billing cycle mein reset hote hain aur aage nahi jaate.

### Publish karna: labels, watermarks aur rights

Ad live hone se pehle ye check karo (28 November 2026 tak).

- **Watermarks.** Har Flow clip mein **SynthID** hota hai, ek invisible watermark jise Google ke tools pakad sakte hain. Flow ka help page ye bhi kehta hai ki agar aap Bharat mein rehte ho to ek visible watermark apne aap lagta hai; press reports kehti hain ki wahan sirf AI Ultra subscribers use band kar sakte hain. Shots aise frame karo ki woh kisi zaroori cheez ko na dhake, aur use kabhi crop ya blur mat karo.
- **Bharat ke IT Rules.** 20 February 2026 se laagu amendments "synthetically generated" audio aur video ko cover karte hain. Bade social media platforms ko users se poochna hai ki upload synthetic hai ya nahi, jawaab check karna hai, synthetic content par saaf label lagaana hai, aur labels ya metadata hataane nahi dena hai. Aapka hissa: sach jawaab do aur platform ka AI label use karo.
- **Instagram aur Facebook.** Meta kehta hai ki photorealistic AI video ya realistic AI audio post karte samay woh logon se apna AI disclosure tool use karwa sakta hai, aur "AI info" label jod sakta hai.
- **Advertising code.** Bharat ke advertising self-regulator ASCI ne September 2026 mein ads mein AI content par label lagaane ki guidelines jaari keen, jo kareeb teen mahine baad laagu honi hain. Ye risk-based hain: jahan AI content kisi buyer ko gumraah kar sakta hai wahan "Audio/Video created using AI" jaisa label chahiye, aur bina consent bane fake endorsements ya deepfakes label ke saath bhi allowed nahi hain.
- **Log aur brands.** Koi public figures nahi, doosri companies ke logos nahi; reference mein kisi bhi asli chehre ke liye written consent.
- **Music.** Jo track personal Reel mein allowed hai, woh ads ke liye licensed na ho, aisa ho sakta hai. Advertising ke liye cleared music use karo, jaise Meta ka Sound Collection (uski terms Meta ke apps ke andar commercial use allow karti hain) ya koi stock licence jo paid ads cover karta ho.
- **Commercial use.** Google kehta hai ki aap jo generate karte ho us par woh ownership claim nahi karta, aur Flow ka help page kehta hai ki outputs kaise use ho sakte hain ye uski Terms of Service tay karti hain. Paid campaign se pehle maujooda terms padho.

## Ek example se samjho

Rohan ek chhote Banarasi handloom label ki marketing sambhaalta hai. Label film crew ka kharch nahi utha sakta, par use Diwali se pehle 30-second ki Instagram Reel chahiye. Uske paas Google AI Pro hai (₹1,950 mahina, 1,000 Flow credits, 28 November 2026 tak). Ye numbers planning ka ek illustration hain, measured results nahi.

**Step 1: shot list,** sab portrait 9:16 mein.

| Shot | Kya dikhta hai | Mode | Length |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Workshop wali gali mein subah, ek cycle guzarti hai, mandir ki ghantiyaan | text to video | 6 s |
| 2 | Loom par weaver ke haathon ka close-up, shuttle ki khat-khat | ingredients (weaver) | 5 s |
| 3 | Loom par sunehre aur laal silk ke dhaage, slow dolly in | ingredients (saree photo) | 5 s |
| 4 | Bengaluru ke flat mein Meera box kholti hai, cardboard ki sarsaraahat | ingredients (Meera, saree) | 6 s |
| 5 | Meera aaine ke saamne saree pehenti hai | ingredients (Meera, saree) | 5 s |
| 6 | Title card: brand name, "Handwoven in Varanasi" | editor mein bana | 3 s |

Paanch generated shots kul 27 seconds ke hain; title card editor mein banta hai.

**Step 2: references.** Woh asli saree ko daylight mein bichhaakar uski photo kheenchta hai, taaki ad mein asli pattern dikhe. Woh Meera, ek kaalpanik young woman, ki ek saamne dekhti image banata hai, aur ek weaver ki, har ek ki ek tay description ke saath jise woh har prompt mein paste karta hai.

**Step 3: saste drafts.** Framing aur movement check karne ke liye har shot ka ek draft, 360p par Omni Flash 1.2 se: 27 seconds × 1 credit = **27 credits**.

**Step 4: final takes.** 720p par har shot ke teen takes: 27 seconds × 3 credits × 3 takes = **243 credits**.

**Step 5: fixes.** Shot 2 mein weaver ki ek extra ungli hai. Shot 5 mein saree ka border laal se maroon ho jaata hai.

- Shot 2: woh prompt waisa hi rakhta hai, "two hands, natural anatomy, hands stay on the loom" jodta hai aur do aur takes banata hai: 5 × 3 × 2 = **30 credits**.
- Shot 5: aur adjectives se kuch nahi hoga, kyunki model ko pata nahi ho sakta ki woh kaun-sa laal chahta hai. Woh shot 4 se ek still leta hai jismein border sahi hai aur shot 5 ko us still se **frames to video** par switch karta hai. Do takes: 5 × 3 × 2 = **30 credits**.

**Total:** 27 + 243 + 30 + 30 = **330 credits**, mahine ke 1,000 ka kareeb ek-tihaai, ya plan price ke hisaab se lagbhag ₹640 (330 ÷ 1,000 × ₹1,950). Free account par roz ke 50 credits se ismein saat din lagenge; AI Plus ke 200 monthly credits akele iske liye kaafi nahi honge.

Kya Veo 3.1 Lite sasta padega? Shot 1 ke liye, haan: 6 seconds ke 10 credits, jabki 720p par Omni Flash 1.2 par 18. Rohan shot 1 dono par try karta hai, par reference shots Omni Flash 1.2 par hi rakhta hai.

**Step 6: edit aur publish.** Woh sabse achhe takes CapCut mein kaat-ta hai, loudness barabar karta hai, aur ads ke liye licensed ek sitar track jodta hai. Brand name aur "Handwoven in Varanasi" editor text ki tarah jaate hain. Visible watermark plain background par rehta hai. Upload karte samay woh Instagram ke AI sawaal ka jawaab yes deta hai, aur caption batata hai ki film AI se, label ki asli sarees ke saath bani hai.

Brand references aur descriptions sambhaalkar rakhta hai, taaki uske agle festival posts wahin se shuru hon.

## Aam galtiyaan

**Poori video ke liye ek adhoora prompt.** Model ek shot banata hai, film nahi. Shot list plan karo, har shot mein ek action.

**Shots ke beech descriptions badalna.** "A woman in a red saree" aur "a lady in maroon" do alag log ban jaate hain. Exact wahi wording aur wahi references dobara use karo.

**Ek shot mein bahut kuch.** Kai actions, crowds aur tez haath artefacts paida karte hain. Shots simple aur dheeme rakho; energy cuts se laao.

**Model se text, prices ya logos maangna.** Letters tedhe ho jaate hain. Saara text editor mein jodo.

**Galat aspect ratio.** Reel ke liye crop ki gayi landscape clip frame ka zyaadatar hissa kho deti hai. Pehle take se pehle 9:16 ya 16:9 chuno.

**Kharaab take ke baad poora prompt dobara likhna.** Jo kaam kar raha tha woh kho jaata hai. Ek cheez badlo, ya mode badlo, jaise kisi sahi still se frames to video.

**Puraane tutorial ke models ya prices par bharosa karna.** Jis din shuru karo, us din Flow ka model menu aur costs check karo.

**AI hone ki baat chhipaana, ya woh use karna jiske rights aapke paas nahi.** Watermark rehne do, platform ke AI sawaal ka sach jawaab do, aur sirf licensed music aur consent wale chehre use karo.

## Practice karo

1. Watch & learn panel mein Flow aur Veo ke tutorials dekho. Note karo ki woh kaun-se models use karte hain, aur aaj ke Flow model menu se compare karo.
2. Upar wali "Shot ko direct karo aur theek karo" exercise karo.
3. Neeche linked Google Flow kholo. Free account ke roz ke 50 credits bhi kai 360p tests ke liye kaafi hain: text to video, ingredients aur, agar aapke plan mein hai, frames to video try karo. Note karo ki har ek mein kitne credits lage.
4. Agar aapke plan mein dono models hain, to ek 6-second ka establishing shot Omni Flash 1.2 aur Veo 3.1 Lite dono par chalao, aur note karo ki kis tarah ke shot ke liye kaun-sa use karoge.
5. Project check karo, AI-generated label ke saath.
