## Ye kya hai

**Veo** Google DeepMind ke **text-to-video** models ka parivaar hai. Aap ek shot describe karte ho, ya images dete ho, aur ye ek chhota, realistic clip generate karta hai. Veo 3 aur Veo 3.1 jaise naye versions **synchronised sound** bhi banate hain: ambience, effects aur bole hue lines tak, jisse AI video kaafi zyada asli lagne laga.

**Flow** Google ka AI filmmaking tool hai jo Veo ke around bana hai. Ek-ek clip ke bajaye aap director ki tarah kaam karte ho: shots generate karo, apne characters aur objects ko reusable references ki tarah rakho, shots ko extend karo, aur unhe scenes mein lagao. Flow tak Google ke AI plans se pahunchte hain; current plans aur credits Google ki site par dekho, kyunki ye aksar badalte hain.

Image generators ki tarah Veo bhi **diffusion models** par based hai: ye noise se shuru karta hai aur aapke prompt ke guide par use refine karke aise frames banata hai jo waqt ke saath ek saath bane rehte hain.

```flow
Idea -> Shot list | 4-8 shots: subject, action, camera, light, sound -> Flow + Veo
Flow + Veo -[text to video]-> Sirf shabdon se ek clip
Flow + Veo -[frames to video]-> Pehli aur aakhri image se ek clip
Flow + Veo -[ingredients]-> Character, product ya style images se ek clip
Sirf shabdon se ek clip -> Review
Pehli aur aakhri image se ek clip -> Review
Character, product ya style images se ek clip -> Review
Review -> Kamzor shots dobara generate karo -> Extend ya trim karo -> Edit | order, music, titles -> Export
```

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Shots mein socho, video mein nahi.** Har generation kuch seconds ki hoti hai. 30 second ka ad paanch ya chhe shots ko editor mein jodkar banta hai. Asli director ki tarah pehle **shot list** likho.

**Prompts aise likho jaise cinematographer bolta hai.** Mazboot prompt mein ye sab hota hai:

| Element | Kya kehna hai | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | kaun ya kya, specific details ke saath | an elderly weaver with silver hair, cream kurta |
| Action | har shot mein ek saaf action | slides the shuttle across the loom |
| Setting | jagah, time, mausam | a dim workshop in Varanasi, morning light through a window |
| Camera | shot size, angle, movement, lens | close-up, slow dolly in, 50 mm, shallow depth of field |
| Lighting aur look | mood, colour grade | warm, soft window light, gentle film grain |
| Sound (audio waala Veo) | ambience, effects, dialogue | the clack of the loom, distant temple bells |

**Flow mein generate karne ke teen main tareeke use karo.**

- **Text to video:** sirf prompt. Establishing shots aur ideas ke liye achha.
- **Frames to video:** aapki banayi ya khinchi image se shuru karo, chaho toh doosri image par khatam karo, taaki composition aapke control mein rahe. Product shots aur continuity ke liye best.
- **Ingredients to video:** kai reference images do, jaise aapka character, aapka product aur ek style frame, aur Veo aisa clip banata hai jisme sab consistent rehta hai.

**Cheezein consistent rakho.** Har character ya product ki pehle ek mazboot reference image banao (image model se, agli guide dekho). Use har shot mein dobara use karo aur har prompt mein wahi chhota description doharao.

**Iterate karo.** Har shot ke do se chaar versions generate karo aur sabse achha rakho. Problems ko poora rewrite karne ke bajaye specific badlaav se theek karo ("hands stay on the loom", "no text on screen").

**Edit karo.** Clips download karo aur kisi bhi editor mein kaato, jaise CapCut, DaVinci Resolve ya Premiere, ya Flow ke apne scene tools mein. Music, voice-over aur title card jodo.

**Apne credits ka budget banao.** Har generation aapke plan se credits khaati hai, aur higher-quality modes zyada. Shots pehle kaagaz par plan karo, jahan ho wahan tez ya sasti modes se ideas test karo, aur best settings final takes ke liye bacha ke rakho.

## Ek example se samjho

Rohan ek chhote Banarasi handloom label ka marketing sambhaalta hai. Unke budget mein film crew nahi aati, par use Diwali se pehle Instagram ke liye 30 second ka ad chahiye.

Woh chhe shots likhta hai: workshop ki gali ka subah ka bahar ka drishya; weaver ke haathon ka loom par close-up; sone aur laal rang ke silk threads; Bengaluru ki ek young woman saree unbox karti hui; use sheeshe mein drape karti hui; aur brand name waala title card.

Woh asli saree ko din ki roshni mein flat rakhkar photo khinchta hai aur use shots 3 se 5 mein **ingredient** ki tarah use karta hai, taaki pattern sahi rahe. Young woman ki reference image woh ek image model se banata hai aur shots 4 aur 5 mein dobara use karta hai. Uske prompts camera moves aur sound saaf likhte hain: "clack of the loom", "soft temple bells", "cardboard rustle".

Pehle pass mein do problems hain: ek shot mein weaver ki chhe ungliyan hain, aur shot 5 mein saree ki border ka rang badal jaata hai. Woh haath waala shot "close-up of two hands, natural anatomy" ke saath dobara generate karta hai aur shot 5 ko **frames to video** par badal deta hai, sahi border ke still se shuru karte hue. Woh best takes CapCut mein edit karta hai, licensed sitar track aur title card jodta hai, aur post ko AI se bana hua label karta hai.

Ad par ek din ka kaam aur kuch sau credits lage, shoot nahi. Brand baad mein wahi references teen aur festival posts ke liye use karta hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

**Poore video ke liye ek dhundhla prompt.** Shots plan karo. Har prompt ek action waale ek shot ko describe kare.

**Shots ke beech description badalna.** Model "a woman in a red saree" aur "a lady in maroon" ko alag insaan maanta hai. Wahi shabd aur reference images dobara use karo.

**Ek shot mein bahut kuch.** Kai actions, bahut saare log aur mushkil haath ki movements se artefacts aate hain. Har shot simple rakho.

**Sound ko ignore karna.** Veo ka audio realism ka hissa hai. Use describe karo, ya band karke apna daalo.

**Bina permission ke asli logon ya brands ko use karna.** Public figures ya doosri companies ke logos generate mat karo, aur jis insaan par character base karo, uski consent lo.

**AI content ko label na karna.** Platforms ab ise zyada maangte hain, aur audience un brands par bharosa karti hai jo is baare mein khule hote hain.
