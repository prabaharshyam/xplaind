## Ye kya hai

Ek AI engagement aam taur par teen stages se guzarta hai:

- **Proof of concept (POC)**, 2 se 6 hafte: kya ye client ke real data par kaam kar sakta hai? Jaldi build, purane cases par offline chalaya, evals se judge. Abhi real users nahi.
- **Pilot**, 6 se 12 hafte: kya ye real logon ko roz ke kaam mein madad karta hai? Ek limited group safeguards ke saath use karta hai, aur aap KPI, quality aur adoption measure karte ho.
- **Production**: jin sabke liye socha gaya unhe rollout, ek normal system ki tarah chalaya aur support kiya, client ki team ke ownership mein.

Har stage ke beech ek **stage gate** hai: ek meeting jahan agreed evidence review hota hai aur client decide karta hai ki aage badhna hai, course badalna hai ya rukna hai.

```flow
dir: LR
POC, 2-6 hafte | purane cases offline, ek golden set aur evals, sabse risky assumption -> Gate 1: go, change ya stop?
Gate 1: go, change ya stop? -[go]-> Pilot, 6-12 hafte | safeguards ke saath real users, KPI, guardrails, adoption; sign-on, logs, security poora
Pilot, 6-12 hafte -> Gate 2: go, change ya stop?
Gate 2: go, change ya stop? -[go]-> Production | sabke liye, waves mein; runbooks, on-call, dashboards; measure karte raho
```

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek lender ke credit-file assistant ko uske pehle offline test se 180 officers tak rollout ke saath follow karta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**POC.** Pehle sabse risky assumption ko target karo. Document extraction ke liye woh aam taur par "kya hum unke real, messy documents ko accurately padh sakte hain?" hota hai, user interface nahi. Real (zaroorat ho toh masked) data use karo, client ke experts ke saath golden set banao, aur accuracy, failure types, har case ki cost aur latency report karo. Polish chhod do. Gate criteria SOW se aate hain: jaise critical fields par kam se kam 95% accuracy.

**Pilot.** Aise users chuno jo bade group ko represent karein, sirf utsahi nahi. Decisions ki zimmedaari insaanon ke paas rakho. Production ko jo chahiye woh itni jaldi jodo ki usse seekh sako: sign-on, logging, monitoring, feedback buttons. KPI ko baseline ke against, guardrail measures aur adoption ko har hafte measure karo. Cheezein badalne ki ummeed rakho; wahi point hai. Gate: KPI hila, guardrails tike, users ise rakhna chahte hain, aur support aur security ready hain.

**Production.** Waves mein rollout karo (ek region, phir aur). Operations handover karo: runbooks, on-call, dashboards, training. Ek support period agree karo. Measure karte raho; production evaluation ka end nahi hai.

**Rukna ek valid outcome hai.** Ek POC jo dikhaye ki documents padhe nahi ja sakte, ya ek pilot jahan users tool ko ignore karte hain, apna kaam kar chuka hai agar woh sasta ruke aur wajah samjhaye. Write-up karo; seekhna valuable hai.

**Pilot purgatory se bacho.** Aam wajahein: agreed gate criteria nahi, business owner nahi, production requirements (security, integration) ko pilot ke baad par chhodna, aur rollout ke liye budget plan na karna. Chaaron ko SOW mein address karo.

**Har stage ke liye gate evidence:**

| Gate | Review hone waala evidence | Aam criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| POC se pilot | Golden-set accuracy, failure types, har case ki cost, latency | Critical fields par kam se kam 95%; jaani-pehchaani failures handle hui hon |
| Pilot se production | Baseline ke against KPI, guardrails, adoption, user feedback, security sign-off | KPI hila; guardrails tike; users chahte hain; support ready |
| Production ke dauran | Mahine ka KPI, cost, incidents | Usage badhne par bhi results tikte hain |

**Gate meeting chalana.** Meeting se do din pehle evidence pack bhejo: agreed criteria, unke bagal mein measured results, mukhya failures aur fixes, cost, aur ek recommendation. Meeting mein criteria ek-ek karke dekho aur business owner se kaho ki go, change ya stop decide karein. Decision aur conditions usi din likhit mein record karo. Pehle se agree criteria waala gate chhota hota hai; criteria ke bina gate is baat par behas ban jaata hai ki success ka matlab kya hai.

**Stop ka write-up likhna.** Agar evidence stop kehta hai, toh do page likho: kya test kiya, kya mila, ab ye kaam kyun nahi karta, aur kya badalna hoga, jaise behtar scans ya alag process. Clients us team ko yaad rakhte hain jo ईमानदारी se aur sasta ruki, aur aksar agla project unhi ko dete hain.

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai lender ka credit-file assistant:

**POC, hafta 1 se 4.** 300 purani files, masked. Critical-field accuracy 93%, 95% gate se neeche. Error analysis: zyadatar misses parchhai waali bank statements ki phone photos par hain. Aap ek image clean-up step aur ek "low confidence, please check" flag jodte ho; accuracy 96.4% tak badhti hai. Gate pass, confidence flag pilot design mein joda gaya.

**Pilot, hafta 5 se 14.** Do branches mein 20 officers. Weekly numbers: har file ka median time 41 se 24 minute; rework 7% baseline ke against 5.1%; adoption 88%. Officers document aur extracted fields ka side-by-side view maangte hain, jo aap hafta 8 mein jodte ho. Security review aur sign-on pilot ke baad nahi, pilot ke dauran poore hote hain. Gate pass.

**Production, hafta 15 se.** 180 officers tak teen waves mein rollout, har ek ek mahine ka. Lender ki IT team aapke runbook aur dashboards se system chalati hai; aap aath hafte ka support dete ho. Mahine ki KPI reports chalti rehti hain.

| Stage | Result | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| POC, hafta 1 se 4 | Critical fields par 93%, phir image clean-up aur confidence flag ke baad 96.4% | Gate pass; flag pilot mein joda gaya |
| Pilot, hafta 5 se 14 | Har file 41 se 24 minute; rework 5.1%; adoption 88%; security aur sign-on poore | Gate pass |
| Production, hafta 15 se | 180 officers tak teen mahine ke waves; client IT aath hafte ke support ke saath chalati hai | Mahine ki KPI reports chalti rehti hain |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Saaf sample data par polished POC.** Ye real documents ke baare mein kuch saabit nahi karta.
- **Gate criteria ke bina pilots.** Ye kabhi khatam nahi hote, aur kabhi scale nahi hote.
- **Security aur integration ko pilot ke baad par chhodna.** Ye sabse bure waqt par mahine jod deta hai.
- **Pilot mein sirf dost-jaise users.** Results baaki sab par nahi tikte.
- **"Stop" ko failure maanna.** Sasta, achhi reasoning ke saath ruk jaana ek achha outcome hai.

## Practice karo

GOV.UK ki alpha, beta aur live phases ki guides padho aur note karo ki AI kaam mein kya lagu hota hai.

1. Apne use case ke liye POC plan likho: sabse risky assumption, data, golden set, measures aur gate criteria.
2. Pilot plan likho: users, safeguards, weekly measures, end tak production ke liye kya taiyaar hona chahiye, aur gate criteria.
3. Rollout plan likho: waves, handover, support period aur kya aapko pause karwa dega.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap teeno stages aur har gate par zaroori evidence ke saath ek one-page plan dikha sako.
