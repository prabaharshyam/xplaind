## Ye kya hai

Artificial intelligence naya lagta hai, par ye hamare zyadatar mummy-papa se bhi purana hai. Ye phrase **1956** mein United States ke Dartmouth College ki ek summer workshop ke liye banaya gaya tha, jahan researchers maante the ki machines ko jaldi hi insaanon ki tarah reason karna sikhaya ja sakta hai. "Jaldi" ke baare mein woh galat the, par unka shuru kiya field sattar saal se chal raha hai.

AI josh aur nirashaa ki kai lehron se guzra hai, aur uske teen bade tareeke rahe hain:

| Saal | Kya hua | Era |
|---|---|---|
| 1956 | "Artificial intelligence" ka naam rakha gaya | Rules |
| 1980s | Expert systems ka boom, phir AI winter | Rules |
| 1997 | Chess world champion haara | Rules |
| 2012 | Deep learning images par kaam karta hai | Learning |
| 2017 | Transformer design publish hua | Learning |
| 2022 | Chat assistants public tak pahunche | Large models |

| Era | Mukhya idea | Aam examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Insaanon ke likhe rules** (1950s se 1980s) | Experts logic haath se likhte hain | Chess programs, diagnosis ke expert systems |
| **Data se seekhna** (1990s se 2010s) | Programs examples mein patterns dhoondhte hain | Spam filters, recommendations, credit scores, image recognition |
| **Bade data par large models** (2017 se aage) | Bahut bade neural networks bahut saare text aur images se general skills seekhte hain | Chat assistants, image generators, coding assistants |

Ye kahani jaanne se do practical fayde hain. Aap AI ko kisi ko bhi kuch minute mein samjha sakte ho, aur aaj ke claims behtar parakh sakte ho, kyunki har era ne pehle bhi zyada waade kiye hain.

Ye lesson ek hi example ke saath chalta hai: India mein ek bank ne tees saal mein loan applications kaise check ki hain.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Rules ka daur

Shuruaati AI ne jaankari ko rules ki tarah likhne ki koshish ki: "agar patient ko bukhaar aur rash hai, toh measles ke baare mein socho". 1970s aur 1980s mein sainkdon ya hazaaron aise rules waale **expert systems** medicine, chemistry aur business ke liye bane.

Ye chhote areas mein kaam karte the par aasaani se toot jaate the. Duniya mein itne exceptions hain ki sab likhe nahi ja sakte, aur ek hospital ke liye likhe rules doosre mein fail ho gaye. Do baar waade nateejon se aage nikal gaye aur funding gir gayi. In dheemi daur ko, lagbhag 1970s mein aur 1980s ke ant mein, **AI winters** kehte hain.

### Learning ka daur

Bada badlaav ye tha ki rules likhna band karke unhe seekhna shuru kiya jaaye. Computer ko jawab ke saath bahut saare examples do, aur use pattern khud nikaalne do. Ise **machine learning** kehte hain.

- Spam filters ne un lakhon emails se seekha jinhe spam ya not spam mark kiya gaya tha.
- Recommendation systems ne seekha ki logon ko kya pasand hai, unke click aur kharid se.
- Credit scores ne seekha ki kaunse patterns se pata chalta hai ki paisa wapas hoga.

1997 mein IBM ke Deep Blue ne world chess champion Garry Kasparov ko haraaya. Usne zyadatar tez search aur haath se tune kiye evaluation use kiye, par isne dikhaya ki computer us kaam mein bhi sabse achhe insaanon ko hara sakte hain jise kabhi intelligence ka kaam maana jaata tha.

2010s mein **deep learning**, yaani bahut saari layers waale bade neural networks, ne images aur speech pehchaanne mein bahut badi badhat hasil ki. 2012 mein ek deep network ne ek bade image-recognition competition ko bade fark se jeeta, aur field neural networks ki taraf mud gaya. Do cheezon ne ise possible banaya: bahut zyada data, aur **GPUs** naam ke tez chips jo arithmetic ek saath parallel mein kar sakte the.

### Large model ka daur

**2017** mein Google ke researchers ne ek naya model design publish kiya jise **transformer** kehte hain. Ye lambe text ko achhe se sambhaalta hai aur bahut bade datasets par efficiently train ho sakta hai. Companies ne bahut saare text par aur bade transformer models train kiye, ek hi kaam ke saath: agla word predict karo.

Kaafi bade size par ye models likh sakte the, summarise, translate, sawaalon ke jawab aur code likh sakte the, aise skills jo kisi ne seedha program nahi kiye the. Jab inpar bane chat assistants **2022 ke ant** mein public ke liye aaye, toh kuch hi hafton mein lakhon log AI ko seedha use karne lage.

### Raftaar kyun badhi

```flow
Zyada data | internet -> Bade, behtar models
Zyada computing | GPUs, data centres -> Bade, behtar models
Behtar designs | transformers -> Bade, behtar models
Bade, behtar models -> Kaam ke products, zyada users -> Bada investment -> Zyada computing
```

Teen cheezein saath-saath badhi aur ek-dusre ko khaad deti rahi. Behtar nateejon ne investment khincha, jisse zyada computing ka kharcha nikla, jisse aur behtar nateeje aaye.

### Jo nahi badla

Har era ne zyada waade kiye. 1960s ke experts ne bees saal mein human-level AI ki bhavishyavaani ki thi. Aaj ke models pehle ke kisi bhi cheez se kahin zyada capable hain, par woh ab bhi confident galtiyaan karte hain, jaisa aage ke lessons mein samjhaya gaya hai. Itihaas se ek achhi aadat: aaj ke systems jo kar sakte hain usse impress ho, par woh jaldi kya karenge is par confident predictions par shak rakho.

## Ek worked example

Socho India mein ek bank ne personal loan application saalon mein kaise check ki hai.

| Daur | Kaise kaam hota tha | Kaunsa era |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1990s | Loan officer rulebook use karta tha: minimum income, ek guarantor, loan aur income ka fixed ratio, documents haath se check karna | Rules |
| 2000s aur 2010s | Credit bureaus aur scoring models ne lakhon purane loans se seekha ki kaunse patterns se repayment hota hai; faisle tez aur zyada consistent ho gaye | Learning |
| Aaj | Ek language model applicant ke bank statements aur salary slips padhkar seconds mein officer ke liye ek page ka summary likh deta hai, credit score ke saath | Large models |

Har step pichhle par bana. Rulebook gayab nahi hua; uske rules system ke andar checks ban gaye. Credit score gayab nahi hua; summary uske bagal mein baithta hai.

Aur teeno daur mein mushkil cases par aakhri faisla insaan hi leta hai. Jo badla woh ye hai ki us insaan ko kitna padhna, calculate karna aur check karna haath se karna padta hai. Ye pattern, yaani tools ka insaani faisle ke aas-paas ka kaam badalna, AI ke zyadatar itihaas mein dohraata hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Ye sochna ki AI chat assistants se shuru hua.** Ye sattar saal purana field hai; chat assistants uska sabse naya aur sabse dikhne waala roop hain.
- **Ye maan lena ki progress ek-si raftaar se hoti hai.** Ye jhatkon mein aayi, beech mein lambe dheemi daur ke saath.
- **Har prediction par bharosa karna.** Experts timelines ke baare mein aksar galat rahe hain, dono taraf.
- **Terms ko mix karna.** AI bada field hai, machine learning uska ek tareeka hai, aur language models machine learning ki ek kism hain.
- **Purane tareeke khatam maan lena.** Rules aur classical machine learning ab bhi banks, factories aur apps ke andar chalte hain, aksar language models ke saath.
