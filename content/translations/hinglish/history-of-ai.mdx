## Ye kya hai

Artificial intelligence naya lagta hai, par ye hamare zyaadatar mummy-papa se bhi puraana hai. **1950** mein British mathematician Alan Turing ne poocha ki kya machines soch sakti hain. **1956** mein United States ke Dartmouth College ki ek summer workshop ke liye "artificial intelligence" phrase bana. Ye field ab sattar saal se zyada se chal raha hai.

Kahani chhote mein: logon ne pehle intelligence ko rules ke roop mein likha, phir computers ko examples se rules khud seekhne diye, phir bahut saare text, images aur awaaz par bahut bade models train kiye. Haal mein ye models problems ko reason karke suljhaane aur actions lene lage hain.

| Approach | Mukhya idea | Kisme achha | Kab toot-ta hai | Aaj bhi kahan use hota hai |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Insaanon ke likhe rules** (1950s se 1980s tak sabse mazboot) | Experts logic ko if-then rules aur search steps ke roop mein likhte hain | Kisi narrow, fixed area mein saaf, check karne laayak faisle | Koi aisa case aa jaaye jiske liye kisi ne rule nahi likha | Eligibility checks, billing aur tax rules, safety checks |
| **Data se seekhna** (1990s se 2010s) | Program jaane-pehchaane jawaabon waale bahut saare examples mein pattern dhoondhta hai | Ek saaf tay prediction, din mein laakhon baar | Naya data training data se alag dikhe | Spam filters, fraud alerts, credit scores, recommendations |
| **Bade data par large models** (2017 se aage) | Ek bahut bada neural network bahut saare text, images aur audio se general skills seekhta hai | Padhna, likhna, coding, baatcheet aur, haal mein, planning aur tools use karna | Koi fluent andaaza galat ho, aur wo use confidently bol de | Chat assistants, coding assistants, agents |

In saalon ko mota-mota padho. Era ka matlab hai wo approach jo zyaadatar naye kaam mein use hui, koi pakki seema nahi. Puraani approaches kabhi gaayab nahi hotin. IBM ke chess computer Deep Blue ne 1997 mein world champion ko haraaya par rules approach use ki, aur banks aaj bhi apne sabse naye models ke bagal mein rule checks chalaate hain.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

```flow
title: Aath steps mein AI ke sattar saal
1950 | Turing poochte hain ki kya machines soch sakti hain -> 1956 | field ko naam milta hai -> 1970s and 1980s | expert systems aur do AI winters -> 1997 | Deep Blue chess mein jeetta hai
1997 -> 2012 | images par deep learning jeetti hai -> 2017 | transformer -> 2022 | chat assistants public tak pahunchte hain -> 2024 to 2026 | reason karne aur kaam karne waale models
```

### Sawaal aur naam (1950 se 1956)

Turing ne "sochne" ko define karne ki koshish nahi ki. Uski jagah unhone ek test sujhaya jise unhone **imitation game** kaha, ab ise Turing test kehte hain. Ek judge text par ek insaan aur ek machine se chat karta hai. Agar judge bharose se nahi bata paaye ki kaun kaun hai, toh machine ne kuch aisa kiya hai jo intelligent dikhta hai.

Aaj ke chat assistants is tarah baat karte hain ki bahut logon ko insaan se farq karna mushkil lagta hai, phir bhi wo basic galtiyaan karte hain. Insaan jaisa sunaai dena aur sahi hona alag-alag baatein hain.

1955 mein John McCarthy aur unke teen saathiyon ne Dartmouth workshop ka prastaav rakha. Unhone likha ki machines se language use karwaane aur problems suljhwaane par ek "significant advance" ek summer saath kaam karke aa sakta hai. Ye bahut saari ummeed bhari timelines mein se pehli thi.

### Rules era

Shuruaati AI ke paas do mukhya tools the. **Rules**, jaise "agar patient ko fever aur rash hai, toh measles par vichaar karo". Aur **search**, yaani bahut saare possible moves ya steps aazmaana aur score ke hisaab se sabse achha chunna.

1970s aur 1980s mein **expert systems** ne kisi expert ki jaankari ko saikdon ya hazaaron rules mein bhar diya. Sabse mashhoor, MYCIN, 1970s mein Stanford University mein blood infections ke liye antibiotics suggest karne ke liye bana tha. Ek test mein specialists ne iski salaah ko lagbhag utna hi acceptable maana jitna Stanford ke apne doctors ki, phir bhi ye patients par kabhi niyamit roop se use nahi hua. Ise hospital ke kaam mein fit karna, aur galti hone par zimmedaar kaun hai ye tay karna, mushkil nikla.

Aaj ke Andhra Pradesh ke ek gaon mein paida hue Raj Reddy ne aise computers ki shuruaat ki jo lagaataar boli gayi baat samajhte hain. Unhone computing ka sabse bada sammaan, 1994 ka Turing Award, expert systems ke ek leader Edward Feigenbaum ke saath baanta.

Rules kyun toote? Kaam par kisi bhi rule-heavy system mein aapko yahi teen wajahein milengi:

- **Experts apni poori jaankari likh nahi sakte.** Bahut saari skill judgement hoti hai, aur judgement saaf-suthre rules mein nahi badalta.
- **Duniya mein exceptions ka ant nahi.** Ek hospital ke liye likhe rules doosre mein fail ho gaye.
- **Rules badhne par aapas mein takraate hain.** Ek rule jodne se chupchaap doosra toot sakta hai, isliye updates dheeme aur risky ho jaate hain.

1997 mein Deep Blue ne world chess champion Garry Kasparov ko haraaya. Ye har second bhaari sankhya mein positions search karta tha aur unhe zyaadatar insaanon ke tune kiye tareekon se score karta tha. Ye rules aur search ka shikhar tha. Par chess fixed rules waali ek band duniya hai. Hospital ya bank aise nahi hain.

Do baar waade nateejon se aage bhaage aur funding gir gayi. 1973 mein mathematician James Lighthill ki ek report ne UK ki Science Research Council ko bataaya ki AI apne bade lakshyon tak nahi pahuncha, aur research ka paisa sookh gaya. 1980s ke aakhir mein expert-system ka business dhah gaya. In dheeme dauron ko **AI winters** kehte hain: bade waade, narrow nateeje, phir paisa kahin aur chala gaya.

### Neural networks: ek puraana idea jo apne waqt ka intezaar kar raha tha

Rules ke saath-saath ek alag idea chupchaap chal raha tha. 1950s ke aakhir mein Frank Rosenblatt ne **perceptron** banaaya, ek simple artificial neuron jo apne inputs ke weights adjust karke examples se seekhta tha. 1969 mein Marvin Minsky aur Seymour Papert ki ek mashhoor kitaab ne dikhaaya ki aise neurons ki ek akeli layer kitni limited hai, aur dilchaspi kam ho gayi.

Hal tha kai layers ko ek ke upar ek rakhna. 1986 mein David Rumelhart, Geoffrey Hinton aur Ronald Williams ne **backpropagation** ko popular kiya, kai layers ko ek saath train karne ka tareeka. Network ek andaaza lagaata hai, naapta hai ki wo kitna galat tha, aur us error ko peeche bhejkar har weight ko thoda adjust karta hai. 2024 mein Hinton aur John Hopfield ne un khojon ke liye Physics ka Nobel Prize baanta jinse neural networks ke saath machine learning possible hui.

Toh 1990s mein neural networks chha kyun nahi gaye? Unhe us samay ke computers jitna de sakte the usse kahin zyada examples aur hisaab-kitaab chahiye tha. Idea data aur hardware se bahut pehle taiyaar tha.

### Learning era

1990s aur 2000s ka bada badlaav tha rules likhna band karke unhe seekhna shuru karna. Computer ko jaane-pehchaane jawaabon waale bahut saare examples do, aur pattern use khud nikaalne do. Yahi **machine learning** hai. Internet ne naye paimaane par data banaakar madad ki.

- Spam filters ne spam ya not spam mark kiye gaye laakhon emails se seekha.
- Recommendation systems ne logon ke click aur khareedaari se seekha ki unhe kya pasand hai.
- Credit scores ne seekha ki puraane loans mein kaun se patterns repayment ka andaaza dete the.

Engineer ka kaam rules likhne se badalkar achhe examples ikattha karna ho gaya. Kamzoriyaan bhi badlin: model ko labelled examples chahiye, aamtaur par ek hi task karta hai, naya data alag dikhe toh chupchaap fail hota hai, aur examples mein jo bhi bias ho use copy kar leta hai.

2012 mein ek deep neural network, jise ab AlexNet kehte hain, ImageNet mein utra, jo photos mein objects pehchaanne ka ek bada contest hai. Ye apne top five andaazon mein sahi jawaab 15.3% baar chooka. Agli sabse achhi entry 26.2% baar chooki. Us antar ne field ko **deep learning** ki taraf mod diya. Teen cheezein milin: puraana backpropagation idea, das laakh se zyada labelled photos, aur **GPUs**, games ke liye bane graphics chips jo zaroori hisaab parallel mein karte hain.

2016 mein DeepMind ke AlphaGo ne board game Go ke top player Lee Sedol ko chaar-ek se haraaya. Isne learning aur search ko milaaya: approaches ek-doosre ki jagah nahi letin, mil jaati hain.

### Large model era

**2017** mein Google ke researchers ne **transformer** naam ka ek naya design publish kiya. Do ideas ne ise khaas banaaya. Pehla, **attention**: har shabd apna matlab samajhne ke liye passage ke baaki har shabd ko dekhta hai, isliye "river bank" aur "bank loan" mein "bank" alag tarah padha jaata hai. Doosra, ye shabd-dar-shabd nahi balki poora passage ek saath padhta hai, jo GPUs ke liye sahi baithta hai aur bahut bade datasets par training ko practical banaata hai.

Phir companies ne transformers ko ek seedhe task par train kiya: agla shabd predict karna. Isme labels ki zaroorat nahi, kyunki koi bhi text pehle se ek worked example hai. Isse learning era ki sabse badi seema, data label karne ka kharcha, hat gayi.

2020 mein researchers ne paaya ki model size, data aur computing power saath-saath badhne par ye models smoothly aur predictably behtar hote hain. Ise **scaling** kehte hain. Isne AI par kharche ko ek socha-samjha daanv bana diya. Usi saal 175 billion adjustable weights waale GPT-3 ne prompt mein diye kuch examples se naye tasks kiye.

Sirf text predict karne ke liye train hua model bas wahi aage badhaata hai jo aap type karte ho. Use assistant banaane ke liye companies ne use achhe example answers aur uske replies par logon ki ratings par aur train kiya. Isi tarah bana ChatGPT jab 30 November 2022 ko release hua, toh bank UBS ke analysts ne andaaza lagaaya ki ye lagbhag do mahine mein 100 million monthly users tak pahunch gaya.

### 2023 se 2026: models jo dekhte, reason karte aur kaam karte hain

2023 se kahani teen dishaaon mein badhi hai, jinse aaj aapke use kiye tools samajh aate hain.

- **Dekhna, sunna aur bolna.** Models text ke saath images aur voice bhi lene lage. May 2024 mein OpenAI ke GPT-4o ne text, audio aur images liye aur baatcheet mein insaan jitne samay mein zor se jawaab de saka.
- **Reason karna.** September 2024 mein OpenAI ne o1 release kiya, ek model jise jawaab dene se pehle problem par zyada samay lagaane ke liye train kiya gaya. Isse ek naya lever juda: computing power sirf training mein nahi, jawaab dete samay bhi kharch karna. January 2025 mein Chinese company DeepSeek ne R1 release kiya, open weights waala ek reasoning model jise koi bhi download kar sakta tha. Reasoning maths, code aur multi-step problems mein madad karta hai, par ye dheema hai aur har jawaab par zyada kharch karta hai, isliye simple sawaalon par bekaar hai.
- **Kaam karna.** October 2024 mein Anthropic ne apne model ko computer screen chalaane diya, pointer hilaana aur type karna. November 2024 mein isne Model Context Protocol release kiya, assistants ko tools aur data se jodne ka ek open standard. 2026 tak badi labs aise **agents** de rahi thin jo apne sandboxed computer mein lambe samay tak kaam karte hain, tab bhi jab aap door ho ya kisi schedule par. Lambe tasks par ye ab bhi galtiyaan karte hain, isliye log tay karte hain ki ye kya-kya kar sakte hain aur nateeje check karte hain.

AI ne science ko bhi badla: 2024 ke Chemistry Nobel Prize ka aadha hissa Google DeepMind ke Demis Hassabis aur John Jumper ko AlphaFold ke liye mila, jo proteins ka aakaar predict karta hai.

Bharat seedhe is kahani mein aaya. 7 March 2024 ko Union Cabinet ne computing, datasets, Indian models aur skills ke liye ₹10,371.92 crore ke saath IndiaAI Mission ko manzoori di. February 2026 mein Bharat ne New Delhi mein AI Impact Summit host kiya, jo New Delhi Declaration ke saath khatam hua, aur Bengaluru ki company Sarvam AI ne Indian languages ke liye bane do models, Sarvam-30B aur Sarvam-105B, pesh kiye aur baad mein unhe open models ke roop mein release kiya.

### Ye tez kyun hua

```flow
title: Wo loop jisne AI ko tez kiya
Zyada data | internet, labels ki zaroorat nahi -> Bade, behtar models
Zyada computing | GPUs aur data centres -> Bade, behtar models
Behtar designs | transformers, reasoning -> Bade, behtar models
Bade, behtar models -> Useful products | laakhon users
Useful products -> Bada investment
Bada investment -> Zyada computing
```

Teen inputs saath-saath badhe aur ek-doosre ko khilaate rahe. Behtar nateejon ne investment kheencha, jisne zyada computing ka paisa diya, jisse behtar nateeje aaye.

Scaling ki seemaayein hain, aur unhe jaanna hi samajhdaar nazariye ko hype se alag karta hai:

- **Kharcha.** Size mein har bade kadam ke liye kai guna zyada computing lagi hai.
- **Data.** Achhe, public, insaanon ke likhe text ki supply seemit hai, aur ye utni tezi se nahi badhti jitni computing badhti hai.
- **Chalaane ka kharcha.** Bada model sirf ek baar nahi, apne har jawaab par zyada kharch karta hai.

Yahi ek wajah hai ki haal ke faayde doosre levers se bhi aate hain: mushkil sawaalon par zyada der sochna, aur search ya code runner jaise tools. Jab koi kahe ki AI isi raftaar se behtar hota rahega, toh poocho ki kaun sa input badhta rahega, aur paisa kaun dega.

### Kya nahi badla

Har daur ne over-promise kiya. Dartmouth proposal ko ek summer mein bade advance ki ummeed thi. 1960 mein economist aur AI ke pioneer Herbert Simon ne likha ki bees saal ke andar machines wo har kaam kar paayengi jo insaan kar sakta hai. Aaj ke systems pehle ki kisi bhi cheez se kahin zyada capable hain, par phir bhi confident galtiyaan karte hain.

Itihaas aapko kisi bhi naye claim ke liye teen sawaal deta hai:

1. **Aaj ye kya karta hai**, mere jaise tasks par, bechne waale ke alaawa kisi aur ke check karne par?
2. **Claim kis par tika hai**: zyada data, zyada computing, zyada paisa? Kya wo badhta reh sakta hai?
3. **Galat hone par kya hota hai**, aur check kaun karta hai?

## Ek example se samjho

Tees saal mein ek loan application ko follow karo. Pune ki teacher Meera teen saal ke liye ₹3 lakh ke personal loan ke liye apply karti hain. Yahan ke saare numbers sirf samjhaane ke liye hain.

Maan lo interest rate 14% saalaana hai. Unki monthly instalment (EMI) lagbhag ₹10,253 banti hai. Wo pehle se scooter loan par har mahine ₹8,000 bharti hain, aur unki take-home pay ₹45,000 hai. Maan lo bank ki apni rulebook kehti hai ki unki saari EMIs milaakar take-home pay ke 50% se kam rehni chahiye. Unka total ₹18,253 hoga, unki pay ka lagbhag 41%, toh wo us rule mein pass hain.

| Daur | Kaam kaun karta hai | Kisme achha | Aam failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1990s | Ek officer rulebook haath se laagu karta hai: income, ek guarantor, EMI-to-pay ratio, documents | Har faisle ki saaf wajah | Bina salary slip waale ek achhe self-employed applicant ko lauta deta hai |
| 2000s aur 2010s | Ek credit bureau score aur laakhon puraane loans se seekha ek scoring model | Bade paimaane par tez, ek jaise faisle | Bina credit history waale first-time borrowers ke saath mushkil |
| Aaj, kuch lenders ke paas | Ek language model bank statements aur salary slips padhkar officer ke liye ek page ka summary likhta hai | Uljhe documents seconds mein padh leta hai | Koi number galat padhkar use confidently bata sakta hai |
| 2026, ek bank jod sakta hai | Ek agent Meera se missing documents maangta hai, checklist bharta hai aur kuch bhi ajeeb flag karta hai | Officer ke ghanton ka follow-up bachaata hai | Koi check na kare toh galat reading par kaam kar sakta hai |

Ab ek failure pakde jaate dekho. Maan lo summary ₹60,000 ke ek baar ke Diwali bonus ko monthly income gin leta hai aur ₹1,05,000 report karta hai, jisse unki EMIs income ka sirf 17% lagengi. Rule check ab bhi unki salary slips use karta hai, jinme ₹45,000 dikhta hai, isliye officer ko mismatch dikh jaata hai. Sabse nayi layer ne padhna tez kiya; puraani layers ne uski galti pakdi.

Har kadam pichhle par bana. Rulebook automatic checks ban gayi. Credit score summary ke bagal mein baithta hai. Har daur mein mushkil cases par aakhri faisla ek insaan leta hai; badla ye ki wo kitna padhna, hisaab lagaana aur peeche padna haath se karta hai. Ye pattern, yaani tools ka ek insaani faisle ke aaspaas ke kaam ko badalna, AI ke poore itihaas mein dohraata hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Ye sochna ki AI chat assistants se shuru hua.** Ye sattar saal puraana field hai. Chat assistants iska sabse naya aur sabse dikhne waala roop hain.
- **Eras ko pakki taareekhein maanna.** Deep Blue 1997 mein jeeta par rules approach use ki. Eras us samay ki mukhya approach batate hain, aur puraani approaches chalti rehti hain.
- **Ye sochna ki neural networks naye hain.** Ye idea 1950s ke aakhir ka hai. 2010 ke baad jo aaya wo tha bade networks chalaane laayak kaafi data aur computing power.
- **Ye maanna ki progress ek jaisi rehti hai.** Ye jhatkon mein aayi, aur jab bhi waade nateejon se bahut aage bhaage, winters aaye.
- **Ye maanna ki bada hi hamesha jawaab hai.** Kharcha, data aur chalaane ka kharcha scaling ko seemit karte hain, isiliye reasoning aur tools naye levers bane.
- **Har prediction par bharosa karna.** Experts timelines ke baare mein aksar galat rahe hain, dono taraf. Dekho ki koi system aaj kya karta hai.
- **Terms ko gadbad karna.** AI field hai, machine learning uski ek approach hai, language models ek tarah ki machine learning hain, aur agent ek language model hai jise tools aur ek goal diya gaya hai.
- **Ye sochna ki puraani approaches khatam ho gayin.** Rules aur classical machine learning aaj bhi banks aur apps mein chalte hain, aksar language model ke aaspaas ke checks ke roop mein.

1. **Kahani sunao.** 1950 se 2026 tak AI ki kahani paanch vaakyon mein, bina notes, kisi dost ya gharwaale ko sunao. Ek AI winter aur 2023 ke baad badli ek cheez shaamil karo.
2. **Apne tools ko approach ke hisaab se chhaanto.** Is hafte use kiye teen AI tools likho, jaise maps, spam filter ya kisi payment app ka fraud alert. Batao ki har ek mukhya roop se kaun si approach use karta hai aur use kya fail karwa sakta hai.
3. **Ek puraani prediction ko parkho.** Das saal se bhi pehle AI ke baare mein ki gayi koi prediction dhoondho. Kya wo sach hui, aur usne kis input (data, computing, designs) ka galat andaaza lagaaya?

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** bina notes ek minute mein samjha sako ki 2017 ke baad AI ne itni tez progress kyun ki, aur us progress ki ek seema bata sako.
