## Ye kya hai

**Scope of work (SOW)** woh agreement hai jo discovery ko plan mein badalta hai. Ye aam taur par kuch pages ka hota hai, jispar client ka business owner aur aapki taraf sign karte hain. Ek achhe AI SOW mein ye hota hai:

- **Objective**: problem aur primary KPI, baseline aur target ke saath.
- **In scope**: aap kya banaoge aur karoge, specific tareeke se.
- **Out of scope**: aap kya nahi karoge, utni hi saaf tarah.
- **Deliverables**: har concrete output (deployed service, eval report, runbook, training sessions).
- **Milestones**: dates, proof of concept, pilot aur production ke beech stage gates ke saath.
- **Acceptance criteria**: har deliverable ke liye testable conditions.
- **Assumptions aur dependencies**: aap client se kis cheez ke liye nirbhar ho (kisi date tak data access, ek IT contact, sign-on set-up, subject experts ka time).
- **Roles**: kaun decide karta hai, kaun review karta hai, kaun support karta hai.
- **Change process**: nayi requests kaise assess aur approve hoti hain.
- **Risks**: jo maloom hain, owners ke saath.

| Scope of work: kuch pages, business owner sign karta hai | |
|---|---|
| **Objective** | Ek file par 41 se 22 min; rework 7% se upar nahi |
| **In scope** | 24 fields, ek draft summary, ek review screen, ... |
| **Out of scope** | Credit decisions, customers, non-English, LOS |
| **Deliverables** | Service, eval report, pilot report, runbook, ... |
| **Milestones** | POC gate hafta 4, pilot gate hafta 14, phir production |
| **Acceptance** | 300 files par 10 critical fields mein kam se kam 95%; citations |
| **Dependencies** | 10 July tak file access; ek senior officer hafte mein 4 ghante |
| **Roles** | Kaun decide, review aur support karta hai |
| **Changes** | Logged, 3 din mein sized, likhit mein approved |
| **Risks** | Named owners ke saath |

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Acceptance criteria ko tests ki tarah likho.** "Assistant accurately jawab deta hai" ko accept ya reject nahi kiya ja sakta. "Agreed 150-sawaal ke golden set par kam se kam 85% jawab do credit officers dwara sahi mane jayein, aur 100% jawab ek source document cite karein" ko kiya ja sakta hai.

**AI uncertainty ke baare mein saaf raho.** Model outputs probabilistic hote hain. Ek defined test set par quality bar likho, jo human review bana rahega woh, aur agar gate par bar poora na ho to kya hoga (badhao, scope chhota karo, ya roko).

**Naam lo ki kya bahar hai.** "Out of scope: core banking system ke saath integration; Tamil-language documents; mobile app; customer-facing use." Zyadatar disputes un cheezon se aate hain jo kisi ne likhi hi nahi.

**Dependencies ko dated banao.** "Client 15 July tak document store ka read access dega; har hafte ki deri milestone 2 ko ek hafta aage karegi."

**Chhota aur saada rakho.** Business owner ko ise pandrah minute mein padh paana chahiye. Technical detail appendix mein daalo.

**Sahi signatures lo.** Jo insaan keh sake "haan, ye ho gaya", use sign karna chahiye.

**Dhundhla aur testable wording:**

| Section | Dhundhla | Testable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | "AI se efficiency improve karna" | "20 pilot officers ke liye ek file par median time 41 se 22 minute" |
| Acceptance | "Achhe se kaam karta hai" | "Pichhli 300 files par 10 critical fields mein kam se kam 95%, officers ki entries ke against" |
| Out of scope | Zikr nahi | "Credit decisions; customer-facing features; non-English documents" |
| Dependency | "Client data dega" | "10 July tak do saal ki anonymised files ka read access; deri milestone 2 ko hafte-ke-badle-hafta khiskaayegi" |
| Change process | Zikr nahi | "Logged, teen working days mein sized, head of operations dwara likhit mein approved" |

**Draft ko review karna.** SOW ko kisi aise insaan ko do jo discovery meetings mein nahi tha. Usse teen sawaal poochho: exactly kya deliver hoga, kya nahi hoga, aur har deliverable kaise accept hoga? Har jhijhak ek ambiguity dikhati hai. Sign se pehle unhe theek karo, kyunki har ek acceptance ke time ki ek future behes hai.

**Miss hue gate ki planning.** Pehle se tay karo ki agar kisi gate ke criteria poore na hon to kya hoga. Options hain: fix plan ke saath chhota extension, scope ko us tak chhota karna jo kaam karta hai, ya write-up ke saath rok dena. Ye tab tay karna jab sab optimistic hon, baad ki mushkil baat ko shaant aur tez bana deta hai.

## Ek worked example

Chennai lender ke credit-file assistant ke SOW ka ek extract:

> **Objective.** 20 pilot officers ke liye har loan file par median officer time ko 41 minute (baseline, June) se 22 minute karna, bina credit committee rework 7% se upar gaye.
>
> **In scope.** Bank statements, salary slips aur bureau reports (English, PDF aur phone photos) se 24 agreed fields ka extraction; lender ke template mein ek draft credit summary; officers ke liye ek review screen; lender ke Azure tenancy mein deployment; eval aur pilot reports; runbook; do training sessions.
>
> **Out of scope.** Credit decisions; customer-facing features; English ke alawa doosri bhashaon ke documents; file export se aage loan origination system ke saath integration.
>
> **Acceptance (proof of concept, hafta 4).** 300 pichhli files par, 10 critical fields par kam se kam 95% field accuracy, officers ki entries ke against measure ki hui; saare summaries source pages cite karte hain.
>
> **Dependencies.** 10 July tak do saal ki anonymised files ka read access; ek senior credit officer hafte mein 4 ghante.
>
> **Change process.** Nayi requests logged hoti hain, teen working days mein sized hoti hain aur kaam shuru hone se pehle head of operations dwara likhit mein approved hoti hain.

**Isse kaise faayda hua.** Teen hafte baad collections team ne wahi tool maanga. Kyunki SOW ne scope aur change process ka naam liya tha, request logged hui, sized hui aur pilot ke baad ke liye schedule hui, credit-file ke kaam ko chupke se late karne ki jagah.

## Common galtiyan

- **Dhundhla acceptance.** "Achhe se kaam karta hai" anant behes ko bulaawa deta hai. Tests aur data define karo.
- **Kya bahar hai us par chuppi.** Bina likhi assumptions disputes ban jaati hain.
- **Bina date ki dependencies.** Phir client ki deri aapki missed deadlines ban jaati hai.
- **Test set ke bina accuracy ka vaada.** Quality ko hamesha ek agreed dataset aur judging method se jodo.
- **Galat insaan se sign karwana.** SOW par woh insaan chahiye jo kaam accept karega.

## Practice

Atlassian ka project scope guide aur GOV.UK ka user stories guide padho.

1. Apne discovery aur KPI exercises ke use case ke liye upar ke har section ke saath do-page ka SOW likho.
2. Teen deliverables ke acceptance criteria aise tests ki tarah likho jo koi aur chala sake.
3. Kisi dost se badlo aur unse ek ambiguity dhoondhne ko kaho; use theek karo.

**Aap tab done ho jab** apna SOW kisi aise insaan ko de sako jo kamre mein nahi tha, aur woh bilkul bata de ki kya deliver hoga aur kya nahi, aur kaise accept hoga.
