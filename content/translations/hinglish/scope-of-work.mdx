## Ye kya hai

**Scope of work (SOW)** wo document hai jo discovery ko aise plan mein badalta hai jis par dono paksh sign karte hain. Ismein likha hota hai ki aap kya deliver karoge, kya nahi karoge, kab tak, har deliverable kaise accept hoga, client ko kya dena hai, aur changes aur paise ko kaise handle kiya jaayega. Kuch firms ise statement of work kehti hain. Dono naam aksar ek hi document ke liye use hote hain, haalaanki kuch firms scope of work ko statement of work ka ek section maanti hain.

SOW aamtaur par ek **master services agreement (MSA)** ke neeche aata hai. MSA ek baar sign hota hai aur usmein aam legal terms hote hain: confidentiality, liability, data protection, aur koi bhi paksh contract kaise khatam kar sakta hai. Phir har project ko uski specifics ke saath apna SOW milta hai. Agar SOW aur MSA mein takraav ho, toh contract aamtaur par batata hai ki kiski baat maani jaayegi, isliye check kar lo. Agar MSA nahi hai, toh wo legal terms SOW mein hi ya ek alag contract mein daalne honge, aur client ki legal team unhe review karna chahegi.

Ek achhe AI SOW mein ye hisse hote hain:

- **Objective**: samasya, aur baseline aur target ke saath primary KPI.
- **In scope** aur **out of scope**: aap kya banaoge aur karoge, aur kya nahi, dono baraabar saaf-saaf.
- **Deliverables**: har thos output, jaise deploy ki hui service, eval report, runbook ya training sessions.
- **Milestones**: taarikhein, proof of concept (POC), pilot aur production ke beech ke stage gates ke saath.
- **Acceptance criteria**: wo test jo har deliverable ko pass karna hai.
- **Assumptions aur dependencies**: aap kin cheezon ke liye client par nirbhar ho, taarikhon ke saath.
- **Roles**: kaun faisla karta hai, kaun accept karta hai, kaun review karta hai, kaun support karta hai.
- **Change process**: nayi requests ko kaise size aur approve kiya jaata hai.
- **Risks**: jaane-pehchaane risks, har ek ka ek owner.
- **Commercial terms**: price, payments, model aur cloud costs kaun dega, kaun-si cheez kiski hogi.

Ye raha ek page ka skeleton jise aap copy karke bhar sakte ho. Koi section chhota ho tab bhi headings rakho; khaali heading ek aisa sawaal hai jiska jawaab aapne abhi tak nahi diya.

```markdown
# Scope of work: [project name]
Under MSA [reference], dated [date]

1. Objective
   KPI: [measure], baseline [x], target [y], by [date]
   Guardrail: [measure] must not pass [z]
2. In scope: [features, users, documents, languages]
3. Out of scope: [named exclusions]
4. Deliverables: [service, eval report, runbook, training]
5. Milestones and gates
   Gate 1 (POC), week [n]: criteria in section 6
   Gate 2 (pilot), week [n]: criteria in section 6
6. Acceptance
   Test set: [size], held out from build work
   Ground truth checked by: [names or roles]
   Bar: [measure and threshold]
   Applies to model version: [provider, model, version]
   If a gate is missed: extend, narrow or stop
7. Dependencies (client): [item, owner, date, effect of delay]
8. Roles: decides [name]; accepts [name]; supports [name]
9. Change process: logged, sized in [n] days, approved by [name]
10. Risks: [risk, owner, mitigation]
11. Commercial
    Price model: [fixed / time and materials / mix]
    Payments: [share] on signing, [share] at each gate
    Model and cloud costs: [who pays, monthly cap]
    IP: [code, prompts, golden sets, eval harness]
    Data protection: [DPA reference]
12. Signatures: [client signatory], [acceptance authority], [your side]
```

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek lender ke credit-file assistant ke SOW ko follow karta hai, wahi case jo is poore path mein use hota hai. Ye case aur iske saare figures method dikhaane ke liye gadhe gaye hain.

## Ye kaise kaam karta hai

### Acceptance ko aise test ki tarah likho jise koi aur chala sake

"Assistant sahi jawaab deta hai" ko na accept kiya ja sakta hai, na reject. Test ho sakne waale criterion ke chaar hisse hote hain:

1. **Ek naam waala test set.** Kaun-si files ya questions, kitne, aur kisne chune.
2. **Ek bar.** Jaise, 10 critical fields par kam se kam 95% field accuracy.
3. **Ek judge.** Sahi answer kya hai ye kaun tay karega, aur asahmati kaise suljhegi.
4. **Ek version.** Result kis model aur configuration par laagu hota hai.

Pehle aur teesre hisse mein do traps chhipe hain. Pehla, **test set held out hona chahiye**. Proof of concept ke dauraan team failures study karke unhe fix karti hai. Agar use unhi files par judge kiya jaaye jo usne study kin, toh wo un files par fit hokar gate pass kar sakti hai, aur phir nayi files par kharaab karegi. Isliye data ko baanto: ek hissa build aur debug karne ke liye, aur ek held-out hissa, build team se taale mein band, jo gate ka faisla kare. Ek kaam ka rule: held-out set chunne waala vyakti client ki taraf ka ho.

Doosra, **puraana process apne-aap sahi answer nahi hota**. Ground truth sahi answers ka wo set hai jiske against aap score karte ho. Agar staff ke figures dobaara type karne se hi wo rework hota hai jise aap kam karna chahte ho, toh unki original entries mein bhi wahi errors honge. AI ko unke against score karoge toh AI ko tab saza milegi jab wo sahi tha aur wo galat the. Har test item ko client ke do anubhavi staff check karein, aur pehle se tay kar lo ki asahmati hone par teesra vyakti use kaise suljhaayega.

### AI ki anishchitata ke baare mein imaandaar raho

Model outputs badalte rehte hain, aur client ke asli documents dekhe bina koi 95% ka waada nahi kar sakta. Ise chhipaao mat; likh do. Batao ki tay test set par bar kya hai, acceptance ke baad bhi kaun-sa human review jaari rahega, aur gate miss hone par kya hoga. Teen options hain: fix plan ke saath ek chhota extension, scope ko utna chhota karna jitna kaam kar raha hai, ya likhit report ke saath ruk jaana. Jab sab optimistic hon tabhi ye tay kar lo, toh baad ki mushkil baatcheet shaanti se aur jaldi nipat jaati hai.

### Model version pakka karo

Model providers engagement ke beech mein hi models update aur retire karte hain. Jaise, November 2026 tak Anthropic ka model deprecations page waada karta hai ki kisi publicly released model ko retire karne se pehle kam se kam 60 din ka notice dega, aur kehta hai ki retired model ko bheji gayi requests fail hongi. Doosre providers aur cloud platforms apne-apne schedules rakhte hain. Ek model par accept hua system uski jagah aane waale model par alag tarah se behave kar sakta hai.

Isliye SOW ko kehna chahiye: acceptance ek naam waale model version par laagu hota hai. Agar provider use badle ya retire kare, toh aap naye version par acceptance test dobaara chalaoge. Agar system bar se neeche gire, toh SOW batata hai ki fixes ka paisa kaun dega. Ek aam bantwaara ye hai ki aapke support period ke dauraan ke fixes aapke, aur uske baad wo change requests hain.

### Jo out hai uska naam do, aur jis par nirbhar ho uski taarikh daalo

"Out of scope: core banking system ke saath integration; Tamil-language documents; mobile app; customer-facing use." Zyaadatar disputes un cheezon se aate hain jo kisi ne likhi hi nahi.

Dependencies ko ek owner, ek taarikh aur ek asar chahiye: "Client 10 July tak document store ka read access dega; har hafte ki deri milestone 2 ko ek hafta aage khiskaayegi." Asar likhe bina, client ki deri chupchaap aapki missed deadline ban jaati hai.

### Sahi vyakti se accept karwaao

Kai Indian enterprises mein SOW par procurement ya IT sign karta hai, kyunki vendor budget unke paas hota hai. Lekin "Haan, ye kaam poora hai" kehne waala aamtaur par business owner hota hai, jaise head of operations. Agar ye alag-alag log hain, toh SOW mein business owner ko **acceptance authority** ke roop mein naam do. Phir sign karne se pehle acceptance criteria par unki likhit sahmati le lo; ek email reply kaafi hai. Warna aap document ka har test pass kar loge aur phir bhi sunoge "Humein ye nahi chahiye tha".

### Commercial terms tay karo

Clients sabse pehle commercial terms par aapatti karte hain, isliye inhe bhi technical hisson jitni saavdhaani se draft karo. Yahaan aap legal advice nahi de rahe. Aapka kaam hai har sawaal uthaana, ek samajhdaar default propose karna, aur client ki legal aur procurement teams ko, aur apni teams ko bhi, wording review karne dena.

- **Pricing model.** **Fixed price** us kaam ke liye theek hai jise aap size kar sakte ho, jaise tay test waala chaar hafte ka POC. **Time and materials** (effort ke hisaab se paisa, aksar ek cap ke saath) us kaam ke liye theek hai jismein asli anishchitata ho, jaise aisa pilot jahaan users changes maangenge. Kai AI projects dono ko milaate hain: POC ke liye fixed, pilot ke liye cap waala time and materials.
- **Gates se jude payments.** Jaise, sign karte samay ek hissa aur har pass hue gate par ek hissa. Isse paisa evidence se judta hai, aur gate 1 par rukne waala client sirf kiye gaye kaam ka paisa deta hai.
- **Model aur cloud costs.** Batao ki model API calls aur hosting ka paisa kaun dega. Aksar ye client ke apne cloud account mein chalte hain aur client seedhe payment karta hai. Agar aap pay karke aage bill karte ho, toh ek monthly cap rakho jise paar karne ke liye likhit approval chahiye. Pilot safal hote hi usage tezi se badh sakta hai.
- **Intellectual property (IP).** Batao ki code, prompts, golden sets aur eval harness (tests chalaane waali scripts) kiske hain. Golden sets client ke data aur experts ke samay se bante hain, isliye clients aksar unhe apna maante hain. Aap project se pehle banaaye apne reusable tools apne paas rakhna chah sakte ho. Dono baatein likh do.
- **Data protection.** India ke Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ke tahat, personal data kyun aur kaise use hoga ye tay karne waala client **Data Fiduciary** hai. Uski or se data process karne waale aap **Data Processor** ho, aur Act kehta hai ki processor ko sirf ek valid contract ke tahat hi engage kiya ja sakta hai. Wo contract aamtaur par data processing agreement (DPA) hota hai. DPDP Rules November 2025 mein notify hue, aur zyaadatar duties May 2027 se laagu hongi, isliye abhi sign hone waale contracts unhi ke tahat chalenge.
- **Confidentiality aur liability.** Ye aamtaur par MSA mein hote hain. AI ke liye ek baat check karo: system ke output ke aadhaar par liye gaye faisle ki zimmedaari kiski hai? Saaf jawaab hai ki loop mein ek insaan decision-maker rakho aur AI se liye jaane waale faislon ko out of scope rakho, jaisa worked example mein kiya gaya hai.

### Ise chhota rakho, phir kisi anjaan vyakti par test karo

Business owner ko main SOW pandrah minute mein padh paana chahiye. Field lists aur test-set design jaisi technical details ek appendix mein daalo.

Sign karne se pehle draft kisi aise vyakti ko do jo discovery meetings mein nahi tha. Teen sawaal poochho: theek-theek kya deliver hoga, kya nahi hoga, aur har deliverable kaise accept hoga? Har hichkichaahat ek ambiguity dikhaati hai, aur har ambiguity acceptance ke samay ki ek aane waali bahas hai.

**Golmol aur test ho sakne waali wording:**

| Section | Golmol | Test ho sakne waali |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective | "AI se efficiency badhaana" | "20 pilot officers ke liye prati file median samay 41 se 22 minute" |
| Acceptance | "Achha kaam karta hai" | "200 held-out files par 10 critical fields mein kam se kam 95%, do senior officers dwara check kiya gaya" |
| Model | Zikr nahi | "Naam waale model version par laagu; provider change hone par test dobaara chalega" |
| Out of scope | Zikr nahi | "Credit decisions; customer-facing features; non-English documents" |
| Dependency | "Client data dega" | "10 July tak do saal ki masked files ka read access; deri milestone 2 ko hafte-dar-hafta khiskaayegi" |
| Change process | Zikr nahi | "Log hoga, teen working days mein size hoga, head of operations likhit mein approve karenge" |
| Costs | "All inclusive" | "Model aur cloud costs client apni Azure tenancy mein dega; hum har mahine report karenge" |

## Ek worked example

Chennai lender ke credit-file assistant ke SOW ka ek hissa. Is par lender ke IT head sign karte hain, jinke paas vendor budget hai, aur ismein head of operations ko acceptance authority ke roop mein naam diya gaya hai.

> **Objective.** 20 pilot officers ke liye prati loan file officer ka median samay 41 minute (baseline, June) se ghataakar 22 minute karna, credit committee rework ko 7% se upar le jaaye bina.
>
> **In scope.** Bank statements, salary slips aur bureau reports (English, PDF aur phone photos) se tay 24 fields nikaalna; lender ke template mein ek draft credit summary; officers ke liye ek review screen; lender ki Azure tenancy mein deployment; eval aur pilot reports; runbook; do training sessions.
>
> **Out of scope.** Credit decisions, jo officers aur credit committee ke paas hi rahenge; customer-facing features; English ke alaawa doosri bhaashaon ke documents; file export se aage loan origination system ke saath integration.
>
> **Acceptance (proof of concept, week 4).** Lender ki credit team dwara chuni gayi 300 masked puraani files. 100 development ke liye build team ke saath share hongi. 200 held out hain: build team ko kabhi nahi dikhaayi jaayengi, aur gate ka score inhi se hoga. Held-out files ka ground truth do senior credit officers check karenge, aur asahmati credit manager suljhaayenge. Bar hai 10 critical fields par kam se kam 95% field accuracy, aur har summary apne source pages cite kare. Acceptance appendix mein naam diye gaye model version par laagu hota hai.
>
> **Dependencies.** 10 July tak do saal ki masked files ka read access; har hafte 4 ghante ke liye ek senior credit officer. Har hafte ki deri milestone 2 ko ek hafta aage khiskaayegi.
>
> **Change process.** Nayi requests log hongi, teen working days mein size hongi, aur kaam shuru hone se pehle head of operations likhit mein approve karenge.
>
> **Commercial.** POC fixed fee par; pilot cap ke saath time and materials par; payments signing aur dono gates mein bante hue. Model aur hosting costs lender ke Azure account mein chalenge aur lender dega. Golden sets, prompts aur project code lender ke honge; hamare pehle se bane tools hamare paas rahenge. DPDP Act ke tahat ek data processing agreement saath mein laga hai.

Dekho har clause kya rokta hai. Held-out split team ko un files par fit hokar gate pass karne se rokta hai jin par usne debug kiya. Do senior officers test ko puraane retyping errors ka inaam dene se rokte hain. Cost clause production mein usage badhne par achaanak aane waale bill ko rokta hai.

**Iska faayda kaise hua.** Week 7 mein, pilot ke dauraan, collections head ne borrower correspondence ki summaries maangi. Kyunki SOW mein scope aur change process ka naam tha, request log hui aur lagbhag chaar hafte ki size ki gayi. Iske liye naye document types, naye users aur ek naya golden set chahiye tha. Head of operations ne ise production gate ke baad phase 2 ke roop mein approve kiya, bajaay iske ki ye chupchaap credit-file ke kaam ko der kare. Phase 2 baad mein doosra contract bana. Scope creep manage karne waala lesson us hafte ko vistaar se follow karta hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Golmol acceptance.** "Achha kaam karta hai" se kabhi na khatam hone waali bahas shuru hoti hai. Test set, bar, judge aur model version ka naam do.
- **Tuning set par test karna.** Jin files par team ne debug kiya un par maapa gaya acceptance quality ko asal se zyada dikhaata hai. Ek hissa held out rakho jise build team kabhi na dekhe.
- **Puraane process ko answer key maan lena.** Original entries mein wahi errors hote hain jinhe aap theek kar rahe ho. Experts se ground truth check karwaao.
- **Kya out hai is par chuppi.** Bina likhi assumptions disputes ban jaati hain.
- **Bina taarikh ki dependencies.** Phir client ki deri aapki missed deadlines ban jaati hai.
- **Miss hue gate ka koi plan nahi.** Kaam shuru hone se pehle extend, narrow ya stop tay kar lo.
- **Model changes ko nazarandaaz karna.** Acceptance ke baad provider ka update quality badal sakta hai. Version pakka karo aur re-acceptance kaise hoga ye tay karo.
- **Khule kaam par fixed price.** Anjaani cheezon se bhare pilot ke liye fixed fee aapko corners cut karne ya har request par bahas karne ki or dhakelti hai.
- **Model costs ka koi owner nahi.** Agar API bill kisi ka nahi hai, toh pehla bada invoice dispute ban jaata hai.
- **IP par chuppi.** Clients maan lete hain ki sab kuch unka hai, un golden sets samet jinhe aap reuse karne waale the. Ownership likh do.
- **Sign karne waala accept karne waala nahi hai.** Procurement sign karta hai, operations reject karta hai. Acceptance authority ka naam do aur criteria par unki sahmati lo.

## Practice karo

Atlassian ki project scope guide aur GOV.UK ki user stories guide padho. Phir:

1. Upar waala skeleton copy karo aur apni discovery aur KPI exercises waale use case ke liye bharo. Main document do page ke andar rakho.
2. Teen deliverables ke liye acceptance criteria likho. Har ek mein test set aur wo kaise held out hai, bar, judge aur model version ka naam ho.
3. Commercial section likho: har stage ke liye fixed price ya time and materials chuno aur ek vaakya mein batao kyun. Batao model costs kaun dega aur golden set kiska hoga.
4. Kisi aise dost se swap karo jisne aapke discovery notes nahi dekhe. Unse teenon stranger questions poochho aur unhe mili har ambiguity theek karo.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** apna SOW kisi aise vyakti ko de sako jo room mein nahi tha, aur wo theek-theek bata sake ki kya deliver hoga aur kya nahi, har hissa kaise accept hoga, aur kis cheez ka paisa kaun dega.
