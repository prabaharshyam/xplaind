## Ye kya hai

Har automation project ko aakhir mein ek sawaal ka saamna karna padta hai: **kya ye worth it tha?** Business owners aur managers paisa dene se pehle bhi poochhenge aur launch ke baad bhi. **Return on investment (ROI)** compare karta hai ki automation kya wapas deta hai aur uski cost kya hai.

| Gains | Costs |
| --- | --- |
| **Time ki bachat**: staff ke hours ab us task par nahi lagte | **Build**: aapka time ya fee |
| **Errors se bachav**: rework, penalties, galat payments ya lost orders rukte hain | **Running**: tool subscriptions, AI usage, messaging fees, hosting |
| **Speed**: tez responses ya processing, jiska matlab zyada sales ya khush customers ho sakta hai | **Maintenance**: automation ko theek karna aur update karna |
| **Capacity**: hire kiye bina zyada volume sambhaalna | **Human review**: log jo time ab bhi check karne ya exceptions sambhaalne mein lagate hain |

| | Pehle (baseline) | Baad mein (measured) |
|---|---|---|
| Din ke orders | 150 | 150 |
| Order par time | 3 min | 1.2 min |
| Galat quantity | 2% | 0.9% |

```flow
dir: LR
Pehle aur baad mein -> Farak x uski value = gains -> Gains minus costs -> Net benefit
```

Is lesson mein hum Indore ke ek distributor ko follow karenge, jiski WhatsApp order automation ko owner ko apni value prove karni hai. End tak aap saaf assumptions ke saath ROI estimate pesh kar paoge aur jaan paoge ki use prove karne ke liye kya naapna hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### 1. Pehle baseline naapo

Kuch badalne se pehle naapo: mahine ka volume, har item par time, error rate aur turnaround time. Jahan ho sake real data use karo: emails ke timestamps, system logs, ya do hafte ka sample jisme staff har item ka start aur end time note kare. Baseline ke bina baad mein aap kuch prove nahi kar sakte.

### 2. Banane se pehle estimate karo

```text
  Monthly hours saved  =  items per month  x  minutes saved per item  /  60

  Value of time        =  hours saved  x  loaded hourly cost

  Value of errors      =  errors avoided per month  x  cost per error

  Net monthly benefit  =  value of time + value of errors (+ careful revenue gains)
                          - running costs - maintenance

  Payback (months)     =  build cost  /  net monthly benefit
```

**Loaded hourly cost** salary plus overheads hoti hai, jaise benefits, office aur equipment, working hours se divide karke. Ye aam taur par sirf salary se kaafi zyada hoti hai.

**Speed se aane waale revenue** ke baare mein conservative raho. Tez replies se zyada orders mil sakte hain, par ye prove karna mushkil hai, isliye ise alag dikhao ya chhod do.

### 3. Assumptions ke baare mein honest raho

- Har input dikhao, taaki doosre check karke badal sakein.
- **Ranges** use karo: low, expected aur high.
- Yaad rakho ki bacha hua staff time tabhi valuable hai jab use kisi kaam ki cheez mein lagaya jaaye, ya hiring bache.

| Input | Low | Expected | High |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Order par bache minutes | 1.5 | 2 | 2.5 |
| Error reduction | 25% | 50% | 70% |
| Mahine ka running cost | ₹11,000 | ₹9,000 | ₹8,000 |

### Jo benefits rupees mein nahi hain

Kuch gains asli hain par unka daam lagana mushkil hai: staff jo ab month-end se nahi darte, customers jinhe ek din ki jagah minutes mein jawab milte hain, owner ko raat ko kam calls. Inke liye rupee values gadhna mat. Unhe "other benefits" ke neeche alag list karo, jo bhi evidence ho uske saath, jaise complaints ya overtime hours mein kami. Decision-makers yahan bade number se zyada honesty ko value dete hain, aur jab measured figures aate hain toh ye aapki credibility bachata hai.

### 4. Launch ke baad naapo

Ek se teen mahine baad wahi measures baseline se compare karo: automatically process hue items, exceptions aur reviews par bacha human time, error rates, aur estimates nahi balki real invoices se running costs.

### 5. Simple report do

Ek page: baseline, abhi, savings, costs, net benefit, payback, aur aage kya behtar karoge. Owners ek page padhte hain; barah nahi padhte.

## Ek example se samjho

Indore ka ek distributor 300 shops se WhatsApp par orders leta hai. Staff har order ko billing system mein haath se type karta hai. Automation WhatsApp orders padhta hai, AI step se items aur quantities nikaalta hai, aur ek quick human confirmation ke liye unhe billing system mein daal deta hai.

**Baseline** (do hafte ka sample): 26 working days mein din ke 150 orders, har ek ko type karne mein lagbhag 3 minute. 2% orders mein quantity galat thi, har ek par returns aur re-delivery mein lagbhag ₹400 lagte the.

**Estimate:**

| Line | Calculation | Monthly value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mahine ke orders | 150 × 26 | 3,900 |
| Hours saved | 3,900 × (3 − 1) ÷ 60 | 130 hours |
| Time ki value | 130 × ₹250 loaded cost | ₹32,500 |
| Bache errors | 3,900 ka 2% se 1% = 39 kam × ₹400 | ₹15,600 |
| Running costs | WhatsApp provider, AI usage, hosting | −₹9,000 |
| **Net benefit** | | **₹39,100** |
| Payback | ₹90,000 build fee ÷ ₹39,100 | **lagbhag 2.3 mahine** |

**Teen mahine baad, measured:**

| Measure | Baseline | Estimated | Measured |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sirf quick confirmation se handle hue orders | 0% | 100% | 82% |
| Average handling time | 3 min | 1 min | 1.2 min |
| Galat quantities | 2% | 1% | 0.9% |
| Net monthly benefit | | ₹39,100 | lagbhag ₹36,000 |

Measured benefit estimate se thoda kam hai, kyunki voice-note orders, jo scope se bahar the, ab bhi type karne padte hain. Ek page ki report saaf bolti hai aur voice notes ke liye phase 2 recommend karti hai, uske apne estimate ke saath. Owner approve kar deta hai, kyunki pehla estimate honest nikla.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Baseline nahi.** Baad mein aap kuch prove nahi kar sakte.
- **Sirf build cost ginna.** Running aur maintenance costs har mahine chalte hain.
- **Bache hue human kaam ko ignore karna.** Reviews aur exceptions mein ab bhi time lagta hai.
- **Ek optimistic number.** Ranges aur conservative assumptions use karo.
- **Aisi savings claim karna jo koi use nahi karta.** Bache hours tabhi matter karte hain jab woh valuable kaam mein jaayein ya hiring bachayein.
- **Loaded cost ki jagah salary use karna**, ya ulta, bina bataye ki kaunsa.
