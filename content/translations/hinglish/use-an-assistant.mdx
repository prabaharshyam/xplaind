## Ye kya hai

AI seekhne ka sabse tez tareeka hai use kisi asli kaam ke liye use karna. Chat assistant tab sabse kaam ka hota hai jab aap use **kaabil naye colleague** ki tarah dekhte ho: woh aam taur par bahut kuch jaanta hai par aapki situation, aapke customers ya aapki company ke baare mein kuch nahi. Aap use saaf brief karte ho, jo woh deta hai use review karte ho, aur use karne se pehle jo maayne rakhta hai use check karte ho.

```flow
dir: LR
Brief | kaun, task, context, format -> Review | kya ye wahi hai jo maanga tha? Feedback do
Review -[dohraao]-> Brief
Review -> Check | jo facts maayne rakhte hain kya woh sahi hain?
Check -> Use | bhejo, apne naam ke saath
```

Ye lesson isi ko ek baar, theek se, aapke apne kaam ya padhai ke task par karne ke baare mein hai: drafting, summarising, explaining, planning ya brainstorming. Iska check ek chhota project hai, toh end tak aapke paas teen prompts, teen jawab aur check kiye hue claims ki list hogi.

Aap Rohit ke saath chaloge, jo Chennai ki ek broadband company mein customer support mein kaam karta hai, aur assistant ke saath ek help article likhta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Achha task chuno

| Achhe pehle tasks | Kharab pehle tasks |
| --- | --- |
| Koi email ya message jo aapko bhejna hai | Legal ya tax deadline |
| Lambe article ya meeting ka summary | Dawai ki dose |
| Aise topic ka explanation jo aap aadha jaante ho | Kuch bhi jise aap khud parakh na sako |
| Event ya hafte ka plan | Aisa faisla jo palta nahi ja sakta |
| Prepare karne ke liye interview questions | Unapproved tool mein confidential data waali koi bhi cheez |

Achhe tasks woh hain jisme draft se time bache aur aap aasaani se achha-bura pehchaan sako. Jab aap output parakh sako tab zyada tezi se seekhte ho.

### Poora prompt likho

Poore prompt mein chaar hisse hote hain:

| Hissa | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **Aap kaun, ye kiske liye** | "I'm a final-year B.Com student preparing for a campus interview at a bank." |
| **Task** | "List ten questions they are likely to ask." |
| **Kaam ka context** | "It's a public sector bank, for a probationary officer role. I did an internship at a credit co-operative." |
| **Format** | "As a numbered list, with a one-line tip for each." |

### Dohraao

Pehla jawab ek draft hai. Badlaav maango: chhota, zyada specific, alag tone, zyada examples, kam clichés. Jo galat hai woh batao. Do-teen round aam taur par ek perfect prompt likhne se behtar rehte hain.

### Ek hi task ke liye alag prompts try karo

Ye lesson ka dil hai. Teen versions likho:

1. **Ek-line ka prompt**, jaise zyadatar log shuru karte hain.
2. **Detailed prompt** chaaron hisson ke saath.
3. **Detailed prompt jo assistant se pehle aapse sawaal poochhne ko kahe:** "Likhne se pehle mujhse teen sawaal poochho."

Results aksar bahut alag hote hain. Inhe compare karne se prompting ke baare mein kisi bhi tips ki list se zyada seekhne ko milta hai.

### Claims check karo

Jawab mein **factual claims** dhoondho: numbers, dates, naam, rules, product details. Kam se kam jo zaroori hain unhe kisi reliable source se check karo: official website, original document, textbook ya expert.

```flow
Jawab | "Routers ko har hafte restart karna chahiye." Ek claim -> Source | network team se poochho, router manual padho
Source -> Nateeja | "Sirf problem ho tab restart karo."
Nateeja -> Line theek karo
```

Agar koi zaroori cheez check na kar sako, toh us par depend mat karo.

### Dhyaan rakho kya share karte ho

Personal data, passwords ya confidential work documents un tools mein paste mat karo jinhe aapke organisation ne approve nahi kiya. "Kaam par AI safely use karo" lesson isse detail mein cover karta hai.

### Jawab kyun badalte hain

Agar aap wahi sawaal do baar poochho, toh alag jawab mil sakte hain. Assistants har agla word thodi randomness ke saath chunte hain, aur wording mein chhota badlaav bhi result badal deta hai. Ye ek aur wajah hai ki kisi ek jawab par bharosa karne ki jagah facts check karo.

## Ek worked example

Rohit Chennai ki ek broadband company mein customer support mein kaam karta hai. Uska manager use ek chhota help article likhne ko kehta hai: "Mera Wi-Fi slow kyun hai?"

**Prompt 1, ek line:**

> "Write an article about slow Wi-Fi."

Result 600 words ka hai, generic, aur aise products ka zikr karta hai jo uski company nahi bechti.

**Prompt 2, detailed:**

> "I work for a broadband provider in Chennai. Write a 250-word help article for home customers titled 'Why is my Wi-Fi slow?' Cover router placement, too many devices, and when to restart the router. Friendly, simple English, with numbered steps. Do not mention brands."

Result kaam-chalau ke kareeb hai.

**Prompt 3, detailed plus pehle sawaal:**

Prompt 2 ke jaisa hi, saath mein: "Likhne se pehle mujhse hamare customers aur router models ke baare mein teen sawaal poochho." Assistant poochhta hai ki customers ke paas kaunse router models hain, kaunsi plan speeds aam hain, aur kya customers router ki settings mein login kar sakte hain. Rohit jawab deta hai. Ab article mein woh dual-band router aata hai jo company deti hai aur tez band par kaise switch karna hai.

**Claims check karna.** Article kehta hai "routers should be restarted every week". Rohit network team se poochhta hai, jo kehte hain ki restart sirf problem hone par chahiye, aur hafte-hafte restart se bekaar ke support calls aate hain. Woh line theek kar deta hai.

| Prompt | Length | Accuracy | Hamare customers ke liye fit | Use hua? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ek line | 600 words | Doosre brands ka zikr | Kharab | Nahi |
| 2. Detailed | 250 words | Ek galat claim | Achha | Lagbhag |
| 3. Detailed plus sawaal | 250 words | Ek galat claim, phir theek kiya | Bahut achha | Haan, review ke baad |

Final article manager ke review ke liye jaata hai aur agle hafte publish ho jaata hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Ek-line ke prompts.** Kaun, task, context aur format do.
- **Pehla jawab maan lena.** Specific feedback ke saath dohraao.
- **Kuch bhi check na karna.** Jo claims maayne rakhte hain unhe dhoondho aur verify karo.
- **Unapproved tools mein confidential ya personal data paste karna.**
- **Aisa task chunna jise aap parakh nahi sakte.** Wahan se shuru karo jahan achha-bura pehchaan sako.
- **Ek jawab par bharosa karna.** Zaroori ho toh alag tareeke se dobara poochho ya source check karo.

## Practice karo

AI Fluency course lo (neeche link hai); ye AI ke saath kaam karte waqt delegation, description, discernment aur diligence cover karta hai.

Is lesson ka check ek chhota project hai:

1. **Task chuno** apne kaam ya padhai se.
2. **Teen prompts likho** uske liye: ek-line ka prompt, detailed prompt, aur detailed prompt jo pehle aapse sawaal poochhne ko kahe.
3. **Har jawab ka ek claim check karo** kisi aise source se jis par aapko bharosa ho.
4. **Likh daalo:** kaunsa prompt sabse achha raha aur kyun, kya sahi tha, kya galat tha, aur aapko kaise pata chala.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap teen prompts, unke jawab, check kiye hue claims aur agli baar kya badloge, ye dikha sako.
