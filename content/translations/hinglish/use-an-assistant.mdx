## Ye kya hai

AI seekhne ka sabse tez tareeka hai use roz kisi asli kaam ke liye use karna. Chat assistant sabse zyada kaam ka tab hota hai jab aap use ek **kaabil naye colleague** ki tarah treat karte ho: use aam taur par bahut kuch pata hai, par aapki situation, aapke customers ya aapki company ke baare mein kuch nahi. Aap use brief karte ho, uska kaam review karte ho aur jo bhi zaroori hai use check karte ho.

Iske hisse doosre lessons mein gehraai se hain: "Jargon ke bina prompting" brief likhna sikhaata hai, "AI kya nahi kar sakta" limits batata hai, aur "AI ke answer ko check karna" verification sikhaata hai. Ye lesson us **daily practice** ke baare mein hai jo in sabko jodti hai:

- ek assistant chunna aur uske controls seekhna,
- har task ke liye sahi feature chunna (new chat, web search, attach ki hui file, voice),
- chats ko saaf-suthra rakhna taaki assistant bhatke nahi,
- ye jaanna ki aapki privacy settings kis cheez se bachaati hain aur kisse nahi.

```flow
title: Assistant ke saath ek daily loop
dir: LR
Chuno | new chat, search on ya off, file attach karni hai? -> Brief | kiske liye, task, context, format
Brief -> Review | kya ye wahi hai jo maine maanga?
Review -[specific feedback]-> Brief
Review -> Check | wo claims jin par koi bharosa karega
Check -> Save | jo prompt chala use rakh lo
```

Is lesson ka check ek chhota project hai. Aap apne kaam ka ek task teen tareekon se chalaoge, har jawab mein ek claim check karoge aur jo seekha use likhoge. Aap Rohit ko follow karoge, jo Chennai ki ek broadband company mein customer support mein kaam karta hai, aur wo bhi yahi karta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Ek assistant chuno aur use achhe se seekho

Is lesson ke liye aapko paise dene ki zaroorat nahi hai. November 2026 tak, bade paimaane par use hone waale sabhi general assistants ke free plans hain:

| Assistant | Kisne banaya | Jaanne laayak baat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | OpenAI | Free plan mein web search hai, par paid plans se file uploads aur voice ki limits kam hain |
| Gemini | Google | Aapke Google account se sign in hota hai; app Hindi aur kai doosri Indian languages mein chalta hai |
| Claude | Anthropic | Free plan mein web search, file uploads aur voice mode hain |

Free plans ki limits aksar badalti hain, isliye kisi puraane article ki jagah app ke andar ka plan page check karo. Ek chuno aur compare karne se pehle do hafte use use karo. Is lesson ki aadatein ek assistant se doosre par bhi kaam aati hain; aap use kaise use karte ho, uske muqaable brand bahut kam maayne rakhta hai.

Sign up karne se pehle do saavdhaaniyaan:

- **Free trials renew hote hain.** Jaise, November 2025 mein OpenAI ne India ke users ko apna paid Go plan ek saal free diya. Aise offers, phone plans ke saath bundle hokar aane waale bhi, cancel na karne par normal keemat par renew ho sakte hain. Jis din sign up karo, usi din renewal date note kar lo.
- **Work aur personal accounts alag hote hain.** Agar aapka employer ya college aapko koi approved assistant deta hai, toh kaam ke liye wahi use karo. Storage aur training par uski terms ek contract se tay hoti hain, aapki apni settings se nahi. "Kaam par AI ko safely use karo" batata hai kyun.

### Jo controls maayne rakhte hain unhe seekho

Roz ke use mein zyaadatar skill ye jaanna hai ki kaun-sa control uthaana hai. Naam assistant-assistant mein alag hote hain, par lagbhag sabmein ye hote hain:

| Control | Ye kya karta hai | Kab use karo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New chat** | Bilkul saaf shuruaat karta hai | Jab aap doosra task shuru karo, ya maujooda chat bhatak gaya ho |
| **Web search** | Maujooda pages dhoondhta hai aur links dikhaata hai | Jab jawab kisi badalne waali cheez par tika ho: rates, prices, rules, dates, news |
| **File ya photo attach karna** | Use aapka document, spreadsheet ya screenshot padhne deta hai | Jab jawab ko kisi khaas source ko follow karna ho, jaise aapki company ki guide |
| **Voice input** | Aapki boli ko prompt mein badalta hai | Jab typing dheemi ho, ya aap bolkar behtar sochte ho, apni bhasha mein bhi |
| **Thinking ya reasoning mode** | Jawab dene se pehle problem par zyada der tak kaam karta hai | Multi-step problems, plans aur calculations; chhote email ke liye zaroorat nahi |
| **Saved instructions ya projects** | Wo background rakhte hain jo use hamesha pata hona chahiye | Jab aap baar-baar ek hi baat samjhaate ho, jaise apna role ya pasandeeda style |
| **Temporary ya incognito chat** | Aapki history mein save nahi hota, training ke liye use nahi hota | Koi ek baar ka sawaal jo aap apni history mein nahi rakhna chahte |

Ek kaam ka default: har naya task new chat mein shuru karo, facts waali har cheez ke liye search on rakho, aur jab bhi source ho use attach karo. Assistant ki yaaddaasht par bane jawab ke muqaable aapke apne source par bana jawab check karna aasaan hota hai.

### Brief do, phir iterate karo

Ek achhe **prompt** mein hota hai ki aap kaun ho aur ye kiske liye hai, task kya hai, wo context jo assistant ko pata nahi ho sakta, aur aapko kis format mein jawab chahiye. Phir pehle jawab ko draft maano aur specific feedback do, jaise "ise 120 words ka karo aur doosra paragraph hata do". Ek perfect prompt likhne ki koshish se do-teen rounds aam taur par behtar rehte hain. "Jargon ke bina prompting" ye theek se sikhaata hai; yahaan aapko bas ise karna hai.

Roz ke kaam mein ek move aksar use karne laayak hai: apna brief is line se khatam karo, "Shuru karne se pehle, jo bhi sawaal poochhne hain mujhse poochh lo." Tab assistant wo facts maangta hai jinka wo varna andaaza lagaata.

### Chats ko saaf-suthra rakho

Har assistant ki ek **context window** hoti hai: text ki wo maatra jo wo ek baar mein le sakta hai, ab tak ki poori baatcheet samet. Bahut lambe chat mein shuru ke instructions bheed mein dab sakte hain, toh assistant unhe ignore karne lagta hai. Teen aadatein ise rokti hain:

- **Ek task, ek chat.** Aapka CV aur aapki current-affairs revision ek hi baatcheet mein nahi hone chahiye.
- **Summary ke saath restart karo.** Jab chat bhatke, toh usse kaho "humne jo tay kiya use paanch bullets mein summarise karo", phir use new chat mein paste karo.
- **Jo dohraate ho use save karo.** Apna hamesha waala background, jaise "main final-year B.Com student hoon; jawab chhote rakho", saved instructions ya kisi project mein rakho, taaki use dobaara type na karna pade.

Jo prompts chale, unka apne phone par ek simple note rakho. Ek mahine baad wahi note aapka personal toolkit hai.

### Files, photos aur voice dhyaan se use karo

**Files.** Document attach karne se jawab aapke source ko follow karne mein madad milti hai. Par ye guarantee nahi hai: assistant phir bhi ek aisi sentence jod sakta hai jo sahi lage par document mein na ho. Usse kaho "batao har step kis section se aaya hai", phir un sections ko khud spot-check karo. Ye bharosa mat karo ki assistant apni bina support waali lines ko khud flag karega.

**Photos aur screenshots.** Names, phone numbers, account numbers aur jo bhi use nahi chahiye, wo crop kar do. Assistants photo ke figures galat padh sakte hain, isliye numbers ko original se check karo. Photos se documents padhne ke baare mein "Images, voice aur doosre AI tools" zyada gehraai se batata hai.

**Voice aur Indian languages.** Voice input shuru karne ka ek tez tareeka hai, aur kai assistants Hindi aur doosri Indian languages lete hain. Jaise, Google ne June 2024 mein India mein Gemini app nau Indian languages ke saath launch kiya. Us par bharosa karne se pehle use apni bhasha mein kisi aise task par test karo jise aap judge kar sako, aur transcript hamesha padho: speech recognition names aur numbers par hi phisalti hai.

### Jaano ki aapki privacy settings kya karti hain

Har assistant mein ek switch hota hai jo tay karta hai ki aapke chats uske models ko behtar banaane mein use ho sakte hain ya nahi. November 2026 tak, ChatGPT ise "Improve the model for everyone" kehta hai, Claude ki privacy settings mein ek model-improvement setting hai, aur Gemini ise apni Keep Activity setting se hatakar ek alag training setting mein le ja raha hai (Google ne ye badlaav November 2026 mein announce kiya, isliye aapke account mein dono mein se koi bhi dikh sakti hai). Ise dhoondho aur soch-samajhkar tay karo; default par mat chhodo.

Saaf samajh lo ki ye settings kya karti hain:

| Setting | Ye kya badalti hai | Ye kya nahi badalti |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model-improvement switch off | Aapke naye chats future models ko train karne mein use nahi hote | Chats phir bhi kuch samay ke liye store ho sakte hain, jaise safety checks ke liye |
| Temporary ya incognito chat | Aapki history mein save nahi hota, training ke liye use nahi hota | Use phir bhi thode samay ke liye rakha jaata hai, aur wo phir bhi personal account hai |

Inmein se koi bhi ye theek nahi banaati ki aap customer records, colleagues ki personal details ya confidential documents kisi aise tool mein paste karo jise aapki organisation ne approve nahi kiya.

### Tay karo ki kya check karna hai

Assistants **hallucination** de sakte hain: ek dhaaraapravaah, pakke andaaz mein kaha gaya statement jo galat hai. Aap sab kuch check nahi kar sakte, isliye stakes ke hisaab se check karo. Har claim ke baare mein do sawaal poochho: "Kya koi is par bharosa karega?" aur "Agar ye galat hua toh kya hoga?" Numbers, dates, names, rules, prices aur aapki organisation ke apne steps aam taur par ye test paas karte hain; aam wording aur ideas aam taur par nahi. "AI ke answer ko check karna" ek zyada poora tareeka deta hai.

Ek doosra assistant ideas ke liye aur asahmati pakadne ke liye kaam ka hai. Agar do assistants kisi fact par asahmat hon, toh aap jaan jaate ho ki use check karna hi hai. Agar wo sahmat hon, toh wo proof nahi hai: ho sakta hai dono ne ek hi puraane page se seekha ho.

## Ek example se samjho

Rohit, Chennai ki ek broadband company mein customer support mein kaam karta hai. Uska manager ek chhota help article maangta hai: "Mera Wi-Fi slow kyun hai?" Uski company ne customer data ke liye koi AI tool approve nahi kiya hai, isliye wo apna personal free account aur sirf public material use karta hai: company ki website par router setup guide. Wo model-improvement switch off rakhta hai aur customer tickets ko usse door rakhta hai.

Wo task teen tareekon se chalaata hai, har baar new chat mein.

**Set-up 1: ek line, koi feature nahi.**

> "Slow Wi-Fi ke baare mein ek article likho."

Nateeja lagbhag 600 words ka hai, generic hai, aur mesh Wi-Fi kits recommend karta hai jo company bechti hi nahi.

**Set-up 2: poora brief.**

> "Main Chennai mein ek broadband provider ke liye kaam karta hoon. Ghareloo customers ke liye 'Mera Wi-Fi slow kyun hai?' title waala 250 words ka help article likho. Router ki placement, bahut zyada devices, aur router kab restart karna hai, ye cover karo. Friendly, simple English, numbered steps, koi brand name nahi."

Nateeje ki lambaai aur tone sahi hai. Ek line kehti hai: "Apna router hafte mein ek baar restart karo taaki wo tez chale."

**Set-up 3: wahi brief, saath mein company ki guide attach karke.** Wo jodta hai:

> "Steps aur settings ke liye sirf attach ki hui setup guide use karo, aur batao har step kis section se aaya hai."

Ye version router ke do bands ke beech switch karne ke liye guide ke apne steps use karta hai, aur hafte waale restart ki line chali gayi hai. Par ye jod deta hai: "5 GHz band poore ghar ko cover karta hai." Rohit cite kiya hua section check karta hai. Guide ulta kehti hai: 5 GHz router ke paas zyada tez hai, aur 2.4 GHz zyada door tak pahunchta hai, isliye door ke kamron mein 2.4 GHz behtar chal sakta hai. Assistant ne ek asli section ko us claim ke liye cite kiya tha jo wo section karta hi nahi.

**Check karna.** Wo network team se hafte waale restart ke baare mein poochhta hai. Wo kehte hain ki restart sirf tab chahiye jab koi problem ho, aur har hafte restart karne se bewajah support calls aati hain.

Uska write-up, aur aapke project write-up ka bhi yahi dhaancha hona chahiye:

| Set-up | Check kiya claim | Source | Verdict | Use kiya? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Ek line | Mesh kits recommend karta hai | Company ka product page | Hamaare liye galat: hum unhe bechte nahi | Nahi |
| 2. Poora brief | "Apna router hafte mein ek baar restart karo" | Network team | Galat | Lagbhag |
| 3. Brief aur guide | "5 GHz band poore ghar ko cover karta hai" | Setup guide, cite kiya section | Galat: guide kehti hai 2.4 GHz zyada door pahunchta hai | Haan, check karke theek karne ke baad |

**Uska nateeja:** "Help articles ke liye main hamesha apni guide attach karoonga, kyunki usse restart waali galti hati aur article hamaare router par fit baitha. Par main phir bhi har step ko us section se check karoonga jo wo cite karta hai, kyunki guide attach karne se uska ek galat line jodna nahi ruka." Article review ke liye uske manager ke paas gaya aur agle hafte publish hua.

Dekho farq kisse pada. Behtar prompt ne fit theek kiya; attach kiye source ne zyaadatar facts theek kiye; baaki sirf checking ne pakda.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Sab kuch ek lambe chat mein chalaana.** Har task ke liye new chat shuru karo, aur jab wo bhatke toh summary ke saath restart karo.
- **Search off rakhkar current facts poochhna.** Rates, rules aur prices ke liye search chahiye, aur uske baad bhi aap link kholte ho.
- **Citation ko proof maan lena.** Cite kiya page ya section shaayad wo na kahe jo assistant daawa karta hai. Use padho.
- **Ye maan lena ki attach ki hui file se jawab sahi ho jaata hai.** Wo madad karti hai; jode gaye claims ko nahi rokti.
- **Voice transcript par bharosa karna.** Us par aage badhne se pehle use padho aur names aur numbers theek karo.
- **Ye sochna ki temporary chat se confidential data safe ho jaata hai.** Wo history aur training badalta hai, ye nahi ki aap wo data share kar sakte ho ya nahi.
- **Defaults kabhi na badalna.** Model-improvement switch dhoondho aur soch-samajhkar tay karo.
- **Trial ki renewal date bhool jaana.** Free offers aksar paid plans mein badal jaate hain.

## Practice karo

Neeche linked AI Fluency course karo. Uske delegation, description, discernment aur diligence waale ideas is lesson ke daily loop se mel khaate hain.

Phir is lesson ka project karo:

1. **Set up karo.** Ek assistant chuno. Uske new chat, web search, file upload, voice aur model-improvement controls dhoondho, aur apni privacy setting tay karo.
2. **Ek task chuno** apne kaam ya padhaai se, jismein kam se kam ek check karne laayak fact ho, koi personal ya confidential data na ho, aur jise aap khud judge kar sako.
3. **Use teen tareekon se chalao**, har baar new chat mein: ek one-line prompt; poora brief; poora brief aur saath mein wo feature jo task ko chahiye (current facts ke liye web search, apna document attach karke, ya "pehle mujhse sawaal poochho").
4. **Har jawab mein ek claim check karo** kisi aise source se jis par aap bharosa karte ho: koi official website, original document ya koi jaankaar insaan.
5. **Use likho** Rohit jaisi table mein, do ya teen sentences ke conclusion ke saath.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab** aapka write-up ye dikhaaye:

- teenon prompts shabdashah, unke jawabon ke saath;
- har jawab mein ek check kiya claim, source ke naam aur verdict ke saath: sahi, galat, ya confirm nahi ho saka;
- ek conclusion jo bataaye ki agli baar is tarah ke task ke liye aap kaun-sa set-up use karoge, is aadhaar par ki aapki checks mein kya nikla, is par nahi ki kaun-sa jawab sabse achha laga.
