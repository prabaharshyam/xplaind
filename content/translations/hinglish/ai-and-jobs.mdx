## Ye kya hai

"Kya AI meri naukri kha jaayega?" India mein AI ko lekar logon ke sabse aam sawaalon mein se ek yahi hai, final-year students se lekar senior managers tak. Sachcha jawaab ye hai: ye aapke **tasks** par depend karta hai, job title par nahi.

Naukri asal mein tasks ka ek guchha hoti hai. Ek customer-support executive routine queries ke jawaab deta hai, gussaaye customers ko shaant karta hai, couriers ke peeche padta hai aur notes likhta hai. Ek teacher lessons plan karta hai, ideas samjhaata hai, homework check karta hai aur class sambhaalta hai. AI inmein se kuch tasks mein bahut achha hai, kuch mein madadgaar hai, aur kuch mein uska zyada kaam nahi.

Ye lesson teen shabdon par tika hai:

- **Exposure** ka matlab hai aaj AI kisi task ka kitna hissa kar sakta hai ya usmein kitni madad kar sakta hai. Ye sambhaavna ka paimaana hai, bhavishyavaani nahi.
- **Automation** ka matlab hai task insaan ki jagah AI karta hai.
- **Augmentation** ka matlab hai AI task ka kuch hissa karta hai aur insaan zyada, tez ya behtar kaam karta hai.

```flow
title: Naukri yaani tasks ka guchha
Ek job -> Task A | routine, text waala
Ek job -> Task B | drafting jise check karna padta hai
Ek job -> Task C | bharosa aur judgement
Ek job -> Task D | physical, uljha hua
Task A -> High exposure | zyaadatar AI kar sakta hai
Task B -> High exposure, check ke saath | AI draft karta hai, insaan check karta hai
Task C -> Low exposure | AI taiyaari karta hai, insaan faisla karta hai
Task D -> Low exposure, physical | mauke par insaan chahiye
```

Exposure par sabse achhe global saboot International Labour Organization (ILO) se aate hain. Poland ke research institute NASK ke saath May 2025 mein chhape uske refined index ne lagbhag 30,000 work tasks ko score kiya. Ismein paaya gaya ki duniya bhar mein lagbhag **har chaar mein se ek worker** aise occupation mein hai jismein generative AI ka kuch exposure hai, par sirf **3.3%** sabse zyada exposure waale group mein hain. **Clerical kaam** (data entry, routine drafting, records rakhna) sabse zyada exposed hai. Auratein aadmiyon se zyada exposed hain: auraton ki 4.7% jobs sabse oonche group mein aati hain, jabki aadmiyon ki 2.4%. ILO ka mukhya nishkarsh ye hai ki kyunki logon ki zaroorat ab bhi hai, zyaadatar naukriyon ke redundant hone se zyada **transform** hone ki sambhaavna hai.

Is lesson mein hum Arun ke saath chalenge, jo Hyderabad mein customer-support executive hain, aur unki company ek AI assistant la rahi hai. Lesson ke ant tak aap kisi bhi job ko tasks mein baant paoge, har task kitna exposed hai ye aank paoge, naukriyon waali headlines ko parakh kar padh paoge aur kaam ke bane rehne ka plan bana paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Exposure tasks ki hoti hai

Kisi bhi job ko aankne ke liye uske tasks ki list banao, har ek ke saath samay ka mota-mota hissa likho, aur har task se teen sawaal poochho:

1. Kya AI aaj ye task theek-thaak quality mein kar sakta hai?
2. Kya AI iska kuch hissa kar sakta hai, jise koi insaan check kare?
3. Kya ismein wo cheezein chahiye jo AI ke paas nahi hain: shareer, bharosa, zimmedaari (accountability), ya uljhi hui haalat mein judgement?

| Task ka prakaar | Udaaharan | Exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine text aur data | Data entry, standard emails, simple translation, form bharna | High |
| Drafting aur summarising | Reports, meeting notes, code ke first drafts | High, par output check karna zaroori |
| Judgement waala analysis | Supplier chunna, kisi problem ki jad pakadna | Medium: AI madad karta hai, insaan faisla karta hai |
| Log aur bharosa | Negotiation, counselling, team sambhaalna, client ko bechna | Low |
| Physical kaam | Electrical repairs, nursing care, construction | Low |

### Exposure aur job loss ek cheez nahi

Koi exposed task AI ko tabhi saunpa jaata hai jab kuch aur baatein bhi sach hon. Experts chaar filters check karte hain:

```flow
title: Kya exposed task sach mein AI ko jaayega?
Exposed task -> Quality kaafi hai?
Quality kaafi hai? -[nahi]-> Logon ke paas rehta hai | AI thodi madad kar sakta hai
Quality kaafi hai? -[haan]-> Insaan se sasta hai?
Insaan se sasta hai? -[nahi]-> Logon ke paas rehta hai
Insaan se sasta hai? -[haan]-> Galti ho toh safe hai?
Galti ho toh safe hai? -[nahi]-> AI draft karta hai, insaan check karta hai
Galti ho toh safe hai? -[haan]-> Ijaazat aur bharosa hai?
Ijaazat aur bharosa hai? -[nahi]-> AI draft karta hai, insaan check karta hai
Ijaazat aur bharosa hai? -[haan]-> AI ko saunpa jaata hai
```

India mein cost kai deshon se zyada maayne rakhti hai, kyunki yahaan logon par kharcha kam aata hai. Jo task London mein automate karne laayak hai, wo Lucknow mein haath se karna ab bhi sasta ho sakta hai. Risk bhi maayne rakhta hai: galat order-status reply bas jhunjhlaahat hai, par galat loan decision ya dawa ki galat dose nahi. Aur rules aur bharosa dheere badalte hain, khaaskar banking, healthcare aur government mein, jahaan regulators aur customers chaahte hain ki koi naamzad insaan zimmedaar ho.

Organisations ko samay bhi chahiye. Unhe processes badalne hain, staff ko train karna hai, customer data bachaana hai aur bachat saabit karni hai. Isliye badlaav aam taur par raatonraat nahi, saalon mein, team dar team aata hai.

### Kisi role ke teen nateeje

Kisi role ka nateeja sirf exposed tasks ke hisse se tay nahi hota. Ye is par depend karta hai ki AI ke exposed kaam le lene ke baad kya bachta hai, aur kaam ki maatra badhti hai ya nahi.

- **Automated.** Lagbhag saare tasks exposed hain aur jo bachta hai wo chhota hai, toh role khatam ho jaata hai ya kisi doosre role mein mil jaata hai. Ye zyaadatar pure routine roles ke saath hota hai, jaise wo data-entry operator jo bas forms bharta hai.
- **Augmented.** Kai tasks exposed ho sakte hain, par jo bachta hai wahi job ka core hai: judgement, bharosa aur zimmedaari. Log us core par zyada samay lagaate hain. Ye sabse aam nateeja hai.
- **Created.** AI ke ird-gird naye roles aate hain: AI applications banaane waale engineers, businesses ke andar use deploy karne waale log, use test karne waale evaluators, workflows ko naye sire se design karne waale automation specialists, aur AI product managers.

Expert waali baareek baat: **role augment hone par bhi team chhoti ho sakti hai.** Maan lo 10 logon ki team apna 60% samay routine queries par lagaati hai aur ek assistant unmein se zyaadatar le leta hai. Agar queries ki ginti utni hi rahe, toh usi kaam ke liye 6 ya 7 log kaafi ho sakte hain. Agar company bache hue samay se badhne, jaldi jawaab dene ya nayi services dene ka kaam le, toh wo sabhi 10 ko rakh sakti hai. Sirf exposure nahi, demand par bhi nazar rakho.

### Faayda kise: experience aur skill

AI ki madad sabko baraabar upar nahi uthaati. Ek badi field study mein economists Erik Brynjolfsson, Danielle Li aur Lindsey Raymond ne business software bechne waali ek Fortune 500 company mein AI assistant aane ke dauraan 5,000 se zyada customer-support agents ko follow kiya. Working-paper version mein assistant waale agents ne ausatan har ghante lagbhag **14%** zyada issues suljhaaye. Sabse naye aur sabse kam skilled agents ko lagbhag **ek tihaai** ka faayda hua, jabki sabse experienced logon ko thoda hi. Ye study 2025 mein Quarterly Journal of Economics mein chhapi.

Wajah samajhna kaam ka hai. Authors ka kehna hai ki assistant ne sabse productive agents ke tareeke naye logon tak pahuncha diye. Experts pehle se usi tarah kaam karte the. Toh routine kaam mein AI naye aur experienced staff ke beech ka faasla kam karta hai, aur experience ki value un cases ki taraf khisak jaati hai jinhe assistant nahi sambhaal paata.

### Entry-level par dabaav

Yahi pattern students ke liye sabse bada risk banaata hai. Jo routine tasks AI sabse achhe se karta hai, unhi par freshers pehle seekhte the.

Stanford Digital Economy Lab ke researchers (Brynjolfsson, Bharat Chandar aur Ruyu Chen) ne millions workers ke US payroll data ko study kiya. Unke paper ke August 2026 version mein, sabse zyada AI-exposed jobs mein 22 se 25 saal ke workers ki employment us level se lagbhag **19%** neeche thi jahaan wo hoti agar kam exposed jobs waale usi umar ke workers ke saath-saath chalti, jabki authors ke data ki pehli wave mein ye 15% tha. Chaar baatein dhyaan dene waali hain:

- Giraavat zyaadatar **kam hiring** se aayi, isse nahi ki zyada young workers apni jobs chhod rahe the.
- Unhi jobs mein badi umar ke workers sthir rahe ya badhe.
- Paper ke pehle ke versions mein, badlaav jobs ki ginti mein aaya, salary mein nahi.
- Giraavat un jobs mein zyada thi jahaan AI tasks ko **automate** karne mein use hota hai, na ki **augment** karne mein.

Ise dhyaan se padho. Ye US ka data hai, aur authors ise early warning batate hain, is baat ka saboot nahi ki giraavat AI ki wajah se hui. Wo ye bhi kehte hain ki unhe AI se juda, poori economy mein phaila job displacement nahi dikhta. Par disha Indian freshers ke liye saaf signal hai: aapko judgement jaldi dikhaana hoga, kyunki kam employers aapko routine kaam par use seekhne ke paise denge. Jo teams juniors ko rakhna poori tarah band kar deti hain, wo apna hi nuksaan karti hain, kyunki aaj ke juniors hi kal ke seniors hain.

### India mein iska kya matlab hai

India mein IT services, business-process aur back-office ka bada workforce hai, aur uska kaafi entry-level kaam, jaise routine coding, testing, support aur data processing, exposed hai. October 2025 mein NITI Aayog ne NASSCOM aur BCG ke saath taiyaar kiya *Roadmap for Job Creation in the AI Economy* chhaapa. Ye chetaavni deta hai ki India ke tech services sector par 2031 tak bade paimaane par jobs jaane ka khatra hai, par agle paanch saal mein 4 million tak nayi jobs banaane ka mauka bhi hai, aur ye ek National AI Talent Mission ka prastaav rakhta hai. Ye sambhaavnaayein hain, forecasts nahi. Baat kis taraf jaayegi, ye kaafi had tak is par depend karta hai ki log aur companies kitni tezi se reskill karte hain.

### Headline kaise padhein

AI-aur-jobs waali zyaadatar headlines paanch tarah ke sabooton mein se kisi ek par tiki hoti hain. Har ek alag sawaal ka jawaab deta hai.

| Saboot ka prakaar | Udaaharan | Ye kya batata hai | Ye kya nahi batata |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exposure index | ILO, May 2025 | AI kin tasks mein madad kar sakta hai | Jobs jaayengi ya nahi, ya kab |
| Field study | Support-agent study, 2025 | Ek tool ne ek company mein kya kiya | Kya aapki company mein bhi aisa hoga |
| Payroll data | Stanford young-worker study | Abhi hiring ke saath kya ho raha hai | Wajah, pakke taur par |
| Employer survey | WEF Future of Jobs Report, January 2025 | 1,000 se zyada employers 2030 tak kya ummeed karte hain (170 million jobs banengi, 92 million hatengi) | Aisa hoga ya nahi; headline waale kul aankdon mein sirf AI nahi, saari wajahein mili hain |
| Scenario report | NITI Aayog, October 2025 | Kisi sector ke liye nateejon ki range | Asal mein kya hoga |

Jab koi headline kahe "AI X jobs khatam kar dega", toh poochho ki ye kis tarah ka saboot hai. Exposure nuksaan nahi hai, survey naap nahi hai, aur scenario vaada nahi hai.

### Apni company mein kin signals par nazar rakhein

Badlaav aapke role tak pahunchne se pehle aam taur par chhote signals mein dikhta hai. In par nazar rakho:

- Aapki team ke tasks par kisi AI tool ka pilot, "time saved" ya "tickets deflected" ke targets ke saath.
- Aapke role mein chhodkar jaane waalon ki jagah naye log na rakhna, ya sirf ek role ke liye hiring freeze.
- Team ka size utna hi rehte hue har insaan ke targets badhna.
- Job descriptions mein "AI output review karna" ya "knowledge base maintain karna" jaise naye tasks aana.
- Reviews mein managers ka "productivity per head" ki baat karna.

Inmein se kisi ka matlab ye nahi ki aapki job ja rahi hai. Har ek ka matlab hai ki tasks ka mix badal raha hai, aur jo pehle kadam badhaate hain wo apne naye tasks khud chun paate hain.

### Kaam ke bane rehna

Lagbhag har nateeje mein chaar kadam madad karte hain:

1. **Pehle apne kaam par AI use karo.** Aise task se shuru karo jo aap aksar karte ho, routine hai, check karna aasaan hai aur jismein galti sasti padti hai. Apne sabse bade task se shuru mat karo agar usmein judgement chahiye.
2. **Check karne mein maahir bano.** Seekho ki tool kahaan fail hota hai aur uski galtiyaan pakdo. Jo log AI output ki zimmedaari le sakte hain, unki value sirf prompts type karne waalon se zyada hai.
3. **Judgement, rishton aur zimmedaari ki taraf badho.** Exceptions, escalations, customers aur decisions ke liye khud aage aao.
4. **AI-focused role ke baare mein socho**, agar wo aapko suit kare. Kai roles ko computer science degree nahi, domain knowledge aur AI skills chahiye. Xplaind ke paths dikhaate hain ki har role ko kya chahiye.

### Apne manager se baat karna

Ek chhoti baatcheet maango aur saboot saath laao: "Maine do hafte routine replies par assistant try kiya. Isse mera roz lagbhag ek ghanta bacha, aur maine policy ke do galat jawaab pakde. Main wo samay escalations par aur assistant ke jawaab behtar karne mein lagaana chahunga." Isse dikhta hai ki aap tool use karte ho, use check kar sakte ho, aur bache hue samay ka aapke paas plan hai. Kisi ke batane ka intezaar karne se ye kahin mazboot position hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Arun Hyderabad ki ek e-commerce company mein customer support mein kaam karte hain, 12 logon ki team mein. Company ek AI assistant laati hai jo chat mein order-status aur return-policy ke sawaalon ke jawaab deta hai.

Ye raha unka hafta pehle aur baad mein, samay ke hisse ke roop mein. Ye aankde samjhaane ke liye hain, naape hue nahi.

| Task | Pehle | Chhah mahine baad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine status aur return queries | 60% | 10% (sirf wo jo assistant escalate karta hai) |
| Complex complaints | 25% | 45% |
| Warehouses aur couriers se coordinate karna | 15% | 20% |
| Assistant ke jawaab review karke behtar karna | 0% | 25% |

Arun ke hafte ka saath pratishat exposed tha, phir bhi unka role automate nahi, augment hua. Jo bacha (complaints, coordination, exceptions par judgement) wahi job ka core tha, aur wo badha. Jab assistant ne puraani return policy bata di, toh Arun ne sahi jawaab uske knowledge base mein likh diya. Unhone assistant ki galtiyon ke baare mein jutaaye sabooton ke saath apne manager se ye bhi kaha ki unhe assistant ke escalations review karne diye jaayein.

Team sach mein chhoti hui. Order volume utna hi raha, isliye jab saal bhar mein chaar log gaye, toh sirf ek ki jagah naya aadmi rakha gaya, aur team 12 se 9 ki ho gayi. Kisi ko nikaala nahi gaya, par naye support executives ki hiring dheemi ho gayi.

Unhone company ka internal AI tools course kiya, seekha ki assistant kaise tay karta hai ki kab escalate karna hai, aur ek naye role mein chale gaye: **support automation specialist**, jo assistant aur uske aaspaas ke workflows ko behtar karta hai.

Mushkil case Sana ka tha, us saal hire hui akeli fresher, jo saatvein mahine mein aayin. Arun ne saikdon aasaan queries par kaam seekha tha. Sana ko aisi queries lagbhag nahi milin, kyunki ab wo sab assistant sambhaalta tha. Pehle kuch hafton mein unhe sirf complex complaints di gayin aur unhe bahut mushkil hui. Team ne ise teen tareekon se theek kiya:

- Apne escalations lene se pehle unhone do hafte team ke sabse experienced agent Kiran ke saath escalations par **shadowing** ki.
- Har din unhone ek senior ke saath **assistant ke 20 jawaab review kiye** aur galat waale mark kiye, jisse unhone policies aasaan queries ke jawaab dene se bhi jaldi seekhin.
- Team ne "ye escalate kyun hua" ka ek shared log rakha, taaki har pecheeda case agle insaan ke liye sabak bane.

Teams ke liye sabak: jab AI seekhne ki zameen chheen le, toh aapko jaan-boojhkar nayi zameen banaani padti hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Job titles mein sochna.** "Accountants replace ho jaayenge" se kuch pata nahi chalta. Job ko tasks mein todo aur har ek ko dekho.
- **Exposure ko job loss samajhna.** Exposure wo hai jo AI kar sakta hai. Asal mein kya hoga, ye quality, cost, risk, rules aur demand tay karte hain.
- **Kisi ek ati par yakeen karna.** "AI saari jobs le lega" aur "kuch nahi badlega", dono zyaadatar roles ke liye galat hain. Asli kahaani badlaav ki hai, kabhi-kabhi kam logon ke saath.
- **Team ko bhool jaana.** Aapka role augment ho sakta hai jabki aapki team hiring rok de. Sirf apne tasks nahi, signals par bhi nazar rakho.
- **Sabse bade task se shuru karna.** Routine, aksar hone waale, aasaani se check hone waale task se shuru karo, na ki usse jo sabse zyada samay leta hai agar usmein judgement chahiye.
- **Seekhne ka kaam AI par chhod dena.** Jo freshers AI ke jawaab bina check kiye copy karte hain, wo wo judgement kabhi nahi bana paate jiske liye employers ab paise dete hain.
- **Pakke daavon waali predictions par bharosa karna.** Bhavishya mein jobs jaane ya banne ke sahi aankde kisi ko nahi pata. Poochho ki koi number kis tarah ke saboot se aaya hai.

## Practice karo

Neeche linked ILO ke 2025 index ka summary padho, aur OpenAI Academy ka AI at work par ek session try karo.

1. **Apni job ka map banao.** Apni current ya target job ke mukhya tasks ki list banao, har ek ke saath samay ka mota-mota hissa, kul milaakar 100%. Har ek ko high, medium ya low exposure mark karo, aur batao ki jo bachta hai us par kaun-sa nateeja (automated ya augmented) fit baithta hai.
2. **Ek task test karo.** Is hafte apna sabse routine, aasaani se check hone waala task ek AI assistant ke saath karo. Kitna samay bacha aur aapko kaun-kaun si galtiyaan theek karni padin, sab note karo.
3. **Signals pehchaano.** Upar waale signals mein se jo bhi aapko apni company mein ya jin companies mein aap jaana chahte ho unmein dikhein, likh lo.
4. **Ek skill chuno.** Agle teen mahinon mein banaane ke liye ek skill batao jo aapko AI ke saath zyada valuable banaaye, aur pehla resource jo aap use karoge.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** apni job ko tasks mein tod sako, bata sako ki kaun-se badal sakte hain aur kyun, bata sako ki aapki team ke saath kya ho sakta hai, aur aage rehne ka apna plan samjha sako.
