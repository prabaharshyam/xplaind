## Ye kya hai

"Kya AI meri job le lega?" India mein AI ke baare mein sabse common sawaal hai, final-year students se lekar senior managers tak. Honest jawab ye hai: ye aapke **tasks** par depend karta hai, job title par nahi.

Job kai tasks ka bundle hoti hai. Customer-support executive routine queries ke jawab deta hai, gusse waale customers ko sambhaalta hai, couriers se coordinate karta hai aur notes likhta hai. Teacher lessons plan karta hai, ideas samjhata hai, homework check karta hai aur class sambhaalta hai. AI in mein se kuch tasks mein bahut achha hai, kuch mein madadgaar hai, aur kuch mein bekaar.

```flow
title: Job, tasks ka ek bundle hai
Job -> Task A | routine, text based
Job -> Task B | check karna padta hai
Job -> Task C | bharosa aur judgement
Job -> Task D | physical, uljha hua
Task A -> AI ke liye exposed
Task B -> AI se madad milti hai
Task C -> Safe
Task D -> Safe
```

International Labour Organization (ILO) ki generative AI aur kaam par research ne paya ki **clerical kaam** (data entry, routine drafting, record keeping) sabse zyada exposed hai, aur baaki zyadatar occupations mein asar **augmentation** ka hone ki sambhaavna hai, yaani AI kaam ke ek hisse mein madad karta hai, na ki full **automation**, jahan AI insaan ki jagah kaam karta hai.

Is lesson mein hum Arun ke saath chalenge, jo Hyderabad mein customer-support executive hai, jab uski company AI assistant laati hai. Lesson ke end tak aap kisi bhi job ko tasks mein tod paoge, har ek ki exposure judge kar paoge, aur useful bane rehne ka plan bana paoge.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Exposure tasks ki hoti hai

Kisi bhi job ko judge karne ke liye uske tasks ki list banao aur har ek se teen sawaal poochho:

1. Kya AI ye task aaj achhi-khaasi quality mein kar sakta hai?
2. Kya AI iska ek hissa kar sakta hai, jab koi insaan check kare?
3. Kya ismein woh cheezein chahiye jo AI ke paas nahi hain: body, bharosa, accountability, uljhi situation mein judgement?

Jo tasks routine hain, rules follow karte hain aur zyadatar text ya numbers ke hain, woh sabse zyada exposed hote hain. Jin tasks mein log, physical kaam ya zimmedaari shaamil hai, woh sabse kam exposed hote hain.

| Task ka type | Examples | Exposure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine text aur data | Data entry, standard emails, simple translation, form bharna | Zyada |
| Drafting aur summarising | Reports, meeting notes, code ke pehle drafts | Zyada, par output check karna padega |
| Judgement ke saath analysis | Supplier chunna, kisi problem ka diagnosis | Medium: AI madad karta hai, insaan decide karta hai |
| Log aur bharosa | Negotiate karna, counselling, team sambhaalna, client ko bechna | Kam |
| Physical kaam | Electrical repairs, nursing care, construction | Kam |

### Ek role ke teen nateeje

Tasks jodne par ek role aam taur par teen mein se ek raaste par jaata hai:

- **Automated.** Zyadatar tasks AI kar sakta hai, isliye kam logon ki zaroorat padti hai. Ye mukhya roop se routine, text-based, rule-following kaam ke saath hota hai.
- **Augmented.** AI kaam ke kuch hisse sambhaalta hai aur log zyada, tez ya behtar kaam karte hain. Professional jobs mein ye sabse common nateeja hai.
- **Created.** AI ke aas-paas naye roles aate hain: AI applications banane waale engineers, businesses ke andar AI deploy karne waale log, use test karne waale evaluators, AI product managers, automation specialists aur trainers.

| Job ka kitna hissa AI kar sakta hai | Kam | Beech mein | Zyada |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kya hota hai | Zyadatar safe | Augmented | Automated |
| Examples | Nurse, sales | Analyst, coder | Data entry |

### Badlaav headlines se dheere kyun aata hai

AI kisi task ko kar sake, tab bhi organisations ko use apnaane mein time lagta hai. Unhe process badalne, staff train karne, regulators ko santusht karne, quality check karne, customer data protect karne aur kharche ko justify karne hote hain. Bharosa dheere banta hai, khaaskar banking, healthcare aur government mein. Isliye badlaav aam taur par saalon mein, team-by-team aata hai, raaton-raat nahi.

### India ke liye iska matlab

India ke paas bada IT services, business-process aur back-office workforce hai. Is kaam ke kuch hisse, jaise routine coding, support aur data processing, exposed hain. Wahi workforce duniya bhar ki companies ke liye AI banane aur deploy karne ke liye bhi achhi position mein hai, aur Indian job postings mein AI skills ki demand tezi se badh rahi hai. Dono baatein ek saath sach hain.

### Useful bane rehna

Chaar kadam lagbhag har nateeje mein kaam aate hain:

1. **Apni maujooda job mein AI ko achhe se use karo.** AI tools achhe se use karne waale log aksar un colleagues se zyada kaam nipta lete hain jo nahi karte.
2. **Judgement, relationships aur zimmedaari ki taraf badho.** Ye tasks logon ke paas hi rehte hain.
3. **Aise skills banao jo AI ke saath kaam aayein.** AI output check karna, domain knowledge, saaf communication aur data ke saath kaam karna.
4. **Agar aapko suit kare toh AI-focused role sochiye.** Xplaind ke paths dikhate hain ki har role ko kya chahiye.

## Ek example se samjho

Arun Hyderabad mein ek e-commerce company ke liye customer support mein kaam karta hai. Uski company ek AI assistant laati hai jo chat mein order-status aur return-policy ke sawaalon ke jawab deta hai.

Ye raha uska hafta pehle aur baad mein, apne time ke hisse ke roop mein:

| Task | Pehle | Chhe mahine baad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine status aur return queries | 60% | 10% (sirf woh jo assistant escalate karta hai) |
| Complex complaints | 25% | 45% |
| Warehouses aur couriers se coordinate karna | 15% | 20% |
| Assistant ke answers review karna aur behtar banana | 0% | 25% |

Assistant ne zyadatar routine queries sambhaal li, isliye Arun ki team chhoti ho gayi, logon ke chhodne par naye na rakhne se, layoffs se nahi. Arun ka kaam khatam nahi hua; woh khisak gaya. Ab woh zyadatar time mushkil cases par aur assistant ko behtar banane mein lagata hai: jab woh purani return policy quote karta hai, toh Arun sahi jawab uske knowledge base mein likh deta hai.

Usne company ka internal course AI tools par liya, seekha ki assistant kab escalate karne ka decide karta hai, aur chhe mahine baad ek naye role mein chala gaya, "support automation specialist", jahan woh assistant aur uske aas-paas ke workflows ko behtar banata hai.

Uski colleague Divya ne assistant use karne se mana kar diya. Uska kaam un cases tak simat gaya jo abhi tak kisi ne automate nahi kiye the, aur woh set ghatta ja raha hai. Dono ke beech ka farak talent ka nahi tha. Farak ye tha ki Arun un tasks ki taraf badha jo badh rahe the.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Job titles mein sochna.** "Accountants replace ho jaayenge" se kuch pata nahi chalta. Job ko tasks mein todo aur har ek ko dekho.
- **Dono extremes mein se kisi par bharosa karna.** "AI saari jobs le lega" aur "kuch nahi badlega", dono zyadatar roles ke liye galat hain. Asli kahaani badlaav ki hai.
- **Dekhte rehna ki kya hota hai.** AI use karna abhi seekhne se fayda hota hai, chaahe aapka role automate ho, augment ho ya bilkul na chhua jaaye.
- **Naye roles ko ignore karna.** Kai naye AI roles ko domain knowledge plus AI skills chahiye, computer science degree nahi.
- **Judgement ke bina sirf tools seekhna.** Employers un logon ko value dete hain jo AI output check kar sakein aur nateeje ki zimmedaari le sakein, sirf prompt type karne waalon ko nahi.
- **Confident predictions par bharosa karna.** Job losses ya gains ke exact numbers kisi ko nahi pata. Jo ye claim kare, usse saavdhaan raho.

## Practice karo

ILO ki generative AI aur jobs par research ka summary padho, jo neeche link hai, aur AI at work par OpenAI Academy ka ek session try karo.

1. **Apni job ka map banao.** Apni maujooda ya target job ke mukhya tasks likho, har ek ka andaazan time share ke saath. Har ek ko mark karo: shaayad automate hoga, madad milegi ya chhua nahi jaayega.
2. **Sabse zyada madad waale task ko test karo.** Is hafte woh task AI assistant ke saath karo. Note karo kitna time bacha aur kya check ya theek karna pada.
3. **Ek skill chuno.** Agle teen mahine mein banane ke liye ek skill chuno jo AI ke saath aapko zyada kimti banaye, aur pehla resource jo aap use karoge.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap apni job ko tasks mein tod sako, bata sako ki kaunse badlenge aur kyun, aur aage rehne ka apna plan samjha sako.
