## Ye kya hai

Research ka matlab hai kisi sawaal ko aise faisle mein badalna jiska aap bachaav kar sako. AI assistant dheeme hisse tezi se kar sakta hai: sources dhoondhna, lambe documents padhna, summarise karna, compare karna aur calculate karna. Par teen cheezein ab bhi aapki zimmedaari hain: **sawaal, sources aur nateeja.**

Ye lesson research workflow ke baare mein hai: sawaal kaise frame karein, kaun-sa assistant mode use karein, usse evidence kaise dikhwaayein, aur jo mila use kaise likhein. Kisi bhi ek jawaab ko check karne ka general routine (stakes, check ho sakne waale claims, bharosemand sources) "AI ke answer ko check karna" lesson mein hai. Yahaan hum usi par lambe research kaamon ke liye aage badhte hain.

Pehla faisla hai **kaun-sa mode use karein**, kyunki har mode apne alag tareeke se fail hota hai:

```flow
title: Sawaal ke hisaab se mode chuno
Aapka research sawaal -[pehla mota map]-> Plain chat
Aapka research sawaal -[aaj ke facts aur rules]-> Links waala web search
Aapka research sawaal -[kai sources ka chauda survey]-> Deep research report
Aapka research sawaal -[apni files se jawaab]-> Document notebook
Aapka research sawaal -[sheet ke numbers]-> Spreadsheet analysis
```

| Mode | Kiske liye achha | Aam failure | Aapka check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plain chat (sirf memory) | Key terms, ek overview, aage kya search karein | Puraane ya gadhe hue facts, kholne ko koi source nahi | Har fact ko finding nahi, ek lead maano |
| Links waala web search | Aaj ke rules, figures aur news | Asli link jo claim ko support nahi karta; official page ki jagah blog chun lena | Page par wo exact sentence dhoondho |
| Deep research report | Poora landscape: kai sources, options, arguments | Lambi reports jinmein kai citations kamzor support karte hain | Sirf un claims ko trace karo jin par faisla tika hai |
| Document notebook ya upload | Aapki reports, policies, papers par sawaal | Gaps memory se bharna; scanned PDFs mein tables galat padhna | Aisa sawaal poochho jiska jawaab document mein nahi hai |
| Spreadsheet analysis | Totals, trends, charts | Galat column, chhooti rows, kharaab data | Row count check karo, ek sample dobaara compute karo |

Hum Madurai mein garments ki dukaan chalaane waali Meena ke saath chalenge, jo ek tax faisle ko shuru se aakhir tak research karne ke liye assistant use karti hain.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Poochhne se pehle sawaal frame karo

Sawaal ki wording jawaab ko shape karti hai. Assistants aksar poochhne waale ki haan mein haan milaane lagte hain, is aadat ko researchers **sycophancy** kehte hain, isliye leading sawaal par ektarfa jawaab mil sakta hai.

- **Leading:** "Chhoti dukaanon ke liye GST composition scheme kyun behtar hai?"
- **Neutral:** "Tamil Nadu ki ek garments dukaan ke liye GST composition scheme ke faayde aur nuksaan kya hain, aur kise ise use karne ki ijaazat nahi hai?"

Achha research sawaal **jagah** (rules state aur desh ke hisaab se badalte hain), **samay** ("is saal ke hisaab se"), **scope** (kiske liye, kitne bade business ke liye) aur aapko chahiye wo **output** (ek table, faayde-nuksaan ki list, kisi expert se poochhne waale sawaal) batata hai.

Do prompts bias aur kam karte hain:

- "Iske khilaaf sabse mazboot case kya hai?"
- "Kaun-sa evidence aapka jawaab badal dega?"

### Explore karo, aur taareekh par nazar rakho

Chaudi shuruaat karo: "Is topic ko aasaan bhasha mein samjhaao, key terms list karo, aur batao kaun-se official sources ise cover karte hain." Us overview ko **map maano, asli zameen nahi**. Wo batata hai kahaan dekhna hai, ye nahi ki sach kya hai.

Har model ek **knowledge cutoff** tak ke data par train hota hai, aur uske baad kya badla ye use nahi pata. Research ki sabse aam galti hai koi rule, rate, limit ya price jo kuch saal pehle sach tha, use aaj ka bata dena. Isliye ek rule maano: **jo kuch bhi badalta hai, uske source ki taareekh dhoondho aur official site par confirm karo.** Agar jawaab sirf memory se aaya hai, toh maan lo ki wo purana ho sakta hai.

### Web jawaabon se unka evidence dikhwao

Jab assistant web par search karta hai, toh links dikhaata hai. Unhe kholna shuruaat hai, ant nahi. Asli test hai **support**: kya page sach mein wahi kehta hai jo jawaab claim karta hai? Ek asli, official page ko aise claim ke liye cite kiya ja sakta hai jo wo kehta hi nahi.

Is kaam ka kuch hissa assistant se hi karwao:

- "Har claim ke liye source ka wo exact sentence quote karo jo use support karta hai, aur page ki taareekh do."
- "In sources mein kaun-sa original hai, aur kaun-se kisi aur ka figure report kar rahe hain?"

Phir **upstream** jao. Agar koi blog NPCI ka figure quote kare, toh NPCI kholo. Agar koi news story kisi government circular ko cite kare, toh wo circular kholo. Secondary sources ek-doosre se copy karte hain, isliye ek hi number dohraane waale paanch pages bhi ek hi source hain.

Ye koi kabhi-kabhaar waali dikkat nahi hai. Tow Center for Digital Journalism ke March 2025 ke ek test mein aath AI search tools se chhote news excerpts ka source pehchaanne ko kaha gaya, aur unhone 60% se zyada queries ke galat jawaab diye.

### Deep research modes

Kai assistants ab **deep research** mode dete hain (OpenAI ne February 2025 mein ChatGPT mein ek launch kiya, aur doosre assistants ke apne hain). Ek tez search ki jagah, ek agent kai minute tak bahut saare pages search karta aur padhta hai, phir darjanon citations ke saath ek lambi report likhta hai. OpenAI ne khud launch par chetaaya tha ki ye phir bhi galat facts bata sakta hai aur galat nateeje nikaal sakta hai.

Deep research ko **chaude pehle survey** ke liye use karo: "Kaun-se options hain, har ek ke baare mein sources kya kehte hain, aur wo kahaan asahmat hain?" Ise aakhri baat mat maano. Pench ye hai ki **zyada citations ka matlab hai zyada checking, zyada bharosa nahi.** 20 pages ki report poori lagti hai, par wo puraane aur naye sources mila sakti hai, copy kiye pages ko sahmati gin sakti hai, aur aise sentence par citation laga sakti hai jise source bas halka-sa support karta hai.

Ek practical rule: report padhne se pehle, **wo teen se paanch claims likh lo jin par aapka faisla tika hai**. Assistant se un claims ki ek table maango, har ek ke source, taareekh aur support karne waale quote ke saath. Unhe original sources se check karo, aur report ke baaki hisse ko background maano.

### Jawaabon ko apne documents mein ground karo

**Grounding** ka matlab hai model se memory ki jagah aapke diye material se jawaab dilwaana. Aap chat mein file upload kar sakte ho, ya isi ke liye bana notebook tool use kar sakte ho, jaise Google ka Gemini Notebook (July 2026 tak iska naam NotebookLM tha). Google kehta hai ki uska chat aapke jode sources se jawaab deta hai aur aise citations deta hai jin par click karke source mein quote kiya gaya hissa dekh sakte ho.

Kai tools ye **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** se karte hain: wo aapke documents mein aapke sawaal se mel khaane waale passages search karte hain, phir unhi passages se jawaab likhte hain. Agar search galat passage utha le, toh jawaab galat hoga, bhale hi wo aapki file cite kare.

Ek mazboot grounding prompt:

> Sirf attach kiye gaye documents se jawaab do. Har point ke liye sentence quote karo aur page number do. Agar documents mein is sawaal ka jawaab nahi hai, toh "Not in the documents" reply karo.

Phir **canary test** karo: aisa ek sawaal poochho jiska jawaab aapko pata hai ki documents mein nahi hai. Agar assistant phir bhi jawaab de, toh wo gaps memory se bhar raha hai, aur aapko uske baaki jawaab aur sakhti se check karne chahiye. Scanned PDFs ki tables se liye numbers bhi check karo, wo galat padhe ja sakte hain.

### Academic sources

Students aur analysts aksar research papers maangte hain. Models asli dikhne waale citations gadh sakte hain, jo **hallucination** ka ek roop hai: asli journal ka naam, yakeen karne laayak authors aur aisa title jo hai hi nahi. Scientific Reports mein chhapi 2023 ki ek study mein GPT-3.5 ke banaaye 55% citations aur GPT-4 ke banaaye 18% citations fabricated the. Aap koi bhi tool use karo, kisi paper par bharosa karne se pehle use check karo:

1. Exact title Google Scholar mein ya publisher ki site par search karo. Agar nahi milta, toh cite mat karo.
2. Abstract padho. Check karo ki paper ne wahi study kiya jo claim kehta hai (wahi log, wahi outcome).
3. Wo paper cite karo jo aapne padha, assistant ka uska summary nahi.

### Spreadsheets

Kai assistants peeche code likhkar aur chalaakar spreadsheet analyse karte hain. Maths aam taur par sahi hota hai; gadbad **data aur column choice** mein hoti hai:

| Dikkat | Kya galat hota hai | Kaise pakdein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galat column | "amount" ki jagah "invoice number" ka total | Poochho kaun-sa column use kiya |
| Merged cells ya extra header rows | Rows chhod deta hai ya do baar ginta hai | Uska row count apne se compare karo |
| Text ki tarah rakhe numbers | CSV mein lakh commas ke saath type kiye 1,00,000 jaise values text ki tarah padhe jaate hain ya chhoot jaate hain | Poochho har total mein kitni rows use huin |
| Positive numbers ki tarah rakhe returns | Refunds sales mein jud jaate hain | Ek mahina haath se dobaara calculate karo |
| Blank ya "NA" cells | Rows chupchaap averages se baahar ho jaati hain | Poochho missing values kaise handle keen |

Hamesha poochho: "Calculation aur code dikhao, aur batao kitni rows use keen." Phir ek-do numbers khud dobaara calculate karo.

### Jo mila use likho

Aakhir mein ek chhota **research note** banao, AI ka paste kiya jawaab nahi. Har claim ke liye mark karo ki evidence kitna mazboot hai:

- **Verified:** aapne kisi original ya official source mein padha, aur taareekh note ki.
- **Likely:** koi bharosemand secondary source kehta hai, par aapne original nahi dekha.
- **Unverified:** sirf assistant kehta hai. Us par bharosa mat karo.

Yahi note aap apne manager, teacher ya accountant ke saath share karte ho. Isse dikhta hai ki aapko kya pata hai, kaise pata hai, aur kya abhi open hai.

## Ek example se samjho

Meena ki Madurai mein garments ki dukaan hai. Wo jaanna chahti hain ki **GST composition scheme** join karein ya nahi, jismein chhote businesses turnover par fixed rate se tax bharte hain aur filing aasaan hoti hai. Neeche dukaan ke numbers is example ke liye banaaye gaye hain; GST rules asli hain aur November 2026 mein check kiye gaye.

**Step 1: frame aur explore.** Unka pehla draft tha "Composition scheme meri dukaan ke liye kyun achhi hai?". Wo ise neutral banaakar dobaara likhti hain:

> Meri Tamil Nadu mein garments ki dukaan hai. 2026 ke hisaab se GST composition scheme ke faayde aur nuksaan kya hain, turnover limit kya hai, aur ise kaun use nahi kar sakta? Har rule ke liye taareekh aur source do.

Web search on hone par assistant jawaab deta hai ki **₹1 crore tak** turnover waali dukaanein join kar sakti hain, aur do pages cite karta hai: ek official government page aur ek tax blog.

**Step 2: support aur taareekh check karo.** Wo poochhti hain, "₹1 crore limit bataane waala sentence page ki taareekh ke saath quote karo." Quote 2018 mein likhe blog se aata hai. Official page kuch aur kehta hai. Wo official GST aur government sites par confirm karti hain ki Tamil Nadu samet zyaadatar states mein goods suppliers ke liye limit **₹1.5 crore** hai, jo 1 April 2019 se ₹1 crore se badhaayi gayi thi. (Kuch special category states mein limit ₹75 lakh hai.) Assistant ne ek secondary source ka purana figure use kiya tha.

**Step 3: unke data mein ground karo.** Wo pichhle saal ki sales sheet upload karti hain:

> Sirf is sheet se monthly sales aur yearly total do. Calculation dikhao aur batao kitni rows use keen.

Assistant yearly total **₹1,41,10,000** batata hai. Wo October check karti hain, jo Deepavali ki wajah se unka sabse busy mahina hai. Assistant ₹19,02,000 dikhaata hai. Unka apna jod ₹17,78,000 aata hai: ₹18,40,000 ki sales mein se ₹62,000 ke returns ghataakar. Returns positive numbers ki tarah rakhe the, isliye ghatne ki bajaay **jud** gaye. Is galti ka asar doguna hota hai: farq 2 × ₹62,000 = ₹1,24,000 hai.

Wo returns column theek karke dobaara chalaati hain. Poore saal mein sales ₹1,38,00,000 aur returns ₹3,10,000 the, isliye unki net sales ₹1,41,10,000 nahi, **₹1,34,90,000** hain.

Ab Step 2 ki galti ki keemat dikhti hai. Puraani ₹1 crore limit se wo khud ko galat tareeke se baahar maan letin. Agar net sales hi gina jaane waala turnover hai (ye unke accountant se poochhne ka sawaal hai), toh wo ₹1.5 crore limit se ₹15,10,000 neeche hain, aur agle saal lagbhag 11% growth unhe limit ke paar le jaayegi.

**Step 4: khilaaf waala case maango.** Wo poochhti hain, "Meri jaisi dukaan ko join na karne ke sabse mazboot kaaran kya hain?" Do baatein saamne aati hain. Composition taxpayers apni purchases par input tax credit claim nahi kar sakte, aur goods ki inter-state supply nahi kar sakte. Meena Kerala ke customers ko orders bhejna shuru karne ka plan bana rahi thin. Wo dono rules ek official source par confirm karti hain.

**Step 5: note likho.**

| Claim | Evidence | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tamil Nadu mein goods ke liye limit ₹1.5 crore hai | Official GST pages, 1 April 2019 se laagu | Verified (November 2026 mein check kiya) |
| Unki net sales ₹1,34,90,000 thin | Unki apni sheet, theek karke dobaara compute ki | Verified |
| Input tax credit nahi; goods ki inter-state supply nahi | Official source | Verified |
| Kya net sales hi gina jaane waala turnover hai, aur isliye unke paas kitna headroom hai | Abhi nahi mila | Open: accountant se poochhna hai |

Wo note aur chaar sawaal lekar apne accountant ke paas jaati hain, jinmein ye bhi hai ki saal ke beech limit paar ho jaaye toh kya hoga aur Kerala waala plan faisle ko kaise badalta hai. Assistant ne unke ghanton ki padhaai bachaayi; aakhri faisla accountant ne kiya.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Leading sawaal poochhna.** "X kyun behtar hai?" par X ke paksh ka case milta hai. Dono pehlu aur khilaaf waala case maango.
- **Aaj ke rules ke liye sirf memory waale jawaab use karna.** Limits, rates aur prices badalte hain. Taareekh aur official page dhoondho.
- **Secondary source par ruk jaana.** Koi figure dohraane waala blog ya news story original nahi hai. Upstream jao.
- **Lambi hone ki wajah se report par bharosa karna.** Darjanon citations ka matlab hai check karne ko zyada. Bas un kuch claims ko trace karo jin par faisla tika hai.
- **Canary test chhod dena.** Agar aap kabhi aisa sawaal nahi poochhte jiska jawaab document mein nahi hai, toh aapko pata nahi ki wo grounded hai ya nahi.
- **Bina khole papers cite karna.** Har title Google Scholar mein dhoondho aur abstract padho.
- **Row count ke bina spreadsheet total maan lena.** Jab tak aap na poochho, chhooti ya do baar gini rows dikhti nahi.
- **AI ka jawaab apni finding banaakar paste karna.** Ek chhota note likho jo bataaye ki kya verified hai aur kya abhi open hai.
- **Private documents kisi unapproved tool par upload karna.** Kaun-se tools kaun-sa data dekh sakte hain, is par apni organisation ke rules maano.

## Practice karo

Neeche linked OpenAI Academy ke research sessions dekho, aur Civic Online Reasoning se ek lateral reading lesson try karo.

1. **Pehle frame, phir search.** Kaam ya padhaai ke liye aapko chahiye koi rule ya figure chuno. Sawaal ka ek leading aur ek neutral version likho, dono chalao, aur note karo ki jawaab kaise alag hain. Neutral waale mein har claim ke liye support karne waala quote aur taareekh maango, aur original source tak upstream jao.
2. **Ground karo aur test karo.** Koi public report upload karo aur page references aur quotes ke saath paanch sawaal poochho. Ek canary sawaal jodo jiska jawaab report mein nahi hai. Har reference check karo aur note karo ki assistant ne canary ko kaise handle kiya.
3. **Ek sheet check karo.** Assistant ke saath ek chhoti spreadsheet analyse karo. Uska row count apne se compare karo aur do numbers haath se dobaara calculate karo.

**Aapka kaam poora hai jab aap** ek chhota research note dikha sako jismein kam se kam ek aisa claim ho jise aapne galat, purana ya unsupported pakda, aur wo original source jisne baat saaf ki.
