## Ye kya hai

AI assistants research ke ghante bacha sakte hain, par woh aapko bade confidence se galat jawab bhi thama sakte hain, jise aap meeting mein dohra do. Skill "research ke liye AI use karna" nahi hai. Skill hai **research ke liye AI use karna aur use tezi se check karna**.

Assistants teen tareeke se madad karte hain:

- **Kisi topic ko explore karna:** overview, key terms, mukhya arguments aur aage kya dekhna hai.
- **Apne material se sawaal poochhna:** report, policy ya spreadsheet upload karke uske baare mein poochhna.
- **Compare karna aur sochna:** options tolna, pros aur cons ki list banana, kisi plan ke chhed dhoondhna.

Answer par aap kitna bharosa kar sakte ho, ye is par depend karta hai ki **woh aaya kahan se**:

```flow
dir: LR
title: Answer kahan se aata hai, aur use check karna kitna aasaan hai
Sirf model ki memory -> Mushkil: kholne ke liye koi source nahi
Links ke saath web search -> Achha: links kholo
Aapka upload kiya document -> Sabse achha: page check karo
Spreadsheet aur calculation -> Achha: ek sample dobara nikaalo
```

Is lesson mein hum Meena ke saath chalenge, jo Madurai mein chhoti dukaan chalati hain, jab woh ek tax decision samajhne ke liye assistant use karti hain. Lesson ke end tak aap jaan jaoge ki AI ke saath research ko explore, ground aur check kaise karna hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Topic explore karna

Pehle broad shuru karo, phir narrow karo. "Ek chhote trader ke liye GST input tax credit kaise kaam karta hai, simple shabdon mein, example ke saath samjhao" aapko ek map deta hai. Phir poochho, "Official GST portal par mujhe kaunse key terms search karne chahiye?" taaki aap source tak ja sako. Overview ko map samjho, zameen nahi: ye batata hai ki kahan dekhna hai, ye nahi ki kya sach hai.

### Sources ke saath answers

Jab assistant web par search karta hai, toh woh links dikhata hai. Unhe kholo. Har ek ke liye teen cheezein check karo:

1. **Kya ye hai bhi?** Links tooTe hue ya banaye hue ho sakte hain.
2. **Kya ye reliable hai?** Official sites (government portals, RBI ya SEBI jaise regulators, UPI ke liye NPCI) aur jaane-maane publishers, anonymous blogs se behtar hain.
3. **Kya ye wahi kehta hai jo answer claim karta hai?** Models kabhi-kabhi asli page cite kar dete hain jo claim ko support nahi karta.

Fact-checkers kisi page ko chhodkar dekhne ki aadat ko, ki doosre sources kya kehte hain, **lateral reading** kehte hain. Ye ek hi page ko ghoorkar uske design se judge karne se tez aur zyada reliable hai.

### Apne documents mein grounding

Document upload karne se answer uspar **grounded** ho jaata hai. Saaf kaho ki aap kya chahte ho: "Sirf is document se jawab do aur har point ke liye page number do." Phir cite kiye pages check karo. Agar document mein jawab nahi hai, toh achha assistant bol deta hai. Agar phir bhi jawab de de, toh shaayad woh gap ko memory se bhar raha hai.

### Spreadsheets

Kai assistants spreadsheets ko background mein code likhkar aur chalakar analyse karte hain: totals, averages, charts aur trends. Ye powerful hai, aur boring tareeko se fail hota hai:

| Problem | Kya galat hota hai | Kaise pakdein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Column galat padhna | "Amount" ki jagah "invoice number" jod deta hai | Poochho ki kaunsa column use kiya |
| Merged cells ya extra headers | Rows chhod deta hai ya do baar ginta hai | Row count check karo |
| Indian number formats | 1,00,000 ko galat padhta hai | Bade numbers spot-check karo |
| Returns ya refunds positive mein | Totals phool jaate hain | Ek mahina haath se dobara nikaalo |
| Number columns mein text | Chupchaap rows chhod deta hai | Poochho ki kitni rows use hui |

Hamesha poochho, "Dikhao ki har number kaise calculate kiya," aur do-teen khud dobara nikaalo.

### Thinking partner

Kuch use-cases mein perfect facts ki zaroorat nahi hoti. "Ye meri doosri dukaan kholne ka plan hai; kya galat ho sakta hai?" ya "Ulta view rakho" aapko saaf sochne mein madad karte hain, chaahe ek point kamzor ho.

## Ek example se samjho

Meena Madurai mein garments ki dukaan chalati hain. Woh jaanna chahti hain ki GST composition scheme mein register karein ya nahi, jo chhote businesses ke liye simpler filing deti hai.

**Step 1: explore.** Woh scheme ka simple explanation maangti hain, kaun use kar sakta hai aur uske mukhya pros aur cons. Jawab saaf aur achhe se organised hai. Woh jaanti hain ki tax rules badalte rehte hain, isliye official sources maangti hain. Web search on karne par assistant GST portal aur ek government FAQ ka link deta hai. Woh dono kholti hain. Answer mein ek turnover limit purani hai; official page par maujooda figure hai. Woh sahi figure note kar leti hain.

**Step 2: apne data mein ground karna.** Woh pichhle saal ki sales spreadsheet upload karti hain aur monthly turnover aur saal ka total maangti hain, calculation dikhane ke saath. Woh do mahine haath se check karti hain. Ek total zyada hai: customer returns ki ek row positive number ki tarah dali gayi thi. Woh sheet theek karti hain aur analysis dobara chalati hain.

**Step 3: socho.** Woh poochhti hain, "In numbers ko dekhte hue, switch karne ke baare mein mujhe apne accountant se kaunse sawaal poochhne chahiye?" Unhe chhe kaam ke sawaal milte hain, jinmein ye bhi hain ki input tax credit ka kya hoga aur kya woh doosre states mein bech sakti hain.

| Step | AI ne kya kiya | Meena ne kya check kiya | Kya mila |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Explore | Scheme samjhayi | Official GST pages | Ek limit purani thi |
| Ground | Unki sales ka total kiya | Do mahine haath se | Returns sales mein ginti hui |
| Think | Sawaalon ki list banayi | Kuch nahi chahiye tha | Chhe achhe sawaal |

AI ne unke ghante padhne ke bacha diye aur accountant ke saath unki baatcheet kaafi behtar kar di. Final faisla accountant ne kiya, assistant ne nahi.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Bina source ke answers par bharosa karna.** Sources maango, aur unhe kholo.
- **Citations ko bina padhe maan lena.** Asli link bhi claim ko support na kare, ye ho sakta hai.
- **Bina source ke statistics maan lena.** Bina source ke sahi-sunayi dene waale numbers ek classic hallucination hain.
- **Spreadsheet maths check na karna.** Totals haath se spot-check karo, aur poochho ki kaunse columns use hue.
- **Tax, law ya health par AI ko final authority maan lena.** Use taiyaari ke liye use karo, phir official sources ya professionals se confirm karo.
- **Private documents ko unapproved tools mein upload karna.** Aapki organisation ke rules follow karo ki kaunsa tool kaunsa data dekh sakta hai.

## Practice karo

OpenAI Academy ke research par sessions dekho, neeche linked, aur Civic Online Reasoning ka lateral reading par ek lesson try karo.

1. **Sources ke saath research.** Kaam ya padhai ke liye zaroori ek topic par research karo. Sources maango, har ek kholo, aur mark karo ki kaunse claims sahi nikle, kaunse purane the aur kinka koi support nahi tha.
2. **Document mein ground karo.** Ek public report upload karo aur page references ke saath paanch sawaal poochho. Har reference check karo.
3. **Spreadsheet check karo.** Ek chhoti spreadsheet assistant se analyse karwao aur uske do numbers haath se verify karo.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap ek aisa claim dikha sako jo aapne galat ya purana pakda, aur bata sako ki aapko exactly kaise pata chala.
