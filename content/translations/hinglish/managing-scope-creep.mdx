## Ye kya hai

**Scope creep** time, budget ya baaki scope mein matching badlaav ke bina project ko jo deliver karna hai uska dheere-dheere badhna hai. AI projects mein ye aksar bhola-bhaala dikhta hai:

- "Kya ye Tamil documents bhi handle kar sakta hai?"
- "Collections team ko bhi yahi chahiye."
- "Kya ye sirf summary nahi, credit decision bhi le sakta hai?"
- "Chalo WhatsApp bhi jod dete hain."

Har request theek ho sakta hai. Saath mein, informally maan liye jaane par, ye core deliverable ko uski date ke aage dhakel dete hain aur success ka matlab dhundhla kar dete hain.

```flow
Request aata hai | "jab kar hi rahe ho toh..." -> "Main size karke Thursday tak aata hoon" -> Size karo | kaam, risk, time, cost, kya displace hoga
Size karo -> Options
Options -> Swap | ise jodo, kuch same size ka hatao
Options -> Extend | ise jodo, date khisakao ya budget jodo
Options -> Later | agla phase, maujooda gate ke baad
Options -> No | purpose ya rules se takrata hai
Swap -> Business owner decide karta hai
Extend -> Business owner decide karta hai
Later -> Business owner decide karta hai
No -> Business owner decide karta hai
Business owner decide karta hai -> Change request log | kisne, kya, decision, date
```

Ye lesson Chennai ke ek lender ke credit-file pilot ke hafta saat ko follow karta hai, jab ek hafte mein teen requests aaye.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Pehchaano.** "Jab kar hi rahe ho toh" se shuru hone waale requests, agreed users ke bahar ke logon ke requests, aur "done" ka matlab badalne waale changes par nazar rakho. Har ek ko scope of work se check karo.

**Wahin haan mat kaho.** Kaho: "Achha idea. Main ise size karke Thursday tak batata hoon ki ismein kya lagega aur kya khisakega."

**Size karo.** Har request ke liye: involve kaam, risks (nayi language ko naya eval data chahiye aur shaayad alag model bhi), timeline aur cost par asar, aur ye ki yeh kya displace karega.

**Refusals nahi, options do.** Aam taur par:

1. *Swap*: ise jodo aur same size ka kuch hatao.
2. *Extend*: ise jodo aur date khisakao ya budget jodo.
3. *Later*: ise agle phase ki list mein daalo, maujooda gate ke baad.
4. *No*: jab ye project ke purpose ya client ke apne rules se takraye (jaise AI ko regulatory review ke bina credit decisions lene dena).

**Decisions record karo.** Ek change request log jisme request, kisne manga, sizing, decision, kisne approve kiya aur kab. Approve karne waala SOW mein naam waala business owner ho, jisne maanga woh nahi.

**Relationship bachao.** Log zyada isliye maangte hain kyunki kaam valuable hai. Shukriya kaho, dikhao ki aap ise serious lete ho, aur trade-off ko dikhne do; zyadatar clients ko choice diye jaane ki value hoti hai.

**Ek change request log:**

| Request | Kiska | Sizing | Recommendation | Decision | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Collections correspondence summaries | Collections head | Lagbhag 4 hafte, naya golden set | Baad mein: phase 2 | Head of operations ne accept kiya | Hafta 7 |
| Tamil salary slips | Regional credit head | Lagbhag 8 din, Tamil OCR, 50 examples | PDF export se swap | Accept hua | Hafta 7 |
| Approve ya reject recommendations | Senior officer | Regulatory aur fairness review chahiye | Is project ke liye na | Accept hua | Hafta 7 |

**AI kaam ko honestly size karna.** Kisi bhi nayi language, document type ya task ke liye ye shaamil karo: golden set ke liye examples jama karke label karna, check karna ki maujooda model aur parsing ise handle karte hain ya nahi, evals chalana aur failures fix karna, guardrails aur unke tests update karna, aur users ki training. Jo teams sirf code change ko size karti hain woh aksar aadha kam estimate karti hain.

**Achhe se na kehna.** Vyakti ko shukriya kaho, wajah ek sentence mein batao jo project ke purpose ya client ke rules se juda ho, aur ek raasta do: alag assessment, baad ka phase, ya poochne ke liye sahi team. Log aam taur par raaste ke saath clear na maan lete hain; bharosa woh vague haan todta hai jo kabhi aati nahi.

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai lender pilot ka hafta 7. Ek hafte mein aapko teen requests milte hain:

1. Collections head borrower correspondence ke summaries maangte hain.
2. Ek regional credit head Tamil-language salary slips ko support karne ko kehte hain.
3. Ek senior officer poochta hai ki kya system approve ya reject recommend kar sakta hai.

Head of operations ko aapka change request note:

- *Collections summaries*: naye document types, naye users, naya golden set. Lagbhag chaar hafte. **Recommend: baad mein**, production gate ke baad phase 2 ke roop mein; warna ye rollout ko ek mahine khisaka dega.
- *Tamil salary slips*: do regions mein 6% files; Tamil OCR aur 50 labelled examples chahiye. Lagbhag aath din. **Recommend: swap** planned PDF export feature ke saath, jise officers kam hi use karte hain.
- *Approve ya reject recommendations*: system ko credit ke liye assistance se decision support mein badal deta hai, regulatory aur fairness implications ke saath. **Recommend: na** is project ke liye; chahiye toh risk aur compliance teams ke saath alag assessment.

**Phir kya hua.** Head of operations ne teeno recommendations ek meeting mein accept kar li. Collections team ko phase 2 ki date mili, jo doosra contract ban gaya. Tamil swap pilot ki end date khisakaye bina hafta 9 mein ship hua.

| Request | Recommendation | Nateeja |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collections summaries | Baad mein, phase 2 ke roop mein | Date waala phase 2, phir doosra contract |
| Tamil salary slips | PDF export se swap | Hafta 9 mein ship; end date wahi |
| Approve ya reject recommendations | Is project ke liye na | Alag assessment offer hua |

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Meeting mein hi haan kehna.** Hamesha pehle size karo.
- **Options ke bina na kehna.** Ye madad na karne jaisa lagta hai aur logon ko aapko bypass karne par majboor karta hai.
- **Maangne waale ko approve karne dena.** Sirf agreed business owner changes approve kare.
- **Likhna nahi.** Bina record ke changes acceptance par jhagde ban jaate hain.
- **AI ke khaas costs ko ignore karna.** Nayi languages, document types ya tasks ko naya eval data aur testing chahiye, sirf code nahi.

## Practice karo

Atlassian ka scope creep par article padho.

1. Apne use case ke liye alag-alag logon ke teen believable change requests sochkar likho.
2. Har ek ko size karo (kaam, risk, timeline aur cost par asar) aur swap, extend, later ya no recommend karo, ek line ki reasoning ke saath.
3. Change request log entries aur woh chhota note likho jo aap business owner ko bhejoge.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap meeting ke beech ke request par shaanti se "main ise size karta hoon" keh sako aur options ke saath wapas aa sako jinmein se client chun sake.
