## Ye kya hai

"AI job" koi ek job nahi hai. Companies roles ke ek poore parivaar ke liye hire karti hain, aur titles har company mein badalte rehte hain. Ek firm ka "GenAI developer" wahi karta hai jo doosri firm ka "AI engineer" karta hai, aur "AI engineer" naam ki post asal mein data analysis ho sakti hai. Isliye AI career ka pehla niyam hai: **title nahi, job description mein likhi duties padho.**

Roles ko baantne ka sabse kaam ka tareeka ye hai ki ek aam hafte mein tum kitna code likhte ho:

```flow
dir: LR
title: Role ko kitna code chahiye
Code nahi | AI product manager, AI governance, apni field mein AI -> Thoda code | AI automation specialist, AI security engineer, AI evaluation
Thoda code -> Roz code | AI, GenAI aur agentic AI engineers, FDE, ML engineer, MLOps, data engineer
```

### Roz code likhne waale roles

- **AI engineer** AI systems shuru se aakhir tak banata hai. India mein is title ka matlab aamtaur par language models par applications hota hai, par kai posts classical machine learning ki basics bhi maangti hain.
- **Generative AI and LLM engineer** language models mein gehraai tak jaata hai: prompts, structured output, RAG, evals aur serving costs.
- **Agentic AI engineer** aise agents banata hai jo plan karte hain, tools call karte hain aur actions lete hain, aur unhe safe aur observable banata hai.
- **Forward deployed engineer (FDE)** yahi systems kisi customer ke business ke andar banata hai. Aadha kaam engineering hai; baaki aadha sahi problem dhoondhna aur logon se tool use karwaana.
- **ML engineer** company ke apne prediction models train aur deploy karta hai: fraud, credit risk, ranking, forecasting, vision. Pehchaan hai labelled data, features aur retraining.
- **MLOps engineer** production mein models chalaata hai: serving, drift aur cost ki monitoring, safe releases aur shared platform.
- **Data engineer** wo pipelines banata hai jo har model ke liye zaroori data ko move aur clean karti hain. Iske bina koi AI system nahi chalta, isliye AI teams mein ghusne ka ye ek aam aur pakka raasta hai.
- **Data scientist aur applied scientist** data analyse karte hain, experiments chalaate hain aur naye models design karte hain. Big tech aur research labs mein applied scientist roles ke liye aksar master's ya PhD aur published work chahiye hota hai.

### Thode code waale roles

- **AI automation specialist** n8n, Make ya Power Automate jaise tools se business apps jodta hai aur unmein AI steps daalta hai. Tum APIs aur JSON padhoge aur chhoti scripts likhoge.
- **AI security engineer** AI systems ko bachaata hai aur bachaav ke liye AI use karta hai. Ek aam hafte mein kisi chatbot ka threat model banana, use prompt injection aur data leaks ke liye test karna, aur ye review karna ki koi agent kin cheezon ko chhoo sakta hai, sab shaamil hai. Ye security basics aur light Python par tika hai, aur dono skill sets waale log kam hain.
- **AI evaluation aur data quality roles** test questions, grading rubrics aur labelling guidelines likhte hain, aur model ke jawaab judge karte hain. Kai log annotation ya rating ke kaam se shuru karte hain. Pakka entry point domain expertise hai: legal answers rate karne waala lawyer, ya Tamil output judge karne waala Tamil speaker. Sirf rating ka kaam shaayad hi senior role tak badhta hai; engineer bankar evals banana badhta hai.

### Bina code waale roles

- **AI product manager** tay karta hai ki kaunse AI features banein, quality kaise measure ho, aur model galat ho toh kya kiya jaaye.
- **AI governance and compliance specialist** check karta hai ki AI safe, fair aur kaanoon ke hisaab se hai: risk assessments, policies, audits aur incident handling, India ke Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act jaise kaanoonon ke tahat, aur EU customers ke liye EU AI Act ke tahat.
- **Tumhari maujooda field, saath mein AI.** AI samajhne waale marketers, HR staff, teachers, lawyers aur doctors ki kadr title badle bina bhi hoti hai.

Xplaind par in das naamon waale roles mein se har ek ka roadmap hai: AI Engineer, Generative AI and LLM Engineer, Agentic AI Engineer, Forward Deployed Engineer, ML Engineer, MLOps Engineer, AI Automation Specialist, AI Security Engineer, AI Product Manager, aur AI Governance and Compliance Specialist.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Jobs kahaan hain

| Employer ka type | Kya banaate hain | Kya dhoondhte hain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IT services firms | Global clients ke liye AI solutions | Delivery, client skills, kabhi-kabhi cloud certifications; badi sankhya mein freshers lete hain |
| Product companies aur startups | Apne products mein AI features | Mazboot builders, speed, deployed projects |
| Global capability centres (GCCs) | Kisi multinational ke apne business ke liye AI | Engineering quality, domain knowledge, security awareness |
| Consulting firms | Strategy aur implementation | Communication, problem framing, delivery |
| Videshi companies, remote | Product ka kaam | Behtareen portfolio aur saaf likhna; bahut competition |

GCCs par khaas dhyaan do. Nasscom-Zinnov India GCC Landscape report (May 2026) ne FY26 mein 2,117 GCCs gine, jinmein lagbhag 2.36 million log kaam karte the, aur Bengaluru sabse bada hub hai. Kisi bank ya retailer ka GCC apne business ke liye AI banaane ko engineers hire karta hai, isliye wahaan IT services firm se zyada domain knowledge ki kadr hoti hai.

Kul milaakar AI hiring kitni badi hai? foundit ke job-posting tracker ne 2025 mein India mein 2,90,256 AI-linked job posts gine, jinmein IT-software aur banking and finance aage the, aur Bengaluru top hub tha. Usne 2026 mein 32% growth ka forecast diya. Aise numbers dhyaan se padho: post ka matlab hire nahi hai, ek hi opening kai baar post ho sakti hai, aur "AI-linked" mein wo jobs bhi ho sakti hain jinmein AI ka bas zikr hai.

### Hiring kaise hoti hai

**Kahaan dhoondhein.** Naukri aur LinkedIn se shuru karo. Instahyre aur foundit bhi khoob use hote hain, aur Wellfound par kai startups milte hain. GCCs aksar apne careers pages se hire karte hain. Andar ke kisi insaan ka referral aksar kisi insaan tak tumhari application pahunchaane ka sabse tez raasta hai, isliye sau cold applications se zyada alumni aur seniors maayne rakhte hain.

**Engineering role ke aam rounds.** Steps company ke hisaab se badalte hain, par aam pattern ye hai:

1. Ek online coding test ya take-home task.
2. Python, APIs aur AI basics par ek technical round, jaise "Is RAG app ko jawaab gadhne se kaise rokoge?"
3. Ek project deep dive. Wo tumhare resume se ek project uthaate hain aur poochte hain ki har choice kyun ki, use kaise measure kiya aur kya fail hua. Fake ya copy kiye projects yahin bikhar jaate hain.
4. Experienced hires ke liye ek design round: "Roz 10,000 users ke liye ek support assistant design karo".
5. Teamwork, salary aur notice period par ek manager ya HR round.

Product, governance aur security roles mein coding test ki jagah case studies hoti hain: ek spec likhna, kisi feature ka risk assess karna, ya kisi chatbot ki kamzori dhoondhna.

### Employers kya dhoondhte hain

- **Engineering roles:** Python, APIs, Git aur ek cloud; prompting, RAG, agents aur evals jaisi language-model skills; aise projects jo notebook nahi, ek deployed aur measured system dikhaayein.
- **Product, governance aur automation roles:** ye jaanna ki AI kya kar sakta hai aur kya nahi, problems frame karna, metrics define karna, processes map karna aur saaf likhna.
- **Sabke liye:** evidence. Test set aur ek number waala project, ya kaam par kuch deliver karne ki kahaani, certificates ki list se bhaari padti hai. Certificates zyaadatar IT services firms mein aur cloud-heavy roles mein tiebreaker ki tarah madad karte hain, mukhya proof ki tarah nahi.

### Pay aur seniority

Ye beech ke bands hain, offers nahi. Big tech, global AI labs aur Bengaluru zyada dete hain; IT services firms aur chhote shehar kam dete hain. Jobs page har quarter mein re-check hota hai, isliye dono mein farq ho toh is table ki jagah us par bharosa karo.

Ye table padhte waqt teen gotchas:

- **Oonche early bands shaayad hi fresher jobs hote hain.** Zyaadatar FDE aur AI product manager posts pehle ka engineering ya product experience maangti hain, isliye "0 se 2 saal" waala figure un gine-chune logon par laagu hota hai jo jaldi ghus paate hain.
- **CTC ka matlab haath mein aane waali salary nahi.** Ismein aksar variable pay, joining bonuses aur aisa stock shaamil hota hai jo shaayad kabhi poora na mile.
- **Specialist pay insaan ke saath chalti hai, title ke saath nahi.** Bank mein fraud models ship karne waala ML engineer us "GenAI engineer" se zyada kama sakta hai jisne sirf demos banaaye hain.

### Timelines aur fresher market

Mote taur par, Xplaind ke roadmaps mein lagaataar padhaai ka itna samay lagta hai: product, automation aur governance paths ke liye lagbhag teen mahine, agentic AI ke liye chaar, FDE, MLOps aur AI security ke liye chhah, aur AI engineer, GenAI engineer aur ML engineer ke liye nau. Ye sirf seekhne ka samay hai. Job search ke liye kuch mahine aur jodkar plan karo.

Freshers ke liye sabse mushkil hai. Kai posts do ya zyada saal ka experience maangti hain, aur jis routine kaam par juniors pehle seekhte the, uska bada hissa ab AI karta hai (lesson "AI aur naukriyaan" dekho). Freshers IT services hiring, convert hone waali internships, GCC graduate programmes aur aise chhote startups ke raaste ghuste hain jo degree se zyada projects ko maante hain. Experienced log aamtaur par **apni hi company ke andar** kisi AI pilot mein judkar sabse tez switch karte hain.

## Ek example se samjho

### Ek job post ko decode karna

Maan lo Chennai ki final-year B.Tech student Sneha ko ye post milti hai (gadha hua example, par aam jaisa):

> **GenAI Engineer, fintech, Chennai, 0 to 2 years.** UPI transaction data se features banao. Fraud flag karne ke liye gradient-boosted models train aur tune karo. Precision aur recall har hafte track karo aur har mahine retrain karo. Python, SQL, scikit-learn. LLMs ka exposure plus hai.

Title GenAI kehta hai. Duties kehti hain features, training, precision, recall aur retraining, aur LLMs bas "plus" hain. Table ke hisaab se ye **ML engineer** ki job hai. Job achhi hai, par agar Sneha teen RAG projects ke saath apply karegi toh galat fit lagegi. Wo ise "ML, tabhi apply karungi jab ek tabular project jod loon" mark karke aage badh jaati hai.

Wo isi tarah bees posts padhti hai. Chaudah asli AI engineer ka kaam hain, chaar ML engineer ka aur do AI label waali analyst jobs. Un chaudah mein baar-baar aane waali skills hain Python, RAG, evaluation aur deployment, toh yahi uska plan hai.

### Sneha ka switch, mahine-dar-mahine

Neeche ke numbers ek realistic raaste ka illustration hain, survey nahi.

- **Mahine 1 se 3:** AI Engineer roadmap par Git, APIs aur uski pehli language-model calls. Use Python basics pehle se aati hain, isliye wo pehli stage ka kuch hissa skip karti hai aur seekhna nau ki jagah saat mahine mein poora kar leti hai.
- **Mahine 4 se 7:** teen projects, har ek deployed aur har ek ke saath ek chhoti evaluation report.
  1. **Apni university ke 200 pannon waale academic regulations par ek question-answering assistant.** Wo sahi jawaabon ke saath 60 test questions likhti hai. Shuru mein answer accuracy 68% hai, zyaadatar isliye ki do pannon mein bante rules kho jaate the. Text ko split karne ka tareeka badalne se ye 85% pahunch jaati hai. Wo ye bhi measure karti hai ki cite kiya gaya page kitni baar sach mein jawaab ko support karta hai.
  2. **Ek invoice extractor** jo scan kiye GST invoices ko JSON mein badalta hai: GSTIN, date, total aur tax. 100 invoices par wo field-dar-field accuracy measure karti hai. Ye check jodne se ki har GSTIN theek 15 characters ka ho, kai reading errors pakde jaate hain.
  3. **Ek support-ticket agent** jo complaint padhta hai, tool call se order dhoondhta hai aur reply draft karta hai. Wo measure karti hai ki ye kitni baar sahi tool chunta hai aur har ticket par kitna cost aata hai.
- **Mahine 8 se 10:** applications. Pehli 30 par lagbhag koi jawaab nahi aata. Wajah uska resume hai: usmein results ki jagah tools ki list hai ("LangChain, FAISS, FastAPI"). Wo har bullet ko result ke roop mein dobaara likhti hai: "raised answer accuracy from 68% to 85% on a 60-question test set". Agli 50 applications aur paanch alumni referrals se use aath first rounds milte hain.
- **Kya galat hua.** Ek project deep dive mein interviewer poochta hai ki uska assistant bache hue 15% questions par kyun fail hua. Usne dekha hi nahi tha, aur wo reject ho jaati hai. Tab se wo har project ke failure cases ki ek likhi hui list rakhti hai.
- **Result:** Chennai ki ek mid-size product company se ₹9 lakh CTC par AI engineer offer, 0 se 2 saal waale 6 to 12 band ke andar.

### Do chhote cases

Us safeguard ke saath wahi pilot AI product lead ke taur par uska naya role ban jaata hai.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Title, hype ya sabse oonchi salary dekhkar chunna.** Commit karne se pehle bees asli job descriptions padho aur us role mein kaam kar rahe kisi se baat karo.
- **Title mein "AI" waali har post par apply karna.** GenAI label lagi ML ya analyst job tumhari application aur recruiter ka samay dono barbaad karti hai.
- **Projects ki jagah certificates jama karna.** Certificates dikhaate hain ki tumne padhaai ki. Employers ko proof chahiye ki tum bana ya deliver kar sakte ho.
- **Bina numbers waale projects.** Bina test set ka deployed demo baaki har resume jaisa dikhta hai. Use measure karo aur jaano ki wo kahaan fail hota hai.
- **Apni maujooda taakat ko nazarandaaz karna.** AI skills jodne waala security analyst, banker ya nurse, ek aur fresh GenAI engineer se zyada durlabh hai.
- **Sab kuch seekhne ki koshish.** Ek roadmap chuno aur gehraai mein jao; baad mein phaila sakte ho.
- **Raaton-raat results ki ummeed.** Mahinon ki padhaai aur mahinon ki search ka plan banao.
- **Sirf sabse bade naamon par apply karna.** GCCs, mid-size product firms aur IT services ki AI practices milkar aksar zyada log hire karti hain.

## Practice karo

Skills ko bade nazariye se dekhne ke liye Watch & learn panel mein video lessons dekho.

1. **Das job descriptions decode karo.** Naukri ya LinkedIn par India ki das AI posts dhoondho. Har ek ke liye title, sabse zyada bataayi gayi teen duties, wo asal mein kaunsa role hai (upar ki table use karo) aur usmein coding chahiye ya nahi, ye likho.
2. **Khud ko match karo.** Apne background se sabse zyada mel khaane waale do roles chuno. Har ek ke liye likho ki tumhare paas pehle se kya hai, kya kami hai, aur Jobs page par salary band kya hai.
3. **Evidence ka ek tukda plan karo.** Ek project ya work pilot chuno jo tum aath hafte mein poora kar sako, aur likho ki use kaise measure karoge.
4. **Kisi insaan se baat karo.** Un roles mein se kisi ek mein kaam kar rahe kisi insaan ko message karo, jaise koi senior, alumnus ya LinkedIn contact, aur poocho ki ek aam hafta kaisa dikhta hai.

**Tumhara kaam poora tab hai jab tum** apne liye sabse sahi AI role ka naam bata sako, hype nahi balki job posts ke aadhaar par teen wajahein de sako, aur bata sako ki evidence ka pehla tukda kya banaoge.
