## Ye kya hai

"AI job" ek job nahi hai. Companies kai roles ke families ke liye hire karti hain, aur titles company-company badalte hain. Ek firm ka "GenAI developer" wahi karta hai jo doosri firm ka "AI engineer". Isliye AI career ka pehla rule hai: **sirf title nahi, job description padho.**

Ye mukhya roles ka map hai, is hisaab se group kiya ki unhe kitna code chahiye:

```flow
dir: LR
title: Role ko kitna code chahiye
Coding nahi ya kam | Aapke field mein AI (HR, law, sales), AI governance aur compliance -> Thodi coding | AI product manager, automation specialist
Thodi coding -> Bahut coding | AI ya GenAI engineer, forward deployed engineer, agent aur RAG specialist, MLOps, ML engineer, data scientist
```

### AI ke saath banana (coding roles)

- **AI engineer ya GenAI engineer** language models ke upar applications banata hai: prompts, documents par retrieval (RAG), agents, evaluation aur deployment. Ye sabse tezi se badhte AI titles mein se ek hai.
- **Forward deployed engineer** ek AI engineer hai jo seedhe customers ke saath kaam karta hai, solve karne layak problem dhoondhta hai aur system ko roz ke use mein laata hai.
- **Agent aur RAG specialists** un agents ko banane mein gehre jaate hain jo actions lete hain, ya company documents par search karte hain.
- **MLOps, LLMOps ya AI platform engineer** ML aur AI systems ko bade scale par bharosemand tareeke se chalata hai: serving, monitoring, cost aur security.
- **ML engineer aur data scientist** prediction, ranking, fraud aur forecasting ke liye machine learning models train aur deploy karte hain.

### AI ko shape karna aur use karna (coding kam ya nahi)

- **AI product manager** decide karta hai ki kaunse AI features banane hain aur ye kaise measure karna hai ki woh kaam kar rahe hain.
- **AI automation specialist** low-code tools aur AI steps se business workflows automate karta hai.
- **AI governance aur compliance specialist** check karta hai ki AI safe, fair aur lawful hai: risk assessments, policy, audits aur incident handling, India ke DPDP Act jaise laws ke tahat aur EU customers ke liye EU AI Act ke tahat.
- **Aapka maujooda field, plus AI skills.** AI-literate marketers, analysts, HR staff, teachers, lawyers aur doctors ki value badh rahi hai, job title badle bina bhi.

Is lesson mein hum Chennai ke teen logon ke saath chalenge, ek student, ek developer aur ek hospital manager, jab har ek apne liye sahi role chunta hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Jobs kahan hain

India mein AI hiring paanch tarah ke employers se aati hai:

| Employer ka type | Kya banate hain | Kya dhoondhte hain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IT services firms | Global clients ke liye AI solutions | Deliver kar sakne waale engineers, client skills, kabhi-kabhi certifications madad karte hain |
| Product companies aur startups | Apne products mein AI features | Strong builders, speed, deployed projects |
| Global capability centres (GCCs) | Multinational ke apne business ke liye AI | Engineering quality, domain knowledge, security awareness |
| Consulting firms | Strategy aur implementation | Communication, problem framing, delivery |
| Overseas companies, remote | Product kaam | Shaandaar portfolio, written communication; bahut competitive |

GCCs multinational banks, retailers aur manufacturers ke in-house technology offices hain, jo Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai aur NCR mein hain. Ye AI roles ka ek bada source ban gaye hain.

### Employers kya dhoondhte hain

- **Engineering roles:** Python, APIs, Git aur kam se kam ek cloud; language-model skills jaise prompting, RAG, agents aur evals; aur aise projects jo deployed, measured system dikhayein, notebook nahi.
- **Product aur automation roles:** ye jaanna ki AI kya kar sakta hai aur kya nahi, problems achhe se chunna, metrics define karna, processes map karna, saaf communicate karna, aur tools ka hands-on use.
- **Sabke liye:** saboot. Real projects ka portfolio, ya kaam par aapne kuch deliver kiya uski kahaani, certificates ki list se zyada kaam aati hai.

### Pay aur seniority

Pay company, city aur experience ke hisaab se bahut badalta hai. AI roles aam taur par comparable non-AI roles se zyada pay karte hain, par articles mein salary figures sirf mote signals hote hain, aksar chhote samples par based. Online padhe kisi number ke liye hi role mat chuno.

### Entry ke raaste

| Shuruaat ka point | Realistic raasta |
| --- | --- |
| Student ya fresher | Strong project portfolio plus internships; AI engineer ya ML engineer path |
| Software engineer | Apne maujooda stack mein LLM, RAG aur agent skills jodo; AI Engineer, Generative AI & LLM Engineer ya FDE path |
| Data analyst | ML engineering ya AI evaluation mein badho |
| Domain depth waale non-engineer | AI product, automation ya apne field mein AI roles |

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai ke teen log AI careers ke baare mein sochte hain.

**Sneha**, Python mein comfortable final-year B.Tech student, AI engineer banna chahti hai. Woh bees job descriptions padhti hai aur wahi skills baar-baar dekhti hai: Python, RAG, evaluation, deployment. Woh AI Engineer path follow karti hai, teen deployed projects banati hai, har ek ke saath chhoti evaluation report, aur startups aur GCCs mein apply karti hai. Uski marks nahi, uska portfolio use interviews dilata hai.

**Rahul** ke paas ek IT services firm mein Java backend developer ke roop mein paanch saal hain. Use zyadatar engineers se zyada clients se baat karna pasand hai. Woh forward deployed engineering chunta hai. Woh FDE path follow karta hai, apne backend aur integration experience ko taakat banata hai, aur ek internal pilot ke liye volunteer karke apni company ki AI practice mein chala jaata hai.

**Kavitha** ek hospital chain mein operations manager hain aur unhone kabhi code nahi kiya. Woh healthcare operations mein AI adoption lead karna chahti hain. Woh AI Product Manager path aur automation lessons leti hain. Woh ek pilot lead karti hain jo doctors ke review ke liye discharge summaries draft karta hai, bacha hua time aur pakdi gayi galtiyan measure karti hain, aur wahi pilot unka naya role ban jaata hai.

| Insaan | Background | Chuna hua role | Mukhya saboot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sneha | Student, Python | AI engineer | Evals ke saath teen deployed projects |
| Rahul | Java developer, client-friendly | Forward deployed engineer | Internal AI pilot deliver kiya |
| Kavitha | Hospital operations, coding nahi | AI product lead | Apne hi hospital mein measured pilot |

Har kisi ne apne background aur interests ke hisaab se role chuna, jisme sabse zyada buzz tha woh nahi.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Title ya hype dekhkar chunna.** Commit karne se pehle real job descriptions padho aur us role mein kaam kar rahe logon se baat karo.
- **Projects ki jagah certificates jama karna.** Certificates dikhate hain ki aapne padhai ki. Employers saboot chahte hain ki aap bana ya deliver kar sakte ho.
- **Apni maujooda strengths ko ignore karna.** Domain knowledge plus AI skills ek rare aur kimti combination hai.
- **Sab kuch seekhne ki koshish karna.** Ek path chuno aur gehre jao; baad mein chaude ho sakte ho.
- **Raaton-raat nateeje ki ummeed.** AI mein zyadatar career switches job ke saath-saath mahino ki lagaataar mehnat maangte hain.
- **Sirf sabse bade naamon mein apply karna.** GCCs, mid-size product firms aur IT services ki AI practices aksar zyada logon ko hire karti hain.

## Practice karo

Skills ka wider view paane ke liye Watch & learn panel ke video lessons dekho.

1. **Das job descriptions padho.** Kisi bhi job site par India ke das AI job postings dhoondho. Har ek ke liye title, sabse zyada mention hue teen skills aur ye note karo ki coding chahiye ya nahi.
2. **Khud ko match karo.** Apne background ke hisaab se sabse achhe do roles chuno. Har ek ke liye likho ki aapke paas kya hai aur kya kam hai.
3. **Kisi insaan se baat karo.** In roles mein se kisi mein kaam kar rahe ek insaan ko message karo, jaise koi senior, alumnus ya LinkedIn contact, aur poochho ki ek normal hafta kaisa hota hai.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap bata sako ki kaunsa AI role aapke liye sabse achha hai aur saboot par based, hype par nahi, teen wajah de sako.
