## Ye kya hai

AI product utna hi achha hota hai jitna uska data: woh documents jo woh jawab dete waqt padhta hai, woh examples jinse use judge kiya jaata hai, aur woh feedback jo use behtar banata hai. **Data strategy** in sab ka plan hai, jo build shuru hone se pehle likha jaata hai, launch ke baad dhoondha nahi jaata.

Yeh chhe sawaalon ka jawab deti hai:

| Sawaal | Example jawab |
| --- | --- |
| Product ko run time par aur improvement ke liye **kaunsa data** chahiye? | Run time par policy documents; evaluation ke liye asli customer sawaal aur experts ke jawab |
| Woh **kahan se** aata hai, aur **kiska** hai? | Documents policy team ke hain; question log support ka hai |
| Kya hum use is purpose ke liye **use kar sakte hain**? | Customers ko bataya gaya ki chats jawab behtar karne mein madad karti hain, aur woh opt out kar sakte hain |
| Woh **kitna achha** hai, aur current kaise rakhte hain? | Har quarter documents review; duplicates hataye gaye |
| Usse product ko **evaluate kaise** karein? | Har release se pehle 150 sawaalon ka golden set chalaya jaata hai |
| **Use se behtar data kaise** banta hai? | Thumbs down aur reason, har hafte review; fixes golden set mein jode jaate hain |

```flow
title: AI product mein teen tarah ka data
Run-time data | jawab dete waqt kya padhta hai: documents, records, history -> Ek behtar product
Evaluation data | kis par judge hota hai: asli sawaalon ka golden set -> Ek behtar product
Feedback data | baad mein users kya batate hain: thumbs, edits, overrides -> Ek behtar product
```

Is lesson mein hum Lakshmi ke saath chalenge, jo Chennai ke ek agri-tech startup mein product manager hai, jab woh ek voice assistant ka data plan likhti hai jo kisaano ke fasal ke sawaalon ke jawab deta hai. Lesson ke end tak aap ek page ka data plan likh paoge jise privacy officer aur engineer dono maan lein.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### Run-time data

Jo product documents se jawab deta hai, uske liye tay karo ki woh kaunse document collections mein search karta hai, unhe fresh kaise rakhte hain, aur kaun kya dekh sakta hai. Personalisation ke liye tay karo ki consent ke saath kaunsa user data use hota hai. Gayab ya purana run-time data aksar model se zyada galat jawab deta hai.

### Evaluation data

**Golden set** asli jaise inputs ka collection hota hai, expected outputs ya quality judgements ke saath, domain experts ke saath banaya hua. 50 se 100 cases se shuru karo, asli users se, banaye hue nahi. Har zaroori failure ke saath ise badhao. Golden set product ka quality contract hai: iske bina "kya ab behtar hai?" bas ek raay hai.

### Feedback data

Product ko aise design karo ki pehle din se feedback capture ho:

- thumbs up aur down, chhote reason ke saath;
- drafts mein users ke kiye edits, jo saaf dikhate hain ki kya galat tha;
- human reviewers ke overrides;
- kisi insaan ko escalations.

Feedback tabhi kaam ka hai jab koi use schedule par review kare aur fixes aur naye golden-set cases mein badle.

### Labelling

Kuch improvements ke liye labelled data chahiye: sahi categories, sahi extractions, sahi jawab. Tay karo ki label kaun karega (domain experts, trained staff ya vendor), kin likhit guidelines ke saath, aur label quality kaise check hogi, jaise do logon se ek sample label karwakar compare karna.

### Permission aur privacy

India ke **DPDP Act** aur milte-julte laws ke tehat, personal data sirf unhi purposes ke liye use ho sakta hai jinke baare mein logon ko bataya gaya aur unhone maana, ya kisi aur lawful ground par. Model behtar karne ke liye customer chats use karne ke liye saaf notice aur consent chahiye ho sakta hai. Business clients ke contracts unke data ko unki apni service ke alawa kisi aur kaam mein use karne se rok sakte hain. Yeh sab collect karne se **pehle** tay karo.

### Quality aur coverage

| Check | Sawaal | Ignore kiya toh risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage | Kya hamare paas saare user types, languages aur regions hain? | Gayab groups ke liye failures aur bias |
| Freshness | Kya data current hai? | Purane jawab |
| Duplicates | Kya ek hi cheez kai baar ginti ja rahi hai? | Tedhe results |
| Errors | Kya labels aur records sahi hain? | Product galtiyan seekhta hai |

Data ka bias product ka bias ban jaata hai.

### Ownership aur governance

Har dataset ka owner tay karo, kitne samay rakhna hai aur kab delete karna hai yeh define karo, aur likho ki woh kahan se aaya. Bina owner ka data chupchaap purana ho jaata hai.

### Flywheel

```flow
dir: LR
title: Flywheel
Zyada use -> Zyada feedback -> Behtar data -> Fixes aur naye golden cases -> Behtar product -> Zyada use
```

Achhe products use ko aise data mein badalte hain jo product ko behtar banata hai. Flywheel tabhi ghoomta hai jab feedback capture ho, permitted ho aur sach mein use ho.

## Ek example se samjho

Chennai ka ek agri-tech startup ek voice assistant bana raha hai jo kisaano ke fasal ke sawaalon ke jawab Tamil aur English mein deta hai. Product manager Lakshmi data plan likhti hai.

| Data | Source | Owner | Permission | Kitne samay rakhna |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Run-time: crop advisories | Agricultural university ke advisories aur state agriculture department ki guidance, har season update | Content lead | Public sources | Badalne tak |
| Run-time: weather aur prices | Weather aur market price feeds | Engineering | Licensed feeds | Rolling 30 din |
| Evaluation: golden set | Field staff ke saath jama kiye 200 asli kisaan sawaal, paanch fasalon aur teen districts se, jawab do agronomists ne check kiye | Lakshmi | Kisaano ne collection ke dauran haan kaha | Chalta rahega |
| Feedback | Har jawab ke baad voice prompt "Kya isse madad mili?", callback option, aur field staff ke hafte ke corrections | Support lead | Pehli baar use par Tamil mein notice, opt-out ke saath | Recordings 90 din, jab tak kisaan aur samay ke liye haan na kahe |

**Ek coverage gap.** Pehle 200 sawaal review karte waqt woh dekhti hai ki mahila kisaano se lagbhag koi sawaal nahi aaya, jabki target districts mein khet ka zyadatar kaam wahi karti hain. Field staff mahilaon ke self-help groups ke saath do sessions karte hain aur 60 sawaal jodte hain, jinmein kai aisi fasalon aur keedon ke hain jo pehle sample mein chhoot gaye the.

**Chhe mahine baad:**

| Measure | Launch par | Chhe mahine baad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Golden set ka size | 200 sawaal | 650 sawaal |
| Accuracy reporting | Sirf overall | Fasal aur district ke hisaab se, mahine ka |
| Sabse aam failure | Local boli mein keedon ke naam | Feedback se bane glossary se theek hua |

Glossary seedha flywheel se aaya: kisaano ne "madad nahi mili" dabaya, field staff ne recordings suni, aur team ne local naam jod diye.

## Aam galtiyaan

- **Maan lena ki jo data aapke paas hai woh use kar sakte ho.** Pehle consent, law aur contracts check karo.
- **Evaluation set nahi.** Iske bina quality raay ban jaati hai aur har change ek jua.
- **Feedback buttons jinhe koi nahi padhta.** Sirf button nahi, hafte ka review routine banao.
- **Coverage ko ignore karna.** Gayab groups aur languages failures aur bias ban jaate hain.
- **Owner nahi.** Bina owner ka data purana ho jaata hai aur kisi ko pata nahi chalta.
- **Test questions khud banana.** Asli users ke sawaal team ke socche hue sawaalon se zyada uljhe aur kaam ke hote hain.

## Practice karo

Neeche link kiya hua data collection aur evaluation par PAIR guidebook ka chapter aur AI products behtar karne par Hamel Husain ka field guide padho.

1. **Data ki list banao.** Kisi AI product idea ke liye run-time, evaluation aur feedback data likho, har ek ke source aur owner ke saath.
2. **Permission.** Har ek ke liye permission ka aadhar likho aur kitne samay rakhoge.
3. **Golden set.** Domain jaanne waale kisi ke saath 30 evaluation cases ka draft banao, aur ek coverage gap note karo.

**Aapka kaam poora jab:** aap ek page ka data plan present kar sako jise privacy officer aur engineer dono maan lein.
