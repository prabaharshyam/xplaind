## Ye kya hai

AI risk frameworks kisi organisation ko batate hain ki AI risks ko kaise dhoondhna hai, manage karna hai aur ye kaise saabit karna hai ki wo control mein hain. Kuch law hain, kuch voluntary, ek certify ho sakta hai. Indian teams ka aamtaur par paanch se waasta padta hai.

| Framework | Kis tarah ka | Maujooda version (28 November 2026 ko check kiya) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU AI Act | Law, fines ke saath | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, jise Digital Omnibus on AI, Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 ne amend kiya, 27 July 2026 se force mein |
| NIST AI Risk Management Framework | Voluntary | AI RMF 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1, January 2023) aur saath mein Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1, July 2024). NIST kehta hai ki 1.0 revise ho raha hai; abhi koi naya version publish nahi hua |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Certify hone waala standard | ISO/IEC 42001:2023, first edition. Europe ne 2026 mein ise bina badlaav ke EN ISO/IEC 42001:2026 ke roop mein adopt kiya |
| India ki AI Governance Guidelines | Guidance | MeitY ne 5 November 2025 ko release keen |
| RBI FREE-AI aur model risk guidance | Regulated finance ke liye guidance | FREE-AI committee report, 13 August 2025; draft model risk management guidance, 24 June 2026 (koi final version nahi mila) |

**EU AI Act** AI ko is hisaab se baant-ta hai ki wo kis kaam ke liye use hota hai, is hisaab se nahi ki wo kaun-sa model use karta hai.

- **Banned practices**, jaise social scoring aur aisi manipulation jo vulnerable logon ka faayda uthaaye. Omnibus "nudifier" apps aur AI-generated child sexual abuse material par ban jodta hai.
- **High-risk uses**, jo Annex III mein list hain: workers ko hire aur manage karna, individuals ki credit scoring, education tak access, essential services, kuch biometrics aur baaki kuch, aur saath mein wo AI jo medical devices jaise regulated products ka safety part hai (Annex I). Duties: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, users ke liye instructions, human oversight, aur accuracy, robustness aur security.
- **Transparency duties** (Article 50): chatbot ko logon ko batana hoga ki wo AI se baat kar rahe hain, aur generated ya manipulated content ko mark karna hoga.
- **Minimal risk**: spam filters, drafting aids. Staff ke liye basic AI literacy ke alaawa kuch extra nahi.

General-purpose models banaane waalon ki alag duties hain. Ye Act har us company tak pahunchta hai jiska system EU market mein rakha gaya ho ya jiska output EU mein use hota ho, chahe wo kahin bhi based ho. Fines banned practices ke liye 35 million euros ya worldwide turnover ke 7% tak, aur zyaadatar baaki breaches ke liye 15 million euros ya 3% tak jaate hain.

**Dates maayne rakhti hain**, kyunki Act dheere-dheere stages mein laagu hota hai:

| Date | Kya laagu hona shuru hota hai |
| --- | --- |
| 1 August 2024 | Act force mein aata hai |
| 2 February 2025 | Banned practices; staff ke liye AI literacy duty |
| 2 August 2025 | General-purpose AI models ke liye duties; EU governance bodies |
| 2 August 2026 | Article 50 transparency duties, jaise chatbot disclosure |
| 2 December 2026 | Naya nudifier ban; 2 August 2026 se pehle se market mein maujood generative systems ke liye marking grace period khatam |
| 2 December 2027 | Annex III uses, jaise hiring aur credit, ke liye high-risk duties (2 August 2026 se aage khiskaai gayin) |
| 2 August 2028 | Regulated products ke andar waale AI ke liye high-risk duties (2 August 2027 se aage khiskaai gayin) |

Ye delay chhutti nahi hai: buyers abhi se evidence maangte hain, aur ek saal ki quarterly fairness reports deadline se ek mahina pehle taiyaar nahi ki ja saktin.

**NIST AI RMF** ek vocabulary hai jo bahut-se buyers share karte hain. Iske chaar functions hain **govern** (policies, roles, culture), **map** (context aur risks), **measure** (tests aur metrics) aur **manage** (prioritise karo aur act karo). Large language models par build karne waali teams iski **Generative AI Profile** se map karti hain, jo 12 risks list karti hai, jaise confabulation (poore bharose se diya gaya galat output), data privacy, information security, harmful bias aur human-AI configuration (logon ka system par zaroorat se zyada bharosa).

**ISO/IEC 42001** ek AI management system describe karta hai: policy, roles, risk aur impact assessment, controls, internal audit aur improvement. Accredited bodies iske against certify karti hain (ISO/IEC 42006:2025 unke rules tay karta hai), aur ISO/IEC 42005:2025 impact assessment ke liye guide karta hai. Certificate dikhata hai ki aapka process kaam karta hai; ye saabit nahi karta ki koi ek model fair ya safe hai.

**India** mein koi AI-specific law nahi hai. MeitY ki guidelines saat principles ("sutras") tay karti hain, jaise people first, fairness and equity, accountability aur understandable by design, aur ek AI Governance Group, AI Safety Institute se testing aur ek national AI incident database ki sifaarish karti hain. Binding rules maujooda laws se aate hain: amended IT Rules (20 February 2026 se) synthetic content par label zaroori karte hain, aur DPDP Act personal data ko cover karta hai, jiske Rules ke tahat zyaadatar duties May 2027 mein shuru hoti hain. Finance mein RBI ki FREE-AI report ne 26 non-binding recommendations deen, jaise board-approved AI policy aur AI models ki inventory. June 2026 ki draft model risk guidance final hone par banks aur NBFCs se inventories, independent validation aur human oversight ki maang karegi.

Is lesson mein hum Bengaluru ki ek HR-tech company ko follow karte hain, jo apne resume-screening assistant ko apne pehle European client ke liye taiyaar kar rahi hai.

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

### 1. Model ko nahi, use ko classify karo

Poochho ki system kya decide ya influence karta hai, kiske baare mein, aur galti kitni buri hogi. Wahi model emails draft karte hue minimal risk hai aur job applicants ko rank karte hue high risk.

```flow
title: EU AI Act classification ka pehla pass
Use describe karo -> Banned practice hai?
Banned practice hai? -[haan]-> Ruko | redesign karo
Banned practice hai? -[nahi]-> Regulated product ka safety part hai?
Regulated product ka safety part hai? -[haan, third-party checks zaroori]-> High risk | poori duties
Regulated product ka safety part hai? -[nahi]-> Annex III area hai?
Annex III area hai? -[haan]-> Logon ko profile karta hai?
Logon ko profile karta hai? -[haan]-> High risk
Logon ko profile karta hai? -[nahi]-> Sirf narrow ya preparatory task?
Sirf narrow ya preparatory task? -[nahi]-> High risk
Sirf narrow ya preparatory task? -[haan]-> High risk nahi | document karo aur register karo
Annex III area hai? -[nahi]-> Logon se baat karta hai ya content generate karta hai?
Logon se baat karta hai ya content generate karta hai? -[haan]-> Transparency duties
Logon se baat karta hai ya content generate karta hai? -[nahi]-> Minimal risk
```

Yahaan gotcha Article 6(3) hai. Annex III system high risk se tabhi bachta hai jab wo koi narrow procedural ya preparatory task kare, poore ho chuke human work ko improve kare, ya pichhle decisions se hatne waale cases ko human review ke liye flag kare, aur wo bhi decision ko materially influence kiye bina. Jo system logon ko profile karta hai, jaise candidates ya borrowers ko score karna, wo hamesha high risk hai, final call chahe koi bhi le. Exception par bharosa karne ka matlab hai ki wajah document karo aur phir bhi EU database mein register karo.

### 2. Tay karo ki aap provider ho ya deployer

High-risk systems ke liye yahi tay karta hai ki kaun kya karega.

| Role | Kaun | Mukhya duties |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Provider | Jo ise banata hai aur apne naam se market karta hai | Design duties (risk management, data governance, logging, documentation), quality management system, conformity assessment, CE marking, EU database registration, post-market monitoring, incident reporting, aur agar EU ke bahar based hai toh ek EU authorised representative |
| Deployer | Jo ise apne kaam mein use karta hai | Instructions ke mutaabik use, adhikaar waale trained overseers, kam se kam chhah mahine logs rakhna, workers aur prabhavit logon ko batana; aur agar wo public body ya public-service provider hai, ya credit score karta hai ya life ya health insurance ki price tay karta hai, toh ek fundamental-rights impact assessment |

Zyaadatar Annex III uses ke liye, hiring aur credit samet, conformity assessment internal hai: provider khud check aur document karta hai, koi notified body nahi. Jo deployer system ko rebrand kare, uska purpose badle ya use substantially modify kare, wo provider ban jaata hai.

### 3. Ek hi risk register rakho

Har risk (wrong output, bias, privacy breach, prompt injection, over-reliance, misuse) ke liye likelihood, impact, controls, ek owner aur dated evidence record karo. Ek hi register har framework se map hota hai: "unfair ranking" waali row EU AI Act mein data governance aur accuracy hai, NIST mein map aur measure, aur ISO/IEC 42001 mein ek risk assessment. Ise data ki tarah rakho, aur jab kisi serious risk mein control, owner ya fresh evidence na ho toh ek script ko review fail karne do:

```python
from datetime import date

LEVEL = {"low": 1, "medium": 2, "high": 3}
RISKS = [
    {"id": "R1", "risk": "Unfair ranking by college tier", "likelihood": "medium", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["college names removed from input", "quarterly fairness eval"],
     "owner": "Head of data science", "evidence_date": date(2026, 9, 30)},
    {"id": "R2", "risk": "Candidate data sent to a vendor that trains on it", "likelihood": "low", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["zero-retention contract"], "owner": "", "evidence_date": None},
    {"id": "R3", "risk": "Recruiters accept shortlists without reading them", "likelihood": "high", "impact": "high",
     "controls": [], "owner": "Head of product", "evidence_date": date(2026, 5, 2)},
]

def review(risks, today=None, max_age_days=120):
    today = today or date.today()          # a default of date.today() would freeze at import time
    findings = []
    for r in risks:
        score = LEVEL[r["likelihood"]] * LEVEL[r["impact"]]   # 1 to 9
        if score < 3:
            continue
        if not r["controls"]:
            findings.append(f"{r['id']}: score {score} with no controls")
        if not r["owner"]:
            findings.append(f"{r['id']}: no owner")
        if not r["evidence_date"] or (today - r["evidence_date"]).days > max_age_days:
            findings.append(f"{r['id']}: evidence missing or older than {max_age_days} days")
    return findings

for f in review(RISKS, today=date(2026, 11, 28)):
    print(f)
```

28 November 2026 ko ye print karta hai:

```text
R2: no owner
R2: evidence missing or older than 120 days
R3: score 9 with no controls
R3: evidence missing or older than 120 days
```

R1 pass hota hai: controls, ek owner aur 59 din puraana evidence. Har control ko kisi chalne waali cheez ki taraf point karna chahiye (ek eval, ek CI test, ek alert, ek scheduled review); jo control kisi cheez ki taraf point nahi karta, wo bas ek iraada hai.

### 4. Group ke hisaab se fairness test karo, un groups ko bhi jinki aapne planning nahi ki

Ek labelled test set par groups ke beech selection rates compare karo. Ek common flag hai **four-fifths rule**: jo group top group ke rate ke 80% se kam par select ho, uski jaanch honi chahiye. Ye US employment guidance (1978 Uniform Guidelines on Employee Selection Procedures) se aata hai, jahaan ye ek rule of thumb hai, aur ye EU AI Act ya Indian law ke tahat legal test nahi hai. Ise alarm samjho, faisla nahi.

```python
from collections import defaultdict

def by_group(rows, key):
    """rows: dicts with candidate fields plus 'selected' and 'qualified' (bools, from the labelled set)."""
    g = defaultdict(lambda: {"n": 0, "sel": 0, "qual": 0, "qual_sel": 0})
    for r in rows:
        s = g[key(r)]
        s["n"] += 1; s["sel"] += r["selected"]; s["qual"] += r["qualified"]
        s["qual_sel"] += r["selected"] and r["qualified"]
    rates = {k: v["sel"] / v["n"] for k, v in g.items()}
    top = max(rates.values())
    for k, v in sorted(g.items()):
        ratio = rates[k] / top if top else 1.0
        tpr = v["qual_sel"] / v["qual"] if v["qual"] else float("nan")
        note = "  <- below 0.8, investigate" if ratio < 0.8 else ""
        note += "  (small group)" if v["n"] < 100 else ""
        print(f"{k:<22} n={v['n']:<4} selected {rates[k]:>4.0%}  qualified picked {tpr:>4.0%}  ratio {ratio:.2f}{note}")
```

"Qualified picked" qualified candidates ka wo hissa hai jise model ne shortlist kiya; barabar selection rates ke peechhe aisa model chhip sakta hai jo kisi ek group mein zyada qualified logon ko miss karta hai. `key` argument se aap combinations test kar sakte ho, jaise tier aur gender. Teen traps experienced teams ko bhi pakad lete hain:

- **Proxies.** Column hataane se uska signal nahi hat-ta. PIN code, city, school board, free text mein English fluency ya resume ke gap years, region, caste, class ya gender ki jagah le sakte hain. Group input na ho, tab bhi outcomes ko group ke hisaab se test karo.
- **Intersections.** Koi model har attribute ke liye alag-alag fair dikh sakta hai aur kisi combination ke liye unfair, jaise tier-3 colleges ki women.
- **Small groups.** 60 logon se nikla rate sanyog se hi kai points upar-neeche ho jaata hai. Nateeje par pahunchne se pehle aur cases jama karo.

India mein gender, state ya region, input language, college tier aur urban banaam rural check karo.

### 5. Mehnat ko tier ke hisaab se rakho

Har system ka tier record karo aur use badalne par use dobaara dekho, jaise jab koi internal tool customers ke liye cheezein decide karne lage.

| Tier | Rakhne waale artefacts |
| --- | --- |
| Minimal | One-page system card, normal security review, staff AI training |
| Transparency | Wo card, saath mein disclosure text aur content marking, product mein test kiye hue |
| High risk | Poora register, technical documentation, schedule par group ke hisaab se fairness evals, oversight records aur override rates, logs, incident drills, post-market monitoring plan |

Evidence ko engineering ka side effect banao: har eval report ko uski date aur model version ke saath save karo, logs ko queryable rakho, approvals record karo, aur system card tabhi likho jab design dimaag mein taaza ho.

## Ek worked example

Bengaluru ki ek HR-tech company Indian employers ko resume-screening assistant bechti hai. November 2026 mein Netherlands ka ek retailer ise store staff ko shortlist karne ke liye use karna chahta hai. Neeche ke numbers sirf samjhaane ke liye hain.

**Classify karo.** Applicants ko screen karna Annex III ka employment use hai, aur individuals ko score karna profiling hai, isliye final call chahe koi bhi le, ye high risk hai. Duties 2 December 2027 se laagu hoti hain, lekin client sign karne se pehle evidence chahta hai.

**Roles baanto.** Bengaluru company provider hai; retailer deployer hai.

| Bengaluru company ki zimmedaari | Dutch retailer ki zimmedaari |
| --- | --- |
| Technical documentation aur ek quality management system | Trained recruiters se, instructions ke mutaabik use |
| Internal conformity assessment, EU declaration of conformity, CE marking | Asli human oversight, tool ki ranking reject karne ke adhikaar ke saath |
| EU database registration aur ek EU authorised representative | Apne control waale logs kam se kam chhah mahine rakhna |
| System mein built-in logging; instructions for use | Apni works council aur candidates ko batana ki tool use ho raha hai |
| Post-market monitoring aur serious incident reporting | Serious incidents aur risks provider ko report karna |

**Register banao.** Team 14 risks list karti hai, jinmein ye shaamil hain:

- **Unfair ranking**, gender, college ya region ke hisaab se: scoring se pehle names, photos aur addresses hataaye jaate hain; group ke hisaab se quarterly fairness evals.
- **Recruiters ka over-reliance:** shortlists ke saath wajahein aati hain, har rejection review hota hai, override rates monitor hote hain.
- **Data protection:** EU candidates ke liye GDPR notices aur retention, hiring cycle ke baad deletion, aur May 2027 se pehle DPDP Rules poore karne ka plan.

**Measure karo.** 400 labelled puraane applicants par, tier-1 college ke candidates 30% par shortlist hue aur tier-3 ke 19% par: 11-point ka gap, ratio 0.63. Scoring input se college names hataane ke baad:

```text
tier-1                 n=200  selected  30%  qualified picked  55%  ratio 1.00
tier-3                 n=200  selected  28%  qualified picked  52%  ratio 0.93
```

Ab gap 2 points ka hai. Tier aur gender se split karne par:

```text
tier-1, men            n=120  selected  30%  qualified picked  55%  ratio 0.98
tier-1, women          n=80   selected  30%  qualified picked  55%  ratio 0.98  (small group)
tier-3, men            n=140  selected  31%  qualified picked  57%  ratio 1.00
tier-3, women          n=60   selected  22%  qualified picked  40%  ratio 0.71  <- below 0.8, investigate  (small group)
```

Tier-3 colleges ki women four-fifths se neeche aati hain, lekin group mein sirf 60 log hain. Team ek owner aur ek date ke saath open finding log karti hai, 300 aur labelled cases jama karti hai, aur gap years aur pichhli job ki city jaise proxies check karti hai.

Retailer pack accept kar leta hai: system card, instructions for use, group ke hisaab se eval reports, logging design aur register. Yahi pack Indian enterprise clients ke saath risk reviews ko bhi chhota kar deta hai.

## Aam galtiyan

- **Build ke baad likha gaya governance.** Fairness tests, logging aur oversight ko design ko shape karna chahiye.
- **India mein hain, isliye EU AI Act ko ignore karna.** Ye tab laagu hota hai jab system ya uska output EU mein use ho.
- **Delay ko raahat samajhna.** December 2027 legal date hai; buyers abhi evidence chahte hain.
- **Technology ke hisaab se classify karna.** "Ye toh bas ek chatbot hai" kuch nahi batata; output kis kaam aata hai, yahi tier tay karta hai.
- **"Faisla toh insaan karta hai" par bharosa karna.** Profiling system ko high risk mein rakhti hai, aur jo reviewer kabhi override nahi karta, wo oversight nahi hai.
- **Ye maan lena ki client provider hai.** Agar aap ise banate ho aur apne naam se bechte ho, toh provider duties aapki hain.
- **Fairness ek baar, overall numbers par check karna.** Group aur combinations ke hisaab se, schedule par measure karo.
- **Four-fifths ko law maanna, ya column hataane ko fix maanna.** Ek US rule of thumb hai; doosra proxies ko wahin chhod deta hai.

## Jab framework zaroorat se zyada hai

- **Team productivity tools** non-sensitive data par: ek chhota card aur normal security kaafi hai.
- **Prototypes** jo kabhi real users ya real decisions tak nahi pahunchte.
- **Internal drafting tools** jahaan hamesha ek insaan output review karta hai aur uski zimmedaari leta hai.

Poora set un systems ke liye rakho jo logon ke baare mein cheezein decide ya zordaar tareeke se influence karte hain: hiring, lending, insurance, health, education aur public services.

## Demo se production tak

- **Register check ko CI mein chalao**, har release se pehle.
- **Fairness evals schedule karo**, group aur combination ke hisaab se, aur har report ko uski date aur model version ke saath store karo.
- **Har serious risk ke liye ek owner tay karo**; register ko quarterly review karo.
- **Har system ka tier aur role record karo**, aur use badalne par unhein dobaara check karo.
- **Controls ko ek baar map karo** EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF aur ISO/IEC 42001 se, aur usi mapping ko buyer questionnaires ke liye reuse karo.
- **Har quarter dates dobaara check karo.** EU timeline, RBI draft aur DPDP Rules, sab abhi bhi badal rahe hain.
