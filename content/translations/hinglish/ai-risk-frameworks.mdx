## Ye kya hai

AI governance frameworks organisations ko batate hain ki AI risks ko kaise pehchaanein, manage karein aur jawabdeh rahein. Indian teams ke liye chaar sabse zaroori hain:

- **EU AI Act**: ek kanoon jo AI uses ko risk ke hisaab se classify karta hai. Kuch uses banned hain (jaise authorities ki social scoring). **High-risk** uses, jaise hiring mein AI, credit scoring, education access, essential services aur kuch safety components, obligations laate hain: risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy aur robustness. Limited-risk uses (chatbots, generated content) par transparency duties hain. General-purpose AI models ke providers ki apni obligations hain. Ye EU market mein rakhe ya EU mein use hone waale systems par lagu hota hai, company kahin bhi ho, aur obligations kai saalon mein charno mein lagu hoti hain.
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework**: ek voluntary US framework jismein chaar functions hain: **govern** (policies, roles, culture), **map** (context aur risks), **measure** (testing aur metrics) aur **manage** (prioritise karke action lena). Ye ek saajha vocabulary ke roop mein bahut use hota hai.
- **ISO/IEC 42001**: AI management system ke liye ek international standard. Security ke ISO 27001 ki tarah, organisations ke khilaf audit aur certify ho sakti hain.
- **India ke AI Governance Guidelines** (MeitY, November 2025): safe aur trusted AI ke principles, jaise log pehle, fairness, accountability aur transparency, institutions aur industry ke liye recommendations ke saath. Ye alag AI kanoon nahi banate; ye IT Act aur DPDP Act jaise maujooda kanoonon aur sector regulators par nirbhar hain. RBI jaise regulators ne apne sectors ke liye apni guidance jaari ki hai.

| EU AI Act tier | Examples | Iska matlab |
|---|---|---|
| Banned | Authorities ki social scoring | Anumati nahi |
| High risk | Hiring, credit, education access, essential services | Poori obligations |
| Limited | Chatbots, generated content | Transparency duties |
| Minimal | Spam filters, drafting aids | Kuch extra nahi |

## Ye kaam kaise karta hai

**Use case ko classify karo.** System kya tay karta hai ya prabhavit karta hai, kiske liye, aur galti kitni gambhir hai? Internal emails ke liye drafting assistant low risk hai; loan applicants ya job candidates ko rank karne waala model EU AI Act ke tahat high risk hai aur har jagah sensitive.

**Risk register banao.** Har risk ke liye (galat output, bias, privacy breach, security attack, over-reliance, misuse), likelihood, impact, controls aur owner record karo. Frameworks overlap karte hain; ek register sab par map ho sakta hai.

**Controls ko requirements se map karo.** Typical controls aur woh kahan dikhte hain:

- documented purpose aur limits (ek AI system card);
- data governance: sources, quality checks, personal data handling;
- groups aur languages mein fairness waali evaluation;
- override karne ki asli authority waali human oversight;
- logging aur audit trails;
- users ke liye transparency ("aap ek AI assistant se baat kar rahe hain");
- incident response aur post-market monitoring;
- models aur prompts ke liye change management.

**Iraade nahi, evidence.** Auditors aur risk committees artefacts chahte hain: eval reports, group ke hisaab se test results, logs, approval records, training records. Inhein normal engineering ke hisse ke roop mein banao.

**Proportionality.** Low-risk systems ko halka documentation chahiye; high-risk ko poora set. Low-risk tools ko over-govern karna effort barbaad karta hai aur adoption ko hatotsahit karta hai.

**Frameworks ki tulna:**

| Framework | Type | Kahan asar | Mukhya maang |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| EU AI Act | Kanoon | EU mein use hone waale systems | Risk tier ke hisaab se obligations |
| NIST AI RMF | Voluntary framework | Bahut use hone waala vocabulary, US buyers | Govern, map, measure, manage |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | Certify hone layak standard | Enterprise procurement | Ek audited AI management system |
| India ke AI Governance Guidelines | Principles aur recommendations | Indian deployments, sector rules ke saath | Safe, trusted, accountable AI |

**Risk register ki ek poori row:**

| Field | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Risk | College tier ke hisaab se candidates ki unfair ranking |
| Likelihood and impact | Madhyam likelihood, high impact |
| Controls | Scoring input se college names hatao; har quarter group ke hisaab se fairness eval |
| Owner | Head of data science |
| Evidence | Quarterly fairness report; hataye gaye fields dikhata input schema |
| Mapped to | EU AI Act data governance aur accuracy duties; NIST measure aur manage |

**Chalte-chalte evidence banana.** Sabse sasti compliance achhi engineering ka side effect hai. Har eval report ko uski date aur version ke saath save karo, logs ko query karne laayak rakho, approvals record karo, aur design taaza ho tabhi system card likho. Mahino baad auditor ke liye ye evidence dobara banana kahin zyada samay leta hai aur kam vishwasniya hota hai.

**Ek register jise script check kar sake.** Risks ko data ki tarah rakho, score karo, aur jab kisi gambhir risk ke paas controls, owner ya taaza evidence na ho to review fail karo:

```python
from datetime import date

LEVEL = {"low": 1, "medium": 2, "high": 3}
RISKS = [
    {"id": "R1", "risk": "Unfair ranking by college tier", "likelihood": "medium", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["remove college names from input", "quarterly fairness eval"],
     "owner": "Head of data science", "evidence": "fairness-2026-Q3.pdf", "evidence_date": date(2026, 9, 30)},
    {"id": "R2", "risk": "Candidate data sent to a vendor that trains on it", "likelihood": "low", "impact": "high",
     "controls": ["zero-retention contract"], "owner": "", "evidence": None, "evidence_date": None},
]

def review(risks, today=date.today(), max_age_days=120):
    findings = []
    for r in risks:
        score = LEVEL[r["likelihood"]] * LEVEL[r["impact"]]
        if score >= 3:
            if not r["controls"]:
                findings.append(f"{r['id']}: score {score} with no controls")
            if not r["owner"]:
                findings.append(f"{r['id']}: no owner")
            if not r["evidence_date"] or (today - r["evidence_date"]).days > max_age_days:
                findings.append(f"{r['id']}: evidence missing or older than {max_age_days} days")
    return findings
```

**Code mein fairness checks.** Screening ya ranking system ke liye, labelled test set par groups ke beech outcomes compare karo. Do simple numbers bahut kaam aate hain:

```python
from collections import defaultdict

def by_group(rows):
    """rows: dicts with 'group', 'selected' (bool) and 'correct' (bool)."""
    g = defaultdict(lambda: {"n": 0, "selected": 0, "correct": 0})
    for r in rows:
        s = g[r["group"]]
        s["n"] += 1; s["selected"] += r["selected"]; s["correct"] += r["correct"]
    rates = {k: v["selected"] / v["n"] for k, v in g.items()}
    top = max(rates.values())
    for k, v in g.items():
        ratio = rates[k] / top if top else 1
        flag = "  <- below four-fifths" if ratio < 0.8 else ""
        print(f"{k:<16} n={v['n']:<4} selected {rates[k]:.0%}  accuracy {v['correct'] / v['n']:.0%}  ratio {ratio:.2f}{flag}")
```

India mein jin groups ko check karna chahiye unmein aksar gender, state ya region, input ki language, college tier aur urban versus rural aate hain. Chhote groups noisy rates dete hain, isliye nishkarsh nikaalne se pehle har group ke liye kaafi cases jama karo, aur selection rates ke saath-saath group ke hisaab se accuracy bhi dekho.

**Risks ko tests se jodo.** Register ka har control kisi chalne waali cheez ki taraf ishaara kare: ek eval, ek CI test, ek dashboard alert ya ek scheduled review. Agar control kisi cheez ki taraf ishaara nahi kar sakta, to woh iraada hai, control nahi.

**Proportionality, practice mein.** Har system ka tier likho aur us tier ko kaunsa documentation chahiye. Low-risk tools ke liye ek-page card aur halka register; high-risk ke liye poora register, fairness evals, human oversight records aur incident drills. Jab use badle to tier dobara dekho, jaise koi internal tool customers ke liye faisle lene lage.

## Ek worked example

Bengaluru ki ek HR-tech company Indian employers ko resume-screening assistant bechti hai aur apna pehla European client chahti hai.

Classification: job candidates ko screen karna EU AI Act ke tahat high-risk use hai, aur India mein bhi sensitive.

Team 14 risks ka ek register banati hai. Examples: gender, college ya region ke hisaab se unfair ranking (controls: groups mein fairness evaluation, scoring se pehle names, photos aur addresses hataana, quarterly bias audits); recruiters ki over-reliance (controls: tool reasons ke saath ek shortlist suggest karta hai, recruiters ko har rejection review karna hoga, override rates monitor hote hain); data protection (controls: DPDP aur GDPR ke anukool consent aur retention, hiring cycle ke baad deletion).

Woh har control ko EU AI Act ki high-risk obligations aur NIST functions se map karte hain, aur technical documentation, ek system card aur ek logging design banate hain. Ek fairness evaluation dikhata hai ki same skills waale tier-3 colleges ke candidates ki shortlist rate kam hai; team scoring input se college names hata deti hai aur gap 11 points se 2 par aa jaata hai.

European client ki procurement team documentation pack sweekaar kar leti hai. Wahi pack Indian enterprise clients ke saath reviews bhi chhote karta hai.

| Finding | Badlaav | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barabar skills waale tier-3 college candidates ki kam shortlist rate | Scoring input se college names hataye gaye | Gap 11 points se 2 par aaya |
| Recruiters bina review ke shortlists maan rahe hain | Har rejection review hona zaroori; override rates monitor | Asli oversight |
| Data hamesha ke liye rakha gaya | Hiring cycle ke baad deletion | DPDP aur GDPR retention poora |

## Aam galtiyan

- **Build ke baad frameworks ko paperwork ki tarah dekhna.** Fairness testing aur oversight jaise controls ko design banana chahiye.
- **Sab kuch ke liye ek hi level ka governance.** Effort ko risk se match karo.
- **India mein hone ki wajah se EU AI Act ko ignore karna.** Ye EU mein use hone waale systems par lagu hota hai.
- **Sirf overall accuracy par fairness test karna.** Group ke hisaab se naapo, language aur region samet.
- **Sirf naam ki oversight.** Jo insaan override nahi kar sakta ya nahi karta, woh oversight nahi hai.
- **Aise controls jo kisi chalne waali cheez ki taraf nahi dikhate.** Har control ko ek test, eval ya alert se jodo.
- **Fairness sirf launch par ek baar check karna.** Data aur users badalte hain; schedule par dohrao.
- **Chhote groups se nishkarsh nikaalna.** Pehle kaafi cases jama karo.

## Jab framework zaroorat se zyada ho

- **Non-sensitive data par personal ya team productivity tools**: ek chhota card aur normal security practice kaafi hain.
- **Prototypes** jo asli users tak kabhi nahi pahunchte.
- **Kam-asar waale internal drafting tools**, jahan koi insaan hamesha output review karke uska zimmedaar hota hai.

Poora register, mapping aur evidence un systems ke liye use karo jo logon ke baare mein cheezein tay karte hain ya unhein zyada prabhavit karte hain: hiring, lending, insurance, health, education aur public services.

## Demo se production tak

- **Register ko repository mein rakho** aur har release se pehle review check chalao.
- **Group ke hisaab se fairness evals schedule karo**, results ko evidence ke roop mein store karke.
- **Har gambhir risk ka ek owner naam se likho**, aur register ko quarterly review karo.
- **Har system ka tier record karo** aur woh kya maangta hai.
- **Controls ko ek baar map karo** un frameworks se jo aapke buyers aur regulators use karte hain, aur mapping ko dobara use karo.
