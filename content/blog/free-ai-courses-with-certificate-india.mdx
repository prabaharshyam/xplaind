Search for "free AI course with certificate" and you get hundreds of lists, many of them selling something. This one is shorter. Every course below is free to learn, and the certificate or badge is free too, unless we say otherwise. We checked each one, and we show the date we checked.

A word before the list: a certificate shows you finished a course, not that you can do the work. Use these courses to learn, then prove it with projects. More on that at the end.

## From the Government of India

**YUVA AI for All.** A free, self-paced course of about 4.5 hours in six short modules, launched in November 2025 by the Ministry of Electronics and IT under the IndiaAI Mission. It covers what AI is and how to use it responsibly, and learners who complete it get an official Government of India certificate. It is offered on [FutureSkills Prime](https://futureskillsprime.in/) and iGOT Karmayogi, among other portals. Good for: anyone starting from zero, including students and non-technical professionals. (Source: [News On AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/government-launches-yuva-ai-for-all-free-national-ai-learning-course); checked 10 Nov 2026.)

**NPTEL and SWAYAM.** IIT and IISc professors teach full-semester courses on AI, machine learning and deep learning on [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in/). Learning is free. The certificate is optional and needs a proctored exam, which has a fee, so check the fee on the course page before you register. Good for: students who want university-level depth and a certificate some colleges accept for credit. (Checked on nptel.ac.in, 10 Nov 2026.)

## Which one should you take first?

It depends on the job you want, so start there:

- **You want to use AI well at work:** YUVA AI for All or Anthropic's AI Fluency, then OpenAI's Apply AI at Work.
- **You want to build AI apps:** CS50 Python, then Anthropic's Claude API course, then the Hugging Face agents course. Our [AI Engineer roadmap](/roadmap/ai-engineer) puts these in order with a lesson for each topic.
- **You want to automate work without much code:** n8n's course, then the [AI Automation Specialist roadmap](/roadmap/ai-automation-specialist).
- **You want university depth:** an NPTEL machine learning course alongside your degree.
