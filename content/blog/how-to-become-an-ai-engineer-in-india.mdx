An AI engineer builds software that uses AI models: a chatbot that answers from a company's documents, an agent that files support tickets, a feature that reads invoices. Most AI engineering jobs in India today are about building with models such as GPT, Claude, Gemini or Llama, not inventing new ones. That is good news, because it means you can learn the job from free resources, in order, and prove it with projects.

This guide gives you that order. Every step links to a lesson on Xplaind and the course we think teaches it best.

## What an AI engineer does

Read a few AI engineer job posts on Naukri or LinkedIn and the same skills come up again and again. Our [AI jobs and salaries page](/jobs) lists them for every role. For an AI engineer they are:

- Python for production code
- calling LLM APIs from OpenAI, Anthropic and Google
- retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with a vector database
- machine learning basics with scikit-learn
- SQL
- Docker and one cloud: AWS, Azure or Google Cloud
- Git and REST APIs

Notice what is missing: research papers, PhD maths and training giant models from scratch. Those matter for research roles. For AI engineering, what matters is building something that works, testing it and running it reliably.

## The roadmap, step by step

Our [AI Engineer roadmap](/roadmap/ai-engineer) has 68 topics in 7 stages. Here is what each stage is for.

### Stage 1: Python and data

Python is the language of AI. Learn variables, loops, functions, lists and dictionaries, then files, errors, the terminal and Git, and how to call a web API and read JSON. Finish with pandas for tables.

Start with [Python basics](/stop/python-basics) and Harvard's free [CS50 Python course](/go/cs50p), which ends in a free certificate. You can write and run code in our [online Python playground](/playground) with nothing to install.

### Stage 2: Machine learning essentials

You need to know what a model learns and how to tell if it is any good: regression, classification, decision trees, cross-validation, data leakage and choosing a metric. School-level maths is enough to start; our [maths for machine learning](/stop/maths-for-ml) lesson covers the rest in plain words.

### Stage 3: Deep learning and transformers

Learn how neural networks train, the basics of PyTorch, and how the transformer behind every chat model works. Our lesson on [transformers, explained](/stop/transformers-explained) is the place to start.

### Stage 4: Building with language models

This is the heart of the job. Make your [first LLM API call](/stop/first-llm-api-call), then learn prompt design, tokens and context windows, structured JSON output, and handling errors, retries and cost. Then build [RAG](/stop/rag-app): embeddings, chunking and vector search, so a model answers from your own documents. Finish with [tool calling](/stop/tool-calling) and the [agent loop](/stop/agent-loop).

### Stage 5: Testing and safety

Anyone can build a demo. AI engineers are paid to make it reliable. Learn to build a [test set and evals](/stop/evals-test-sets), use an LLM as a judge, catch hallucinations and defend against [prompt injection](/stop/prompt-injection).

### Stage 6: Production

Package your app with Docker, serve it with FastAPI, deploy and monitor it, cache to cut latency, and [control cost](/stop/cost-control). These are the skills that separate a fresher who built a notebook from one who shipped a product.

### Stage 7: System design and interviews

Learn when to prompt, when to use RAG and when to fine-tune, how to [design an AI system](/stop/ai-system-design) on a whiteboard, and how to talk through trade-offs. Then practise the [AI engineer interview questions](/interview/for/ai-engineer) out loud.

Already know some of this? The [AI Engineer skills test](/start/ai-engineer) takes 10 minutes and skips what you know.

## Projects that get you shortlisted

Recruiters see hundreds of resumes with the same course certificates. Projects you can demo are what stand out. Build three, each a step up:

1. **A first LLM app**: a small tool that calls a model and returns structured output. Our guided project: [your first LLM app](/projects/first-llm-app).
2. **A RAG app with a test set**: questions over real documents, with a set of test questions that shows how often it is right. Guided project: [a RAG app with a test set](/projects/rag-app-with-test-set).
3. **A deployed app with monitoring**: live on the internet, with logs and a cost report. Guided project: [a deployed app with monitoring](/projects/deployed-app-with-monitoring).

Put each on GitHub with a short README: the problem, how it works, how you tested it and what you would improve. Then run your resume through our [AI resume review](/ai/resume).

## AI engineer salary in India

Typical yearly pay for AI engineers in India, from published salary data we checked on 2 Nov 2026:

| Experience | Typical pay |
| --- | --- |
| 0 to 2 years (freshers) | ₹6 to 12 lakh |
| 3 to 5 years | ₹12 to 25 lakh |
| 6 years and more | ₹25 to 50 lakh |

These are middle-of-the-road bands, not offers. Big tech companies, global AI labs and Bengaluru pay more; IT services companies and smaller cities pay less. Our [jobs page](/jobs) has the bands for all nine AI roles, the sources, and links to live openings.

## Degree or no degree?

You do not need a particular degree to build AI applications. Most companies hiring AI engineers decide on projects and interviews. A degree still helps:

- **After 12th:** a B.Tech in computer science or AI and data science, or a BCA or B.Sc in computer science, gives you campus placements and time to learn. Start Python in your first year and build projects from your second.
- **Already working, not in tech:** start with the first stage and keep going a few hours a week. If you would rather not code, the [AI Product Manager](/roadmap/ai-product-manager) and [AI Automation Specialist](/roadmap/ai-automation-specialist) roadmaps lead to AI jobs with little or no programming.
- **Already a developer:** you can move faster. Take the skills test, skip what you know and spend most of your time on stages 4 to 6.
