## Flagged for the owner

1. **DeepLearning.AI cost labels (7 files: dlai-*).** I moved all seven from `free` to `free_cert_paid`, because the videos are free but the certificate or "accomplishment" needs a paid Pro plan (or $49 on Coursera for AI for Everyone). Nothing else in the repo uses `free_cert_paid` yet, and the label changes how resources rank. Please confirm this is the intended use. Also, for the five short courses, the pages say access is free only "for a limited time during the learning platform beta", so free access could end.
2. **detect-fakes.** The study's consent form says "you may only participate if you are 18 years of age or older". That may clash with PV-2 for under-18 learners if this resource appears on a B1 path they can see. Please decide whether to keep it.
3. **aicpa-soc.** The AICPA page is now a hub of news and paid publications, not an explainer. I softened why_picked, but a plainer free SOC 2 explainer might serve learners better.
4. **Anthropic Academy certificates (anthropic-agent-skills, anthropic-ai-capabilities-limitations, anthropic-claude-api-course, anthropic-claude-code-in-action, anthropic-deploying-enterprise, anthropic-mcp-advanced, anthropic-mcp-course).** All are marked FREE. Claude 101 and AI Fluency name a "Certificate of completion" in the curriculum. For the other seven, I only found the shared Skilljar FAQ line about "completion certificates" plus a quiz or final assessment. I left `certificate: true`, but I could not confirm a certificate on each course page.
5. **aws-ai-practitioner-prep.** The Skill Builder page is built in JavaScript, so I could not read it. The official AWS certification page confirms the plan link, the AIF-C01 exam code, the USD 100 exam price and "free AI foundational training ... included in the Exam Prep Plans". Third-party sources say the extra practice exams need a paid subscription, and a free promotion ended in January 2026. The current why_picked is accurate but should be checked by hand. https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-practitioner/
6. **Week files cs50p-exceptions, cs50p-file-io and cs50p-functions** stay `certificate: false`, because one week alone does not earn the CS50 certificate. Please confirm this convention.
7. **3Blue1Brown URLs now point to YouTube.** The 3blue1brown.com topic pages no longer list lessons. If you prefer the 3blue1brown.com site, its lesson pages (for example https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/essence-of-calculus/) still work, but each covers one lesson, not the whole series.
